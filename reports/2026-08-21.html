Epic 週：EHR 巨頭把 AI 做成作業系統，同一週 FTC 走進了門 Epic Week: the EHR giant turns AI into an operating system — and the FTC walks in

2026 年 8 月 21 日（五）· 涵蓋 8/12–8/21 · 台北時間 Friday, August 21, 2026 · Covering Aug 12–21 · Taipei time

這一週美國醫療 AI 的重心只有一個座標：威斯康辛州 Verona。Epic 的年度使用者大會（UGM）於 8 月 17–20 日舉行、逾 2 萬人到場，端出的不是單一功能而是一整層作業系統：Ergo、Agent Factory 與 Cosmos Curiosity，並宣稱超過 85% 的客戶已在使用 Epic 的 AI。而在慶典的另一面，路透社 8 月 14 日獨家披露 FTC 正就競業條款與資料存取調查 Epic；同一週，估值 70 億美元的 Commure 在 STAT 的調查報導後終止了推薦金方案。今天的主題很清楚：當 AI 從功能變成平台，護城河與反托拉斯風險是同一道牆。 American health AI had one coordinate this week: Verona, Wisconsin. Epic's annual Users Group Meeting ran Aug 17–20 with more than 20,000 people on site, and what it shipped was not a feature but an operating layer: Ergo, plus Agent Factory and Cosmos Curiosity — with the claim that more than 85% of Epic customers now use Epic AI. On the other side of the party, Reuters reported on Aug 14 that the FTC is probing Epic over non-competes and data access; the same week, $7-billion-valued Commure terminated its referral-payment programmes after a STAT investigation. Today's theme is blunt: once AI becomes a platform rather than a feature, the moat and the antitrust exposure are the same wall.

Epic 推出 Ergo：不是新功能，是把 EHR 介面整層換成 AI 組裝的「醫療智慧」 Epic launches Ergo: not a feature, but an AI-assembled replacement for the EHR interface itself

發生什麼What

Epic 創辦人 Judy Faulkner 在 8 月 18 日 UGM 主場次發表 Ergo（拉丁文「因此」）——一個會隨臨床情境動態重組的醫師介面，預定 2026 年 11 月推出。其中的 Ergo Visit 把三樣東西縫在同一個門診流程裡：環境式病歷 Art、病人端聊天助理 Emmie，以及靠 Cosmos 資料庫回答「跟這位病人相似的人，哪種治療效果最好」的 Best Care Choices for My Patient（TechTarget）。目前已在 Ochsner Health 及 Epic「Garden Plot」合作方案中的五位醫師端上線。 Epic founder Judy Faulkner used the Aug 18 UGM general session to launch Ergo (Latin for "therefore"), a clinician interface that reassembles itself around the clinical context, due in November 2026. Inside it, Ergo Visit stitches three things into one outpatient flow: ambient charting (Art), the patient-facing assistant (Emmie), and Best Care Choices for My Patient, which queries the Cosmos database for what worked in medically and demographically similar patients (TechTarget). It is live today at Ochsner Health and with five physicians in Epic's Garden Plot programme.

為什麼重要Why it matters

過去兩年醫療 AI 的商業戰場是「誰的 scribe 比較準」，Ergo 把戰場整個往上抬一層。當推薦治療方案的證據來源是 Epic 自家的 Cosmos（3.2 億人、230 億次就醫紀錄），第三方 AI 供應商就算模型更強，也拿不到同一份對照資料。這是資料護城河直接轉譯成產品護城河的教科書案例——也正好是本週 FTC 調查所關切的那條線。 For two years the commercial fight in health AI was "whose scribe is more accurate." Ergo lifts the fight a layer. When the evidence behind a treatment suggestion comes from Epic's own Cosmos — 320 million people and 23 billion encounters — a third-party vendor with a better model still cannot reach the same comparator set. It is a textbook case of a data moat converting directly into a product moat, which is precisely the line the FTC probe is drawn along.

公司與產品Players

Epic Systems（威斯康辛州 Verona，私人公司，1979 年創立）；早期客戶 Ochsner Health；Chart with Art 已覆蓋 70 個以上專科，其環境式語音層由 Microsoft 的 Dragon 平台驅動（On Healthcare Technology）。 Epic Systems (Verona, Wisconsin; private; founded 1979); early adopter Ochsner Health; Chart with Art now spans 70+ specialties, with its ambient voice layer powered by Microsoft's Dragon platform (On Healthcare Technology).

FTC 調查 Epic：競業條款與「擋不擋得住別人拿資料」兩條線同時開 The FTC is probing Epic on two fronts: non-competes, and whether it blocks rivals from data

發生什麼What

路透社 8 月 14 日獨家報導，FTC 已向其他健康科技公司發出調查令（investigative demands），詢問 Epic 的資料存取實務。STAT 同日報導指調查仍在早期、至少已接觸四人，兩條問題線分別是：(1) 限制員工跳槽至競爭者的競業／保密約定；(2) 是否利用市場地位阻擋對手取得病人資料。Epic 發言人回應：「我們是互通性的領導者……我們沒有從事反競爭行為。」 Reuters reported exclusively on Aug 14 that the FTC has issued investigative demands to other health-tech firms seeking information on Epic's data-access practices. STAT reported the same day that the probe is early, that at least four people have been contacted, and that it runs along two lines: (1) non-compete and confidentiality agreements restricting staff movement to rivals; (2) whether Epic uses its dominance to block competitors from patient data. An Epic spokesperson said: "We're leaders in interoperability to support patient care, and we do not engage in anticompetitive behavior."

為什麼重要Why it matters

這不是孤立事件。德州檢察長 Paxton 已於去年 12 月控告 Epic 以資料障礙與費用限制競爭者，病歷平台 Particle Health 亦已提起反托拉斯訴訟（Epic 正力求駁回）。放在 UGM 的脈絡下看格外諷刺：Epic 一邊展示 Cosmos 帶來的 AI 優勢，一邊要向監管者論證這份資料優勢不是靠排除對手取得的。Epic 主張控制病歷存取的是醫療院所而非 Epic。 This is not isolated. Texas AG Ken Paxton sued Epic in December over data barriers and fees said to restrict competitors, and records platform Particle Health has its own antitrust suit (Epic is pushing to have it dismissed). Against the UGM backdrop the irony is sharp: Epic is demonstrating the AI advantage Cosmos confers while arguing to regulators that the underlying data advantage was not obtained by excluding rivals. Epic's position is that providers, not Epic, control access to records.

規模數字Scale

Epic 的 EHR 與 MyChart 服務的病人數超過 2.8 億美國人，其研究平台涵蓋 3.1 億人（Epic 在 UGM 自述 Cosmos 為 3.2 億人）。FTC 拒絕評論。 Epic's EHR and MyChart cover more than 280 million U.S. patients, and its research platform spans 310 million (Epic put Cosmos at 320 million at UGM). The FTC declined to comment.

發生什麼What

為什麼重要Why it matters

產業媒體 Digital Health Wire 整理的細節包括：物理治療診所若未帶進 75 萬美元年度轉介額，須支付 6.6 萬美元「轉介義務費」；聯盟夥伴可抽轉介合約 2% 佣金。前聯邦檢察官向 STAT 表示，涉及 Medicare／Medicaid 產品的付費轉介可能觸及反回扣法（Anti-Kickback Statute）——NextGen Healthcare 與 athenahealth 都曾因類似指控達成鉅額和解。這對整個「AI 賣進診所」的通路模式是個警訊：成長速度與合規曝險往往用的是同一組槓桿。 Details compiled by Digital Health Wire include physical-therapy clinics facing a $66,000 "referral obligation fee" if they failed to generate $750,000 in annual referrals, and affiliate partners earning 2% commissions on referred contracts. Former federal prosecutors told STAT that paying for referrals tied to Medicare/Medicaid products can implicate the Anti-Kickback Statute — NextGen Healthcare and athenahealth both settled similar allegations. The warning generalises to the whole "sell AI into clinics" channel model: growth velocity and compliance exposure often ride the same lever.

公司狀況Company

Customer outcomes are polarised: some report materially better collections, while a Wyoming rehab clinic lost $500k in collections and an Arizona FQHC saw $1 million in denied dental claims it was never told about. Commure calls the practices "industry standard" affecting "a small minority of customers."

兩個產品拆解：平台級的 Ergo vs. 單點深耕的 Humata Two products, taken apart: platform-scale Ergo vs single-point Humata

Epic Ergo / Ergo Visit

臨床介面層 · Art + Emmie + Cosmos · Epic Systems（美國）Clinical interface layer · Art + Emmie + Cosmos · Epic Systems (US)

功能與定位。Ergo 不是一個工具，而是把環境式病歷、病人端助理、群體資料查詢三種能力整合到同一個門診流程裡的介面層，2026 年 11 月推出；Ergo Visit 已在 Ochsner Health 與五位 Garden Plot 醫師端上線。 Function and position. Ergo is not a tool but an interface layer that folds ambient charting, a patient-facing assistant and population-scale lookup into a single outpatient flow, shipping November 2026; Ergo Visit is already live at Ochsner Health and with five Garden Plot physicians.

  • 優勢Strength ：分發通路無可比擬——Epic 佔急症照護 EHR 市場 43.7%，且逾 85% 客戶已在用 Epic AI。新功能不需要另簽採購案就能到醫師桌上。 : unmatched distribution — 43.7% of the acute-care EHR market and 85%+ of Epic customers already using Epic AI. A new feature reaches the physician's desk without a fresh procurement cycle.
  • 優勢Strength ：Cosmos 的 3.2 億人、230 億次就醫紀錄是任何第三方都複製不了的對照資料庫；Epic 也把發版週期從 18 個月縮短到 3 個月，過去「大廠慢」的劣勢正在消失。 : Cosmos's 320 million people and 23 billion encounters is a comparator set no third party can reproduce; Epic has also cut its release cycle from 18 months to three, eroding the classic "incumbents are slow" advantage challengers relied on.
  • 疑慮Concern ：幾乎沒有公開的臨床終點證據。目前見到的數字是節省工時（ECU Health 每週 20 小時、Ochsner Ask Emmie 十個月省 1,000 餘客服工時）與拒付率，皆為廠商或客戶自述，非同儕審查資料。 : almost no public clinical-outcome evidence. The numbers on offer are hours saved (20 hours a week at ECU Health; 1,000+ call-centre hours over ten months from Ask Emmie at Ochsner) and denial rates — all vendor- or customer-reported, none peer-reviewed.
  • 疑慮Concern ：核心的環境式語音層由 Microsoft Dragon 驅動，這在 Epic 四十餘年「什麼都自己做」的歷史中是異例；意味著 Epic 在最關鍵的一層對外部供應商有依賴。加上 FTC 調查，資料護城河本身即是法律風險。 : the core ambient voice layer is powered by Microsoft Dragon — an anomaly in four decades of building in-house, and a dependency at the most strategically sensitive layer. Add the FTC probe and the data moat is itself a legal exposure.

Humata Health

事前授權自動化 · 即將併入 R1 RCM Phare OSPrior-authorisation automation · being folded into R1 RCM's Phare OS

功能與定位。用 AI 自動處理醫療院所端的事前授權送件與追蹤，屬於「單一痛點做到底」的路線；R1 RCM 已簽最終協議收購，預計 2026 年 Q3 底完成。 Function and position. Automates submission and tracking of provider-side prior authorisations — a single-pain-point play taken deep; R1 RCM has a definitive agreement to acquire it, closing end of Q3 2026.

  • 優勢Strength ：終點指標直接可換算成錢——首次通過率 96%、沖銷減少 30%、改期預約減少 83%。相較之下多數臨床 AI 的終點是「省時間」，難以定價。 : its endpoints convert straight into money — 96% first-pass approval, 30% fewer write-offs, 83% fewer rescheduled appointments. Most clinical AI can only offer "time saved," which is far harder to price.
  • 優勢Strength ：市場規模由對手方的行為直接撐住——KFF 分析顯示 2025 年標準事前授權拒絕率 12–18%，只要保險方維持這個門檻，需求就存在。 : demand is underwritten by the counterparty's own behaviour — KFF puts 2025 standard prior-auth denial rates at 12–18%. As long as payers hold that bar, the market exists.
  • 疑慮Concern ：上述效能數字全為廠商自述、無獨立驗證；「首次通過率 96%」的分母（案件組合、專科別）未公開，跨機構外推性不明。 : every performance figure above is vendor-reported and independently unverified; the denominator behind "96% first-pass" (case mix, specialty) is not public, so generalisability across organisations is unknown.
  • 疑慮Concern ：被平台夾擊。Epic 同週宣布 Medical Necessity Insights 已 180 餘處上線、省下 12,500 小時，且 即時事前授權已與 UnitedHealthcare、Aetna 等在四家醫療系統上線。這也解釋了為何獨立生存不如被 R1 收購。 : squeezed by platforms. The same week, Epic said Medical Necessity Insights is live at 180+ sites having saved 12,500 hours, and that real-time prior auth is live at four health systems with UnitedHealthcare, Network Health and Aetna. Which is a good explanation of why selling to R1 beat standing alone.

04 — Taiwan Angle

台灣沒有 Epic，這既是弱點也是機會 Taiwan has no Epic — which is both a weakness and an opening

結構差異決定政策角色。美國的跨院資料互通由私人廠商的網路承擔——Epic 的 Care Everywhere 光是 6 月就交換了 9.14 億筆紀錄，也因此才會有 FTC 調查「誰能拿到這些資料」的問題。台灣的醫院多半自建或委外 HIS，沒有單一主宰廠商，於是這個角色由國家接手：衛福部與國科會共同開發的「FHIR Box」平台，依行政院政委兼國科會主委吳誠文的說法，目標是 2026 年底全台醫學中心病歷互通、2027 年擴及區域與地區醫院，再逐步推至診所。 Structure decides who plays the role. In the U.S., cross-institution interoperability runs through a private vendor's network — Epic's Care Everywhere handled 914 million record exchanges in June alone, which is exactly why the FTC is asking who gets to reach that data. Taiwan's hospitals mostly build or contract their own HIS, with no dominant vendor — so the state takes the role instead. The "FHIR Box" platform, jointly developed by the Ministry of Health and Welfare and the National Science and Technology Council, is targeted, per NSTC chair and Executive Yuan minister Wu Cheng-wen, at cross-hospital record exchange across all medical centres by end-2026, regional and district hospitals in 2027, and clinics thereafter.

今天這批新聞對台灣的三個具體意義。(1) 沒有 Cosmos，就沒有 Ergo 那一層。Epic 的「相似病人怎麼治比較好」建立在 3.2 億人的去識別資料上；台灣真正對等的資產是健保資料庫，但其研究取用與 AI 訓練的條件遠比 Cosmos 嚴格。若 FHIR Box 只做到「調閱」而未處理「二次利用」，台灣就會有互通、卻沒有可用於決策支援的群體證據層。(2) 治理框架的時間窗正在關閉。衛福部資訊處長李建璋於 8 月 6 日立法院「智慧醫療委員會」成立時表示，將於三個月內提出 AI 醫療細則草案，以透明、隱私、可課責、安全、公平、永續、人類自主七項原則為核心——換算下來大約落在 11 月，正好與 Epic Ergo 上市同期。(3) 台灣廠商的可攻擊面在「非 EHR 綁定」的層。今天表格裡唯一不被 Epic 內建化直接壓制的是 OpenEvidence 那種繞開工作流、直接被醫師自發採用的模式，以及影像 AI 這類以法規清關與給付為門檻的路線。 Three concrete implications of today's stories for Taiwan. (1) No Cosmos, no Ergo layer. Epic's "what worked in similar patients" rests on de-identified data covering 320 million people. Taiwan's genuinely comparable asset is the NHI research database — but its terms for research access and AI training are far tighter than Cosmos's. If FHIR Box delivers retrieval without addressing secondary use, Taiwan gets interoperability with no population-evidence layer to support decisions. (2) The governance window is closing. At the Legislative Yuan's Aug 6 launch of a Smart Medical Committee, MOHW information division head Li Jian-zhang said a draft set of AI-in-medicine implementing rules would come within three months, built on seven principles: transparency, privacy, accountability, safety, equity, sustainability and human autonomy — which lands around November, the same month Epic ships Ergo. (3) Taiwan's attackable surface is the layer that is not EHR-bound. The only player in today's table not directly squeezed by Epic bundling is the OpenEvidence model of bypassing workflow for bottom-up physician adoption — plus imaging AI, where regulatory clearance and reimbursement, not EHR integration, are the gate.

一個值得留意的訊號。Epic 本週宣布柏林 Charité 成為德國第一家 Epic 客戶。當 Epic 開始有系統地進入歐洲公醫體系，「一國一套自建 HIS」的模式會被迫回答一個問題：自建系統要花多久，才能追上一個每三個月發一次版、且內建 3.2 億人對照資料的平台？ One signal worth tracking. Epic announced this week that Berlin's Charité is Germany's first Epic organisation. As Epic moves systematically into European public health systems, the "every country builds its own HIS" model has to answer an uncomfortable question: how long would a home-built system need to catch a platform that ships every three months and carries a 320-million-patient comparator set inside it?
