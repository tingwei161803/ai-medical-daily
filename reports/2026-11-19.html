把生成整個拆掉：MedJev 用單次前向運算回答醫療影像問題，開源權重快 6.04 倍 Generation removed entirely: MedJev answers medical imaging questions in one forward pass, open weights 6.04x faster

發生什麼What

11 月 17 日，一篇由 Fan Zhang、Hao Wu、Jinyang Wu、Hongyu Qu、Zheng Lian、Dongfu Yin、Qingsong Wen 與 Pheng-Ann Heng 等人掛名的論文上線 arXiv（arXiv:2611.20992），把醫療視覺語言模型的做法反過來寫。現行模型是自迴歸逐字生成推理與答案，推論成本隨回答長度增長，而且答案還要從自由文字裡解析出來；MedJev 直接把醫療影像、問題與所有候選答案一起吃進去，在單一次多模態前向運算裡替每個候選答案打分，完全不生成文字。做法是砍掉語言模型上層與生成輸出路徑、保留完整視覺路徑與所有視覺 token，再接一個用預訓練 query-key 匹配打分的決策頭，外加一層輕量 MLP 殘差做任務專屬的非線性區辨（ArXivSignals 的論文摘要頁）。主模型 MedJev-4B 建在 Qwen3.5-4B 上，Hugging Face 的模型卡寫明以 Apache 2.0 釋出、訓練張量合計 25,768,961 個參數（其中決策頭 1,966,593 個），在 HAM10000、PneumoniaMNIST、MedThinkVQA 與 PMC-VQA 四個 benchmark 的訓練切分共 174,360 筆資料上跑了一個 epoch。程式碼在 GitHub 上公開。 On November 17 a paper credited to Fan Zhang, Hao Wu, Jinyang Wu, Hongyu Qu, Zheng Lian, Dongfu Yin, Qingsong Wen and Pheng-Ann Heng went up on arXiv (arXiv:2611.20992), inverting how medical vision-language models work. Current models generate reasoning and answer tokens autoregressively, so inference cost grows with response length and the answer still has to be parsed out of free text. MedJev takes the image, the question and every candidate answer together and scores each candidate in a single multimodal forward pass, generating no text at all. It strips out the upper language layers and the generative output pathway, keeps the full vision pathway and all visual tokens, then adds a decision head that scores candidates by pretrained query-key matching, with a lightweight MLP residual for task-specific nonlinear discrimination (ArXivSignals' paper page). MedJev-4B is built on Qwen3.5-4B; the Hugging Face model card states Apache 2.0 release, 25,768,961 trained tensor parameters (1,966,593 of them in the decision head), and one epoch over 174,360 examples from the training splits of four benchmarks: HAM10000, PneumoniaMNIST, MedThinkVQA and PMC-VQA. The code is public on GitHub.

數字Numbers

依模型卡上唯一那張評測表，準確率分別是 PneumoniaMNIST 94.23%、HAM10000 68.39%、PMC-VQA 61.68%、MedThinkVQA 51.94%，平均 69.06%；單張 H20、batch size 1 的平均延遲分別為 64.8、73.3、65.0 與 250.7 毫秒，三個單張影像 benchmark 平均 113.4 毫秒。作者自述較骨幹模型平均準確率高 7.93 個百分點、端到端快 6.04 倍，並聲稱在 4B–9B 級別的 20 個通用與醫療視覺語言模型比較中同時拿下準確率與推論速度的 state of the art。 Per the single evaluation table on the model card: 94.23% on PneumoniaMNIST, 68.39% on HAM10000, 61.68% on PMC-VQA and 51.94% on MedThinkVQA, averaging 69.06%. Mean latency on one H20 at batch size 1 was 64.8, 73.3, 65.0 and 250.7 ms respectively, averaging 113.4 ms across the three single-image benchmarks. The authors report a 7.93-point average accuracy gain over the backbone and a 6.04x end-to-end speedup, and claim state-of-the-art accuracy and inference speed against 20 general and medical VLMs in the 4B–9B range.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本週唯一一則「東西可以下載、數字可以重跑」的技術消息。Apache 2.0 加上 4B 規模意味著它跑得動單張消費級或院內 GPU，不需要把病人影像送出院外——對無法把資料送雲端的醫院來說，架構層的取捨比準確率小數點更重要。更值得注意的是它的方法論立場：把推理生成視為可砍的成本而非必要能力，直接挑戰過去兩年「讓模型把思路寫出來才可信」的主流路線。若這條路線成立，醫療影像問答的部署成本結構會整個換一遍。 This is the week's one technical item where the artefact can be downloaded and the numbers rerun. Apache 2.0 plus 4B scale means it runs on a single consumer or on-premise GPU, with no need to send patient images outside the hospital — and for institutions that cannot ship data to a cloud, that architectural trade-off matters more than a decimal point of accuracy. More notable is its methodological stance: it treats generated reasoning as a removable cost rather than a necessary capability, which cuts directly against the past two years' orthodoxy that a model must write out its chain of thought to be trustworthy. If the approach holds, the cost structure of deploying medical image QA changes wholesale.

Most important: there is no clinical validation of any kind — no prospective study, no reader study, no regulatory filing. This is an arXiv preprint, not yet peer reviewed, and both the 20-model comparison and the state-of-the-art claim are the authors' own.

11 月 18 日，羅馬尼亞健康科技公司 Synaptiq 宣布把腫瘤描繪與危急器官（OAR）輪廓合進 Mediq RT 平台的同一套放射治療規劃流程，臨床醫師保留輪廓核准權。技術路徑是「自動分割後接互動式修正」，公司稱之為 Active Intelligence：醫師只畫一張 gross tumor volume 切面，AI 把輪廓延伸到整個體積，在某一張切面上的修正會引導其他切面一併修改。平台支援跨解剖癌症部位的 250 多個 OAR 與淋巴結區域，每位病人初次輪廓處理約 30 秒，互動提示的反應約 1 到 2 秒；輸入是成人具放射治療適應症的 3D CT 影像，使用者設定為受訓過的放射腫瘤科醫師與醫學物理師，透過 DICOM 接既有治療規劃系統與 PACS，可雲端或院內部署。 On November 18 Romanian health-tech company Synaptiq announced that it had folded tumour delineation and organ-at-risk (OAR) contouring into a single radiotherapy planning workflow in its Mediq RT platform, with clinicians retaining approval of contours. The platform supports more than 250 OAR and lymph node regions across anatomical cancer sites; initial contour processing takes about 30 seconds per patient and responses to interactive prompts 1 to 2 seconds. 數字Numbers

為什麼重要Why it matters

輪廓描繪是放射治療規劃最耗人力的一段，而且它是少數「省下的時間可以直接換算成病人等待天數」的環節——頭頸部病例一個醫師畫 97 分鐘，排程瓶頸就在那裡。92.1% 無需修改這個數字比單純的省時百分比更有意義，因為它量的是臨床醫師實際接受程度，而非演算法與標準答案的幾何相似度。它也是本週唯一一則拿到歐盟醫療器材認證、且把對照系統具名比較的技術消息。 Contouring is the most labour-intensive stage of radiotherapy planning, and one of the few where saved time converts directly into patient waiting days — when a clinician spends 97 minutes on a head-and-neck case, that is where the scheduling bottleneck sits. The 92.1% accepted-unedited figure means more than a bare time-saving percentage, because it measures what clinicians actually accepted rather than geometric similarity between algorithm and ground truth. It is also the week's only technical item carrying an EU medical device certification and a named comparison against competing systems.
