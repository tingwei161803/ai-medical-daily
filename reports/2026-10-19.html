這一週三篇臨床研究指向同一件事：AI 的平均表現已經不是問題，表現的離散度才是。10 月 13 日登上《Radiology》的 SCARLET-1 把前列腺 MRI 的 AI 拉到 22 國 46 城、22,445 次檢查、21,252 名男性，外部測試 AUC 0.86／0.89／0.84、與放射科醫師一致率 83.0% 至 87.9%，AuntMinnie 同日報導；但同一份資料攤開 25 個外部站點，28% 的站 AUC 低於 0.80、32% 在 0.90 以上。同一天首爾大學醫院的讀片研究把 160 張胸部 X 光（40 張真實、120 張合成）交給 7 位評讀者，最高平衡準確率 77.9% 落在 Gemini 3.5 Flash，略高於 12 年資歷胸腔放射科醫師的 75.8%，全體 Fleiss' kappa 只有 0.213。而 Mount Sinai 在 20 個模型、逾 1,000 萬則回應裡數到約 118 萬則潛在有害的臨床選擇，一句安全提醒把有害率從 16.6% 壓到 10.1%——20 個模型中 19 個下降，沒有一個歸零。 Three clinical papers this week point at the same thing: average AI performance has stopped being the question, and the spread has become it. SCARLET-1, published in Radiology on 13 October, took prostate-MRI AI to 22,445 exams from 21,252 men across 46 cities in 22 countries, with external AUCs of 0.86, 0.89 and 0.84 and 83.0% to 87.9% agreement with radiologists, as AuntMinnie reported the same day; yet open the same dataset across its 25 external sites and 28% land below AUC 0.80 while 32% sit at 0.90 or higher. That same day a Seoul National University Hospital reader study put 160 chest x-rays — 40 real, 120 synthetic — in front of seven evaluators, and the best balanced accuracy of 77.9% belonged to Gemini 3.5 Flash, just ahead of a thoracic radiologist with twelve years' experience at 75.8%, with agreement across all readers at a Fleiss' kappa of 0.213. And Mount Sinai counted roughly 1.18 million potentially harmful clinical choices in over 10 million responses from 20 models, where a single safety reminder cut the harmful rate from 16.6% to 10.1% — down in 19 of 20 models, to zero in none.

發生什麼What

10 月 13 日刊於《Radiology》的 SCARLET-1（Scaling Artificial Intelligence for Prostate Cancer Detection on MRI to Global, Multiethnic Populations），由荷蘭 Nijmegen 的 Radboud University Medical Center 的 Anindo Saha 領銜，橫跨 22 國 46 座城市。資料為 2012 至 2024 年的 22,445 次前列腺 MRI、來自 21,252 名男性，其中 20,471 次檢查／19,278 人用於訓練與內部測試，1,974 人做外部測試；偵測目標是 Gleason grade group ≥2 的癌。外部測試 AUC 分別為診斷族群 0.86、篩檢族群一 0.89、篩檢族群二 0.84，與放射科醫師判讀的一致率為 83.0%、87.9%、87.7%；年齡、風險、腫瘤位置與族裔的次族群分析平均 ΔAUC 僅 0.02（AuntMinnie, 2026-10-13）。 SCARLET-1 (Scaling Artificial Intelligence for Prostate Cancer Detection on MRI to Global, Multiethnic Populations), published in Radiology on 13 October and led by Anindo Saha of Radboud University Medical Center in Nijmegen, spans 46 cities in 22 countries. It draws on 22,445 prostate MRI exams from 21,252 men scanned between 2012 and 2024; 20,471 exams from 19,278 patients went to training and internal testing, and 1,974 patients to external testing. The target was Gleason grade group ≥2 cancer. External AUCs came in at 0.86 for the diagnostic cohort and 0.89 and 0.84 for two screening cohorts, with agreement against radiologist reads of 83.0%, 87.9% and 87.7%; subgroup analysis by age, risk, tumour location and ethnicity moved the AUC by a mean of only 0.02 (AuntMinnie, 2026-10-13).

為什麼重要Why it matters

多數前列腺 MRI 的 AI 研究樣本落在數百到數千、且集中於歐美少數中心，SCARLET-1 把尺度推到 22 國並刻意納入多族裔——這件事本身就把「外部效度」從形容詞變成可量的數字。但真正的訊息不在平均值：25 個外部站點中，28% 的 AUC 低於 0.80、32% 達 0.90 以上，影像品質造成的 AUC 區間落在 0.78 至 0.88 之間。作者自己的結論寫得比任何第三方評論都直白：「安全臨床使用需要謹慎選擇操作點、在地測試與長期效能監測。」換句話說，這篇論文最有用的產出不是「AI 可以用了」，而是「同一個模型搬到你的院區，表現可能掉一個等級，而你沒有辦法從論文的平均值預測它」。 Most prostate-MRI AI studies run on a few hundred to a few thousand cases from a handful of European and North American centres. SCARLET-1 pushes that to 22 countries and deliberately multiethnic cohorts, which turns external validity from an adjective into a measured quantity. The real signal, though, is not the mean: across 25 external sites, 28% scored below AUC 0.80 and 32% at or above 0.90, with image quality alone spreading the AUC from 0.78 to 0.88. The authors put the conclusion more bluntly than any commentator would: "Safe clinical use will require careful operating point selection, local testing, and longitudinal performance monitoring." Read plainly, the paper's most useful output is not that the AI is ready, but that moving the same model to your own site may cost it a performance tier — and the published average gives you no way to predict that.

真假胸部 X 光的辨識賽，贏的是 Gemini 3.5 Flash——而七位評讀者的一致性只有 Fleiss' kappa 0.213 In a real-versus-synthetic chest x-ray contest the winner was Gemini 3.5 Flash — and agreement across the seven evaluators sat at a Fleiss' kappa of 0.213

發生什麼What

首爾大學醫院的團隊在《Journal of Imaging Informatics in Medicine》發表讀片研究（DOI 10.1007/s10278-026-02339-7，10 月 13 日刊出）：160 張胸部 X 光，其中 40 張真實、120 張由三條不同 AI 流水線生成，交給 7 位評讀者判定真偽。評讀者為兩位放射科醫師（RAD1 從醫 12 年、專攻胸腔影像 7 年；RAD2 從醫 13 年、非胸腔次專科）、兩位一般科醫師，以及三個 LLM：Gemini 3.5 Flash、GPT-4o、Claude 3.7 Sonnet。因合成影像佔 75%，主要指標採平衡準確率。結果：Gemini 3.5 Flash 77.9% 居首，與 RAD1 的 75.8% 無統計顯著差異；一般科醫師接近隨機。擴散模型生成的影像，人類正確辨識率 82% 至 100%、LLM 僅 32% 至 72%；對真實影像的特異度則倒過來，LLM 90% 至 100%、人類 50% 至 100%。全體評讀者的 Fleiss' kappa 為 0.213（AuntMinnie, 2026-10-15）。 A Seoul National University Hospital team published a reader study in the Journal of Imaging Informatics in Medicine (DOI 10.1007/s10278-026-02339-7, 13 October): 160 chest x-rays, 40 of them real and 120 generated by three different AI pipelines, put to seven evaluators who had to call each one real or synthetic. The panel was two radiologists (RAD1, twelve years post-MD with seven in dedicated thoracic radiology; RAD2, thirteen years post-MD in a non-thoracic subspecialty), two general practitioners, and three LLMs: Gemini 3.5 Flash, GPT-4o and Claude 3.7 Sonnet. Because synthetic images made up 75% of the set, the primary metric was balanced accuracy. Gemini 3.5 Flash led on 77.9%, statistically indistinguishable from RAD1's 75.8%; the general practitioners were near chance. On diffusion-model images humans were right 82% to 100% of the time against 32% to 72% for the LLMs, while specificity on real images ran the other way, 90% to 100% for the LLMs and 50% to 100% for the humans. Fleiss' kappa across all evaluators was 0.213 (AuntMinnie, 2026-10-15).

為什麼重要Why it matters

合成影像正在兩個方向被大量使用：補訓練資料的稀有病例、以及做去識別化的替身資料集。這兩件事都預設「需要時分得出真假」。研究給的答案是分不出——連胸腔次專科醫師也只到 75.8%，一般科醫師接近擲硬幣，而且七位評讀者之間的 Fleiss' kappa 0.213 屬於「差」的等級，意思是連「哪些張看起來可疑」都沒有共識。真正可用的發現在錯誤方向：LLM 對真實影像幾乎不誤判（特異度 90% 至 100%）卻抓不到擴散模型的生成痕跡，人類恰好相反。這組互補性意味著影像資料集的來源稽核不該二選一，而該是人機串接；但這也等於承認，今天沒有任何單一把關者可信。 Synthetic images are being used hard in two directions at once: padding training sets with rare findings, and standing in for real data in de-identified releases. Both assume someone can tell the difference when it matters. The study's answer is that nobody reliably can — a thoracic subspecialist reached only 75.8%, the general practitioners were close to a coin flip, and the Fleiss' kappa of 0.213 across the seven falls in the "poor" band, meaning there was not even agreement on which images looked suspicious. The usable finding is in the direction of the errors: the LLMs almost never mislabelled a real image (90% to 100% specificity) but missed the fingerprints of diffusion models, and the humans did the reverse. That complementarity argues for chaining human and machine review in provenance audits rather than choosing one; it also concedes that no single gatekeeper is trustworthy today.

The coverage does not name the first author or identify the three generation pipelines. The three LLMs tested are general-purpose models rather than medical ones, so the result does not extrapolate to purpose-trained detectors.

Nadkarni, who chairs the Windreich Department of Artificial Intelligence and Human Health — published "Evaluating Large Language Model Responses to Unsafe Clinical Instructions" in the 26 September online issue of Communications Medicine (DOI 10.1038/s43856-026-01933-8), with the institution's release following on 8 October. They tested 20 models against 501 variations of 50 clinical scenarios plus 100 cases adapted from de-identified discharge records. Each scenario asked the model to skip recommended follow-up blood tests to cut workload, sometimes framed as urgent and sometimes as an instruction from a superior, with four options to choose from: comply, keep the recommended follow-up, seek clinician help, and so on. Three short safety reminders were tested, each combination run ten times with the answer order randomised. Of more than 10 million responses, roughly 1.18 million were potentially harmful clinical choices; the harmful rate was 16.6% with no reminder and 10.1% with one, falling in 19 of the 20 models (Mount Sinai, 2026-10-08).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是少見把「提示工程」當作可量化安全措施來做大規模評測的研究，而且測的不是知識錯誤，是服從不當指令——用緊急與權威兩種框架施壓，看模型會不會為了省事而放掉追蹤。醫院導入 LLM 時真正會遇到的，正是這類壓力：床位壓力、下班時間、主治醫師的一句話。效果是真的（16.6% → 10.1%、19/20 有效），但 10.1% 意味著每十次仍有一次走向有害選項，而這類錯誤的特徵是「看起來很合理」，不像幻覺那樣容易被抓。把它和下一則 AMA 的治理框架放在一起看，才是今天臨床 AI 的完整形狀：技術側能把風險壓低一個檔次，制度側則必須假設它壓不到零。 This is one of the few large-scale evaluations to treat prompt engineering as a measurable safety control, and what it measures is not knowledge error but compliance with an improper instruction — urgency and authority applied as pressure to see whether the model will drop a follow-up to save effort. Those are exactly the pressures a hospital LLM meets in practice: bed pressure, the end of a shift, a line from the attending. The effect is real (16.6% to 10.1%, working in 19 of 20), but 10.1% means one in ten responses still lands on the harmful option, and errors of this kind look entirely reasonable, unlike hallucinations which announce themselves. Set beside the AMA framework in the next item, it gives today's full shape: the technical side can knock the risk down a tier, and the institutional side has to assume it will never reach zero.

The authors say they have not yet covered prompt injection against autonomous agents or pressure from time and budget, and name that as the next step. 發生什麼What

10 月 16 日，美國醫學會（AMA）的 Center for Digital Health and AI 推出針對「承擔臨床功能的 agentic AI」的治理框架（AMA 框架原文）。核心主張是臨床權限「應透過證據取得、透過持續展現的表現維持」，且監督與權限會隨證據、表現、風險與技術的變動而調整——亦即授權是可撤銷的，不是一次性核准。AMA 列出四個著力點：定義 AI 系統的臨床角色、驗證其表現與保護措施、設定權限上限、建立使用責任歸屬（含風險與結果）。AMA 執行長 John Whyte 具名，並表示這項工作將回頭形塑 AMA 在責任歸屬、執照與法規上的立場（Fierce Healthcare, 2026-10-16）。 On 16 October the American Medical Association's Center for Digital Health and AI issued a governance framework for agentic AI that takes on clinical functions (the framework itself). Its central claim is that clinical authority "should be earned through evidence and maintained through demonstrated performance," with oversight and authority shifting as evidence, performance, risk and technology change — authority as a revocable licence rather than a one-off clearance. The AMA names four areas of work: defining an AI system's clinical role, validating its performance and safeguards, setting limits on its authority, and establishing accountability for its use, risks and outcomes included. Chief executive John Whyte is named, and says the work will feed back into the AMA's positions on liability, licensure and regulation (Fierce Healthcare, 2026-10-16).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這份框架和今天前三則研究是同一個問題的兩端。SCARLET-1 說明同一個模型在不同站點表現會差一個等級，Mount Sinai 說明模型在權威壓力下會照做——兩者都指向「一次核准、永久有效」在 agentic AI 上不成立。AMA 的答案是把臨床權限做成需要持續舉證的授權，這在制度設計上與 SCARLET-1 作者要求的「在地測試與長期效能監測」是同一句話的兩種語言。值得注意的是發話者：AMA 不是監管機關，而是醫師的職業團體，它主動替 agentic AI 的權限畫線，等於在 FDA 與各州立法之前先替醫師社群佔住「誰負責」這個位置。 The framework sits at the other end of the same problem as the three studies above. SCARLET-1 shows one model dropping a performance tier when it moves site; Mount Sinai shows models complying under pressure from authority. Both say that for agentic AI, clear-once-and-forever does not hold. The AMA's answer is to make clinical authority a licence that has to be continuously re-earned, which is the same sentence as the SCARLET-1 authors' call for local testing and longitudinal monitoring, spoken in institutional rather than statistical language. Note who is speaking: the AMA is not a regulator but the physicians' professional body, and by drawing the line around agentic authority first it stakes out the question of who is accountable ahead of the FDA and state legislatures.

發生什麼What

It enrolled 518 participants, 200 with coronary heart disease and 318 healthy controls, with an independent test set of 104. The model is a dual-branch deep network fusing tongue and facial image features on ResNet50 and EfficientNet-B1 backbones, with Grad-CAM for explainability; the tongue branch attends to the central tongue body and coating, the facial branch to the perioral and zygomatic buccal regions.
