醫療 AI 的真實使用量第一次有了同儕審查的數字：2.71 億次查詢、952,008 名美國臨床人員，而最重的使用者是最資淺的醫師 The first peer-reviewed count of how much medical AI is really used: 271 million queries, 952,008 US clinicians — and the heaviest users are the most junior doctors

發生什麼What

10/15 登上 JAMA 的橫斷面研究分析 OpenEvidence 去識別化查詢紀錄：2024 年 1 月 1 日至 2026 年 6 月 30 日共 2.71 億次查詢、952,008 名已驗證 NPI 的美國臨床人員（其中 556,200 名醫師、171,034 名 NP、93,241 名 PA、35,819 名藥師）。月活躍使用者從 2024 年 1 月的 355 人長到 2026 年 6 月的 703,265 人，月查詢量從約 6,000 次到 2,780 萬次，每位活躍使用者每月查詢數從 18.0 升到 39.5，月留存從約 70% 升到約 90%（2026 年 6 月有 92.5% 的活躍使用者是回訪者）。最刺眼的一組是年資分布：畢業未滿十年的醫師只占醫師人力的 24.3%，卻送出 61.8% 的醫師查詢。跨專科使用從血液腫瘤科的 35% 到急診醫學的 88%；外科醫師有 70% 的查詢問的是非外科題目。JAMA, 2026-10-15（DOI 10.1001/jama.2026.21868）；Becker's 同日整理。 The cross-sectional study published in JAMA on 15 Oct analysed de-identified OpenEvidence query logs from 1 January 2024 to 30 June 2026: 271 million queries from 952,008 NPI-verified US clinicians (556,200 physicians, 171,034 nurse practitioners, 93,241 physician assistants, 35,819 pharmacists). Monthly active users rose from 355 in January 2024 to 703,265 in June 2026; monthly queries from roughly 6,000 to 27.8 million; queries per active user from 18.0 to 39.5 a month; month-over-month retention from about 70% to about 90% (92.5% of June 2026 actives were returning users). The sharpest figure is the seniority split: physicians within ten years of graduation are 24.3% of the physician workforce but filed 61.8% of physician queries. Out-of-specialty querying ran from 35% in haematology-oncology to 88% in emergency medicine; surgeons aimed 70% of their queries at non-surgical topics. JAMA, 2026-10-15 (DOI 10.1001/jama.2026.21868); Becker's, same day.

為什麼重要Why it matters

在這篇之前，「有多少醫師在用 AI」只有廠商自述。現在有了一個下界，而這個下界的形狀比總量更有意思：使用強度與年資成反比（NP 與 PA 每人每月 44.5 次、醫師 37.1 次、執業滿二十年者使用比只有 0.31），而且 51.9% 的醫師查詢落在臨床決策上。換句話說，這不是在查參考資料，是在補判斷。那就把本週第二則與第三則變成同一個問題的兩面：這麼大量的判斷輔助，上線後沒人在量它表現，而監管框架還沒寫完。 Before this paper, the answer to how many clinicians use AI came only from vendors. Now there is a lower bound, and its shape matters more than its size: intensity runs inversely to seniority (44.5 queries per active user-month for NPs and PAs, 37.1 for physicians, a use ratio of just 0.31 for those twenty-plus years out), and 51.9% of physician queries sit in clinical decision-making. That is not reference lookup; it is judgement support. Which makes the week's second and third items two faces of one question: judgement support at this volume, with nobody measuring its performance after go-live and the regulatory framework still unwritten.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這篇回顧提供了本週最有用的一組對照數字：同一份文獻裡既有「規管級證據」的成功案例，也有部署後崩掉的案例。成功的那些很硬——FDA 授權的肺栓塞工具在 11,736 次測試中陰性預測值 99.9%、工作清單偵測時間從 7,714 分鐘降到 87 分鐘、漏診從 44.8% 降到 2.6%；某敗血症預測演算法把抗生素送達時間壓到 2.76 小時、院內死亡率從 21.3% 降到 9.0%（相對降幅 58%）。崩掉的那些同樣硬——某 COVID-19 胸部 X 光工具在公開資料集 AUC 逾 0.95，前瞻部署後掉到約 0.70（19 週間四分位區間 0.66–0.74）。差別不在模型，在有沒有人在上線後量它。而量它的那套制度，全美只有略過半數的醫院有。 This review supplies the week's most useful contrast, because it holds both the regulatory-grade successes and the post-deployment collapses in one place. The successes are hard numbers: an FDA-authorised pulmonary embolism tool reached 99.9% negative predictive value across 11,736 tests, cut worklist detection time from 7,714 minutes to 87 and missed cases from 44.8% to 2.6%; a sepsis prediction algorithm got antibiotics delivered within 2.76 hours and cut in-hospital mortality from 21.3% to 9.0%, a 58% relative reduction. So are the failures: a COVID-19 chest radiograph tool scoring above 0.95 AUC on open datasets fell to roughly 0.70 in prospective deployment (0.66–0.74 across quartiles over 19 weeks). The difference is not the model; it is whether anyone measures it after go-live. And barely half of US hospitals have the machinery to do that.

治理Governance AMAagentic AIAgentic AI10/13

在聯邦還沒答的地方，AMA 自己先答了：臨床權限要靠證據取得、靠已展現的表現維持 Where the federal answer is still missing, the AMA wrote its own: clinical authority must be earned through evidence and maintained through demonstrated performance

發生什麼What

10/13 美國醫學會（AMA）發布 agentic AI 治理框架，核心主張是 AI 系統的「臨床權限必須靠證據取得、靠持續表現維持」，且其權限與監督層級會隨證據、表現、風險或底層技術的變化而升降。框架以四個問題展開：功能（執行哪項臨床任務，從文書與監測到分流、診斷、治療、處方）；證據與風險（憑什麼證明它能做，失敗的後果是什麼——風險與自主性愈高，證據門檻愈高）；權限與監督（可以採取哪些行動、在什麼條件下，從提供意見到自行決策並執行）；問責（誰承擔風險與結果，責任應隨權限、控制力、風險認知與防止傷害的能力分配，可能落在開發者、部署者、醫療體系、醫師或支付方）。AMA 並明確界定此處的 licensure 指「授權一個 AI 系統在定義好的臨床角色中運作」，不是行醫執照。Becker's, 2026-10-13；MedTech Dive；Fierce Healthcare。 On 13 October the American Medical Association published a governance framework for agentic AI, whose core claim is that an AI system's clinical authority must be earned through evidence and maintained through ongoing performance, with authority and oversight rising or falling as evidence, performance, risk or the underlying technology change. It runs on four questions. Function: which clinical task, on a span from documentation and monitoring to triage, diagnosis, treatment and prescribing. Evidence and risk: what demonstrates the system can do it and what failure costs — higher stakes and greater autonomy demand stronger evidence. Authority and oversight: which actions it may take and under what conditions, from offering input to making and executing decisions. Accountability: who bears the risk and the outcome, with responsibility tracking authority, control, knowledge of risk and ability to prevent harm — developers, deployers, health systems, physicians or payers. The AMA explicitly defines licensure here as authorising an AI system to operate in a defined clinical role, not a licence to practise medicine. Becker's, 2026-10-13; MedTech Dive; Fierce Healthcare.

為什麼重要Why it matters

AMA 這份框架與 FDA 討論文件在同一週出現、用的還是同一個比喻（授證／能力評估），差別是 AMA 把「可升可降」寫進了設計，而 FDA 連上市後控制手段是否在現行法定授權內都還沒答。這代表治理的實質內容正在從聯邦往專業團體與醫療體系自己身上移——對廠商來說，未來擋在前面的可能不是一張 510(k)，而是每一家醫療體系自己的權限分級與再評估週期。 The AMA framework and FDA's discussion paper landed in the same week on the same metaphor — credentialing, competency assessment — with one difference: the AMA builds revocability into the design, while FDA has not yet answered whether its postmarket levers even sit inside existing statutory authority. The substance of governance is migrating from the federal level to professional bodies and health systems themselves. For vendors, the gate ahead may not be a 510(k) but each health system's own authority tiers and reassessment cycle.

AMA 框架不具法律強制力，也未定義正式的層級或分級門檻——Becker's 明確指出文件描述的是權限的連續帶與持續循環，而非成文的級距。本則數字性內容為零，是一份原則文件。 The AMA framework carries no legal force and defines no formal tiers or numeric thresholds — Becker's notes explicitly that the document describes a continuum of authority and a continuous cycle rather than codified levels. There are no statistics in it; it is a principles document.

功能與定位。臨床人員以自然語言提問，平台回傳附引用的綜合答案。本週的 JAMA 研究給出了它的使用形狀：51.9% 的醫師查詢用於臨床決策、33.1% 關於治療與處置、14.1% 關於藥物資訊、13.9% 關於診斷，而跨專科查詢占 35% 至 88%——也就是說，它的主要戰場不是「查自己的專科」，而是「處理不在自己射程內的病人」。商業模式方面，新聞稿與研究均載明對已驗證美國臨床人員免費，JAMA, 2026-10-15。 Function and position. Clinicians ask in natural language and get a synthesised, cited answer. This week's JAMA study gives the shape of its use: 51.9% of physician queries serve clinical decision-making, 33.1% concern treatment and management, 14.1% medication information, 13.9% diagnosis — and out-of-specialty queries run 35% to 88%. Its real battleground is not looking things up inside one's own specialty but handling patients outside one's range. On the business model, both the release and the study state it is free to verified US clinicians, JAMA, 2026-10-15.

  • 優勢Strength ：留存。月留存從約 70% 升到約 90%，2026 年 6 月有 92.5% 的活躍使用者是回訪者，只有 9.6% 的臨床人員用過一天就沒再回來——這種曲線在臨床工具裡罕見，JAMA 研究。 : retention. Month-over-month retention rose from about 70% to about 90%, 92.5% of June 2026 actives were returning users, and only 9.6% of clinicians queried on a single day and never came back — a curve rarely seen in clinical tools, JAMA study.
  • 優勢Strength ：機構正當性。10/13 與 Johns Hopkins Medicine 的合作直接接上醫學教育端，JHM 新聞稿。 : institutional legitimacy. The 13 Oct collaboration with Johns Hopkins Medicine plugs straight into medical education, JHM release.
  • 疑慮Concern ：那份量到規模的研究，資料與部分作者都來自公司本身（Zack 為首席醫務長），而研究完全沒有量結果——沒有準確率、沒有臨床結局、沒有誤導率。使用量與有效性是兩件事，這週只證明了前者。 : the study that measured the scale drew both its data and several authors from the company itself (Zack is its chief medical officer), and it measures no outcomes at all — no accuracy, no clinical endpoints, no error rate. Usage and effectiveness are different things, and this week only established the first.
  • 疑慮Concern ：年資分布是雙面刃。畢業未滿十年者占 24.3% 人力、送出 61.8% 查詢，這既是最強的成長引擎，也是 Endpoints 10/15 那場專題座談（UCI Health 的 Pandita、BIDMC 的 Rodman 對談）爭論的核心：臨床推理能力會被養成還是被外包，目前沒有縱貫資料可答。 : the seniority distribution cuts both ways. Physicians within ten years of graduation are 24.3% of the workforce and file 61.8% of queries — the strongest growth engine and also the crux of the debate at the Endpoints panel on 15 Oct (Pandita of UCI Health and Rodman of Beth Israel Deaconess): whether clinical reasoning gets trained or outsourced. No longitudinal data answers it yet.

Suki

環境臨床文書與 agent 平台 · Suki（美國）· 以嵌入式通路為主Ambient clinical documentation and agent platform · Suki (US) · embedded-channel first

功能與定位。核心是環境聽寫與臨床文書，但本週 9,000 萬美元 E 輪的用途明確寫成「打造處理轉診與事前授權的 AI agent」。它與 Abridge、Ambience、Nabla 最大的策略差異在通路：逾 30 家技術夥伴把 Suki 嵌入自家平台（Optum、Zoom、athenahealth），而非一家一家簽醫療體系；公司同時自述逾 450 家醫療機構使用其臨床端應用，Business Wire, 2026-10-13。 Function and position. The core is ambient dictation and clinical documentation, but this week's $90M Series E names its use of funds plainly: AI agents for referrals and prior authorisation. Its biggest strategic difference from Abridge, Ambience and Nabla is channel — more than 30 technology partners embed Suki inside their own platforms (Optum, Zoom, athenahealth) rather than signing health systems one by one, while the company also claims over 450 healthcare organisations on its clinician-facing app, Business Wire, 2026-10-13.

  • 優勢Strength ：通路的擴散速度。夥伴業務營收兩年成長逾十倍、2026 年支援看診人次年成長逾 190%，都是嵌入式路徑的直接結果，公司新聞稿。 : channel diffusion speed. Partner-business revenue up more than tenfold in two years and encounters supported up more than 190% year over year in 2026 are both direct consequences of the embedded route, company release.
  • 優勢Strength ：有第三方可引述的經濟效益。KLAS 驗證的每位醫師每月逾 2,600 美元財務效益，是這條賽道少數不是純自述的數字，Healthcare Dive。 : a citable third-party economics figure. KLAS-validated financial impact of more than $2,600 per provider per month is one of the few numbers in this lane that is not purely self-reported, Healthcare Dive.
  • 疑慮Concern ：通路租來的客戶關係淺。嵌在 Optum 或 athenahealth 裡的 Suki，換掉的成本由夥伴決定，而不是由使用它的醫師決定；執行長自己說要往既有夥伴做深，某種程度上是承認這一點。 : rented customer relationships are shallow. For a Suki embedded inside Optum or athenahealth, the switching decision belongs to the partner, not to the physician using it; the CEO's stated plan to go deeper with existing partners is, in part, an acknowledgement of that.
  • 疑慮Concern ：轉診與事前授權是 agent 真正會做決定的地方，而這正好落在本週 AMA 框架與 FDA 討論文件都還沒定案的那一格。公司新聞稿對 agent 的監督機制、錯誤率、責任歸屬一個字都沒提——缺什麼數據就直說缺，這裡缺的是全部。 : referrals and prior authorisation are exactly where an agent makes real decisions, and that falls precisely in the cell that both the AMA framework and FDA's discussion paper left unsettled this week. The company's release says nothing at all about agent oversight, error rates or accountability — and where a figure is missing it should be said: here all of them are.

04 — Taiwan Angle

本週美國量到的那個缺口，台灣在 2024 年就為它蓋了機構；現在要驗的是機構有沒有在運作 The gap measured in the US this week is one Taiwan built institutions for back in 2024; what needs checking now is whether those institutions are working

(1) 美國缺的「取證」與「給付」環節，台灣在兩年前就立了名字。衛福部 2024 年 10 月 7 日啟動三大 AI 中心：負責任 AI 執行中心（管理規範、資安與隱私、模型與資料與效能資訊公開、可解釋性分析、模型生命週期管理）、臨床 AI 取證驗證中心（與資訊處及 TFDA 合辦，串接醫院網絡驗證模型、加速審查）、AI 影響性研究中心（與資訊處及健保署合辦，跨體系臨床試驗以評估臨床效益並建立給付的科學定價基礎）。該計畫 2024 年 7 月 23 日公告、8 月 1 日截止收件，共 30 家醫院提出 48 件計畫，經三輪國內外委員審查後由 16 家醫院、19 件計畫入選，名單包含台大、中榮、北榮、成大、中國醫、三總、林口長庚等，衛福部新聞稿, 2024-10-07。把這份架構疊到本週那篇 Frontiers 回顧的四個缺口上，對應關係幾乎是一比一：上市後監測對負責任 AI 執行中心、公平性與次族群表現對取證驗證中心、給付與臨床效益對影響性研究中心。台灣缺的不是制度設計，而是公開的執行數據——這三個中心運作兩年後，有多少模型完成驗證、有多少進入給付、上線後監測的覆蓋率是多少，目前在公開資料中都查不到。美國那篇回顧之所以有力，正是因為它拿得出 58.1%、47.4%、51.4% 這種難看的數字。 (1) The evidence-generation and reimbursement links the US is missing are ones Taiwan named two years ago. On 7 October 2024 the Ministry of Health and Welfare launched three AI centres: a Responsible AI Implementation Centre (management rules, information security and privacy, publication of model, data and performance information, explainability analysis, model lifecycle management); a Clinical AI Evidence Validation Centre, run with the Ministry's Department of Information Technology and the TFDA, linking hospital networks to validate models and speed review; and an AI Impact Research Centre, run with the Department of Information Technology and the National Health Insurance Administration, running cross-system clinical trials to assess clinical benefit and set a scientific pricing basis for reimbursement. The subsidy programme was announced on 23 July 2024 and closed on 1 August; 30 hospitals submitted 48 proposals, and after three rounds of review by domestic and international committee members 16 hospitals and 19 proposals were selected, including National Taiwan University Hospital, Taichung Veterans General, Taipei Veterans General, National Cheng Kung University Hospital, China Medical University Hospital, Tri-Service General Hospital and Linkou Chang Gung, MOHW release, 2024-10-07. Lay that architecture over the four gaps in this week's Frontiers review and the mapping is almost one to one: post-market monitoring to the Responsible AI centre, equity and subgroup performance to the Validation centre, reimbursement and clinical benefit to the Impact Research centre. What Taiwan lacks is not institutional design but published execution data — two years in, how many models have completed validation, how many have entered reimbursement, and what share of deployments are monitored after go-live are all unavailable in public sources. The US review carries weight precisely because it can produce ugly numbers like 58.1%, 47.4% and 51.4%.

(2) AI 基本法的醫療細則草案，原本就該在這幾週出現。2026 年 8 月 6 日衛福部表示，AI 基本法之下的醫療領域 AI 實施細則草案預計三個月內提出——換算就是 11 月前後，也就是現在。同一則報導中的 FHIR Box 進度是：2025 年由三家醫學中心試辦，2026 年底上線、全台醫學中心病歷互通，三到五年內擴及全國約 80% 醫療機構（報導並以歐盟約需十年、單一醫院重建病歷系統成本可能逾百億作為對照），聯合報, 2026-08-06。把本週的國際消息換算成台灣尺度：AMA 10/13 那份框架處理的是「權限分級與再評估」，FDA 10/19 關窗的討論文件處理的是「上市前能力評估與上市後監測」，而台灣即將出現的細則草案若只寫原則、不寫誰在上線後量、量什麼、多久量一次，就會重複美國這週被量出來的那個缺口——制度齊備、執行空白。對產業端的實際意涵是：現在是提意見的時間窗，不是草案出來之後。 (2) The draft medical implementation rules under the AI Basic Act were due in exactly these weeks. On 6 August 2026 the Ministry said a draft of AI implementation rules for the medical field under the AI Basic Act was expected within three months — which lands around November, meaning now. The same report put FHIR Box's progress as: three medical centres piloting in 2025, launch at end of 2026 with records interoperable across Taiwan's medical centres, and expansion to roughly 80% of medical institutions nationwide within three to five years, set against an EU approach the report describes as taking about ten years and a note that rebuilding one hospital's record system alone could cost over NT$10 billion, United Daily News, 2026-08-06. Converting this week's international news to Taiwanese scale: the AMA's 13 Oct framework addresses authority tiers and reassessment; FDA's paper closing on 19 Oct addresses premarket competency assessment and postmarket monitoring. If Taiwan's forthcoming draft states principles only — without saying who measures after go-live, what they measure and how often — it will reproduce the very gap the US had measured this week: complete institutions, empty execution. The practical implication for industry is that the window for comment is now, not after the draft appears.

(3) 本土產品端的一個具體對照。10/5 中央社報導，智捷醫學科技（董事長孫繼信，已併購日本醫學影像公司 Holoeyes）的 AI 3D 醫療影像系統可將 CT 與 MRI 在約 3 至 5 分鐘內轉為彩色 3D 模型、辨識並分離逾 260 個器官與組織，網頁版可在電腦、手機、平板檢視或輸出至混合實境頭戴裝置，目前在衛福部資料中心測試，自動定位功能正在申請專利、目標毫米級精度；公司目標是配合 2028 年推進至全台 29 家醫學中心，Holoeyes 在日本已有逾百家醫院網絡，Newtalk 轉載中央社, 2026-10-05。這一則放在本週脈絡裡的意義是：台灣有能力把產品做到可輸出 IP 的程度，但新聞中既無任何核准或認證、也無臨床結果數據，在衛福部資料中心測試不等於取證。這恰好是三大 AI 中心裡「取證驗證」那一環該產出的東西——如果它在運作，這類產品的下一則新聞應該是驗證結果，而不是導入家數目標。 (3) One concrete domestic comparison. CNA reported on 5 October that Chih-Chieh Medical Technology (chaired by Sun Chi-hsin, which has acquired the Japanese medical imaging firm Holoeyes) has an AI 3D medical imaging system that converts CT and MRI scans into coloured 3D models in about 3 to 5 minutes, identifying and separating more than 260 organs and tissues, viewable on computers, phones and tablets or exportable to mixed-reality headsets, currently under test in the Ministry's data centre, with a patent pending on automatic positioning and a millimetre-accuracy target. The company's stated goal is to roll out to Taiwan's 29 medical centres by 2028, and Holoeyes already has a network of more than 100 hospitals in Japan, Newtalk reposting CNA, 2026-10-05. What this means in this week's context: Taiwan can build products to the point of exporting intellectual property, but the report contains neither any clearance or certification nor any clinical outcome data, and testing in the Ministry's data centre is not evidence generation. That is precisely what the validation link among the three AI centres exists to produce — if it is working, the next news item about products like this should be validation results, not a target number of hospitals.
