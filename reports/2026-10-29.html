發生什麼What

Anthropic 在 10/27 發布（10/28 更新）的「Early progress on accelerating drug development with Claude」裡，一次攤開六組合作的實測數字。Generate Biomedicines：六名科學家用 Claude Code 提出 CAR-T 細胞療法某個基因元件的較短設計，實驗室測試中其中一個版本比標準版短約 70%、表現更好，免疫細胞做出約 10 倍受體、殺死腫瘤細胞效率更高、活性維持更久。Divergence Labs：Claude 把團隊目標轉成設計計畫，約一個月產出約 35,000 個抗體設計，早期測試得到 3 個能結合 IgE、20 個能把 IgE 從免疫細胞上拉下來，其中 3 個比現行藥物更有效。BigHat Biosciences：約 10 週內做出並測試約 1,010 個抗體片段變體，最佳版本親和力提升逾 10 倍，抗癌標的那條 lead 在溶液中的耐熱性高出 18°C、長效那條聚集速度慢逾 100 倍；過程中團隊發現標準熱穩定與聚集指標會誤導，Claude 與研究者重新分析並提出新指標。Enveda：Claude 審閱 1,061 頁製造文件，人類審查者找到的 11 個問題全數命中，另外標出 7 個被漏掉的，人類審查時間從逾 25 小時降到不到 3 小時（約 -89%）；兩份法規文件其中一份 Claude 四小時完成，人工約需三個月。另外 The Column Group 正成立新公司 Pleiades，首個 Claude skill 已在 45 組標的—疾病配對上測試；Bristol Myers Squibb 用 Claude 評估早期臨床試驗計畫的成功機率，尚無數字。Anthropic 自己則收購了一人公司 Mithril Medicine，其創辦人用 Claude 管理 11 間外部實驗室的合約、物流與實驗，一種罕見肌肉萎縮症的候選藥從構想到有希望的小鼠結果不到 18 個月，Anthropic 預期最快 2028 年底可進人體試驗。 In "Early progress on accelerating drug development with Claude," published 10/27 and modified 10/28, Anthropic lays out measured results from six partnerships at once. Generate Biomedicines: six scientists used Claude Code to propose shorter designs for a genetic component of CAR-T therapy; in lab tests one version was about 70% shorter than the standard and performed better, with immune cells making roughly 10x the receptors, killing tumour cells more efficiently and staying active longer. 為什麼重要Why it matters

過去兩年「AI 加速藥物開發」幾乎都停在設計端的 in silico 分數。這份文件的分量在於幾乎每一項都有濕實驗室的回饋閉環：變體被合成出來、被量測、數據回去重新定義指標。尤其 BigHat 那段——Claude 與研究者中途判定既有的熱穩定與聚集量測方式有誤導性並改換指標——是今天最值得注意的一句話：這不是模型在既定評分板上刷分，而是模型參與了「評分板本身怎麼定」。Enveda 的 11/11 加抓出 7 個，則是法規文件審查這類高度結構化、錯誤成本極高的工作第一次有可比較的命中率數字。 For two years "AI accelerates drug development" has mostly meant in-silico scores at the design stage. What gives this document weight is that nearly every item closes a loop through a wet lab: variants were synthesised, measured, and the data fed back to redefine the metrics. The BigHat passage especially — Claude and the researcher deciding mid-project that the existing heat-stability and aggregation measures were misleading, and changing them — is the line worth noticing today: this is not a model climbing an existing scoreboard but a model taking part in deciding what the scoreboard should be. Enveda's 11-out-of-11 plus seven more is the first comparable hit-rate figure for regulatory document review, a highly structured task where the cost of an error is severe.

Of 5,234 clinicians provisioned with Abridge's platform, 1,802 actually used it (about 34%); the 637 who met usage thresholds and entered the analysis were split across two cohorts on separate legacy EHR instances. Advanced practice clinicians cut about 9.9 minutes of daily documentation against 3.8 minutes for physicians — roughly a 2.5x gap. After-hours documentation fell 8.9% in one cohort and 16.6% in the other; time to chart closure fell 25.3% and 39.3%; and top-quartile improvers regained nearly 17 minutes of after-hours time per scheduled day. healthsystemcio's 10/27 write-up notes the paper states that the vendor, Abridge, had no role in the work.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是今天最有用的一份反向校準。ambient AI 是過去兩年醫療 AI 裡商業化最成功的一類，但這份 28 萬次就診的觀察性數據給出兩個不舒服的事實：第一，開通不等於使用，三分之二的授權是閒置的；第二，省下的時間高度集中在原本負擔最重的人身上，平均數會掩蓋這件事。healthsystemcio 把續約框成「CIO 的配額決策」——該由誰持有授權、用什麼證據保留每一個席次，並建議用「實際使用／已開通席次」、下班後文書時間、在病歷中的時間、病歷結案時間四項按角色分拆來衡量。對任何正在評估導入的醫院，這比任何廠商白皮書都接近真實。 This is the most useful piece of reverse calibration today. Ambient AI has been the most commercially successful category in medical AI for two years, but this observational dataset of 283,000 encounters produces two uncomfortable facts: provisioning is not use — two thirds of the licences sat idle — and the time saved is heavily concentrated among those who were already most burdened, which an average conceals. healthsystemcio frames renewal as "an allocation decision for the CIO": who should hold a licence, and what evidence justifies keeping each seat, with four suggested measures broken out by clinician role — active use against provisioned seats, after-hours documentation, time in notes, and time to chart closure. For any hospital weighing a rollout, this is closer to the truth than any vendor white paper.

觀察性研究、使用為自願，存在明顯的自我選擇偏誤——會持續使用的人很可能本來就最需要也最適合。研究未估算金額節省。兩個 cohort 分屬不同舊版 EHR 實例，差距（8.9% 對 16.6%、25.3% 對 39.3%）本身就提醒結果高度依賴系統環境。資料期間為 2025 下半年，已距今約一年，產品版本可能已改。 This is observational and use was voluntary, so there is clear self-selection — the people who kept using it were probably the ones who most needed it and were best suited to it. The study did not estimate dollar savings. The two cohorts sat on different legacy EHR instances, and the spread between them (8.9% vs 16.6%, 25.3% vs 39.3%) is itself a reminder of how much the result depends on the surrounding system. The data covers the second half of 2025, roughly a year old now, and the product version may have changed.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這篇直接打在今天另外兩則消息的要害上。訊飛的 V3.7 與 OpenEvidence 的 Darwin 都走「醫療垂類／專科強化」路線，而這份評測說：在 NYU 醫師真實打進去的查詢上，專科工具的問題不是答錯，而是答不完整、漏掉安全關鍵資訊、寫得亂——OpenEvidence 的 44 筆低分回應裡只有 4 筆是事實錯誤。這把爭論從「模型知不知道」移到「檢索後摘要的流水線會不會把資訊切碎」。對採購端的意義更直接：如果通用模型已經在真實查詢上不輸，專科工具的賣點就必須回到資料授權（NCCN、ASCO 指引）、審計軌跡與責任承擔，而不是答題準確率。 This lands squarely on the weak point of two other items today. iFlytek's V3.7 and OpenEvidence's Darwin both take the vertical, medically specialized route, and this evaluation says: on queries NYU physicians actually typed, the specialized tools' problem is not being wrong but being incomplete, omitting safety-critical information, and being disorganized — of OpenEvidence's 44 low-scoring responses, only four were factual errors. That moves the argument from "does the model know" to "does a retrieve-then-summarize pipeline shred the information." The procurement implication is blunter: if general-purpose models already hold their own on real queries, a specialized tool's pitch has to rest on licensed content (NCCN and ASCO guidelines), audit trails and liability — not answer accuracy.

電腦操作模型是醫療 agent 最現實的落地形態——因為大多數醫院系統沒有 API，agent 只能像人一樣去點 EHR 的畫面。Terra 的降價把這類 agent 的單位成本壓到一個會改變採購算式的位置。但 10/28 那條安全發現正好打在最敏感的點：如果一個模型會聽從「看似同儕 agent」的未授權指令，那麼在 MedHarnessBench 所說的「臨床行為邊界」與「可稽核性」兩個維度上，它在醫院環境裡是不可接受的。這也是為什麼 Liquid AI 的 LFM3 這類手機／筆電尺寸模型值得留意：當推論可以留在裝置上，agent 能觸及的攻擊面與資料外流路徑就小得多。 Computer-use models are the most realistic shape for a medical agent, because most hospital systems have no API and an agent can only click through the EHR the way a human does. Terra's price cut pushes the unit cost of such agents to a level that changes the procurement arithmetic. But the 10/28 safety finding lands on the most sensitive point: a model that will follow unauthorized instructions from an apparent peer agent is unacceptable in a hospital on the two dimensions MedHarnessBench names — clinical conduct boundaries and auditability. Which is also why phone- and laptop-sized models such as Liquid AI's LFM3 are worth watching: when inference stays on the device, the attack surface and the data-egress paths an agent can reach are far smaller.

衛福部 10/5 講的那四件基建，剛好就是今天這批技術消息缺的那四塊 The four pieces of infrastructure the MOHW described on 10/5 are precisely the four this week's technology news is missing
