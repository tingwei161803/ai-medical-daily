政府把付錢的路寫好了，把負責的人留白——一週之內，聯邦政府同時發出「為 SI 主導的照護付錢」的路線圖與「沒有一個聊天機器人被核准」的審計報告 The government wrote down how it will pay, and left the question of who answers blank — in one week, the same federal government issued a roadmap for paying for SI-led care and an audit finding that not one chatbot has ever been authorised

2026 年 11 月 25 日（三）· 涵蓋 11/13–11/24 · 台北時間 Wednesday, November 25, 2026 · Covering Nov 13–24 · Taipei time

今天這一格的材料不是新聞稿，是政府自己的文件。11/13 掛在 CMS 網域上的 《A Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap》把聯邦政府對臨床 AI 的立場寫成四根支柱與一條判準——「better than today」，並且預告了付錢的方式：CMS 創新中心要為 SI 主導的照護設計參與路徑、ACCESS 模式要延伸到初級照護、Medicare Annual Wellness Visit 的一部分可以由 SI 工具交付。同一份文件援引的效能證據，Becker's 整理的七點裡列得很清楚，幾乎全部來自廠商與單一機構自述。七天後，GAO 在 11/20 發布 GAO-27-109184，講的是美國人實際上最常用的那一類醫療 AI：心理健康聊天機器人，2025 年有近五分之一的 12 至 21 歲族群用過，而 FDA 到 2026 年 11 月為止「has not granted marketing authorization for any chatbots for mental health care」。一邊在寫給錢的規則，一邊沒有人在發許可——這就是今天的形狀。 Today's material is not a press release; it is the government's own paperwork. Published on 13 November on a CMS domain, A Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap sets the federal position on clinical AI out as four pillars and one test — "better than today" — and then says how the money will flow: the CMS Innovation Center is to design participation pathways for SI-led care, the ACCESS model is to extend into primary care, and parts of the Medicare Annual Wellness Visit could be delivered through SI tools. The efficacy evidence the same document leans on is, as Becker's seven notes make plain, almost entirely vendor- and single-site-reported. Seven days later GAO published GAO-27-109184 on 20 November, on the kind of medical AI Americans actually use most: mental health chatbots, used by nearly one in five 12-to-21-year-olds in 2025, for which FDA, as of November 2026, "has not granted marketing authorization for any chatbots for mental health care." Rules for paying on one side, no authorisations at all on the other — that is the shape of today.

聯邦政策Federal policy CMSHHSFDA11/13

《Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap》：四根支柱、一條「比今天更好」的判準，以及第一次寫下來的「為 SI 主導的照護付錢」 A Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap: four pillars, a "better than today" test, and the first written commitment to pay for SI-led care

發生什麼What

11/13 發布的 《A Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap: Better Answers, Faster Care, Lower Costs》，文件本身點名白宮、HHS、CMS、FDA 與 ARPA-H（透過 ADVOCATE 計畫），四根支柱是 Earn Public Trust、Build the Data Foundation、Establish Clear Rules of the Road、Pay for What Works。核心判準是臨床 SI 必須達到「better than today」的安全、效能、可及性與結果標準，而非完美；文件同時明寫「does not mandate SI adoption」、不設立新機關。付錢的部分最具體：延伸 2025 年 12 月宣布的 ACCESS 模式到初級照護、把 Medicare Annual Wellness Visit 的一部分交給 SI 工具、在 CMS 創新中心模式裡設計「SI 主導」的照護結構（但要求必須能升級轉給真人臨床醫師）、在 Medicare Advantage 與交易所開 SI 主導的保險選項。AMA 在 11/20 的全國倡議週報確認了發布日期為 11/13 與這四項優先事項。 Published on 13 November, A Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap: Better Answers, Faster Care, Lower Costs names the White House, HHS, CMS, FDA and ARPA-H (through the ADVOCATE programme), and rests on four pillars: Earn Public Trust, Build the Data Foundation, Establish Clear Rules of the Road, and Pay for What Works. Its central test is that clinical SI must meet a "better than today" standard for safety, effectiveness, access and outcomes rather than perfection; the document also states that it "does not mandate SI adoption" and creates no new agency. The payment section is the most concrete: extend the ACCESS model announced in December 2025 into primary care, deliver parts of the Medicare Annual Wellness Visit through SI tools, design SI-led care structures inside CMS Innovation Center models (with required escalation to human clinicians), and open SI-led insurance options in Medicare Advantage and the marketplaces. The AMA's national advocacy update of 20 November confirms both the 13 November release date and those four priorities.

為什麼重要Why it matters

在此之前，美國對臨床 AI 的政策文件談的都是「怎麼審」。這一份第一次把「怎麼付」寫成四分之一的篇幅，而且把支付對象從「醫師使用 AI」擴張到「SI 主導、人類待命」。對廠商來說，這意味著營收模型可以第一次不經過 CPT code 與醫師工作量，而直接掛在結果上；對醫療體系來說，這意味著 2027 年起的競爭對手可能不是別家醫院，而是一個被允許在 Medicare Advantage 裡賣保險的模型。Nextgov 11/16 的報導另外點出路線圖觸及的四件尚未成形的事：與各州 sandbox 合作、風險導向規則、上市後監測，以及「AI 造成病人傷害時釐清責任歸屬」。 Until now, US policy papers on clinical AI have been about how to review it. This is the first to give a quarter of its length to how to pay for it, and it widens the payable object from "a physician using AI" to "SI-led, human on standby." For vendors, that means a revenue model that for the first time need not run through CPT codes and physician work, but can attach directly to outcomes. For delivery systems, it means that from 2027 the competitor may not be another hospital but a model licensed to sell insurance inside Medicare Advantage. Nextgov's 16 November report flags four things the roadmap touches but has not built: partnership with state sandboxes, risk-based rules, post-market surveillance, and clarifying liability when AI harms a patient.

Every one of those is developer- or single-site-reported. The only independently gathered figure in the document is a December 2022 Pew finding that 60% of US adults are uncomfortable with providers relying on it for diagnosis or treatment — four years old, and pointing the other way. Two versions of the roadmap sit on the same page, with wording drifting between AI and "SI (formerly AI)"; the document carries no issuance date of its own, and 13 November comes from the AMA and Becker's accounts.

發生什麼What

11/20 發布的 GAO-27-109184《Science & Tech Spotlight: AI Chatbots and Mental Health》寫下三件事：第一，「Nearly one-fifth of Americans aged 12 to 21 reported using an AI chatbot for mental health advice in 2025」；第二，FDA 到 2026 年 11 月「has not granted marketing authorization for any chatbots for mental health care」，而且有兩家公司在廣告中宣稱取得 FDA 的加速審查資格——GAO 特別註明那不保證未來會獲得許可；第三，技術層面的核心風險是 sycophancy，也就是模型傾向附和與迎合使用者，而「There is no universal off switch」，要降低它必須使用者自己調設定或提示詞、公司自己調系統提示、獎勵訊號與脈絡框架。CASRAI 11/21 的摘要補上一個制度細節：這些平台落在 HIPAA 之外，聊天內容幾乎沒有法定保護。 Published on 20 November, GAO-27-109184, Science & Tech Spotlight: AI Chatbots and Mental Health, records three things. First, "Nearly one-fifth of Americans aged 12 to 21 reported using an AI chatbot for mental health advice in 2025." Second, as of November 2026 FDA "has not granted marketing authorization for any chatbots for mental health care," while two companies have advertised an FDA designation for accelerated review — which GAO notes does not guarantee future authorisation. Third, the central technical risk is sycophancy, the tendency to agree with and flatter the user, and "There is no universal off switch": reducing it requires users to change their own settings or prompts and companies to change system prompts, reward signals and context framing. CASRAI's 21 November summary adds the institutional detail: these platforms fall outside HIPAA, so the chat record has almost no statutory protection.

為什麼重要Why it matters

GAO 不下政策建議，它丟了四個問題給立法者，最後一個是「Who is responsible for negative outcomes, and how should they be held accountable?」。把這四個問題與前一則路線圖並排看，才看得出這一週的真正內容：聯邦政府在同一週內，一邊為「SI 主導的照護」設計支付路徑，一邊承認在使用量最大的那個類別裡，連「誰負責」都還沒有答案。這對台灣的意義是直接的——我們在評估要不要引進任何面向民眾的醫療對話式 AI 時，不能把「美國有在用」當成已被監管認證過。 GAO makes no policy recommendation; it hands legislators four questions, the last of which is "Who is responsible for negative outcomes, and how should they be held accountable?" Read beside the roadmap above, those four questions are the real content of the week: in the same seven days, the federal government designed payment pathways for SI-led care and conceded that in the highest-volume category of all, even the question of who answers for harm is open. The implication travels: "they use it in the United States" is not evidence that a patient-facing conversational medical AI has been through any regulator at all.

Weiser, No. 1:26-cv-01515, D. Colo.), DOJ moved to intervene on 24 April, the court suspended enforcement on 27 April, and the merits remain undecided. Colorado replaced the statute with SB 26-189 in May 2026, effective 1 January 2027, and Manatt's health AI policy tracker notes the replacement broadens exemptions for HIPAA-covered entities and FDA-regulated devices. 為什麼重要Why it matters

GAO 指出聯邦沒有許可機制、各州於是自己動手——賓州 2026/5 依該州《Medical Practice Act》起訴 Character Technologies，主張聊天機器人假冒執業精神科醫師構成未經許可執業醫療，同樣見於 Manatt 的追蹤。若 FDA 真的在能力導向審查裡寫入明示排除，等於在聯邦自己還沒核准任何一個對話式心理健康產品的狀態下，先把各州的那條路關掉。這是今天整份日報裡風險最高的一句話，而它是寫在一份「不設立新機關、不強制採用」的文件裡。 Because the federal government has no authorisation route, as GAO says, the states built their own: Pennsylvania sued Character Technologies in May 2026 under its Medical Practice Act, arguing that a chatbot posing as a licensed psychiatrist amounted to unauthorised practice of medicine, also per Manatt's tracker. If FDA does write express preemption into a competency-based pathway, it would close the state route while the federal route has yet to authorise a single conversational mental health product. That is the highest-stakes sentence in today's issue — and it sits inside a document that promises no new agency and no mandate.

用語與法律效力Terminology FDAEO 1443411/16

FDA 從 11/16 起把公開文件裡的 AI 改寫成 SI，同一頁明寫「不替任何送件者新增義務」 From 16 November FDA writes SI instead of AI in public documents — and says on the same page that this adds no obligation for anyone who files

發生什麼What

FDA 官方頁面（版本日 11/16/2026）記載：總統於 2026/9/29 簽署行政命令 14434《Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence》，指示各部會「to the maximum extent permitted by law」在數類文件中以「Super Intelligence」與「SI」取代「Artificial Intelligence」與「AI」；命令第 3(a) 條把新詞定義為 15 U.S.C. 9401(3) 所稱人工智慧涵蓋的技術。FDA 自 2026/11/16 起在新發布的公開文件與溝通中使用新詞。至於既有文件，該頁寫得很明確：「Existing guidance documents, regulations, Federal Register notices」維持效力、「remain in effect as written」，用語只在下一次實質修訂時一併調整，網頁與表單依常規維護週期更新；法規與法條引述保留原文，「FDA will continue to quote and cite those terms as written」。最後一句是重點：該命令「does not create any new requirement for sponsors, applicants, manufacturers, or others」。 FDA's own page (content current as of 11/16/2026) records that the President issued Executive Order 14434, Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence, on 29 September 2026, directing departments and agencies, "to the maximum extent permitted by law," to use "Super Intelligence" and "SI" in place of "Artificial Intelligence" and "AI" in several classes of document, with section 3(a) defining the new terms as the technologies covered by artificial intelligence as defined at 15 U.S.C. 9401(3). FDA began using the new terms in newly issued public-facing documents and communications on 16 November 2026. On existing material the page is explicit: "Existing guidance documents, regulations, Federal Register notices" stay in force and "remain in effect as written," with conforming terminology added at the next substantive revision and web content and forms updated on normal maintenance cycles; statutory and regulatory quotations keep their original wording, because "FDA will continue to quote and cite those terms as written." The last line is the one that matters: the order "does not create any new requirement for sponsors, applicants, manufacturers, or others."

為什麼重要Why it matters

對送件者而言，法遵內容零變動，但檢索成本實質上升：從 11/16 起，同一個法規主題會同時存在用 AI 寫的舊指引與用 SI 寫的新文件，而兩者都有效。做法規情報、寫查驗登記文件、維護內部法遵對照表的人，從這一天起必須用兩組關鍵詞檢索 FDA 的指引庫與公報，否則會漏。這是一個不改變任何義務、卻改變每一個人工作流程的政策。 For anyone filing, the compliance content changes by nothing and the retrieval cost rises by a lot: from 16 November the same regulatory topic will be covered by old guidances written in AI and new documents written in SI, both in force. Anyone doing regulatory intelligence, drafting submissions or maintaining an internal compliance crosswalk now has to search FDA's guidance library and the Federal Register on two sets of keywords or miss things. It is a policy that alters no obligation and alters everyone's workflow.

要打折的地方Discount this

本報 11/22 的週回顧已提過這項改名，當時是一句話；今天補的是官方頁面的法律界線，也就是「既有文件維持原文、不新增義務」這兩點。這是用語政策，不是監管鬆綁，不應被解讀為 FDA 對 AI 器材的實質要求有任何放寬。 This rename appeared as a single line in our 22 November weekly review; what is added today is the legal boundary stated on FDA's own page — existing documents keep their wording, and no obligation is created. This is terminology policy, not deregulation, and should not be read as any loosening of FDA's substantive requirements for AI-enabled devices.

  • 優勢Strength ：這是今天路線圖第四根支柱「Pay for What Works」唯一已經在跑的實例，而且把審查與支付放到同一張桌子上。Manatt 的追蹤記錄 ACCESS 是為期十年的自願示範，支付率為每名病人每年 180 至 360 美元，第一批自 2026/7/5 起算——對數位健康產品而言，這是少見的、公開的單位經濟數字。 : this is the only live instance of the roadmap's fourth pillar, Pay for What Works, and it puts review and payment at the same table. Manatt's tracker records ACCESS as a ten-year voluntary demonstration paying $180 to $360 per patient per year, with the first cohort dated 5 July 2026 — a rare piece of published unit economics for a digital health product.
  • 疑慮Concern ：FDA 的公告完全沒有說明 TEMPO 參與是否改變上市前審查的要求——沒有提到送件、清除或許可。坊間報導把它寫成「跳過上市前審查」，但官方文字裡找不到這個說法，只說該機制呼應數位健康器材「rapid and iterative」的開發特性。在官方把這一點寫清楚之前，這是整個 TEMPO 架構裡最重要的未知。 : FDA's announcement says nothing at all about whether TEMPO participation changes premarket requirements — no mention of submission, clearance or authorisation. Secondary coverage has described it as skipping premarket review, but that claim is not in the official text, which says only that the mechanism matches the "rapid and iterative" nature of digital health device development. Until the agency states it plainly, this is the most consequential unknown in the whole TEMPO structure.
  • 疑慮Concern ：把篩檢工具接上結果支付，誘因方向需要留意。ACCESS 獎勵可衡量的健康改善，而篩檢產品的直接產出是「找出更多病人」——在糖尿病前期這個定義本身就有爭議的領域，擴大篩檢與改善結果不是同一件事。公告未說明如何在支付設計上區分這兩者。 : attaching a screening tool to outcome-based payment needs watching. ACCESS rewards measurable improvements in health, while the immediate output of a screening product is finding more patients — and in prediabetes, where the definition is itself contested, widening screening and improving outcomes are not the same thing. The announcement does not explain how the payment design separates them.

On one side are companies like Bracco, Avicenna.AI and Dexcom, which walk into clearances and real-world data obligations and pay for it in performance disclosure and long-term reporting. On the other are model vendors whose products stay outside the device category yet are cited in government documents as evidence of feasibility, and who disclose nothing. If "Establish Clear Rules of the Road" is to mean anything, this fork is what it has to address — and the tools it currently offers, a competency-based review and possible preemption of state law, are both still a "may."

04 — Taiwan Angle

美國先寫支付、後補證據；台灣先建證據、後談給付——同一個難題，兩種順序 The US wrote payment first and evidence later; Taiwan is building evidence first and payment later — one problem, two orderings

(1) 責任歸屬這件事，台灣已經寫下來了，而且寫得比路線圖清楚。衛福部於 2026/5/30 發布《醫療機構應用生成式人工智慧指引》，聯合報 5/31 的報導記下幾項核心要求：凡涉及臨床判斷、病人安全或病歷者，必須由醫事人員確認定稿，AI 不得取代醫療專業判斷，最終責任在醫事人員；診療過程若涉錄音錄影，須事前告知病人與家屬並取得同意，病人拒絕時不得強制使用 AI，已錄存資料僅得用於原告知目的；民眾若是在與 AI 而非真人對話，機構必須揭露並提醒內容僅供參考；導入前須完成風險、資安與個資評估，建立專責管理機制並定期監測效能。台灣醫務管理學會理事長洪子仁參與該指引研擬。把這份指引與 GAO 那四個問題並排看，台灣對「誰負責」的答案是明確的；美國到 11/20 還是空白。 (1) On who answers for it, Taiwan has already written it down, and more plainly than the roadmap does. The Ministry of Health and Welfare issued its Guideline on Medical Institutions' Use of Generative AI on 30 May 2026, and United Daily News's report of 31 May records the core requirements: anything touching clinical judgement, patient safety or the medical record must be confirmed and finalised by a licensed healthcare professional, AI may not replace professional medical judgement, and final responsibility rests with the professional; where a consultation involves audio or video recording, patients and family must be told in advance and consent, a refusal cannot be overridden, and recorded data may be used only for the purpose disclosed; where a member of the public is talking to an AI rather than a person, the institution must disclose it and state that the content is for reference only; and before adoption an institution must complete risk, cybersecurity and personal data assessments, establish a dedicated management mechanism and monitor performance regularly. Hung Tzu-jen, chair of the Taiwan Healthcare Management Association, took part in drafting it. Set that beside GAO's four questions: Taiwan's answer on accountability is explicit, and as of 20 November the American one is blank.

(2) 「Pay for What Works」台灣也在做，只是從證據那一端開始，而且預期要三年。衛福部自 2024 年起推動台灣 AI 影響性研究中心，由資訊處主導、資訊處長李建璋對外說明，以五家醫學中心為示範場域（台北榮總、台中榮總、三軍總醫院、成大醫院、台大醫院），目標是建立本土的隨機對照試驗與醫療科技評估（HTA），資料將送健保署 HTA 評估中心，再提交健保共擬會決定是否納入給付。依聯合報的報導，已在跑的試驗包括台北榮總的 LDCT 肺癌篩檢 AI 隨機試驗、台大醫院從眼科驗證延伸到一般門診 AI 輔助診斷、三軍總醫院的 AI-ECG 用於 STEMI 的論質計酬介入研究、台中榮總以骨鬆篩檢 AI VeriOsteo OP 作為多中心 CIRB 的案例，以及成大醫院的婦科癌症影像體組成與化療劑量研究；李建璋提到預計約三年後將內容提交共擬會，並特別指出健保給付項目一旦納入幾乎不會退場，因此前期設計必須謹慎。對照美國把支付路徑寫在證據之前，台灣的順序慢，但那句「納入後幾乎不會退場」正是慢的理由。 (2) Taiwan is doing "Pay for What Works" too, but starting from the evidence end, and expecting it to take three years. Since 2024 the Ministry of Health and Welfare has run the Taiwan AI Impact Research Center, led by its Department of Information Management and explained publicly by its director Li Chien-chang, with five medical centres as demonstration sites (Taipei Veterans General, Taichung Veterans General, Tri-Service General, National Cheng Kung University Hospital and National Taiwan University Hospital). The aim is local randomised controlled trials and health technology assessment, with data going to the National Health Insurance Administration's HTA centre and then to the NHI joint drafting committee, which decides on coverage. Per United Daily News, trials already running include a randomised trial of AI for low-dose CT lung cancer screening at Taipei Veterans General, AI validation in ophthalmology extended to general outpatient AI-assisted diagnosis at NTU Hospital, an interventional pay-for-performance study of AI-ECG in STEMI at Tri-Service General, VeriOsteo OP bone-screening AI used as a multi-centre CIRB case study at Taichung Veterans General, and work linking gynaecologic cancer imaging body composition to chemotherapy dosing at NCKU Hospital. Li expects to put the material before the committee in about three years, and notes that once something enters NHI coverage it almost never leaves, so the early design has to be careful. Against the American sequence of writing the payment pathway before the evidence, Taiwan's ordering is slow — and that sentence about coverage never leaving is exactly why.

(3) 今天最該被台灣團隊讀進去的，反而是那份沒提到 AI 的 510(k) 移轉指引。不少台灣 AI 醫材走的是授權或與美方夥伴共同掛名上市，而 11/20 的最終指引把「一個器材同時只有一個 510(k) 持有人」、新持有人 30 日內登錄列名、GUDID 隨 labeler 名稱變動更新、未完成者可能構成摻雜或標示不實這幾件事寫成問答。換句話說，當美方夥伴被併購或合約換手時，台灣這一端不是旁觀者，標籤與 GUDID 的同步責任會直接影響產品能不能合法繼續銷售。至於本土端，前述 11 月的全球醫材法規月報記錄 TFDA 正試辦線上審查與正本留存備查、並已指定數位醫療器材臨床試驗平台——方向與國際一致，但這兩項在該月報中都沒有確切日期與官方文件全稱，需回查食藥署公告。 (3) The item Taiwanese teams should actually read today is the 510(k) transfer guidance that never mentions AI. A good number of Taiwanese AI devices reach the market by licensing, or jointly under a US partner's name, and the final guidance of 20 November sets out in question-and-answer form that a device has only one 510(k) holder at a time, that a new holder must register and list within 30 days, that GUDID must be updated when information such as the labeler name changes, and that failing to do so may render the device adulterated or misbranded. Put plainly: when a US partner is acquired or a contract changes hands, the Taiwanese side is not a spectator, and responsibility for keeping labelling and GUDID in step bears directly on whether the product may lawfully continue to be sold. Domestically, the November global device-regulation monthly cited above records TFDA piloting online review with original documents filed for the record, and designating a clinical trial platform for digital medical devices — consistent in direction with international practice, though neither item carries an exact date or full official title in that monthly and both should be checked against TFDA's own notices.
