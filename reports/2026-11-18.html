付得更多，揭露得更少——同一個月裡，美國把醫療 AI 的名字、價錢與揭露義務推往三個不同方向 Paying more, disclosing less — in one month Washington pushed the name, the price and the disclosure duty of medical AI in three different directions

2026 年 11 月 18 日（三）· 涵蓋 10/28–11/18 · 台北時間 Wednesday, November 18, 2026 · Covering Oct 28 – Nov 18 · Taipei time

今天這一輪法規新聞可以用三個動作讀完。第一個是改名：依 2026 年 9 月 29 日的行政命令 14434「Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence」，FDA 從 11 月 16 日起在新發出的對外文件裡把「artificial intelligence／AI」寫成「Super Intelligence／SI」，同一頁明寫這不對送件者「create any new requirement」、既有指引與法規「remain in effect as written」。第二個是喊方向：CMS 在 11 月 13 日發布的 《A Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap》，要求用國家標準取代「a confusing patchwork of state rules」，並點明某些 SI 醫材的核准可能觸發對州與地方要求的明示優先排除——但這份文件自己說不強制任何人採用，也沒有一個期限。第三個才是真的動到錢與義務：11 月 6 日與 9 日公告的 CY2027 兩份最終規則給軟體開了獨立付費的身分碼 O1，最高一碼 2,250.50 美元；而 10 月 28 日公告、12 月 28 日生效的 HTI-5 把預測型決策支援工具的訓練資料與各族群表現揭露義務整個刪掉。方向相反的兩隻手，出自同一個部會。 This week's regulatory news reads as three moves. The first is a rename: under Executive Order 14434, "Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence," signed September 29, 2026, the FDA began on November 16 writing "Super Intelligence" and "SI" in place of "artificial intelligence" and "AI" in newly issued public-facing documents — while the same page states the order does not "create any new requirement" for submitters and that existing guidance and regulations "remain in effect as written." The second is a direction: CMS published on November 13 A Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap, calling for national standards in place of "a confusing patchwork of state rules" and noting that some SI-enabled devices may travel pathways triggering express preemption of state and local requirements — yet the document says it mandates adoption by no one, and sets no deadline. The third actually moves money and duties: the two CY2027 final rules published November 6 and 9 give software its own separately paid status indicator, O1, topping out at $2,250.50 a code; while HTI-5, published October 28 and effective December 28, deletes the training-data and subgroup-performance disclosures required of predictive decision support tools. Two hands moving in opposite directions, from the same department.
