付得更多，揭露得更少——美國把醫療 AI 的名字、價錢與揭露義務推往三個不同方向 Paying more, disclosing less — Washington moved the name, the price and the disclosure duty of medical AI in three directions

2026 年 11 月 18 日（三）· 涵蓋 10/28–11/18 · 台北時間 Wednesday, November 18, 2026 · Covering Oct 28 – Nov 18 · Taipei time

三個動作。改名：依 2026/9/29 行政命令 14434，FDA 自 11/16 起在新發文件把「AI」寫成「SI」，同頁明寫不對送件者「create any new requirement」。喊方向：CMS 於 11/13 發布《Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap》，要用國家標準取代「a confusing patchwork of state rules」，但不強制、無期限。真的動到錢與義務的是第三個：11/6 與 11/9 的 CY2027 兩份最終規則給軟體開了付費身分碼 O1，最高一碼 2,250.50 美元；而 10/28 公告、12/28 生效的 HTI-5 把預測型決策支援工具的訓練資料與各族群表現揭露義務整個刪掉。方向相反的兩隻手，出自同一個部會。 Three moves. The rename: under Executive Order 14434 of September 29, 2026, the FDA from November 16 writes "SI" for "AI" in newly issued documents, the same page stating it does not "create any new requirement" for submitters. The direction: CMS published on November 13 A Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap, seeking national standards over "a confusing patchwork of state rules" — mandating nothing, with no deadline. The third moves money and duties: the two CY2027 final rules of November 6 and 9 give software a paid status indicator, O1, topping out at $2,250.50 a code; while HTI-5, published October 28 and effective December 28, deletes the training-data and subgroup-performance disclosures for predictive decision support tools. Two hands moving in opposite directions, from one department.

用語變更Terminology FDAEO 1443411/16

FDA 自 11/16 起把新發文件的「AI」改寫成「Super Intelligence」，同一頁明寫不新增任何義務 From Nov 16 the FDA writes "Super Intelligence" for "AI" — and says on the same page that nothing new is required

發生什麼What

依 2026/9/29 簽署的行政命令 14434，各機關須以「Super Intelligence」與「SI」取代「artificial intelligence」與「AI」，範圍是「to the maximum extent permitted by law」。FDA 專頁說明自 11/16 起在新發出的對外文件採用新詞，過渡期兩種詞彙並存；命令第 3(a) 節以 15 U.S.C. 9401(3) 對 AI 的法定定義來定義新詞，定義本身沒變。 Executive Order 14434, signed September 29, 2026, directs agencies to use "Super Intelligence" and "SI" for "artificial intelligence" and "AI," "to the maximum extent permitted by law." The FDA's page states the new terms appear in newly issued public-facing documents from November 16, both in use during the transition; Section 3(a) defines them by reference to the statutory definition of AI at 15 U.S.C. 9401(3), so the definition is unchanged.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是檢索問題而不是合規問題：11/16 之後的 FDA 文件要用「SI」才找得到，但法條、現行法規、聯邦公報與既有指引仍寫「AI」，FDA 明說引用時照原文。同一套規則從此有兩個名字，兩個都得查。 This is a retrieval problem, not a compliance one: FDA documents issued after November 16 must be searched under "SI," while statutes, regulations, the Federal Register and existing guidance still say "AI," which the FDA quotes as written. One body of rules now carries two names, and both must be searched.

要打折的地方Discount this

實質上什麼都沒改。FDA 自己寫明命令「does not create any new requirement for sponsors, applicants, manufacturers, or others who submit information to FDA」；既有指引與法規「remain in effect as written」。讀成鬆綁或加嚴都是誤讀。 Substantively nothing changed. The FDA writes that the order "does not create any new requirement for sponsors, applicants, manufacturers, or others who submit information to FDA"; existing guidance and regulations "remain in effect as written." Reading it as loosening or tightening would both be wrong.

政策路線圖Policy roadmap CMS11/13

CMS 路線圖要用國家標準取代州規則、替「SI 主導的照護」付錢——但不強制、無期限、舉證全是 benchmark The CMS roadmap seeks national standards over state rules and payment for SI-led care — mandating nothing, with no deadline, on benchmark evidence

發生什麼What

CMS 於 2026/11/13 發布《A Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap》，四大支柱為 Earn Public Trust、Build the Data Foundation、Establish Clear Rules of the Road、Pay for What Works。付費面提出以成果為基礎的慢病管理與預防保健支付、SI 主導的 accountable care（建立在 ACCESS、AHEAD、LEAD 模型上）、Medicare 年度健康檢查部分環節可由 SI 執行。法規面要以國家標準取代「a confusing patchwork of state rules」，並點明某些 SI 醫材可走「可能觸發對州或地方要求之明示優先排除」的途徑，另提 competency-based 上市前途徑與以 PCCP 為核心的上市後監測。放行標準是「better than today」。 CMS published on November 13, 2026 A Clinical Super Intelligence Roadmap, on four pillars: Earn Public Trust, Build the Data Foundation, Establish Clear Rules of the Road, and Pay for What Works. On payment it proposes outcome-based chronic disease management and preventive care, SI-led accountable care building on the ACCESS, AHEAD and LEAD models, and delivery of parts of the Medicare annual wellness visit by SI. On regulation it seeks national standards over "a confusing patchwork of state rules," notes some SI-enabled devices may pursue authorization through actions that "may trigger express preemption of state or local requirements," and floats a competency-based premarket approach with post-market surveillance anchored on predetermined change control plans. The release bar is "better than today."

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是美國第一份把「付錢給 AI 主導的照護」寫成政策方向的聯邦文件，而且把支付與優先排除綁在同一份路線圖裡。對醫材廠商，這決定未來要過幾套關——一套還是五十套。路線圖自列的風險倒是誠實：幻覺、不同族群表現不一致、隱私與資安漏洞、商業利益衝突。 It is the first federal document to make "paying for AI-led care" a stated direction, and it binds payment and preemption into one text. For device makers it decides how many gates they face — one or fifty. Its own risk list is candid: hallucinations, inconsistent performance across patient populations, privacy and security vulnerabilities, and commercial conflicts of interest.

要打折的地方Discount this

沒有法律效力：律師的評語是 aspirational、non-binding、無採用義務，文件自己有一節「What will not be done」明寫不強制採用、不設新機關、不要求完美，也沒有生效日。更該打折的是舉證——o3 在複雜病例把正確診斷排第一的 60% 對內科醫師 15.9%、MAI-DxO 的 85.5% 對醫師「約 20%」、Cedars-Sinai 的 77.1% 對 67.1%，全是 benchmark 與病例小品而非臨床結果；公眾接受度引的是 2022 年 12 月的調查，在 2026 年底已經四年。AMA 執行長 John Whyte 9 月的反駁正是這點：許多研究是模擬而非盲法試驗，他說這些工具「have to be utilized in the context of a care plan that's governed by a physician」。 It has no legal force: the lawyers call it aspirational, non-binding, with no duty to adopt, and a section titled "What will not be done" states it mandates no adoption, creates no new agency, demands no perfection, and sets no effective date. The evidence deserves the sharper discount — o3 ranking the final diagnosis first in 60% of complex cases against 15.9% for internal medicine physicians, MAI-DxO at 85.5% against "roughly 20%," Cedars-Sinai at 77.1% against 67.1%, all benchmark and vignette work rather than clinical outcomes; and public comfort is sourced to a December 2022 survey, four years old by late 2026. AMA chief executive John Whyte's September rebuttal went to exactly this: many studies are simulations rather than blinded trials, and these tools, he said, "have to be utilized in the context of a care plan that's governed by a physician."

支付制度Payment CMS-1850-FCO1 / SaMS11/6–11/9

CY2027 兩份最終規則真的開始付錢：14 個 CPT 碼離開檢驗給付表、O1 給軟體獨立付費、最高 2,250.50 美元——病人第一次要負擔部分負擔 The CY2027 final rules actually pay: 14 CPT codes leave the lab fee schedule, O1 funds software separately at up to $2,250.50 — and beneficiaries now owe coinsurance

發生什麼What

門診前瞻支付最終規則 CMS-1850-FC 於 2026/11/6、醫師費用表最終規則 CMS-1848-F 於 11/9 刊於聯邦公報。CMS 定調：跑在既有檢驗資料上的獨立演算法屬「other diagnostic tests」而非臨床檢驗，因此離開臨床檢驗給付表（CLFS）。據整理共定案 14 個 CPT 碼（提案時 10 個）；OPPS 下移入 new technology APC 並給新身分碼 O1「Software as a Medical Service」，PFS 下改全國定價、procedure status A。價格由 2026 年 CLFS 費率 crosswalk 後取整成級距：81417 在 APC 1505 的 350.50 美元、0510U 在 APC 1506 的 450.50 美元、含 1063T 與 1064T 等 11 個碼在 APC 1509 的 750.50 美元、81427 在 APC 1522 的 2,250.50 美元（均未經地區工資指數調整）。相對提案，0511U 與 81416 因含濕式實驗工作留在 CLFS，新增 81417 與 81427，並取消原提案對 O1 的多項目折扣與 PFS 的 MAC 定價。這些服務不受 14 天檢驗服務日期規則限制。 The outpatient final rule CMS-1850-FC appeared in the Federal Register on November 6, 2026 and the physician fee schedule final rule CMS-1848-F on November 9. CMS settled that stand-alone algorithms run on existing laboratory data are "other diagnostic tests" rather than clinical laboratory tests, and so leave the Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule. By one tally 14 CPT codes were finalized, up from 10 proposed; under the OPPS they move into new technology APCs under a new status indicator O1, "Software as a Medical Service," and under the PFS they get national pricing with procedure status A. Rates were crosswalked from 2026 CLFS levels and rounded into bands: 81417 at $350.50 in APC 1505, 0510U at $450.50 in APC 1506, eleven codes including 1063T and 1064T at $750.50 in APC 1509, and 81427 at $2,250.50 in APC 1522, all before wage-index adjustment. Against the proposal, 0511U and 81416 stay on the CLFS as they include wet-lab work, 81417 and 81427 were added, and both the proposed multiple-procedure discount for O1 and MAC pricing under the PFS were dropped. These services are not subject to the 14-day laboratory date-of-service rule.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是今天唯一「有碼、有價、有生效日」的一則，也是路線圖缺的那一半。O1 付費行為等同 status S：獨立給付、不吃多項目折扣，且 new technology APC 內的碼免除 comprehensive APC 打包——演算法第一次不被包進手術或就診的包裹價，而是自己收一筆。過去「醫院買軟體、自己吸收成本」，現在有了向 Medicare 直接請款的軌道。代價也第一次出現：離開 CLFS 後病人要負擔部分負擔，而 CLFS 上原本不用；以 2,250.50 美元那一碼估算約 450 美元。 This is the only item today with a code, a price and an effective date — the half the roadmap lacks. O1 pays like status S: separately, without multiple-procedure discounting, and codes in new technology APCs are exempt from comprehensive APC packaging. For the first time an algorithm is not folded into a bundled procedure price but bills on its own. The old sale, where the hospital buys the software and absorbs the cost, now has a path to billing Medicare directly. The cost appears too: off the CLFS these services carry beneficiary coinsurance where before they carried none — roughly $450 on the $2,250.50 code.

要打折的地方Discount this

一是 CMS 自己說 2027 年是 interim，完整估值方法論留待 CY2028，級距是過渡價且來源是 2026 年 CLFS 費率的 crosswalk 加四捨五入，不是對演算法本身的價值評估。二是碼數有疑：引用的整理自述「14 個」與它列出的增刪對不上，該文作者亦標注數字不清，本報照實轉述並標注。三是級距均未經地區工資指數調整，實際支付隨院所所在地變動。 First, CMS itself calls 2027 interim, deferring a full valuation methodology to CY2028; the bands are transitional and derive from a crosswalk of 2026 CLFS rates plus rounding, not from any valuation of the algorithms. Second, the code count is uncertain: the tally cited says "14" while its own list of additions and removals does not reconcile, and its author flags the figures as unclear; this report passes that through with the caveat. Third, all bands precede wage-index adjustment, so actual payment varies by location.

透明度倒退Transparency rollback ASTP / ONCHTI-510/28 → 12/28

HTI-5 刪掉預測型決策支援工具的訓練資料與族群表現揭露義務，12/28 生效——認證不再保證醫院拿得到 HTI-5 deletes the training-data and subgroup-performance disclosures for predictive decision support, effective Dec 28 — certification no longer guarantees access

發生什麼What

HTI-5 最終規則於 2026/10/28 刊於聯邦公報（91 FR 67916，文件號 2026-21943），修訂 45 CFR Part 170 的健康 IT 認證計畫，2026/12/28 生效，部分條文 2027/1/1 生效。ONC 最終刪除 14 項條件（提案 34 項）、修訂 7 項，60 項中 39 項不變；Part 171 的資訊阻擋規定未動。關鍵是 § 170.315(b)(11) 這條 HTI-1 建立的決策支援介入（DSI）條件被修訂：預測型與證據型 DSI 的 source attributes 支援及其存取與修改要求全部移除——涵蓋書目引用、訓練細節與各病患族群的表現；開發者不再須對預測型 DSI 施行風險管理實務。ONC 的理由是「reducing the Department's regulatory footprint」，並表示資訊依然存在、使用者可向開發者索取。 The HTI-5 final rule appeared in the Federal Register on October 28, 2026 (91 FR 67916, document 2026-21943), amending the health IT certification program at 45 CFR Part 170, and takes effect December 28, 2026, with certain provisions effective January 1, 2027. ONC finalized 14 removals (down from 34 proposed) and 7 revisions, leaving 39 of 60 criteria unchanged, and finalized nothing on information blocking in Part 171. The pivot is the revision of § 170.315(b)(11), the decision support intervention criterion HTI-1 created: source attribute support for predictive and evidence-based DSIs, and the requirements for accessing and modifying those attributes, are removed — covering bibliographic citations, training details and performance across patient groups; and developers no longer must apply risk management practices to predictive DSIs. ONC's rationale is "reducing the Department's regulatory footprint," adding that the information still exists and users may request it from developers.

為什麼重要Why it matters

它與 CMS 路線圖方向相反，而且有生效日。HTI-1 的 source attributes 是美國唯一一條「買方有權知道模型用什麼資料訓練、在不同族群表現如何」的制度管道，且綁在認證上——醫院不必自己去談。HTI-5 之後它變成議約事項：資訊還在，但給不給、給多少回到談判桌。對把美國認證當參考基準的體系，「有 ONC 認證」從 12/28 起不再等於「有模型卡」。值得注意的是 §1557 對病患照護決策支援工具的不歧視義務不受影響，族群表現的法律風險並未消失——只是更難查證。 It runs against the roadmap's direction and it has an effective date. HTI-1's source attributes were the only institutional channel in the US guaranteeing a buyer the right to know what data a model was trained on and how it performs across groups, and it was tied to certification, so hospitals did not have to negotiate. After HTI-5 it becomes a contract term: the information exists, but whether and how much is handed over returns to the table. For systems treating US certification as a baseline, "ONC certified" stops meaning "comes with a model card" on December 28. Note that Section 1557 nondiscrimination duties for patient care decision support tools are untouched, so the legal exposure around subgroup performance does not vanish — it merely becomes harder to verify.

要打折的地方Discount this

不要讀成「AI 透明度在美國結束了」。ONC 刪的是認證條件而非揭露本身：§1557 仍在，FDA 對受管制醫材的標示要求仍在，州法仍在且多數方向相反。本報的條文細節引自 McDermott 的分析而非逐條回查聯邦公報；該分析亦指出「AI 模型卡」一詞從未出現在法規文字裡，只是業界對 source attributes 的俗稱。 Do not read this as the end of AI transparency in the US. ONC removed a certification criterion, not disclosure itself: Section 1557 stands, FDA labeling requirements stand, and state law stands, mostly pointing the other way. This report's provision-level detail comes from McDermott's analysis rather than a line-by-line reading of the Federal Register; that analysis also notes the phrase "AI model card" never appears in the regulatory text and is industry shorthand for the source attributes.

事前審查Prior auth CMS WISeREFF FOIA11/2

實證往反方向走：WISeR 的 FOIA 紀錄顯示一家廠商三個月否決逾 6,000 件中的 53%、一件申請 83 天沒有回覆 The evidence runs the other way: WISeR's FOIA records show one vendor rejecting 53% of over 6,000 requests in three months, and one request unanswered for 83 days

發生什麼What

Medicare 的 WISeR 模型自 2026/1/1 起在六州（New Jersey、Ohio、Oklahoma、Texas、Arizona、Washington）試辦 AI 輔助事前審查，六家廠商執行，預計跑到 2031/12/31，參與自願，涵蓋十多項處置。電子前哨基金會（EFF）以 2026 年 3 月對 CMS 提起的資訊自由法訴訟取得並公開紀錄，據 MedCity News 於 2026/11/2 整理：Virtix Health 三個月內否決超過 6,000 件申請中的 53%（公司歸因於申請量暴增造成的處理問題）；一件送到 Genzeon 的申請 83 天未獲回覆；CMS 曾因未達處理時限等問題致函 Virtix（3/17）與 Zyter（2 月）。廠商報酬與「被認定為浪費而擋下」的支出按比例掛鉤，CMS 要求每件否決建議都須經執業臨床人員覆核。 Medicare's WISeR model has run AI-assisted prior authorization since January 1, 2026 in six states — New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Texas, Arizona and Washington — through six vendors, scheduled to December 31, 2031, participation voluntary, covering more than a dozen procedures. The Electronic Frontier Foundation obtained and published the records via a FOIA lawsuit filed against CMS in March 2026; as compiled by MedCity News on November 2, 2026: Virtix Health rejected 53% of more than 6,000 requests over three months, attributing it to processing problems from a claims surge; one request sent to Genzeon went unanswered for 83 days; and CMS sent letters over missed turnaround times to Virtix (March 17) and Zyter (February). Vendors can receive a percentage of spending tied to care deemed wasteful and prevented, and CMS requires a licensed clinician to review every denial recommendation.

為什麼重要Why it matters

WISeR 是美國政府自己營運中的 AI 決策系統，管的是「准不准做」而非「怎麼做」。它的紀錄因此是檢驗「better than today」最直接的材料：路線圖說比現狀好就可放行並持續監測，而 WISeR 正是一個已在跑、已在監測、而監測結果需要訴訟才取得的案例。更關鍵的是誘因結構——報酬與擋下的支出掛鉤——這與路線圖自列的風險「商業利益衝突」是同一件事，只是出現在聯邦自家的試辦裡。 WISeR is an AI decision system the US government runs itself, governing whether care happens rather than how. Its records are the most direct test of "better than today": the roadmap says a tool may be released once it beats the status quo and is monitored — and WISeR is a case already running, already monitored, whose monitoring results took a lawsuit to obtain. The sharper point is the incentive structure, pay tied to spending prevented: the same thing as the roadmap's own listed risk of commercial conflicts of interest, inside the federal government's own pilot.

要打折的地方Discount this

53% 本身不證明否決是錯的——MedCity 的報導也這樣寫。沒有公布的是這些否決有多少被申訴推翻，而那才是判斷準確性的數字。「CMS 曾致函」不等於罰款：報導未給出罰款金額、被標記次數或廠商報酬比例。最後，「每件否決都有臨床人員覆核」紙面上成立，但紀錄不足以顯示臨床人員實際上能否推翻 AI 的建議。 The 53% does not by itself show the denials were wrong — the MedCity report says as much. What is unpublished is how many were overturned on appeal, the number that would settle accuracy. "CMS sent a letter" is not a fine: the report gives no penalty amount, no count of flags and no percentage for the vendors' share. And while clinician review of every denial holds on paper, the records cannot show whether those clinicians can in practice overturn the AI.

02 — Product Analysis

一條新開的付費軌道，和同一週走完常規核准的一套影像軟體。對比在於哪一個真的被要求交出數據。 A newly opened payment rail, and an imaging package cleared by the routine route the same week. The contrast is which was required to produce data.

O1 / Software as a Medical Service (SaMS)

Medicare 門診支付身分碼 · CMS（美國）· CY2027 起Medicare outpatient payment status indicator · CMS (US) · from CY2027

功能與定位。名稱從提案時的「Software as a Service」改來，CMS 的理由是後者易與一般雲端運算混淆。它讓演算法服務在門診支付體系裡有自己的身分：付費等同 status S，且 new technology APC 內的碼免除 comprehensive APC 打包。級距與碼別整理見此。買方是醫院門診與醫師診間，付款人是 Medicare。 Function and position. Renamed from the proposal's "Software as a Service," which CMS said could be confused with generic cloud computing. It gives algorithmic services their own identity in outpatient payment: it pays like status S, and codes in new technology APCs are exempt from comprehensive APC packaging. Bands and code assignments are compiled here. The buyers are hospital outpatient departments and physician offices; the payer is Medicare.

  • 優勢Strength ：演算法第一次不被包進包裹價，且 CMS 取消了多項目折扣與 PFS 的 MAC 定價，支付可預測性大幅提高；脫離 CLFS 也不再受 14 天檢驗服務日期規則限制。 : for the first time an algorithm is not folded into a bundled price, and CMS dropped both the multiple-procedure discount and MAC pricing under the PFS, sharply raising predictability; leaving the CLFS also lifts the 14-day date-of-service rule.
  • 疑慮Concern ：價格不是算出來的而是換算來的（2026 年 CLFS 費率 crosswalk 加取整），CMS 自稱 2027 為 interim；而取得 O1 不需要證明臨床效益——病人卻開始負擔部分負擔，約 450 美元。「付錢」與「有效」仍是兩件事。 : the prices were converted rather than calculated (a crosswalk of 2026 CLFS rates plus rounding), and CMS calls 2027 interim; earning O1 requires no demonstration of clinical benefit — yet patients now owe coinsurance, roughly $450. "Paid for" and "works" remain two things.

功能與定位。據 2026/11/17 報導取得 FDA 510(k)：偵測鼻胃管並標示導管路徑、管尖與側孔，同時標出氣道與橫膈作為解剖參考點，適用成人與兒科，為該演算法首度支援兒科。軟體直接跑在 GE 的行動 X 光機上，置管後推到床邊拍一張，結果就地出現。 Function and position.   • 優勢Strength ：廠商表示不需網路或雲端，在加護環境是真實優勢，也讓資料治理與跨境傳輸大幅簡化。定位亦保守正確：不判斷置放是否正確，只標出評估所需的參考點，把判斷留給人。 : the vendor says it needs no internet or cloud — a real advantage in critical care, and one that simplifies data governance and cross-border transfer. 04 — Taiwan Angle

美國是先付錢再補證據，台灣是先要證據再談付錢——今天的新聞把這個差異量化了 America pays first and gathers evidence after; Taiwan demands evidence first — today's news puts numbers on the difference

(1) 美國給演算法開了價，台灣健保還沒有。CY2027 最終規則替軟體開出 350.50 到 2,250.50 美元的四個級距，而取得身分碼不需要證明臨床效益。台灣剛好相反：AI 判讀 X 光、測骨鬆、篩眼疾的工具在台灣都已取證，但健保一項都不給付。衛福部的回應是建制度：資訊處主辦的「AI 影響性研究中心」自 2024 年起推動，設北榮、中榮、三總、成大、台大五個示範據點，資料將送健保署 HTA 評估中心再轉共擬會；聯合啟動大會於 2025/11/20 舉行，時任資訊處長李建璋表示預計三年後才能送交共擬會。美國賭「先付錢，市場會把證據做出來」，台灣賭「先把證據做對，付錢才不會錯」——而美國那一邊今天已經出現第一張帳單：病人要負擔的那約 450 美元。 (1) America has priced algorithms; Taiwan's NHI has not. The CY2027 rules opened four bands from $350.50 to $2,250.50, and earning the indicator requires no demonstration of clinical benefit. Taiwan is the inverse: AI tools reading x-rays, measuring bone density and screening for eye disease are all licensed in Taiwan, and the NHI pays for none. The ministry's answer was institutional: the AI Impact Research Center, run by its Department of Information Management since 2024, with five demonstration sites at Taipei Veterans General, Taichung Veterans General, Tri-Service General, NCKU and NTU hospitals, its data going to the NHIA's HTA center and then the joint drafting committee; the launch was November 20, 2025, and then department head Li Chien-chang said it would be about three years before anything reached that committee. America bets paying first will make the market produce evidence; Taiwan bets getting evidence right first keeps payment from being wrong — and America's side produced its first bill today: the roughly $450 the patient owes.

(2) 從 12/28 起，「有 ONC 認證」不再等於「拿得到模型卡」——這直接影響台灣醫院的採購條款。HTI-1 的 source attributes 是台灣與其他把美國認證當基準的體系實質上免費搭的便車：只要過了 ONC 認證，書目引用、訓練細節、各族群表現就是必須提供的。HTI-5 移除後，ONC 自己說資訊還在、使用者可向開發者索取——從制度保障變成議約能力。具體待辦是把這些項目從「假設廠商本來就有」改寫進合約附件：訓練資料組成、各年齡與性別子群表現、模型更新的變更管理程序。台灣的制度位置反而稍前：《人工智慧基本法》已於 2025/12/23 三讀通過、以國科會為主管機關，上位法架構已在，落差會出現在醫療領域的下位規範有沒有把揭露義務寫到採購與查核層級。衛福部的三大 AI 中心分別針對負責任 AI、取證與健保給付——恰好就是今天美國、英國與歐盟各自選的那一題；優勢是三題同時有專責單位，風險是三題都還在建制度。 (2) From December 28, "ONC certified" no longer means "comes with a model card" — which bears directly on Taiwanese procurement terms. HTI-1's source attributes were a free ride for systems treating US certification as a baseline: pass ONC certification and bibliographic citations, training details and subgroup performance had to be supplied. With HTI-5 removing them, ONC says the information still exists and users may request it — an institutional guarantee becomes bargaining power. The concrete task is to move these out of "assume the vendor has them" and into the contract annex: training data composition, performance by age and sex subgroup, and change management for model updates. Taiwan's position is slightly ahead: the Artificial Intelligence Basic Act passed its third reading on December 23, 2025, with the NSTC as competent authority, so the framework exists; the gap will show in whether healthcare's subordinate rules carry the duty down to procurement and audit. The ministry's three AI centers target responsible AI, licensing and NHI payment — precisely the three questions America, Britain and the EU each picked today; the advantage is a dedicated body on all three, the risk that all three are still building.
