抄寫被大廠收走之後，所有人都擠到醫師問問題的那一刻——而這個位置第一次有了公開價格：1 億美元 After the EHR vendors took scribing, everyone crowded into the moment a clinician asks a question — and that position just got its first public price: $100 million

2026 年 11 月 27 日（五）· 涵蓋 11/11–11/26 · 台北時間 Friday, November 27, 2026 · Covering Nov 11–26 · Taipei time

今天拆的是同一個產品類別裡的兩套打法。Menlo Ventures 的 2026 年度報告把結構講得很直白：醫療 AI 總支出 37 億美元、成長 2.5 倍，但錢的成長不在抄寫——環境抄寫 9.5 億美元只長 1.6 倍，臨床 copilot 5.5 億美元長 5.5 倍；內建在 EHR 裡的抄寫功能市佔從 33% 升到 46%，拿走新增抄寫支出的 68%、營收成長 120%，而 AI-native 抄寫商營收成長 28% 卻掉了 13% 市佔。於是 11 月這兩週，三家公司各自用錢、用授權、用免費在搶「醫師問問題」這個位置：Wolters Kluwer 11/13 以最高 1 億美元買下 AgileMD、Wiley 11/16 把近 1 萬篇 Cochrane 系統性回顧授權給 Abridge、OpenEvidence 11/11 把 Access Intelligence 做成免費功能。而真正把這個類別的毛利天花板按下去的，是 11/16 OpenAI 把後勤任務標到每件 8.5 美分。 Today takes apart two strategies inside one product category. Menlo Ventures' 2026 annual report states the structure plainly: healthcare AI spend reached $3.7B, up 2.5x, but the growth is not in scribing — ambient scribes at $950M grew only 1.6x while clinical copilots at $550M grew 5.5x; EHR-native scribes went from 33% share to 46%, took 68% of new scribe dollars and grew revenue 120%, while AI-native scribes grew revenue 28% and lost 13% of share. So across two weeks in November, three companies went after the moment a clinician asks a question — one with cash, one with a licence, one with free: Wolters Kluwer agreed on 11/13 to buy AgileMD for up to $100M, Wiley licensed nearly 10,000 Cochrane systematic reviews to Abridge on 11/16, and OpenEvidence shipped Access Intelligence as a free feature on 11/11. What actually pressed down on the category's gross-margin ceiling was OpenAI pricing back-office tasks at 8.5 cents each on 11/16.

發生什麼What

11/13，Wolters Kluwer Health 宣布將收購 AgileMD，總價最高 1 億美元，其中 4,000 萬美元為前付現金、其餘遞延並綁定業績目標。AgileMD 2011 年成立於舊金山，共同創辦人 Borna Safabakhsh（執行長）與 Dana Edelson 醫師（醫務長），全公司 23 人將全數加入 Wolters Kluwer Health，2026 年底預估年經常性收入（ARR）為 700 萬美元；兩項產品是把院內指引落進 EHR 流程的 Pathways，以及已取得 FDA 許可的即時臨床監測引擎 eCART。交易預計 2027 年第一季或更早完成，買方自訂的財務門檻是三到五年內投資報酬率（ROIC）達到或高於 8% 的稅後加權平均資金成本（WACC），且對調整後每股盈餘影響不顯著。 On 11/13 Wolters Kluwer Health agreed to acquire AgileMD for up to $100M: $40M upfront in cash, the remainder deferred and contingent on performance targets. AgileMD was founded in San Francisco in 2011 by CEO Borna Safabakhsh and CMO Dana Edelson, M.D.; all 23 employees join Wolters Kluwer Health, and the company expects $7M in annual recurring revenue by the end of 2026. Its two products are Pathways, which operationalises local guidelines inside EHR workflows, and eCART, an FDA-cleared real-time clinical surveillance engine. Closing is expected in Q1 2027 or sooner, and the buyer set its own bar: return on invested capital at or above its 8% after-tax weighted average cost of capital within three to five years, with an immaterial impact on adjusted earnings.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是今年第一次有人替「把指引變成診間裡的下一步」這件事開出可查證的價格——約 14 倍 ARR，而且其中六成要靠業績才拿得到。被保護的資產寫在同一份新聞稿裡：UpToDate Expert AI 於 2025 年 9 月上線，目前已被逾 2,700 家美國醫院與醫療系統採用，佔其 UpToDate 企業版客戶逾 95%。換句話說，Wolters Kluwer 的渠道已經鋪好、缺的是把答案接到醫院自己的指引與 EHR 工作流那一段，而這一段它選擇買、不選擇自建。對照組是一家 2025 年營收 61 億歐元、約 21,100 名員工、業務橫跨 180 多國的老牌出版商（同一份公告）。 It is the first verifiable price this year on turning a guideline into the next step inside the exam room — roughly 14x ARR, with 60% of it contingent on hitting targets. The asset being defended is named in the same release: UpToDate Expert AI launched in September 2025 and has been adopted by more than 2,700 US hospitals and health systems, over 95% of its UpToDate Enterprise Edition customers. Wolters Kluwer already has the distribution; what it lacked was the wiring from an answer into each hospital's own guidelines and EHR workflow — and it chose to buy that rather than build it. For scale, this is a publisher with €6.1B of 2025 revenue, about 21,100 employees and customers in over 180 countries (same announcement).

公告未揭露 AgileMD 的客戶數、eCART 的許可編號與臨床成效數據；700 萬美元 ARR 是「預估」而非已實現；最高 1 億美元是上限、實際對價取決於未公開的業績條件。UpToDate 的 2,700 家與 95% 皆為賣方自述。 The release discloses no customer count for AgileMD, no clearance number or clinical outcome data for eCART, and the $7M ARR is expected rather than booked. 發生什麼What

11/16，Wiley（NYSE: WLY）與 Abridge 宣布多年期合作，將《Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews》近 1 萬篇系統性回顧與約 4,000 則 Cochrane Clinical Answers 授權進 Abridge Assistant 的臨床決策支援，回應中會附帶簡要且標註引用的摘要，全文則保留給 Wiley 訂閱者。協議範圍限於美國的醫療照護機構。同一份稿件引述 Abridge 自述：平台已用於逾 300 家美國醫療系統、今年將支援逾 1.2 億次就診、支援 28 種以上語言，並連兩年（2025、2026）獲 Best in KLAS 環境式 AI。 On 11/16 Wiley (NYSE: WLY) and Abridge announced a multi-year partnership licensing nearly 10,000 systematic reviews from the Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews and about 4,000 Cochrane Clinical Answers into clinical decision support inside the Abridge Assistant. Responses carry concise, cited summaries, with full text reserved for Wiley subscribers. The agreement covers US care-delivery organisations. The same release carries Abridge's own figures: more than 300 US health systems, more than 120 million patient encounters supported this year, 28-plus languages, and Best in KLAS for Ambient AI in both 2025 and 2026.

為什麼重要Why it matters

Cochrane 是全球臨床指引最常引用的證據來源之一，把它接進一個已經在聽診間對話的產品，等於把「有沒有證據」和「這位病人現在怎麼了」綁在同一個介面裡——這正是 Abridge 相對於純文獻搜尋工具唯一不可取代的那一格。Cochrane 執行長 Karla Soares-Weiser 的說法是「AI 正在改變人們取得證據的方式」，而 Abridge 執行長、同時是執業心臟科醫師的 Shiv Rao 說法是，可信證據連上照護情境才真正有用。這筆授權同時解釋了為什麼 Wolters Kluwer 要在三天前買下 AgileMD：證據層與工作流層正在互相併購。 Cochrane is among the most-cited evidence bases behind clinical guidelines worldwide, and wiring it into a product that is already listening to the encounter binds "what does the evidence say" to "what is wrong with this patient" in one interface — the one square a pure literature-search tool cannot occupy. Cochrane CEO Karla Soares-Weiser framed it as AI changing how people reach evidence; Abridge CEO Shiv Rao, a practising cardiologist, framed it as trusted evidence only becoming useful once it connects to the context of care. The licence also explains why Wolters Kluwer bought AgileMD three days earlier: the evidence layer and the workflow layer are now acquiring each other.

The licence fee, revenue split and actual Assistant adoption rate were not disclosed. Best in KLAS is a user-satisfaction survey, not a clinical-performance validation.

OpenEvidence 把「病人拿不到、付不起」做成免費功能：逾 2,500 個支援方案、代擬事前授權 OpenEvidence turns "the patient cannot get it or afford it" into a free feature: 2,500+ support programmes and drafted prior authorisations

發生什麼What

11/11，OpenEvidence 發表 Access Intelligence，把費用與保險給付的問題接到臨床決定之後：涵蓋逾 2,500 個病人支援方案（自付額補助、病人協助、保險支援服務）的可用性與資格條件並聲稱每日更新；可透過另一項產品 Patient TakeHome 把折扣直接送給病人；需要雙方申請的方案可下載預填表單或直接線上申請；並代擬事前授權申請、申覆函與醫療必要性信函，依臨床指引、事前授權檢核表與相關 ICD-10 碼生成，醫師可編輯，在進階的 Sackett 與 Snow 模型上還能以追問方式反覆修改。公司自述平台上已寫過逾 50 萬份事前授權文件，所有功能對經認證的美國臨床人員免費。 On 11/11 OpenEvidence launched Access Intelligence, bolting cost and coverage onto the clinical decision: availability and eligibility for more than 2,500 patient support programmes (copay savings, patient assistance, insurance support services), which the company says are updated daily; savings pushed to patients through a separate product, Patient TakeHome; pre-filled forms or direct online enrolment for programmes that require both clinician and patient; and drafted prior authorisation requests, appeal letters and letters of medical necessity, grounded in clinical guidelines, prior-authorisation checklists and relevant ICD-10 codes, editable by the clinician and, on the advanced Sackett and Snow models, refinable through follow-up questions. The company says more than 500,000 prior-authorisation documents have already been written on the platform. Everything is free to verified US clinicians.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這一步把 OpenEvidence 從「查文獻」推進到「把處方真的送到病人手上」，而且用的是免費。Menlo 的報告指出臨床 copilot 的營收約有 64% 集中在 OpenEvidence 一家、超過第二名的六倍（Menlo Ventures）；而它的商業模式是對醫師免費、靠廣告收錢（OpenEvidence 公開資料整理）。當一個已有六成以上類別份額的免費產品開始覆蓋事前授權，付費賣事前授權工具的公司就同時被定價壓縮與渠道繞過。公司副總裁 Ania Bilski 醫師的那句話把定位說得很完整：「選對治療本來就該是難的那一部分，讓病人拿到它不該是。」 This moves OpenEvidence from looking things up to actually getting the prescription into the patient's hands — and does it for free. Menlo's report puts about 64% of clinical copilot revenue with OpenEvidence alone, more than 6x the next vendor (Menlo Ventures), and its model is free to physicians, paid for by advertising (compiled public record on OpenEvidence). When a free product already holding more than 60% of a category starts covering prior authorisation, vendors selling prior-authorisation tools get squeezed on price and bypassed on distribution at the same time. VP of Clinical AI Ania Bilski, M.D., put the positioning in one line: "Choosing the right treatment should be the hard part. Getting the patient access to it shouldn't be."

抄寫長 1.6 倍、copilot 長 5.5 倍：AI-native 抄寫商營收成長 28%，市佔卻掉 13% Scribes up 1.6x, copilots up 5.5x: AI-native scribes grew revenue 28% and still lost 13% of share

發生什麼What

Menlo Ventures 的《2026: The State of AI in Healthcare》由 Morning Consult 於 2026 年 9 月訪問 550 位總監級以上、實際參與 AI 採購的決策者（150 位醫療機構技術決策者、100 位保險與福利主管、300 位藥廠與生技主管）。結果：醫療 AI 支出 37 億美元、成長 2.5 倍，成為最大的垂直市場，是法律（18 億美元、成長 2.8 倍）的兩倍以上；醫療機構端 29 億美元（77%）、保險端約 1.5 億美元（約 4%）。分項為環境抄寫 9.5 億（1.6 倍）、編碼與帳務 6 億（1.3 倍）、臨床 copilot 5.5 億（5.5 倍）、病人互動與前台 4.5 億（4.5 倍）、藥物探索由 3,000 萬升至 7 億。抄寫市場的結構變化最明顯：2025 年新創拿下 6 億美元市場的 67%，2026 年 EHR 內建功能拿下 9.5 億美元市場的 46%，並吃掉新增支出的 68%、成長 120%。導入率從 22% 升到 42%（醫療系統 52%、院外提供者 34%、生技 31%、保險 23%）。另一面是採購變慢：醫療系統從 7 個月拉長到 10 個月，院外提供者從 5 個月拉到 11 個月。 Menlo Ventures' "2026: The State of AI in Healthcare" was fielded by Morning Consult in September 2026 among 550 director-level-and-above executives who had taken part in buying AI (150 provider technology decision-makers, 100 health plan and benefits executives, 300 pharma and biotech executives). The findings: healthcare AI spend hit $3.7B, up 2.5x, making it the largest vertical AI market, more than double legal at $1.8B (up 2.8x); providers account for $2.9B (77%) and payers about $150M (roughly 4%). By category: ambient scribes $950M (1.6x), coding and billing $600M (1.3x), clinical copilots $550M (5.5x), patient engagement and front office $450M (4.5x), and drug discovery from $30M to $700M. The sharpest structural shift is in scribing: startups held 67% of a $600M market in 2025; in 2026 EHR-native add-ons hold 46% of a $950M market, took 68% of new dollars and grew 120%. Adoption of domain-specific AI rose from 22% to 42% (health systems 52%, outpatient providers 34%, life sciences 31%, payers 23%). The other side is slower buying: procurement stretched from 7 to 10 months for health systems and from 5 to 11 months for outpatient providers.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這份報告是今天所有併購與授權動作的背景解釋：抄寫被證明是可以被渠道吃掉的功能，而擁有病歷的人就擁有渠道。報告直接寫出「單純的紀錄工作已大致商品化」，並補上一句更難受的觀察——隨著 AI 寫程式能力提升，產品優勢的耐久性正在下降。對買方而言另一個訊號是保險端的反作用力：43% 的保險公司（大型保險公司 57%）表示醫療機構端的 AI 編碼推升了醫療成本趨勢，一年前只有 23%；52% 把它列入成本驅動前三名；事前授權則是保險公司的 AI 首要項目，比重從 59% 升到 82%。換句話說，醫療機構用 AI 多申報，保險公司就用 AI 多擋，而雙方的工具目前出自同一批供應商。 This report is the backdrop to every acquisition and licence above: scribing proved to be a feature that distribution can swallow, and whoever owns the record owns the distribution. The report states outright that standalone note-taking has largely commoditised, and adds a harder observation — product advantages are becoming less durable as AI coding improves. The other signal for buyers is the payer counter-reaction: 43% of payers (57% of large payers) say provider-side AI coding raised their medical cost trend, up from 23% a year ago; 52% rank it among their top three cost drivers; and prior authorisation is now payers' top AI priority, up from 59% to 82%. Providers use AI to bill more, payers use AI to deny more, and for now both sides buy from the same vendors.

UpToDate 同時出現在兩則新聞的兩端：它是 Wolters Kluwer 花最高 1 億美元要保護的資產，也是 ChatGPT for Healthcare 的一個外掛。這就是這個類別今天的尷尬——內容方把自己接進通用平台換取分發，同時又要防止通用平台把自己變成一個下拉選單。8.5 美分這個數字真正的意義不在它有多準，而在它替行政類任務設了一個公開的價格地板；一旦買方知道同一件事可以用 8.5 美分做到 91.8%，按席次或按件計價的專用工具就必須解釋剩下那幾個百分點值多少錢。 UpToDate appears on both ends of two different stories: it is the asset Wolters Kluwer is paying up to $100M to defend, and it is also a plugin inside ChatGPT for Healthcare. That is the category's current bind — content owners plug into the general platform for distribution while trying to stop that platform from reducing them to an entry in a dropdown. The real significance of 8.5 cents is not its accuracy but that it sets a public price floor for administrative work. Once buyers know the same task can be done to 91.8% for 8.5 cents, per-seat and per-task specialist tools have to explain what the remaining few percentage points are worth.

02 — Product Analysis

同一個位置、兩套相反的打法：Abridge 從「聽對話」往上接證據，OpenEvidence 從「查證據」往下接病人。差別在誰付錢。 One position, two opposite strategies: Abridge works up from listening to the conversation, OpenEvidence works down from the literature toward the patient. The difference is who pays.

Abridge Assistant

把病歷、對話與證據綁在同一個答案裡 · Abridge（美國，匹茲堡）One answer bound to the chart, the conversation and the evidence · Abridge (US, Pittsburgh)

功能與定位。Assistant 是在抄寫產品之上長出來的決策支援層：它已經在聽診間對話、已經接著病歷，所以它給的答案可以同時帶著「這位病人現在怎麼了」與「文獻怎麼說」。11/16 起，近 1 萬篇 Cochrane 系統性回顧與約 4,000 則 Cochrane Clinical Answers 以多年期授權進入這一層（Wiley／Abridge 新聞稿）。賣給誰很清楚：已經買了它抄寫產品的逾 300 家美國醫療系統，包含 Northwestern Medicine、Emory Healthcare 與 Johns Hopkins（Fortune, 2026-06-11）。 Function and position. Assistant is a decision-support layer grown on top of a scribe: it is already listening to the encounter and already connected to the chart, so its answer can carry both what is wrong with this patient and what the literature says. From 11/16, nearly 10,000 Cochrane systematic reviews and about 4,000 Cochrane Clinical Answers enter that layer on a multi-year licence (Wiley/Abridge release). Who buys it is unambiguous: the 300-plus US health systems that already bought the scribe, including Northwestern Medicine, Emory Healthcare and Johns Hopkins (Fortune, 2026-06-11).

  • 優勢Strength ：擁有別人沒有的那一條線——病歷、對話、院內指引三者的接線。這是純文獻工具做不到的，也是 Wolters Kluwer 願意付最高 1 億美元去買 AgileMD 的 Pathways 來補的那一段（Wolters Kluwer, 11/13）。 : it owns wiring nobody else has — the chart, the conversation and each system's own guidelines joined up. A pure literature tool cannot do this, and it is exactly the gap Wolters Kluwer paid up to $100M for AgileMD's Pathways to fill (Wolters Kluwer, 11/13).
  • 優勢Strength ：已有公部門的量可以證明使用率而非只有安裝數。Assistant 上個月回答了逾 40,000 個退伍軍人事務部臨床人員的問題，而 Abridge 與 Knowtex 共享一份 7.76 億美元的 VA 合約（The Inference；Menlo Ventures）。 : it has public-sector volume that evidences use rather than installs. Assistant answered more than 40,000 questions from Department of Veterans Affairs clinicians last month, and Abridge shares a $776M VA contract with Knowtex (The Inference; Menlo Ventures).
  • 疑慮Concern ：本業正在被渠道侵蝕。環境抄寫市場 2026 年規模 9.5 億美元但只成長 1.6 倍，EHR 內建功能市佔從 33% 升到 46%、吃掉新增支出的 68%，AI-native 抄寫商整體營收成長 28% 卻掉了 13% 市佔（Menlo Ventures）。Assistant 若賣不動，抄寫這一側不會回來。 : the original business is being eaten by distribution. Ambient scribing was a $950M market in 2026 but grew only 1.6x; EHR-native share went from 33% to 46% and took 68% of new dollars, while AI-native scribes grew revenue 28% and lost 13% of share (Menlo Ventures). If Assistant does not sell, the scribe side is not coming back.
  • 疑慮Concern ：它要收錢的對手是免費的。OpenEvidence 對經認證美國醫師免費、靠廣告收錢，且已握有臨床 copilot 約 64% 營收（Menlo Ventures）。Abridge 目前未公布 Assistant 的定價模式、付費採用率或任何臨床結果數據；Cochrane 授權的金額亦未揭露，而全文仍需 Wiley 訂閱才能看。 : it has to charge against something free. OpenEvidence is free to verified US physicians, funded by advertising, and already holds about 64% of clinical copilot revenue (Menlo Ventures). Abridge has published no pricing model for Assistant, no paid adoption rate and no clinical outcome data; the Cochrane licence fee is undisclosed, and full text still requires a Wiley subscription.

OpenEvidence + Access Intelligence

對醫師免費、靠廣告收錢，現在連「病人付不起」也一起處理 · OpenEvidence（美國，邁阿密）Free to physicians, paid for by advertising, now handling affordability too · OpenEvidence (US, Miami)

功能與定位。核心是一個以同儕審查文獻為基礎的臨床問答引擎，對以國家執業者識別碼驗證身分的美國醫師免費，營收來自廣告；內容側有 NEJM 與 JAMA 系列期刊授權，組織側有 AMA、NCCN、AAFP 與 ACEP（公開資料整理）。11/11 的 Access Intelligence 把產品邊界從「該開什麼」推到「病人拿不拿得到」：逾 2,500 個病人支援方案的資格查詢、預填申請表、以及依指引與 ICD-10 碼代擬的事前授權、申覆與醫療必要性信函，平台上已寫過逾 50 萬份（OpenEvidence, 11/11）。 Function and position. At its core is a clinical question-answering engine built on peer-reviewed literature, free to US physicians verified by National Provider Identifier and funded by advertising; on the content side it licenses NEJM and the JAMA family of journals, and on the organisational side it has the AMA, NCCN, AAFP and ACEP (compiled public record). Access Intelligence, launched 11/11, pushes the product boundary from what to prescribe to whether the patient can get it: eligibility lookups across more than 2,500 patient support programmes, pre-filled enrolment forms, and prior authorisations, appeals and letters of medical necessity drafted against guidelines and ICD-10 codes — more than 500,000 already written on the platform (OpenEvidence, 11/11).

  • 優勢Strength ：類別份額已經是壟斷等級。臨床 copilot 營收約 64% 在它一家、超過第二名六倍以上（Menlo Ventures）。公開資料顯示 2025 年 7 月有逾 43 萬名註冊美國醫師（約全美 40%）、每月逾 850 萬次諮詢，到 2025 年 12 月為約 76 萬名醫師、每月約 1,800 萬次諮詢（公開資料整理）。 : its category share is already monopoly-grade. About 64% of clinical copilot revenue sits with it, more than 6x the next vendor (Menlo Ventures). The public record shows more than 430,000 registered US physicians (about 40% of the US total) and over 8.5M consultations a month as of July 2025, rising to about 760,000 physicians and roughly 18M consultations a month by December 2025 (compiled public record).
  • 優勢Strength ：免費是渠道武器而不只是定價選擇。在醫療系統採購周期從 7 個月拉長到 10 個月、院外提供者從 5 個月拉到 11 個月的環境下（Menlo Ventures），一個不需要過採購的產品直接繞過整條流程——醫師自己註冊就能用。 : free is a distribution weapon, not just a price point. With procurement stretching from 7 to 10 months for health systems and 5 to 11 months for outpatient providers (Menlo Ventures), a product that needs no procurement bypasses the whole pipeline — the physician just signs up.
  • 疑慮Concern ：廣告驅動的商業模式與 Access Intelligence 的功能之間存在結構性利益衝突。產品現在同時決定「要不要提這個治療」與「連到哪一個自付額補助方案」，而付錢的是廣告主。新聞稿未說明與支援方案贊助者是否存在商業安排，也未說明廣告與臨床回答之間的區隔機制。 : there is a structural conflict between an advertising-funded model and what Access Intelligence does. The product now decides both whether to surface a treatment and which copay programme it links to, while advertisers pay the bills. The release says nothing about commercial arrangements with programme sponsors, and nothing about the separation between advertising and clinical answers.
  • 疑慮Concern ：它缺的正是 Abridge 有的那一條線。除了 2026 年 5 月與 Cedars-Sinai 的病歷整合之外，它基本上不在 EHR 工作流裡、不接院內指引；而這一段正是 Wolters Kluwer 願意花錢買、Abridge 當成護城河在講的東西。它也沒有公布任何臨床結果研究，公司自述的 USMLE 分數（2023 年 90%、2025 年 100%）不是臨床效能證據。 : what it lacks is precisely the wiring Abridge has. Apart from the Cedars-Sinai chart integration in May 2026, it largely sits outside the EHR workflow and does not connect to local guidelines — the very segment Wolters Kluwer paid to acquire and Abridge pitches as its moat. It has also published no clinical outcome studies, and its self-reported USMLE scores (90% in 2023, 100% in 2025) are not clinical-performance evidence.

兩者的真正差別不在模型，在收錢的對象。Abridge 向醫療系統收錢，所以它必須證明省下的時間與增加的收入值那個價，也因此必須擁有 EHR 那條線；OpenEvidence 向廣告主收錢，所以它只需要醫師每天打開它，也因此可以不管採購流程、不管院內指引、不管誰簽約。這兩條路在 2026 年都還成立，但它們不可能同時贏——因為當 OpenAI 把後勤任務標到每件 8.5 美分（The Inference, 11/17），按席次收費的那一方要先解釋自己的價格。 The real difference is not the model, it is who pays. Abridge charges health systems, so it has to prove the hours saved and the revenue gained are worth the price — which is why it must own the EHR wiring. OpenEvidence charges advertisers, so it only needs physicians to open it daily — which is why it can ignore procurement, local guidelines and who signed the contract. Both paths still work in 2026, but they cannot both win: once OpenAI prices back-office tasks at 8.5 cents each (The Inference, 11/17), the side charging per seat has to justify its price first.

04 — Taiwan Angle

同一週台灣也發表了一個產品，但它站在這張夾心圖的另一層 Taiwan shipped a product the same week — but it stands on a different layer of that sandwich

(1) 台灣這一週的發表在物理層，不在 copilot 層。11/26，鴻海與臺北榮總的 AI 化療自動化智慧場域正式亮相，同時啟用北榮的「北海 AI 中心」。合作源於 2025 年 10 月簽署的意向書，三年投入新台幣 3 億元，聚焦智慧醫院自動化、AI 多模態醫療模型與居家照護。化療自動化由三段組成：日本湯山製作所的調配設備搭配川崎重工雙機械手臂以降低人員接觸高風險藥物、法博智能會自己搭電梯送藥的自主移動機器人、以及鴻海自家的 Nurabot——後者接上醫院 HIS，以生物辨識核對病人身分、醫令與護理人員身分，運算跑在 NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 邊緣模組上，並以 NVIDIA Omniverse 建數位孿生做流程驗證。北榮院長陳威明把界線講得很清楚：「智慧醫療並非透過機器人取代藥師與護理師」；衛福部長石崇良則說智慧醫療可能成為台灣的「第二座護國神山」。要誠實標註的是：這套系統目前處於臨床場域驗證階段，報導未提供部署規模、病人數或成本節省數字。 (1) Taiwan's launch this week was in the physical layer, not the copilot layer. On 11/26, Foxconn and Taipei Veterans General Hospital unveiled their AI chemotherapy automation site and opened TVGH's Beihai AI Center. The partnership dates to a letter of intent signed in October 2025, with NT$300 million over three years across smart hospital automation, AI multimodal medical models and home care. The chemo automation has three segments: compounding equipment from Japan's Yuyama paired with Kawasaki Heavy Industries' dual-robotic-arm technology to cut staff contact with high-risk drugs; FARobot autonomous mobile robots that take the lifts to deliver the drugs; and Foxconn's own Nurabot, connected to the hospital information system, verifying patient identity, orders and nurse identity biometrically, running on NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin edge modules, with a digital twin built in NVIDIA Omniverse for process validation. TVGH superintendent Chen Wei-ming drew the line plainly: smart healthcare is not robots replacing pharmacists and nurses. Health Minister Shih Chung-liang said it could become Taiwan's "second sacred mountain of national protection." To be honest about it: the system is in clinical site validation, and the coverage gives no deployment scale, patient counts or cost savings.

(2) copilot 那一層的商業模式在台灣幾乎無法複製，這不是技術問題。OpenEvidence 之所以能免費，是因為它向廣告主收錢，而付得起的廣告主是美國藥廠與醫材商；它之所以能繞過採購，是因為美國醫師個人就能用 NPI 註冊。台灣兩者都不成立：市場規模支撐不起同等廣告收入，而健保體系下醫師的工具採用高度集中在院方決策。所以台灣要進的不是 copilot 那一格，而是夾心中間那一層裡還沒被壓扁的位置：查核別人的輸出、以及集中管理別人的模型。這一層賣的是治理與編排，而台灣恰好有一個別人沒有的條件：單一支付者＋已完成跨院病歷交換試行的基礎建設。鉅亨網 8/21 的報導指出長庚、馬偕與中山附醫已完成即時跨院病歷交換，衛福部並在推三大 AI 中心。 (2) The copilot layer's business model is close to unreproducible in Taiwan, and that is not a technical problem. OpenEvidence can be free because it charges advertisers, and the advertisers who can pay are US pharma and device makers; it can bypass procurement because a US physician can self-register with an NPI. Neither holds in Taiwan: the market cannot support equivalent advertising revenue, and under single-payer national health insurance tool adoption is concentrated in hospital-level decisions. So the square for Taiwan is not the copilot one but the part of the middle layer not yet crushed: checking someone else's output, and centrally managing someone else's models. That layer sells governance and orchestration, and Taiwan happens to hold a condition others do not: a single payer plus infrastructure that has already piloted cross-hospital record exchange. Anue's 8/21 report notes that Chang Gung, Mackay and Chung Shan Medical University Hospital have completed real-time cross-hospital record exchange, with the Ministry of Health and Welfare advancing three AI centres.

(3) 給付與治理這兩件事，台灣的時間窗口比美國短。DeepHealth 的 Mammo Risk 拿到 FDA 許可的同時只拿到一個第三類 CPT 代碼——意思是美國也還沒有給付，先用一個編號去累積使用證據（DeepHealth）。台灣沒有對應的過渡編碼機制，健保要不給付、要不不給付，因此「先上線累積實證再談給付」這條路在台灣走不通，醫院自費導入的動機就只剩下省人力。而治理那一側的數字值得所有正在推 AI 中心的單位看一次：CHIME 的調查顯示生成式 AI 的完整導入率一年內從 31% 升到 55%，但只有 16% 的 AI 使用者建立了治理與監督政策（HIStalk, 11/18）。導入速度是監督速度的三倍多，這個落差在美國是風險，在單一支付者體系裡會直接變成給付爭議。 (3) On reimbursement and governance, Taiwan's window is shorter than America's. DeepHealth's Mammo Risk got FDA clearance and, with it, only a Category III CPT code — meaning the US does not reimburse this either, and uses a tracking number to accumulate usage evidence first (DeepHealth). Taiwan has no equivalent transitional code: national health insurance either pays or it does not, so the route of deploying first, gathering evidence, then arguing for payment is not available, and a hospital's remaining incentive to self-fund is labour saved. On the governance side, one figure deserves a look from every unit now standing up an AI centre: CHIME's survey found full adoption of generative AI rose from 31% to 55% in twelve months while only 16% of AI users have governance and oversight policies (HIStalk, 11/18). Adoption is running more than three times ahead of oversight — a risk in the US, and in a single-payer system a reimbursement dispute waiting to happen.
