週五是深度拆解日。這一週剛好把醫療 AI 的兩條金流並排放在桌上：11 月 2 日 Hippocratic AI 發表 Polaris 6 Ultra，把 671B 的主生成模型包進 30 個專科監督模型與 142 個生產監控器、整個 constellation 衝到 7.4 兆參數，公司累計募資 4.44 億美元、2.5 億通語音互動，向醫院收的是一小時一小時的 agent 工時。另一邊 10 月 27 日 Vera Health 用 12 名員工拿下 2,850 萬美元 A 輪，800,000 名臨床醫師、190 國、永久免費，而同一個賽道上估值 150 億美元的 OpenEvidence 把帳單轉嫁給藥廠廣告——Sacra 估 7 月年化約 3 億美元、CPM 逾 1,000 美元卻只賣出約 5% 存貨。然後 10 月 29 日 Nature Health 的 883,403 筆回答把這條金流釘在牆上：5 個模型的第一建議平均 97.5% 收斂到同一個商品名，原廠藥勝算是學名藥的 20.5 倍。本期拆的不是誰的技術好，是誰的錢從哪裡來、以及那條金流會把建議推往哪裡。 Friday is deep-dive day, and this week set medical AI's two revenue lines side by side. On November 2 Hippocratic AI shipped Polaris 6 Ultra, wrapping a 671B generative core in 30 specialist supervisors and 142 production monitors for a 7.4-trillion-parameter constellation, on $444M raised and 250 million voice interactions — and it bills hospitals by the agent-hour. On October 27, Vera Health closed a $28.5M Series A with twelve employees, 800,000 clinicians in 190 countries, free for good. In the same lane the $15B OpenEvidence sends the bill to pharma instead — roughly $300M annualized by July on Sacra's estimate, CPMs above $1,000, only about 5% of inventory sold. Then on October 29, 883,403 answers in Nature Health pinned that revenue line to the wall: across five models the first recommendation converged 97.5% onto a single brand name, with originator drugs carrying 20.5x the odds of a generic. Today is not about whose model is better. It is about where each company's money comes from, and which way that money bends the advice.

Polaris 6 Ultra：把 7.4 兆參數堆在「不要聽錯藥名」這一件事上 Polaris 6 Ultra: 7.4 trillion parameters spent on not mishearing a drug name

發生什麼What

Hippocratic AI 於 11 月 2 日自加州 Menlo Park 發表 Polaris 6 Ultra，架構是 671B 參數的主生成模型加上 30 個專科監督模型與 142 個持續運行的生產監控器，整個 constellation 7.4 兆參數，前一代 Polaris 5 為 5.0 兆（官方新聞稿日期為 10/31）。首句延遲 278 毫秒，官方在相同 prompt 上比對的四家最快商業供應商為 0.71–0.94 秒，端到端速度為前代 1.3 倍。自家醫療 TTS 在 629 個藥名上唸對 90%，對照商業 TTS 的 75%。最硬的一組數字來自 2,778 通由臨床人員覆核的實際通話：藥名或症狀聽錯從每百通 1.19 降到 0.20、數字姓名與聯絡方式聽錯從 0.57 降到 0.00、純噪音音訊上的幻聽從 24% 降到 0.6%、電視人聲被誤當成病人發言從 64.8% 降到 3% 以下。訓練語料為 3.23 億通真實病人對話。 Hippocratic AI announced Polaris 6 Ultra from Menlo Park on November 2. The architecture is a 671B-parameter generative core plus 30 specialist supervisor models and 142 continuously running production monitors — 7.4 trillion parameters of constellation, against 5.0 trillion in Polaris 5 (the company's own release is dated Oct 31). Time to first sentence is 278 ms, against 0.71–0.94 seconds for the four fastest commercial providers on byte-identical prompts, and end-to-end speed is 1.3x the previous generation. Its in-house medical TTS pronounced 90% of 629 drug names correctly, versus 75% for a leading commercial TTS. The hardest numbers come from 2,778 clinician-reviewed production calls: misheard medications or symptoms fell from 1.19 to 0.20 per hundred calls, misheard numbers, names and contact details from 0.57 to 0.00, hallucinated words on noise-only audio from 24% to 0.6%, and television speech attributed to the patient from 64.8% to under 3%. Training drew on 323 million real patient conversations.

為什麼重要Why it matters

注意這些數字的性質：它們不是「診斷準確率」，而是「電話品質」。把電視聲音當成病人說話、在沒人講話的噪音裡幻聽，這些是語音 agent 真正會害死人的失效模式，而過去一年大多數廠商的新聞稿都不報這一類。Hippocratic 願意把 64.8% 這種難看的基線寫出來，本身就是這則消息最有價值的部分——它把討論從「模型多聰明」拉回「在一間有電視開著的病房裡，這通電話會不會記錯劑量」。 Note what kind of numbers these are. They are not diagnostic accuracy; they are call quality. Attributing a television voice to the patient, or hallucinating words out of room noise, are the failure modes that actually hurt people on a voice line — and most vendor releases over the past year have not reported them at all. That Hippocratic published an ugly 64.8% baseline is the most useful thing in this announcement: it moves the argument from how clever the model is to whether, in a room with the TV on, the call records the right dose.

「比基礎模型高 18 分、比最強前沿模型高 11 分」用的是 Hippocratic 自家的內部醫療智能 benchmark，無第三方稽核，也沒公開題庫。「輕微傷害率 0.00%」同樣是自家臨床輸出測試。新聞稿只說「彈性定價更低」，沒有給任何每小時單價；也沒有具名客戶，只提到「多家醫療體系」身兼投資人。連監督模型的數量在同一份稿裡都出現 30 與 31 兩個版本。 The "18 points above base, 11 points above the best frontier model" figures come from Hippocratic's own internal healthcare-intelligence benchmark, with no third-party audit and no published item set. The "0.00% minor-harm rate" is likewise in-house clinician output testing. The release says only that pricing is newly flexible and lower; it gives no per-hour rate, and names no customer, referring only to unnamed "health systems" among its investors. 發生什麼What

Mayo Clinic Platform 於 11 月 3 日商業上線一套 AI 臨床決策支援方案，由兩個元件組成：涵蓋 35 個以上次專科的 LLM 臨床聊天（回答自然語言問題並引用同儕審查文獻），以及 22 個可用文字或語音叫出的風險與診斷計算器。知識庫每日吸收新的臨床試驗結果、改版指引與藥物安全警訊；計算器混用業界標準風險分層演算法與 Mayo 研究者自建並驗證的工具。使用對象是 Mayo 自己的臨床人員，以及參與 Mayo Clinic Platform 醫療提供者方案的外部機構。官方強調重點是讓醫師不必離開 EHR 去翻多個參考來源。 On November 3 Mayo Clinic Platform commercially launched an AI clinical decision support solution built from two pieces: an LLM-powered clinical chat spanning 35-plus subspecialties that answers natural-language questions with citations to peer-reviewed literature, and 22 risk and diagnostic calculators reachable by text or voice dictation. The knowledge base ingests new trial results, revised guidelines and drug safety warnings daily; the calculators mix industry-standard risk stratification algorithms with proprietary tools developed and validated by Mayo investigators. It is available to Mayo's own clinicians and to outside organizations in Mayo Clinic Platform's provider programs. Mayo's framing is that clinicians should not have to leave the EHR to consult several references.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是今天兩條金流之外的第三條路：醫療體系自己做，用品牌與自家驗證過的演算法當護城河，不靠廣告也不賣 agent 工時。對 OpenEvidence、Vera Health、UpToDate 來說，Mayo 的名字本身就是最難複製的那一項——引用同一批文獻，但掛 Mayo 的招牌。值得注意的是 Mayo 把它開放給「參與平台方案的外部機構」，意思是這套東西最終會變成一個賣給別家醫院的產品，而不只是內部工具。 This is a third revenue line beside today's two: the health system builds it itself, with its brand and its own validated algorithms as the moat — no ads, no agent-hours. For OpenEvidence, Vera Health and UpToDate, the Mayo name is the one input they cannot copy: the same literature, under a different signature. Worth noting that Mayo is opening it to outside organizations in its platform programs, which means this ends up as a product sold to other hospitals, not merely an internal tool.

要打折的地方Discount this

公開報導裡沒有任何定價、沒有支援的 EHR 廠商名單、沒有已導入的外部機構家數，也沒有一個臨床成效或準確率數字——只有「35 個以上次專科」、「22 個計算器」、「每日更新」這三個規模數字。對一套聲稱要取代醫師查多個來源的工具，完全沒有誤答率或引用正確率的揭露。 Public coverage carries no pricing, no list of supported EHR vendors, no count of outside organizations live, and not one clinical outcome or accuracy figure — only three scale numbers: 35-plus subspecialties, 22 calculators, daily updates. For a tool pitched as replacing several reference lookups, nothing is disclosed about error rate or citation fidelity.

88 萬筆回答：AI 的第一個藥名建議，97.5% 收斂到同一個商品 883,403 answers: AI's first drug pick converges 97.5% onto one brand

發生什麼What

大阪公立大學與美國 MD Anderson 癌症中心的團隊在 Nature Health 發表〈Large language models show product name convergence in drug recommendations〉。設計是 123 組「常見疾病 × 指定有效成分」配上 1,440 種社會人口背景，生成 177,120 個合成臨床情境，丟給 GPT-5.4、Claude Sonnet 4.6、Gemini 3.1 Flash、Llama 4、MedGemma 五個模型，每次請它提出最多 3 個 FDA 核准商品名，共收得 883,403 筆至少含一個建議的回答，再由 4 名醫師核對品名與核准資訊。結果：每組疾病平均有 5.5 種 FDA 核准產品可選，但第一建議中最常出現的單一產品平均占 97.5%，123 組裡有 96 組主導產品占比超過 90%，模型間一致性 Fleiss' κ = 0.895。對照美國 2019–2023 年真實處方調查，最常開立的產品平均只有 29.7% 與 AI 第一選擇一致、依處方量加權後僅 17.0%。偏誤方向很清楚：原廠藥被推薦的勝算是學名藥的 20.5 倍、Google 搜尋量較高者 1.70 倍、有獨立 Wikipedia 條目者 3.00 倍。成本模擬顯示 51 種有完整價格資料的成分裡有 38 種（74.5%）在 AI 建議分布下更貴。 A team from Osaka Metropolitan University and MD Anderson published "Large language models show product name convergence in drug recommendations" in Nature Health. The design crossed 123 condition-and-active-ingredient pairs with 1,440 sociodemographic profiles into 177,120 synthetic clinical scenarios, put them to GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Flash, Llama 4 and MedGemma asking for up to three FDA-approved brand names each, collected 883,403 answers containing at least one recommendation, and had four physicians verify names and approval status. Each pair had 5.5 approved products available on average — yet the single most frequent product took 97.5% of first recommendations, 96 of the 123 pairs had a dominant product above 90%, and inter-model agreement reached Fleiss' κ = 0.895. Against US prescribing data for 2019–2023, the most-prescribed product matched the AI's first pick only 29.7% of the time, and 17.0% weighted by prescription volume. The direction of the bias is unambiguous: originator brands carried 20.5x the odds of a generic, products with higher Google search volume 1.70x, and products with their own Wikipedia entry 3.00x. In a cost simulation, 38 of 51 ingredients with complete pricing (74.5%) came out more expensive under the AI's recommendation distribution.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這篇與今天的主題直接相扣：它證明了即使完全沒有廣告，模型也會因為「商品名在網路語料裡的聲量」而系統性偏向原廠藥。Wikipedia 條目 3.00 倍這一項尤其值得咀嚼——那不是臨床證據，是維基編輯活動。於是 OpenEvidence 這類靠藥廠廣告維生的平台處在一個很尷尬的位置：它可以誠實地說廣告不影響答案，但這篇論文顯示，不必影響答案，只要影響語料就夠了。而藥廠行銷本來就在影響語料。 This bolts directly onto today's theme: it shows that even with no advertising at all, models skew systematically toward originator brands purely because of how loudly a brand name appears in web text. The 3.00x for having a Wikipedia entry is the one to sit with — that is not clinical evidence, it is editing activity. Which puts an ad-funded platform like OpenEvidence in an awkward spot: it can honestly say advertisers do not touch the answers, while this paper shows you never needed to touch the answers — touching the corpus is enough. And pharma marketing has always been in the business of touching the corpus.

全部是合成情境，沒有真實病人、沒有真實處方行為。那個最搶眼的「每年採購成本增加 5,560 萬美元、增幅 1,019%」是在「假設醫師照 AI 建議分布開藥」前提下的推估，不是實際發生的費用，轉述時務必帶上這個前提。價格資料為美國基礎，不能直接套進台灣健保。模型版本或提示詞換掉，結果可能不同。本文數字引自中文二手報導，原始論文頁面在本次產製時無法取回全文。 Everything here is synthetic: no real patients, no real prescribing behavior. The eye-catching "$55.6M a year, a 1,019% increase" is a projection conditional on physicians prescribing according to the AI's recommendation distribution — not money anyone has spent, and the conditional must travel with the figure. Pricing is US-based and does not transfer to Taiwan's single payer. Different model versions or prompts could move the results. The figures here are taken from Chinese-language secondary coverage; the primary paper page could not be retrieved in full during this run.

Vera Health 於 10 月 27 日公布 2,850 萬美元 A 輪，由 Threshold Ventures 領投，Mantis VC 與 SV Angel 參與，既有股東 Y Combinator 與 Gradient 加碼，累計資本 3,200 萬美元。公司自述使用者超過 800,000 名臨床醫師、遍及 190 國，一年前只有數千名活躍醫師；在西班牙與義大利約每 4 名執業醫師有 1 人在用、法國每 7 人有 1 人。自 2026 年 6 月起每月都是 Apple App Store 與 Google Play 全球下載量最高的臨床 App，9 月下載量超過 OpenEvidence 與 Doximity 相加。團隊 12 人、企業行銷預算為零，成長幾乎全靠醫師口碑。 Vera Health announced a $28.5M Series A on October 27, led by Threshold Ventures with Mantis VC and SV Angel, and existing backers Y Combinator and Gradient increasing their stakes, for $32M in total capitalization. It has been the most-downloaded clinical app worldwide on both the Apple App Store and Google Play every month since June 2026, and logged more September downloads than OpenEvidence and Doximity combined. Headcount is twelve, the enterprise marketing budget is zero, and growth has come almost entirely from peer referral.

為什麼重要Why it matters

架構上的差別才是重點：Vera 的設計是先檢索並評分證據、再生成答案，而不是先生成文字再補引用。它索引 6,000 萬篇以上同儕審查文獻、臨床指引、共識聲明與 FDA／EMA 藥品標籤，並對研究設計、樣本數、世代檢力、時效、期刊影響係數與引用速度打分，再把主張依證據強度上色——證據薄弱的用紅色標出來。這是把「不確定性」做成 UI 元件，而不是藏在免責聲明裡。企業版 Vera Spaces 讓醫院上傳自家醫令套組、處方集與流程，與全球文獻並列優先，示範情境是按該院抗生素敏感性報告調整的抗微生物劑量。 The architectural difference is the point: Vera retrieves and grades evidence before generating an answer, rather than generating text and attaching citations after. It indexes more than 60 million peer-reviewed publications, guidelines, consensus statements and FDA/EMA drug labels, scores study design, sample size, cohort power, recency, journal impact factor and citation velocity, and then color-codes each claim by evidence strength, flagging thin evidence in red. That makes uncertainty a UI element rather than a disclaimer. The enterprise tier, Vera Spaces, lets hospitals upload their own order sets, formularies and protocols to be prioritized alongside global literature — the worked example is antimicrobial dosing tuned to a hospital's own antibiogram.

所有使用者數字都是公司自述，沒有第三方稽核；「下載量最高」是下載量而不是活躍使用量，而一個 12 人團隊要服務 800,000 名醫師，這個比例本身就該讓人追問實際日活。估值、營收、Vera Spaces 定價全未揭露。對醫師永久免費的承諾，在沒有廣告也沒有公開企業營收的情況下，長期由誰補貼是未解問題。 Every user figure is company-reported with no third-party audit, and "most downloaded" measures downloads, not active use — a twelve-person team serving 800,000 physicians is a ratio that invites a question about daily actives. Valuation, revenue and Vera Spaces pricing are all undisclosed. With no advertising and no published enterprise revenue, who subsidizes a permanent free tier for clinicians is an open question.

為什麼重要Why it matters

「Patient Aware」這三個字是這則消息的全部重量所在。OpenEvidence 原本是一個不碰病歷的文獻問答工具，所以它靠廣告維生時，藥廠買到的只是醫師的注意力。一旦它讀得到病歷，同一個版位買到的就是「這位醫師此刻正在處理哪一類病人」。NYU Langone 之前的 Mount Sinai 與 Sutter Health 也是同一套 Epic 嵌入，而 Sacra 指出這些醫療體系的部署跑的是同一個廣告支撐模型。這是今天整份日報最該被追問的一條線。 The weight of this item sits entirely in the words "Patient Aware." OpenEvidence began as a literature Q&A tool that never touched the chart, so what pharma bought with an ad slot was a physician's attention. Once it can read the chart, the same slot buys knowledge of which kind of patient that physician is working through right now. Mount Sinai and Sutter Health run the same Epic embedding, and Sacra notes those health-system deployments run on the same ad-supported model. 04 — Taiwan Angle

同樣的技術，到台灣會長成另一個形狀——因為沒有藥廠 DTC 廣告市場，也因為指引早就寫了一條 The same technology grows a different shape here — because there is no DTC pharma ad market, and because the guidance already has a clause for it

(1) 廣告模型在台灣沒有對應市場，所以免費的那一端只剩兩條路。OpenEvidence 的整個商業模式建立在美國允許藥廠向醫師大規模投放處方藥廣告，CPM 可以喊到 1,000 美元以上。台灣沒有這個規模的醫藥廣告市場，意思是「對醫師免費」在台灣只能靠兩種方式支撐：醫院付企業授權（Vera Spaces 或 UpToDate 那條路），或由公部門／學會補貼。這一點決定了台灣醫院評估這類工具時，該直接跳過廣告支撐的版本去談企業版條件，而不是先讓醫師免費用起來、再處理資料治理——因為免費版的資料流向在台灣沒有相應的廣告法規可以約束。 (1) The ad model has no counterpart market in Taiwan, so "free" here has only two roads. OpenEvidence's entire business rests on the US permitting pharmaceutical companies to advertise prescription drugs to physicians at scale, with CPMs quoted above $1,000. Taiwan has no pharmaceutical advertising market of that size, which means "free for clinicians" can only be carried here by a hospital paying an enterprise license (the Vera Spaces or UpToDate road) or by public or society subsidy. That should change how Taiwanese hospitals evaluate these tools: go straight to enterprise terms rather than letting physicians adopt a free tier first and sorting out data governance later, because the data flows of a free tier have no corresponding advertising regulation here to constrain them.

(2) Polaris 這種病人端外撥 agent，在台灣的第一道門是一條揭露義務。衛福部 115 年 5 月 29 日以衛部醫字第 1151663164 號函頒的「醫療機構應用生成式人工智慧指引」已經明文要求：AI 工具若直接與民眾對話，須主動揭露其為 AI，不能等民眾詢問才說明；若具錄影功能亦應事先告知病患。這條規定對 Polaris 6 Ultra 的核心賣點是正面衝撞——它花了 7.4 兆參數在讓對話聽起來自然、加入呼吸聲與停頓節奏，而台灣的指引要求它一開口就說「我是 AI」。這不是不能做，而是導入前就該把揭露話術、病人拒接後的人工接續流程，以及錄音告知寫進 SOP，不要等上線才補。 (2) For an outbound patient-facing agent like Polaris, the first gate in Taiwan is a disclosure duty. The MOHW's Guidance on the Use of Generative AI by Medical Institutions, issued on May 29, 2026 under letter 1151663164, already requires that an AI tool speaking directly with the public proactively disclose that it is AI rather than waiting to be asked, and that patients be told in advance if recording is involved. That collides head-on with Polaris 6 Ultra's central pitch: it spends 7.4 trillion parameters making the conversation sound natural, complete with breaths and pacing, while Taiwan's guidance requires it to open by saying it is AI. Not a blocker — but the disclosure script, the human fallback path when a patient declines, and the recording notice all belong in the SOP before go-live, not after.

(3) 台灣已經在做的，是「交班文書」而不是「打電話給病人」。10 月 22 日大甲與苑裡李綜合醫院同步導入易晨智能的生成式 AI 語音交班紀錄系統，支援國語、台語、客語、英語與醫療專有名詞，具即時辨識、贅字過濾與語者分離，再由生成式 AI 彙整交班重點並產出 Care Plan，兩院同時升級護理行動推車、HIS 與病房電子白板。注意這個選擇的合理性：交班紀錄的失效後果可控、不涉病人直接互動、不需處理揭露義務，而多語（尤其台語與客語）才是台灣真正的技術門檻——Polaris 的 629 個英文藥名發音測試在台灣完全不適用。台灣廠商的機會窗口就在這裡：不是追 7.4 兆參數，是把台語與客語的臨床語音做到可用。 (3) What Taiwan is actually deploying is shift-handover documentation, not outbound patient calls. On October 22 the Dajia and Yuanli branches of the Lee's General Hospital system both went live with Yichen Intelligence's generative-AI voice handover record system, covering Mandarin, Taiwanese, Hakka, English and medical terminology, with real-time recognition, filler removal and speaker separation, then generative AI summarizing handover points and producing a care plan, alongside upgrades to nursing carts, the HIS and ward electronic whiteboards. Note why that choice is sound: the consequences of a handover error are contained, there is no direct patient interaction, no disclosure duty to handle — and multilingual recognition, Taiwanese and Hakka above all, is the real technical bar here. Polaris's 629-English-drug-name pronunciation test does not transfer at all. That is the window for Taiwanese vendors: not chasing 7.4 trillion parameters, but making clinical speech in Taiwanese and Hakka actually work.

(4) 那篇 Nature Health 對台灣的意義在健保，不在成本。論文的成本推估是美國藥價基礎，報導本身也說明不能直接套用至台灣健保。但「原廠藥被推薦的勝算是學名藥的 20.5 倍」這一項與定價無關，它是語料偏誤，會原封不動地跟著模型進台灣。在台灣的制度下，這不會變成採購成本爆增（健保有給付規則與藥價基準），而會變成另一種摩擦：AI 建議的品項與健保給付的品項不一致，產生更多的事前審查與事後核刪爭議。要在導入前處理的不是價格，是把健保給付規則與醫院處方集餵進檢索層——這正好是 Vera Spaces 那種「上傳自家處方集並優先排序」的功能在台灣最值得買的理由。 (4) For Taiwan, the Nature Health paper matters through the single payer, not through cost. Its cost projection is built on US drug prices, and the coverage itself says it cannot be transferred to Taiwan's NHI. But the 20.5x odds favoring originator brands has nothing to do with pricing — it is corpus bias, and it travels into Taiwan with the model untouched. Under Taiwan's system that will not show up as an acquisition-cost explosion, because reimbursement rules and reference pricing already bound that. It will show up as a different friction: the AI recommends an item the NHI does not reimburse on those terms, generating more prior-review and post-hoc disallowance disputes. What has to be handled before go-live is not price but feeding NHI reimbursement rules and the hospital's own formulary into the retrieval layer — which is exactly why a Vera Spaces-style "upload your formulary and prioritize it" feature is the thing most worth paying for here.
