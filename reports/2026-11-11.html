美國在同一週把臨床 AI 軟體變成一個付費類別、也開始問自主手術機器人該怎麼評——CMS 新設 SaMS 與狀態指標 O1、36 碼改派 New Technology APC、14 個演算法檢驗碼整批離開 CLFS，而 FDA 同週徵詢自主與遠端操控機器人的效益風險框架，ONC 卻反向刪掉 AI 模型卡揭露 In one week the US turned clinical AI software into a payment category and started asking how to judge an autonomous surgical robot — CMS creates SaMS and status indicator O1, moves 36 codes to New Technology APCs and pulls 14 algorithmic laboratory codes off the CLFS, while FDA opens consultation on benefit-risk for autonomous and teleoperated robots and ONC deletes the AI model-card disclosure

今天的法規主軸不是「允不允許」，而是「怎麼收錢」。CMS 在 11 月 4 日一口氣發布 CY2027 的醫師費表（PFS）、醫院門診（OPPS／ASC）與居家照護三份終局規則，OPPS 那份於 11 月 6 日刊登 Federal Register。其中最少被報導、但對臨床 AI 影響最深的一段，是 CMS 把沿用多年的「Software as a Service」改名為 Software as a Medical Service（SaMS）、新設狀態指標 O1，並把 36 個 HCPCS 碼改派到 New Technology APC——Reed Smith 稱之為一次「interim」政策，CMS 自己在規則裡寫的是「an interim first step」。同一週 11 月 6 日，FDA CDRH 發出自主與遠端操控機器人醫材的效益風險討論文件，而 10 月 26 日 ONC 的 HTI-5 終局規則才剛把 AI 模型卡揭露要求刪掉。給錢的門打開、揭露的門關小，這是今天要看清的一組反向動作。 Today's regulatory story is not about permission; it is about payment. On November 4 CMS released three CY2027 final rules at once — the Physician Fee Schedule, hospital outpatient (OPPS/ASC) and home health — with the OPPS rule appearing in the Federal Register on November 6. The least-covered passage, and the one that matters most to clinical AI, is where CMS renames its long-standing "Software as a Service" to Software as a Medical Service (SaMS), creates status indicator O1, and reassigns 36 HCPCS codes to New Technology APCs — Reed Smith reads it as an interim policy, and CMS's own words in the rule are "an interim first step". In the same week, on November 6, FDA's CDRH issued a benefit-risk discussion paper on robotic devices with autonomous or remote teleoperation capabilities, while on October 26 ONC's HTI-5 final rule had just deleted the AI model-card disclosure requirement. The payment door opens as the disclosure door narrows — that opposing pair is what to watch today.

Medicare 第一次給臨床 AI 軟體一個自己的付費類別：SaMS 與狀態指標 O1，36 碼改派 New Technology APC Medicare gives clinical AI software a payment category of its own: SaMS, status indicator O1, and 36 codes moved to New Technology APCs

發生什麼What

CY2027 OPPS／ASC 終局規則與 PFS 終局規則同時把「Software as a Service」改名為 Software as a Medical Service（SaMS），理由是前者在雲端運算語境裡太泛。定義維持為「以演算法分析支持臨床決策的軟體技術，含具臨床或診斷功能者」。新設狀態指標 O1：單獨給付、不適用多項手術折扣，付款規格比照狀態 S；CMS 明確否決了採用 T 型（會打多項折扣）的替代方案。Reed Smith, 2026-11-06 另指出一項新增的硬門檻：非檢驗類的 SaMS 分析必須取得 FDA 上市許可才能獲得 Medicare 給付。36 個 HCPCS 碼被指定為 SaMS，原本走臨床 APC 的改派到 New Technology APC，費率刻意貼齊 CY2026；提案階段的拆解（meddeviceguide，依 91 FR 41734）顯示 36 碼中有 21 個是從臨床 APC（狀態 S）搬過去的，原本就在 New Technology APC 的維持 APC、只換成 O1。生效日 2027 年 1 月 1 日。 The CY2027 OPPS/ASC final rule and the PFS final rule together rename "Software as a Service" to Software as a Medical Service (SaMS), on the ground that the old label also covers general cloud computing. The definition stands: software-based technologies that support clinical decision making through algorithmic analysis, including those with clinical or diagnostic functionality. A new status indicator O1 pays separately and is not subject to multiple-procedure discounting, with the same payment specifications as status S; CMS expressly rejected the "T"-type alternative that would have applied discounting. Reed Smith, 2026-11-06 flags one newly hard condition: a non-laboratory SaMS analysis must have FDA marketing authorisation to be paid by Medicare. Thirty-six HCPCS codes are designated SaMS; those previously paid under clinical APCs move to New Technology APCs at rates deliberately pinned to CY2026. A proposal-stage breakdown (meddeviceguide, citing 91 FR 41734) puts 21 of the 36 as moves out of clinical APCs (status S); codes already in New Technology APCs keep their APC and simply switch to O1. Effective January 1, 2027.

為什麼重要Why it matters

過去幾年醫療 AI 公司最常講的瓶頸不是 FDA、是帳單。CMS 自己在規則裡列了三個理由說明臨床 APC 為什麼不適合軟體：軟體在醫院成本報告裡沒有實體材料成本；醫院取得軟體的商業模式多樣、很難被 cost-to-charge ratio 正確捕捉；軟體成本被攤到寬廣臨床 APC 的大量 claim 裡會把中位成本稀釋、導致給付不足。把 SaMS 統一丟進 New Technology APC，等於承認軟體的成本結構跟手術器材不同，需要自己的通道。這是結構性的一步——但要記得它只決定「若被涵蓋，怎麼付」，不等於建立 Medicare 涵蓋範圍，覆蓋與醫療必要性的要求照舊。 For years the bottleneck medical-AI companies named was not FDA but the bill. CMS sets out three reasons in the rule why clinical APCs fit software badly: software carries no physical material cost on hospital cost reports; hospitals acquire it through varied commercial models that cost-to-charge ratios rarely capture; and its cost gets diluted across the many claims in a broad clinical APC, depressing median costs and producing underpayment. Routing all SaMS into New Technology APCs is an admission that software's cost structure differs from that of surgical hardware and needs its own channel. It is a structural step — but it only settles how a service may be paid if covered. It does not establish Medicare coverage, and coverage and medical-necessity requirements still apply.

14 個「純演算法」檢驗碼被整批拉出臨床檢驗費表，病人開始要付部分負擔 Fourteen pure-algorithm laboratory codes are pulled off the Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule — and patients start owing coinsurance

發生什麼What

這是今天最具體、也最容易被漏掉的一段。CMS 把 14 個 CPT 碼從臨床檢驗費表（CLFS）移出，改以「other diagnostic tests」在 OPPS 給付、全部掛狀態 O1；提案階段是 10 碼，終局版本增為 14 碼（見 OPPS 終局規則 Table 85）。Discoveries in Health Policy 的逐碼拆解（2026-11）列出：81417（exome 再評估）→ APC 1505（$301–400 級距，標準給付 $350.50）；0510U → APC 1506（$401–500，$450.50）；0512U、0513U、0414U、0418U、0220U、0376U、1106T、1107T、1097T、1063T、1064T → APC 1509（$701–800，$750.50）；81427（全基因組再評估）→ APC 1522（$2,001–2,500，$2,250.50）。與提案相比：0511U 與 81416 因描述詞含濕式實驗室步驟而被 CMS 同意留在 CLFS，0208U（2022 已刪碼）剔除；新增 81417 與 81427；1063T／1064T 從提案的 $301–400 級距上調到 APC 1509 的 $750.50。PFS 端放棄提案的地區承保商定價，改為全國定價、以 OPPS 費率交叉比對（PFS 終局規則 Table A-D8，同樣 14 碼、狀態 A）。 This is the most concrete passage of the day, and the easiest to miss. CMS moves 14 CPT codes off the Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule (CLFS) and pays them under OPPS as "other diagnostic tests", all carrying status O1; the proposal had 10 codes, the final rule has 14 (OPPS final rule Table 85). A code-by-code reading by Discoveries in Health Policy (Nov 2026) lists: 81417 (exome re-evaluation) → APC 1505 ($301–400 band, standard payment $350.50); 0510U → APC 1506 ($401–500, $450.50); 0512U, 0513U, 0414U, 0418U, 0220U, 0376U, 1106T, 1107T, 1097T, 1063T, 1064T → APC 1509 ($701–800, $750.50); 81427 (genome re-evaluation) → APC 1522 ($2,001–2,500, $2,250.50). Against the proposal: 0511U and 81416 stay on the CLFS after CMS accepted that their descriptors include wet-lab work; 0208U (deleted in 2022) was dropped; 81417 and 81427 were added; and 1063T/1064T moved up from the proposed $301–400 band to APC 1509 at $750.50. On the PFS side CMS abandoned the proposed contractor pricing, switching to national pricing crosswalked to the OPPS rates (PFS final rule Table A-D8, the same 14 codes, status A).

為什麼重要Why it matters

三個連帶效果比金額本身重要。（一）病人開始付錢。CLFS 項目不收 Part B 自付額與部分負擔，OPPS 會；以 $2,250.50 的全基因組再評估為例，約有 450 美元（兩成）落到病人身上。（二）CLIA 門檻鬆動。CMS 的邏輯是：資料既已存在，純演算法分析未必需要 CLIA 實驗室，所以它是診斷檢查而非檢驗。這移除了檢驗業者長期倚賴的進入障礙。（三）14 天檢驗服務日期規則（42 CFR 414.510）不再適用。這些服務脫離了檢驗計費的時序限制。另一個被報導忽略的細節：CMS 承認這 14 碼「should have been included」在提案的 Addendum B（狀態 C）裡，但當時漏了。 Three knock-on effects matter more than the dollar figures. (1) Patients start paying. CLFS items carry no Part B deductible or coinsurance; OPPS items do. On the $2,250.50 genome re-evaluation, roughly $450 (about 20%) lands on the patient. (2) The CLIA barrier loosens. CMS's logic is that once the data exists, a pure algorithmic analysis does not necessarily require a CLIA laboratory, so it is a diagnostic test rather than a laboratory test — removing an entry barrier laboratory incumbents long relied on. (3) The 14-day laboratory date-of-service rule (42 CFR 414.510) no longer applies, freeing these services from laboratory billing's timing constraint. One detail the coverage missed: CMS concedes these 14 codes "should have been included" in the proposed Addendum B at status C, and were not.

FDA 第一次正式開口問：手術機器人的「自主性」該怎麼評——討論文件、1/5 截止、12/2–3 工作坊 FDA asks the question for the first time: how should a surgical robot's autonomy be judged — discussion paper, Jan 5 deadline, Dec 2-3 workshop

發生什麼What

11 月 6 日，CDRH 發布《Evaluating Benefit-Risk in Robotic Medical Devices with Autonomous or Remote Teleoperation Capabilities》討論文件與意見徵求，docket FDA-2026-N-13135，意見截止 2027 年 1 月 5 日。文件涵蓋四個主題：術語定義、自主能力的效益與風險、遠端操控的效益與風險、風險緩解措施。範圍明確寫「Both current and potential future robotic medical devices are in the scope」，American Health Intel（2026-11-08）補充其涵蓋診斷、治療與復健任務，手術與非手術用途都算。FDA 同時強調身分：「This paper is intended for discussion purposes only and does not represent draft or final guidance.」Holland & Knight 的 11 月 10 日政策摘要另載明一場 12 月 2–3 日的公開混合式工作坊（FDA 行事曆上較早的列項只標 12 月 2 日）。本報未下載該份 8.5 MB 的 PDF 全文，因此自主性分級與 FDA 的具體問題清單未能報導；FDA 網頁版也未提及 AI／機器學習、網路安全或延遲。 On November 6 CDRH published Evaluating Benefit-Risk in Robotic Medical Devices with Autonomous or Remote Teleoperation Capabilities as a discussion paper and request for feedback, docket FDA-2026-N-13135, with comments due January 5, 2027. It covers four topics: terminology, benefit and risk of autonomous capabilities, benefit and risk of remote teleoperation, and risk mitigations. The scope is explicit — "Both current and potential future robotic medical devices are in the scope" — and American Health Intel (2026-11-08) adds that it reaches diagnostic, therapeutic and rehabilitative tasks, surgical and non-surgical alike. FDA is careful about its status: "This paper is intended for discussion purposes only and does not represent draft or final guidance." Holland & Knight's November 10 policy digest also records a public hybrid workshop on December 2-3, 2026 (an earlier FDA calendar listing showed only December 2). We did not download the 8.5 MB full PDF, so the autonomy levels and FDA's specific question list go unreported here; FDA's web version also does not mention AI/machine learning, cybersecurity or latency.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這份文件的價值在於它問的問題，而非它給的答案——它沒給答案。FDA 現行的機器人醫材審查邏輯是建立在「器械由醫師操作」的前設上；一旦器械自己決定下一刀，或操作者在幾百公里外，效益風險的計算基底就換了：延遲多久算不安全、自主程度要怎麼分級、人類監督要監督到哪一層、失效時的回退路徑是什麼。把「術語」列為四大主題的第一項，本身就是承認現在連共同語言都還沒有。對業者的實際意義是：這是在規則成形之前能影響定義的窗口，而窗口只開到 1 月 5 日。 The paper's value lies in the question it asks, not the answer it gives — it gives none. FDA's existing logic for robotic devices assumes a clinician operating the instrument. Once the instrument decides the next cut, or the operator sits hundreds of kilometres away, the basis of the benefit-risk calculation changes: how much latency is unsafe, how autonomy should be graded, at what layer human oversight must sit, what the fallback is on failure. Listing "terminology" first among the four topics is itself an admission that a shared vocabulary does not yet exist. For manufacturers the practical point is that this is the window to shape definitions before rules harden — and it closes on January 5.

EU AI ActEHDS10/11–11/06

歐盟的兩條時鐘：AI Act 對醫材的高風險義務推到 2028，但 EHDS 的三個實施條例已經生效 Two European clocks: the AI Act's high-risk duties for medical devices slip to 2028, while three EHDS implementing regulations are already in force

發生什麼What

先把今年最常被講錯的日期釘住。數位簡化包（Digital Omnibus on AI）已成為 Regulation (EU) 2026/1744，修的是 AI Act 的時程而非結構：歐洲議會 2026 年 6 月 16 日表決、理事會 6 月 29 日通過、7 月 24 日刊登 OJ、7 月 27 日生效。對醫療最關鍵的一行：Annex I（受歐盟產品法規管的產品，醫療器材即在此列）的高風險義務從 2027 年 8 月 2 日推遲到 2028 年 8 月 2 日；Annex III 從 2026 年 8 月 2 日推到 2027 年 12 月 2 日；Article 57 的國家監理沙盒須在 2027 年 8 月 2 日前運作。Article 50 的透明度義務仍自 2026 年 8 月 2 日適用（機器可讀標記有到 2026 年 12 月 2 日的寬限，僅限 2026 年 8 月 2 日前上市者）。另一條時鐘跑得更快：EHDS 的三個實施條例已於 10 月 11–12 日生效——2026/2083（MyHealth@EU 跨境基礎建設，2027 年 3 月 26 日適用）、2026/2099（跨境身分識別與驗證，2027 年 3 月 26 日適用，成員國須於 2028 年 3 月 26 日前通報國家醫療屬性集）、2026/2098（二次利用的最小元資料集，基於 HealthDCAT-AP，2029 年 3 月 26 日適用）。而在 notified body 這一端，11 月 6 日 RAPS 巴塞隆納 AI in Regulatory 會議上，TÜV AI.Lab 的 Kerstin Kaspar 直說「Human oversight is super important」、驗證本身並不消除風險；Edwards Lifesciences 法規總監 Simona Jerabkova 則說「We are not relying fully on the AI」；MedQAIR 的 Leon Doorn 指出 IEC 63450（AI 醫材的驗證與測試）預計明年出版。 First, pin down the date most often misstated this year. The Digital Omnibus on AI is now Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, and it amends the AI Act's timing rather than its structure: the European Parliament voted on June 16, 2026, the Council adopted on June 29, publication in the Official Journal followed on July 24 and entry into force on July 27. The line that matters most for health: high-risk obligations for Annex I products — those covered by EU product law, medical devices among them — move from August 2, 2027 to August 2, 2028. Annex III slips from August 2, 2026 to December 2, 2027, and national regulatory sandboxes under Article 57 must be operational by August 2, 2027. Article 50's transparency obligations still apply from August 2, 2026, with a grace period to December 2, 2026 for machine-readable marking, and only for systems placed on the market before August 2, 2026. The other clock runs faster: three EHDS implementing regulations entered into force on October 11-12 — 2026/2083 (the MyHealth@EU cross-border infrastructure, applying March 26, 2027), 2026/2099 (cross-border identification and authentication, applying March 26, 2027, with member states to notify national healthcare attribute sets by March 26, 2028) and 2026/2098 (minimum metadata for secondary use, built on HealthDCAT-AP, applying March 26, 2029). At the notified-body end, at the RAPS AI in Regulatory conference in Barcelona on November 6, TÜV AI.Lab's Kerstin Kaspar said plainly that "Human oversight is super important" and that validation alone does not eliminate risk; Edwards Lifesciences regulatory director Simona Jerabkova said "We are not relying fully on the AI"; and MedQAIR's Leon Doorn noted that IEC 63450, on verification and testing of AI-enabled medical devices, is expected next year.

為什麼重要Why it matters

歐盟給了醫材業者多一年（到 2028 年 8 月），但那一年不是空白：EHDS 的管線正在同一期間鋪下去，而 notified body 的審查習慣也在這段時間成形。從 11 月 6 日那場會議可以看出一個方向：審查方關心的已經不只是「這個 AI 模型驗證過了嗎」，而是「你的品質系統裡用到的 AI 有沒有被驗證、怎麼持續監測、誰負責錯誤、什麼情況要重新驗證」。Doorn 建議的做法是驗證深度隨流程風險等比例調整——低風險用途可以只做非腳本化測試，用來產生使用說明這類高風險用途就要腳本化測試，依據是 ISO 13485:2016。對打算進入歐盟的台灣業者，實質的提醒是：延後的是義務生效日，不是準備期。要打折的是：本報引用的 AI Act 時程與條號來自第三方彙整頁而非 Official Journal 原文，該頁自己提醒應以 Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 的 OJ 正式文本核對；EHDS 那篇則完全沒有提到 AI，「二次利用」是否涵蓋 AI 訓練不能由該文推論。 Europe has handed device makers an extra year, to August 2028 — but the year is not empty. The EHDS plumbing is being laid in the same period, and notified bodies' review habits are forming now. The November 6 conference shows the direction: reviewers increasingly ask not only "is this AI model validated" but "is the AI you use inside your quality system validated, how is it monitored, who owns its mistakes, and what triggers revalidation". Doorn's proposal is to scale testing depth to process risk — unscripted testing may suffice for low-risk uses, while a high-risk use such as generating instructions for use calls for scripted testing, on the basis of ISO 13485:2016. For Taiwanese manufacturers aiming at the EU, the practical reading is that what was postponed is the compliance date, not the preparation period. Discount this: the AI Act dates and article numbers cited here come from a third-party summary page rather than the Official Journal, and that page itself warns they should be checked against the OJ text of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744; the EHDS article never mentions AI, so whether "secondary use" covers AI training cannot be inferred from it.

兩件事，方向相同。（一）ONC 的 HTI-5 終局規則（10 月 26 日）刪除 14 項、修訂 7 項、維持 39 項健康 IT 認證標準，其中包含移除 HTI-1 對「決策支援介入（DSI）」標準所附的模型卡（model card）揭露要求；資訊封鎖（information blocking）的相關提案未定案，HHS 仍在考慮意見，可能回到預期的 HTI-6 規則制定，該規則預計處理 API 能力與 AI 驅動的互通性。（二）WISeR——CMS 在六州（亞利桑那、紐澤西、俄亥俄、奧克拉荷馬、德州、華盛頓，合計約 640 萬名 Medicare 受益人）對原始 Medicare 施行的 AI 輔助事前審查試辦——10 月 15 日，CMS 副署長 Kimberly Brandt 在新墨西哥州聖塔菲的一場會議上說：「There are a lot of things about WISeR that are not working.」該試辦自 2026 年 1 月（實際上因技術問題延到 1 月 15 日）起跑、預定執行到 2031 年，涵蓋電刺激器、疼痛用硬膜外類固醇注射、頸椎融合、部分膝關節手術與部分州的慢性腿部傷口皮膚替代物，急診服務排除在外。GAO 已於 2026 年 5 月 12 日認定 WISeR 構成《行政程序法》定義下的規則，可觸發《國會審查法》的快速廢止程序。 Two items pointing the same way. (1) ONC's HTI-5 final rule (October 26) removes 14 health IT certification criteria, revises 7 and leaves 39 unchanged — including removal of the model-card disclosure requirement attached to the HTI-1 Decision Support Intervention (DSI) criterion. The information-blocking proposals were not finalised; HHS is still weighing comments and they may return in the anticipated HTI-6 rulemaking, expected to address API capabilities and AI-enabled interoperability. (2) WISeR — CMS's AI-assisted prior authorisation pilot in Original Medicare across six states (Arizona, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Texas and Washington, about 6.4 million Medicare beneficiaries) — drew this from CMS Deputy Administrator Kimberly Brandt at a conference in Santa Fe, New Mexico, on October 15: "There are a lot of things about WISeR that are not working." 為什麼重要Why it matters

這一則是今天的收斂點。美國這一週同時做了三件方向不同的事：給 AI 軟體開支付通道（SaMS）、開始問自主機器人該怎麼評（FDA 討論文件）、以及撤掉讓醫院能看懂手上 AI 的揭露要求（HTI-5）。第三件的後果會落在醫院身上——當模型卡不再是認證要求，本地驗證的責任就從開發者移到採購與使用的那一方。WISeR 則示範了同一條邏輯的下游：在 AI 介入給付決定的場景裡，政府自己當了第一個踩雷的買方。德州的一段資料顯示初次核准率約 62%、醫師覆核後升到 84%——也就是約 38% 的初次否決裡，有超過一半在人工覆核時被翻轉。要打折的是：Brandt 的發言沒有說明哪些部分失效、且經 Nextgov/FCW 轉引，CMS 至今未發布完整公開評估；德州數字為單一州有限期間資料，38% 為本報自 62% 反推，非來源直接給出的否決率。 This item is where today converges. In a single week the US did three things pointing in different directions: opened a payment channel for AI software (SaMS), started asking how autonomous robots should be judged (the FDA discussion paper), and withdrew the disclosure requirement that let hospitals understand the AI they hold (HTI-5). The consequence of the third lands on hospitals: once the model card is no longer a certification requirement, the burden of local validation shifts from the developer to whoever buys and runs the system. WISeR shows the downstream of the same logic — where AI sits inside a payment decision, the government made itself the first buyer to hit the mine. One slice of Texas data showed an initial approval rate of about 62%, rising to 84% after physician review — meaning more than half of that roughly 38% of initial denials were reversed by a human. Discount this: Brandt did not say which parts are failing and the quote reaches us via Nextgov/FCW; CMS has published no full public evaluation; and the Texas figures cover one state over a limited period, with the 38% being our own inference from the 62% rather than a denial rate any source states.

04 — Taiwan Angle

台灣正好站在「指引」與「細則」之間的那個縫，而美國今天示範了縫的另一側長什麼樣 Taiwan stands in the gap between a guideline and a binding rule — and today the US showed what the far side looks like

(1) 台灣的 AI 醫療細則草案，按原訂時程就該在這幾週出現。衛福部 2026 年 5 月 29 日以衛部醫字第 1151663164 號函頒布《醫療機構應用生成式人工智慧指引》，性質是行政指導、不具法律約束力：以生成式 AI 與民眾互動或錄音錄影時應告知用途與限制、供應商契約須揭露所用的大型語言模型、使用須符合個資法目的限制與醫療法病歷規範、功能涉及診斷治療預防者應向食藥署申請屬性判斷，以及最關鍵的一句——「凡涉及臨床判斷、病人安全或醫療紀錄者，仍應由具相應資格之醫事人員負最終確認與責任」。8 月 6 日立法院厚生會成立智慧醫療委員會時，衛福部資訊處長李建璋表示將依《人工智慧基本法》七大原則（透明性、隱私、問責、安全、平權、永續、人類自主）訂定具實質約束力的細則、界定哪些行為構成傷害，預計三個月內提出草案。三個月落在 11 月——也就是現在。本報未查證草案是否已於 11 月公布；三個月是 8 月 6 日的預告，不是已完成的事實。 (1) On the original timetable, Taiwan's draft AI medical rules are due about now. On May 29, 2026 the Ministry of Health and Welfare issued the Guidelines for the Application of Generative Artificial Intelligence by Medical Institutions (letter no. 1151663164) — administrative guidance, not legally binding: patients must be told the purpose and limits when generative AI interacts with them or records audio or video; vendor contracts must disclose the large language model used; use must respect the Personal Data Protection Act's purpose limitation and the Medical Care Act's rules on records; a function touching diagnosis, treatment or prevention needs a classification determination from the FDA; and, crucially, "wherever clinical judgement, patient safety or the medical record is involved, a suitably qualified health professional must bear final confirmation and responsibility". At the August 6 founding of the Legislative Yuan Hou Sheng Association's smart healthcare committee, MOHW information division director Li Chien-chang said binding rules would be drafted on the seven principles of the Artificial Intelligence Basic Act — transparency, privacy, accountability, safety, equity, sustainability and human autonomy — defining which conduct constitutes harm, with a draft within three months. Three months lands in November — now. We have not verified whether the draft appeared in November; the three-month timetable was an August 6 forecast, not a completed fact.

(2) 美國今天最實用的一課，是「給付碼先行」會把責任推到哪裡。台灣的落差很清楚：食藥署已核准相當數量的 AI 醫材，健保卻沒有 AI 軟體的專屬給付類別——這幾乎就是 CMS 在 CY2027 之前的狀態。而 CMS 的解法同時製造了三個台灣遲早要面對的問題：第一，軟體被移出臨床 APC 的理由是「在成本報告裡沒有實體材料成本、商業模式難以被 cost-to-charge ratio 捕捉」，健保點值制度有完全相同的結構困難，AI 軟體的成本無處可放。第二，CMS 讓 FDA 器材身分成為給付入場券（非檢驗類 SaMS 須有上市許可、非器材 CDS 直接出局），等於把屬性判斷變成給付的前置條件，而台灣現在只是「建議業者去申請」。第三，檢驗類演算法移出 CLFS 讓病人開始付部分負擔；台灣若為 AI 開碼，要不要落入部分負擔或自費差額，是同一個必須先回答的問題。本報沒有台灣健保給付 AI 醫材的件數或金額數字，故此處為定性描述。 (2) The most usable lesson today is where a payment code, once created, pushes responsibility. Taiwan's gap is plain: the FDA has authorised a fair number of AI devices, yet National Health Insurance has no dedicated payment category for AI software — almost exactly where CMS stood before CY2027. And CMS's fix created three problems Taiwan will meet sooner or later. First, software was pulled out of clinical APCs because it carries no physical material cost on cost reports and its commercial models resist capture by cost-to-charge ratios — Taiwan's NHI point-value system has the identical difficulty, with nowhere to put the cost of AI software. Second, CMS made FDA device status the ticket to payment (non-laboratory SaMS needs marketing authorisation, non-device CDS is out), turning a TFDA classification determination into a precondition for payment, where Taiwan today merely suggests vendors seek one. Third, moving laboratory algorithms off the CLFS starts charging patients coinsurance; if Taiwan creates codes for AI, whether they attract co-payment or a self-pay differential is the same question that has to be answered first. We have no figures for NHI payment of AI devices by count or value, so this is a qualitative description.

(3) 資料基礎建設這塊，台灣的時程跑在歐盟前面。歐盟的 EHDS 三個實施條例 10 月 11–12 日生效，但 MyHealth@EU 與跨境身分驗證要到 2027 年 3 月 26 日才適用，二次利用元資料框架更要等到 2029 年 3 月 26 日。台灣這邊，衛福部預計 2026 年底讓全部 27 家醫學中心完成次世代電子病歷平台串接（長庚、馬偕、中山醫已試接），區域與地區醫院約 200–300 家 2027 年陸續完成；雙重同意機制預計 2027 年 3 月前公布——第一層同意跨院調閱、第二層同意去識別化資料供公益性 AI 研究。醫學中心覆蓋約八到九成病人、重症又集中於此，重大疾病的病歷互通年底就到位。李建璋對路徑選擇的說明值得記下：全面換次世代病歷成本過高、訂國家標準約需十年才落地，所以政府自建交換平台（FHIR Box）讓醫院介接；他並以美國約八成醫院使用 Epic 對比台灣數十種不同系統，說明互通為何是台灣的瓶頸。此段進度引自 10 月 19 日《聯合報》，屬預計值。 (3) On data infrastructure, Taiwan runs ahead of the EU. The EU's three EHDS implementing regulations entered into force on October 11-12, but MyHealth@EU and cross-border authentication do not apply until March 26, 2027, and the secondary-use metadata framework not until March 26, 2029. In Taiwan, MOHW expects all 27 medical centres connected to its next-generation EMR platform by the end of 2026 (Chang Gung, Mackay and Chung Shan have completed pilots), with some 200 to 300 regional and district hospitals following through 2027; a dual consent mechanism is due by March 2027 — the first consent for cross-hospital access, the second for de-identified data in public-interest AI research. Medical centres serve some 80 to 90 per cent of patients and seriously ill patients concentrate there, so record sharing for major illness is in place by year end. Li Chien-chang's account of the path is worth noting: replacing every hospital's EMR would cost too much and a national standard would take about a decade to land, so the government built its own exchange platform (FHIR Box) for hospitals to connect to; he contrasts the roughly 80 per cent of US hospitals on Epic with Taiwan's dozens of different systems to explain why interoperability is the local bottleneck. This progress comes from an October 19 United Daily News report and states expectations.

(4) 真正的選擇題：細則要抄美國模板還是英國模板。美式路徑把 AI 軟體塞進既有的支付與分類機器，同時鬆開揭露要求（HTI-5），結果是上市與請款門檻下降、本地驗證責任移到醫院。英式路徑重寫監理時間軸，承認風險主要在上市後發生——英國政府 10 月 6 日宣布全盤接受 National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare 的全部 44 項建議，MHRA AI Airlock 第三期改以上市後監測為主題、11 月選定第一批參與者，2026 年 12 月出變更管理指引草案、2027 年就資格與分級辦諮詢、2027 年春季完成完整路線圖，結果是長期維運義務墊高（但須注意：目前尚無任何新規則生效，這是政策承諾與時程表）。台灣的條件其實偏英式：醫學中心集中度高、單一保險人、年底就有全國性病歷互通管道——這些都是「持續監測」才用得上的資產。把 FHIR Box 當成 AI 醫材上市後效能監測的基礎設施、而不只是病歷調閱管道，是此刻值得放進草案討論的事。本點為本報分析判斷，不代表任何官方立場。 (4) The real choice: whether the rules copy the American template or the British one. The American path fits AI software into existing payment and classification machinery while loosening disclosure (HTI-5): entry and billing thresholds fall, and local validation shifts to hospitals. The British path rewrites the regulatory timeline, accepting that risk mostly materialises after launch — on October 6 the UK government accepted in full all 44 recommendations of the National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare, re-themed MHRA's AI Airlock Phase 3 around post-market surveillance with the first cohort selected in November, draft change-management guidance in December 2026, a consultation on qualification and classification in 2027 and a full roadmap by spring 2027, so long-run maintenance obligations rise (note that no new rules are yet in force — this is a policy commitment and a timetable). Taiwan's conditions favour the British shape — high concentration in medical centres, a single payer, a nationwide record-sharing channel by year end. Those are assets that pay off under continuous monitoring, not one-off review. Treating FHIR Box as infrastructure for post-market performance monitoring, not merely a pipe for retrieving records, is worth putting into the draft discussion now. This point is this report's own analysis and represents no official position.
