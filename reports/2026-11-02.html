AI 愈來愈會「看」，但完全不會「節制」 AI is getting better at seeing, and no better at restraint

2026 年 11 月 2 日（一）· 涵蓋 10/19–11/01 · 台北時間 Monday, November 2, 2026 · Covering Oct 19–Nov 1 · Taipei time

這一週落地的臨床證據全部擠在同一條分界線上。Nature Medicine 10/29 刊出新加坡 Duke-NUS 團隊的 EVLF-FM，用 1,326,315 筆樣本、11 種影像模態訓練，內部 12 個資料集診斷平均準確率 0.858、F1 0.797，9 種模態的像素級定位平均 mIOU 達 0.743——它不只給答案，還把答案指到那幾顆像素。同一週，NEJM AI 把 24 套 AI 的處置計畫跟主治醫師逐案配對：AI 平均每次就診開出 314 美元、醫師 97 美元，差 216 美元（95% CI 177–257），約 3.2 倍。而一篇 10/29 見報的肺癌篩檢統合分析把話說到底：合併敏感度 85.4%、特異度 83.5%，但「不支持自主 AI 診斷或由 AI 觸發的侵入性處置」。看得更準、指得更細、開得更貴——三件事同時成立。 Every piece of clinical evidence that landed this week sits on the same line. Nature Medicine published Duke-NUS's EVLF-FM on 29 October: 1,326,315 training samples across 11 imaging modalities, mean diagnostic accuracy 0.858 and F1 0.797 over 12 internal datasets, and mean pixel-level grounding mIOU of 0.743 across nine modalities — it does not just answer, it points at the pixels it answered from. The same week, NEJM AI paired 24 AI systems' plans against the treating physician's, case by case: $314 of care per visit from the AI against the physician's $97, a gap of $216 (95% CI 177–257), about 3.2x. And a lung-screening meta-analysis reported on 29 October put it bluntly: pooled sensitivity 85.4%, specificity 83.5%, and the evidence "does not support autonomous AI diagnosis or AI-triggered invasive management." Sharper sight, finer pointing, bigger bill — all three are true at once.

發生什麼What

Nature Medicine 於 10/29 刊出「A clinical multimodal vision language foundation model with fine-grain explainability」，第一作者 Yang Bai、通訊作者為新加坡 Duke-NUS／新加坡國家眼科中心的 Daniel Shu Wei Ting，共 43 位作者。arXiv 預印本版本揭露的訓練量是 1,326,315 筆樣本（診斷 554,391、視覺定位 415,378、視覺問答 208,230），來自 23 個資料集、11 種影像模態（CT、MRI、X 光、超音波、PET、組織病理、皮膚鏡、彩色眼底、OCT、內視鏡等），涵蓋皮膚科、肝膽科、眼科、病理科、胸腔科、放射科六個專科。內部 12 個資料集的診斷平均準確率 0.858、F1 0.797，勝過 DINO（0.813／0.730）、ViT（0.807／0.712）、InternVL 8B（0.766／0.648）與 LLaVA v1.5 13B（0.759／0.624）。9 種模態的像素級視覺定位平均 mIOU 0.743、Acc@0.5 0.837，其中 X 光 0.934、皮膚鏡 0.866、超音波 0.821。外部驗證用 10 個資料集、8,884 筆獨立樣本、5 種影像模態。程式碼放在 GitHub。 Nature Medicine published "A clinical multimodal vision language foundation model with fine-grain explainability" on 29 October, first-authored by Yang Bai with Daniel Shu Wei Ting of Duke-NUS and the Singapore National Eye Centre as senior author, 43 authors in all. The arXiv preprint puts the training set at 1,326,315 samples — 554,391 diagnostic, 415,378 grounding, 208,230 visual question answering — drawn from 23 datasets across 11 imaging modalities (CT, MRI, X-ray, ultrasound, PET, histopathology, dermatoscopy, colour fundus, OCT, endoscopy among them) and six specialties: dermatology, hepatology, ophthalmology, pathology, pulmonology and radiology. Mean diagnostic accuracy across 12 internal datasets is 0.858 with F1 0.797, ahead of DINO (0.813 / 0.730), ViT (0.807 / 0.712), InternVL 8B (0.766 / 0.648) and LLaVA v1.5 13B (0.759 / 0.624). Pixel-level grounding averages mIOU 0.743 and Acc@0.5 0.837 across nine modalities, reaching 0.934 on X-ray, 0.866 on dermatoscopy and 0.821 on ultrasound. External validation ran on 8,884 independent samples from 10 further datasets in five modalities. The code is on GitHub.

為什麼重要Why it matters

醫療影像 AI 的兩個老毛病是「一個模型只看一種片子」與「說不出為什麼」。EVLF-FM 的做法是疾病級視覺編碼器加上像素級編碼器並行，各自接一個可學習的 connector 進入語言模型的嵌入空間；訓練分兩段，先監督微調（LoRA），再用 GRPO 強化學習，獎勵設計是診斷正確 +1、定位用 IoU、開放式問答用 F1——而 RL 階段只用了約 1% 的監督微調資料量。換句話說，把「指出病灶在哪裡」直接當成訓練訊號，而不是事後再補一張熱力圖。這條路線的意義在於：監管機關與臨床醫師要的從來不只是正確率，而是「你憑哪一塊影像這樣說」。 Medical imaging AI has two chronic weaknesses: one model reads one kind of scan, and it cannot say why. EVLF-FM runs a disease-level vision encoder alongside a pixel-level one, each feeding the language model's embedding space through its own learnable connector. Training comes in two stages — supervised fine-tuning with LoRA, then GRPO reinforcement learning rewarding a correct diagnosis (+1), localisation by IoU and open-ended answers by F1 — and the RL stage used only about 1% of the SFT data volume. The point is that "show me where the lesion is" becomes a training signal rather than a heat map bolted on afterwards. That matters because what regulators and clinicians have always wanted is not accuracy alone but which part of the image the claim rests on.

發生什麼What

NEJM AI 的「Comparative Cost of Care Recommended by Large Language Models versus Treating Physicians」（Aran D、Goldhaber-Fiebert JD、Many I、Nguyen A、Yang D、Shelly S；10/15 線上刊出，收在 2026 年 11 月號 3(11)）拿 200 份去識別化的門診與急診病歷，把病歷在「醫師評估」之前切斷，讓每套 AI 看完全相同的臨床呈現後自己寫 assessment and plan，再與配對的醫師計畫逐案比較；檢查與轉診照 2026 年 Medicare 費率定價，新開立藥物只計數不計價。結果：AI 計畫平均每次就診 314 美元、醫師 97 美元，平均差距 216 美元（95% CI 177–257），約 3.2 倍；其中診斷檢查貢獻 105 美元、專科轉診貢獻 111 美元。新開藥物平均 1.14 項對醫師的 0.59 項。在 133 次「醫師完全沒開任何檢查」的就診中，各套 AI 分別在 45–79% 的案例裡加上了檢查。TechTarget 10/27 的報導補上另一組切面：診斷檢查單項是每次 176 美元對 71 美元，單一系統的平均落在 209 到 599 美元之間，而且近五分之一的 AI 計畫確實抓到了醫師漏掉、但符合指引的檢查或處置。 "Comparative Cost of Care Recommended by Large Language Models versus Treating Physicians" (Aran D, Goldhaber-Fiebert JD, Many I, Nguyen A, Yang D, Shelly S; online 15 October, in the November 2026 issue, 3(11)) took 200 de-identified outpatient and emergency department notes, cut each one before the physician's assessment, let every AI system write its own assessment and plan from the identical presentation, then compared it against the paired physician plan. Tests and referrals were priced at 2026 Medicare schedules; newly prescribed medications were counted but not priced. The AI plans averaged $314 per visit against the physicians' $97 — a mean difference of $216 (95% CI 177–257), about 3.2x — with diagnostic testing contributing $105 and specialist referrals $111. New medications averaged 1.14 per AI plan against 0.59 per physician plan. In the 133 encounters where the physician ordered no diagnostic testing whatsoever, the AI systems added testing in 45–79% of cases. TechTarget's 27 October write-up adds another cut: diagnostic testing alone ran $176 per visit against $71, per-system averages spanned $209 to $599, and close to a fifth of the AI plans did catch guideline-concordant tests or orders the physician had left out.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是目前少見把「AI 建議的經濟後果」當主要終點、而且用真實醫師計畫當對照的研究。它的發現有一個很不舒服的結構：最容易被賣進醫院的那種價值主張（「AI 不會漏掉」）與最難被支付方接受的那種後果（「AI 什麼都開」）其實是同一件事的兩面。更麻煩的是干預的結果——19 套系統在加上「成本意識」提示後成本比確實下降，但其中 17 套與醫師診斷的一致度同時掉下來。也就是說，目前還沒有一個提示詞能同時買到節制與準確。 Studies that make the economic consequence of an AI recommendation the primary endpoint — and use real physician plans as the comparator — are still rare. The finding has an uncomfortable shape: the value proposition easiest to sell into a hospital ("the AI won't miss anything") and the consequence hardest to sell to a payer ("the AI orders everything") are two faces of the same behaviour. The intervention result is worse news still. Cost-conscious prompting did lower the cost ratio in 19 systems, but 17 of those showed reduced diagnostic agreement with the physician. No prompt on offer yet buys restraint and accuracy at the same time.

Counting tests and referrals but not pricing medications understates the drug-cost side of the gap. Watch the inconsistencies in secondary coverage: TechTarget says "20 general-purpose LLMs" and that physicians ordered no tests in 66.5% of cases, while 2minutemedicine says 24 systems (20 general-purpose plus 4 medical-specific) and 133 no-test encounters — 133/200 is exactly 66.5%, so the denominators agree and only the system count differs. Neither names the models tested, so there is no way to tell which generation these figures describe; this report uses the 24-system figure and flags the discrepancy. Finally, the prices are Medicare's, and do not transfer to any other payment system, Taiwan's NHI included.

發生什麼What

以色列 Clalit Health Services 的 Dean Ariel 等人在 10/19 於 Annals of Internal Medicine 發表研究，拿 15 條 Choosing Wisely「別做」建議展開成 225 個情境，對 6 套商用 LLM 以「臨床醫師視角」提示，收集 4,050 則回應。整體而言，模型只在 5.4% 的案例建議了過度檢查，15 條裡有 8 條完全沒有過度建議。破口集中在兩條：「偶發、小於 1 公分的甲狀腺結節做專門超音波」42.8%，「低風險成人的例行心電圖」28.3%，其餘五條落在 0.6% 到 3.3%。反方向的「過度保守」整體只有 0.6%，11 條完全沒有，最高的是 PSA 篩檢的 3.3%。 Dean Ariel and colleagues at Clalit Health Services in Israel published in the Annals of Internal Medicine on 19 October, expanding 15 Choosing Wisely "don't do this" recommendations into 225 scenarios, prompting six commercial LLMs from a clinician's perspective and collecting 4,050 responses. Overall the models recommended unnecessary care in only 5.4% of cases, with 8 of the 15 recommendations drawing no overuse at all. The breaches cluster in two places: dedicated ultrasound for incidental sub-centimetre thyroid nodules at 42.8%, and routine electrocardiography in low-risk adults at 28.3%, with the remaining five between 0.6% and 3.3%. Undertreatment ran the other way at just 0.6% overall, absent for 11 recommendations, highest for PSA screening at 3.3%.

為什麼重要Why it matters

42.8% 那一條不是模型失控，而是模型忠實地把人類的分歧照抄了一遍——這 15 條裡唯一存在學會公開互相矛盾的就是它：美國甲狀腺學會主張對偶發的小結節做專門超音波，美國放射學會則主張依年齡在 1.0–1.5 公分以下不必追查。作者寫得很準：「分開它們、也正是模型面對的，恰恰就是『要不要照這張影像』。」把這一條與前一則 NEJM AI 的 3.2 倍並讀，結論相當清楚：在指引乾淨、立場一致的題目上，LLM 的節制力其實不差；真正讓成本爆開的是沒有共識的灰色地帶，而臨床工作的大半就是灰色地帶。這也呼應了 Mount Sinai 團隊 9 月底發表在 Communications Medicine 的另一組數字——20 套模型、501 種情境變體、超過一千萬則回應，在沒有安全提示時有 16.6% 的回應做出潛在有害的臨床選擇，加一句簡短的安全提醒後降到 10.1%，20 套裡有 19 套改善。提示詞有用，但剩下的 10.1% 不會被提示詞解決。 The 42.8% is not a model going rogue; it is a model faithfully reproducing a human disagreement. Of the 15 recommendations, this is the only one where the societies publicly contradict each other: the American Thyroid Association advises dedicated ultrasound for incidental small nodules, while the American College of Radiology advises no further workup below 1.0–1.5 cm depending on age. The authors put it precisely: "What divides them, and what the models confronted, is precisely whether to image." Read alongside the 3.2x in the previous story, the conclusion is fairly clear. Where guidance is clean and consistent, LLM restraint is actually decent; the cost explodes in the grey zones with no consensus — and grey zones are most of clinical work. It also echoes a separate set of numbers from the Mount Sinai team published in Communications Medicine in late September: 20 models, 501 variations of 50 clinical scenarios, over 10 million responses, with potentially harmful clinical choices at 16.6% of responses without a safety reminder and 10.1% with one, improving in 19 of 20 models. Prompts help. The remaining 10.1% is not a prompting problem.

發生什麼What

一篇刊於 Diagnostic and Prognostic Research 10(1) 第 21 號（DOI 10.1186/s41512-026-00235-w）的系統性回顧與統合分析在 10/29 被報導。檢索 MEDLINE、Embase、Web of Science 自 2010 年 1 月到 2025 年 12 月，篩 2,207 筆紀錄、評讀 56 篇全文，最後納入 11 篇（2019–2025 年發表；8 篇回溯、3 篇前瞻、7 篇多中心），但只有 5 篇的資料足以做量化合併，涵蓋 2,220 人（232 例癌症、1,988 例非癌症），單篇規模從 100 到 1,139 人。結果：合併敏感度 85.4%、特異度 83.5%，各研究敏感度 75.0–95.5%、特異度 64.0–92.7%，陽性似然比 5.17、陰性似然比 0.18、診斷勝算比 29.53；11 篇中有 10 篇報告 AUC，範圍約 0.76 到 0.99。 A systematic review and meta-analysis in Diagnostic and Prognostic Research 10(1), article 21 (DOI 10.1186/s41512-026-00235-w) was reported on 29 October. It searched MEDLINE, Embase and Web of Science from January 2010 to December 2025, screened 2,207 records, assessed 56 full texts and included 11 studies (published 2019–2025; 8 retrospective, 3 prospective, 7 multicentre) — but only 5 had data sufficient for quantitative pooling, covering 2,220 participants (232 cases, 1,988 non-cases), individual studies ranging from 100 to 1,139 people. The results: pooled sensitivity 85.4% and specificity 83.5%, with per-study sensitivity 75.0–95.5% and specificity 64.0–92.7%, a positive likelihood ratio of 5.17, a negative likelihood ratio of 0.18 and a diagnostic odds ratio of 29.53; 10 of the 11 reported AUCs, spanning roughly 0.76 to 0.99.

可解釋的多模態醫療影像基礎模型 · Duke-NUS／新加坡國家眼科中心（新加坡）Explainable multimodal medical imaging foundation model · Duke-NUS / Singapore National Eye Centre (Singapore)

功能與定位。一個模型吃 11 種影像模態，同時做疾病診斷、視覺問答與像素級病灶定位，賣點不是準確率而是「可被稽核的推理」——它會把結論連到影像上的具體區域。權重與程式碼公開在 GitHub，8B 等級，對應的客戶是想自建、不想把影像送出院的醫學中心與國家級計畫，論文見 Nature Medicine 10/29。 Function and position. One model ingests 11 imaging modalities and performs disease diagnosis, visual question answering and pixel-level lesion grounding at once. The pitch is not accuracy but auditable reasoning: it ties each conclusion to a specific region of the image. Weights and code are public on GitHub at the 8B scale, aimed at academic centres and national programmes that want to build in-house and keep images inside the hospital. Paper in Nature Medicine, 29 Oct.

  • 優勢Strength ：像素級定位不是事後熱力圖，而是用 IoU 當強化學習獎勵直接訓出來的，9 種模態平均 mIOU 0.743、X 光達 0.934，明顯高於 InternVL 3 8B 的 0.656。 : grounding is not a post-hoc heat map but trained directly with IoU as a reinforcement learning reward, averaging mIOU 0.743 across nine modalities and 0.934 on X-ray, clearly above InternVL 3 8B's 0.656.
  • 優勢Strength ：開源且規模適中（8B），對主權 AI 與聯邦學習路線是現成的起點——這正是衛福部資訊處 2026/10/20「臨床AI取證驗證中心國際研討會」設定的三大主題之一。 : open weights at a tractable 8B scale make it a ready starting point for sovereign-AI and federated-learning strategies — exactly one of the three themes set for Taiwan MOHW's Clinical AI Validation Center international seminar on 20 October 2026.
  • 疑慮Concern ：外部零樣本表現崩得很嚴重，RetinalOCT 僅 0.481 準確率、US3M 超音波 0.526、ATPOS 眼底 F1 0.325，而且後兩項輸給 MedGemma 27B——「一個模型通吃」在跨院資料上還不成立。 : external zero-shot performance collapses in places — 0.481 accuracy on RetinalOCT, 0.526 on US3M ultrasound, F1 0.325 on the ATPOS fundus set, with the latter two losing to MedGemma 27B. One-model-does-everything does not yet hold on outside data.
  • 疑慮Concern ：零前瞻驗證、零法規路徑，而且 arXiv 版本的表格與正文數字互相矛盾（平均 mIOU 一處 0.743、一處 0.626）；基礎視覺／語言模型未具名，想複製的團隊缺一塊關鍵資訊。 : no prospective validation, no regulatory pathway, and the arXiv version's tables contradict its own narrative (mean mIOU appears as both 0.743 and 0.626); the underlying vision and language backbones are never named, leaving anyone trying to reproduce it short of a key detail.

AMIE (Articulate Medical Intelligence Explorer)

病人端對話式診斷 AI · Google DeepMind／Google Research（美國）Patient-facing conversational diagnostic AI · Google DeepMind / Google Research (US)

功能與定位。病人預約急件門診後，先在家裡用文字跟 AMIE 對話，系統收集症狀與病史、列出待與醫師討論的鑑別診斷，並產生一份給醫師的門診前摘要。底層是 Gemini 2.5 Pro（無領域微調），研究中途為降延遲改為 Gemini 2.5 Flash。它賣的不是診斷結論，而是「把問診時間從診間搬出去」。研究由 Alphabet 資助，10/8 刊於 The Lancet。 Function and position. After booking an urgent primary care appointment, the patient chats with AMIE from home; the system gathers symptoms and history, lists differentials to raise with the doctor, and produces a pre-visit summary for the clinician. It runs on Gemini 2.5 Pro with no domain-specific fine-tuning, switched mid-study to Gemini 2.5 Flash for latency. What it sells is not a diagnosis but the relocation of history-taking out of the exam room. Alphabet-funded, published in The Lancet on 8 October.

  • 優勢Strength ：這是目前唯一走完真實病人前瞻評估的病人端診斷 AI，98 位完成、零次監督醫師強制中止、僅 1 次幻覺，安全性評分 93% 對醫師 95%。 : it is the only patient-facing diagnostic AI to have completed a prospective real-patient evaluation — 98 completions, zero supervisor-forced stops and a single hallucination, with safety rated favourable in 93% of cases against clinicians' 95%.
  • 優勢Strength ：對醫師端有可量測的實際價值：44 位事前看過紀錄的醫師中 75% 覺得有助準備、57% 說可能改變了自己的做法，而且鑑別診斷前 7 名命中正確答案 90%。 : measurable value on the clinician side — of the 44 who previewed the record, 75% said it helped them prepare and 57% that it may have shifted their approach, with the correct answer inside the top 7 differentials 90% of the time.
  • 疑慮Concern ：安全性是在「每一場對話都有專科醫師即時監看」下取得的，評論作者直指這種人力在規模化時無法維持；而排第一的診斷只對 56%，適當性 83% 對醫師 94%、成本效益 85% 對 97%。 : that safety record was obtained with a specialist watching every conversation in real time, which the commentary authors say cannot be sustained at scale; the top-ranked diagnosis is correct only 56% of the time, with appropriateness at 83% against clinicians' 94% and cost-effectiveness 85% against 97%.
  • 疑慮Concern ：n = 98、單臂無對照、單一中心，病人有最多 50 美元報酬，排除已知懷孕與精神科主訴，且 AMIE 看不到病歷——結構上還不足以支撐任何療效主張，作者自己也只宣稱可行性。 : n = 98, single-arm, single-centre, patients paid up to $50, known pregnancy and mental health complaints excluded, and no chart access — structurally insufficient for any effectiveness claim, which the authors themselves do not make.

對比在哪。EVLF-FM 處理的是「看」，AMIE 處理的是「問」，而兩者的失敗模式剛好互補：EVLF-FM 在它沒見過的外部資料上準確率可以掉到 0.481，但它至少能告訴你它在看哪一塊；AMIE 的安全性紀錄漂亮，但它完全沒有可稽核的推理軌跡，而且在「適當性」與「成本效益」這兩個跟支付方直接相關的維度上，離醫師還有 11 到 12 個百分點。把兩者的證據疊起來，今天真正可以買的東西其實只有一種：有明確範圍、有人簽名、且被量過成本的輔助工具。 Where the contrast lies. EVLF-FM handles seeing, AMIE handles asking, and their failure modes are complementary. EVLF-FM's accuracy can fall to 0.481 on external data it has not seen, but it can at least tell you which region it was looking at; AMIE has the handsome safety record but no auditable reasoning trace at all, and on appropriateness and cost-effectiveness — the two dimensions a payer actually cares about — it trails clinicians by 11 to 12 percentage points. Stack the two bodies of evidence and only one kind of product is buyable today: an assistive tool with a defined scope, a human signature on the output, and a measured price tag.

04 — Taiwan Angle

治理的架子已經搭好，本土證據還在路上——而今天最該被搬到台灣尺度的是「成本」那一條 The governance scaffolding is up and the local evidence is still coming — and the figure most worth translating to Taiwanese scale today is the cost one

(1) NEJM AI 的 3.2 倍在總額支付下會變成另一種東西。那篇研究是用 2026 年 Medicare 費率算的，而 Medicare 是按服務計酬——AI 多開的檢查就是多出來的支出。台灣健保是總額支付：AI 多開的檢查不會讓總額變大，只會在同一個池子裡排擠別人。換句話說，同一個行為在美國顯示為「醫療費用上升」，在台灣會顯示為「點值下降」與「排擠其他科別」，而後者在帳面上看不見，也因此更難被當成問題處理。衛福部於 2025/11/20 啟動的台灣AI影響性研究中心正是為了補這一塊：以台北榮總、台中榮總、三軍總醫院、成大醫院、台大醫院五家示範醫院執行本土 RCT 與醫療科技評估，資訊處處長李建璋當時說明過去的 RCT 都依賴國外數據，「若成效好，將研議是否可納入健保」，結果預計約三年後送健保共擬會。NEJM AI 這類把成本當主要終點的設計，正好是這五家醫院該照抄的題型。 (1) The NEJM AI 3.2x becomes a different thing under a global budget. That study priced care at 2026 Medicare rates, and Medicare pays fee-for-service — an extra AI-ordered test is extra spending. Taiwan's NHI runs a global budget: extra tests do not enlarge the pot, they crowd others out of it. The same behaviour that appears in the US as rising expenditure shows up in Taiwan as a falling point value and as displacement of other specialties, and the latter does not appear on any ledger, which makes it harder to treat as a problem. The Taiwan AI Impact Research Center, launched by the MOHW on 20 November 2025, exists to fill exactly this gap: local RCTs and health technology assessment across five demonstration sites — Taipei Veterans General, Taichung Veterans General, Tri-Service General, National Cheng Kung University Hospital and National Taiwan University Hospital. Information Division director Li Chien-chang noted then that past RCTs had relied on overseas data and that "if the results are good, the ministry will consider whether they can be included in NHI", with findings expected to reach the NHI Joint Decision-Making Committee in about three years. A study design that makes cost the primary endpoint is precisely the template those five hospitals should copy.

(2) EVLF-FM 證明「主權級可解釋模型」在亞洲做得出來。新加坡一個醫學院加一間眼科中心就交出了 11 種模態、1,326,315 筆樣本的開源多模態基礎模型，而且刊在 Nature Medicine——這不是需要前沿實驗室規模才能做的事。衛福部資訊處 2026/10/20 的「臨床AI取證驗證中心國際研討會」把主權 AI、聯邦學習、基礎模型設為三大主題，講者包含雪梨大學 Adam Dunn（從聯邦學習到臨床實證的跨院驗證）、麻州總醫院 Long Phi Le（支援醫療主權的多模態醫療基礎模型）與耶魯 Joyce Oen-Hsiao（從驗證到醫師採用）。議程設定是對的，但台灣目前缺的不是議程而是一個可以被引用的模型——EVLF-FM 的 8B 規模與開源授權，恰好是跨院聯邦學習可以直接起步的尺寸。 (2) EVLF-FM proves a sovereign-grade explainable model is buildable in Asia. One medical school and one eye centre in Singapore produced an open-source multimodal foundation model spanning 11 modalities and 1,326,315 samples, and placed it in Nature Medicine — this is not frontier-lab-scale work. The MOHW Information Division's Clinical AI Validation Center international seminar on 20 October 2026 set sovereign AI, federated learning and foundation models as its three themes, with speakers including Adam Dunn of the University of Sydney (federated learning to clinical evidence across health systems), Long Phi Le of Massachusetts General Hospital (multimodal medical foundation models supporting medical sovereignty) and Joyce Oen-Hsiao of Yale (from validation to physician adoption). The agenda is right; what Taiwan lacks is not an agenda but a citable model of its own — and EVLF-FM's 8B scale and open licence are exactly the size at which cross-hospital federated learning can begin.

(3) 治理制度已經鋪到比證據更前面。衛福部負責任AI執行中心 2024/9/28 啟動，到 2025/4/21 成果發表時已補助 10 家醫院（林口長庚、台北榮總、高醫大附設中和醫院、中國醫藥大學附設醫院、彰化基督教醫院、奇美、聯新國際、嘉義長庚、土城長庚、新北仁康），架構是九大透明性原則、資安與隱私保護、AI 生命週期管理三根柱子，並規劃「負責任AI標章」作為產品審查與信任機制的核心，同時要與食藥署建立智慧醫材的上市後監測。資訊處處長李建璋稱台灣是繼美國與歐盟之後、亞洲第一個把負責任 AI 落實到臨床場域的國家。制度先行本身是對的選擇，但本期七篇研究裡沒有一篇是台灣做的——治理架子搭在前面，裡面要放的本土證據還沒長出來，這個時間差就是未來三年最該盯的風險。 (3) The governance framework has run ahead of the evidence. The MOHW's Responsible AI Execution Center launched on 28 September 2024, and by its results presentation on 21 April 2025 had funded 10 hospitals (Linkou Chang Gung, Taipei Veterans General, Kaohsiung Medical University's Chung-Ho Memorial, China Medical University Hospital, Changhua Christian, Chi Mei, Lien Hsin International, Chiayi Chang Gung, Tucheng Chang Gung and New Taipei's Jen-Kang), structured on three pillars — nine transparency principles, information security and privacy protection, and AI lifecycle management — with a Responsible AI certification mark planned as the core of product review and trust-building, alongside post-market surveillance for smart medical devices to be built with the Food and Drug Administration. Information Division director Li Chien-chang describes Taiwan as the first country in Asia, after the US and EU, to put responsible AI into clinical practice. Leading with institutions is the right call, but not one of the seven studies in this edition came from Taiwan. The scaffolding is up and the local evidence meant to fill it has not grown yet; that lag is the risk worth watching over the next three years.

(4) 影像端的本土題目已經有了，缺的是終點設計。2026/10/5 的「國產 AI 3D 醫療影像暨元宇宙應用大會」由衛福部資訊處主辦，台大醫院胸腔外科江旴恒與一般外科郭庭均分享 2D 影像轉 3D 在肋骨骨折判讀、手術規劃與病人溝通上的應用，李建璋說「現在關鍵是讓 2D 醫療影像轉 3D 技術普惠全國」，並指未來最重要的工作是把標準與平台（含 FHIR 這類國際標準）建起來。把本期 Mayo REDMOD 的教訓搬過來：那篇最被質疑的不是敏感度，而是「沒公布偽陽性率」。台灣在推 2D 轉 3D 普惠全國時，如果只量「多找到幾個骨折」而不同時量「多做了幾次後續檢查、多花了多少點值」，三年後送到健保共擬會的資料就會缺掉支付方唯一在意的那一欄。 (4) Taiwan already has its imaging question; what it lacks is endpoint design. The Domestic AI 3D Medical Imaging and Metaverse Application Conference on 5 October 2026, hosted by the MOHW Information Division, featured National Taiwan University Hospital's Chiang Hsu-heng (thoracic surgery) and Kuo Ting-chun (general surgery) on converting 2D images to 3D for rib-fracture reading, surgical planning and patient communication, with Li Chien-chang saying the key now is making 2D-to-3D conversion available nationwide, and naming standards and platforms — FHIR among them — as the ministry's most important future task. Carry over the lesson from Mayo's REDMOD in this edition: what drew the sharpest questions was not its sensitivity but the absence of a published false-positive rate. If Taiwan's nationwide 2D-to-3D push measures only how many extra fractures were found, and not how many additional downstream tests were ordered and how many points they consumed, then the dossier that reaches the NHI committee in three years will be missing the only column a payer reads.

挑選標準：直接碰到「臨床 AI 的證據該長什麼樣」這個問題，而不是再報一次成績單 Chosen for one thing: each takes on what clinical AI evidence ought to look like, rather than reporting another scorecard
  1. Comparative Cost of Care Recommended by Large Language Models versus Treating Physicians — NEJM AI (2026-10-15)

    本期最該讀原文的一篇。它示範了一個別人幾乎都跳過的研究設計：不比診斷正確率，而是比「照這個計畫走要花多少錢」，而且對照組是真的那位主治醫師。The one paper here most worth reading in the original. It demonstrates a design nearly everyone else skips: not whose diagnosis is right, but what following the plan costs — against the actual treating physician as comparator.

  2. Prospective evidence for conversational medical AI is hard, but non-negotiable — Nature Medicine (2026)

    配著 AMIE 那篇讀。它處理的正是本期反覆出現的那個結構問題：可行性研究能證明的事情非常有限，而從可行性跳到療效之間少掉的那幾步，目前整個領域都還沒補上。Read it alongside the AMIE paper. It addresses the structural problem that recurs throughout this edition: what a feasibility study can establish is narrowly bounded, and the field has not yet filled in the steps between feasibility and effectiveness.

  3. General-purpose large language models outperform specialized clinical AI tools on medical benchmarks — Nature Medicine (2026)

    把「要不要買專用醫療模型」這個問題問到底。與同作者群用醫師真實問題做的後續研究一起看，對照本期 EVLF-FM 的外部驗證在兩個資料集上輸給 MedGemma 27B，會更有意思。It presses the question of whether a specialised medical model is worth buying at all. Pair it with the same group's follow-up using physicians' real-world questions, and read both against EVLF-FM losing to MedGemma 27B on two external datasets in this edition.

  4. AI-induced never-skilling in medical education — Nature Medicine (2026)

    本期有三篇研究的結論都是「AI 當助手可以、當結論不行」，但沒有一篇問：如果住院醫師從第一天就有這個助手，他會不會根本不長出那個被輔助的能力。這篇問了。Three papers in this edition conclude that AI works as an assistant and not as a conclusion, but none asks what happens if a resident has that assistant from day one and never develops the skill being assisted. This one asks.

  5. Evaluating Large Language Model Responses to Unsafe Clinical Instructions — Communications Medicine / Mount Sinai (2026-09-26)

    規模大到值得單獨記住：20 套模型、501 種情境變體、逾千萬則回應。真正的價值在那個「加提示詞從 16.6% 降到 10.1%」的結構——它同時證明提示詞有效，也證明提示詞有上限。Large enough to remember on its own: 20 models, 501 scenario variants, over 10 million responses. The value is in the shape of the result — a safety reminder cutting harmful choices from 16.6% to 10.1% proves both that prompting works and that prompting has a ceiling.
