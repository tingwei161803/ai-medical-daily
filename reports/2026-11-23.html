開幕前一週，廠商量的是周轉時間，大會量的是病人結果 A week before the meeting, vendors measure turnaround time and the society measures patient outcomes

2026 年 11 月 23 日（一）· 涵蓋 11/16–11/23 · 台北時間 Monday, November 23, 2026 · Covering Nov 16–23 · Taipei time

RSNA 2026 在 11/29 於芝加哥開幕，開幕前一週是醫療影像 AI 一年當中新聞稿最密的七天。今年這七天有一個很乾淨的對照組：廠商發出來的數字幾乎都是流程端點——周轉時間、敏感度、描述被接受的比例；而 RSNA 自己排進議程 的題目幾乎都是結果端點——病人結果、假警報、認知負荷、診斷風險往哪裡移。兩邊量的不是同一件事，而今天值得記下來的是這個落差本身。本期每一個數字都就地附上出處；凡是廠商自述、會議摘要尚未同儕審查、或我讀不到全文的，都在該則的「要打折的地方」寫明。 RSNA 2026 opens in Chicago on Nov 29, and the week before it is the densest seven days of the year for medical-imaging AI press releases. This year those seven days came with an unusually clean control group: almost every number the vendors put out is a process endpoint — turnaround time, sensitivity, the share of AI descriptors a radiologist leaves alone — while almost every topic the society put on its own podium is an outcome endpoint: patient outcomes, false alarms, cognitive load, where diagnostic risk moves to. The two sides are not measuring the same thing, and that gap is what is worth recording today. Every figure below carries its source inline; anything vendor-reported, anything from a conference abstract not yet peer-reviewed, and anything whose full text I could not read is flagged in that item's "Discount this" row.

51 萬張肌肉骨骼 X 光：骨折報告從兩天縮到八小時，降幅 83.8% 510,000 musculoskeletal X-rays: fracture reports go from two days to eight hours, down 83.8%

發生什麼What

SimonMed 的 CMO 兼創新長 Sean Raj 將在 RSNA 2026 報告兩篇研究，11/16 發稿、11/19 刊出。第一篇評估 AI 工作清單重新排序對報告周轉時間的影響：分析全美超過 200 個中心、51 萬張以上肌肉骨骼 X 光，涵蓋 2022 年導入前到 2025 年全面整合，骨折陽性檢查的平均周轉時間下降 83.8%、從約兩天降到八小時以內，骨折陰性檢查的周轉也改善而未拖慢常規流程（12/2 口頭報告）。第二篇評估一個視覺語言模型自動產生門診胸部 X 光報告：以 500 多萬組影像—報告配對預訓練，再用 15 萬多筆 SimonMed 成人門診檢查做機構專屬微調，於 143 個門診中心的連續世代測試，微調後敏感度上升、特異度小幅下降，其中肺結節敏感度從 62.6% 升到 80.8%，是所有發現中增幅最大的一項（12/2 壁報）。 Sean Raj, SimonMed's chief medical and innovation officer, will present two studies at RSNA 2026, datelined Nov 16 and published Nov 19. The first evaluates AI worklist reprioritisation against report turnaround time across more than 200 US centres and over 510,000 musculoskeletal radiographs, spanning the period before the system's 2022 introduction through full integration in 2025: mean turnaround for fracture-positive exams fell 83.8%, from about two days to under eight hours, while fracture-negative studies also improved without slowing routine workflow (podium, Dec 2). The second evaluates a vision-language model generating outpatient chest X-ray reports: pre-trained on more than 5 million image-report pairs, then fine-tuned on more than 150,000 SimonMed adult outpatient exams and tested on a consecutive cohort from 143 outpatient centres. Fine-tuning raised sensitivity with a modest loss of specificity, and pulmonary nodule sensitivity rose from 62.6% to 80.8%, the largest gain of any finding (poster, Dec 2).

為什麼重要Why it matters

周轉時間是 AI 最可靠能推動的端點，因為它量的是排隊順序而不是判讀本身——把骨折片插到隊伍前面，不需要模型比人準。而肺結節那 18 個百分點值得注意的不是絕對值，是它來自「機構專屬微調」：同一個通用模型在換到自家資料前後差這麼多，等於替「通用基礎模型可以開箱即用」這個說法標了一個價。 Turnaround time is the endpoint AI most reliably moves, because it measures queue order rather than interpretation — putting fracture films at the front of the list does not require the model to beat a human. What is notable about the 18-point nodule gain is not its size but its source: institution-specific fine-tuning. A generic model differing that much before and after it meets local data puts a price on the claim that general-purpose foundation models work out of the box.

要打折的地方Discount this

兩篇都是會議摘要、尚未同儕審查，數字來自公司新聞稿。周轉時間那篇是回溯設計、跨 2022–2025 三年，這段期間機構的人力、排班與 PACS 都在變，83.8% 不能全部歸給 AI。第二篇的特異度「小幅下降」究竟降多少，新聞稿沒寫；模型本身被標為 investigational。 Both are conference abstracts, not peer-reviewed, and the figures come from a company release. The turnaround study is retrospective and spans 2022–2025, years in which staffing, scheduling and the PACS itself all changed, so the 83.8% cannot all be attributed to AI. How far specificity fell in the second study is not stated, and the model is labelled investigational.

發生什麼What

RadNet 旗下 DeepHealth 11/19 公布 五篇口頭報告與兩篇壁報，涵蓋乳房、甲狀腺、肺與攝護腺。最硬的一個數字是甲狀腺：在美國九萬多件真實世界檢查的前瞻部署中，放射科醫師對 88.7% 的結節保留了全部五項 AI 產生的描述、未作修改（11/29 報告）。乳房緻密組織那篇建立在已發表於 Nature Health 的 ASSURE 研究之上（579,583 件檢查、2,762 例癌症），12/1 報告稱緻密乳房族群的整體癌、侵襲癌與侵襲性較高的癌偵測率都提升。新聞稿另引兩篇已發表論文：European Radiology（Guenzel 等）稱結合放射科醫師判讀與 AI 風險分級，可在維持顯著癌 98.4% 敏感度下避免近五分之一的攝護腺切片；Radiology（Paramasamy 等）在 Erasmus MC 的 39,323 件胸部 CT 中，肺部 AI 與校正後報告時間中位數減少 14.6% 相關，而該肺部 AI 在美國未上市。 DeepHealth, a RadNet subsidiary, announced on Nov 19 five podium presentations and two posters across breast, thyroid, lung and prostate imaging. The hardest number is the thyroid one: in a prospective real-world deployment covering more than 90,000 US exams, radiologists left all five AI-generated descriptors unmodified in 88.7% of nodules (Nov 29). The dense-breast work builds on the ASSURE study already published in Nature Health (579,583 exams, 2,762 cancers); the Dec 1 talk reports higher overall, invasive and aggressive cancer detection in women with dense breasts. The release also cites two published papers: in European Radiology (Guenzel et al.), combining radiologist assessment with AI risk classification could have avoided nearly one prostate biopsy in five while holding 98.4% sensitivity for clinically significant cancer; in Radiology (Paramasamy et al.), across 39,323 chest CTs at Erasmus Medical Center the lung AI was associated with a 14.6% reduction in adjusted median reporting time — and that lung AI is listed as not available in the US.

為什麼重要Why it matters

88.7% 這個指標本身很有意思，因為它量的不是對錯，是「醫師懶得改」。在結構化報告的世界裡，高接受率既可以讀成 AI 寫得好，也可以讀成自動化偏誤的前兆——同一個數字在廠商簡報裡是賣點，在 RSNA 的認知負荷場次裡是風險指標。而 DeepHealth 是這週唯一把會議摘要掛在已發表同儕審查論文上的廠商，這個做法本身就值得其他家學。 The 88.7% figure is interesting precisely because it does not measure correctness; it measures how often a physician did not bother to edit. In a structured-reporting world a high acceptance rate reads either as the AI writing well or as the leading edge of automation bias — the same number is a selling point in a vendor deck and a risk indicator in RSNA's cognitive-load sessions. DeepHealth is also the only vendor this week to hang its conference abstracts on already peer-reviewed publications, which is a habit the rest of the field could copy.

要打折的地方Discount this

這是促銷新聞稿。七篇 RSNA 發表當中，只有甲狀腺那篇在稿裡給了數字；緻密乳房「偵測率提升」沒有任何率值，攝護腺多讀者研究、影像風險模型、乳房動脈鈣化、納入舊片降低假陽性四篇一個數字都沒有。98.4% 與 14.6% 來自已發表論文但我未取得全文，僅憑新聞稿轉述。 This is a promotional release. Of the seven RSNA presentations, only the thyroid one carries a number in the release; the dense-breast item gives no detection rate at all, and the prostate multi-reader study, the image-only risk model, the breast arterial calcification poster and the priors-for-false-positives poster give none. The 98.4% and 14.6% come from published papers whose full texts I did not obtain, and are relayed here from the release only.

發生什麼What

HeartLung.AI 11/16 公布 九篇 RSNA 摘要，全部圍繞「用別的理由做的影像順便讀心血管風險」。最強的一筆在 MESA 與 Framingham Heart Study：傳統 Agatston 分數為零的人當中，21.7% 仍被 Agatston-2.0 驗出可偵測的鈣化，而這群人的二十年冠心病發生率是 7.7%、未驗出者 3.8%（12/1 報告）。胸部 X 光風險模型 AI-CX-CVD 在 MIMIC-CXR 的外部驗證中，對複合心血管事件 AUC 0.751、對心血管死亡 AUC 0.790（12/2）。Sybil 肺癌風險模型用冠狀動脈鈣化 CT 追蹤十五年，一年 AUC 0.83、十五年降到 0.70（11/30）。Miami Heart Study 則顯示低鈣化族群中，心包脂肪最高四分位的非鈣化斑塊體積中位數 34.5 mm³、最低四分位 11.9 mm³（12/1）。另一篇比較 Sybil 與流行病學模型 PLCOm2012，Sybil 在西班牙世代的區辨力顯著較好，美國世代差異未達顯著。 HeartLung.AI announced nine RSNA abstracts on Nov 16, all built on reading cardiovascular risk off scans acquired for some other reason. The strongest sits in MESA and the Framingham Heart Study: among people whose conventional Agatston score was zero, 21.7% still had calcium detectable by Agatston-2.0, and their 20-year coronary heart disease incidence was 7.7% against 3.8% for those without (Dec 1). On external validation in MIMIC-CXR, the chest-radiograph risk model AI-CX-CVD reached AUC 0.751 for incident composite cardiovascular disease and AUC 0.790 for cardiovascular mortality (Dec 2). The Sybil lung cancer risk model, run on coronary artery calcium CT over fifteen years, scored AUC 0.83 at one year, falling to 0.70 at fifteen (Nov 30). In the Miami Heart Study, among low-CAC participants median noncalcified plaque volume was 34.5 mm³ in the highest epicardial fat quartile against 11.9 mm³ in the lowest (Dec 1). A further abstract compares Sybil with the epidemiologic PLCOm2012 model: Sybil discriminated significantly better in the Spanish cohort, while the US difference did not reach significance.

為什麼重要Why it matters

零分那一組是目前被明確告知「低風險」的人，而 21.7% 加上兩倍的二十年發生率，意味著一個現行分級把相當比例的人分錯邊。這類「機會式篩檢」不需要新的檢查、不需要新的輻射劑量，只需要重讀既有影像——這是醫療 AI 少見的、成本結構對自己有利的場景。Sybil 的 AUC 從 0.83 掉到 0.70 也是個誠實的數字：影像模型的預測力有時效，而大多數廠商不會把十五年那一欄印出來。 The zero-score group is the population currently told it is low risk, and 21.7% plus a doubled 20-year incidence implies that current stratification puts a sizeable share of people on the wrong side of the line. This kind of opportunistic screening needs no new exam and no new radiation dose, only a re-read of images already acquired — one of the rare settings where the cost structure favours medical AI. The Sybil AUC falling from 0.83 to 0.70 is an honest number too: an imaging model's predictive power has a shelf life, and most vendors do not print the fifteen-year column.

要打折的地方Discount this

全部是觀察性世代研究加會議摘要，沒有任何介入——沒有人證明依這些分級改變處置會改善結果。數字由公司自己整理公布，九篇中有四篇在公布頁上完全沒有數值。Sybil 在美國世代的差異未達顯著這點，公司在同一頁也寫了，但「分類更多人為高風險」在沒有結果數據時既可能是更敏感、也可能只是更吵。Agatston-2.0 與 AI-CVD 在這些世代中的表現無第三方稽核。 All of it is observational cohort work presented as conference abstracts, with no intervention anywhere — nobody has shown that acting on these classifications improves outcomes. The figures were compiled and released by the company, and four of the nine abstracts carry no numbers at all on that page. The company does state that the US Sybil difference was not significant, but "classifies more people as high risk" without outcome data could mean more sensitive or merely noisier. Agatston-2.0 and AI-CVD performance in these cohorts is not independently audited.

發生什麼What

Lunit（KOSDAQ: 328130）11/17 宣布 在 RSNA 2026 發表 17 篇研究，比去年的 14 篇增加，其中 11 篇獲選口頭報告。15 篇聚焦乳癌，涵蓋偵測、風險預測、乳房緻密度分析與影像品管。最值得注意的是一篇大規模比較四個已獲 FDA 許可的 AI 產品在乳癌偵測上的表現；另有一篇評估 AI 預測五年乳癌風險的準確度，一篇檢視 AI 輔助判讀的效益如何隨醫師資歷不同。兩篇進入學會推薦的乳房影像場次：一篇是 AI 獨立判讀低度懷疑的乳房攝影以標出需進一步檢查者，一篇是為緻密乳房女性指引後續檢查。兩篇胸部 X 光研究分別處理自動報告產生，以及 AI 獨立判讀「很可能正常」影像的安全性。執行長徐範錫表示會在現場積極行銷，讓研究成果轉為業務成果。 Lunit (KOSDAQ: 328130) said on Nov 17 it will present 17 studies at RSNA 2026, up from 14 last year, with 11 selected for oral presentation. Fifteen concern breast cancer, spanning detection, risk prediction, density analysis and imaging quality control. The most notable is a large-scale comparison of four FDA-cleared AI products on breast cancer detection; another assesses how accurately AI predicts five-year breast cancer risk, and another examines how the benefit of AI-assisted reading varies with physician experience. Two appear in a society-recommended breast imaging session: one on AI independently reading mammograms with low suspicion of cancer to flag patients needing further testing, one on guiding follow-up exams for women with dense breast tissue. Two chest X-ray studies cover automated report generation and the safety of AI independently reading images likely to be normal. CEO Suh Beom-seok said the company would market actively on site so that the research leads to business results.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這個領域最缺的就是同一批影像上跑不同廠商模型的比較，因為各家用自己的資料集報自己的數字，彼此的敏感度不可相互比較。四個 FDA 許可產品的頭對頭如果方法站得住，會是今年最有用的一篇。另一個軸是「AI 獨立判讀」——讓 AI 自己清掉很可能正常的片子，是降低放射科人力瓶頸最直接的做法，也是監管上最硬的一關；Lunit 同時在乳房攝影與胸部 X 光兩邊推這件事。 What the field most lacks is any comparison of different vendors' models on the same images, because each reports its own numbers on its own dataset, which makes their sensitivities mutually incomparable. A head-to-head of four FDA-cleared products, if the methodology holds, would be the most useful paper of the year. The other axis is autonomous reading — letting AI clear the probably-normal studies by itself is the most direct answer to the radiologist bottleneck and the hardest regulatory ask; Lunit is pushing it on both mammography and chest radiography at once.

The announcement contains no figures at all — no sample size, no sensitivity, not even which four products — and names no collaborating institution. AuntMinnie 的 RSNA 2026 AI 議程預覽 列出的二十場裡，有一整組是負面或限定性結果：綜合型中風中心導入 AI 沒有量到病人結果改善（12/2, W3-SSNR10-3）；腦 MRI 微出血 AI 標出的病灶遠多於放射科醫師、假警報也更多（12/2, W3-SSNR10-6）；廠商自述指標不保證臨床表現（12/1, T7-SSNPM02-4）；AI 只是把診斷風險移轉而非消除（12/2, W3-SSIN06-3）；AI 不一定降低放射科醫師的認知負荷，有時還增加（11/30, M3-SSIN02-3）；還有一場談放射科醫師如何使用與記錄 AI 可能影響陪審團、進而影響醫療糾紛（11/30, M3-SSIN02-8）。同一份預覽裡的正面題目同樣具體：一套開源系統可在臨床診斷前將近一年於常規 CT 上偵測胰臟癌（11/30, M3-SSGI03-3）；胸部 X 光深度學習預測心血管風險約與 AHA PREVENT 分數相當（11/30, M6-SSCA03-1）；以及11 套分流演算法在三家醫院實際推行後結果不一（12/3, R3-SSIN07-1）。 Of the twenty sessions listed in AuntMinnie's RSNA 2026 AI programme preview, a whole cluster reports negative or qualified findings: AI at a comprehensive stroke centre did not measurably improve patient outcomes (Dec 2, W3-SSNR10-3); microbleed AI on brain MRI flagged far more lesions than radiologists and far more false alarms (Dec 2, W3-SSNR10-6); vendor-reported metrics do not guarantee clinical performance (Dec 1, T7-SSNPM02-4); AI shifts diagnostic risk rather than eliminating it (Dec 2, W3-SSIN06-3); AI does not always reduce radiologists' mental workload and sometimes increases it (Nov 30, M3-SSIN02-3); and one talk asks how radiologists' use and documentation of AI could sway jurors, and with them malpractice outcomes (Nov 30, M3-SSIN02-8). The positive items in the same preview are equally specific: an open-source system detecting pancreatic cancer on routine CT nearly a year before clinical diagnosis (Nov 30, M3-SSGI03-3); deep learning on chest radiographs predicting cardiovascular risk about as accurately as the AHA PREVENT score (Nov 30, M6-SSCA03-1); and eleven triage algorithms rolled out across three hospitals with uneven real-world results (Dec 3, R3-SSIN07-1).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這不是評論者的抱怨，是學會自己的科學委員會把這些題目選上講台。放在一起看，本週廠商的新聞稿與大會的議程構成一組完整的對照：前者量周轉時間、敏感度、描述接受率，後者量病人結果、假警報率、認知負荷與法律責任。醫療 AI 的採購決策幾乎都是用前者那組數字做的，而真正決定能不能留下來的是後者。微出血那一場尤其值得一聽——「抓得更多」在摘要標題裡是優點，在臨床工作流裡可能是每天多出來的幾十個需要排除的病灶。 This is not critics complaining; it is the society's own scientific committee choosing these topics for the podium.   • 疑慮Concern ：分流裝置的真正風險是假警報帶來的疲乏，而這正是 RSNA 12/3 那場「11 套分流演算法在三家醫院結果不一」要談的題目；同時部署多套分流 AI 的醫院已經在量這個代價。 : the real risk in a triage device is alarm fatigue, which is precisely the subject of RSNA's Dec 3 session on eleven triage algorithms producing uneven results across three hospitals — departments running several triage models at once are already measuring that cost.

04 — Taiwan Angle

台灣的制度設計剛好押在今天這個落差上：不是再多幾篇準確度論文，而是隨機分派的結果試驗 Taiwan's institutional design is bet on exactly today's gap: not more accuracy papers, but randomised outcome trials

(1) 衛福部的三大 AI 中心，第三個中心做的就是 RSNA 今年在講的事。衛福部補助設立的三大中心共 19 處：負責任 AI 執行中心 10 處（治理、透明揭露、可解釋性、生命週期監控）、臨床 AI 取證驗證中心 4 處（以 FHIR 跨院驗證資料庫與聯邦學習平台加速取證）、AI 影響性研究中心 5 處——而第三類的任務白紙黑字寫著「以隨機對照試驗衡量 AI 相對於傳統照護的真實臨床效益，作為未來定價與給付決策的科學依據」。對照今天本報的主線：廠商這一週交出的全是周轉時間、敏感度、描述接受率，而台灣的制度把給付資格綁在隨機分派的結果數據上。這不是跟風，是把全球醫療 AI 現在最缺的那一格直接寫成預算項目。 (1) The third of the MOHW's three AI centres does exactly what RSNA is talking about this year. The ministry subsidises 19 centres across three types: 10 Responsible AI Execution Centres (governance, transparency disclosure, explainability, lifecycle monitoring), 4 Clinical AI Validation Centres (a FHIR-based cross-hospital validation database and a federated learning platform to speed up approval), and 5 Clinical AI Impact Evaluation Centres — whose stated mission is, in so many words, to use randomised controlled trials to measure the real-world clinical benefit of AI against conventional care, as the scientific basis for future pricing and reimbursement decisions. Set that against today's through-line: everything the vendors filed this week is turnaround time, sensitivity and descriptor acceptance, while Taiwan's institutions tie reimbursement eligibility to randomised outcome data. That is not following a trend; it writes the field's emptiest square directly into a budget line.

(2) 三個問題的框架出自衛福部資訊處自己，而它點名的失敗案例是台灣公司。資訊處長李建璋在衛福部部落格把醫療 AI 的障礙拆成落地、取證、給付三題：落地對應負責任 AI 中心，要求廠商公布九項透明性指標並提供可解釋性分析，且因資料漂移必須每三到六個月定期重新檢核；取證的門檻是上萬筆等級的驗證資料集，單一醫院湊不出來，所以要跨層級醫院聯盟共用；給付則因為多數 AI 工具無法自費、全額納保又不現實，必須靠對照試驗證明「AI 加醫師優於醫師」。同一篇點名台灣首家取得美國 FDA 許可的 Deep01 在四到五年後停止營運，作為本地生態尚未成熟的證據。這段文字值得台灣的醫療 AI 團隊逐句讀——它是主管機關對自己市場的診斷書。 (2) The three-problem framing comes from the ministry's own information office, and the failure it names is a Taiwanese company. Information Department director Lee Chien-chang, writing on the MOHW blog, splits the obstacles into implementation, validation and reimbursement: implementation maps to the Responsible AI centres, where vendors must publish nine transparency indicators and supply explainability analysis, with mandatory re-checks every three to six months because clinical data drifts; validation faces a bar of tens of thousands of cases, which no single hospital can assemble, hence the cross-tier hospital alliance pooling data; and reimbursement, since most AI tools cannot be paid for out of pocket while full insurance coverage is unrealistic, has to rest on controlled trials showing that AI plus a clinician beats the clinician alone. The same post names Deep01 — the first Taiwanese company to win US FDA clearance, which ceased operations after four to five years — as evidence that the local ecosystem is not yet mature. It is worth reading line by line, because it is the regulator's own diagnosis of its own market.

(3) 資料標準先行這一步，已經換到國際合作的議價能力。衛福部與羅氏醫療診斷 2026 年 8 月 21 日簽署 MOU，首波聚焦慢性腎臟病，用常規檢驗資料做風險分層與腎功能惡化預警。值得注意的是羅氏方的說法：全球臨床決策支援業務負責人 Matthew Prime 表示 AI 模型必須以真實世界資料持續驗證才能適應各國醫療體系，而台灣的標準化資料與跨院驗證使其成為有力夥伴。衛福部長石崇良的對應說法是「面對全球 AI 醫療快速發展，臺灣選擇先建構資料標準與治理制度」。同一篇提到衛福部採用 FHIR 並自行開發 FHIR Box，長庚、馬偕、中國醫藥大學附設醫院已完成即時跨院病歷交換。把這三則放在一起讀：今天國際廠商在搶的是「連續資料」這個護城河，而台灣正在用制度把全國尺度的連續資料變成可驗證、可給付的公共基礎設施——這是六家廠商裡沒有任何一家能自己做到的事。 (3) Putting data standards first has already bought bargaining power in international partnerships. The MOHW and Roche Diagnostics signed an MOU on August 21, 2026, with chronic kidney disease as the first phase, using routine laboratory data for risk stratification and early warning of declining kidney function. What is notable is Roche's framing: Matthew Prime, who heads its global clinical decision support and algorithms business, said AI models must be continuously validated against real-world data to suit different countries' health systems, and that Taiwan's standardised data and cross-hospital validation make it a strong partner. Health Minister Shih Chung-liang's matching line was that "faced with the rapid global development of AI in medicine, Taiwan chose to build data standards and governance systems first." The same report notes the ministry's adoption of FHIR and its own FHIR Box technology, with Chang Gung, Mackay and China Medical University Hospital having completed real-time cross-hospital record exchange. Read the three together: what the international vendors are fighting over today is the moat of consecutive data, and Taiwan is using institutions to turn nationwide consecutive data into verifiable, reimbursable public infrastructure — something none of the six companies above can do on its own.

一個要誠實標出的不一致：驗證中心的數量在兩份官方文件裡不同——三大中心網站（2026-06 更新）寫 4 處臨床 AI 取證驗證中心、5 處 AI 影響性研究中心，而資訊處的部落格文章（2025-03 發布）寫 5 處驗證中心。兩份都是衛福部來源，本報同時列出而不選邊。 One inconsistency worth flagging: the number of validation centres differs between two official documents — the three-centres site (updated 2026-06) gives 4 Clinical AI Validation Centres and 5 Impact Evaluation Centres, while the information office's blog post (published 2025-03) gives 5 validation centres. Both are MOHW sources; this report gives both rather than picking one.
