Radiotherapy is one of medicine's best test beds for orchestration: it is already a long pipeline with a fixed order — indication, target and organ-at-risk delineation, dose prescription, plan optimisation, QA, follow-up — each stage already has its own AI tool, and the missing piece has always been the layer that joins them. The paper's own evaluation statement is unambiguous: the authors write that the results "do not establish clinical correctness, clinical utility or prospective benefit" (arXiv cs.AI new submissions, 2026-10-07).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這句自陳不是謙虛，是現在整個領域的真實狀態。放射治療的編排一旦出錯，錯的不是一句建議，而是照進病人身體裡的劑量分布；而能證明「這條鏈子接對了」的評估工具，恰好是這一週另外三篇論文在說還不存在的東西。把 RadOnc-Agent 跟那三篇擺在一起讀，會看到同一個缺口從兩邊被指出來：工程端已經能串 26 個函式，量尺端連「串對了」要怎麼量都還沒收斂。 That disclaimer is not modesty; it is the field's actual state. When radiotherapy orchestration goes wrong, what is wrong is not a sentence of advice but the dose distribution delivered into a patient's body — and the evaluation tooling that could show the chain was joined correctly is precisely what three other papers this week say does not yet exist. Read RadOnc-Agent next to those three and the same gap is being pointed at from both sides: engineering can already chain 26 functions, while the measurement end has not even settled how to score whether the chain is right.

Four properties carry the weight: the centers fire only on their aligned clinical narratives; they influence model behaviour causally, in both constrained and open-ended settings; under adversarial role-based priming the models stay internally coherent and keep using the relevant centers; and steering along the centers yields downstream gains in simulated deployment settings. 為什麼重要Why it matters

這篇的論證針對的正是本期主線上那個缺口。作者明說，模型寫出來的推理過程可能漏掉真正驅動答案的特徵——也就是說，拿輸出文字評分這條路有結構性的上限。如果臨床概念真的是可定位、可因果驗證的內部表徵，那麼「這個模型在這個情境下可不可靠」就有機會從讀輸出變成讀內部，而且 steering 還能反過來當成提升手段。對照 同一週那份 rubric 盤點把 faithfulness 列為六個維度裡最弱的一項，這兩篇其實是從兩個方向咬同一個問題。 This paper aims straight at the gap running through today's edition. The authors state plainly that a model's stated reasoning can omit the features actually driving its answer — meaning that scoring the output text has a structural ceiling. If clinical concepts really are locatable, causally verifiable internal representations, then "is this model reliable in this setting" could move from reading outputs to reading internals, with steering available as a lever on top. Set against the same week's rubric review, which ranks faithfulness the weakest of its six dimensions, the two papers are biting the same problem from opposite ends.

發生什麼What

10 月 1 日投稿的 A rubric landscape for evaluating clinical reasoning in large language models（arXiv:2610.01938），作者為 Zhangshu Joshua Jiang、Zina Ibrahim 與 James T. Teo，把三支互不相通的文獻擺到一起比對：醫學教育的評量工具、2023 年起的臨床 LLM benchmark，以及一般領域的長文生成評估方法。它先把臨床推理定義成「跨時間與來源更新證據、以形成並辯護病人的問題表徵與處置計畫」，再拆成六個維度檢視——problem representation、temporal synthesis、differential 與 management reasoning、counterfactual reasoning、calibrated uncertainty、reasoning faithfulness。結論是沒有任何單一工具涵蓋全部六項：problem representation 與 differential／management reasoning 覆蓋算合理（但信度隨工具與場景浮動），TIMER-Eval 針對 temporal synthesis、ER-Reason 評序列式診斷信念更新，uncertainty 與 counterfactual 的專屬評估剛冒出來、用在長程自由文字推理上還很有限，而最弱的是 faithfulness——只找到一份臨床因果消融研究，而且用的是選擇題。 Submitted October 1, A rubric landscape for evaluating clinical reasoning in large language models (arXiv:2610.01938), by Zhangshu Joshua Jiang, Zina Ibrahim and James T. Teo, sets three literatures that normally never meet alongside each other: medical-education assessment instruments, clinical LLM benchmarks from 2023 onward, and general-domain methods for evaluating long-form generation. It defines clinical reasoning as updating evidence across time and sources to form and justify a patient's problem representation and plan, then examines six dimensions — problem representation, temporal synthesis, differential and management reasoning, counterfactual reasoning, calibrated uncertainty and reasoning faithfulness. The finding: no single instrument covers all six. Problem representation and differential/management reasoning are reasonably covered, though reliability varies by instrument and setting; TIMER-Eval targets temporal synthesis and ER-Reason assesses sequential diagnostic belief updating; dedicated uncertainty and counterfactual evaluations are emerging but barely applied to longitudinal free-text reasoning; and faithfulness is weakest — one identified clinical causal-ablation study, which used multiple-choice questions.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這份盤點解釋了為什麼 RadOnc-Agent 那句自陳是誠實而非推託：要證明一條編排鏈「臨床上是對的」，需要的正好是這六個維度一起成立，而現有工具連湊都湊不齊。作者的補救方案本身就是技術建議，而且幾乎每一條都在反對目前流行的單一總分做法——改用二元 rubric 項目、把 completeness 與 correctness 分開計分、個案重要性加權搭配不可補償的安全上限（safety cap 一破，高分不能救）、加入時序一致性檢查、報告chance-corrected 的信度。換句話說：醫療 AI 的評分表得先從「平均分」改成「否決制」。 This review explains why RadOnc-Agent's disclaimer is honest rather than evasive: showing an orchestration chain is clinically right requires all six dimensions to hold at once, and the existing tools cannot be assembled to cover them. The authors' remedies are themselves technical proposals, and nearly every one argues against today's fashion for a single aggregate score — binary rubric items, separate completeness and correctness scores, case-specific importance weighting with non-compensable safety caps (break the cap and no amount of high scoring rescues it), temporal order-consistency checks, and chance-corrected reliability reporting. Put another way: medical AI's scorecard has to move from averaging to veto.

(arXiv:2610.00562), by Taye Akinrele, Noorbakhsh Amiri Golilarz, Subash Neupane, Sudip Mittal and Shahram Rahimi, uses the MedLoCoMo dataset and four open-weight models to compare five ways of handing a patient history to a model: Full, Recent, Episodic, Semantic and Hybrid. It measures three things — answer correctness, robustness as the distance between a query and its supporting evidence grows, and the ability to abstain on questions whose premises the history does not support. The results: Episodic and Hybrid give the strongest overall accuracy; Recent Context degrades worst as evidence gets more distant, while Episodic and Hybrid hold the highest accuracy at long range; and the line most worth copying down — strong performance on answerable questions does not reliably carry over to abstaining on adversarial ones.

為什麼重要Why it matters

作者把結論寫成一句可以直接拿去改架構的話：可靠的長程推理，取決的不是模型能取用多少病史，而是相關證據怎麼被挑選與呈現。這對過去兩年「把 context window 加長就好」的直覺是正面否定——真正要投資的是檢索與組織那一層。而棄答那個發現更要緊：臨床上最危險的失敗模式不是答錯一題，是對一個前提就不成立的問題給出一個自信的答案，而這份實驗說明準確率高的策略不會自動帶來這項能力，得分開量、分開練。 The authors state the conclusion in a form you can act on architecturally: reliable longitudinal reasoning depends less on how much history a model can access than on how relevant evidence is selected and presented. That is a direct refusal of the last two years' instinct that a longer context window settles the matter — the investment belongs in the retrieval and organisation layer. The abstention finding matters more still: clinically, the dangerous failure is not getting one question wrong but answering a false-premise question confidently, and this experiment shows the strategies that win on accuracy do not acquire that capability for free. It has to be measured, and trained, separately.

  • 疑慮Concern ：整篇沒有任何對照模型——0.871 旁邊沒有 baseline，無法知道這是領先還是追平；參數量未揭露；NIH 外部世代有評估但結果未給，等於外部效度這一欄是空白。「判讀與報告時間少 50%」是初步、未給樣本數的作者自述。釋出聲明也沒附 repo 連結與授權。 : there is no comparator model anywhere in the paper — nothing sits beside 0.871, so leading or merely matching cannot be told; parameter count is undisclosed; a held-out NIH cohort was evaluated but no results are reported, leaving the external-validity column blank. The "50% reduction in interpretation and reporting time" is a preliminary author claim with no sample size. The release statement carries no repository link or licence.

兩條路線的真正差別不在架構，在誰承擔驗證成本。編排型把驗證負擔推給整條鏈——每多一個函式，要證明的組合就多一層，而 10/1 那份 rubric 盤點說這種組合式效度目前無工具可量。單一模型型把驗證收在一個介面上，數字好報、也確實報了，但它少的是對照：0.871 沒有 baseline，跟 42% 有十個代理人互相比的 HealthAgentBench 恰成反例。這一週最值得學的方法論，反而來自 HealthAgentBench——它同時跑三種 harness，才讓「同一個模型換殼掉 7 個百分點」這件事被看見。 The real difference between the two routes is not architecture but who carries the cost of validation. The orchestration route pushes that burden onto the whole chain: each added function adds a layer of combination to be proven, and the Oct 1 rubric review says no instrument currently measures that compositional validity. The single-model route gathers validation behind one interface, where numbers are easy to report — and were reported — but what it lacks is a comparator: 0.871 with no baseline, the exact inverse of HealthAgentBench's 42% arrived at by racing ten agents against each other. The methodological lesson of the week comes from HealthAgentBench instead: only because it ran three harnesses at once did the fact that one model loses 7 points on a change of wrapper become visible at all.

04 — Taiwan Angle

台灣這一週同時推進了管線與政策，但評估那一格還是空的 Taiwan advanced both plumbing and policy this week, and left the evaluation box empty

(1) FHIR Box 不只是互通，它是「證據可挑選」的前置條件。9/30 那份長程推理實驗把結論寫得很白：可靠的長程臨床推理取決於相關證據怎麼被挑選與呈現，而不是模型能讀多少（arXiv:2610.00562）。台灣這一週推的兩件事剛好都在這一層：10/5 大會的 FHIR Box（不抽換核心系統、把院內資料轉成 HL7 FHIR）與新一代 3D PACS。更早一點，衛福部長石崇良在 9/15 的受訪裡給了時程：2026 年底全國醫學中心關鍵資料連上線、2027 年底擴及全國醫院，並提到以 FHIR 搭配聯邦學習做臨床試驗收案——在保護隱私的前提下快速找出合格病人與機構，電子病歷可直接轉換、減少人工收集。換算成技術語言：台灣正在建的不是模型，是 Episodic 與 Hybrid 策略賴以成立的那個可檢索、可跨院的證據層。 (1) FHIR Box is not only interoperability; it is the precondition for evidence being selectable at all. The Sept 30 long-horizon experiment states it plainly: reliable longitudinal clinical reasoning depends on how relevant evidence is selected and presented, not on how much a model can read (arXiv:2610.00562). Both of Taiwan's moves this week sit on that layer: FHIR Box (converting hospital data to HL7 FHIR without replacing core systems) and the next-generation 3D PACS, from the Oct 5 conference. Earlier, Health Minister Shih Chung-liang gave the timetable in a Sept 15 interview: key data from all national medical centres connected by the end of 2026, extended to hospitals nationwide by the end of 2027, with FHIR paired with federated learning for trial recruitment — finding eligible patients and sites quickly while protecting privacy, converting electronic records directly and cutting manual collection. In technical terms: what Taiwan is building is not a model but the retrievable, cross-institution evidence layer that Episodic and Hybrid strategies depend on.

(2) 10/7 生技論壇講了錢、法規與資料，沒有講評估。石崇良在 10/7 的 2026 生技論壇上把政策盤點了一輪：再生醫療雙法 1 月上路、《全民健康保險資料管理條例》8 月上路（健保資料庫可在法定條件下供學術與產業使用）、「信賴 100」力求 100 天內完成國產新藥與關鍵藥品審查、健保今年 9,883 億元、明年可達 1 兆 434 億元、藥物韌性整備計畫四年 240 億元、健康台灣深耕計畫五年 489 億元、生技醫藥發展基金十年 100 億元、HTA 中心行政法人化待公布。注意這整份清單裡沒有任何一項是關於 AI 效度評估的——資料可用、錢到位、審查加速都在推進，但「誰來驗這些模型在台灣病人身上有沒有效」沒有對應的機構或預算項目。 (2) The Oct 7 biotech forum covered money, law and data — not evaluation. At the 2026 Biotech Forum on Oct 7, Minister Shih ran through the policy ledger: the twin regenerative-medicine laws effective in January, the National Health Insurance Data Management Act effective in August (opening the NHI database to academic and industry use under legal conditions), "Trust 100" targeting 100-day review for qualifying domestic new drugs and key medicines, NHI spending of NT$988.3bn this year and possibly NT$1.0434tn next, a four-year NT$24bn drug resilience plan, a five-year NT$48.9bn Healthy Taiwan plan, a ten-year NT$10bn biotech development fund, and an HTA centre awaiting promulgation as an administrative legal entity. Note that not one item on that list concerns AI validity assessment. Data access, funding and faster review are all moving; who verifies whether these models work on Taiwanese patients has neither an institution nor a budget line.

(3) 給付缺口與評估缺口是同一個缺口。台中榮總榮譽院長陳適安在 10/5 說得最實際：更廣的臨床使用需要服務與給付機制，現在是部分醫院在申請 3D 影像自費代碼、未來才可能接上健保（經濟日報，10/5）。這兩件事其實綁在一起：健保要給付，就得有人說得出這項技術的效益有多大；而本期三篇論文的共同結論是，說出效益需要的那套量尺目前連國際上都還沒湊齊——六個維度沒有單一工具涵蓋得完、最強代理人在 54 項真實形態任務上只有 42%。所以台灣現在的位置其實是一個機會：資料層（FHIR Box）2027 年底就要鋪完，而評估這一格全世界都還空著。能把本地化的效度評估建起來的人，手上會有別人沒有的東西——而那正好是 9/15 提到的聯邦學習架構最適合做的事：不必集中資料，就能跨院驗證一個模型在真實病人身上的表現。 (3) The reimbursement gap and the evaluation gap are the same gap. Taichung Veterans General Hospital honorary superintendent Chen Shih-an put it most practically on Oct 5: broader clinical use needs service and payment mechanisms, and today some hospitals are applying for self-pay billing codes for 3D imaging, with any NHI link still prospective (Economic Daily News, Oct 5). The two are tied together: for NHI to reimburse, somebody has to be able to state the size of the benefit — and the common conclusion of this edition's three papers is that the measuring apparatus needed to state it has not been assembled anywhere, let alone here: no single instrument covers all six dimensions, and the strongest agents manage 42% on 54 realistic tasks. Which makes Taiwan's position an opportunity: the data layer (FHIR Box) is due to be laid by the end of 2027, while the evaluation box is empty worldwide. Whoever stands up localised validity assessment will hold something nobody else has — and it is precisely what the federated-learning architecture mentioned on Sept 15 is best suited to: validating a model's performance on real patients across hospitals without centralising the data.

05 — Further Reading

挑的是「告訴你要怎麼量」的五篇，不是「宣布又做出一個模型」的那些 Five that tell you how to measure, rather than announcing one more model
  1. A rubric landscape for evaluating clinical reasoning in large language models — arXiv:2610.01938 (2026-10-01)

    如果這一週只讀一篇，讀這篇。它給的六個維度可以直接當成你評一個臨床 LLM 時的檢查表，而「不可補償的安全上限」那條建議值得抄進任何內部評估規範。If you read one thing from this week, read this. Its six dimensions work as a checklist the next time you assess a clinical LLM, and the non-compensable safety cap belongs in any internal evaluation policy.

  2. HealthAgentBench: A Unified Benchmark Suite of Realistic Agentic Healthcare Environments — Microsoft Research, arXiv:2606.31179 (2026-06-30)

    讀它的分項表而不是總分。七類任務的落差（格式轉換 100% 對資料品質稽核不到 50%）比任何一個整體百分比都更能告訴你，代理人現在能接哪種工作、不能接哪種。Read the category table, not the headline. The spread across its seven categories — 100% on format conversion against under 50% on data quality auditing — tells you more about which jobs agents can take today than any aggregate percentage.

  3. Benchmarking large language model-based agent systems for clinical decision tasks — npj Digital Medicine (2026-02-18)

    今年最該被引用卻最常被忽略的一篇：Kather 團隊把代理人系統與 zero-shot 基線並排後發現增益「modest」且多數不顯著，而代價是 10 倍以上的 token（MedQA 每題約 93,000 對 14,600）；更該記住的是內建防護擋掉 89.9% 幻覺之後，幻覺仍影響約 30% 的臨床情境。The most citable and least cited paper of the year: Kather's group set agent systems beside zero-shot baselines, found the gains "modest" and mostly not significant, and priced them at over 10x the tokens (about 93,000 per MedQA scenario against 14,600). Remember the other number too — after in-agent safeguards blocked 89.9% of hallucinations, hallucinations still touched roughly 30% of clinical scenarios.

  4. A benchmark study of vision and pathology foundation models for computational pathology — Nature Communications (2026-07-24)

    讀它是為了那個反直覺的結論：Gevaert 團隊用 32 個基礎模型跑 41 項任務、53 個資料集、逾 17,500 張全切片影像，結果病理專用視覺模型與通用視覺模型之間沒有統計顯著的 AUROC 差異，而且模型大小與預訓練規模不能穩定預測表現。下次有人用「我們是醫療專用模型」當賣點時，這篇是你的對照。Read it for the counterintuitive finding: Gevaert's group ran 32 foundation models over 41 tasks, 53 datasets and more than 17,500 whole-slide images, and found no statistically significant AUROC difference between pathology-specific and general vision models, with model size and pretraining scale not consistently predicting performance. The next time someone pitches "ours is a medical-specific model," this is your comparator.

  5. OpenEvidence & TEMPO: No One is Reviewing AI — GridHealth (2026-09-22)

    把本期的技術主題翻譯成商業與監理語言的一篇短評。它的核心句——「廠商自己有信心，不會讓一個主張變成證據」——值得貼在任何醫院 AI 採購會議室的牆上；另外它順手點出 FDA TEMPO 試辦讓生成式 AI 器材在正式上市許可前就接觸病人，計畫擴到 40 個參與者、四個臨床領域。A short piece that translates this edition's technical theme into commercial and regulatory language. Its core line — a claim is not evidence because the vendor is confident — belongs on the wall of any hospital AI procurement room. It also notes in passing that the FDA's TEMPO pilot lets generative-AI devices reach patients before formal marketing authorisation, with plans to scale to 40 participants across four clinical areas.
