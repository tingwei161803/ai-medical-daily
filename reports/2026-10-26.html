乳房 MRI 直接算出 10 年復發風險：SimBioSys 拿到 510(k)，驗證世代 2,138 人、高低風險組 10 年無復發存活率差 19.3 個百分點 Reading 10-year recurrence risk off a breast MRI: SimBioSys clears 510(k) on a 2,138-patient cohort where high and low risk differ by 19.3 points of 10-year recurrence-free survival

發生什麼What

SimBioSys 於 10 月 15 日發布消息，其 TumorSight Risk 取得 FDA 510(k) 許可，適應症為「荷爾蒙受體陽性、HER2 陰性、第 I 至 IIIA 期成年乳癌病人」，從既有的乳房 MRI 推導 5 年與 10 年復發風險分數。OncLive 整理的驗證結果來自一份 2026 ASCO 年會發表的回溯多中心研究：2,138 名病人中 183 人（8.5%）復發，低風險組 1,815 人、高風險組 323 人；5 年無復發存活率 95.5%（95% CI 94.6–96.3）對 89.6%（86.4–92.6），10 年 88.9%（86.5–90.9）對 69.6%（59.1–77.9）。未校正 HR 2.81（2.03–3.88），納入臨床病理因子校正後仍為 2.18（1.35–3.52），P = .002。分數每增加 10 分，相對復發風險上升 22%；分數最低區間（0–10）的 10 年復發估計為 6.2%，最高區間（58–100）為 34.2%。AuntMinnie 與 ITN 同步報導，Health IT Answers 的 10/22 彙整也收錄此案。 SimBioSys announced on October 15 that TumorSight Risk had cleared FDA 510(k) for "adult patients with hormone receptor–positive, HER2-negative stage I to IIIA breast cancer", deriving 5- and 10-year recurrence risk scores from an existing breast MRI. The validation compiled by OncLive comes from a retrospective multicentre study presented at the 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting: of 2,138 patients, 183 (8.5%) recurred; 1,815 were classified low risk and 323 high risk. Five-year recurrence-free survival was 95.5% (95% CI 94.6–96.3) versus 89.6% (86.4–92.6); at ten years, 88.9% (86.5–90.9) versus 69.6% (59.1–77.9). The unadjusted hazard ratio was 2.81 (2.03–3.88), and 2.18 (1.35–3.52) after adjustment for clinicopathologic factors, P = .002. Each 10-point rise in score corresponded to a 22% increase in relative recurrence risk; estimated 10-year recurrence ran from 6.2% in the lowest score band (0–10) to 34.2% in the highest (58–100). AuntMinnie and ITN covered it in parallel, and it appears in Health IT Answers' October 22 roundup.

為什麼重要Why it matters

HR+／HER2− 是乳癌裡人數最多的族群，而「要不要加做輔助化療」這個決定，過去二十年幾乎被基因表現檢測壟斷——組織要送出去、要等報告、要自費或爭取給付。TumorSight Risk 走的是另一條路：病人本來就已經照了 MRI，影像已經在院內，推論可以當天完成、不消耗組織。如果它真能提供「超出標準臨床病理因子以外」的預後資訊（這正是驗證研究用多變項校正後 HR 2.18 想證明的事），受影響的不只是一家公司的營收，而是整個復發風險分層的經濟結構。 HR+/HER2− is the largest subgroup in breast cancer, and the decision on adjuvant chemotherapy has for two decades been dominated by gene-expression assays — tissue shipped out, a report waited on, a bill paid out of pocket or fought through reimbursement. TumorSight Risk takes a different route: the patient has already had the MRI, the images are already inside the hospital, inference finishes the same day and consumes no tissue. If it genuinely adds prognostic information beyond standard clinicopathologic factors — which is precisely what the multivariable HR of 2.18 is meant to establish — what shifts is not one company's revenue but the cost structure of recurrence risk stratification itself.

要打折的地方Discount this

驗證是回溯性的，不是前瞻隨機試驗；OncLive 的報導中樣本數出現 2,138 與 2,129 兩個互相不一致的數字，且未提供 AUC，也沒有與 Oncotype DX、MammaPrint 等既有基因檢測做頭對頭一致性比較——也就是說，目前還無法回答「它能不能取代基因檢測」或「兩者不一致時聽誰的」。5–10 年區間的 HR 3.4（2.0–5.6）被原研究標為探索性分析。最關鍵的一點：510(k) 許可的是「提供預後資訊」，不是「據此改變治療決策能改善結果」，後者需要一個完全不同等級的試驗。 The validation is retrospective, not a prospective randomised trial. OncLive's write-up carries two mutually inconsistent sample sizes, 2,138 and 2,129, reports no AUC, and offers no head-to-head concordance comparison against established genomic assays such as Oncotype DX or MammaPrint — so the questions of whether it could substitute for one, and whose answer wins when the two disagree, remain open. The 5–10 year hazard ratio of 3.4 (2.0–5.6) is flagged in the original study as exploratory. Most important: a 510(k) clears the device to supply prognostic information, not to establish that acting on it improves outcomes, which would take a trial of an entirely different order.

「低風險」這個標籤可能漏掉的東西：CaRi-Heart 美國首份數據，42.9% 無症狀成人驗出冠狀動脈發炎，沒有斑塊的人裡約 47% 也發炎 What the label "low risk" may be missing: CaRi-Heart's first US data finds coronary inflammation in 42.9% of asymptomatic adults — and in roughly 47% of those with no plaque at all

發生什麼What

10 月 23 日 Cardiovascular Business 報導，牛津大學衍生公司 Caristo Diagnostics 的 CaRi-Heart 完成在美國的第一份研究，由會員制診所 Biograph 執行。CaRi-Heart 以冠狀動脈周圍脂肪衰減指數（fat attenuation index, FAI）分析常規冠狀動脈電腦斷層血管攝影（CCTA），不需額外輻射或對比劑。研究結果：42.9% 的受試者驗出冠狀動脈發炎；17.9% 在至少一條主要血管有「極高」發炎；而在完全沒有偵測到斑塊的受試者中，約 47% 仍有升高的冠狀動脈周圍發炎。Biograph 執行醫療總監 Michael Doney 的說法是，超過 42% 的「無症狀、依傳統分類屬低風險」成人有升高的冠狀動脈發炎。該技術於 7 月底取得 FDA de novo 授權，是美國第一個也目前唯一獲准從 CCTA 量化冠狀動脈發炎的技術，並對應 2026 年 1 月 1 日生效的 AMA Category III CPT 代碼 0992T／0993T。 On October 23 Cardiovascular Business reported that CaRi-Heart, from the Oxford spin-out Caristo Diagnostics, had completed its first US study, run by the concierge clinic Biograph. CaRi-Heart analyses routine coronary CT angiography using the fat attenuation index of pericoronary adipose tissue, with no additional radiation or contrast. The findings: coronary inflammation in 42.9% of participants; "very high" inflammation in at least one major artery in 17.9%; and, among participants with no detectable plaque whatsoever, elevated pericoronary inflammation in roughly 47%. Biograph executive medical director Michael Doney puts it as more than 42% of "asymptomatic, conventionally low-risk adults" carrying elevated coronary inflammation. The technology received FDA de novo authorisation in late July, making it the first and so far only technology cleared in the US to quantify coronary inflammation from CCTA, with AMA Category III CPT codes 0992T and 0993T effective January 1, 2026.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這份數據最有意思的不是 42.9%，而是「沒有斑塊但有發炎」那一群人。現行的冠狀動脈風險評估——鈣化分數、斑塊負荷、Framingham 類的風險因子模型——量的都是結構已經壞掉的程度。發炎是另一個維度，理論上在結構變化之前。如果這個維度真的獨立且可預測事件，那麼今天被判為「低風險、不需要治療」的人裡，有一部分其實分類錯了。這是醫療 AI 少見的、能在既有檢查上「長出一個新變項」的案例，而不只是把放射科醫師的工作做得快一點。 The interesting number here is not 42.9% but the group with inflammation and no plaque. Current coronary risk assessment — calcium scores, plaque burden, Framingham-style risk factor models — all measure how far structural damage has already gone. Inflammation is a different axis, in principle upstream of structural change. If that axis is genuinely independent and genuinely predictive of events, then some share of the people classified today as low risk and left untreated are misclassified. This is one of the rarer cases in medical AI of growing a new variable out of an existing scan, rather than doing a radiologist's existing job faster.

要打折的地方Discount this

打折的幅度在這則裡特別大。報導沒有給出世代人數、追蹤時間、風險比，也沒有指明發表在哪本期刊或是否經同儕審查——只有百分比。研究場域是會員制自費診所，受試者是主動付費做全身檢查的人，和一般人口的事前機率完全不同。報導自己也寫「還需要更多研究來驗證並建立 FAI 的預後價值」。換句話說，「42.9% 的人有發炎」目前只是一個盛行率數字，它還不等於「這 42.9% 的人會出事」。 The discount here is unusually large. The report gives no cohort size, no follow-up duration, no hazard ratios, and does not name a journal or state whether the work was peer reviewed — only percentages. The setting is a self-pay concierge clinic and the participants are people who chose to pay for a full-body workup, a pre-test probability nothing like the general population's. The article itself says more research is needed to validate this and establish the prognostic value of FAI. In other words, "42.9% have inflammation" is for now a prevalence figure, and not yet a statement that those 42.9% will have events.

年度最佳論文獎頒給一個壞消息：17 位放射科醫師只有 7 人自己察覺 X 光片是 AI 生成的，而年資完全不影響辨識率 Paper of the year goes to bad news: only 7 of 17 radiologists spontaneously noticed the X-rays were AI-generated, and years of experience made no difference at all

發生什麼What

RSNA 於 10 月 20 日宣布，2026 年 Alexander R. Margulis 科學卓越獎（表彰當年度《Radiology》最佳原創科學論文）頒給 Mount Sinai 伊坎醫學院 Mickael Tordjman 與 Murat Yuce 等人的〈The Rise of Deepfake Medical Imaging: Radiologists' Diagnostic Accuracy in Detecting ChatGPT-generated Radiographs〉，AuntMinnie 與 DOTmed（10/23）均有報導，獎項將於 11 月 29 日 RSNA 2026 芝加哥年會頒出。該研究原發表於 2026 年 3 月 24 日的《Radiology》：來自 6 國 12 個中心、年資 0 至 40 年的 17 位放射科醫師，閱讀 264 張 X 光片（約半數真實、半數 AI 生成），分為 GPT-4o 生成與史丹佛開源擴散模型 RoentGen 生成兩組非重疊資料集。結果：在不知道研究目的時，只有 7 位（41%）自行察覺有 AI 生成影像；被告知後，GPT-4o 組平均辨識準確率 74.8%（個別範圍 58–92%），RoentGen 組約 70%（62–78%）。年資與辨識率無相關，但肌肉骨骼次專科醫師顯著較佳。同時測試的四個多模態大模型中，GPT-4o 自己辨識出最多（85.1%）、GPT-5 為 82.5%，均優於 Gemini 2.5 Pro 與 Llama 4 Maverick，但沒有任何一個模型抓出全部合成影像。另一項發現同樣值得注意：醫師對真實 X 光片的異常診斷準確率 91.3%，對合成 X 光片 92.4%——也就是說，合成影像「看起來足夠真」到不影響判讀行為。 RSNA announced on October 20 that the 2026 Alexander R. Margulis Award for Scientific Excellence, which honours the best original scientific article published in Radiology that year, goes to "The Rise of Deepfake Medical Imaging: Radiologists' Diagnostic Accuracy in Detecting ChatGPT-generated Radiographs" by Mickael Tordjman, Murat Yuce and colleagues at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, as reported by AuntMinnie and DOTmed on October 23; it will be presented at RSNA 2026 in Chicago on November 29. The study was originally published in Radiology on March 24, 2026: 17 radiologists from 12 centres across six countries, with 0 to 40 years of experience, read 264 radiographs, about half authentic and half AI-generated, split into two non-overlapping datasets — one from GPT-4o, one from Stanford's open-source diffusion model RoentGen. Unaware of the study's purpose, only 7 readers (41%) spontaneously flagged the synthetic images. Once told, mean classification accuracy was 74.8% on the GPT-4o set (individual range 58–92%) and about 70% on the RoentGen set (62–78%). Years of experience showed no correlation with detection; musculoskeletal subspecialists did significantly better. Of four multimodal LLMs tested, GPT-4o — the model that made the fakes — caught the most at 85.1%, with GPT-5 at 82.5%, both ahead of Gemini 2.5 Pro and Llama 4 Maverick, and none caught every synthetic image. One further result deserves attention: readers' accuracy in diagnosing abnormalities was 91.3% on authentic radiographs and 92.4% on synthetic ones — the fakes looked real enough not to disturb reading behaviour at all.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這個獎的意義在於它改變了議題的位置。一年來醫療 AI 的討論幾乎都繞著「模型判讀得準不準」，而這篇論文問的是另一件事：當影像本身可以被生成，整個數位病歷的可信度基礎在哪裡。Tordjman 在原始新聞稿裡列出三種具體情境——詐欺性訴訟、醫院網路被入侵後注入合成影像以操縱診斷、以及「破壞數位病歷根本可靠性」。放射科是最早被 AI 滲透的科別，也因此最早撞上這個問題：用來訓練判讀模型的那套生成技術，同時也是偽造證據的技術。更微妙的是「肌肉骨骼次專科較佳」與「年資無關」這兩個發現擺在一起的含意——辨識合成影像靠的不是臨床經驗的累積，而是某種對紋理與解剖細節的特定敏感度，那不是現行訓練體系在教的東西。 The award matters because it relocates the question. For a year the medical AI conversation has circled around whether models read images accurately; this paper asks something else — when the image itself can be generated, what is left holding up the credibility of the digital record. In the original press release Tordjman named three concrete scenarios: fraudulent litigation, synthetic images injected after a hospital network breach to manipulate diagnoses, and the undermining of the fundamental reliability of the medical record. Radiology was the first specialty AI reached and is therefore the first to hit this: the same generative technology used to train reading models is the technology that forges evidence. Subtler still is what the two findings imply together — that musculoskeletal subspecialists did better while years of experience did nothing. Spotting a synthetic image does not draw on accumulated clinical experience but on some specific sensitivity to texture and anatomical detail, and that is not what the present training system teaches.

要打折的地方Discount this

本週的新聞是「獲獎」，不是新研究——論文本身發表於 2026 年 3 月，數據並非新鮮。17 位閱片者是很小的樣本，且為回溯設計、非臨床情境下的閱片實驗，受試者知道自己在參加研究（即使不知道確切目的）。生成影像用的是通用模型，不是專為偽造病歷最佳化的對抗式工具，所以 74.8% 這個數字在真正的惡意情境下大概會更差，而不是更好。另外要注意，模型版本資訊（GPT-4o、GPT-5、Gemini 2.5 Pro、Llama 4 Maverick）反映的是論文投稿當時的狀態。 This week's news is the award, not a new study — the paper itself was published in March 2026 and the data are not fresh. Seventeen readers is a small sample, the design is retrospective, and the reading task was an experiment rather than a clinical setting, with participants aware they were in a study even if not of its exact purpose. The images were made with general-purpose models, not with adversarial tools optimised for forging medical records, so 74.8% would likely get worse rather than better under genuinely malicious conditions. Note too that the model versions named — GPT-4o, GPT-5, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Llama 4 Maverick — reflect the state of play at the time of submission.

04 — Taiwan Angle

把「同一張影像疊層」這個邏輯換算成台灣的尺度：健保、自費，與影像完整性的空窗 Translating the stacked-image logic to Taiwanese scale: NHI, self-pay, and the open gap in image integrity

(1) 給付這一關，台灣卡的位置和美國不一樣。本週四張許可背後的共同前提是「新代碼」——Caristo 有 CPT 0992T／0993T（HIT Consultant），Coreline 的策略直接寫著要靠機會性篩檢賣進美國。台灣的單一支付者體系沒有對應的 Category III 暫時代碼機制，所以影像 AI 的落地路徑實際上被推往自費。這一點在 10 月 5 日衛福部那場 3D 醫療影像大會上已經講得很白：台中榮總榮譽院長陳適安直接點出 3D 影像「需要服務與給付機制」，而部分醫院正在申請自費收費代碼。換句話說，美國的影像 AI 公司在爭取保險給付，台灣的醫院在爭取自費項目——這會長出完全不同的採用曲線，也會讓「哪些病人用得到」變成支付能力問題而非臨床適應症問題。 (1) Reimbursement is the gate, but Taiwan is stuck at a different point on it than the US. The common premise behind this week's four clearances is a new code — Caristo has CPT 0992T/0993T (HIT Consultant), and Coreline's strategy says outright that opportunistic screening is how it sells into the US. Taiwan's single-payer system has no equivalent temporary Category III mechanism, so the route to adoption for imaging AI gets pushed towards self-pay. That was said plainly at the Ministry of Health and Welfare's 3D medical imaging conference on October 5: Taichung Veterans General Hospital honorary president Chen Shih-ann noted that 3D imaging "needs service and payment mechanisms", while some hospitals are applying for self-pay billing codes. Put differently, US imaging AI companies are fighting for insurance coverage while Taiwanese hospitals fight for a self-pay line item — which grows an entirely different adoption curve, and turns "which patients get this" into a question of ability to pay rather than clinical indication.

(2) 台灣在影像 AI 的位置其實更像 Coreline，不像 SimBioSys。10 月 5 日的大會上，國產業者智捷醫學科技捐出 10 台 AI 3D 影像工作站，衛福部將部署到醫學中心與公立醫院作為示範單位；台大醫院院長余忠仁說明 2028 年要完成全院的 AI 3D 影像自動轉換架構，馬偕院長張文瀚提到建模時間可壓到約 5 分鐘（聯合報）。衛福部早先也成立三大 AI 中心。這條路線的本質和 Coreline 一樣，是在既有影像上疊加工具與工作流，靠整合深度競爭，而不是靠一份 2,138 人的回溯世代挑戰國際指引。這不是壞策略——台灣的醫療資訊整合度與單一支付者的資料集中度，在全世界都算難得的條件。但本週 SimBioSys 的例子提醒了另一件事：真正能改變治療決策的，是那種敢去做前瞻驗證、敢去和指引正面對話的證據，而這需要的是臨床試驗能量與長期經費，不是示範工作站。 (2) Taiwan's position in imaging AI actually resembles Coreline's more than SimBioSys's. At the October 5 conference the domestic firm Anatomy Cloud donated 10 AI 3D imaging workstations, which the ministry will deploy at medical centres and public hospitals as demonstration units; NTUH president Yu Chong-jen set out a 2028 target for a hospital-wide automated AI 3D conversion framework, and Mackay Memorial president Chang Wen-han noted modelling time compressed to around five minutes (United Daily News). The ministry had earlier established three national AI centres. The essence of this route is Coreline's: stack tools and workflow onto existing images and compete on depth of integration, rather than challenge international guidelines with a 2,138-patient retrospective cohort. That is not a bad strategy — Taiwan's degree of health IT integration and the data concentration of a single payer are genuinely rare conditions globally. But this week's SimBioSys case is a reminder of something else: what actually changes treatment decisions is evidence willing to go prospective and argue with a guideline head-on, and that takes clinical trial capacity and long-horizon funding, not demonstration workstations.

(3) Margulis 獎那篇論文指出的風險，在台灣目前完全沒有對應規範。該研究顯示放射科醫師在被告知後仍只有約 74.8% 的辨識準確率，且年資無助（RSNA）。台灣的影像資料在 PACS 與健保申報之間有大量流轉，而衛福部明年才擬訂生成式 AI 指引，目前的醫療器材與資安規範並未針對「合成醫學影像」這個類別設計。具體要問的問題有三個：影像進入 PACS 時有沒有來源驗證與完整性簽章？保險理賠與司法鑑定採用的影像，如何證明未經生成或篡改？以及在醫院導入生成式影像增強（用於去噪、超解析）之後，怎麼在稽核上區分「合法的生成」與「非法的生成」？第三個問題尤其棘手，因為台灣正在推的 AI 3D 影像轉換，本質上就是在既有影像上做生成。 (3) The risk the Margulis paper identifies has no corresponding rule in Taiwan at all. The study found radiologists at roughly 74.8% detection accuracy even after being warned, with experience offering no help (RSNA). Imaging data in Taiwan moves heavily between PACS systems and NHI claims, while the ministry is only drafting generative AI guidance for next year, and current medical device and information security rules were not designed for the category of synthetic medical images. Three concrete questions follow: is there provenance verification or an integrity signature when an image enters the PACS? For images used in insurance claims and forensic assessment, how is it shown they were neither generated nor altered? And once hospitals adopt generative image enhancement for denoising or super-resolution, how does an audit distinguish legitimate generation from illegitimate? The third is the hardest, because the AI 3D image conversion Taiwan is actively promoting is itself generation performed on an existing image.
