同一條賽道、兩種相反的打法：12 個人、3,200 萬美元、190 國；與 1,000 美元 CPM、150 億美元估值 One market, two opposite bets: twelve people, $32M and 190 countries — against $1,000 CPMs and a $15B valuation

2026 年 10 月 30 日（五）· 涵蓋 10/20–10/29 · 台北時間 Friday, October 30, 2026 · Covering Oct 20–29 · Taipei time

「醫師問答引擎」是過去兩年醫療 AI 裡商業化最快的一塊，今天這一份把它拆開來看。10 月 27 日，成立於 2024 年、全公司只有 12 名員工的 Vera Health 宣布由 Threshold Ventures 領投的 2,850 萬美元 A 輪，累計募資僅 3,200 萬美元，卻聲稱 190 個國家、逾 80 萬名臨床人員在用，西班牙與義大利每四位執業醫師就有一位、法國每七位有一位；公司自述自 2026 年 6 月起月月是全球下載量最高的臨床 App，9 月下載數超過 OpenEvidence 與 Doximity 相加。對照組是 OpenEvidence：9 月 25 日以 2.5 億美元募到 150 億美元估值、一年內募逾 10 億美元，Sacra 估其 2026 年 7 月年化營收約 3 億美元、毛利率約 90%，廣告 CPM 逾 1,000 美元但只賣掉約 5% 版位。兩家都對醫師免費，但一家把成本押在藥廠廣告與美國市場，另一家押在 12 個人與 190 個國家——這是今天要拆的對比。 The clinician answer engine has been the fastest-monetising corner of medical AI for two years. Today takes it apart. On October 27, Vera Health — founded in 2024, twelve employees in total — announced a $28.5M Series A led by Threshold Ventures, bringing lifetime funding to just $32M, on a claimed 800,000-plus clinicians across 190 countries: roughly one in four practising physicians in Spain and Italy, one in seven in France. The company says it has been the most-downloaded clinical app worldwide every month since June 2026, outdownloading OpenEvidence and Doximity combined in September. The counterweight is OpenEvidence: $250M at a $15B valuation on September 25, more than $1B raised inside a year, with Sacra estimating roughly $300M annualized revenue as of July 2026 at about 90% gross margin, ad CPMs above $1,000 and only about 5% of inventory sold. Both are free to doctors. One has bet its cost base on pharma advertising inside the United States; the other on twelve people and 190 countries. That is the contrast.

12 名員工、3,200 萬美元累計募資，換來 190 國 80 萬名臨床人員：Vera Health 的 2,850 萬美元 A 輪 Twelve employees and $32M raised in total, against 800,000 clinicians in 190 countries: Vera Health's $28.5M Series A

發生什麼What

10 月 27 日，Vera Health 宣布 2,850 萬美元 A 輪，由 Threshold Ventures 領投，Mantis VC 與 SV Angel 為新投資人，既有股東 Y Combinator 與 Gradient 跟投，累計資本額 3,200 萬美元。公司全職員工 12 人，橫跨工程與臨床。產品索引逾 6,000 萬篇同儕審查文獻、臨床指引、共識聲明與 FDA／EMA 藥品仿單，在生成答案前先依研究設計、證據等級、世代規模、時效、期刊品質與引用影響力為每一筆證據評級，並把每個主張依證據強度標上顏色——單一病例報告之類的薄證據會被標紅。共同創辦人 Taieb Bennani 與 Maxime Allouch 都是 MIT 畢業、醫師之子，2024 年在 Y Combinator 創立，總部設於舊金山。 On October 27 Vera Health announced a $28.5M Series A led by Threshold Ventures, with Mantis VC and SV Angel joining as new investors and existing backers Y Combinator and Gradient following on, taking total capitalisation to $32M. The company has twelve full-time staff across engineering and clinical roles. The product indexes more than 60 million peer-reviewed publications, clinical guidelines, consensus statements and FDA/EMA drug labels, and grades every piece of evidence before composing an answer — by study design, level of evidence, cohort size, recency, journal quality and citation impact — then colour-codes each claim by evidence strength, flagging thin support such as a single case report in red. Co-founders Taieb Bennani and Maxime Allouch are MIT graduates and sons of physicians; the company was founded in 2024 at Y Combinator and is headquartered in San Francisco.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是第一次有人用「不到 OpenEvidence 單輪募資零頭」的資本，在同一條賽道上拿出可比較的分佈數字。Vera 稱企業行銷支出為零，成長幾乎全來自同儕口碑；西班牙與義大利約每四位執業醫師有一位、法國每七位有一位在用。更關鍵的是兩者的地理互補：OpenEvidence 的免費模式綁在美國藥廠廣告上，天然無法外溢到廣告預算薄的市場；Vera 從第一天就是 190 國。企業版 Vera Spaces 讓醫院把自家醫囑集、處方集與抗生素敏感性報告（antibiogram）與國際文獻一起檢索，是它唯一已揭露的變現方向。 This is the first time anyone has produced comparable distribution figures in this market on less capital than OpenEvidence raises in a single round. Vera reports zero enterprise marketing spend and growth almost entirely by peer referral; about one in four practising physicians in Spain and Italy use it, and one in seven in France. The sharper point is geographic complementarity: OpenEvidence's free tier is tethered to US pharma advertising and cannot naturally spill into markets with thin ad budgets, whereas Vera has been in 190 countries from day one. Vera Spaces, the enterprise tier that lets hospitals index their own order sets, formularies and antibiograms alongside the literature, is the only disclosed route to revenue.

要打折的地方Discount this

80 萬臨床人員、190 國、下載量第一與「9 月超過 OpenEvidence 加 Doximity」全部是公司自述，下載資料引自 Appark，未經第三方稽核；該篇發布本身就是 PR Newswire 付費稿。「使用者」是註冊數還是月活、下載量與留存的關係，公司都沒揭露。更重要的是：目前沒有任何第三方的準確率或臨床結果驗證，證據分級方法學也未經同儕審查。Vera Spaces 定價未公開，公司沒有已揭露的營收。 The 800,000 clinicians, 190 countries, download ranking and the claim of outdownloading OpenEvidence plus Doximity in September are all company-reported, with download data from Appark and no third-party audit; the release itself is paid distribution via PR Newswire. Whether a user is a registration or a monthly active, and how downloads relate to retention, is not disclosed. More importantly there is no third-party accuracy or clinical-outcome validation, and the evidence-grading methodology has not been peer reviewed. Vera Spaces pricing is undisclosed and the company reports no revenue.

當同業築牆，Zocdoc 開門：掛號網路正式對 AI agent 開放，agent 流量自 7 月起成長近 30 倍 While peers build walls, Zocdoc opens the door: its booking network now accepts AI agents, with agent traffic up nearly 30x since July

發生什麼What

10 月 28 日，Zocdoc 宣布經註冊與核可的 AI agent 可代病人搜尋醫師、查詢可約時段、驗證保險並完成預約或取消，前提是取得病人同意。Google Gemini、Amazon Health AI 與語音／簡訊 agent 新創 Instinct 已接上，其他 agent 可申請。條款禁止 agent 拿這些資料訓練模型、轉售或再發佈，但 Zocdoc 未說明如何稽核。平台上的 agent 流量自 7 月以來成長近 30 倍。創辦人暨執行長 Oliver Kharraz 稱這是「逆勢之舉」，並寫了一封公開信歡迎 agent 進來。 On October 28 Zocdoc said registered and approved AI agents may search for providers, check availability, verify insurance and book or cancel appointments on a patient's behalf, provided they obtain consent. Google Gemini, Amazon Health AI and Instinct, a voice-and-text agent startup, are already connected; others may apply. The terms bar agents from training on the data, reselling it or republishing it, though Zocdoc has not said how it will enforce that. Agent traffic on the platform is up nearly 30-fold since July. Founder and CEO Oliver Kharraz called the move "contrarian" and published an open letter welcoming agents in.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本週最清楚的「護城河重新定價」案例。Zocdoc 的資產從來不是演算法，而是透過 EHR 與排程系統整合、經過驗證的醫師網路；當 agent 開始代替病人上網找醫師，爬公開網頁得到的是過期的可約時段，走 Zocdoc 得到的是確定性的。公司 9 月才推出 Care Access Network（把排程技術嵌進第三方 App、保險公司網站與 AI 工具），10 月初併購自動化保險資格查核新創 Sohar Health——這是它近 20 年來的第一樁併購。三步棋連起來，是把自己定位成 agent 時代的基礎設施層而不是終端 App。 This is the week's clearest repricing of a moat. Zocdoc's asset was never an algorithm but a verified provider network wired into EHRs and scheduling systems; once agents start shopping for doctors on a patient's behalf, scraping the open web returns stale availability while Zocdoc returns a deterministic answer. The company launched Care Access Network in September, embedding its scheduling into third-party apps, insurer sites and AI tools, and in early October bought Sohar Health, which automates insurance eligibility checks — its first acquisition in nearly 20 years. Read together, the three moves position Zocdoc as infrastructure for the agent era rather than a consumer destination.

要打折的地方Discount this

「近 30 倍」沒有給基期絕對值，從極小的基數成長 30 倍並不代表規模。報導也未指明接取用的是 MCP、公開 API 或專屬介面，Zocdoc 未說明資料使用限制如何執行，也未揭露 agent 端如何分潤。 The "nearly 30x" figure comes with no absolute base, and thirtyfold growth from a tiny base says little about scale. The coverage does not specify whether the integration uses MCP, a public API or a bespoke interface, Zocdoc has not said how the data-use restrictions will be policed, and the revenue split with agent operators is undisclosed.

Tuesday's funding wave already showed capital moving toward the channel that delivers AI into existing organisations; this is the same logic executed on a public company's balance sheet.

發生什麼What

據 Politico Pro 報導並經 HIStalk 10/28 轉述，聯邦政府正撤回「含 AI 的健康資訊科技須附上列明安全與開發資訊的『營養標示』」這項要求。這與 10/26 定案的 HTI-5 終局規則同一方向，該規則主旨為降低 ONC 健康資訊科技認證計畫開發者的行政負擔，評論者 Eugene Vestel 認為關鍵的 EHR 內 AI 決策被延後或交還廠商自決。本報 10/28（星期三／法規）已詳述 HTI-5 的刪改條目與成本估算，此處只記產品面的後果。 Politico Pro reported, and HIStalk relayed on October 28, that the federal government is rolling back the requirement for AI-containing health IT to carry a "nutrition label" listing safety and development information. It runs with the grain of the HTI-5 final rule issued on October 26, which is framed around reducing administrative burden for developers in the ONC Health IT Certification Program and which commentator Eugene Vestel reads as deferring key in-EHR AI decisions or handing them back to vendors. This daily covered HTI-5's struck provisions and cost estimates in Wednesday's regulation edition; what is recorded here is only the product-side consequence.

為什麼重要Why it matters

把這條與今天的主線並讀才有意思：當法規不再強制揭露訓練資料、驗證方法與適用族群，產品自己選擇揭露多少，就成了差異化本身。Vera 把證據分級與色標做成使用者看得到的介面、公開宣稱不以醫師提問訓練模型並持有 SOC 2 Type II；這在有強制標示的世界只是合規，在沒有的世界是賣點。反過來說，這也意味著採購端再也不能靠一張官方標籤做比較，醫院得自己建立評估能力。 The item is worth reading against today's lead. Once disclosure of training data, validation method and intended population is no longer mandatory, how much a product chooses to disclose becomes differentiation in itself. Vera puts evidence grading and colour-coding in the interface where users see it, states publicly that it never trains on clinicians' queries, and holds SOC 2 Type II — compliance in a world with mandatory labels, a selling point in a world without. The flip side is that buyers can no longer compare products against an official label, so hospitals must build the evaluation capability themselves.

一項刊於 JAMA Network Open、針對 1,185 名美國成人的調查發現，面對處方續領之類的行政性詢問，57% 偏好五分鐘內得到 AI 回覆；但涉及症狀、治療或臨床疑慮時，63% 寧願等臨床人員審核過的答覆。受訪者即使要等更久，也偏好真人治療師。研究者的解讀是：願意拿人為監督換速度的程度，取決於問題的風險高低。 A survey of 1,185 US adults published in JAMA Network Open found that for logistical questions such as prescription refills, 57% preferred an AI response within five minutes; but for symptoms, treatment or clinical concerns, 63% would rather have a clinician-reviewed reply. Respondents preferred human therapists even at the cost of a longer wait. The authors read it as evidence that willingness to trade human oversight for speed scales with how much is at stake.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這組數字正好解釋了為什麼本週的錢都流向「醫師面」而非「病人面」的 AI。把答案直接交給病人，在行政問題上有市場、在臨床問題上沒有；把答案交給醫師、由醫師背書，兩端都成立。Vera 與 OpenEvidence 都賣給臨床人員、都把「可追溯到原始文獻」當成核心介面，不是巧合。 The figures explain why this week's money went to clinician-facing rather than patient-facing AI. Handing an answer straight to the patient has a market for logistics and none for clinical questions; handing it to a clinician who then underwrites it works at both ends. That Vera and OpenEvidence both sell to clinicians and both make traceability to the primary literature their core interface is not a coincidence.

02 — Product Analysis

兩套「問一句、回一段、附出處」的介面，背後是兩套完全不同的成本結構與分佈假設 Two interfaces that take a question and return a sourced paragraph — built on entirely different cost structures and assumptions about distribution

Vera Health

證據分級式臨床搜尋 · Vera Health（美國舊金山，2024 年創立）Evidence-graded clinical search · Vera Health (San Francisco, founded 2024)

功能與定位。索引逾 6,000 萬篇同儕審查文獻、臨床指引、共識聲明與 FDA／EMA 藥品仿單，先檢索、先評級，再生成答案。對全球持照臨床人員與實習醫師免費；企業版 Vera Spaces 讓醫院把自家醫囑集、處方集與抗生素敏感性報告併入檢索範圍，使結果反映在地實務。已與美國急診醫學會（ACEP）合作，把其臨床政策與決策樹嵌入並標註來源。 Function and position. Indexes more than 60 million peer-reviewed publications, clinical guidelines, consensus statements and FDA/EMA drug labels, retrieving and grading evidence before generating an answer. Free to licensed clinicians and trainees worldwide; the Vera Spaces enterprise tier lets hospitals fold their own order sets, formularies and antibiograms into the search so results reflect local practice. A partnership with the American College of Emergency Physicians embeds ACEP clinical policies and decision trees with attribution.

  • 優勢Strength ：把「不確定性」做成介面而不是免責聲明。每個主張依證據強度上色，薄證據（例如單一回溯性病例系列）標紅並說明侷限，ACEP 的 Scott M. Silvers 醫師說「大型隨機試驗與單一病例報告絕不該被賦予相同份量」。這解掉了通用聊天機器人在臨床場景最致命的問題：所有句子看起來一樣有自信。 : it turns uncertainty into interface rather than a disclaimer. Every claim is coloured by evidence strength, with thin support such as a small retrospective case series flagged red and its limitations shown; ACEP's Dr Scott M. Silvers puts it as "a large, randomized trial and a case report should never carry the same weight." That addresses the single most dangerous property of a general chatbot at the bedside: every sentence sounds equally confident.
  • 優勢Strength ：成本結構極輕。12 名員工、零企業行銷支出、成長靠同儕轉介，卻覆蓋 190 國。這讓它能服務廣告收入無法支撐的市場，剛好是對手結構上去不了的地方。 : the cost base is extremely light. Twelve employees, zero enterprise marketing spend, growth by peer referral — and 190 countries of coverage. That lets it serve markets no advertising revenue could support, which is precisely where its rival structurally cannot go.
  • 疑慮Concern ：沒有任何已公開的第三方準確率驗證或臨床結果研究。證據分級的權重如何設定、是否經過臨床驗證，公司未說明，方法學也未經同儕審查。對一個聲稱 80 萬人在用的臨床工具，這是最大的空白。 : there is no published third-party accuracy validation or clinical-outcome study. How the grading weights are set, and whether they have been clinically validated, is not described, and the methodology has not been peer reviewed. For a clinical tool claiming 800,000 users, that is the largest gap on the page.
  • 疑慮Concern ：沒有已揭露的營收。Vera Spaces 的定價、已簽約醫院數、續約率全部未公開；一個 12 人團隊要同時維護 6,000 萬篇文獻的索引、190 國的服務與企業部署，3,200 萬美元能撐多久是公開的問題。 : no disclosed revenue. Vera Spaces pricing, signed hospitals and renewal rates are all undisclosed. How long $32M funds a twelve-person team maintaining a 60-million-document index, service in 190 countries and enterprise deployments is an open question.

OpenEvidence

廣告支撐的臨床問答平台 · OpenEvidence（美國邁阿密，2022 年創立）Ad-funded clinical answer platform · OpenEvidence (Miami, founded 2022)

功能與定位。對經驗證的美國醫師免費，營收來自藥廠與醫材廣告。授權內容包含 NEJM、JAMA 系列、AMA、NCCN、AAFP 與 ACEP，產品線含 DeepConsult（2025 年 7 月，跨研究綜整的推理模式）與 2026 年 5 月上線的語音模式；同年 5 月與 Cedars-Sinai 合作整合進其 EHR，可帶病人情境查詢。9 月再宣布與 Memorial Sloan Kettering 整合進 Epic 流程並納入 OncoKB，同時宣告要用 MSK 與 NCCN 的資料自建藥物開發管線。 Function and position. Free to verified US physicians and funded by pharmaceutical and medical-device advertising. Licensed content spans NEJM, the JAMA network, the AMA, NCCN, AAFP and ACEP; the product line includes DeepConsult (July 2025, a reasoning mode that synthesises across studies) and a voice mode launched in May 2026, alongside a May 2026 Cedars-Sinai integration into that hospital's EHR allowing patient-context queries. In September it added a Memorial Sloan Kettering integration into Epic with OncoKB folded in, and announced it will build its own drug pipeline on MSK and NCCN data.

  • 優勢Strength ：分佈與單位經濟學都已經跑起來。Sacra 估 2026 年 7 月年化營收約 3 億美元（2025 年底 1.5 億、2024 年底 790 萬），毛利率約 90%；2026 年 1 月月諮詢量約 2,000 萬次，一年前約 300 萬次。免費讓它繞過醫院採購週期，這是對手們花兩年都沒走完的路。 : distribution and unit economics are both already running. Sacra estimates roughly $300M annualized revenue as of July 2026, up from $150M at end-2025 and $7.9M at end-2024, at about 90% gross margin, with about 20 million consultations a month in January 2026 against roughly 3 million a year earlier. Being free let it bypass hospital procurement cycles entirely — the road rivals spend two years failing to finish.
  • 優勢Strength ：廣告庫存遠未售罄，代表營收還有結構性空間。CPM 逾 1,000 美元，但僅約 5% 版位售出。換言之，這個估值押的不是「再多拉多少醫師」，而是「同一批醫師的注意力還能賣幾倍」。 : ad inventory is far from sold out, so revenue has structural headroom. CPMs run above $1,000 while only about 5% of inventory is sold. The valuation is therefore a bet not on recruiting more physicians but on how many times over the same physicians' attention can be sold.
  • 疑慮Concern ：藥廠廣告養的「免費實證醫學」是一個結構性的利益衝突，產品端至今沒有公開的隔離機制說明——廣告如何與答案內容分離、藥廠能否針對特定臨床查詢投放，都沒有可查證的揭露。而在聯邦撤回 AI 揭露標示之後，也不會有人強制它說明。 : free evidence-based medicine funded by pharmaceutical advertising is a structural conflict of interest, and there is no published account of the separation — how ads are kept apart from answer content, or whether advertisers can target specific clinical queries, is not disclosed in any verifiable form. With the federal AI disclosure label withdrawn, nothing will compel that account either.
  • 疑慮Concern ：三個已被點名的風險都還沒解。Sacra 列出：建議導致不良結果的醫療責任與法規限制；企業擴張高度依賴 Epic 與 Oracle-Cerner，對方可封鎖整合或要求分潤；NEJM、JAMA、Wiley、Cochrane 等授權費上漲會直接壓縮廣告養免費的模式。另外，自建藥物管線（Nadler 稱首個藥物預計 2026 年底前進入臨床試驗，隔年再推 3–5 個，初期聚焦罕見癌症）把公司從軟體風險帶進臨床開發風險，是完全不同的資本與時間尺度。 : the three named risks are all unresolved. Sacra lists malpractice and regulatory exposure if a recommendation contributes to an adverse outcome; heavy dependence on Epic and Oracle-Cerner for enterprise expansion, either of which could block integrations or demand revenue share; and rising content-licensing fees from NEJM, JAMA, Wiley and Cochrane squeezing the ad-funded free model. Building an in-house drug pipeline also moves the company from software risk into clinical development risk, on an entirely different capital and time scale.

兩者真正的分歧不在模型，在「誰付錢讓醫師免費」。OpenEvidence 的答案是美國藥廠，於是它的地理邊界等於藥廠廣告預算的邊界，產品必須持續把醫師留在自己的介面裡才有版位可賣；Vera 的答案目前是創投，未來押在醫院買 Vera Spaces，於是它的邊界是「醫院願不願意為在地化檢索付費」。前者已經證明營收，後者只證明分佈。接下來 12 個月值得盯的是兩個數字：OpenEvidence 的 5% 版位售出率有沒有往上走，以及 Vera 有沒有公布第一個付費醫院客戶。 The real divergence is not the model but who pays so that doctors do not have to. OpenEvidence's answer is US pharma, so its geographic boundary is the boundary of pharma ad budgets, and the product must keep physicians inside its own interface to have inventory to sell. Vera's answer is venture capital for now and hospitals buying Vera Spaces later, so its boundary is whether hospitals will pay for localised retrieval. The first has proved revenue; the second has proved only distribution. Two numbers are worth watching over the next twelve months: whether OpenEvidence's 5% sell-through rises, and whether Vera names a first paying hospital.

04 — Taiwan Angle

台灣醫師早就在用這類工具，只是用的是別人為別的市場做的版本 Taiwanese physicians already use these tools — just versions built for somebody else's market

(1) 台灣已經是這條賽道的使用者，卻不是它的目標市場。臺北榮總圖書館早在 2024 年 11 月就把 OpenEvidence 列為館藏推薦的 AI 臨床決策輔助平台，高雄榮總圖書館也有同樣的資源推薦。但 OpenEvidence 的免費是美國藥廠廣告買單的——台灣醫師是這個模式的淨成本，不是收入來源，產品策略上沒有理由為台灣做在地化（健保給付規則、台灣藥品品項、本土臨床指引）。今天這則新聞真正對台灣有意義的地方在於：Vera 的 190 國免費模式，結構上是少數會把台灣算進去的打法，而它的企業版正是「醫院把自家處方集與抗生素敏感性報告併進檢索」——那恰好是台灣醫學中心最缺、也最不可能等外商來做的一層。 (1) Taiwan is already a user of this market without being a target of it. The Taipei Veterans General Hospital library listed OpenEvidence as a recommended AI clinical decision support platform as early as November 2024, and Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital's library carries the same recommendation. But OpenEvidence is free because US pharmaceutical advertisers pay for it — Taiwanese physicians are a net cost in that model, not a revenue source, so there is no product reason to localise for Taiwan's reimbursement rules, drug formulary or domestic guidelines. What makes today's story matter here is that Vera's free-in-190-countries structure is one of the few that counts Taiwan in, and its enterprise tier is precisely "let the hospital fold its own formulary and antibiogram into the search" — the layer Taiwan's medical centres most lack and are least likely to get from a foreign vendor unprompted.

(2) 本土已經有人在做同一件事，規模小但命題正確。醫知彼科技的 A-Pen AI 於 2026 年 6 月 InnoVEX 宣布對完成醫事執業證照驗證的執業醫師提供不限次數、不限 Token 的免費方案，內容整合 NEJM、The Lancet、Nature 等國際期刊，並結合台灣健保制度、在地藥品資料與其平台上 8.8 萬名醫事人員的臨床討論紀錄；公司今年初完成 5,000 萬元 A 輪，由日商 DCI 合夥株式會社參與。同一篇報導引述該公司 2025 年調查：66% 醫師已在臨床工作中使用 AI 工具、57% 認為最有價值的用途是減輕行政與資訊整理負擔、68% 認為有實際助益但高度關注資訊正確性、來源可信度與責任歸屬。「責任歸屬」這三個字，正是 Vera 把證據強度做成色標、OpenEvidence 砸錢買期刊授權想解的同一個問題——只是台灣這邊的資本量級差了三個數量級。 (2) Someone in Taiwan is already building the same thing, small but pointed at the right problem. A-Pen AI, from the Taiwanese company behind the country's largest verified medical-professional community app, announced at InnoVEX in June 2026 an unlimited free tier, with no token cap, for physicians who verify their practising licence — combining NEJM, The Lancet and Nature content with Taiwan's National Health Insurance rules, local drug data and the clinical discussions of 88,000 medical professionals on its platform; it closed an NT$50M Series A earlier this year with Japan's DCI Partners participating. The same report cites the company's 2025 survey: 66% of physicians already use AI tools clinically, 57% say the most valuable use is relieving administrative and information-gathering load, and 68% see real benefit while remaining highly concerned about accuracy, source credibility and where responsibility lands. That last concern is exactly what Vera's evidence colour-coding and OpenEvidence's expensive journal licences are each trying to answer — Taiwan is simply doing it with three orders of magnitude less capital.

(3) 給付才是台灣這條賽道的真正瓶頸。衛福部已啟動台灣 AI 影響性研究中心，導入 AI 的臨床試驗要供健保共擬會議給付參考，並明示AI 醫療要取得給付必須先證明能替健保省錢，目前已有項目進入臨床試驗。這一點恰好讓今天的對比有了本地意義：臨床問答工具是最難證明「省下多少健保支出」的一類產品——它改善的是醫師的決策品質與查找時間，不直接對應任何一個給付代碼。也就是說，在台灣，這類工具短期內只可能走「醫院自付或廠商免費」兩條路，而不是健保。醫院端要評估的，就不是給付，而是今天第八則提醒的那件事：不提供合規工具，醫師會自己上傳試算表。 (3) Reimbursement is the real bottleneck for this market in Taiwan. The Ministry of Health and Welfare has stood up a national AI impact research centre whose clinical trials of AI deployment are meant to inform National Health Insurance joint reimbursement deliberations, and has said plainly that medical AI must first show it saves the insurance system money to win payment, with some items already in clinical trials. That gives today's contrast local bite, because a clinical answer engine is among the hardest products to prove savings for: it improves decision quality and lookup time without mapping onto any reimbursement code. In Taiwan, in other words, these tools will for now travel either on hospital budgets or on a vendor's free tier, not on insurance. What hospitals have to weigh is therefore not payment but the warning in today's eighth story: if you do not supply a compliant tool, clinicians will upload the spreadsheet themselves.
