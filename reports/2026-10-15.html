病人用手機跟自己腦中的 EEG 對話：13 人、1,300 多則訊息，偵測誤報降約 80% Patients text the EEG inside their own head: 13 people, 1,300-plus messages, false detections down about 80%

發生什麼What

賓州大學團隊在癲癇監測病房做了一項概念驗證研究，讓 13 名接受連續顱內 EEG 監測的成年病人，透過手機上的對話式代理直接與監測系統往來：詢問自己的腦波、要求視覺化、回報症狀、每天三次填寫睡眠與情緒問卷，並親手把系統偵測到的發作標記為真、假或不確定。研究 10 月 13 日刊於 Nature Medicine（DOI: 10.1038/s41591-026-04680-4）。NIH/NINDS 當天的新聞稿列出的數字是：病人與系統交換逾 1,300 則訊息，發作偵測誤報在研究期間下降約 80%，專家回饋再降 2%，系統對腦波提問給出準確且切題回覆的比例逾 92%，病人標記了逾 67% 的即時偵測結果。 A University of Pennsylvania team ran a proof-of-concept study in an epilepsy monitoring unit in which 13 adults on continuous intracranial EEG dealt with the monitoring system directly through a conversational agent on a phone: asking about their own brain activity, requesting visualisations, reporting symptoms, answering surveys three times a day on sleep and mood, and labelling detected seizures themselves as true, false or uncertain. The study appeared on 13 October in Nature Medicine (DOI: 10.1038/s41591-026-04680-4). The NIH/NINDS release that day gives the figures: more than 1,300 messages exchanged, false seizure-detection alarms down about 80% over the study, a further 2% from expert feedback, over 92% of brain-activity questions answered accurately and relevantly, and more than 67% of real-time detections labelled by patients.

為什麼重要Why it matters

醫材上的 AI 這幾年主要被當成「給醫師看的偵測器」，這份研究把病人放進了訓練迴圈本身——誤報的下降不是來自更大的模型，而是來自病人每天回饋哪一次偵測是假的。資深作者 Brian Litt 在 NIH 新聞稿裡的那句「這些裝置首先是為醫師做的」，正是這篇要翻轉的前提；共同第一作者 Zack Goldblum 補的那句更直接：「好幾位病人完全沒被提示，就主動問能不能把這套系統帶回家。」這也是 Penn Medicine 自己的說明點名下一步要延伸到心律裝置、胰島素幫浦與帕金森氏症深部腦刺激的理由。 AI on medical devices has mostly been built as a detector for clinicians to read. This study puts the patient inside the training loop itself: the drop in false alarms comes not from a larger model but from patients saying, day by day, which detections were wrong. Senior author Brian Litt's line in the NIH release — "these devices are made first and foremost for doctors" — is precisely the premise the paper inverts, and co-first author Zack Goldblum's is blunter: "Completely unprompted, several patients asked if they could take the system home with them." It is also why Penn's own write-up names cardiac devices, insulin pumps and deep brain stimulation for Parkinson's as the next targets.

要打折的地方Discount this

13 人、住院環境、概念驗證。研究沒有報告發作偵測本身的絕對準確度指標，對發作頻率、生活品質、急診次數與醫療使用量的影響都還在評估中。「個人化系統將只需要偶爾的臨床監督」是作者的展望，不是這份資料證明的結論。經費為 NIH Pioneer Award（DP1NS122038，約 560 萬美元）等多筆 NINDS 補助。 Thirteen people, an inpatient setting, a proof of concept. The study reports no absolute accuracy metric for seizure detection itself, and effects on seizure frequency, quality of life, emergency visits and healthcare use are still being evaluated. The claim that personalised systems will need only occasional clinical supervision is the authors' projection, not a result this dataset demonstrates. Funding is an NIH Pioneer Award (DP1NS122038, about US$5.6 million) plus other NINDS grants.

評測落差Benchmark gap NYU LangonearXiv10/08

127,833 筆真實臨床對話對上 58 個公開 benchmark：中位數只覆蓋三成任務，而且完全沒有文書 127,833 real clinician conversations against 58 public benchmarks: the median covers three-tenths of the task mix, and no documentation at all

發生什麼What

NYU Langone 與 NYU Tandon、Stern、Grossman 等單位組成的團隊，取 2025 年 8 月 12 日至 2026 年 4 月 3 日一家學術醫療體系內部安全 LLM 助理的 127,833 筆對話（成功標註 127,625 筆），來自 6,342 名臨床人員、35 個專科，與 58 個公開 benchmark（共 10,659 個標註題目）逐項比對。論文 10 月 8 日上傳 arXiv:2610.11069。真實使用的組成是：文書與行政 36.2%、知識檢索 28.9%、診斷僅 3.7%；護理師與進階執業人員約占一半查詢量。36.1% 的提問以原本問法無法被好好回答，22.2% 缺少病人或在地資訊。而中位數 benchmark 只共享 31% 的真實任務組合；號稱貼近臨床的 benchmark 套組（中位 35%）並沒有比前沿模型技術報告裡的那些（中位 40%）更接近真實。 A team spanning NYU Langone and NYU's Tandon, Stern and Grossman schools took 127,833 conversations (127,625 successfully annotated) logged between 12 August 2025 and 3 April 2026 in one academic health system's secure LLM assistant, from 6,342 clinicians across 35 specialties, and compared them item by item against 58 public benchmarks totalling 10,659 labelled items. The paper went up on 8 October as arXiv:2610.11069. Real use breaks down as 36.2% documentation and administration, 28.9% knowledge retrieval and just 3.7% diagnosis, with nurses and advanced practice providers accounting for about half of queries. 36.1% of queries could not be answered well as posed and 22.2% needed missing patient or local information. The median benchmark shares only 31% of the real task mix — and suites designed to resemble clinical practice (median 35%) sit no closer to real use than those from frontier model reports (median 40%).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是目前把「benchmark 分數」與「臨床實際怎麼用」放在同一張表上、規模最大的一次對照，而且差距不是程度問題而是種類問題：診斷在真實使用裡只有 3.7%，在 MedQA 與腳本一致性測驗裡卻幾乎是全部，後兩者分別有 97.6% 與 97.7% 的題目最安全的回應就是直接作答——真實臨床裡那個比例是 61.2%，另有 26.8% 的最佳回應是反問澄清。換句話說，業界用來排名的題庫，系統性地獎勵了一種在病房裡只占六成場合才正確的行為。 This is the largest side-by-side yet of benchmark scores and actual clinical use, and the gap is one of kind rather than degree. Diagnosis is 3.7% of real use but nearly all of MedQA and a script concordance test, where 97.6% and 97.7% of items are safest answered directly — against 61.2% in real practice, with another 26.8% best served by asking a clarifying question first. The question banks the field ranks itself on systematically reward a behaviour that is correct in only about six of ten situations on the ward.

要打折的地方Discount this

單一學術醫療體系、單一內部助理工具，任務組成未必能外推到社區醫院或其他國家。潛在傷害的 13.2%（高或危急）是作者自承的保守上限，臨床醫師共識下約為 3.7%，兩個數字差 3.6 倍，引用時務必標明是哪一個。這是 preprint，尚未經同儕審查。 One academic health system, one internal assistant: the task mix may not transfer to community hospitals or other countries. The 13.2% rated high or critical for potential harm is the authors' own cautious upper bound; under clinician consensus it is about 3.7% — a 3.6-fold spread, so cite which one you mean. This is a preprint and has not been peer reviewed.

部署後評估Post-deployment Nature MedicineStanford10/14

「逾 90% 已部署、53% 以下覺得好用」：Nature Medicine 十月號把部署後的漂移與放棄當成整期主題 "Over 90% deploying, 53% or fewer finding it useful": the October Nature Medicine builds a whole issue around drift and abandonment after go-live

發生什麼What

10 月 14 日線上刊出的 Nature Medicine 社論〈Making AI work in healthcare〉（第 32 卷第 10 期，第 3499 頁）替整期的部署專題定調，核心句是「醫療 AI 的價值取決於它是否帶來更好的照護，而不只是更好的表現」。社論引述的調查顯示逾 90% 的美國醫療體系已開始部署 AI 工具，但只有 53% 以下給出高度有用的評價；同時點名美國 FDA 已有在正式授權前先行部署臨床決策支援工具的試辦計畫，以及中國 DeepSeek 釋出後據報逾 800 家醫療機構導入。同期由史丹佛 Nigam Shah 等人撰寫的 ChatEHR 部署經驗（8 月 10 日線上、十月號第 3512–3515 頁）直言 benchmark 式評估「不足以監測與評估」臨床人員與 LLM 的實際互動，並附上一張部署期間未獲支持陳述的分析圖。社論也記下一次試辦評估中，臨床人員的採用率在四週內隨投入度下降而下滑。 The Nature Medicine editorial "Making AI work in healthcare", online 14 October (vol 32, issue 10, p 3499), sets the frame for a deployment-themed issue, and its thesis is that "the value of medical AI will depend on whether it helps deliver better care, not just better performance." The survey it cites has over 90% of US health systems beginning to deploy AI tools while 53% or fewer rate them highly useful; it also notes the FDA's pilot for deploying clinical decision support before authorisation, and reports of more than 800 healthcare institutions deploying China's DeepSeek after release. In the same issue, Stanford's Nigam Shah and colleagues report lessons from deploying ChatEHR (online 10 August, pp 3512–3515), stating plainly that benchmark-based evaluation was "insufficient for monitoring and evaluating" clinician-driven interactions with the model, with a figure analysing unsupported claims made during deployment. The editorial also records a pilot in which clinician adoption declined over four weeks as engagement fell away.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這一期把三種失敗模式收在同一個封面底下，而它們都不是模型不準：模型版本更迭與病歷書寫習慣改變造成的效能漂移（Shah 等）、把演算法嵌進照護路徑每一段才可行（Yan 等的中國眼科 AI 系統）、以及同一套糖尿病視網膜病變 AI 在印度、泰國、澳洲需要三種不同導入策略（Tiwari 等）。社論同時記下美國 ADVOCATE 計畫投入 6,270 萬美元，要部署心血管自主 AI 醫材並研究其真實世界影響——這是把「部署後證據」當成一個需要專門預算的研究類別來做。 The issue gathers three failure modes under one cover, none of which is an inaccurate model: performance drift caused by model iterations and changing documentation habits (Shah et al.), an algorithm that only became feasible once embedded at every stage of the care pathway (Yan et al.'s eye-care AI in China), and one diabetic retinopathy system needing three different implementation strategies across India, Thailand and Australia (Tiwari et al.). The editorial also notes US$62.7 million for the US ADVOCATE programme to deploy autonomous cardiovascular AI devices and study their real-world impact — post-deployment evidence treated as a research category with its own budget line.

要打折的地方Discount this

社論是觀點文章，不是新資料；「逾 90%／≤53%」來自 Poon 等人 2025 年刊於 JAMA 系統期刊的調查，是受訪者自評而非量測結果。DeepSeek 的 800 家機構數字，社論引用的是新聞媒體（Global Times 等）而非官方統計。ChatEHR 全文在付費牆後，本報只根據可公開瀏覽的摘要、圖說與書目資料撰寫。 The editorial is a viewpoint, not new data; the "over 90% / 53% or fewer" figures come from a Poon et al. 2025 survey in a JAMA-network journal and are respondent self-assessments, not measurements. The 800-institution DeepSeek figure is sourced by the editorial to news outlets (Global Times among them), not official statistics. The ChatEHR paper is paywalled; this report draws only on the publicly visible abstract, figure caption and bibliographic record.

語音模型Speech model SpeechmaticsOak10/14

Speechmatics 推出醫療語音模型 Oak：不用 WER 當指標，改量「醫學名詞召回率」，自稱 91.1% 對上同業平均 74.3% Speechmatics ships Oak for medical speech and drops WER for medical-term recall: a claimed 91.1% against a 74.3% peer average

發生什麼What

英國劍橋的 Speechmatics 於 10 月 14 日發表醫療語音辨識模型 Oak，主打臨床現場最常見卻最難處理的情況：不預先指定語言、說話者在同一句話中切換語言。官方數字為支援 55 種語言、在英語加 10 種常用語言上測得醫學名詞召回率 91.1%（其他八家供應商平均約 74.3%）、西班牙語 96.3%（同業平均 86.8%）、一小時音檔 18 秒內轉寫完成。模型可雲端使用或在醫療機構環境內自建部署，自建部署以 NVIDIA Dynamo-Triton 提供服務。 Cambridge-based Speechmatics released Oak on 14 October, a medical speech recognition model aimed at the hardest ordinary case in a clinic: no preset language, and speakers switching languages mid-sentence. The company reports 55 supported languages, 91.1% medical-term recall across English plus ten commonly used languages (against roughly 74.3% for eight other providers), 96.3% on Spanish (peer average 86.8%), and an hour of audio transcribed in under 18 seconds. Oak runs in the cloud or self-hosted inside a health organisation's environment, served with NVIDIA Dynamo-Triton.

為什麼重要Why it matters

真正值得注意的不是分數，是換了尺。Speechmatics 公開主張字錯率（WER）對高風險醫療用途不夠嚴謹，因為漏掉一個藥名或劑量跟漏掉一個虛詞在 WER 裡權重相同。這跟今天另外兩則的方向一致：當評測指標跟真實後果對不上，先改指標。對照組很清楚——Google 今年 1 月發表的開源 MedASR 走的正是 WER 路線（胸部 X 光口述 5.2% 對 Whisper large-v3 的 12.5%，內部多專科口述 5.2% 對 28.2%）。兩家在量不同的東西，買方得自己決定哪一個對得上自己的風險。 The number is not the news; the ruler is. Speechmatics argues publicly that word error rate is not rigorous enough for high-stakes medical use, because dropping a drug name and dropping a function word weigh the same in WER. That runs with the other two stories today: when the metric does not match the consequence, change the metric first. The contrast is sharp — Google's open MedASR, released in January, reports exactly on WER (5.2% on chest X-ray dictation against 12.5% for Whisper large-v3; 5.2% against 28.2% on an internal multi-specialty set). The two are measuring different things, and buyers have to decide which one maps onto their own risk.

要打折的地方Discount this

全部數字都出自 Speechmatics 自家 9 月 29 日的內部研究（19 組測試集、乾淨與吵雜音檔、各家預設設定、不加自訂詞彙），對照組為 AWS、AssemblyAI、Deepgram、ElevenLabs、Google、Microsoft、OpenAI、Soniox，未經第三方稽核，也未揭露訓練資料。供應商自選指標、自建測試集、自己宣布勝出，這件事本身就是今天這一期要討論的問題的一部分。 Every figure comes from Speechmatics' own internal study dated 29 September (19 test sets, clean and noisy audio, each provider's default settings, no custom vocabulary), with AWS, AssemblyAI, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI and Soniox as comparators. None of it is independently audited and no training data is disclosed. A vendor choosing the metric, building the test set and announcing the win is itself an instance of the problem this issue is about.

Worth watching is who ends up owning the channel. Epic used its August users' meeting to announce Agent Factory and Curiosity, a model trained on Cosmos, with release as an integrated EHR component planned for March 2027. Until then, third-party evidence tools are taking up position on the same screen.

This is a deployment story, not a results story; in the same week the Nature Medicine editorial records adoption declining over four weeks as engagement fell, the missing adoption curve is worth noting.

用「虛擬孿生」當對照組：Houston Methodist 量到心胸外科手術量增加 7%、每月約多 25 例 A virtual twin as the control arm: Houston Methodist measures a 7% rise in cardiothoracic case volume, about 25 extra cases a month

發生什麼What

Houston Methodist 在 15 間手術室的高複雜度心胸外科區導入 Apella 的影像式 AI（自動辨識圍手術期事件，餵給儀表板、預測性排程工具與報表），並以合成對照法評估：用院內其他尚未導入該技術的手術室加權混合，建出一個在上線前六個月能追蹤該區手術量的「虛擬孿生」。Healthcare IT News 10 月 9 日的報導指出，上線後十個月手術量上升 7%、約相當於每月多 25 例，病例組成維持穩定、總手術時間幾乎未變、周轉時間下降，相對於合成對照的延遲開刀中位數每例減少約 2.7 分鐘（作者自稱為探索性發現，部分次要指標未達統計顯著）。 Houston Methodist put Apella's image-based AI — which automatically identifies perioperative events and feeds dashboards, predictive scheduling and reports — into a high-acuity 15-room cardiothoracic suite, and evaluated it with a synthetic control: a weighted blend of other in-house ORs that had not adopted the technology, calibrated to track the suite's volume over the six months before go-live. Healthcare IT News reported on 9 October that case volume rose 7% over the following ten months, about 25 extra cases a month, with case mix stable, total operative time essentially unchanged, turnover times down, and median late-start minutes about 2.7 lower per case than the synthetic control — which the authors call exploratory, with some secondary measures not reaching significance.

為什麼重要Why it matters

在一個很難做隨機分派的場域（你不能把一半手術室分到對照組），合成對照提供了一條可操作的中間路線，而且它量的是醫院財務長聽得懂的單位：手術量。這一週所有關於「部署後要量什麼」的討論裡，這是少數真的把方法學寫出來的案例。副主題也很誠實：導入 AI 之後，手術沒有變快（總手術時間幾乎未變），多出來的量來自排程與周轉。 In a setting where randomisation is close to impossible — you cannot assign half your operating rooms to a control arm — a synthetic control offers a workable middle path, and it measures in a unit a hospital CFO understands: case volume. Among this week's talk about what to measure after go-live, it is one of the few cases where the method is actually written down. The sub-finding is honest too: surgery did not get faster (total operative time barely moved); the extra volume came from scheduling and turnover.

要打折的地方Discount this

單一醫院、單一科別、非隨機。合成對照的有效性完全取決於「虛擬孿生」是否真的捕捉到反事實，而同期其他營運改變（人力、排程政策、個案來源）無法被完全排除；報導本身也未把它描述為同儕審查研究。延遲開刀那個 2.7 分鐘是作者自己標為探索性的。 One hospital, one service line, not randomised. A synthetic control is only as good as the counterfactual the virtual twin captures, and concurrent operational changes — staffing, scheduling policy, referral patterns — cannot be fully excluded; the report does not describe it as a peer-reviewed study. The 2.7-minute late-start figure is flagged exploratory by the authors themselves.

02 — Product Analysis

今天這兩個產品做同一件事（把臨床語音轉成字），卻拒絕用同一把尺量自己——這組對比把整期的主題縮到了一張表上。 Two products doing the same job — turning clinical speech into text — that refuse to measure themselves with the same ruler. The contrast compresses the whole issue onto one page.

Speechmatics Oak

閉源商用醫療語音辨識 · Speechmatics（英國劍橋）Closed commercial medical ASR · Speechmatics (Cambridge, UK)

功能與定位。支援 55 種語言、不需預先指定語言、容許句中語碼轉換的醫療語音辨識模型，賣給需要在多語病人族群中做臨床文書的醫療機構，可雲端或機構內自建部署（自建以 NVIDIA Dynamo-Triton 服務），並列出 HIPAA、SOC 2 Type II、ISO/IEC 27001:2022 與 GDPR 合規，發表於 10 月 14 日。 Function and position. A medical speech recognition model covering 55 languages with no preset language and tolerance for mid-sentence code switching, sold to health organisations documenting care across multilingual patient populations, available in the cloud or self-hosted inside the organisation (served with NVIDIA Dynamo-Triton), and listing HIPAA, SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and GDPR compliance, announced 14 October.

  • 優勢Strength ：指標選得比對手誠實。把評估從字錯率換成醫學名詞召回率，直接對應「漏掉藥名或劑量」這個真實後果，官方報 91.1% 對同業平均 74.3%。 : the metric is the honest part. Swapping word error rate for medical-term recall maps directly onto the consequence that matters — a missed drug name or dose — with 91.1% claimed against a 74.3% peer average.
  • 優勢Strength ：語碼轉換是台灣、新加坡、美國移民社區這類場域每天都在發生、而單語模型每天都在漏的情況，西班牙語 96.3% 對 86.8% 的差距說明這個缺口確實存在。 : code switching is a daily occurrence in Taiwan, Singapore and US immigrant communities, and a daily failure for monolingual models — the 96.3% against 86.8% gap on Spanish says the hole is real.
  • 疑慮Concern ：自選指標、自建測試集、自行公布勝出，無第三方稽核、無訓練資料揭露。換尺可以讓評估更貼近臨床，也可以讓對手的強項消失在表格外——目前沒有任何獨立資料能分辨是哪一種。 : vendor-chosen metric, vendor-built test sets, vendor-announced win, no independent audit and no disclosed training data. Changing the ruler can bring evaluation closer to the clinic, or make a rival's strength vanish off the table — and no independent data currently distinguishes the two.
  • 疑慮Concern ：11 種語言之外的另外 44 種沒有公布數字；對台灣最關鍵的中文與台語表現，官方新聞稿沒有提供任何拆解。 : no figures are published for the other 44 of its 55 languages, and the release breaks out nothing for Mandarin or Taiwanese Hokkien — the two that matter most for Taiwan.

Google MedASR + MedGemma 1.5

開放權重醫療模型家族 · Google Research（美國）Open-weight medical model family · Google Research (US)

功能與定位。MedASR 是針對醫療口述微調的開放語音辨識模型，MedGemma 1.5 4B 是可離線執行的小型開放多模態醫療模型，兩者皆於 1 月 13 日發布、經 Hugging Face 與 Vertex AI 提供，官方說明維持研究與商業用途免費。定位不是賣終端產品，而是讓醫療機構與廠商自己拿去微調。 Function and position. MedASR is an open speech recognition model fine-tuned for medical dictation; MedGemma 1.5 4B is a small open multimodal medical model that runs offline. Both shipped on 13 January through Hugging Face and Vertex AI, and Google states they remain free for research and commercial use. The play is not a finished product but a base for health organisations and vendors to fine-tune.

  • 優勢Strength ：權重開放、可離線、可自行驗證。MedASR 在胸部 X 光口述的字錯率 5.2%，對照 Whisper large-v3 的 12.5%；內部多專科口述 5.2% 對 28.2%——這些數字別人可以自己重跑，Oak 的不行。 : open weights, offline-capable, independently verifiable. MedASR reports 5.2% word error rate on chest X-ray dictation against 12.5% for Whisper large-v3, and 5.2% against 28.2% on an internal multi-specialty set — figures anyone can rerun, which is not true of Oak's.
  • 優勢Strength ：同一家族涵蓋影像與文字，MedGemma 1.5 4B 相對前代在 MRI 病灶分類 65% 對 51%、胸部 X 光解剖定位 IoU 38% 對 3%、EHRQA 90% 對 68%，讓「語音進來、影像與病歷一起處理」可以在同一套開放堆疊裡做完。 : one family spans images and text. MedGemma 1.5 4B against its predecessor reports 65% versus 51% on MRI finding classification, 38% versus 3% IoU on chest X-ray anatomical localisation and 90% versus 68% on EHRQA — so speech in, images and chart handled together, all inside one open stack.
  • 疑慮Concern ：WER 正是 Speechmatics 指控不適用於高風險醫療的那個指標，Google 沒有公布任何以醫學名詞為單位的召回率，兩家因此無法直接比較——這對買方是真實的資訊缺口，不是修辭。 : WER is precisely the metric Speechmatics says is unfit for high-stakes medical use, and Google publishes no term-level recall at all, so the two cannot be compared directly. For a buyer that is a real information gap, not a rhetorical one.
  • 疑慮Concern ：Google 自己在同一篇裡註明這些模型是起點，使用前需驗證與調整，輸出不應直接影響臨床決策——開放權重把驗證成本轉嫁給導入方，而這正是 Nature Medicine 社論說大多數醫療體系還沒有能力做的事。 : Google states in the same post that these are starting points needing validation and adaptation, and that outputs should not directly inform clinical decisions. Open weights shift the validation cost onto the adopter — exactly the capability the Nature Medicine editorial says most health systems do not yet have.

把兩邊併起來看。Oak 的賣點是「我們量了你真正在乎的東西」，但你只能相信它；MedASR 的賣點是「你可以自己量」，但它量的是一個對臨床後果加權錯誤的指標。這不是誰比較好的問題，而是今天這期的核心命題在產品層的具體化：指標的可信度，現在由誰能重跑它決定。 Side by side. Oak's pitch is "we measured the thing you actually care about" — and you have to take that on trust. MedASR's pitch is "you can measure it yourself" — on a metric that weights clinical consequence wrongly. This is not a question of which is better; it is today's central claim made concrete at product level: a metric's credibility now rests on who is able to rerun it.

At the top sits the channel (Epic), which need prove nothing and simply wait for March 2027. They sell no models, but they are setting the questions buyers will ask next.

04 — Taiwan Angle

台灣已經把錢和算力押在代理型 AI 上，今天缺的那一塊剛好是這三篇論文在補的：部署之後怎麼量。 Taiwan has already put money and compute behind agentic AI; the piece it is missing is the one these three papers supply — how to measure after go-live.

(1) 部署規模已經到位，量測框架還沒有。NVIDIA 6 月 1 日的公告記載，台灣政府投入 15 億美元打造受監管的主權 AI 原生醫療體系，鴻海以生態系整合者身分與長庚、高醫大附設中和紀念醫院、馬偕、台大醫院、台中榮總、台北榮總等醫學中心部署代理團隊（CoDoctor 平台下的 ECG、Corovia 冠狀動脈重建、Endovia 大腸鏡即時病灶偵測，加上手術室 Scrub Bot 與護理協作機器人 Nurabot），並稱台灣醫學中心的 AI 系統每年涵蓋逾 1,400 萬人次就醫、已有 85 項經 FDA 或 TFDA 核准的醫療 AI 解決方案（NVIDIA 台灣官方部落格, 2026-06-01）。這個部署密度，正好讓今天 Nature Medicine 十月號談的三種失敗模式——效能漂移、採用率衰退、同一套系統在不同院所需要不同導入策略——全部適用；而公告裡列出的數字（Nurabot 每天省 2 至 3 小時、導航精確度 98%、Corovia 把兩小時流程縮到一分鐘、模擬優先縮短部署時間 40%）都是廠商自述的能力值，不是部署後的成效量測。 (1) The deployment scale is in place; the measurement framework is not. NVIDIA's 1 June announcement records US$1.5 billion of Taiwanese government money behind a regulated, sovereign AI-native health system, with Foxconn as ecosystem integrator deploying agent teams across Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, Kaohsiung Medical University Chung-Ho Memorial Hospital, MacKay Memorial Hospital, National Taiwan University Hospital, Taichung Veterans General Hospital and Taipei Veterans General Hospital — ECG, Corovia coronary reconstruction and Endovia real-time colonoscopy lesion detection on the CoDoctor platform, plus the Scrub Bot in theatre and the Nurabot nursing robot. It states that AI systems in Taiwan's medical centres cover more than 14 million patient encounters a year and that 85 medical AI solutions have FDA or TFDA clearance (NVIDIA Taiwan blog, 2026-06-01). That density makes all three failure modes in the October Nature Medicine issue directly applicable — performance drift, decaying adoption, and one system needing different implementation strategies at different sites. And the figures in the announcement (Nurabot saving 2–3 hours a day, 98% navigation accuracy, Corovia compressing a two-hour workflow to a minute, simulation-first cutting deployment time 40%) are vendor-stated capability claims, not post-deployment outcome measurements.

(2) FHIR Box 的時程，決定台灣有沒有條件做 NYU 那種研究。NYU 之所以能拿 127,833 筆對話對照 58 個 benchmark，前提是一家醫療體系內部有一個統一的助理工具與可回溯的紀錄。衛福部資訊處長李建璋 8 月 6 日在立法院厚生會智慧醫療委員會成立大會說明的 FHIR Box 路線——政府訂標準、提供轉換工具，去年 3 家醫學中心試辦、今年底全台醫學中心資料互通、3 至 5 年推廣至約 8 成醫療院所——正是這類研究的基礎設施（中央社, 2026-08-06）。衛福部長石崇良 6 月在高醫大論壇提出的「333 政策」也給了同一條時間線：醫學中心病歷 2026 年底互通，區域與地區醫院 2027、2028 年跟上，健康台灣深耕計畫五年 489 億元（聯合報, 2026-06-27）。換句話說，台灣要到 2027 年才會有做「真實使用 vs. benchmark」對照所需的跨院資料底，而 AI 現在就在用。 (2) The FHIR Box timetable decides whether Taiwan can run NYU-style studies at all. NYU could set 127,833 conversations against 58 benchmarks only because one health system had a single internal assistant and a retrievable log. The FHIR Box route described on 6 August by Lee Chien-chang, head of the health ministry's information department, at the founding meeting of the Legislative Yuan Hou-sheng Association's smart healthcare committee — government sets the standard and supplies conversion tools, three medical centres piloted last year, interoperability across all medical centres by the end of this year, roughly 80% of providers within three to five years — is precisely that infrastructure (CNA, 2026-08-06). Health Minister Shih Chung-liang's "333 policy", set out at the Kaohsiung Medical University forum in June, runs on the same clock: medical-centre records interoperable by end-2026, regional and district hospitals in 2027 and 2028, with NT$48.9 billion over five years for the Healthy Taiwan Deep Cultivation Plan (United Daily News, 2026-06-27). The cross-hospital data floor needed for a real-use-versus-benchmark comparison therefore arrives in 2027 — and the AI is in use now.

(3) 多語語音這一項，台灣是被低估的難題市場。Speechmatics 把語碼轉換當成賣點，而台灣門診的日常就是國語、台語、客語與英文名詞在同一句話裡交替，加上東南亞語系的移工與看護。Oak 公布的 11 種語言裡沒有拆出中文表現，Google MedASR 的 WER 數字也沒有中文或台語拆解；這代表任何要導入的台灣醫院，都得自己建測試集來驗——而這件事，正好就是 Nature Medicine 社論說多數機構缺乏能力去做的那一段。相對地，高雄醫學大學附設中和紀念醫院 10 月 14 日在 HIMSS TV 上談的正是跨國協作共建醫院 AI 的模式（Healthcare IT News, 2026-10-14），這條路徑如果能把在地驗證資料集也一起共建，會比單純導入模型有用得多。 (3) On multilingual speech, Taiwan is an underrated hard market. Speechmatics sells code switching, and a Taiwanese outpatient clinic is exactly that: Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka and English terminology alternating within a sentence, plus Southeast Asian languages among migrant workers and carers. Oak publishes no Mandarin breakdown among its eleven tested languages, and Google's MedASR WER figures break out neither Mandarin nor Hokkien — so any Taiwanese hospital adopting either has to build its own test set to verify, which is the capability the Nature Medicine editorial says most institutions lack. Against that, Kaohsiung Medical University Chung-Ho Memorial Hospital used HIMSS TV on 14 October to describe a model of building and deploying hospital AI through worldwide collaboration (Healthcare IT News, 2026-10-14); if that route can co-build local validation datasets rather than only import models, it will be worth considerably more.
