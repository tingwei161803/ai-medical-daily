資本市場在同一週把自己的價格改了，而醫療 AI 的錢全流向通路 The capital market repriced itself this week, and the money in medical AI went to distribution

2026 年 11 月 10 日（二）· 涵蓋 11/02–11/10 · 台北時間 Tuesday, November 10, 2026 · Covering Nov 2–10 · Taipei time

本週最乾淨的一條線索不在醫院裡，在定價表上。11/8–9，OpenAI 完成首筆 110 億美元、估值 1.25 兆美元，低於先前約 1.4 兆美元的目標；同一時間，FT 引述的投資人把 Anthropic 的 IPO 估值區間放在 1.25–1.75 兆美元，而 10 月談的是約 2 兆。醫療 AI 這一側，整週沒有一筆新的「模型」大單——錢全進了通路與後台：11/9 AWS 宣布五年 1 億美元的 Health Impact Initiative，但發的是雲端信用額度、起步上限 1 萬美元；11/5 HCA 把與 Google 共同開發的護理交班 AI 在兩個月內推到約 100 家醫院；而本週兩筆併購買的都是 EHR 介接權，兩筆都沒公布價格。公開市場同時把帳攤開：Doximity 營收只成長 4%，調整後 EBITDA 利潤率從 59.8% 掉到 50.9%。 The cleanest thread this week was not in a hospital but on a price sheet. On Nov 8–9, OpenAI closed a first $11B tranche at a $1.25T valuation, below its earlier target of roughly $1.4T; at the same time investors cited by the FT put Anthropic's IPO valuation at $1.25–1.75T, against the roughly $2T discussed in October. On the medical side, not one new model-scale deal all week — the money went to distribution and the back office instead: on Nov 9 AWS announced a five-year, $100M Health Impact Initiative that pays in cloud credits capped at $10,000 to start; on Nov 5 HCA pushed a Google-co-built nurse handoff tool to about 100 hospitals in two months; and both of the week's acquisitions bought EHR access, neither at a disclosed price. The public market filed the invoice in the same days: Doximity grew revenue 4% while its adjusted EBITDA margin slid from 59.8% to 50.9%.

大廠動作Big Tech AWS / AmazonHealth Impact Initiative11/09

AWS 的「1 億美元投入醫療 AI」是五年期的雲端信用額度，起步上限 1 萬美元 AWS's "$100M for health AI" is five years of cloud credits, with a $10,000 cap to get started

發生什麼What

11/9，AWS 宣布 Health Impact Initiative（HII）：五年、1 億美元的 AI、代理型服務與雲端基礎設施承諾。給付方式是促銷信用額度——額度依 AWS Pricing Calculator 估算的需求規模核給，或先給上限 1 萬美元起步，效期 12 個月、可延長到 24 個月，受 AWS Promotional Credit 條款約束。首批受助者包括 Eve's Ark（女性健康資料保存，超過 1 PB）、Admera Health（基因定序）、Care 2 Communities（海地基層診所，已服務 35 萬人、年近 12 萬人次）、印度 Akshaya Patra Foundation（每日供餐逾 235 萬名兒童）與 U.S. Hunger。前身是 2021 年的 Health Equity Initiative，共投入 6,000 萬美元、400 多個受助者，累計支援 80 多國逾 600 個醫療組織。 On Nov 9, AWS announced its Health Impact Initiative (HII): a five-year, $100M commitment of AI, agentic services and cloud infrastructure. It pays in promotional credits — sized to each organization's estimated need via the AWS Pricing Calculator, or up to $10,000 to get started, valid 12 months and extendable to 24, governed by AWS Promotional Credit terms. First awardees include Eve's Ark (women's health data preservation, over a petabyte), Admera Health (genomic sequencing), Care 2 Communities (primary care in Haiti; 350,000 people served, nearly 120,000 visits a year), India's Akshaya Patra Foundation (over 2.35 million children fed daily) and U.S. Hunger. Its predecessor, the 2021 Health Equity Initiative, put in $60M across 400-plus awardees, supporting over 600 health organizations in 80-plus countries.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這不是慈善支出，是通路補貼：1 億美元的計價單位是 AWS 自己的牌價，花掉的錢回流到發額度的那座雲。前一輪五年 6,000 萬美元、400 多個受助者，平均每案 15 萬美元；這一輪起步上限 1 萬美元，意味著單位是「概念驗證」而不是「上線部署」。對照 Rock Health 的 Q3 2026 平均單筆交易金額 3,100 萬美元，HII 不是在跟創投競爭資金，是在搶下一代醫療應用長在哪一朵雲上。 This is not philanthropy, it is a distribution subsidy: the $100M is denominated in AWS's own list prices, and the money spent flows back to the cloud that issued the credits. The previous round averaged about $150,000 per awardee ($60M across 400-plus over five years); this one opens at $10,000, which makes the unit a proof of concept, not a deployment. Set against Rock Health's $31.0M average deal size in 2026, HII is not competing with venture capital for capital — it is competing for which cloud the next generation of health applications is built on.

要打折的地方Discount this

「1 億美元」是五年期的信用額度上限，不是現金，AWS 也沒說明這 1 億如何在各案之間分配、實際被動用多少；除了 1 萬美元的起步上限，沒有任何單案金額公開。 The "$100M" is a five-year credit ceiling, not cash, and AWS does not say how it is divided across awards or how much is actually drawn down. Beyond the $10,000 opening cap, no per-award figure is published.

HCA 把護理交班 AI 在兩個月內推到 189 家醫院中的約 100 家——史上最快的一次導入 HCA is pushing a nurse handoff AI to about 100 of its 189 hospitals in two months — its fastest rollout ever

發生什麼What

11/5，Becker's 報導，擁有 189 家醫院的 HCA Healthcare 將與 Google 共同開發的護理交班 AI，在未來兩個月內推上約 100 家醫院、從 2 個事業部擴到 8 個事業部，數萬名護理師可用——HCA 稱這是公司史上最快的技術導入。早期單院測試中護理師自評工具準確率 86%、有幫助 90%；臨床長 Michael Schlosser 表示交班相關的安全事件與未遂事件在試點院區「基本上降到零」。同一週 Becker's 另報導，HCA 預計年底前約 1 萬名醫師上線環境式病歷記錄。 On Nov 5, Becker's reported that HCA Healthcare, which runs 189 hospitals, is rolling a Google-co-developed nurse handoff AI to about 100 hospitals over the next two months, expanding from two divisions to eight, with tens of thousands of nurses getting access — the company's fastest technology rollout to date. In earlier single-site testing nurses rated the tool 86% accurate and 90% helpful; chief clinical officer Michael Schlosser said handoff-related safety events and near misses have "basically gone to zero in our pilot sites." Becker's separately reported the same week that HCA expects roughly 10,000 physicians on ambient documentation by year-end.

為什麼重要Why it matters

模型是 Google 的，資產是那 189 家醫院。這一週所有的錢都在重複同一個判斷：在醫療 AI 裡，稀缺的不是模型能力，是能在兩個月內讓數萬名護理師同時改變交班方式的那套組織。一個 OpenEvidence 式的新創要花多少年才能碰到 10 萬名護理師？HCA 用兩個月。 The model is Google's; the asset is the 189 hospitals. Every dollar that moved this week repeats the same judgment: what is scarce in medical AI is not model capability but the organization that can change how tens of thousands of nurses hand off patients within two months. How many years would an OpenEvidence-shaped startup need to reach 100,000 nurses? HCA did it in two months.

要打折的地方Discount this

86% 與 90% 是單一醫院護理師的自評，不是獨立稽核的準確率；「基本上降到零」是高階主管受訪時的口頭說法，沒有分母、沒有時間窗、沒有同儕審查的安全事件統計。整則消息來自 Becker's 對 CEO+CFO 圓桌會的採訪，HCA 未發布對應的新聞稿或資料。 The 86% and 90% are nurse self-ratings at a single hospital, not independently audited accuracy. "Basically gone to zero" is a spoken remark by an executive, with no denominator, no time window and no peer-reviewed safety-event count. The whole item comes from Becker's interview at a CEO+CFO roundtable; HCA published no corresponding release or dataset.

併購M&A Ellipsis HealthKouper Health11/06

Ellipsis Health 買下 Kouper Health：語音 AI 買的是 Epic／Oracle／athenahealth 的寫入權，價格未公布 Ellipsis Health buys Kouper Health: a voice AI company buying write access into Epic, Oracle and athenahealth — price undisclosed

發生什麼What

11/6，Ellipsis Health 宣布併購 Kouper Health，交易條件未揭露。Ellipsis 的語音照護管理員 Sage 建在聲音生物標記與對話引擎上，宣稱以超過 300 萬筆臨床標註的病人互動訓練，自動化照護管理收案、PHQ-9／GAD-7 評估、用藥遵從查核與出院後追蹤。Kouper Health 成立於 2022 年，投資人包括 CVS Health Ventures、General Catalyst 與 25madison，做的是原生 EHR 介接與縱向病人追蹤——把急診、住院、基層、專科與居家照護的接觸點串成連續病程，並在 Epic、Oracle Health、athenahealth 等宿主系統內完成閉環排程。該報導並引用「75% 的美國醫療系統已部署至少一項 AI 解決方案，2025 年為 59%」。 On Nov 6, Ellipsis Health announced its acquisition of Kouper Health with terms undisclosed. Ellipsis's voice care manager, Sage, is built on a vocal-biomarker and conversational engine the company says was trained on more than 3 million clinically labeled patient interactions, automating care-management enrollment, PHQ-9/GAD-7 assessments, medication adherence checks and post-discharge outreach. Kouper Health, founded in 2022 and backed by CVS Health Ventures, General Catalyst and 25madison, does native EHR integration and longitudinal tracking — stitching ER, inpatient, primary, specialty and home-health touchpoints into continuous journeys and closing the loop on scheduling inside host systems such as Epic, Oracle Health and athenahealth. The same report cites a figure that 75% of U.S. health systems have deployed at least one AI solution, up from 59% in 2025.

為什麼重要Why it matters

一家有模型的公司，買的是一家有介接的公司。這是今年醫療 AI 併購最穩定的形狀：模型能力可以租（基礎模型供應商都在削價），但在 Epic 裡「排一個回診」的權限買不到替代品。Sage 打電話問完 PHQ-9 之後要能把結果寫回病歷、把回診排進去，否則它只是一通電話。 A company with a model bought a company with an integration. This is the most stable shape in medical AI M&A this year: model capability can be rented, and foundation-model vendors keep cutting its price, but the right to book a follow-up appointment inside Epic has no substitute. Once Sage finishes a PHQ-9 by phone, it has to write the result back to the chart and get the visit on the calendar — otherwise it is just a phone call.

要打折的地方Discount this

沒有價格、沒有客戶數、沒有雙方營收，所以無法判斷「EHR 介接權」在市場上值多少。「300 萬筆臨床標註互動」是訓練集規模，不是效能指標；聲音生物標記判讀臨床困擾在這份交易資料裡沒有任何前瞻性試驗支撐。「75%／59%」是該報導引用的數字，未標明原始調查與樣本。 No price, no customer counts, no revenue on either side, so there is no way to judge what EHR access is worth on the market. "More than 3 million clinically labeled interactions" is a training-set size, not a performance figure, and nothing in the deal materials supports vocal biomarkers for clinical distress with a prospective trial. The 75%/59% pair is the article's own figure, with no survey or sample identified.

11/3，LeanTaaS 宣布併購 LookDeep Health，把病床邊的電腦視覺（追蹤病房內狀況）加進它既有的醫院產能與排程工具。條件未揭露。LeanTaaS 的本業是手術室、門診與病床產能的最佳化排程；LookDeep 的本業是裝在病房裡的視覺監測。這是本週第二筆由「營運／工作流在位者」買下「感知層」的交易。 On Nov 3, LeanTaaS announced its acquisition of LookDeep Health, adding bedside computer vision that tracks in-room conditions to its existing hospital capacity and scheduling tools. 兩筆併購的方向一致而且相反於直覺：買方是已經在醫院工作流裡的公司，標的是感知／模型。也就是說，在這個市場上「看得到病人」的技術比「能被排進流程」的位置便宜。對新創的含意很直接：如果你的護城河只有模型或感測，你是被買的那一方，而且價格不會被公布。 Both deals point the same way, and it is the counter-intuitive one: the buyers are companies already inside hospital workflow, and the targets are perception and models. In this market, technology that can see the patient is cheaper than a position that can be scheduled into the process. The implication for startups is blunt: if your moat is only a model or a sensor, you are the one being bought, and the price will not be published.

This item rests on HIT Consultant's listing; no primary release from LeanTaaS or LookDeep was found, and neither the integration timeline nor the product roadmap is public.

發生什麼What

11/5，Doximity 公布 2027 財年第二季（止於 2026/9/30）：營收 1.745 億美元、較去年同期 1.685 億成長 4%；GAAP 淨利 3,470 萬美元（去年 6,210 萬），利潤率 19.9% 對 36.8%；調整後 EBITDA 8,890 萬美元、下滑 12%，利潤率從 59.8% 降到 50.9%；非 GAAP 每股稀釋盈餘 0.36 美元（去年 0.45）；自由現金流 9,130 萬美元幾乎持平；季內回購 5,420 萬美元（去年同期 2,190 萬）。公司表示超過 190 家醫療系統已採購其 AI 套件（Ask，前稱 DoxGPT；DoseCheck；以及與 UpToDate 的合作），網路覆蓋超過 85% 的美國醫師。2027 財年指引上調至營收 6.78–6.83 億美元、調整後 EBITDA 3.17–3.25 億美元。營收優於預期，股價仍下跌。 On Nov 5, Doximity reported fiscal Q2 2027 (quarter ended Sept 30, 2026): revenue $174.5M, up 4% from $168.5M; GAAP net income $34.7M against $62.1M, a 19.9% margin versus 36.8%; adjusted EBITDA $88.9M, down 12%, with the margin falling from 59.8% to 50.9%; non-GAAP diluted EPS $0.36 against $0.45; free cash flow $91.3M, essentially flat; and $54.2M of buybacks in the quarter versus $21.9M a year earlier. The company says more than 190 health systems have purchased its AI suite (Ask, formerly DoxGPT; DoseCheck; and the UpToDate partnership), on a network covering over 85% of U.S. physicians. Fiscal 2027 guidance was raised to $678–683M revenue and $317–325M adjusted EBITDA. Revenue beat estimates and the stock still dropped.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是目前最乾淨的一份「把 AI 免費送給醫師、在別處收錢」損益表。4% 的營收成長對上 9 個百分點的 EBITDA 利潤率縮水，說明 AI 功能是在既有的高利潤廣告業務上加成本，而不是加價。注意公司揭露的是「190 多家醫療系統已採購」——家數，不是金額；若 AI 套件真的在貢獻收入，這是最該給出金額的一季。對照上週本報寫過的 OpenEvidence 藥廠廣告模式，Doximity 是同一個金流被公開審計後的樣子。 This is the cleanest income statement yet for "give physicians AI free, charge somewhere else." Four percent revenue growth against nine points of lost EBITDA margin says the AI features add cost to an existing high-margin advertising business rather than price. Note what the company disclosed: 190-plus health systems have purchased — a count, not a sum. If the AI suite were contributing revenue, this was the quarter to put a number on it. Against the pharma-advertising model this report covered last week at OpenEvidence, Doximity is what that same cash flow looks like once it is publicly audited.

The 190-plus health systems is a company statement with no dollar amount attached, and adjusted EBITDA excludes stock compensation, which accounts for most of the gap between $88.9M and $34.7M of GAAP net income.

04 — Taiwan Angle

美國這一週買的是通路與介接；台灣的對應物是國家級的落地預算與尚未定案的給付規則 America spent this week on distribution and integration; Taiwan's counterparts are a national deployment budget and a reimbursement rule not yet settled

(1) AWS 的 1 億美元是雲端信用額度，台灣的對應物買的是院內落地。把 HII 的五年 1 億美元（且是信用額度、起步上限 1 萬美元）放在旁邊看：衛福部「健康台灣深耕計畫」的投入規模是 15 億美元，而且花在長庚、高醫附設中和、馬偕、台大、臺中榮總、臺北榮總等醫學中心的實際導入上——該計畫揭露已有 85 項經 FDA 或 TFDA 核可的醫療 AI 解決方案、涵蓋年逾 1,400 萬人次，鴻海的 Nurabot 宣稱可為前線護理師每日省下約 2 至 3 小時。差別不在金額，在計價單位：一邊是雲端牌價，一邊是醫院工時。台灣廠商該讀的訊息是，HII 這類補貼不會帶來營收，但會決定下一代系統長在哪朵雲上。 (1) AWS's $100M is cloud credits; Taiwan's counterpart buys deployment inside hospitals. Set HII's five-year $100M — in credits, capped at $10,000 to start — beside the Ministry of Health and Welfare's Healthy Taiwan Deep Cultivation Program, at US$1.5 billion, spent on actual deployment at medical centers including Chang Gung, Kaohsiung Medical University Chung-Ho, Mackay, National Taiwan University Hospital, Taichung Veterans General and Taipei Veterans General. That program reports 85 medical AI solutions cleared by the FDA or Taiwan's TFDA, covering over 14 million patient visits a year, with Foxconn's Nurabot claimed to save frontline nurses roughly two to three hours a day. The difference is not the sum but the unit of account: cloud list prices on one side, nursing hours on the other. The message for Taiwanese vendors is that a subsidy like HII will not produce revenue, but it will decide which cloud the next generation of systems grows on.

(2) WISeR 的誘因結構是台灣引入 AI 核刪前必須先避開的那一個。WISeR 把審查委外、並按「省下的支出」付廠商報酬，結果是同一套規則下兩州核駁率差兩倍半，而 CMS 精算人員自己在 2025 年就寫下「參與者會有盡可能多核駁的誘因」。台灣的制度位置不同——單一保險人、核刪在健保署內部——但形狀是同一個：只要績效指標掛在節省金額上，AI 的最適解就是多核駁。11/20 成大的《AI驅動．健康台灣》論壇由健保署署長陳亮妤主講「健康台灣 健保永續」、衛福部部長石崇良致詞，正是這個問題該被問出來的場合：AI 進入給付審查時，指標要掛在哪一邊。 (2) WISeR's incentive structure is the one Taiwan must avoid before AI enters claims review. WISeR outsourced review and paid vendors a share of averted spending; the result was two states' denial rates differing by a factor of two and a half under one rule set, with CMS's own actuaries writing in 2025 that "participants will have an incentive to deny as many claims as possible." Taiwan's institutional position is different — a single payer, with review inside the NHIA — but the shape is identical: as long as the performance metric hangs on money saved, the optimal move for an AI is to deny more. The AI-Driven Healthy Taiwan forum at NCKU on Nov 20, where NHIA Director-General Chen Liang-yu speaks on NHI sustainability and Minister of Health and Welfare Shih Chung-liang opens, is exactly the venue for the question: when AI enters reimbursement review, which side does the metric hang on?

(3) 兩筆併購告訴台灣業者，護城河在 HIS／PACS 介接，不在模型。Ellipsis 買 Kouper 買的是 Epic／Oracle／athenahealth 的寫入權、LeanTaaS 買 LookDeep 買的是把感知接進既有排程——兩筆都是「在位的工作流」吃掉「新的感知／模型」。台灣的醫療資訊市場比美國更碎（各家醫學中心自建 HIS 的比例高），這對本地業者同時是壞消息與好消息：模型授權可以被任何人取代，但把一套流程接進某家醫學中心的 HIS、並通過其資安與流程審查，這件事外商買不走，也是本地業者唯一能累積的資產。 (3) Both acquisitions tell Taiwanese vendors the moat is HIS/PACS integration, not the model. Ellipsis bought Kouper for write access into Epic, Oracle and athenahealth, and LeanTaaS bought LookDeep to wire perception into existing scheduling — in both cases incumbent workflow consumed new perception. Taiwan's health IT market is more fragmented than America's, with a high share of medical centers running HIS systems they built themselves, and that is simultaneously bad news and good news for local vendors: a model license can be replaced by anyone, but wiring a workflow into a given medical center's HIS and passing its security and process review is something a foreign buyer cannot take away — and it is the only asset local vendors can compound.
