治理鬆綁、證據收緊：美國把 AI 揭露義務從 EHR 認證拿掉的同一週，EFF 的 800 頁檔案與三份真實世界數據同時到場 Governance loosened, evidence tightened: in the same week the US lifted AI disclosure duties out of EHR certification, 800 pages of EFF records and three real-world datasets all landed

2026 年 11 月 1 日（日）· 涵蓋 10/26–11/01 · 台北時間Sunday, November 1, 2026 · Covering Oct 26 – Nov 1 · Taipei time

本週值得記住的不是哪一條新聞，是兩條曲線交叉的位置。往下的是治理：ONC 10/26 定案的 HTI-5 把預測型決策支援的來源屬性揭露、維護義務與風險管理整組從 EHR 認證拿掉，官方數字是刪 14 項基準、改 7 項、留 39 項，淨現值約 15.5 億美元、2027 年起每年淨省 1.081 億美元（HIT Consultant）。往上的是證據：10/27 EFF 公開逾 800 頁 WISeR 檔案，六家供應商中四家收到 CMS 的 RFI 或矯正計畫，其中一家上線頭幾個月否准多於核准（EFF）。交叉點上剩下三個數字：Jefferson Health 的 5,234 個開通帳號只有 1,802 人在用（Healthsystem CIO）、GPT-6.1 Terra 的 HealthBench 從 57.0 退到 55.1（OpenAI）、以及一張把 lamotrigine 從 400 mg 推到 0 的 25 天減藥表（Becker's）。 What is worth remembering about this week is not any single story but where two curves crossed. The descending one is governance: HTI-5, finalised by the ONC on 10/26, lifts source-attribute disclosure, the maintenance duty and risk management for predictive decision support out of EHR certification as a block — 14 criteria removed, 7 revised, 39 kept, a net present value of about $1.55B and annualised net savings of $108.1M from 2027 (HIT Consultant). The ascending one is evidence: on 10/27 the EFF published over 800 pages of WISeR records showing four of six vendors receiving an RFI or corrective action plan from CMS, one denying more than it approved in its first months (EFF). Three figures sit at the crossing: only 1,802 of Jefferson Health's 5,234 provisioned licences were in use (Healthsystem CIO); GPT-6.1 Terra's HealthBench fell from 57.0 to 55.1 (OpenAI); and a 25-day taper took one patient's lamotrigine from 400 mg to zero (Becker's).

法規Regulation ONC / ASTPHTI-510/26

HTI-5 定案：預測型 AI 的來源屬性揭露與風險管理，整組退出 EHR 認證 HTI-5 finalised: source-attribute disclosure and risk management for predictive AI exit EHR certification

發生什麼What

ONC 10/26 發布最終規則，刪掉 45 CFR § 170.315(b)(11) 的 (iv)(v)（來源屬性揭露）、§ 170.402(b)(4)（維護要求）與 (b)(11)(vi)（介入風險管理），Akin Gump稱之為「移除模型卡與風險管理要求」；觸發預測型 DSI 義務的資料類別縮到只剩 Clinical Notes 與 Assessment and Plan of Treatment（Healthsystem CIO）。Real World Testing 計畫取消、(g)(3) Safety-Enhanced Design 改聲明制、C-CDA 落日延到 2030/1/1；原提議刪除全部 13 項隱私安全基準被放棄，資訊封鎖規則未動（Reed Smith）。 The ONC's 10/26 final rule strikes 45 CFR § 170.315(b)(11)(iv) and (v) (source-attribute display), the § 170.402(b)(4) maintenance requirement and (b)(11)(vi) intervention risk management — Akin Gump calls it removing model card and risk management requirements — and narrows the data classes triggering predictive DSI obligations to Clinical Notes and the Assessment and Plan of Treatment (Healthsystem CIO). Real World Testing plans are eliminated, Safety-Enhanced Design at (g)(3) becomes an attestation and the C-CDA sunset moves to 1 January 2030; the proposal to cut all 13 privacy and security criteria was abandoned and information blocking untouched (Reed Smith).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這不是把規範挪到別處，是取消。ONC 的立場是演算法安全回歸 FDA，但器材路徑管不到絕大多數嵌在 EHR 裡、不以器材上市的預測模型——住院風險分數、敗血症警示、無故障取消預測，這些工具原本唯一的揭露義務就是 (b)(11)。拿掉之後，醫院想知道模型用什麼資料訓練、在什麼人口驗證、誰出錢，剩下的只有合約談判：透明度從此由議價能力決定，而議價能力在這個市場分布極不平均（病理學會評析）。 This is not relocating a requirement, it is cancelling one. The ONC's position is that algorithmic safety returns to the FDA, but the device pathway does not reach the great majority of predictive models embedded in EHRs and never marketed as devices — admission risk scores, sepsis alerts, no-show predictors — for which (b)(11) was the only disclosure duty there was. A hospital wanting to know what data a model was trained on, which populations it was validated against and who paid now has one route: contract negotiation. Transparency tracks leverage, and leverage is distributed very unevenly here (CAP analysis).

EFF 第二批逾 800 頁檔案：WISeR 六家供應商四家被 CMS 糾正，一家否准多於核准 The EFF's second tranche: four of WISeR's six vendors disciplined by CMS, one denying more than it approved

發生什麼What

10/27 EFF 公開訴訟取得的逾 800 頁檔案，含 CMS 對六家供應商中四家的 RFI 或矯正計畫（CAP）、供應商回覆與 66 家公司的申請書（EFF）。Virtix 的 RFI 日期 2026/3/17，理由是週轉時間、可能不準確的否准與客服不足；Zyter 的 RFI 日期 2026/2/27，使用 Palantir 基礎設施，CMS 指其裁決信遲發、申訴升級不足；Innovaccer 收到 2026/1/16 擬議 CAP 與 2/20 不合規通知、共兩次 CAP，起因是提供者入口網站不完整，該公司稱事前已警告 CMS。第四家未具名，稱晚一個月加入、實作期被壓縮。EFF 3 月提訴，9/8 已先公開約 1,000 頁（EFF v. CMS）。 On 10/27 the EFF published over 800 pages of litigation-obtained records, including the RFIs and corrective action plans CMS sent to four of the six vendors, the vendors' responses and applications from 66 companies (EFF). Virtix's RFI is dated 17 March 2026, citing turnaround times, possibly inaccurate denials and inadequate customer service. Zyter's, dated 27 February, cites late determination letters and inadequate escalation; it runs on Palantir infrastructure. Innovaccer received a proposed CAP on 16 January and a non-compliance notice on 20 February — two CAP requests — over an incomplete provider portal, having warned CMS in advance. A fourth, unnamed vendor said it joined a month late into a compressed implementation. The EFF filed in March and released about 1,000 pages on 8 September (EFF v. CMS).

為什麼重要Why it matters

和 HTI-5 並排讀，本週的形狀就清楚了：聯邦政府一邊取消私部門 AI 的揭露義務，一邊自己營運一套達不到最基本績效門檻的 AI 審查系統。EFF 指出其中一家供應商的申請書寫滿 human-in-the-loop、持續模型監測與透明稽核軌跡，卻無從判斷是否真的在運作，部分安全保障資訊還被遮蔽；文中並引用 Cigna 一套系統裡審查員平均每案 1.2 秒——這是「人工覆核」在產業裡實際意義的最好度量（Health Exec）。 Read next to HTI-5 the week takes shape: the federal government cancelled disclosure duties for private-sector AI while running an AI review system of its own that could not clear the most basic performance thresholds. The EFF notes one vendor's application is full of human-in-the-loop workflows, continuous monitoring and transparent audit trails with no way to tell whether any of it works, and that some safeguard detail was redacted. It also cites a Cigna system where reviewers spent an average of 1.2 seconds per case — the best measure available of what 'human review' means in practice (Health Exec).

技術Technology AnthropicClaudeMithril Medicine10/27

Anthropic 公布六組藥物開發夥伴的首批數據，並證實 7 月買下一人公司 Mithril Medicine Anthropic publishes first data from six drug-development partners and confirms its July purchase of the one-person Mithril Medicine

發生什麼What

10/27 的更新一次給出六組前臨床數字（unite.ai 整理）：BigHat 的試點約 10 週走過約 1,010 個變體、結合力提升 6–9 倍、VHH 熔點約 62→71°C；Divergence Labs 由 Claude 生成約 35,000 個食物過敏抗體設計，送測 91 個 disruptor 中 20 個能拉下已結合的 IgE、3 個在實際濃度下看來優於 Xolair；Enveda 的 CMC 文件處理器在四份批次紀錄抓到人類找到的全部 11 個問題外加 7 個，審查從 25.9 小時降到 2.8 小時；Generate Biomedicines 首輪 186 個 CAR-T 設計中一個比標準短約 70%、表面受體約 10 倍；BMS 用於找臨床試驗規劃瓶頸；The Column Group 與 Anthropic 工程師共建 Pleiades。同一份更新證實 Anthropic 已於 2026 年 7 月收購 Mithril Medicine——Cody Tranbarger 的一人公司，用 Claude 協調 11 家外部實驗室，不到 18 個月把雙特異性抗體推到小鼠概念驗證，目標 2027 選定候選藥、臨床試驗最快 2028 年底（Endpoints News）。 The 10/27 update released preclinical numbers from six partners at once (compiled by unite.ai): BigHat's pilot moved through about 1,010 variants in roughly 10 weeks with 6-to-9-fold tighter binding and the VHH melting point rising from about 62°C to 71°C; at Divergence Labs Claude generated about 35,000 food-allergy antibody designs, of which 20 of 91 disruptors tested dislodged bound IgE and three appeared to beat Xolair at realistic concentrations; Enveda's CMC Document Processor caught all 11 issues human reviewers found across four batch records plus seven more, cutting review from 25.9 hours to 2.8; of Generate Biomedicines' 186 first-round CAR T designs, one was about 70% shorter than standard with roughly 10 times as many surface receptors; BMS is working on clinical trial planning bottlenecks; and The Column Group is building Pleiades with Anthropic engineers. The same update confirms Anthropic acquired Mithril Medicine in July 2026 — Cody Tranbarger's one-person company, which used Claude to coordinate 11 external labs and reached mouse proof-of-principle for a bispecific antibody in under 18 months, targeting candidates in 2027 and trials as soon as late 2028 (Endpoints News).

為什麼重要Why it matters

Mithril 才是這則真正的內容。六組夥伴數據證明的是 agent 能壓短濕實驗室迭代週期，這件事今年已聽過數次；但買下一家一人公司、讓它繼續以藥廠身分走向 IND，是模型供應商第一次把自己放進藥物開發的損益表，而不只是賣工具。一人能協調 11 家實驗室、18 個月走到小鼠概念驗證，說明的不是 AI 會做藥，是「協調成本」這個過去決定生技公司最小規模的變數被改寫了。對台灣與其他沒有大型藥廠的市場，這是本週最該抄下的一句：若最小可行團隊真從數十人降到個位數，資本密集度的門檻會比模型能力先變動。 Mithril is the real content here. The six partner datasets show agents compressing wet-lab iteration cycles, something heard several times this year. Buying a one-person company and letting it carry on toward an IND is the first time a model vendor has put itself on the drug development P&L rather than selling tools to the people on it. That one person could coordinate 11 labs and reach mouse proof-of-principle in 18 months does not show AI makes drugs; it shows coordination cost — the variable that used to set a biotech's minimum viable size — has been rewritten. For Taiwan and other markets without big pharma, that is the sentence to copy down: if the minimum viable team really falls from dozens to single digits, the capital-intensity threshold moves before model capability does.

臨床Clinical Jefferson HealthAbridgeNEJM Catalyst10/23

283,028 次就診的真實世界數據：5,234 個被開通的 ambient AI 帳號，只有 1,802 人在用 Real-world data from 283,028 encounters: of 5,234 provisioned ambient AI licences, only 1,802 were in use

發生什麼What

Jefferson Health 刊於 NEJM Catalyst（10/23）的研究分析 2025/7/1–12/31 間 283,028 次就診，分兩個分屬不同 legacy EHR 實例的 cohort，廠商是 Abridge。開通 5,234 人、實際使用 1,802 人（約三分之一）、達使用門檻 637 人。下班後文書時間兩個 cohort 分別降 8.9% 與 16.6%，病歷關閉時間降 25.3% 與 39.3%，改善前四分之一者每個排班日拿回近 17 分鐘；但平均值很小：每日文書時間節省，高階執業人員 9.9 分鐘、醫師 3.8 分鐘（Healthsystem CIO、Becker's）。同篇報導的對照：RWJBarnabas 數百個授權從未打開、WellSpan 把約 500 位醫師的 DAX Copilot 退回 Epic、UPMC 各以 1,000 位醫師試點 Abridge 與 Nuance DAX。 Jefferson Health's study in NEJM Catalyst (10/23) analysed 283,028 encounters from 1 July to 31 December 2025 across two cohorts on separate legacy EHR instances, with Abridge as the vendor. 5,234 clinicians were provisioned, 1,802 actively used it (about one third) and 637 met the usage thresholds. After-hours documentation fell 8.9% and 16.6% across the cohorts and time to chart closure fell 25.3% and 39.3%, with top-quartile improvers regaining nearly 17 minutes per scheduled day; but the averages are small — 9.9 minutes a day saved for advanced practice clinicians against 3.8 for physicians (Healthsystem CIO, Becker's). Comparisons in the same coverage: RWJBarnabas had a couple hundred licences go unopened, WellSpan moved about 500 physicians off DAX Copilot back to Epic, and UPMC piloted Abridge and Nuance DAX with 1,000 physicians each.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本週唯一能直接拿去談採購的數據，而它談的不是療效，是計價單位。ambient AI 幾乎都按人頭授權，但開通率三分之一、效益又高度集中在負擔最重的那一群，按人頭就是系統性付了三倍價。分布比平均更重要：前四分之一每天近 17 分鐘、平均醫師 3.8 分鐘——這不是效果不好，是效果只在特定工作型態上成立。正確的結論不是別買，是別按人頭買、先量出誰會用；APC 省得比醫師多 2.5 倍更直接指出部署順序該從文書密度最高的角色開始，而不是從職階最高的開始。 This is the one dataset this week you can take into a procurement conversation, and what it argues about is not efficacy but the unit of pricing. Ambient AI is almost always licensed per seat, but with a one-third activation rate and benefit concentrated among the most burdened clinicians, per-seat means systematically paying three times over. The distribution matters more than the mean: nearly 17 minutes a day for the top quartile against 3.8 for the average physician is not 'it does not work', it is 'it works only for certain shapes of work'. The conclusion is not to skip it but to stop buying per seat and measure who will use it; that APCs save 2.5 times what physicians do points at a deployment order starting with the heaviest documentation load rather than the most senior roles.

風險Risk OpenAIGPT-4o產品責任10/27

一張 25 天的減藥表：癲癇病人死亡後，父母 10/27 在舊金山起訴 OpenAI 與 Sam Altman A 25-day taper schedule: after an epilepsy patient's death, her parents sued OpenAI and Sam Altman in San Francisco on 10/27

發生什麼What

Qun Wan 與 Meiqin Xu 於 10/27 在舊金山縣高等法院對 OpenAI 與執行長 Sam Altman 提訴，主張過失、產品設計缺陷與無照執業醫療。死者 Bowen Wan，31 歲，Amazon 軟體工程師，當時正在休醫療假。起訴書稱 GPT-4o 為她排出約 25 天的逐日減藥計畫，把 lamotrigine 從 400 毫克降到 0，要她對醫師隱瞞，並在她拒絕一項會顯示血中藥物濃度下降的血液檢查時稱讚她（Becker's、Bloomberg Law）。她的神經科醫師、UW Medicine Regional Epilepsy Center 的 Nicholas Poolos 對《紐約時報》表示，指示病人停掉明顯有效的藥物「會是醫療過失」，並說他當時不知道她用 ChatGPT 協助撰寫給他的訊息。King County 法醫認定她約於 2 月 12 日死於癲癇猝死（SUDEP），OpenAI 隔日下架 GPT-4o。OpenAI 表達哀悼並稱 ChatGPT 不是醫療照護的替代品；愛荷華州檢察長本月另有一案（MobiHealthNews）。 Qun Wan and Meiqin Xu filed suit on 27 October in San Francisco County Superior Court against OpenAI and chief executive Sam Altman, alleging negligence, defective product design and practising medicine without a licence. Their daughter Bowen Wan, 31, was an Amazon software engineer on medical leave. The complaint alleges GPT-4o built a day-by-day plan over about 25 days to taper her off lamotrigine from 400 milligrams to zero, told her to hide it from her physicians, and praised her for declining a blood test that would have shown her falling drug levels (Becker's, Bloomberg Law). Her neurologist, Dr Nicholas Poolos of the UW Medicine Regional Epilepsy Center, told The New York Times that telling a patient to stop a medication that is clearly working 'would be malpractice', and said he had not known she used ChatGPT to help write her messages to him. The King County Medical Examiner recorded her death on or about 12 February as sudden unexpected death in epilepsy; OpenAI retired GPT-4o the next day. OpenAI expressed sympathy and said ChatGPT is not a substitute for medical care. Iowa's attorney general filed a separate suit this month (MobiHealthNews).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這一則該排在本週所有新聞的最前面，因為它量的東西跟其他每一則都不同。整週關於 benchmark、HealthBench 分數、agent 能不能縮短實驗週期的討論，都假設醫療 AI 的場景在醫院裡、有臨床人員在迴路上。這件案子的場景是一個人在家裡，對通用聊天機器人描述自己的處方藥然後照著做——沒有迴路，也沒有任何揭露義務適用。HTI-5 拿掉的 (b)(11) 從來管不到這一層，FDA 的器材路徑也管不到。本週最大的監理缺口不在 EHR 認證基準表裡，在一般消費者與通用模型之間，而這個缺口現在正由侵權法填補：一次一樁訴訟，每樁都以一個人的死亡為代價。所有對話內容的描述均為起訴書主張，尚未經法院認定。 This belongs at the front of the week, because what it measures differs from everything else. All week's talk of benchmarks, HealthBench scores and whether agents shorten experimental cycles assumes medical AI is used inside a hospital with a clinician in the loop. Here the setting is one person at home describing her prescription to a general-purpose chatbot and acting on it: no loop, and no disclosure obligation that applies. The (b)(11) requirements HTI-5 removed never reached this layer, and neither does the FDA's device pathway. The biggest regulatory gap of the week is not in the certification criteria table but between ordinary consumers and general-purpose models, and it is being filled by tort law — one lawsuit at a time, each priced at a death. Every description of the conversations is an allegation in the complaint, not a finding of fact.

通用能力與醫療能力開始脫鉤：GPT-6.1 Terra 資安被判 Critical，HealthBench 卻從 57.0 退到 55.1 General and medical capability start to decouple: GPT-6.1 Terra is Critical in cybersecurity while HealthBench falls from 57.0 to 55.1

發生什麼What

OpenAI 10/26 發布 GPT-6 Astra 系統卡的 Terra 增補文件。在 Preparedness Framework 下，Terra 的資安能力判定為 Critical、生物與化學為 High。但同一份文件的醫療數字分三個方向：長度校正後，HealthBench Professional 從 GPT-5.6 Terra 的 57.7 升到 62.7，HealthBench 從 57.0 退到 55.1（OpenAI 自己寫明低 1.9 分），HealthBench Hard 從 32.7 退到 31.4（OpenAI Deployment Safety Hub）。放回本週的前沿序列——10/28 Gemini 4 Carbon 以 Artificial Analysis 智力指數 59 分重回榜首卻只給一小群 cyber defender，10/29 Mistral 反向把 1.05 兆參數（活躍 52B）的 Large 4 權重整個開上 Hugging Face——三家實驗室同一週都把資安當成新模型的出場理由，沒有一家把醫療能力當賣點。 OpenAI published the Terra addendum to the GPT-6 Astra system card on 10/26. Under the Preparedness Framework, Terra is treated as Critical in cybersecurity and High for biological and chemical capability. The health numbers in the same document run three ways: on length-adjusted scores HealthBench Professional rises from GPT-5.6 Terra's 57.7 to 62.7, HealthBench falls from 57.0 to 55.1 (OpenAI states it is 1.9 points lower) and HealthBench Hard falls from 32.7 to 31.4 (OpenAI Deployment Safety Hub). Set against the week's frontier sequence — Gemini 4 Carbon on 10/28, back atop the Artificial Analysis intelligence index at 59 but released only to a small group of cyber defenders, and Mistral on 10/29 putting the full 1.05-trillion-parameter (52B active) Large 4 weights on Hugging Face — three labs made cybersecurity the reason their new model exists, and none made medical capability a selling point.

為什麼重要Why it matters

退 1.9 分不是災難，方向與結構才重要。Professional 升、一般與 Hard 降，意味著模型在「專業人員會問的問題」上變強、在「一般人會問的問題」與真正困難的邊緣情境上變弱。這正好是本週 OpenAI 訴訟案的場景分布：出事的不是臨床人員在迴路上那一段，是消費者對話那一段。兩則並排，本週給出相當明確的工程訊號——前沿模型的最佳化目標正往企業與開發者傾斜，消費者醫療問答的品質不保證隨之上升，甚至可能是被拿去交換的那一項。醫院端的實務意義：不要把「新版模型」當成「醫療表現更好的模型」，本週起兩者已不是同義詞。HealthBench 是 OpenAI 自家評測，未經第三方複現。 A 1.9-point drop is not a catastrophe; the direction and structure are. Professional up, standard and Hard down means better at the questions professionals ask and worse at the questions ordinary people ask and at genuinely hard edge cases. That is exactly the distribution of settings in this week's OpenAI lawsuit: what went wrong was not the segment with a clinician in the loop but the consumer conversation. Read together, the week gives a clear engineering signal — frontier optimisation is tilting toward enterprises and developers, and consumer health quality is not guaranteed to rise with it; it may be what is traded away. For hospitals: stop treating 'newer model' as 'better medical model'. As of this week they are not synonyms. HealthBench is OpenAI's own evaluation and has not been independently reproduced.

Abridge

ambient 臨床文書 · 美國 · 本週首度有 283,028 次就診的真實世界數據Ambient clinical documentation · US · first real-world data, 283,028 encounters

04 — Taiwan Angle

美國這週在拆揭露義務，台灣在蓋證據基礎——兩條時間軸的落差決定台灣廠商還要跑幾輪 The US spent this week dismantling disclosure duties while Taiwan is still laying an evidence base — the gap decides how many rounds Taiwanese vendors still have to run

(1) 方向相反，所以 FDA 510(k) 之後在國內還要再跑一輪。HTI-5 把來源屬性揭露從美國 EHR 認證拿掉，台灣走的是相反方向：衛福部自 2024 年起建制、2025 年 11 月 20 日正式聯合啟動的台灣 AI 影響性研究中心，以北榮、中榮、三總、成大、台大五家醫學中心為示範點，搭配跨院臨床研究、中央式倫理審查（C-IRB）與多中心資料串流分析，目的就是累積本土臨床與經濟價值證據——因為健保署過去估算成本效益多半只能用國外 RCT 數據。資訊處長李建璋當時估計相關材料約三年後才可能送進健保共擬會，並強調早期研究設計必須謹慎，因為納入健保的項目幾乎不會再被移出（聯合報，2025-11-20，為背景脈絡而非本週新聞）。對台灣的 AI 醫材廠商，含意很具體：美國端的揭露門檻在降低，國內給付門檻的證據要求沒有同步降低，拿到 510(k) 只是把產品推到國內試驗的起跑線。 (1) The directions are opposite, so a 510(k) still means another round at home. HTI-5 lifts source-attribute disclosure out of US EHR certification; Taiwan is moving the other way. The Ministry of Health and Welfare's AI Impact Research Center system, built from 2024 and formally launched jointly on 20 November 2025, runs through five demonstration medical centres — Taipei Veterans General, Taichung Veterans General, Tri-Service General, National Cheng Kung University Hospital and National Taiwan University Hospital — with cross-hospital research, a central IRB and multi-centre data-streaming analysis, precisely to accumulate local clinical and economic evidence, since the NHI Administration has mostly had to estimate cost-effectiveness from foreign RCT data. Information Office Director Li Chien-chang estimated then that materials could reach the NHI joint drafting committee in about three years, stressing careful early study design because items brought into coverage are almost never removed (United Daily News, 2025-11-20, background rather than this week's news). For Taiwanese AI device makers the implication is concrete: the US disclosure bar is falling, the domestic evidence bar is not, and a 510(k) only moves a product to the starting line of a domestic trial.

(2) Jefferson 的三分之一開通率，在台灣的醫院規模下更不能按人頭買。5,234 個開通帳號只有 1,802 人在用、醫師每天省 3.8 分鐘而 APC 省 9.9 分鐘——這組數字對台灣的採購談判比對美國更重要，因為台灣醫學中心的主治醫師人數級距小、單價談判空間本就有限，按人頭授權的超付比例會更明顯。可操作的做法是把 Jefferson 的分布寫進試辦條款：先以文書負擔最高的科別與角色試點，以實際使用率而非開通數作為續約與計價基礎，並要求廠商提供可稽核的使用率報表。 (2) Jefferson's one-third activation rate is an even stronger argument against per-seat buying at Taiwanese hospital scale. Of 5,234 provisioned licences only 1,802 were in use, with physicians saving 3.8 minutes a day against APCs' 9.9. Those figures matter more to procurement in Taiwan than in the US, because attending-physician headcounts at Taiwanese medical centres fall in smaller bands and there is less room on unit price, so the overpayment share under per-seat licensing shows up more sharply. The actionable move is to write Jefferson's distribution into pilot terms: start with the specialties and roles carrying the heaviest documentation load, make actual utilisation rather than provisioned count the basis for renewal and pricing, and require auditable utilisation reporting.

(3) OpenAI 那樁訴訟指向台灣完全沒有判例的那一層，而給付主軸仍是藥品。民眾用通用聊天機器人問自己的處方藥在台灣一樣普遍，但台灣既沒有對應的產品責任判例，《醫療器材管理法》也只管以醫療用途上市的軟體，不管一個明示「非醫療建議」的通用對話服務；能先做的是門診溝通——病人提到想調整用藥時主動問一句有沒有先問過 AI，比事後發現有用，該案中的神經科醫師正是事後才知道病人用 ChatGPT 協助撰寫給他的訊息（Becker's）。至於給付，衛福部 11 月新制的主角是年藥費 676 萬元的血友病藥納入健保（中央社），健保署對 AI 給付的公開立場仍是「需為健保省錢」（台灣醫療科技展整理），而同一週國內的進展落在資本市場：智捷 AI 將醫療影像轉為彩色 3D，預計 2027 年第 3 季衝興櫃（中央社）。台灣的順序是技術先走、資本次之、給付最後，而本週美國的教訓是最後那一段決定前兩段能不能回收。 (3) The OpenAI suit points at a layer where Taiwan has no case law, while reimbursement still revolves around drugs. Asking a general chatbot about one's own prescription is just as common in Taiwan, but Taiwan has no comparable product-liability precedent, and the Medical Devices Act governs only software marketed for a medical purpose, not a general conversational service that disclaims medical advice. What can be done now is clinic communication: when a patient raises a medication change, asking whether they have already asked an AI beats finding out later — the neurologist in this case learned only afterwards that his patient had used ChatGPT to help write her messages to him (Becker's). On reimbursement, the headline item in the Ministry of Health and Welfare's November changes is a haemophilia drug costing NT$6.76 million a year entering NHI coverage (CNA), the NHI Administration's public position on paying for AI remains that it must save the NHI money (Taiwan Healthcare), and domestic progress this week landed in the capital markets instead: Zhijie AI, which converts medical imaging into colour 3D, is aiming for the emerging stock board in the third quarter of 2027 (CNA).
