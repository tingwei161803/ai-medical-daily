發生什麼What

11 月 4 日，Nature 刊出《An AI-native experimental laboratory for autonomous biomolecular operations》（Wu 等人）。系統名為 AutoDNA，來自上海交通大學，可操作 20 多類儀器，支援核酸合成、轉錄、擴增與定序，應用指向診斷、藥物開發與資訊儲存。架構上把每一個儀器操作包成帶自然語言描述的 Python 物件（atomic services），讓 LLM agent 能推理並直接呼叫硬體；規劃端有 Experiment Planner、Literature Researcher、Reagent Manager、Hypothesis Proposer，執行端有 Program Developer 與 Hardware Executor & Validator，排程器掌握儀器即時狀態並做功能等價改道（加熱器忙碌時改用 thermal cycler）。預印本（arXiv 2507.02379）的數字：整合 25 台儀器；酵素法 DNA 合成經 8 輪 PAGE 優化後逐步產率 97.7%（軌跡自 93.00% 升到 97.79%）；一次 DNA 儲存往返耗時 162.9 小時、9,365 個硬體步驟，把 46 bytes 編成 78 條 strand，除補試劑外無人工介入；多用戶排程比循序執行快 3.60 倍、省 168.8 分鐘；加熱器使用率自 55.0% 升到 79.0%（1.44 倍），thermal cycler 自 47.6% 升到 80.6%（1.69 倍）。HIStalk 11/4 對 Nature 正式版的摘要則寫：配對到專家優化後的 DNA 合成產率、併行實驗快 3.2 倍、並檢出且驗證了一個癌症相關突變。 On November 4, Nature published "An AI-native experimental laboratory for autonomous biomolecular operations" (Wu et al.). The system, AutoDNA, comes from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and operates more than twenty classes of instrument, covering nucleic-acid synthesis, transcription, amplification and sequencing, with applications aimed at diagnostics, drug development and information storage. Architecturally, every instrument operation is wrapped as a Python object carrying a natural-language description — "atomic services" — so LLM agents can reason about hardware and call it directly. Planning is split across an Experiment Planner, Literature Researcher, Reagent Manager and Hypothesis Proposer; execution across a Program Developer and a Hardware Executor & Validator; and the scheduler tracks live instrument status with functional-equivalence rerouting (using a thermal cycler when the heater is busy). The preprint's figures (arXiv 2507.02379): 25 instruments integrated; 97.7% stepwise yield for enzymatic DNA synthesis after an eight-cycle PAGE optimization, with the trajectory climbing from 93.00% to 97.79%; a DNA-storage round trip taking 162.9 hours and 9,365 hardware steps, encoding 46 bytes into 78 strands with no manual intervention beyond reagent refills; a 3.60x speedup over sequential execution in multi-user scheduling, saving 168.8 minutes; heater utilization rising from 55.0% to 79.0% (1.44x) and the thermal cycler from 47.6% to 80.6% (1.69x). HIStalk's Nov 4 summary of the published version adds that it matched expert-optimized DNA synthesis yields, ran concurrent experiments 3.2-fold faster, and detected and validated a cancer-associated mutation.

為什麼重要Why it matters

過去兩年醫療 AI 的 agent 討論幾乎都停在文字層：寫病歷、查指引、送 prior auth。AutoDNA 把 agent 放到「會壞掉、會污染、會耗掉真實試劑」的物理世界，而且挑的是分子診斷最上游的那段流程。對醫療端的直接意義有兩個：第一，伴隨診斷與定序的瓶頸從來不只是演算法，而是誰在半夜三點重跑那一盤 PCR；第二，一旦實驗流程由模型產生程式碼來驅動，「這批檢驗是怎麼做出來的」就從實驗記錄本變成一份可重播的執行軌跡——這對後面第 8 則要談的稽核層是好消息，對 CLIA／LDT 這類以人員資格與 SOP 為核心的監管框架則是個還沒有答案的問題。 For two years, agent talk in healthcare has stayed at the text layer: drafting notes, looking up guidelines, filing prior authorizations. AutoDNA puts agents into a physical world that breaks, contaminates and consumes real reagents — and it picks the most upstream stretch of molecular diagnostics to do it in. Two consequences land directly on the clinical side. First, the bottleneck in companion diagnostics and sequencing was never only the algorithm; it was who re-runs that PCR plate at three in the morning. Second, once a protocol is driven by model-generated code, "how was this assay actually performed" stops being a lab notebook and becomes a replayable execution trace — good news for the audit layer in item 8 below, and an open question for regulatory frameworks such as CLIA and LDT oversight, which are built around personnel qualifications and written SOPs.

261,508 筆劑量、9 個 2026 年模型：至少 2.3% 給錯，被抓到的錯誤裡 3.4% 可能致命 261,508 dosages across nine 2026-vintage models: at least 2.3% wrong, and 3.4% of the errors caught are potentially lethal

發生什麼What

10 月 29 日，Doximity 醫療總監 Alex Blau 公布一份劑量稽核：9 個 2026 年發布的常用 LLM、8,000 多道「依真實醫師提問建模」的劑量題、涵蓋 2,204 種藥，共評估 261,508 筆劑量。結果以 EQUIP 量表評定嚴重度，並以 NCC MERP 量表做敏感度分析。至少 2.3% 的劑量是錯的，作者自稱這是保守下界；每 1,000 個回答約有 20 個含嚴重劑量錯誤，NCC MERP 敏感度分析下約 30.5／1,000 落在 E–H 類（即已造成或可能造成傷害）。被偵測到的錯誤中 43.8% 屬嚴重、3.4% 可能致命；最嚴重的那一群裡，每 5 個有 1 個是單位或小數點錯誤，每 20 個有 1 個是搞混兩種藥或兩種給藥途徑。最清楚的風險分層來自上市時間：2024 年之後核准的藥，出錯勝算是 5.1 倍（95% CI 3.9–6.5）。同一篇同時發表對策 DoseCheck。 On October 29, Doximity medical director Alex Blau published a dosing audit: nine widely used LLMs, all released in 2026, answered more than 8,000 dosing questions "modeled on real physician queries" across 2,204 drugs, for 261,508 dosages evaluated in total. Severity was scored on the EQUIP scale with a sensitivity analysis on NCC MERP. At least 2.3% of doses were wrong, which the author calls a conservative lower bound. Roughly 20 of every 1,000 answers contained a serious dosing error, and about 30.5 per 1,000 fell into NCC MERP categories E–H — errors that caused or could cause harm. Of the errors detected, 43.8% were serious and 3.4% potentially lethal; among the most severe, one in five was a unit or decimal error and one in twenty confused two medications or two routes of administration. The cleanest risk stratifier was recency: drugs approved in 2024 or later carried 5.1 times the odds of error (95% CI 3.9–6.5). The same post launched the countermeasure, DoseCheck.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本週唯一一份把「模型做臨床動作」的錯誤率拆到可行動顆粒度的量測，而它指向的對策方向跟主流相反：不是再疊一個模型去審第一個模型，而是把劑量這類有唯一正確答案的欄位，交給確定性的查表層。單位與小數點錯誤占最嚴重錯誤的五分之一，這種錯誤不需要推理就能抓，但也正是語言模型最不該被信任的地方。而 5.1 倍那個數字給出一條可以立刻寫進政策的規則：對 2024 年後核准的藥，模型輸出的劑量不應在未經結構化資料驗證的情況下進入處方流程。 This is the week's only measurement that breaks an agentic clinical action down to an actionable grain, and the remedy it points to runs against the prevailing instinct: not stacking a second model to review the first, but handing fields with a single correct answer — dosing above all — to a deterministic lookup layer. Unit and decimal errors account for a fifth of the most severe mistakes; catching those requires no reasoning at all, and they are precisely where a language model deserves least trust. The 5.1x figure, meanwhile, yields a rule that can be written into policy today: for drugs approved since 2024, a model-produced dose should not enter a prescribing workflow without validation against structured data.

FlashProve：用不洩漏權重的密碼學指紋，證明「這段輸出究竟出自哪個模型」 FlashProve: proving which model produced an output, with a cryptographic fingerprint that reveals no weights

發生什麼What

11 月 3 日，Anthropic 公布並開源 FlashProve，目標是「provable inference」：模型實驗室為每個發布的模型公布一份指紋，事後任何輸出都能與指紋做密碼學比對。設計上三件事是關鍵——指紋不揭露模型權重，所以封閉商用模型可以參與而不交出最值錢的資產；驗證輕到可以在本地裝置上跑；而且要能抵抗冒充者，包括建造並運行該模型的實驗室自己。Anthropic 同時放出詳細技術規格與各元件的可運作程式碼到 GitHub（文章未指明授權條款）。團隊自述用途包括：抓出業者偷換便宜模型省成本、或偷換昂貴模型美化評測數字，以及讓使用者確認自己用的模型就是以為的那個、驗證第三方「這份程式碼安全掃描／商業計畫審查是由某個先進模型完成」的說法。 On November 3, Anthropic published and open-sourced FlashProve, aimed at "provable inference": a lab publishes a fingerprint for each model it releases, and any output can later be matched against that fingerprint and verified cryptographically. Three design properties carry the idea — the fingerprint does not reveal model weights, so closed commercial models can participate without surrendering their most valuable asset; verification is light enough to run on a local device; and the scheme is meant to resist impostors, including the lab that built and runs the model. Anthropic also released a detailed technical specification and working code for various elements on GitHub (the article does not name the license). Stated use cases: catching a provider that quietly swaps in a cheaper model to cut costs, or a more expensive one to inflate benchmark results; letting users confirm the model they are using is the one they think it is; and verifying third-party claims that a code security scan or business-plan review was performed by a particular advanced model.

為什麼重要Why it matters

醫療是「模型身分」問題最尖銳的地方，因為這裡的每個輸出都可能進入法律紀錄。病歷摘要、鑑別診斷清單、影像報告初稿，事後要追「這是哪個模型、哪個版本做的」，目前唯一的證據就是廠商的日誌與自述——而廠商正是最有動機換模型的那一方（成本、量能、延遲）。把這件事跟 HHS 11 月初定案的 HTI-5 去監管規則（刪 14 項認證基準、改 7 項、預估省逾 15 億美元）放在一起看就很清楚：聯邦剛把預測型 AI 的來源屬性揭露從 EHR 認證拿掉，而同一週一份開源密碼學工具提出了不依賴監管命令也能成立的替代路徑。兩者解的是同一個問題，只是一個靠規則、一個靠數學。 Healthcare is where model identity bites hardest, because every output here can end up in a legal record. For a note summary, a differential list or a draft imaging report, the only evidence of "which model, which version" is today the vendor's own logs and assurances — and the vendor is precisely the party with the strongest incentive to swap models, for cost, capacity or latency. Set this beside the HTI-5 deregulatory rule HHS finalized in early November, which cuts 14 certification criteria, revises 7 more and projects over $1.5 billion in savings, and the shape is clear: federal rules just removed source-attribute disclosure for predictive AI from EHR certification, and in the same week an open-source cryptographic tool proposed a route to the same end that needs no regulatory mandate. Both address one problem; one does it with rules, the other with mathematics.

要打折的地方Discount this

這不是產品。研究者自述「分享的是中間進度，不是完成的研究計畫」，並明說「我們公開的目的就是希望別人來看、拿去、改進」；Anthropic 與任何其他實驗室都沒有部署 FlashProve 的計畫。TechCrunch 的報導未給任何開銷倍數、模型規模或延遲數字（網路上流傳的「72 倍開銷」出自第三方整理，本報未採用為事實）。在真正有實驗室願意公布指紋之前，這套機制對醫院採購沒有可用的槓桿。 This is not a product. The researchers describe the work as "meant to share intermediate progress, and not a completed research agenda," and say plainly that they are publishing it "with the goal that others look at what we have done, take it, and improve it." Neither Anthropic nor any other lab has plans to deploy FlashProve. TechCrunch reports no overhead multiples, model sizes or latency figures (a "72x overhead" number circulating in third-party write-ups is not adopted as fact here). Until a lab actually publishes fingerprints, this gives hospital procurement no usable leverage.

This is the first time a mainstream EHR vendor has turned patient-side record export into an MCP interface a consumer AI can call directly, rather than a traditional FHIR API or a patient-portal download.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這條管線的方向值得注意：它不是醫院把資料給 AI，而是病人行使查閱權後把資料給 AI。在美國法律下這條路繞開了大部分 HIPAA 的業務夥伴協議安排——資料一旦到病人手上再轉給消費級服務，受保護健康資訊的保護傘就換了。對產業結構的意思是，EHR 廠商不必等醫院 IT 委員會同意，就能讓自己的資料進到前沿模型的 context 裡；而對病人安全的意思是，上面第 2 則那 2.3% 的劑量錯誤率，將開始作用在「手裡有自己完整用藥清單、但沒有任何臨床訓練」的人身上。 The direction of this pipe matters: it is not the hospital handing data to an AI, it is the patient exercising a right of access and then handing the data onward. Under US law that route sidesteps most of the HIPAA business-associate machinery — once data is in the patient's hands and passed to a consumer service, the protective umbrella over protected health information changes shape. Structurally, it means an EHR vendor no longer needs a hospital IT committee's blessing to get its data into a frontier model's context. For patient safety, it means the 2.3% dosing error rate in item 2 above starts operating on people who hold their own complete medication list and have no clinical training whatsoever.

02 — Product Analysis

兩個方向相反的產品：AutoDNA 把決策權盡量交給模型，DoseCheck 刻意把決策權從模型手上拿回來。它們在回答同一個問題——模型該被信任到哪一層。 Two products pointing opposite ways: AutoDNA hands as much decision-making as possible to the model, while DoseCheck deliberately takes decision-making back from it. Both answer the same question — how far down the stack a model should be trusted.

AutoDNA

AI 原生自主生物分子實驗室 · 上海交通大學團隊（中國）AI-native autonomous biomolecular laboratory · Shanghai Jiao Tong University team (China)

功能與定位。它不是一台儀器，而是一層把濕實驗室整個包成 API 的軟體。核心設計是「co-design」：模型、實驗設計與儀器一起設計，每個儀器操作成為帶自然語言描述的 atomic service，讓 LLM agent 能夠推理、生成驅動程式碼並直接執行。支援核酸合成、轉錄、擴增與定序，Nature 11/4 刊出，應用指向分子診斷、藥物開發與 DNA 資訊儲存，並能同時處理多個使用者的請求。買家還不存在——這是論文，不是產品——但潛在買家很清楚：中央實驗室、CRO、以及任何需要在固定 SOP 上反覆跑同一套分子流程的單位。 Function and position. It is not an instrument but a software layer that wraps an entire wet lab as an API. The core design idea is co-design — models, experiments and instruments designed together — with each instrument operation becoming an atomic service carrying a natural-language description, so LLM agents can reason, generate driver code and execute directly. It covers nucleic-acid synthesis, transcription, amplification and sequencing, was published in Nature on November 4, targets molecular diagnostics, drug development and DNA information storage, and handles multiple users' requests concurrently. There is no buyer yet — this is a paper, not a product — but the likely buyers are obvious: central labs, CROs, and anyone running the same molecular workflow against a fixed SOP over and over.

  • 優勢Strength ：排程才是真正的貢獻。多用戶排程比循序執行快 3.60 倍、省 168.8 分鐘，加熱器使用率自 55.0% 升到 79.0%、thermal cycler 自 47.6% 升到 80.6%，而且靠的是功能等價改道（加熱器忙碌時改用 thermal cycler）這種人類排程員會懶得做的動作。 : scheduling is the real contribution. Multi-user scheduling ran 3.60x faster than sequential execution, saving 168.8 minutes, with heater utilization climbing from 55.0% to 79.0% and the thermal cycler from 47.6% to 80.6% — achieved through functional-equivalence rerouting (using a thermal cycler when the heater is busy), exactly the kind of move a human scheduler cannot be bothered to make.
  • 優勢Strength ：產率確實到了可用水準。酵素法 DNA 合成經 8 輪 PAGE 優化後逐步產率 97.7%，軌跡自 93.00% 爬到 97.79%——優化過程本身由 agent 提假設、跑實驗、讀結果再提下一輪，這部分比最終數字更有說服力。 : the yield reaches a usable level. Enzymatic DNA synthesis hit 97.7% stepwise yield after eight PAGE optimization cycles, climbing from 93.00% to 97.79% — and the optimization loop itself, where an agent proposes a hypothesis, runs the experiment, reads the result and proposes the next round, is more persuasive than the final number.
  • 疑慮Concern ：失敗率完全沒報。沒有 LLM 產碼失敗次數、重試次數、人工救援次數的統計——而這恰恰是任何實驗室評估要不要採用時第一個會問的數字。缺就是缺。 : failure rates are simply absent. No statistics on LLM code-generation failures, retries or human recovery events — and that is the very first number any lab would ask for when deciding whether to adopt this. It is missing, and missing it stays.
  • 疑慮Concern ：單次執行、無重複、無誤差棒，且「匹配人類專家」比的是別家文獻值而非同批平行對照；吞吐量基準是自己的循序模式。另外預印本未附程式碼與資料可得性聲明（Nature 版的 Zenodo 存放處 DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20624108 補上了這一塊）。 : single runs, no replicates, no error bars, and the "matches human experts" claim is measured against another lab's published value rather than a same-batch parallel control; the throughput baseline is the system's own sequential mode. Doximity DoseCheck

確定性劑量驗證層 · Doximity（美國）Deterministic dosage verification layer · Doximity (United States)

功能與定位。它架在 Doximity Ask 裡，抽出模型輸出中的每一個劑量主張（藥名、劑量、途徑、頻次、適應症），逐項比對自家結構化藥品資料庫 RxIndex——1,900 份藥品專論、36,000 個以上劑量方案，來源為 FDA 標籤、指引與原始文獻。關鍵的架構決定是「不用另一個語言模型去查第一個」：驗證是確定性查表，不是二次推理。對上就給綠色徽章、每個驗證過的劑量連回原始專論，回傳時間少於 100 毫秒。賣給醫師（Doximity 自述為免費的附加安全層），商業模式是把醫師留在 Doximity Ask 裡。 Function and position. It sits inside Doximity Ask, extracts every dosage claim in a model's output — drug, dose, route, frequency, indication — and checks each against Doximity's own structured drug database, RxIndex: 1,900 drug monographs and more than 36,000 dosing regimens, sourced from FDA labeling, guidelines and primary literature. The decisive architectural choice is not using a second language model to check the first: verification is a deterministic lookup, not a second round of reasoning. Matching doses get a green badge, each verified dose links back to its monograph, and results return in under 100 milliseconds. It is aimed at physicians — Doximity describes it as a free additional safety layer — and the business model is keeping physicians inside Doximity Ask.

  • 優勢Strength ：它攻擊的錯誤型態正好是模型最不該被信任、也最容易用查表抓到的那一類。最嚴重錯誤裡每 5 個有 1 個是單位或小數點錯——這類錯誤零推理即可偵測，而且是目前「用 LLM 審 LLM」架構最容易一起錯過的。 : the error class it attacks is exactly the one a model least deserves trust on and a lookup most easily catches. One in five of the most severe errors was a unit or decimal mistake — detectable with zero reasoning, and precisely the kind that an LLM-checking-an-LLM architecture tends to miss in both passes.
  • 優勢Strength ：<100 毫秒與逐項連回專論，解掉了臨床決策支援最老的問題：警示要快到不打斷流程，而且要能當場看到依據。Mayo 11/3 把 22 個計算器拉進聊天介面，走的是同一個方向。 : sub-100-millisecond latency plus a per-item link back to the monograph solves the oldest problem in clinical decision support — an alert has to be fast enough not to break flow, and has to show its basis on the spot. Mayo pulling 22 calculators into its chat surface on November 3 is the same move.
  • 疑慮Concern ：覆蓋率缺口恰好落在最危險的地方。RxIndex 收 1,900 份專論，但稽核涵蓋 2,204 種藥，而出錯勝算最高的正是2024 年後核准的新藥（5.1 倍，95% CI 3.9–6.5）——也就是最可能還沒進資料庫的那一群。Doximity 未公布 RxIndex 的更新頻率與新藥納入時程。 : the coverage gap sits exactly where the danger is. RxIndex holds 1,900 monographs while the audit spanned 2,204 drugs, and the highest odds of error were on drugs approved since 2024 (5.1x, 95% CI 3.9–6.5) — the group least likely to be in the database yet. Doximity publishes no update cadence for RxIndex and no timeline for adding new approvals.
  • 疑慮Concern ：所有數字都是廠商自述，而廠商同時是問題的揭露者與解法的銷售者。研究未公布、同儕審查狀態未說明、9 個受測模型一個都沒點名、沒有分項數據、也沒有獨立複驗。這不代表數字錯，但代表它還不能當成採購依據。 : every figure is vendor-reported, and the vendor is both the discloser of the problem and the seller of the fix. The study is unpublished, its peer-review status unstated, none of the nine tested models named, no per-model breakdown given, and no independent replication performed. That does not make the numbers wrong, but it does mean they cannot yet carry a procurement decision.

It reports 200,000-plus verified providers, 175-plus scheduling integrations and 10,000-plus insurance plans, with agent traffic up nearly 30x since July, doubling roughly every 10 days since early September, and October's first two weeks exceeding the prior three months combined — all company data, with no measurement method defined. 護城河是排程整合與保險驗證這兩件極難複製的管線工作——agent 可以生成文字，但無法生成 175 條排程系統接線。策略上它選擇成為 agent 的供應端而非被繞過的中介。弱點是它把自己放進了一條沒有責任框架的路徑：當 agent 幫病人掛錯科，Zocdoc、agent 開發者與醫師之間的責任分配目前無人定義。 The moat is the two hardest pieces of plumbing to replicate — scheduling integrations and insurance verification. An agent can generate text; it cannot generate 175 scheduling-system connections. Strategically Zocdoc chose to become the supply side for agents rather than the intermediary they route around. The weakness is that it has placed itself on a path with no liability framework: when an agent books the wrong specialty, nothing allocates responsibility between Zocdoc, the agent developer and the clinician.

今天的競爭結構是一條還缺一節的管線。上游有人把能力推到物理世界（AutoDNA、Anthropic 的藥物開發夥伴），中游有人把資料與服務改成 agent 可呼叫的介面（athenahealth、Zocdoc 都選了 MCP），下游有人賣控制與稽核（Corti、Rhapsody、CrowdStrike），旁邊有人賣可驗證性（FlashProve）。缺的那一節是量測：整條管線上，只有 Doximity 公布了一個可以用來拒絕某個輸出的錯誤率，而那還是廠商自己算的。在 HTI-5 把 EHR 認證的 AI 透明度要求拿掉之後，買方手上剩下的工具只有廠商自願公布的數字——而本週七家公司裡，有五家一個數字都沒給。 Today's competitive structure is a pipeline with one section missing. Upstream, capability is being pushed into the physical world (AutoDNA, Anthropic's drug-development partners). Midstream, data and services are being reshaped into agent-callable interfaces, with athenahealth and Zocdoc both choosing MCP. Downstream, control and audit are for sale (Corti, Rhapsody, CrowdStrike), with verifiability alongside (FlashProve). The missing section is measurement: along the whole pipeline, only Doximity published an error rate you could use to reject a given output, and even that one is vendor-calculated. With HTI-5 removing AI transparency requirements from EHR certification, the only instrument left to buyers is whatever vendors volunteer — and of this week's seven companies, five gave no number at all.

04 — Taiwan Angle

本週三件事在台灣各對應到一個已經存在、但還沒接上的機制：模型身分對應負責任 AI 中心、劑量錯誤率對應仿單與健保給藥規則、MCP 管線對應 FHIR 資料平台。 Each of this week's three threads maps onto a mechanism that already exists in Taiwan but has not been wired up: model identity to the responsible-AI centers, dosing error rates to local labeling and NHI prescribing rules, and the MCP pipeline to the FHIR data platform.

(1) FlashProve 給了台灣「負責任 AI 執行中心」一個不靠自述的選項。衛福部 2024 年 10 月 7 日啟動三大 AI 中心：負責任 AI 執行中心（台大醫院與台北榮總同為責任醫院）、臨床 AI 取證驗證中心（台大醫院，與 TFDA 共同執行）、AI 影響性研究中心（台北榮總，與健保署共同執行）；當時 30 家醫院送 48 件申請、16 家醫院 19 案通過，台大與北榮各得 2 案。負責任 AI 執行中心的任務之一就是「公開揭露模型、資料與效能資訊」並建立模型生命週期管理——而這件事目前在全世界都只有一種實作方式，就是相信廠商的日誌。FlashProve 11/3 提出的指紋機制之所以值得台灣的驗證中心注意，不是因為它成熟（Anthropic 自己說沒有部署計畫），而是因為它把「模型版本揭露」從一個信任問題改寫成一個密碼學問題。對一個要替台灣族群做模型驗證、而且驗證完之後模型還會被廠商持續更新的中心來說，這是目前唯一能在不取得權重的前提下確認「上線跑的還是我驗過的那個模型」的技術路徑。 (1) FlashProve hands Taiwan's Responsible AI Execution Center an option that does not rest on vendor assurances. The Ministry of Health and Welfare launched three AI centers on October 7, 2024: a Responsible AI Execution Center (with both National Taiwan University Hospital and Taipei Veterans General Hospital as responsible hospitals), a Clinical AI Evidence Validation Center (NTUH, run jointly with the TFDA), and an AI Impact Research Center (Taipei Veterans General, run jointly with the National Health Insurance Administration). Thirty hospitals filed 48 applications; 16 hospitals passed with 19 projects, NTUH and Taipei Veterans holding two each. One of the Responsible AI Execution Center's charges is public disclosure of model, data and performance information, plus lifecycle management of models — and worldwide that charge currently has exactly one implementation: trusting the vendor's logs. What makes the fingerprint scheme Anthropic proposed on Nov 3 worth the validation center's attention is not maturity — Anthropic says it has no deployment plans — but that it rewrites model-version disclosure from a trust problem into a cryptographic one. For a center that validates models for the Taiwanese population and then watches vendors keep updating them, this is the only technical route available for confirming that what runs in production is still the model that was validated, without ever holding the weights.

(2) Doximity 的 2.3% 不能直接搬到台灣用，而這正是問題。那份稽核是用 FDA 標籤、美國指引與美國原始文獻建的 RxIndex（1,900 份專論、36,000 個以上劑量方案）當真值。台灣的仿單核准劑量、健保給藥規則與常用劑型與美國並不一致，同一個模型在台灣用藥情境下的錯誤率，據本報所見沒有任何公開量測。更要緊的是 5.1 倍那條規律——2024 年後核准的新藥出錯勝算最高——在台灣會被放大，因為新藥在台上市時間與適應症給付範圍往往與美國錯開，模型最可能依美國標籤回答。要複製 DoseCheck 那種確定性驗證層，缺的不是技術（查表而已，<100 毫秒，不需要第二個模型），而是一份結構化、持續更新、涵蓋健保給藥規則的台灣藥品劑量資料。這件事的規模遠小於訓練一個醫療模型，但目前沒有任何一個 AI 中心的任務說明把它列進去。 (2) Doximity's 2.3% cannot be transplanted to Taiwan, and that is exactly the problem. That audit used RxIndex — 1,900 monographs and more than 36,000 dosing regimens built from FDA labeling, US guidelines and US primary literature — as ground truth. Taiwan's approved label doses, NHI prescribing rules and commonly stocked formulations do not line up with the American ones, and as far as this report can find, no public measurement exists of the same models' error rate in a Taiwanese prescribing context. The 5.1x pattern matters more here, not less: drugs approved since 2024 carry the highest odds of error, and in Taiwan that effect is amplified, because local launch timing and reimbursed indications frequently diverge from the US, while the model most likely answers from the US label. Reproducing a deterministic verification layer like DoseCheck does not lack technology — it is a lookup, under 100 milliseconds, with no second model required. What is missing is a structured, continuously updated Taiwanese dosing dataset that encodes NHI prescribing rules. That job is far smaller than training a medical model, and not one of the AI centers' published mandates includes it.

(3) FHIR 解資料格式，MCP 解 agent 怎麼合法地拿到它——台灣目前只有前半。本週 athenahealth 與 Zocdoc 選的都是 MCP。台灣這邊的對照是 2026 年 6 月 26 日醫材公會與工研院宣布的「AI 智慧醫療數據大平台」：號稱台灣第一個以 FHIR 標準建立的智慧醫療數據平台，九家醫材廠參與（遠傳電信、晉弘科技、瑪克多、炳碩生醫、商之器、美商宇心生醫、博鑫醫電、超象科技、緯謙科技），工研院擔任第三方中立法人負責資料整合與 FHIR 落實，涵蓋生理監測、心電圖、醫療影像、超音波、手術室與遠距醫療資料，目標把業界常見的一到兩年驗證期縮短。兩層是互補而非替代：FHIR 讓資料可被程式讀懂，MCP 決定 agent 在什麼授權範圍下讀得到——而第三層（本報第 7 則談的身分驗證、範圍化存取與速率限制）在台灣目前連討論都很少見。醫院若在 FHIR 平台上開放 agent 存取而沒有補上那一層，等於把 Zocdoc 自述的「每 10 天翻倍」流量曲線接到一個沒有配額的介面上。 (3) FHIR solves the data format; MCP decides how an agent legitimately reaches it — Taiwan has only the first half. Both athenahealth and Zocdoc chose MCP this week. Taiwan's counterpart is the AI Smart Healthcare Data Platform announced on June 26, 2026 by the Taiwan Medical Device Association with ITRI: billed as Taiwan's first smart healthcare data platform built on the FHIR standard, with nine device companies taking part (Far EasTone, Crystalvue, MacDo, Point Robotics, EBM Technologies, QT Medical, Bosen, Soundwave and Weichien), ITRI acting as a neutral third-party institution for data integration and FHIR implementation, and coverage spanning physiological monitoring, ECG, medical imaging, ultrasound, operating-room and telemedicine data, with the aim of shortening the one-to-two-year validation cycles the industry commonly faces. The two layers are complementary, not substitutes: FHIR makes data machine-readable, MCP decides under what authorization an agent gets to read it — and the third layer, the identity, scoped access and rate limiting discussed in item 7, is barely even a conversation in Taiwan yet. A hospital that opens agent access on top of a FHIR platform without adding that layer is wiring Zocdoc's self-reported doubling-every-10-days curve into an interface with no quota.
