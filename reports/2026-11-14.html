發生什麼What

11/11 Google DeepMind 發表 AlphaGenome 2（作者 Adam Gayoso、Natasha Latysheva、Pushmeet Kohli、Žiga Avsec）。與前代平行輸出固定組織與細胞株不同，這一代是條件式模型：輸入一個生物條件（組織、細胞型別或狀態）加上約 100 萬個 DNA 字母，一次預測一個條件。輸出包含基因表現、組蛋白修飾、染色質可及性，以及前代沒有的 DNA 甲基化；訓練資料涵蓋人與鼠的 bulk 與單細胞資料，超過 1.07 億個單細胞輪廓、68 種組織（DeepMind Blog）。在 TenK10K 的 28 種原級免疫細胞上，找出的致因變異比例為 30%，對照前代在單細胞資料上微調後的 16%；方向性基因效應 43% 對 23%。它也在運算上重現了一項已發表結果：一個遠端調控開關上的變異使腸道巨噬細胞的 ETS2ETS2 過度活化，與發炎性腸道疾病相關。授權方式是今日起經 AlphaGenome API 開放非商業研究，商業研究為私有預覽，意見回饋表單截止 11/30。 On Nov 11 Google DeepMind published AlphaGenome 2 (authors Adam Gayoso, Natasha Latysheva, Pushmeet Kohli and Žiga Avsec). Where the previous generation emitted fixed parallel outputs for bulk tissues and cell lines, this one is conditional: it takes a biological condition — a tissue, cell type or state — plus about one million DNA letters, and predicts one condition at a time. Readouts cover gene expression, histone modifications and chromatin accessibility, plus DNA methylation, which the previous model did not predict; training draws on bulk and single-cell human and mouse data, over 107 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues (DeepMind Blog). On TenK10K's 28 primary immune cell types it identifies causal variants in 30% of cases against 16% for the original AlphaGenome fine-tuned on single-cell data, and directional gene effects 43% against 23%. It also reproduced computationally a published finding that a variant in a distant regulatory switch overactivates ETS2ETS2 in gut macrophages, linked to inflammatory bowel disease. Access is from today for non-commercial research through the AlphaGenome API, with a private preview for commercial research and a feedback form closing Nov 30.

為什麼重要Why it matters

GWAS 命中點多年來卡在同一個地方：訊號落在非編碼區，而組織層級的模型分不開同一組織裡相鄰的細胞型別。這一代真正的改動是 cell encoder——研究者可以用自己的單細胞資料去條件化模型，DeepMind 稱從幾十個細胞的單細胞 RNA-seq 快照出發、不需重訓練，就能追平或勝過直接在那些資料集上訓練或微調的模型，人類細胞圖譜共同創辦人 Sarah Teichmann 的說法是它「無需重訓練即可直接適應新的單細胞資料」。跨模態補值是另一個實用面：給一種量測（如單細胞基因活性），推另一種未量測的性質（如染色質可及性），這對只負擔得起一種定序的實驗室改變的是成本結構，不是準確率。 GWAS hits have been stuck in the same place for years: the signal sits in non-coding regions, and tissue-level models cannot separate neighbouring cell types within one tissue. The substantive change here is the cell encoder — researchers can condition the model on their own single-cell data, and DeepMind says that starting from a single-cell RNA-seq snapshot of a few dozen cells, with no retraining, it matches or outperforms models trained or fine-tuned directly on those datasets; Human Cell Atlas co-founder Sarah Teichmann's phrasing is that it "adapts directly to new single-cell data without retraining." Cross-modality imputation is the other practical face: given one measurement, such as single-cell gene activity, it predicts an unmeasured property such as chromatin accessibility — which changes the cost structure, not the accuracy, for a lab that can only afford one assay.

  • 疑慮Concern ：公告自己劃掉臨床用途——僅供研究、未經臨床或診斷核准，且方向性預測只在模型有信心時回報，而「有信心」的門檻未公開。 : the announcement rules out clinical use itself — research only, not approved for clinical or diagnostic purposes — and directional predictions are reported only where the model is confident, with the confidence threshold not published.
  • 疑慮Concern ：所有數字都由發表方選定與執行，缺的是第三方在未見過的資料上重跑；公告導言「超過 1 億」與細節「1.07 億」個單細胞輪廓也有小落差。 : every figure was chosen and run by the publisher, and what is missing is a third party rerunning it on unseen data; the post's "more than 100 million" and its detailed "107 million" single-cell profiles also differ slightly.

Lor / Fern (Mirendil)

代理人驅動的 AI 研發堆疊 · Mirendil（美國）Agent-driven AI R&D stack · Mirendil (US)

功能與定位。LorLor 讓代理人寫程式、跑實驗、連續數日迭代，並自行調度算力，目前私有測試；FernFern 是從零預訓練、上下文最高 1,000 萬 token 的首個自有模型。買方是其他 AI 實驗室與研發團隊，公司把使命寫成「讓前沿 AI 研發民主化」（Mirendil）。 Function and position. LorLor has agents write code, run experiments and iterate for days while provisioning their own compute, currently in private beta; FernFern is the first in-house model, pretrained from scratch with a context window up to 10M tokens. The buyers are other AI labs and R&D teams, and the company states its mission as democratising frontier AI R&D (Mirendil).

  • 優勢Strength ：成本主張若成立，確實改變門檻——公司稱 Mirendil-Base 由一位研究者導引代理人、3.5 個月、全部算力不到 1,000 萬美元完成（Tech Times）。 : if the cost claim holds, it does move the threshold — the company says Mirendil-Base was completed by one researcher directing agents over 3.5 months on under US$10m of compute all-in (Tech Times).
  • 優勢Strength ：公司對自己的能力主張有分寸，明說模型並非自主建成、也未達到完全自主的遞歸自我改進——在這個敘事密度下，這種克制本身是訊號。 : the company is measured about its own claims, saying plainly that the model was not built autonomously and has not reached fully autonomous recursive self-improvement — at this narrative density, that restraint is itself a signal.
  • 疑慮Concern ：沒有任何第三方評測。唯一偏外部的指標是七個外部程式碼庫上的 bits-per-byte 0.222，對 Kimi-K3 的 0.193——越低越好，所以它是落後方；1,000 萬 token 僅由基本 RULER 測試支撐，而簡單檢索測試會高估長上下文能力。 : there is no third-party evaluation. The one quasi-external metric is bits-per-byte on seven external code repositories at 0.222 against Kimi-K3's 0.193 — lower is better, so it is the one behind — and the 10M-token figure rests on a basic RULER test, which overstates long-context ability.
  • 疑慮Concern ：缺的不是數據而是護欄。Lor 的代理人自行調度算力，而公開資料裡沒有任何安全檢查、沙箱或支出上限的描述；投資人與算力供應商、晶片賣方高度重疊。 : what is missing is not data but guardrails. Lor's agents provision their own compute, and nothing in the public material describes safety checks, sandboxing or spending limits; the investor list overlaps heavily with the compute supplier and the chip vendor.

04 — Taiwan Angle

三件事：供應鏈的真實風險點、代理人權限與既有法規的落差、以及一條只開放非商業研究的 API Three things: where the supply-chain risk actually sits, the gap between agent authority and existing rules, and an API open only to non-commercial research

(1) 供應鏈的風險不在產能，在接單程序與擔保安排。多家分析機構對台積電的直接衝擊評估偏低：Counterpoint Research 的李威廉指出目前沒有輝達減少下單的跡象，Switchyard Partners 的凱瑟認為台積電能快速把產能轉給其他客戶（公視新聞）。但真正在動的是程序面——經濟日報 引日經報導，一位台灣伺服器業主管親赴東南亞查核小型客戶的 AI 伺服器是否確實啟用、正常運作，過去不需要這個層級的主管出面；另一家伺服器業者已要求業務暫停接受不熟悉的新客戶與小型客戶訂單，涵蓋輝達、超微與英特爾產品；業者也在美方尚未公布新限制前自行加強查核客戶與出貨地點。台新新光金控首席經濟學家李鎮宇的判斷是黃仁勳靠個人魅力整合 AI 供應鏈，未來決策可能轉趨保守；貝恩旗下 Proxima 的漢普登則認為不能因換執行長就推論晶片供應吃緊，真正關鍵是訂單、長期產能承諾、銷售管道與法規變化。對台灣的醫療 AI 團隊來說，可操作的觀察點因此不是輝達股價，而是地端 GPU 的交期與長約條件是否變化。 (1) The supply-chain risk is not capacity — it is order intake and the guarantee arrangements. Several analysts put the direct impact on TSMC low: Counterpoint Research's William Li says there is no sign of Nvidia cutting orders, and Switchyard Partners' Kasser believes TSMC can shift capacity to other customers quickly (PTS News). What is actually moving is procedure — Economic Daily News, citing Nikkei, reports a Taiwanese server executive travelling to Southeast Asia personally to verify that small customers' AI servers are switched on and running, a check that never previously required someone at that level, while another server maker has told sales to stop accepting orders from unfamiliar new and small customers across Nvidia, AMD and Intel products, and Taiwanese firms have tightened customer and destination checks of their own accord before any new US restrictions are published. Taishin Financial Holdings chief economist Lee Chen-yu reads Huang as having held the AI supply chain together through personal standing, with decisions likely to turn conservative; Chris Hampden of Bain's Proxima argues a change of CEO is no basis for inferring a chip squeeze, and that what matters is orders, long-term capacity commitments, sales channels and regulatory change. For a medical-AI team in Taiwan, the actionable indicator is therefore not Nvidia's share price but whether lead times and long-term contract terms for on-premise GPUs change.

(2) 代理人被交付的權限，已經超出〈醫療機構應用生成式人工智慧指引〉寫得到的範圍。衛福部的〈醫療機構應用生成式人工智慧指引〉於民國 115 年 5 月 29 日以衛部醫字第 1151663164 號函頒，重點在病人隱私權與知情權：使用具錄影功能的生成式 AI 應事前告知病患，AI 工具若直接與民眾對話須主動揭露（台灣醫院協會）。用這把尺量這週的三個產品，結果不一樣：Claude Meetings 的「自我標示為參與者、在會議聊天室張貼通知」幾乎是這條揭露原則的工程實作，而且多做了一層失效即關閉——未經管理員授權不加入有外部來賓的通話、外部來賓一加入就離開（TechCrunch）。ChatGPT Wallet 那種「每筆生物辨識核准、但支出上限尚未推出」的模型落在指引之外：揭露與知情處理的是「病人知不知道 AI 在場」，沒有處理「代理人可以代替機構做多大的決定、額度多少、誰事後追得回來」。這是一個規範缺口，不是違規——指引發布時市場上還沒有這類產品。 (2) The authority being handed to agents already exceeds what Taiwan's generative-AI guidance for healthcare institutions addresses. Taiwan's Ministry of Health and Welfare issued its Guidance on the Use of Generative AI in Healthcare Institutions on 29 May 2026 (ref. 1151663164), centred on patient privacy and the right to be informed: patients must be told in advance where generative AI with recording capability is used, and an AI tool that speaks directly with the public must disclose itself (Taiwan Hospital Association). Measured against that ruler, this week's three products land differently. Claude Meetings' self-identification as a participant and its notice in the meeting chat is close to an engineering implementation of that disclosure principle, with a fail-closed layer on top — no joining calls with external guests without admin authorisation, and leaving as soon as one joins (TechCrunch). ChatGPT Wallet's model — biometric approval per transaction, spending limits not yet available — falls outside the guidance entirely: disclosure and informed consent address whether the patient knows AI is present, not how large a decision an agent may take on an institution's behalf, under what limit, and who can reverse it afterwards. That is a regulatory gap rather than a violation — products of this kind did not exist when the guidance was issued.

(3) AlphaGenome 2 對台灣是一條可用的研究工具，但授權條件就是它的天花板。值得注意的是這款模型的取用條件：今日起經 API 開放非商業研究，商業研究為私有預覽，且公告明寫僅供研究、未經臨床或診斷用途驗證或核准（DeepMind）。「不需重訓練即可用自己的單細胞資料條件化、幾十個細胞的快照就能追平微調模型」這一點，對沒有大型算力預算的學術實驗室價值最高，而這正好是台灣多數基因體研究團隊的處境。要說清楚的是，以下屬於本報推論而非引自來源：授權條件意味著任何想把它接進自費檢測、LDTs 或臨床報告流程的做法，在現行條件下都不成立；能做的是假說生成與變異優先排序，之後仍需實驗驗證——這也是 DeepMind 自己寫下的界線。 (3) For Taiwan, AlphaGenome 2 is a usable research tool, and its licence terms are also its ceiling. The access terms deserve attention: the API opens today for non-commercial research with commercial research in private preview, and the announcement states plainly that the model is research-only and not validated or approved for clinical or diagnostic purposes (DeepMind). The property that matters most — conditioning on your own single-cell data without retraining, matching fine-tuned models from a snapshot of a few dozen cells — is worth most to academic labs without a large compute budget, which is where most of Taiwan's genomics groups sit. To be explicit, what follows is this report's inference rather than anything drawn from the sources: those terms mean that wiring the model into self-pay testing, LDTs or a clinical reporting pipeline does not work under the current licence. What it can do is hypothesis generation and variant prioritisation, still followed by experimental validation — which is the boundary DeepMind drew itself.
