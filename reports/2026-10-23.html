環境式病歷廠商花了三年往上爬，爬到的位置 Epic 10/21 說它要自己站——Connectors 用 MCP 當第一個標準，而 Abridge 八天前才把 Assistant 開給每一位臨床人員 The ambient scribe vendors spent three years climbing toward one position. On 10/21 Epic said it intends to stand there itself — Connectors, with MCP as the first standard — eight days after Abridge opened Assistant to every clinician it reaches

2026 年 10 月 23 日（五）· 涵蓋 10/13–10/22 · 台北時間 Friday, October 23, 2026 · Covering Oct 13–22 · Taipei time

今天只拆一件事：臨床人員打開電腦後，第一個跟他講話的 AI 是誰的。Epic 10/21 公告 Connectors，讓第三方開發者把服務接進 Epic、讓 Epic 的 AI 去別人的 App 取資料與下指令；Fierce Healthcare 補上第一階段用 Model Context Protocol、2027 年 3 月才上 Showroom。而 10/13，Abridge 把 Assistant 開給旗下每一位臨床人員，不論是否用它寫病歷；10/7 它簽下德州大學系統八家機構，9/14 又用 pre-bill review 踩進中段營收循環。兩條路線撞在同一塊地上，而這一週的錢（Suki 9,000 萬美元 E 輪、Confido 1,250 萬美元增額）全押在同一個假設上。 One question today: when a clinician opens the computer, whose AI speaks first. Epic announced Connectors on 10/21, letting third-party developers wire their services into Epic so Epic's AI can pull data out of other apps and act inside them; Fierce Healthcare adds that the first standard is the Model Context Protocol and that Showroom listings wait until March 2027. And on 10/13 Abridge opened Assistant to every clinician at every Abridge health system, whether or not they use it for notes; on 10/7 it signed the University of Texas System's eight institutions, and on 9/14 stepped into the mid-revenue cycle with pre-bill review. The two routes have collided on one patch of ground — and this week's money (Suki's $90M Series E, Confido's $12.5M extension) is riding on the same assumption.

平台Platform EpicConnectors10/21

Epic 用 MCP 開放 Connectors，把 Art／Emmie／Penny 推成跨 App 的動作層——卻明說「不會產生新的自主動作」 Epic opens Connectors on MCP, turning Art, Emmie and Penny into a cross-app action layer — while stating flatly that it creates "no new autonomous actions"

發生什麼What

10/21，Epic 在 Open@Epic 大會前公告 Connectors：由 App 開發者建置的連接器，讓 Epic 從其他 App 取資訊、或在其中完成任務，官方說法是省掉「轉椅工作」。能力現在開放給開發者，但醫療機構要到2027 年 3 月才能在 Epic Showroom 找到。Fierce Healthcare 補上三點：第一階段標準是 Model Context Protocol、2026 年底先與部分醫療體系測試、開發者須付費上架且價格未公布。適用病人端 Emmie、臨床端 Art、營收循環端 Penny，以及醫療體系在 Agent Factory 自建的 agent（8 月登場時帶逾 100 個預建功能）。文中引述 Availity、Gozio Health、VPL，但未確認為首發夥伴。 On 10/21, ahead of its Open@Epic conference, Epic announced Connectors: developer-built links letting Epic pull information from other apps or complete tasks inside them, which Epic frames as sparing people "swivel-chair work." The capability goes to developers now, but organizations will not find them on Epic Showroom until March 2027. Fierce Healthcare adds three points: the first standard is the Model Context Protocol, testing with some health systems starts by end-2026, and developers pay to list at undisclosed prices. It works with Emmie for patients, Art for clinicians, Penny for revenue cycle, and agents built in Agent Factory, which debuted in August with over 100 pre-built features. Availity, Gozio Health and VPL are quoted but not confirmed as launch partners.

為什麼重要Why it matters

過去三年環境式病歷廠商的成長故事都是同一句：先用病歷進駐工作流，再往上長成臨床助理。Epic 把那條路的終點畫在自己家門口——若 Art 能透過 Connectors 去別人的 App 下指令，跨系統的 agent 協作層就不必是第三方的。更關鍵的是權限設計：健康資訊副總裁 Johnston Thayer 明說「權限全部在醫療體系層級處理」、「不會因為這些能力而產生新的自主動作」，把風險留給醫院、把編排權留在 Epic。 For three years the ambient scribe growth story has been one sentence: enter the workflow through the note, then grow upward into the clinician's assistant. Epic has drawn the end of that road at its own front door — if Art can issue commands inside other vendors' apps, the cross-system orchestration layer need not be third-party. The permission design matters more: VP of health informatics Johnston Thayer said "permissions are all handled at the health system level" and "there will not be new autonomous actions taken as a result of these capabilities," leaving risk with the hospital and orchestration with Epic.

要打折的地方Discount this

這是公告，不是出貨。Showroom 要等 2027 年 3 月，Epic 未公布任何使用者數、營收或醫療體系首發名單；可引用的只有 Agent Factory 的 100+ 預建功能與 Epic 自己對 Emmie 跑的「數千個測試案例」，沒有第三方稽核數據。 This is an announcement, not a shipment. Showroom listings wait until March 2027, and Epic published no user counts, revenue or named launch partners; all that is quotable is Agent Factory's 100-plus pre-built features and the "thousands of test cases" Epic runs on Emmie itself — no third-party audited data.

企業級合約Enterprise deal AbridgeUT System10/7

Abridge 拿下德州大學系統八家機構：一年近 1,170 萬次門診、逾 200 萬住院日，試行 12 個月錄音臨床人員增 73%、月病歷數增 80% Abridge signs the University of Texas System's eight institutions: nearly 11.7M outpatient visits and over 2M hospital days a year, after a 12-month pilot grew recording clinicians 73% and monthly notes 80%

發生什麼What

10/7 公告、Fierce Healthcare 10/9 報導：Abridge 與 UT REAL Health AI 簽企業級協議，八家機構（UT Southwestern、UT Medical Branch、MD Anderson、Dell 醫學院、UT Health Tyler、UTHealth Houston、UT Health San Antonio、UT Health Rio Grande Valley）皆可取用其平台，實作決定權留在各校。UT Health 自有與聯盟院所最近一年提供近 1,170 萬次門診、逾 200 萬住院日；2025 年起的試行涵蓋五院區、逾 50 個專科，過去 12 個月錄音臨床人員成長 73%、月病歷數成長 80%。Abridge 自述服務 300 家醫療體系、今年支撐逾 1 億次醫病對話。The Daily Texan 10/21 跟進。 Announced 10/7 and reported by Fierce Healthcare on 10/9: Abridge and UT REAL Health AI signed an enterprise agreement giving all eight institutions — UT Southwestern, UT Medical Branch, MD Anderson, Dell Medical School, UT Health Tyler, UTHealth Houston, UT Health San Antonio and UT Health Rio Grande Valley — access to its platform, with implementation decisions left to each campus. UT Health-owned and affiliated sites delivered nearly 11.7 million outpatient visits and more than 2 million hospital days in the last year; the pilot launched in 2025 spanned five locations and more than 50 specialties, and over 12 months grew recording clinicians 73% and monthly notes 80%. Abridge says it serves 300 health systems and will support more than 100 million conversations this year. The Daily Texan followed up on 10/21.

為什麼重要Why it matters

形狀比金額重要：這不是一次採購，而是治理框架——2025 年成立的 UT REAL Health AI 做系統級把關，各校保留在地決策。Becker's 整理的 2026 年 15 筆企業級 AI 合約顯示，同一個德州大學系統 1 月才把生成式 AI 平台給了 Qualified Health。一邊簽治理平台、一邊簽點解決方案，說明大型學術體系已改成分層採購：治理層、介面層、工作流層各自招標，而 Abridge 拿到的是介面層。 The shape matters more than the price: this is not a purchase but a governance framework, with system-level vetting through UT REAL Health AI, established in 2025, and local decision-making retained. Becker's roundup of 15 enterprise AI deals in 2026 shows the same UT System handed its generative AI platform to Qualified Health in January. Signing a governance platform and a point solution separately means large academic systems now buy in layers — governance, interface and workflow tendered apart — and what Abridge won is the interface layer.

要打折的地方Discount this

沒有金額、沒有合約期、沒有授權數——Fierce 明寫未揭露財務條件。300 家體系與 1 億次對話都是自述；73%／80% 是相對成長，沒有絕對基數。「八家都能取用」不等於八家都已部署。 No contract value, no term, no license count — Fierce states that no financial terms were disclosed. The 300 systems and 100 million conversations are self-reported; 73%/80% are relative growth with no absolute base. And "all eight can access it" is not the same as all eight having deployed it.

產品Product AbridgeAssistant / pre-bill10/13

Abridge Assistant 開給每一位臨床人員——包含不用它寫病歷的人；同時用 pre-bill review 往下踩進理賠前的中段循環 Abridge Assistant goes to every clinician — including those who don't use it for notes — while pre-bill review steps down into the mid-cycle before the claim

發生什麼What

10/13 Abridge 官方貼文：Assistant 是文字或語音的臨床助理，在 EHR 內或手機上皆可用，自 9 月版起每一家醫療體系的每一位臨床人員都能使用，不論是否用 Abridge 寫病歷；能力含引用來源的病歷查詢、依指引答題、自動帶值的計算器、轉診與交班草稿、以及多步驟請求的可見推理步驟，接地來源是病歷＋當次對話＋可信證據＋各體系自己的臨床路徑。Fierce 8/17 報導給了數字：自 4 月起逾 300 家體系採用，月活躍超過可用臨床人員的一半、每人查詢數兩個月翻三倍，證據來源含 UpToDate、NEJM、JAMA Network、JCO。另一條線是 9/14 的 pre-bill review：住院理賠送出前比對已編碼診斷與 DRG 跟病歷支撐的落差並列出證據，但不改病歷、不改碼、不改理賠狀態。 In its 10/13 post, Abridge describes Assistant as a chat-and-voice clinical assistant usable inside the EHR or on a phone; as of the September release, every clinician at every Abridge health system can use it, whether or not they use Abridge for documentation. Capabilities include chart queries with each source cited, guideline-based answers, calculators pre-filled with chart values, referral and handoff drafts, and visible reasoning steps on multi-step requests; grounding is the chart plus the conversation plus trusted evidence plus each system's own clinical pathways. Fierce's 8/17 report supplies numbers: adopted by 300-plus systems since April, with monthly actives past half of eligible clinicians and queries per clinician tripling in two months, drawing on UpToDate, NEJM, the JAMA Network and JCO. The other thread is pre-bill review, launched 9/14: before an inpatient claim goes out it compares coded diagnoses and DRG against what the documentation supports and shows the evidence — but changes no documentation, no codes and no claim status.

為什麼重要Why it matters

「開給不用我們寫病歷的人」是把環境式病歷從產品變成通路：同一套已談好的資安、治理與 EHR 整合零邊際成本地載第二個產品，把三年的採購摩擦一次折現。UPMC 的 Robert Bart 說「超過 60% 的使用者是自己發現的」，CHRISTUS 的 Tim Barker 說「它不打斷你，需要時就在那裡」——描述的不是功能，是預設值。而執行長 Shiv Rao 把另一條線的商業模式講得更白：「醫療體系是依照他們所記錄下來、自己提供過的照護獲得給付的。」同一段錄音先省時間、再保收入。 Opening it to clinicians who don't use it for notes turns ambient documentation from a product into a distribution channel: a second product rides security, governance and EHR integrations already negotiated, discounting three years of procurement friction at once. UPMC's Robert Bart says "more than 60% of our Abridge users have adopted it largely through self-discovery" and CHRISTUS's Tim Barker says "it's not interruptive, it's simply there when you need it" — neither describes a feature; both describe a default. CEO Shiv Rao states the other thread's business model outright: health systems "are reimbursed for the care they document that they delivered." One recording first saves time, then protects revenue.

要打折的地方Discount this

全部自述，而缺的那類數字最關鍵：沒有公開的準確率、幻覺率或安全性 benchmark，「臨床人員撰寫 rubric＋人類與 LLM 評審」是內部流程。Sutter Health 的 78% 是工作滿意度，不是正確性，而且被放進一個營收循環產品的發布稿裡。pre-bill review 也沒有任何 DRG 準確率或否決率下降的數字。 All self-reported, and the missing category is the important one: no published accuracy, hallucination or safety benchmark, and "clinician-built rubrics with human and LLM judges" is an internal process. Sutter Health's 78% measures work satisfaction, not correctness — and sits inside a revenue cycle launch. Pre-bill review carries no DRG accuracy or denial-rate figure at all.

自主化Autonomy AKASAAutonomous mid-cycle10/2

AKASA 宣稱出院後 90 秒內全自主完成複雜住院編碼：客戶合計逾 1,800 億美元淨病人收入、約占全美出院人次 10%，準確率百分比一個都沒公布 AKASA claims fully autonomous coding of complex inpatient cases within 90 seconds of discharge — customers at over $180B in net patient revenue and roughly 10% of U.S. discharges — and publishes not one accuracy percentage

發生什麼What

10/2 AKASA 發布中段營收循環自主 AI 平台，把 AI prebill review 延伸成住院編碼與 CDI 的自主化，聲稱可在無人介入下完成全科別高複雜度個案。數字：過去一年住院處理量成長近 6 倍、客戶合計逾 1,800 億美元淨病人收入、約占全美住院出院人次 10%、出院後 90 秒內完成編碼（人類編碼師 30–60 分鐘）。第三方盲測涵蓋「占住院量 65%–85%」的個案，AKASA 稱其 AI 在 MS-DRG 指派、主要診斷、臨床品質擷取與 POA 準確率上「達到或超過」人類編碼師。背景引用 2025 年 npj Health Systems 研究指編碼錯誤率最高達 20%，以及 2026 年 7 月 GAO 報告把可驗證準確率列為核心挑戰。Cleveland Clinic 已用其 prebill 產品、表示「有意探索」自主方案。 On 10/2 AKASA launched an autonomous mid-cycle AI platform, extending AI prebill review into autonomy for inpatient coding and CDI and claiming to fully code highly complex cases across all specialties with no human intervention. The figures: inpatient volume processed up nearly 6x in a year, customers at more than $180 billion in aggregate net patient revenue and roughly 10% of U.S. inpatient discharges, with coding complete within 90 seconds of discharge against 30–60 minutes for a human coder. Third-party blinded evaluations covered encounters making up "65%–85% of inpatient volume," and AKASA says its AI matched or exceeded human coders on MS-DRG assignment, principal diagnosis, clinical quality capture and present-on-admission accuracy. For context it cites a 2025 npj Health Systems study putting coding error rates as high as 20% and a July 2026 GAO review naming verifiable accuracy as a central challenge. Cleveland Clinic, already using its prebill products, says it "intends to explore" autonomy.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是從另一端攻同一塊地：Abridge 從對話往下走到理賠，AKASA 從理賠往上走到文件，兩邊在 CDI 碰頭。差別在對自主性的態度——Abridge 明說不改碼，AKASA 明說無人介入。執行長 Malinka Walaliyadde 說「自主的中段循環多年來是這個產業的聖杯」，a16z 的 Julie Yoo 則說「醫療無法只靠人力滿足未來的需求規模」。同一週 Savista 與 Minset 聲稱達「最高 93% 完全免接觸編碼」，初期 100% 全稽核、之後轉 10% 抽樣——方向一致，口徑保守得多。 This attacks the same ground from the other end: Abridge walks down from the conversation to the claim, AKASA walks up from the claim to the documentation, and they meet at CDI. The difference is their stance on autonomy — Abridge says it changes no codes, AKASA says no human intervenes. CEO Malinka Walaliyadde calls an autonomous mid-cycle "a holy grail in our industry"; a16z's Julie Yoo says "healthcare cannot meet the scale of demand ahead through human labor alone." The same week Savista and Minset claimed up to 93% fully touchless coding, auditing 100% of charts initially before moving to 10% sampling — same direction, far more conservative framing.

要打折的地方Discount this

廠商發布稿，而最該公布的數字正好缺席：「達到或超過人類編碼師」沒有任何百分點，盲測的樣本數、執行者與方法均未公開，「涵蓋住院量 65%–85%」是範圍不是結果。「約占全美出院人次 10%」是客戶規模而非自主處理比例——發布稿自己寫了部署會「依各體系需求調整自主量」。 A vendor release whose most important number is precisely the one missing: "matched or exceeded human coders" comes with no percentage points, and the evaluations' sample size, evaluator and methodology are unpublished — "65%–85% of inpatient volume" is a scope, not a result. "Roughly 10% of U.S. discharges" is customer size, not the share handled autonomously: the release itself says deployments scale autonomous volume to each system's needs.

募資Funding Suki / ConfidoAsk / CareOS10/13–10/14

同一週兩筆錢投在同一個假設上：Suki 9,000 萬美元 E 輪（累計逾 2.5 億）、Confido 1,250 萬美元增額把 A 輪推到 2,250 萬 Two cheques the same week on one assumption: Suki's $90M Series E (past $250M raised) and Confido's $12.5M extension taking its Series A to $22.5M

發生什麼What

10/13 宣布、10/15 報導：Suki 完成 9,000 萬美元 E 輪、累計逾 2.5 億美元（估值未揭露），新投資人 Healthier Capital、Hercules Capital、Crosslink Capital。自述逾 250 家醫療機構、逾 30 家把 Suki AI 嵌進自家平台的技術夥伴、2026 年支撐就診量年增逾 190%。10/8 推出 Suki Ask，是 Wolters Kluwer UpToDate Expert AI API 的首個整合，跨 Epic／Oracle Health／athenahealth／MEDITECH，早期評估顯示臨床人員 83% 偏好帶病人情境的答案（p<0.001）。同一週 Confido Health 10/14 宣布 1,250 萬美元 A 輪增額（Z47 領投，A 輪累計 2,250 萬），CareOS 整合逾 50 個系統、覆蓋逾 1,500 個照護據點、約 3,200 位醫師、27 州。 Announced 10/13 and reported 10/15: Suki closed a $90M Series E, taking total funding past $250M at an undisclosed valuation, with new investors Healthier Capital, Hercules Capital and Crosslink Capital. It reports more than 250 healthcare organizations, over 30 technology partners embedding its AI, and encounters supported up more than 190% year over year in 2026. Suki Ask launched 10/8 as the first integration of Wolters Kluwer's UpToDate Expert AI API across Epic, Oracle Health, athenahealth and MEDITECH, with clinicians preferring answers carrying patient context 83% of the time (p<0.001) in early evaluations. The same week, Confido Health announced a $12.5M Series A extension on 10/14 led by Z47, taking its Series A to $22.5M; CareOS integrates with more than 50 systems across 1,500-plus sites of care, about 3,200 providers and 27 states.

為什麼重要Why it matters

三家公司同一個月做同一件事：Abridge 的 Assistant、Suki 的 Ask、Heidi 的 agentic 路線，全是「病歷之外再加一層決策支援」，而且都接 UpToDate——證據層已被 Wolters Kluwer 標準化成 API，差異化不在內容而在情境，Suki 那個 83% 正是在賣情境。但兩家的賭法相反：Suki 把 AI 嵌進別人的平台（逾 30 家夥伴），選擇做零件而非介面，若 Epic Connectors 成為標準，風險比自建介面低；Confido 則繞過醫療體系的治理審查，先賣給私募股權基金再進投資組合——報導引用 AMA 2025 年數據，PE 持有約 6.5% 的美國醫師執業體、部分地方市場達 50%，而支援人力薪酬約占執業體營收 25–30%。 Three companies did the same thing in one month: Abridge's Assistant, Suki's Ask and Heidi's agentic turn all add a decision-support layer on top of the note, and all wire to UpToDate — the evidence layer has been standardized into an API by Wolters Kluwer, so differentiation moves from content to context, which is what Suki's 83% is selling. But the two bets run opposite: Suki embeds its AI in other companies' platforms (30-plus partners), choosing to be a component rather than an interface, which carries less risk than a front door of its own if Epic Connectors becomes the standard; Confido skips health system governance review entirely, selling to private equity firms that then push it into their portfolios — the report cites AMA 2025 data putting PE ownership at about 6.5% of U.S. physician practices, up to 50% in some local markets, with support staff compensation at roughly 25–30% of practice revenue.

要打折的地方Discount this

Suki 估值未揭露，「營收翻倍」沒有基期金額；83% 衡量偏好，不是正確率或病人結果，報導本身就這樣註明。Confido 的 95% 處理率、3–5 名 FTE 等值產能與 1,000–1,500 萬美元年省全為公司提供且報導明寫未經獨立驗證，年省更標明是 projected；增額在 2 月完成、10 月才公布。 Suki's valuation is undisclosed and "revenue doubled" comes without a base; the 83% measures preference, not accuracy or patient outcomes, as the report itself notes. Confido's 95% handle rate, 3–5 FTE equivalents and $10M–$15M annual savings are company-provided and flagged as not independently verified, with the savings explicitly projected — and an extension that closed in February was announced in October.

02 — Product Analysis

Ambience Chorus / The Ambience Standard

把授權費放上賭桌的結果導向合約 · Ambience Healthcare（美國）An outcome-based contract that puts the license fee at risk · Ambience Healthcare (US)

功能與定位。8/19 推出的 The Ambience Standard 不是功能而是商業模式：不按席次、不按 token、不按功能計價，而是把軟體費用綁在經過查核的臨床、營運與財務結果上。三個組件是風險承擔型績效合約（把廠商費用押在雙方同意的指標上）、前進部署轉型專員（執業臨床人員與系統工程師進駐醫院），以及 Ambience Chorus——橫跨逾 200 個內外科次專科、協調情境與臨床護欄的 AI agent harness。 Function and position. The Ambience Standard, launched 8/19, is not a feature but a commercial model: no per-seat, per-token or per-feature pricing, but software fees tied to verified clinical, operational and financial outcomes. Its three parts are Value-at-Risk Performance Contracts staking vendor fees on mutually agreed metrics, Forward-Deployed Transformation Specialists placing practicing clinicians and systems engineers inside health systems, and Ambience Chorus, an AI agent harness coordinating context and clinical guardrails across more than 200 medical and surgical subspecialties.

  • 優勢Strength ：它拿出的是今天唯一帶同儕審查字樣的數據——St. Luke's 的三個月評估（經同儕審查）顯示醫師 burnout 由 45% 降至 31%、離職意向由 31% 降至 18%；Cleveland Clinic 四個月部署 4,000 位臨床人員、留存使用率 70%、NPS 60；MultiCare 採用率 92%；Ardent 自述 3 倍 ROI。 : it brings the only data today carrying the words peer-reviewed — a peer-reviewed three-month evaluation at St. Luke's showing physician burnout falling from 45% to 31% and turnover intent from 31% to 18%; Cleveland Clinic deployed 4,000 clinicians in four months at 70% sustained utilization and NPS 60; MultiCare reports 92% adoption; Ardent claims 3x verified ROI.
  • 疑慮Concern ：最響的那個數字最薄。「編碼表現超越臨床人員 27%」出自2025 年 5 月 27 日的公司貼文，是對 18 位 board-certified 醫師的相對改善、benchmark 未命名、個案數未揭露、無信賴區間、雙方絕對準確率一個都沒給，且是 RFT 微調模型而非同儕審查研究。 : the loudest number is the thinnest. "Surpasses clinician performance by 27% in medical coding" comes from a company post dated May 27, 2025: a relative improvement over 18 board-certified physicians on an unnamed benchmark, with no case count, no confidence intervals and no absolute accuracy for either side — a reinforcement-fine-tuned model, not a peer-reviewed study.
  • 疑慮Concern ：合約條款與資本節奏都不透明。「押在雙方同意的指標上」未公布任何保證門檻、押注比例或合約期；最後一次揭露的募資是2025 年 7 月 29 日的 2.43 億美元 C 輪、估值逾 10 億美元——比 Abridge 近一年的節奏安靜得多。 : neither the contract terms nor the capital cadence is transparent. "At risk against mutually agreed metrics" comes with no published thresholds, stake percentage or term; and the last disclosed raise is the $243M Series C at a valuation over $1B on July 29, 2025 — a markedly quieter year than Abridge's.

今天的競爭結構是一個夾擠。環境式病歷廠商從對話往兩端延伸——往上是臨床助理與決策支援（Abridge Assistant、Suki Ask），往下是編碼與理賠（Abridge pre-bill、Ambience Chorus）；而兩端都已經有人在等：往上是 Epic 的 Art 與 Connectors，往下是 AKASA 與 Savista／Minset 這些以自主率為賣點的營收循環廠商。能活下來的分化只有三種形狀：手握 Epic 拿不到的原始資料（對話錄音的完整情境）、手握醫院不想自己承擔的風險（Ambience 把費用押上、AKASA 把自主性攬下），或乾脆不爭介面而當零件（Suki 被 30 家平台嵌入）。而今天最該記住的對比：宣稱「自主」或「超越人類」的那幾家，一個百分點都沒公布；同一週真正把準確率算到信賴區間的，是拿到 FDA 510(k) 的 SimBioSys。 Today's competitive structure is a squeeze. The ambient scribe vendors are extending from the conversation in both directions — upward into clinical assistants and decision support (Abridge Assistant, Suki Ask), downward into coding and claims (Abridge pre-bill, Ambience Chorus) — and someone is already waiting at both ends: upward, Epic's Art and Connectors; downward, AKASA and Savista-Minset, selling autonomy rates. Only three shapes of differentiation survive that: holding raw data Epic cannot get (the full context of the recorded conversation), holding risk the hospital would rather not carry (Ambience staking its fees, AKASA claiming the autonomy), or declining the interface fight to become a component (Suki, embedded in 30 platforms). And the contrast worth remembering today: of the vendors claiming autonomy or superhuman performance, not one published a percentage point — while the company that took its accuracy all the way to confidence intervals the same week was SimBioSys, with an FDA 510(k).

04 — Taiwan Angle

Epic 的 Connectors 是一家公司在私有生態裡建的連接層；台灣的同一層，是衛福部在建 Epic's Connectors is a connection layer one company builds inside its own ecosystem; in Taiwan the same layer is being built by the health ministry

(1) 美國的介面層之爭，前提是有一個主導的 EHR；台灣沒有，所以那一層正在由政府補位。Epic Connectors 一宣布就改變所有廠商的算式，是因為「接進 Epic」幾乎等於「接進市場」——聯合報 10/19 的報導就引用這個對比：美國約八成醫院使用同一套商用系統，而台灣各醫院的電子病歷系統多達數十種、彼此沒有共同標準，官員認為這是新技術落地慢的原因之一。台灣的解法是公部門蓋連接層：衛福部的次世代電子病歷平台以 FHIR 為基礎，目標2026 年底前完成全台 27 家醫學中心串接（長庚、馬偕、中山醫大附醫已完成試接），約 200–300 家區域與地區醫院預計 2027 年陸續完成，原始病歷仍留在各醫院、不集中儲存，並計畫2027 年 3 月前推出跨院調閱與 AI 研究的雙重同意機制。實質意義很直接：在美國，agent 要跨 App 做事得走 Epic 的 Connectors 並付上架費；在台灣那條路是公共基礎設施，誰都能接，但也意味著沒有任何一家私人廠商能靠「我是那層」收租。 (1) The U.S. fight over the interface layer presumes one dominant EHR. Taiwan has none, so the state is filling that layer itself. Epic Connectors changed every vendor's arithmetic on announcement because "connect to Epic" is nearly equivalent to "connect to the market" — a contrast United Daily News drew on 10/19, noting that about 80% of U.S. hospitals run the same commercial system while Taiwan's hospitals use dozens of different EMRs with no common standard, which officials call one reason new technology lands slowly. Taiwan's answer is a publicly built connection layer: the health ministry's next-generation EMR platform is built on FHIR, with all 27 medical centers connected by the end of 2026 (Chang Gung, Mackay and Chung Shan Medical University Hospital have completed trial connections) and roughly 200–300 regional and district hospitals finishing through 2027; original records stay at each hospital rather than in one central store, and a dual consent mechanism for cross-hospital access and AI research is planned by March 2027. The consequence is direct: in the U.S. an agent that wants to act across apps goes through Epic's Connectors and pays to list; in Taiwan that path is public infrastructure anyone can join — which also means no private vendor collects rent for being that layer.

(2) 台灣對環境式病歷的法規前提打在這個商業模式的根上：錄音要事前同意，病人可以拒絕；而準確率的責任落在醫師身上。衛福部 2026 年 5 月 30 日發布的「醫療機構應用生成式人工智慧指引」規定：AI 僅為輔助工具，臨床判斷、病人安全與病歷的最終責任在領照醫事人員身上，AI 不得取代醫療專業判斷；以 AI 協助撰寫病歷者須自行查證內容正確性；涉及錄音錄影者須事前告知並取得同意，病人拒絕時醫院不得要求其使用；病人若在與 AI 對話，機構必須說明。把這幾條疊在今天的主線上：Abridge「每一位臨床人員都能用」的鋪法在台灣要先過逐次的病人同意，而「須自行查證」這句話把 Abridge 與 Ambience 都不公開準確率的問題，直接轉成台灣醫師的個人責任。另一面是給付——衛福部科技處長李建璋在一場演講中說明，AI 要進健保給付需以隨機對照試驗證明能替健保省錢，之後才以論值支付，官方估計 2–3 年；這正好說明 Ambience 的 value-at-risk 合約與台灣健保的論值支付是同一種語法，而按席次授權不是。 (2) Taiwan's regulatory premise lands on the root of this business model: recording needs prior consent, the patient may refuse, and responsibility for accuracy sits with the physician. The health ministry's Guidelines for Medical Institutions Using Generative AI, issued 2026-05-30, hold that AI is a support tool only, that final responsibility for clinical judgment, patient safety and the record rests with licensed medical staff, and that AI must not replace professional medical judgment; clinicians using AI to draft records must verify the content's accuracy themselves; where audio or video is recorded, patients must be told in advance and consent, and a hospital cannot require the service if a patient refuses; and if a patient is talking to AI, the institution must say so. Stack that on today's main line: Abridge's "every clinician can use it" rollout must first pass encounter-by-encounter consent in Taiwan, and the duty to verify converts the fact that neither Abridge nor Ambience publishes an accuracy figure into the personal liability of the Taiwanese physician. The other side is payment — Li Chien-chang, director of the ministry's Department of Technology, explained in a talk that NHI reimbursement requires a randomized controlled trial showing the technology saves the NHI money before payment under value-based pricing, a process officials put at two to three years. Which is precisely why Ambience's value-at-risk contracts speak the same grammar as Taiwan's value-based NHI payment, and per-seat licensing does not.

Behind SimBioSys's 510(k) (release dated 10/15) sits a blinded, prospectively designed retrospective multicenter study of 2,138 patients: 5-year recurrence-free survival 95.5% vs 89.6%, 10-year 88.9% vs 69.6%, and a hazard ratio still 2.18 after adjustment. It also models how to read these: the release's "3.3-fold greater risk" does not match the reported HR of 2.81, and the article says so.
