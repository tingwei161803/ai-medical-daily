發生什麼What

10 月 15 日，Microsoft AI 公開了 MAI-Base-2 的預訓練細節：2.8 兆總參數、800 億啟用的 mixture-of-experts，比 MAI-Base-1 更大更稀疏，且每一層都是 MoE（前一代是 MoE 與 dense 層交錯）。它原生帶視覺，用 encoder-free 的 VLM 架構；優化器是 Muon，搭配 dropless multi-round MoE 設計與 activation gradient clipping。訓練於 2026 年 8、9 月間在 16,000 顆 GB200 上跑了 38 天，平均 MFU 21%、goodput 89%，訓練混合 50 兆 token、其中 18% 為 VLM 資料；獨特 token 量較前代成長約 36%，微軟聲明資料皆為有機取得、未從其他模型蒸餾。評測在 held-out 文本（bits-per-byte）、程式、STEM、數學與問答上進行，微軟說 BPB 低於它測過的每一個開放權重模型——DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base、Kimi K3、Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B、MiMo-V2.6-Pro-RL。預訓練已完成、後訓練進行中，計畫透過 Foundry 把權重授權給「可信夥伴」。 On 15 October, Microsoft AI published the pre-training details of MAI-Base-2: a mixture-of-experts model with 2.8T total and 80B active parameters, larger and sparser than MAI-Base-1 and with MoE in every layer where the previous generation alternated MoE and dense. It is natively vision-capable through an encoder-free VLM architecture, and uses the Muon optimizer with a dropless multi-round MoE design and activation gradient clipping. Training ran across August and September 2026 on 16,000 GB200 GPUs for 38 days at 21% average MFU and 89% goodput, over a 50T-token mixture of which 18% was VLM data; the corpus grew about 36% in unique tokens over MAI-Base-1, and Microsoft states all of it is organic rather than distilled from other models. Evaluation is on held-out text (bits-per-byte), code, STEM, maths and Q&A, and Microsoft reports lower BPB than every open-weight model it tested — DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base, Kimi K3, Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B and MiMo-V2.6-Pro-RL. Pre-training is complete and post-training underway, with weights to be licensed to "trusted partners" through Foundry.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是微軟第一次把「我們自己從頭預訓練前沿模型」講到這個層級——GPU 數、天數、MFU、goodput、優化器選擇都攤了出來。對醫療買方的意義不在模型本身（它連 context length 與 expert 數都還沒公布），而在於微軟正把對 OpenAI 的依賴換成第二條腿：醫療體系透過 Epic、Nuance／DAX、Azure 買到的底層模型，未來可能不只有 GPT 系列一種血統。而 encoder-free 原生 VLM 這個架構選擇，對影像密集的醫療場景特別關鍵——它意味著模型不是在文字模型上貼一個視覺編碼器，而是從預訓練第一天就同時看圖與讀字。 This is the most granular account Microsoft has given of doing frontier pre-training itself — GPU count, days, MFU, goodput and optimizer choice all on the page. For healthcare buyers the significance is not the model, whose context length and expert count are still unpublished, but that Microsoft is converting its dependence on OpenAI into a second leg: the base model a health system buys through Epic, Nuance/DAX or Azure may in future have more than one lineage. The encoder-free native VLM choice matters particularly for image-heavy clinical settings — the model is not a text model with a vision encoder bolted on, but something that looked at pictures and read words from the first day of pre-training.

為什麼重要Why it matters

「兩台 Mac Studio 跑得動 8,160 億參數的原生多模態模型」對醫療機構的意義跟對開發者完全不同。醫院要的從來不是最強，而是「病歷與影像不出我的機房」——而這個條件過去只能用小模型換，現在換到的是 100 萬 token 脈絡加原生視覺。一次塞進整份住院病程、所有檢驗趨勢與影像報告，不必切 chunk、不必做 RAG 檢索，這在臨床摘要與病歷審閱上是質的差別。llama.cpp 與 Unsloth 都在支援清單上，代表社群微調的門檻也壓低了：一間醫院用自己的去識別化資料做 LoRA，不需要把資料送出去。 "Two Mac Studios will run an 816B natively multimodal model" means something quite different to a hospital than to a developer. What hospitals want is rarely the strongest model; it is that records and images never leave their own machine room — and until now that condition cost you a small model. Here it buys a 1M-token context with native vision. A whole inpatient course, every lab trend and every imaging report fits in one pass, with no chunking and no RAG retrieval, a qualitative difference for clinical summarization and chart review. llama.cpp and Unsloth are both on the support list, so the bar for community fine-tuning drops too: a hospital can LoRA on its own de-identified data without sending any of it out.

「最有能力的美國開放權重模型」是 Meta 自己的說法，而發布文只連了一份評測方法報告、沒有列出任何具體分數，所以這句排名在發布當天無法驗證，更無從知道它在臨床任務上的表現。授權也不是 MIT 或 Apache：Muse Model License 帶商業限制，實際條款要逐條看，醫療機構若要把它放進收費的臨床產品，法務上不會比開源模型省事。SGLang 的 1.03 至 3.95 倍是一個很寬的區間，下緣幾乎等於沒有加速。「兩台 Mac Studio 跑得動」也沒說跑多快——沒有 tokens/s，而推論延遲在臨床工作流裡往往比峰值能力更決定能不能用。 "The most capable US open-weight model to date" is Meta's own claim, and the release links an evaluation methodology report without listing a single score, so that ranking could not be checked on the day — still less how it performs on clinical tasks. The licence is not MIT or Apache either: the Muse Model License carries commercial limits that have to be read term by term, and a health system putting it inside a paid clinical product will not find the legal work lighter than with a genuinely open model. The 1.03–3.95x SGLang figure is a very wide range whose bottom end is close to no speedup at all. And "runs on two Mac Studios" says nothing about how fast — there is no tokens/s figure, and inference latency decides clinical usability more often than peak capability does.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這篇的方法論比結論更值得注意。棕色脂肪在人體研究裡一直卡在同一個瓶頸：要量它得做冷刺激 PET-CT，收案昂貴、樣本永遠是幾十到幾百人。這篇做的事是把十五年來為了腫瘤分期而拍的十萬份臨床掃描當成一座既有的影像生物庫，用 AI 去撈一個當初沒人打算測的表型——換句話說，它把「不可能的世代研究」變成「已經躺在 PACS 裡的回溯資料」。對整個醫療 AI 領域，這是比任何 benchmark 分數都更具體的能力示範：AI 最立即的價值可能不是取代判讀，而是讓既有影像回答原本問不起的問題。而那條從影像關聯走到 losartan 的 UCP1 與耗氧實驗，示範了這類撈取如何回頭產生可驗證的機制假說，而不只是一堆相關係數。 The method here deserves more attention than the finding. Human brown-fat research has been stuck at the same bottleneck: measuring BAT requires cold-stimulated PET-CT, recruitment is expensive, and cohorts stay in the dozens to the low hundreds. What this paper does is treat fifteen years of clinical scans taken for cancer staging as an existing imaging biobank, and use an AI to pull out a phenotype nobody had set out to measure — turning an unaffordable prospective cohort into retrospective data already sitting in PACS. For medical AI as a field that is a more concrete demonstration of capability than any benchmark score: the most immediate value may not be replacing readers, but letting existing images answer questions that were never fundable. And the line running from an imaging association to UCP1 induction and oxygen-consumption experiments with losartan shows how such mining can generate a testable mechanistic hypothesis rather than just a pile of correlations.

Four days before the letter, on 12 October, William Zhou and Daniel Freeman of Anthropic's Frontier Red Team published research putting numbers on that threat. This race may not be decided by whose benchmark is higher, but by who first works out what hospitals are actually buying.

04 — Taiwan Angle

今天三份開放權重發布，剛好落在台灣《人工智慧基本法》風險分類框架上路、各部會 2028 年前要交卷的這個窗口裡 Today's three open-weight releases land inside the window in which Taiwan's AI Basic Act risk-classification framework has just taken effect and every ministry must report by 2028

(1) 「權重在院內」對台灣醫院的意義比對美國醫院更大。Muse Spark Open 的門檻是兩台 Mac Studio，Nemotron 3.5 Super VL 更低——從 Jetson 一路到 RTX 系統都跑得動。換算成台灣尺度，這是一間區域醫院的資訊室採購得起、而且不必接外網的東西。台灣醫療 AI 一直有個結構性限制：健保資料與病歷的跨境傳輸、去識別化要求與院內稽核流程，使「把病歷送去雲端 API」在許多場景裡光是法遵成本就不划算。原生多模態加 100 萬 token 脈絡的開放權重模型，第一次讓「院內自建、資料不出門、同時有接近前沿的能力」三件事同時成立。但前提是院內要有能維運模型的人——這正是台灣中小型醫院最缺的資源，所以短期實際受益者大概仍集中在幾家醫學中心。 (1) "Weights on the premises" means more to a Taiwanese hospital than to an American one. Muse Spark Open's floor is two Mac Studios; Nemotron 3.5 Super VL's is lower still — it runs from Jetson up to RTX systems. At Taiwanese scale that is something a regional hospital's IT department can buy and never connect to an outside network. Medical AI in Taiwan has a structural constraint: cross-border transfer of NHI data and medical records, de-identification requirements and internal audit processes make "send the chart to a cloud API" uneconomic on compliance cost alone in many settings. An open-weight model that is natively multimodal with a 1M-token context makes on-premise deployment, data that never leaves, and near-frontier capability possible at the same time for the first time. The precondition is staff who can operate a model in-house — precisely what Taiwan's small and mid-sized hospitals lack — so near-term beneficiaries will likely remain a handful of medical centres.

(2) 法規上台灣正處在「框架已訂、細則未出」的階段，而今天的消息剛好測試這個階段的彈性。《人工智慧基本法》2025 年 12 月 24 日三讀通過、2026 年 1 月 14 日公布，行政院 5 月 21 日提出施行規劃，數位發展部 7 月 7 日訂定《人工智慧風險分類框架》（盤點應用情境、識別風險、評估風險、應對風險四步），各目的事業主管機關須在 2028 年 1 月前完成主管領域的風險管理規範、產業指引與法規調適——醫療業的主管機關是衛福部。法律層面，第 4 條列出七項原則（含隱私與資料治理、資安、透明、公平與問責），第 16 條要求數位部推動可與國際介接的風險分類框架，第 17 條要求政府對高風險 AI 明確責任歸屬與救濟補償或保險機制，第 19 條要求政府機關使用 AI 時進行風險評估；而醫療產品的細部判斷仍走 TFDA——它在 2025 年 8 月修正了 CADe/CADx 醫療器材查驗登記技術指引並更新 AI/ML 醫材查驗登記問答集。這個分工的實務意涵是：一家醫院自建的院內模型若只用於行政與文書（如 R1 在美國做的理賠與 RCM 那類），落在基本法的風險分類框架底下而不是 TFDA 的醫材路徑；一旦它開始影響診斷判斷，就換一條門。今天 Nemotron 的兩個醫療用例剛好一邊一個，這條界線在台灣怎麼畫，得等衛福部交卷。另一方面，420 名科學家那封信在台灣對應到的是醫院的 IoT 現況而非前沿實驗室：Anthropic 量的那台 55 美元 Android box 與未認證的 ADB port，放大版就是台灣醫院裡生命週期十年以上、難以更新、不能隨意停機打補丁的床邊終端與影像工作站。開放權重讓院內自建變得可行的同一天，也讓「院內有一個能跑工具、能多步行動的模型」變成資安盤點上的新項目——這兩件事該一起編列預算，而不是分兩個年度。 (2) On the regulatory calendar Taiwan is at the "framework set, details pending" stage — and today's news tests how flexible that stage is. The AI Basic Act passed its third reading on 24 December 2025 and was promulgated on 14 January 2026; the Executive Yuan issued an implementation plan on 21 May, and the Ministry of Digital Affairs set an AI Risk Classification Framework on 7 July — four steps: inventory use cases, identify risk, assess risk, respond to risk. Every competent authority must complete its sector's risk-management rules, industry guidance and legal adaptation before January 2028; for healthcare that authority is the Ministry of Health and Welfare. In the statute, Article 4 lists seven principles including privacy and data governance, security, transparency, fairness and accountability; Article 16 directs the Ministry of Digital Affairs to advance an internationally interoperable risk-classification framework; Article 17 requires clear liability attribution and remedy, compensation or insurance mechanisms for high-risk AI; and Article 19 requires risk assessment when government agencies use AI. Detailed judgements on medical products still run through TFDA, which revised its CADe/CADx device registration technical guidance in August 2025 and updated its AI/ML device registration Q&A. The practical consequence: a hospital's in-house model used only for administration and documentation — the kind of claims and revenue-cycle work R1 does in the US — falls under the Basic Act's framework rather than TFDA's device pathway; the moment it influences diagnostic judgement, it goes through a different door. Nemotron's two healthcare use cases today sit one on each side of that line, and how Taiwan draws it awaits the Ministry's own filing. Separately, the 420-scientist letter maps onto hospital IoT here rather than onto frontier labs: the $55 Android box and unauthenticated ADB port Anthropic measured scale up into the bedside terminals and imaging workstations that run decade-plus lifecycles in Taiwanese hospitals, are hard to update, and cannot casually be taken offline to patch. The same day open weights made on-premise deployment feasible, they also made "there is a model inside our network that can use tools and act over multiple steps" a new line on the security inventory — and those two things belong in one budget, not two fiscal years.
