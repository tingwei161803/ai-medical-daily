2026 年 11 月 13 日（五）· 涵蓋 11/06–11/13 · 台北時間 Friday, November 13, 2026 · Covering Nov 6–13 · Taipei time

本週沒有一筆錢是付給模型的。11/10 Oura 的新聞稿宣布以 6.25 億美元全現金（subject to purchase price adjustments）買下 Counsel Health，一間 70 名員工、PitchBook 估算總融資 3,600 萬美元的公司——相當於每名員工 893 萬美元、交易價是已投入資本的 17 倍。買方自己在 9/29 才把 5,500 萬股、每股 40–44 美元、最高 22 億美元的 IPO 無限期延後，隱含估值正好 150 億美元。同一天 Charta Health 拿到 Insight Partners 領投的 5,500 萬美元 B 輪，賣的是「在 claim 送出之前就把病歷對帳完」；前一天 11/9 美國內科醫學會（ACP）把指引與 Annals of Internal Medicine 授權給 OpenEvidence。三筆交易、三個位置：消費者身上的連續生理訊號、理賠發生之前的病歷真相、醫師下決定那一秒看到的指引。而三筆交易公布的效能數字，沒有一個經過第三方稽核。 Not one dollar this week was paid for a model. On Nov 10 Oura's release announced it would pay $625 million in cash, subject to purchase price adjustments, for Counsel Health — a company with 70 employees and roughly $36 million raised by PitchBook's count. That is $8.93 million per head, and 17 times the capital ever put into the business. The buyer had shelved its own IPO indefinitely on Sept 29 — 55 million shares at $40–44, up to $2.2 billion, implying a valuation of exactly $15 billion. The same day Charta Health closed a $55 million Series B led by Insight Partners on the premise that the chart should be reconciled before the claim is filed; the day before, the American College of Physicians licensed its guidance and Annals of Internal Medicine to OpenEvidence. Three deals, three positions: the continuous physiological signal on the consumer's body, the truth of the chart before the claim exists, and the guideline a physician sees in the second they decide. Not one of the performance figures announced with them has been independently audited.

Founder Justin Liu and CTO Scott Morris were engineering leaders at Rockset, later acquired by OpenAI; the two spent a year earning Certified Professional Coder credentials and auditing with more than 100 health system and payer executives, and the company now requires every engineer and every go-to-market hire to obtain a medical coding certification — a deliberate bet against off-the-shelf generative models. Liu puts it plainly: "Payers and providers should agree on the chart before a claim goes out, not fight about it after." In an industry that earns money from retrospective denial and clawback, moving the point of conflict earlier removes legal costs from both sides at once.

No accuracy rate, no chart volume, no revenue recovered, no false-positive rate — and for a system that proactively suggests adding codes, the false-positive rate is precisely the number that should be published, because systematically over-eager suggestions amount to teaching providers to upcode. The co-development payer is unnamed, so there is no way to judge how deep the payer half of this two-sided story actually goes. Neither total funding nor valuation was disclosed.

04 — Taiwan Angle

本週三個位置，在台灣各自撞到一道不同的牆：遠距醫療法規、健保核刪制度、以及沒人授權的本土指引 The week's three positions each hit a different wall in Taiwan: the telemedicine rules, the NHI clawback regime, and a body of local guidance nobody has licensed

(1) Oura＋Counsel 這套「先跟 AI 聊、必要時叫醫師進來」的模式，在台灣法律上賣不了「看診」，只能賣「分流前的諮詢」。依衛福部 2024 年 1 月 22 日發布、同年 7 月 1 日施行的《通訊診察治療辦法》，通訊診療的特殊情形從 5 類擴大為 10 類（新增慢性病照護計畫收案病人、疾病末期照護、矯正機關收容照護、行動不便照護，以及災害、傳染病或其他重大變故照護），預估約 247 萬人受惠，服務項目包含醫療諮詢、會診、精神科心理治療與開立檢查檢驗單，並有條件開放開立處方。關鍵在於門檻：須經地方衛生局審查通過，或屬健保包裹式給付計畫的個案，醫師才可線上看診；違規視訊看診依《醫師法》可處 2 萬至 10 萬元罰鍰。換句話說，Counsel Health 那套「幾分鐘內有醫師加入對話、當場開處方與轉診」的產品形態，在台灣要落到 10 類之內並經衛生局核可，才是合法的「診療」；落不進去，就只能是健康諮詢。這也解釋了為什麼本土穿戴與健康 app 多年來只能停在「建議您就醫」這一句——不是技術做不到，是那句話後面的法律責任沒有通路。範圍確實在鬆動：2026 年 6 月 26 日衛福部（部長石崇良）宣布監所收容人視訊看診將擴及所有科別，並對「青年百億海外圓夢基金計畫」的海外青年提供遠距資源，目前海外遠距僅覆蓋越南、菲律賓、馬來西亞、印尼四國——但鬆動的方向是特定族群，不是通案開放。 (1) The Oura–Counsel model — chat with the AI, pull in a physician when needed — cannot legally be sold in Taiwan as a consultation. It can only be sold as pre-triage advice. Under the Regulations on Telemedicine Practice issued by the Ministry of Health and Welfare on Jan 22, 2024 and effective July 1 that year, the special circumstances permitting telemedicine expanded from five categories to ten (adding enrolled chronic-disease care patients, end-of-life care, care for inmates in correctional facilities, care for those with limited mobility, and care during disasters, epidemics or other major incidents), covering an estimated 2.47 million people, with services spanning medical advice, consultation, psychiatric psychotherapy and the ordering of tests, plus conditionally permitted prescribing. The threshold is what matters: a physician may see a patient online only where the local health bureau has approved it, or where the case falls under an NHI bundled-payment program; practising video consultation outside the rules carries a fine of NT$20,000 to NT$100,000 under the Physicians Act. So Counsel Health's shape — a physician joining the chat within minutes and prescribing or referring on the spot — counts as lawful care in Taiwan only when it lands inside one of those ten categories with health bureau approval. Outside them, it is health advice and nothing more. This is why local wearables and health apps have spent years stopping at the sentence "please see a doctor": not because the technology could not go further, but because there is no legal channel on the other side of that sentence. The boundary is moving: on June 26, 2026 the MOHW under minister Shih Chung-liang said video consultations for inmates would be opened to all specialties and that remote care resources would be offered to young Taiwanese abroad under the Youth Overseas Dream Fund program, with overseas remote care currently covering only Vietnam, the Philippines, Malaysia and Indonesia — but it is moving by named population, not by general opening.

(2) Charta Health 的那句「在 claim 送出前就談好，不要事後吵」，描述的正是台灣健保最痛的那個結構——而台灣的版本還附帶倍數放大。台灣的爭議不在「拒付」，在抽樣審查後的回推：健保署 2018 年 3 月改革後訂出的隨機抽樣回推倍數上限為——醫院門診 10 倍、住診 3 倍；西醫基層門診 20 倍、住診 5.8 倍；中醫門診 20 倍；牙醫門診不設上限。也就是說，一筆被核刪的費用在基層可以被乘上 20 倍扣回，而那次改革本身就是 2016 年宜蘭一位骨科醫師因核刪回推放大而絕食抗議之後的產物。同一份文件記載：2016 年 10 月起實施專業雙審與公開具名後，爭議審議案件量約減少 30%；2017 年起隨機與立意抽樣件數比例調為 3 比 7。把這組制度放在 Charta 的產品邏輯旁邊，台灣的機會與風險同時放大——機會是「送出前對帳」在一個 20 倍回推的制度裡，價值遠高於在美國；風險是本報在產品分析裡點出的那個偽陽性問題，在台灣會被同樣的倍數放大成反向災難：一個偏向「幫你補碼」的 AI，在抽審制度下等於替醫療機構累積可被 20 倍回推的曝險。台灣若要有這類產品，該先公布的不是準確率，是偽陽性率。 (2) Charta Health's line — agree on the chart before the claim goes out, do not fight about it after — describes precisely the sorest structure in Taiwan's NHI, and Taiwan's version comes with a multiplier. The fight here is not denial but extrapolated clawback after sampled review: under the NHI Administration's March 2018 reform, the caps on random-sampling extrapolation are 10x for hospital outpatient and 3x for inpatient; 20x for primary-care Western medicine outpatient and 5.8x for inpatient; 20x for traditional Chinese medicine outpatient; and no cap at all for dental outpatient. One disallowed charge in a clinic can therefore be clawed back twentyfold — and that reform was itself the product of a 2016 hunger strike by an orthopaedic physician in Yilan over magnified clawbacks. The same document records that dual professional review with named reviewers, introduced in October 2016, cut disputed-review caseloads by about 30%, and that from 2017 the ratio of random to targeted sampling was set at 3 to 7. Place that regime beside Charta's product logic and both the opportunity and the risk magnify. The opportunity: reconciling before submission is worth far more under a 20x extrapolation regime than it is in the US. The risk is the false-positive problem flagged in the product analysis above, multiplied by the same factor in the opposite direction — an AI biased toward "you missed a code" would, under sampled review, be accumulating exposure that gets clawed back twentyfold. If such a product comes to Taiwan, the first number it should publish is not accuracy but the false-positive rate.

(3) OpenEvidence 的授權堆疊對台灣臨床端是直接影響，但台灣這一側沒有對應的資產被授權出去——也沒有人在收。台灣醫師日常查證據讀的本來就是英文指引，所以當 ACP 把指引與 Annals of Internal Medicine 搬進一個由藥廠廣告資助的介面時，影響會原封不動傳到台北的診間——介面換了，但誰付錢決定了排序，而台灣的使用者不在那個廣告市場的計價邏輯裡。更要緊的缺口是另一邊：台灣真正獨有的臨床知識資產，是健保給付規定、各科的本土共識、以及與回推倍數綁在一起的申報規則——這一整套從來不在任何一個醫療 AI 的語料裡。一個把 NEJM、JAMA、Cochrane、NCCN、ACP 都讀完的模型，仍然不知道在台灣這個處置該用哪個代碼申報、會不會被核刪。本週三筆交易真正值得台灣抄的不是模型，是那個動作本身：ACP 意識到自己的指引是一項可授權資產並去談了條件。台灣的學會與健保署手上同樣握著獨有語料，而目前既沒有授權框架，也沒有價格。 (3) OpenEvidence's licensing stack hits Taiwanese clinicians directly, yet nothing on Taiwan's side is being licensed out — and nobody is buying. Physicians here already read English-language guidance, so when the ACP moves its guidance and Annals of Internal Medicine into an interface funded by pharmaceutical advertising, the effect travels unchanged to a Taipei clinic room: the interface changes, who pays sets the ordering, and Taiwanese users are not in that ad market's pricing logic at all. The sharper gap is on the other side. Taiwan's genuinely unique clinical assets are its NHI reimbursement rules, its specialty-level local consensus statements, and the filing rules bound up with those extrapolation multipliers — and that entire body of knowledge has never been in any medical AI's corpus. A model that has read NEJM, JAMA, Cochrane, NCCN and now the ACP still does not know which code this procedure should be filed under in Taiwan, or whether it will survive review. What Taiwan should copy from this week is not a model but the move itself: the ACP recognized its guidance as a licensable asset and went and negotiated terms. Taiwan's specialty societies and the NHI Administration hold equally unique corpora, with neither a licensing framework nor a price.
