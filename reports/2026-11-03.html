這一週有兩個方向相反的資本訊號，而且只隔三天。10/30 英國《金融時報》報導 OpenAI 把本輪募資目標從 300 億美元砍到 200 億、估值自 1.4 兆美元下調到 1.25 兆，據《華爾街日報》目前只拿到約 110 億美元承諾，接下來要往家族辦公室與高淨值個人募；同一天那斯達克 100 跌 3.5%，自 10 月初以來市值蒸發逾 1.2 兆美元，Alphabet 10/29 單日跌 13%、抹去約 5,000 億美元（PYMNTS, 2026-11-01）。但 11/2 兩份醫療 AI 財報走的是反方向：Tempus AI 單季營收 4.115 億美元、成長 23%，調整後 EBITDA 首度轉正至 2,560 萬美元；Hinge Health 營收 2.35 億美元成長 52%、GAAP 毛利率 87%、GAAP 淨利 5,740 萬美元。10/30 HGP 月報則說，未上市 health IT 的營收倍數回到約 5.0x，自 2016 年開始追蹤以來首次把對公開市場企業 SaaS 的估值折價補平。差別不在故事講得好不好，而在有沒有人付錢、付多少、以及這筆錢進不進得了既有工作流——10/29 OpenEvidence 直接進了 NYU Langone 的 Epic，覆蓋年 1,240 萬次門診。 Two capital signals pointed in opposite directions this week, three days apart. On Oct 30 the Financial Times reported OpenAI cutting its current round from $30 billion to $20 billion and its valuation from $1.4 trillion to $1.25 trillion; per the Wall Street Journal it has secured roughly $11 billion in commitments and now plans to raise from family offices and high-net-worth individuals. The same day the Nasdaq 100 fell 3.5%, having shed more than $1.2 trillion in market capitalization since early October, and Alphabet dropped 13% in a single session on Oct 29, erasing about $500 billion (PYMNTS, 2026-11-01). Then on Nov 2 two medical AI filings ran the other way: Tempus AI reported $411.5M in quarterly revenue, up 23%, with adjusted EBITDA positive for the first time at $25.6M, and Hinge Health posted $235.0M, up 52%, on an 87% GAAP gross margin with $57.4M of GAAP net income. HGP's Oct 30 monthly snapshot adds that private health IT revenue multiples are back near 5.0x, closing the sector's valuation discount to public enterprise SaaS for the first time since tracking began in 2016. The dividing line is not narrative quality but whether someone pays, how much, and whether the money reaches an existing workflow — on Oct 29 OpenEvidence went straight into NYU Langone's Epic, covering 12.4 million outpatient visits a year.

發生什麼What

Two segments: Diagnostics $303.5M, up 20% (oncology $172.6M, up 28%; hereditary cancer testing $111.3M), and Data & Applications $108.1M, up 33%, with Insights up 29% and roughly $500M in year-to-date licensing bookings. 為什麼重要Why it matters

醫療 AI 這一輪最常被問的問題是「什麼時候會賺錢」。Tempus 的答案不是靠模型，而是靠把檢測量撐起來（腫瘤檢測量 +32%）再把同一批資料第二次賣給藥廠（Data & Applications +33%、年初至今授權簽約約 5 億）。注意這個結構：Data & Applications 只占營收 26%，卻是成長最快、毛利最厚的那一塊，而它的原料來自 Diagnostics 那 74%。這是一個「診斷業務補貼資料業務、資料業務回頭拉高整體毛利」的飛輪，同業很難單靠模型複製。待決的 Personalis 併購案股東會定在 11/30，過了就再疊一層 MRD 與腫瘤基因資料。 The question asked of every medical AI company this cycle is when it makes money. Tempus's answer does not run through the model: it runs through pushing test volume up (oncology units +32%) and then selling the same data a second time to pharma (Data & Applications +33%, roughly $500M in year-to-date licensing bookings). Note the structure: Data & Applications is only 26% of revenue yet is the fastest-growing and highest-margin piece, and its raw material comes from the 74% that is Diagnostics. That is a flywheel in which the diagnostics business subsidizes the data business and the data business pulls the blended margin up — hard to copy with a model alone. The pending Personalis acquisition goes to a shareholder vote on Nov 30, which would layer on more MRD and tumor genomic data.

The company held full-year adjusted EBITDA at about $65M rather than raising it, effectively conceding that the $34M Q4 figure already absorbs this quarter's upside. The MRD comparison of 12,000 against 4,700 tests is labelled in the secondary summary as being against Q4 2025 rather than Q3 2025 — treat the company's own filing as authoritative on the base period. The BMS collaboration's figure of up to $192.5M in Data & Applications revenue over five years is a ceiling, not recognized revenue.

為什麼重要Why it matters

87% 的毛利率在醫療服務業幾乎不存在——這是把人力密集的肌肉骨骼復健，用動作追蹤與自動化照護流程替換掉大部分人工接觸之後的結果。它給整個產業一個可對照的數字：當 AI 真的取代了交付成本（而不只是加在既有成本之上），毛利率會落在 85% 以上、自由現金流率會接近五成。對照同一週 Teladoc 的 $6.05 億營收衰退 3%，這一題不是「遠距醫療行不行」，而是「交付是人做還是機器做」。要注意的是本季 GAAP 淨利 5,740 萬高於 GAAP 營業利益 5,030 萬，差額來自營業外項目而非本業，單季淨利率不宜直接年化。 An 87% gross margin essentially does not exist in healthcare services — it is what happens when labor-intensive musculoskeletal rehab replaces most of its human contact with motion tracking and automated care pathways. It gives the sector a reference number: when AI actually replaces the cost of delivery rather than sitting on top of it, gross margin lands above 85% and free-cash-flow margin approaches half of revenue. Set against Teladoc's $605M declining 3% in the same week, the question is not whether virtual care works but whether delivery is done by people or by machines. One caveat: GAAP net income of $57.4M exceeds GAAP operating income of $50.3M, with the difference coming from non-operating items, so this quarter's net margin should not simply be annualized.

為什麼重要Why it matters

對醫療 AI 來說這件事有兩個相反方向的後果。壞的一面：醫療 AI 的後期輪與 IPO 定價都錨在通用 AI 的倍數上，前沿實驗室的估值下修會沿著同一條曲線往下傳，尤其是那些還沒有營收、靠「基礎模型 + 醫療」敘事募資的公司。好的一面：當資金從「算力換敘事」轉向「現金流換估值」，Tempus 的首個正 EBITDA 季與 Hinge Health 的 87% 毛利率就不再只是財報細節，而是這一輪少數可以直接拿去對比的硬證據。這也解釋了 HGP 所觀察到的現象——未上市 health IT 的倍數在同一個月回到 5.0x。 For medical AI this cuts two ways. The bad: late-stage rounds and IPO pricing in health AI are anchored to general AI multiples, so a markdown at the frontier labs propagates down the same curve — hardest on companies with no revenue raising on a foundation-model-plus-medicine narrative. The good: as capital rotates from compute-for-narrative toward cash-flow-for-valuation, Tempus's first positive EBITDA quarter and Hinge Health's 87% gross margin stop being line items and become some of the few directly comparable hard evidence this cycle offers. It also helps explain what HGP observed in the same month — private health IT multiples back at 5.0x.

通路Distribution OpenEvidenceNYU LangoneEpic10/29

OpenEvidence 進入 NYU Langone 的 Epic 工作流，全系統上線：年 1,240 萬次門診、逾 42.1 萬次急診、6,000 多名主治與教職、2,000 多名住院醫師與研修醫師 OpenEvidence goes live inside NYU Langone's Epic workflow systemwide: 12.4M outpatient visits a year, over 421,000 ED visits, more than 6,000 physicians and faculty, and more than 2,000 residents and fellows

發生什麼What

10/29 NYU Langone Health 與 OpenEvidence 宣布合作，把 OpenEvidence 的臨床證據問答直接嵌進 Epic：臨床醫師可在既有工作流中用自然語言輸入複雜臨床問題，系統回傳取自同儕審查文獻、並納入該病人個別情境的答案。覆蓋範圍含該系統 7 個住院院區與 400 多個門診據點（跨紐約都會區與佛州）、年 1,240 萬次門診、逾 42.1 萬次急診，Family Health Centers 每年服務逾 10 萬名病人、約 60 萬次就診；使用者包含逾 6,000 名主治與教職、逾 2,000 名住院醫師與研修醫師，以及 NYU Grossman 兩所醫學院的受訓醫師，全系統員工逾 5.4 萬人。資訊長 Nader Mherabi 稱目標是「在安全、可操作的環境中把最新技術規模化地交到臨床醫師手上」；創辦人 Daniel Nadler 稱「全世界的醫學文獻現在出現在這 1,200 萬次就診的每一間診間裡」（Unite.AI, 2026-10-29）。 On Oct 29 NYU Langone Health and OpenEvidence announced a collaboration embedding OpenEvidence's clinical evidence Q&A directly into Epic: clinicians type a complex clinical question in natural language inside their existing workflow and get back an answer drawn from peer-reviewed literature that accounts for the individual patient's context. The footprint covers the system's seven inpatient locations and more than 400 outpatient sites across the New York region and Florida, 12.4 million outpatient visits a year and more than 421,000 ED visits, with the Family Health Centers serving over 100,000 patients across roughly 600,000 visits. Users include more than 6,000 physicians and faculty, more than 2,000 residents and fellows, and trainees at both NYU Grossman medical schools, within a system of more than 54,000 employees. CDIO Nader Mherabi said the aim is to make the latest technology available to clinicians in a safe and practical environment at scale; founder Daniel Nadler said the world's medical literature is now in the room for every one of those twelve million visits (Unite.AI, 2026-10-29).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本週最清楚的「通路勝過模型」案例。OpenEvidence 在 9 月才以 2.5 億美元 D 輪把估值推到 150 億（Axios, 2026-09-25），而支撐這個倍數的不是模型分數——本報 10/29 整理的 Real Clinical Queries 盲評裡，OpenEvidence 在 HealthBench 500 題開放式只拿 62.6 分、低於 GPT 的 88.0——而是它能不能站在醫師每天已經打開的那個畫面裡。進了 Epic，就等於把「要不要多開一個分頁」這個最大的採用障礙移除。對 UpToDate 這類傳統臨床決策支援產品，這是通路層面的直接替代威脅。 This is the week's cleanest case of distribution beating the model. OpenEvidence only pushed its valuation to $15 billion with a $250M Series D in September (Axios, 2026-09-25), and what supports that multiple is not benchmark scores — in the Real Clinical Queries blinded evaluation this publication covered on Oct 29, OpenEvidence scored 62.6 on the 500-item open-ended HealthBench against GPT's 88.0 — but whether it sits inside the screen clinicians already have open. Going into Epic removes the single largest adoption barrier, which is asking a physician to open one more tab. Closing is expected in Q1 2027, subject to shareholder and regulatory approval (Becker's ASC Review, 2026-10).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這一則放在這裡是當尺規用的。整個十月的未上市 health IT 投資年化約 130 億美元（HGP），而一家非營利醫療體系一筆交易就花掉 57 億——相當於全美 health IT 年度投資額的四成多，而且買的是磚瓦、手術室與醫師關係，不是軟體。這就是醫療 AI 的真實資本競爭環境：醫院的大錢在買產能（CMS 已把 289 項術式加入 2026 年 ASC 給付清單、從住院限定清單移除 271 項，門診手術的給付跑道比 AI 清楚得多），AI 預算往往是 IT 線裡的一小格。任何在提案裡講「幫醫院省錢」的醫療 AI 公司，都在跟一條報酬確定得多的替代投資案競爭。 This item is here as a ruler. All private health IT investment in October annualizes to about $13 billion on HGP's data, while a single nonprofit health system committed $5.7 billion in one transaction — more than 40% of the sector's annual investment, spent on bricks, operating rooms and physician relationships rather than software. That is medical AI's actual capital competition: hospital money buys capacity, and the reimbursement runway for it is far clearer than AI's, with CMS adding 289 procedures to the 2026 ASC covered list and removing 271 from the inpatient-only list. AI budgets usually sit in one cell of the IT line. Any medical AI company pitching savings to a hospital is competing against an alternative investment with a much more certain return.

02 — Product Analysis

同一天出現在新聞裡的兩種「賣臨床 AI」的方式：一個把產品送進別人的畫面後免費給醫師用，一個把資料賣給藥廠再用檢測量養資料 Two ways of selling clinical AI, both in this week's news: one puts the product inside someone else's screen and gives it to clinicians free, the other sells data to pharma and feeds the data with test volume

OpenEvidence

臨床證據問答，嵌入 Epic 工作流 · OpenEvidence（美國）Clinical evidence Q&A embedded in the Epic workflow · OpenEvidence (US)

功能與定位。醫師在 Epic 裡用自然語言問一個複雜臨床問題，系統從同儕審查文獻回答並納入該病人情境。賣點不在推理能力，而在「出現在哪裡」——10/29 起覆蓋 NYU Langone 7 個住院院區與 400 多個門診據點、年 1,240 萬次門診、逾 6,000 名主治（Unite.AI, 2026-10-29）。商業模式為對醫師免費、以廣告變現，9 月 2.5 億美元 D 輪推至 150 億美元估值（Axios, 2026-09-25）。 Function and position. A clinician asks a complex clinical question in natural language inside Epic and gets an answer from peer-reviewed literature, fitted to that patient's context. The selling point is not reasoning quality but placement — from Oct 29 it covers NYU Langone's seven inpatient locations and more than 400 outpatient sites, 12.4 million outpatient visits a year and more than 6,000 attending physicians (Unite.AI, 2026-10-29). The model is free to clinicians, monetized through advertising, with a $250M Series D in September putting the valuation at $15 billion (Axios, 2026-09-25).

  • 優勢Strength ：進了 Epic 就把「多開一個分頁」的採用障礙移除，而這是臨床 AI 最常死掉的地方——本報 11/01 引用的 Jefferson Health 數據是 5,234 個開通帳號只有 1,802 人在用（Unite.AI, 2026-10-29）。 : being inside Epic removes the one-more-tab adoption barrier, which is where clinical AI most often dies — the Jefferson Health figures cited here on Nov 1 were 5,234 provisioned accounts against 1,802 actual users (Unite.AI, 2026-10-29).
  • 優勢Strength ：對醫師免費，把採購決策從「要不要買」降級成「要不要開通」，繞過醫院 IT 預算排隊——這是它能在一年內鋪到多個醫學中心的主因。 : free to clinicians, which downgrades the procurement decision from whether to buy to whether to switch on, bypassing the hospital IT budget queue — the main reason it reached several academic centers within a year.
  • 疑慮Concern ：模型分數並不領先。本報 10/29 整理的盲評裡，HealthBench 500 題開放式 OpenEvidence 62.6 分、GPT 88.0 分、UpToDate 61.3 分；也就是說它贏在通路而非答案品質，一旦 Epic 自己的基礎模型或其他廠商拿到同一個位置，護城河就只剩品牌。 : its benchmark scores do not lead. In the blinded evaluation covered here on Oct 29, OpenEvidence scored 62.6 on the 500-item open-ended HealthBench against GPT's 88.0 and UpToDate's 61.3 — it wins on distribution, not answer quality, so if Epic's own foundation models or a rival secure the same slot, the moat reduces to brand.
  • 疑慮Concern ：150 億美元估值對應的營收規模未公開，廣告變現在臨床場景的天花板與利益衝突問題（藥廠廣告出現在處方決策旁）都沒有公開揭露；NYU Langone 合作的金額、期間、付費方同樣未揭露。 : the revenue behind a $15 billion valuation is not public, and neither the ceiling on advertising monetization in a clinical setting nor the conflict-of-interest question — pharma advertising appearing beside a prescribing decision — has been disclosed. The NYU Langone agreement's value, term and paying party are likewise undisclosed.

Tempus Data & Applications（Insights／Lens／agentic tools）

腫瘤多模態資料授權與臨床應用 · Tempus AI（美國，NASDAQ: TEM）Oncology multimodal data licensing and clinical applications · Tempus AI (US, NASDAQ: TEM)

功能與定位。把自家基因檢測業務產生的多模態資料（逾 600 PB，本季新增逾 50 PB）整理成藥廠可授權的資料集與分析產品，再往臨床端延伸成試驗配對與照護缺口的 agentic 工具。11/2 該分部單季營收 1.081 億美元、成長 33%，Insights 成長 29%，年初至今授權簽約約 5 億美元，與 BMS 的腫瘤資料與模型合作上限為五年 1.925 億美元（Investing.com, 2026-11-02）。 Function and position. It turns the multimodal data generated by its own genomic testing business — more than 600 petabytes, with over 50 added this quarter — into datasets and analytics pharma can license, then extends into the clinic as agentic tools for trial matching and care-gap detection. On Nov 2 the segment reported $108.1M for the quarter, up 33%, with Insights up 29%, roughly $500M in year-to-date licensing bookings, and a BMS oncology data and model collaboration capped at $192.5M over five years (Investing.com, 2026-11-02).

  • 優勢Strength ：原料自產。資料來自自家 Diagnostics 分部（本季 3.035 億美元、成長 20%，腫瘤檢測量 +32%），不需要向醫院買資料也不受單一體系合約生死影響，這是純軟體競爭者最難複製的一環（Investing.com, 2026-11-02）。 : it makes its own raw material. The data comes from its Diagnostics segment — $303.5M this quarter, up 20%, with oncology volume up 32% — so it neither buys data from hospitals nor lives and dies by a single system's contract, which is the hardest part for a software-only rival to copy (Investing.com, 2026-11-02).
  • 優勢Strength ：買方是藥廠而非醫院，付費能力與決策速度都高一階；這一塊把整體 non-GAAP 毛利率從 64% 拉到 66%，並直接貢獻了史上第一個正調整後 EBITDA 季（2,560 萬美元）。 : the buyer is pharma rather than a hospital, with a tier better ability and speed to pay; this segment pulled the blended non-GAAP gross margin from 64% to 66% and directly produced the first positive adjusted EBITDA quarter at $25.6M.
  • 疑慮Concern ：這一塊只占營收 26%，其餘 74% 是毛利較薄、受給付價格擺盪的檢測業務；治療選擇 ASP 目前 1,880 美元、公司目標 2,600 美元以上，中間這段差距要靠給付談判而不是技術。 : the segment is only 26% of revenue, with the other 74% in a thinner-margin testing business exposed to reimbursement pricing; therapy-selection ASP stands at $1,880 against a company target above $2,600, and closing that gap depends on payer negotiation rather than technology.
  • 疑慮Concern ：約 5 億美元是「年初至今授權簽約」而非已認列營收，認列節奏取決於藥廠專案進度；BMS 的 1.925 億美元是五年上限值。agentic 工具與基礎模型則沒有公布任何客戶數、使用量或臨床結果數據。 : the roughly $500M is year-to-date licensing bookings rather than recognized revenue, and recognition tracks pharma project timing; the BMS $192.5M is a five-year ceiling. For the agentic tools and foundation models, no customer count, usage figure or clinical outcome data has been published.

兩種模式的交叉點在「誰付錢」。OpenEvidence 把邊際成本壓到接近零、讓醫師免費用、向第三方收廣告，所以它的風險是變現天花板與利益衝突；Tempus 讓藥廠付錢、用自家檢測量養資料，所以它的風險是那 74% 檢測業務的給付價格。值得注意的是兩者都沒有把「模型比較強」當成主要賣點——這一輪臨床 AI 的競爭已經從模型層移到通路層與資料所有權層。 The two models cross at who pays. OpenEvidence drives marginal cost to near zero, gives it to clinicians free and charges a third party for advertising, so its risk is the monetization ceiling and conflicts of interest; Tempus makes pharma pay and feeds the data with its own test volume, so its risk is reimbursement pricing across that 74%. Worth noting that neither leads with having the better model — this round of clinical AI competition has already moved from the model layer to the distribution layer and the question of who owns the data.
