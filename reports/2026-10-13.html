今天這一疊消息要放在一起才看得出形狀。Axios Pro 於 10/9 報導，3M 分拆出來的 Solventum 已開始收 Health Information Systems（HIS）事業部的初始投標，依其中一位消息人士轉述，這個事業部以 55 億至 70 億美元的價格在市場上行銷；而依 Solventum 8/6 宣布分拆意向時的揭露，它年營收 14 億美元、嵌在全美逾 75% 的醫院、營運範圍逾 30 國、每月處理約 6.6 億份臨床文件，核心資產是一套建立在四十年醫療編碼資料上的規則引擎。換句話說，有人願意為「已經長在醫院裡」這件事付出約 4 到 5 倍營收。 Today's pile only takes shape when you read it together. Axios Pro reported on Oct 9 that Solventum, the 3M spinoff, has begun collecting initial bids for its Health Information Systems (HIS) unit, which one source says is being marketed at $5.5 billion to $7 billion. Per Solventum's own Aug 6 separation announcement, the unit turns $1.4 billion in annual sales, sits inside more than 75% of US hospitals, operates in over 30 countries and processes roughly 660 million clinical documents a month, its core asset a rules engine built on four decades of medical coding data. Someone, in other words, is willing to pay four to five times revenue for the fact that it already grows inside the hospital.

同一週有兩份報告把這個價格放進了相反的脈絡。Rock Health 10/12 發布的第三季市場更新算出美國數位健康第三季只募到 37 億美元／114 件、年初至今 110 億美元／358 件，平均單筆衝到 3,100 萬美元，而自 2025 年 8 月 HeartFlow 以來一家新上市都沒有；它的結論是「能不能做出來」已經不再是門檻，護城河移到了部署價值——整合、實證、以及願不願意承擔責任。PitchBook 第二季那份給私募的警告更直接：美國 RCM 產業利潤 2030 年掉約 22%、2040 年掉約 54%，而近乎 AI 原生的 Waystar 年初至今跌約 43%，報告說市場正在「把顛覆計入價格」。買方在為嵌入式通路付價，賣方在為即將被 agent 吃掉的利潤找出口——兩邊都可能是對的，但不會同時都對。 Two reports the same week set that price against the opposite context. Rock Health's Q3 market update, published Oct 12, counts $3.7 billion across 114 deals for the quarter and $11.1 billion across 358 year to date, with the average check up to $31.0 million — and no new public listing since HeartFlow in August 2025. Its conclusion: "can it be built?" has stopped being the limit, and advantage has moved to deployable value — integration, evidence, and a willingness to own liability. PitchBook's Q2 warning to sponsors is blunter: US RCM industry profits down about 22% by 2030 and 54% by 2040, with near-AI-native Waystar off roughly 43% year to date as the market "prices in disruption." Buyers are paying for embedded distribution; sellers are finding an exit from margins agents are about to eat. Both sides may be right — but not at the same time.

Four to five times revenue on $1.4 billion is not a software multiple; it is the price of a switching cost — replace us and you replace the claims workflow in 75% of US hospitals. And it has to win that bet against the industry profit curve PitchBook just drew in the same market.

發生什麼What

PitchBook 第二季那份標題叫《AI Kills the RCM Star》的分析報告把數字攤開來：2017 年以來私募支持的 RCM 交易總值 429 億美元、出場總值 225 億美元，其中包含一筆剛宣布的 120 億美元 Ensemble Health Partners 收購。私募的論點是——把 agentic AI 裝進勞力密集的後台、砍掉境外人力，毛利從約 20% 拉到 30%，再往軟體等級的 60% 至 70% 以上走。PitchBook 認為這是陷阱：同一套 AI 既能擴大既有廠商的毛利，也能讓平台型玩家把 RCM 當作近乎純增量利潤直接外掛上去——EHR 巨頭（如 Epic）、環境錄音與臨床決策支援廠商、以及分析業者（如併下 CaduceusHealth 後的 Innovaccer）都在這條路上。公開市場已經先投票：成長率約 11% 的近乎 AI 原生廠商 Waystar 年初至今跌約 43%，報告形容市場正在「把顛覆計入價格」。最早的實證是 CommonSpirit Health 付給 Tenet 旗下 Conifer Health Solutions 19 億美元提前六年解約，然後把業務收回自營。 PitchBook's Q2 note, titled "AI Kills the RCM Star," lays out the numbers: $42.9 billion in PE-backed RCM deal value since 2017 and $22.5 billion in exit value, including a freshly announced $12 billion acquisition of Ensemble Health Partners. The sponsor thesis is to bolt agentic AI onto the labour-heavy back office, cut offshore staff and lift gross margins from roughly 20% to 30%, with a path to software-like 60–70%-plus. PitchBook calls it a trap: the same AI that expands incumbent margins also lets platform players bolt RCM on as near-pure incremental profit — EHR giants such as Epic, ambient-scribe and clinical decision-support vendors, and analytics players such as Innovaccer after its CaduceusHealth acquisition. Public markets voted first: Waystar, near-AI-native and growing organically around 11%, is down roughly 43% year to date, with the market "pricing in disruption." The earliest hard evidence is CommonSpirit Health paying Tenet's Conifer Health Solutions $1.9 billion to exit an RCM contract six years early, then insourcing the work.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是今天這份日報的反面論證，而且是唯一有長期量化預測的那一面：cost-to-collect 被預期壓向 1% 以下，美國 RCM 產業利潤 2030 年掉約 22%、2040 年掉約 54%。如果這條曲線成立，Solventum 的買方買到的不是現金流，而是一段還沒被 agent 追上的時間差；而 CommonSpirit 願意付 19 億美元提前解約再自營，說明醫院端已經開始相信「自己做比外包便宜」——這對所有把 RCM 當服務賣的公司，都是結構性的壞消息。 This is today's counter-case, and the only side with a long-dated quantitative projection: cost-to-collect compressing toward below 1%, US RCM industry profits down about 22% by 2030 and 54% by 2040. If that curve holds, Solventum's buyer is not buying cash flow but a window of time before agents catch up. And CommonSpirit paying $1.9 billion to exit early and insource says hospitals have started to believe doing it themselves is cheaper than outsourcing — structurally bad news for anyone selling RCM as a service.
