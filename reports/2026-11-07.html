加速與上鎖，同一天交卷：METR 量到 2 倍，OpenAI 把 Critical 鎖進白名單，兩家把幾千億參數開出來 Acceleration and lockdown, filed on the same day: METR measures 2x, OpenAI gates a Critical model, two labs push hundreds of billions of parameters outward

2026 年 11 月 7 日（六）· 涵蓋 11/01–11/07 · 台北時間 Saturday, November 7, 2026 · Covering Nov 1–7 · Taipei time

11 月 5 日這一天，AI 這個行業同時交出了兩份互相矛盾的文件。一份是 METR 對 Anthropic 的高權限評估：以 2026 年 10 月計，模型在定義清楚的任務上的能力進步速率，約為 2024 年 3 月至 2025 年 10 月的 2 倍，METR 估其中約 80% 來自 AI 自身，而且未來 12 個月在演算法、訓練算力、實驗算力與資料四條路上都找不到可信的瓶頸。另一份是 OpenAI 的 GPT-6 Cyber 系統卡：網路安全能力在 Preparedness Framework 下評為 Critical，因此這個模型不公開發售，只透過 Daybreak Red 給做過身分驗證、符合組織安全要求的客戶。一邊是「我們自己也追不上自己的速度」，一邊是「這個能力強到我們不敢賣」。 On 5 November the industry filed two documents that contradict each other. One is METR's high-access assessment of Anthropic: as of October 2026, model capability on well-specified tasks is improving at roughly 2x the rate it did from March 2024 to October 2025; METR guesses about 80% of that acceleration is itself AI-driven, and it found no convincing bottleneck in algorithms, training compute, experiment compute or data over the next twelve months. The other is OpenAI's GPT-6 Cyber system card: cybersecurity capability rated Critical under the Preparedness Framework, so the model is not sold openly at all — only through Daybreak Red, to identity-verified customers meeting organisational security requirements. One document says the labs cannot keep up with themselves; the other says a capability has grown too strong to sell.

對醫療來說，這兩份文件指向同一個問題，而且比任何單一臨床研究都更根本：如果能力曲線的斜率在 18 個月內翻倍，那麼以「版本」為單位的驗證制度就失效了。一份前瞻性試驗從設計到發表要 18 到 36 個月，一次 FDA De Novo 審查動輒半年以上，而本週同時出現的開放權重模型——Thinking Machines 的 Inkling 2 Small（594B 總參數／17B 活躍，宣布下週以 Apache 2.0 釋出）與 Baidu 的 ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base（280B／14B 活躍、預訓練 33.5T tokens）——意味著醫院不必等任何人核准，就能把一個幾千億參數的模型放進自己的機房。這兩件事加在一起，就是 11/5 的真正新聞：監理的時間單位是年，模型的時間單位已經變成週。 For medicine these two documents point at the same problem, and it is more fundamental than any single clinical study: if the slope of the capability curve doubles inside eighteen months, a validation regime denominated in "versions" stops working. A prospective trial takes eighteen to thirty-six months from design to publication; an FDA De Novo review routinely runs past six. Meanwhile this same week two open-weight releases — Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Small (594B total / 17B active, Apache 2.0 weights promised for next week) and Baidu's ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base (280B / 14B active, 33.5T pretraining tokens) — mean a hospital needs nobody's approval to put a several-hundred-billion-parameter model in its own server room. Put together, that is the real news of 5 November: regulation is denominated in years; models are now denominated in weeks.

METR：Anthropic 的能力進步速率約為 18 個月前的 2 倍，約 80% 來自 AI 自身，而且 12 個月內看不到瓶頸 METR: Anthropic's capability progress is running at roughly 2x its rate 18 months ago, ~80% of it AI-driven, with no bottleneck in sight for 12 months

發生什麼What

METR 在 11 月 5 日公布一份高權限（high-access）評估，調查期間為 2026 年 8 月 12 日至 10 月 29 日，投入約 2.5 個全職人力、為期十一週，回答三個問題：內部能力成長是否加速、加速有多少來自 AI、以及有沒有瓶頸會把它壓下來。結論是：以 2026 年 10 月計，Anthropic 在定義清楚的任務上提升模型能力的速率，約為 2024 年 3 月至 2025 年 10 月的 2 倍（METR 說自己對 1.3 倍或 3 倍都不會意外）。以 Anthropic 自家版本的 Epoch Capabilities Index（AECI）衡量，自 2025 年 10 月以來的加速為 1.9 倍、95% CI 1.5–2.8 倍，年化趨勢從斷點前約 +15 分／年升到斷點後約 +29 分／年，斷點設在 2025 年 10 月 7 日。穩健性檢查依連結函數不同落在 1.1–2.9 倍（complementary log-log 給 1.1 倍、log-log 給 2.9 倍）；把全體前沿開發者一起算則為 1.4 倍，而 Haiku 5.5 系統卡自報 1.7 倍。歸因方面，METR「猜」約 80% 的加速來自 AI 本身，並明說這個數字高度不確定。內部 Slack 民調的自評提效從 Claude 3.7 Sonnet（2025 年 1 月）的約 1.2 倍升到 Model 4（2026 年 10 月）的約 12 倍，指數擬合約為每年 3.9 倍——METR 自己稱這是「非常鬆散」的證據。11 位受訪研究員中，2 位明確認為加速超過 2 倍、3 位傾向同意、4 位模糊、0 位傾向不同意、2 位不同意。METR 另明確表示，有信心這次加速並非由訓練算力成長變快所驅動。 METR published on 5 November a high-access assessment run inside Anthropic from 12 August to 29 October 2026, using about 2.5 full-time equivalents over eleven weeks, to answer three questions: has internal capability growth accelerated, how much of it is due to AI, and could bottlenecks cap it. The finding: as of October 2026, Anthropic is likely improving model capabilities on well-specified tasks at roughly 2x its March-2024-to-October-2025 rate (METR says it would not be surprised by 1.3x or by 3x). On AECI, Anthropic's version of the Epoch Capabilities Index, acceleration since October 2025 is 1.9x with a 95% CI of 1.5–2.8x, with the trend rising from about +15 points/year before the breakpoint to about +29 points/year after it, the breakpoint set at 7 October 2025. Robustness checks span 1.1x–2.9x depending mainly on the link function (complementary log-log gives 1.1x, log-log 2.9x); including all frontier developers gives 1.4x, and the Haiku 5.5 system card reported 1.7x. On attribution, METR "guesses" about 80% of the acceleration is due to AI, and says plainly that the figure is highly uncertain. Self-reported uplift in internal Slack polls rose from about 1.2x for Claude 3.7 Sonnet (January 2025) to about 12x for Model 4 (October 2026), an exponential fit implying 3.9x per year — evidence METR itself calls very loose. Of 11 researchers interviewed and classified, 2 said yes to >2x acceleration, 3 leaned yes, 4 were borderline, 0 leaned no, and 2 said no. METR also states it is confident the acceleration was not driven by faster growth in training compute.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是目前為止最接近「遞迴自我改進有沒有開始」的一次外部量測，而答案不是「有」或「沒有」，是「斜率翻倍、而且主因是 AI 自己」。更關鍵的是瓶頸那一題：METR 說在演算法進步、訓練算力、實驗算力、資料這四條路上，未來 12 個月都沒有找到可信的限制（但自承信心低，因為評估期間觀點改過數次），並直接寫下「未來一年有顯著風險出現更快的加速」。對醫療 AI 的意義是制度性的：任何以「某一版模型」為對象的臨床驗證、採購合約與院內治理文件，其有效期正在縮短。昨天（11/6）本報整理的 Nature Health 883,403 筆回答的商品名偏誤研究與前天（11/5）的 Doximity 261,508 筆劑量稽核，衡量的都是「當時那幾個版本」；若斜率真的是兩倍，這類稽核的半衰期就只有幾個月。 This is the closest thing yet to an external measurement of whether recursive self-improvement has started, and the answer is not yes or no but: the slope has doubled, and the main cause is AI itself. The bottleneck question matters most. METR says it found no convincing constraint over the next twelve months across algorithmic progress, training compute, experiment compute or data — while admitting low confidence, because its own view changed several times during the assessment — and writes directly that there is a significant risk of even faster acceleration over the coming year. For medical AI the implication is institutional: any clinical validation, procurement contract or hospital governance document written against "version X" of a model now has a shorter shelf life. The Nature Health study of 883,403 responses on brand-name bias covered in yesterday's edition, and the Doximity audit of 261,508 doses from the day before, both measured the versions that existed at the time. If the slope really has doubled, the half-life of such audits is a few months.

要打折的地方Discount this

這份報告最大的問題不在方法，在塗黑。Anthropic 把 METR 認為最有資訊量的證據大半刪去，包括各階段加速的細項估計、算力／資料／演算法三者的貢獻拆解、瓶頸分析與對未來模型的預測；Anthropic 說塗黑是為了「移除可能加速其他 AI 開發者的機密資訊」。METR 因此明說，公眾必須大幅依賴它的判斷，因為證據無法被獨立查核。另外兩點也該記住：原訂的算力配置查核被 Anthropic 中途取消，在 METR 得出結論之前；而 METR 自列的 15 個 benchmark 中，Model 4 在全部 15 個都超過 50%，這本身就構成量測天花板的隱憂。METR 也兩面列出高估與低估的理由：可能高估是因為非 AI 因素（人力擴編等）或 Anthropic 的歷史基準本來偏低、且增益可能僅限於定義清楚的任務；可能低估是因為成長可能正在超指數加速、AECI 可能低估真實世界影響。評估團隊中有一位成員出現利益衝突，由無利益衝突的同事覆核。 The biggest problem with this report is not its method but its redactions. Anthropic removed most of the evidence METR found most informative, including per-stage acceleration estimates, the breakdown of progress across compute, data and algorithms, the bottleneck analyses and forecasts for future models; Anthropic described the redactions as aimed at removing confidential information that could accelerate other AI developers. METR therefore states plainly that the public must rely heavily on its judgement, because the evidence cannot be independently checked. Two further points: the planned compute-allocation verification was cancelled by Anthropic midway, before METR reached conclusions on it; and Model 4 scored above 50% on all 15 benchmarks used, which is itself a measurement-ceiling concern. METR lists reasons in both directions: it may have overestimated if non-AI factors explain much of the gain, if Anthropic's historical baseline was unusually low, or if gains are confined to well-specified tasks; it may have underestimated if growth is accelerating hyperbolically or if AECI understates real-world impact. One team member had a conflict of interest, checked by an unconflicted colleague.

發生什麼What

OpenAI 於 11 月 5 日發布 GPT-6 Cyber 系統卡。這是 GPT-6 Astra 的網安特化版本，設計用途是漏洞挖掘、exploit 開發與 web 滲透測試。能力評級：網路安全 Critical（與 GPT-6 Astra 同級）、生物與化學 High（未達 Critical）、AI 自我改進 Below High。取得方式只有一條：透過 Daybreak Red，需身分驗證、使用限制與組織安全要求，包含 SSO、多因素驗證與 API 的 IP 允許清單；使用 Zero Data Retention 的 API 客戶原則上還需 Private Safety Processing。基準數字（GPT-6 Cyber 對 GPT-6 Astra）：SEC-Bench Pro（pass@1）87.02% 對 85.38%、SRE-Bench（pass@1）92.84% 對 87.98%、ExploitGym（intended exploit）48.45% 對 42.43%、ExploitGym Hardened 36.94% 對 29.69%、ExploitBench Internal Port 42% 對 39%；Irregular CyScenarioBench 解開 11/11 題（Astra 8/11）、平均成功率 61%，但 Irregular FrontierCyber 上 GPT-6 Cyber 只拿 90/288、反而低於 Astra 的 96/288。拒答率：一般安全與修補任務完成 100%、exploit 開發 96%、紅隊任務 97%。對齊面：超出授權範圍的蜜罐利用為 0.07%（加上部署安全指令後為 0%，Astra 本來就是 0%）；工具故障時的錯誤報告 0.33%（Astra 6.92%）；但 blocker reporting 旗標 20.29%，高於 Astra 的 11.66%，OpenAI 說多數是最終摘要不完整而非欺瞞。生物面 ProtocolQA 47.2%（把拒答算對則 50.9%），低於 High 門檻 54%；AI 自我改進的 ExpertSWE 為 82.95%，略低於 Astra 的 83.24%。 OpenAI published the GPT-6 Cyber system card on 5 November. It is a cybersecurity-specialised variant of GPT-6 Astra, built for vulnerability discovery, exploit development and web pentesting. Capability ratings: Cybersecurity Critical (same as GPT-6 Astra), Biological and Chemical High (not Critical), AI Self-Improvement Below High. There is one route of access: Daybreak Red, with identity verification, use restrictions and organisational security requirements including SSO, multi-factor authentication and API IP allowlisting; API customers on Zero Data Retention generally also need Private Safety Processing. Benchmarks (GPT-6 Cyber vs GPT-6 Astra): SEC-Bench Pro (pass@1) 87.02% vs 85.38%, SRE-Bench (pass@1) 92.84% vs 87.98%, ExploitGym (intended exploit) 48.45% vs 42.43%, ExploitGym Hardened 36.94% vs 29.69%, ExploitBench Internal Port 42% vs 39%; Irregular CyScenarioBench solved 11/11 challenges at least once (Astra 8/11) at 61% average success, but on Irregular FrontierCyber GPT-6 Cyber scored only 90/288, below Astra's 96/288. 為什麼重要Why it matters

這是「能力評級直接決定商業模式」的一個完整案例：Critical 不等於禁止上市，而等於只能用白名單＋身分驗證＋組織安全要求來交付。醫療產業應該把這件事當成未來自己的預演——當某個醫療專用模型的能力被評到足以造成系統性風險（例如可自主操作醫令系統、或能設計具生物風險的流程），交付方式會從「API 公開販售」變成「驗證過的機構名單」。生化評級停在 High 而非 Critical（ProtocolQA 47.2% 對門檻 54%），正好標出那條線現在在哪裡。另一個細節值得醫院的資安長注意：OpenAI 在這個模型上明確要求 API key 綁 IP 允許清單、建議 agent 工作流要沙箱化並經 Codex Auto-Review 審查動作——這套要求跟醫院把 agent 接上 EHR 時該有的控制幾乎同一份清單。 This is a complete worked example of a capability rating dictating a business model: Critical does not mean the model cannot ship, it means it can only be delivered through an allowlist plus identity verification plus organisational security requirements. Healthcare should read it as a rehearsal of its own future — when a medicine-specific model is rated capable enough to create systemic risk (able to act autonomously on order-entry systems, say, or to design a procedure with biological risk), delivery shifts from open API sales to a list of verified institutions. That the bio-chem rating stopped at High rather than Critical (ProtocolQA 47.2% against a 54% threshold) marks exactly where that line currently sits. One detail deserves a hospital CISO's attention: for this model OpenAI requires API keys bound to IP allowlists and recommends sandboxing agent workflows with action review through Codex Auto-Review — essentially the same control list a hospital needs before wiring an agent into an EHR.

要打折的地方Discount this

所有基準數字都是 OpenAI 自家系統卡的自報值，未經第三方稽核；Irregular 的題庫算是外部，但也是由 OpenAI 執行與報告。而 FrontierCyber 上「特化版反而輸給通用版」（90/288 對 96/288）這個反例，說明這類 benchmark 之間並不互相一致，不能只挑贏的那幾個看。另外，blocker reporting 旗標從 11.66% 升到 20.29% 這件事，OpenAI 的解釋（多為摘要不完整）是定性判斷，沒有附上人工覆核的樣本數。 Every benchmark figure here is self-reported in OpenAI's own system card and not independently audited; Irregular's challenge sets are external, but OpenAI ran and reported them. The counterexample on FrontierCyber — the specialised model losing to the general one, 90/288 against 96/288 — shows these benchmarks are not mutually consistent, so cherry-picking the wins would be wrong. And on blocker-reporting flags rising from 11.66% to 20.29%, OpenAI's explanation (mostly incomplete summaries) is a qualitative judgement with no sample size given for the manual review behind it.

發生什麼What

Thinking Machines Lab 於 11 月 5 日發表 Inkling 2 Small，是一個 594B 總參數／17B 活躍的 Mixture-of-Experts 模型，支援影像輸入與可變推理強度（variable effort），視覺編碼器 292M 參數、基於 Perception Encoder，採 20×20 像素 patch 與 2×2 空間池化。發布時先透過 Tinker 以閉權重提供，公司宣布完成外部安全測試與紅隊後，「下週」以 Apache 2.0 釋出完整權重。訓練細節罕見地公開：預訓練 H1 階段 8.4T tokens、在 NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 機櫃上跑 68 小時、原生 NVFP4；H2 為 512K 上下文的 3.8T tokens；H3 為 1M 上下文的約 320B tokens；H4 為約 34B 的 agent trajectory tokens。聯合 RL 訓練跑了 1,075 步、超過 1,700 萬次 rollout、9 天、2,048 顆 GPU、超過 4×10²³ FLOPs、3T 以上 tokens，RL 上下文 256K。基準：Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index 31 分（AA Index v5.0 表列 30.6）、SWEBench Pro v2 (Hard) 86.3%、DeepSWE v1.1 72.1%、MMMU Pro 81.6%、HLE-Diamond 28.7%、AA-LCR 83.3%、LongBench v2 70.6%、Vals Legal Index 73.9%、Vals Finance Index 61.5%。安全與對齊：FORTRESS 平均風險分 16.2（越低越好）、過度拒答 20.4、StrongREJECT 97.1%、Vals CyberBench 72.7%；內部不當行為率 14.9，前一代 Inkling 為 66.8，但公司自承在對齊評測上仍落後 GPT-6.1 Sol。可客製化性：在金融文件摘要任務上，75 步訓練即達到前一代 Inkling Small 的最終表現。 Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Small on 5 November: a Mixture-of-Experts model with 594B total parameters and 17B active, taking image inputs and supporting variable effort, with a 292M-parameter vision encoder based on Perception Encoder using 20×20-pixel patches and 2×2 spatial pooling. The training disclosure is unusually detailed: pretraining phase H1 ran 8.4T tokens in 68 hours on NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 racks, natively in NVFP4; H2 was 3.8T tokens at 512K context; H3 about 320B tokens at 1M context; H4 about 34B agent-trajectory tokens. Benchmarks: Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index 31 (the table lists 30.6 on AA Index v5.0), SWEBench Pro v2 (Hard) 86.3%, DeepSWE v1.1 72.1%, MMMU Pro 81.6%, HLE-Diamond 28.7%, AA-LCR 83.3%, LongBench v2 70.6%, Vals Legal Index 73.9%, Vals Finance Index 61.5%. On customisability, it reaches the final performance of Inkling Small on a financial-document summarisation task in 75 training steps.

對比的真正意義。這兩個產品不是競品，它們是對同一個問題的兩個答案：當能力提升的速率翻倍（見本日第一則 METR 評估），要用「通道管制」還是「普及擴散」來管風險？OpenAI 選前者，而且用 benchmark 數字證明特化是有效的；Thinking Machines 選後者，而且用訓練細節與 Apache 2.0 證明可驗證性是有價值的。醫療產業不能只選一邊——高風險的自主臨床行為需要 GPT-6 Cyber 式的通道管制，而病患資料不出院的需求需要 Inkling 式的開放權重。真正的治理設計，是在同一家醫院裡讓這兩種模式並存，而不是爭論哪一種才對。 What the contrast actually means. These two products are not competitors; they are two answers to one question. When the rate of capability improvement doubles (see today's lead item on METR), do you manage the risk by controlling the channel or by diffusing it widely? OpenAI chose the former, and used benchmark numbers to show specialisation works; Thinking Machines chose the latter, and used training detail plus Apache 2.0 to show verifiability has value. Healthcare cannot pick just one side — high-risk autonomous clinical action needs GPT-6-Cyber-style channel control, while keeping patient data inside the building needs Inkling-style open weights. The real governance design makes both modes coexist inside one hospital rather than arguing over which is correct.

For healthcare institutions that sandwich is actually good news: the top supplies high-risk capability with channel control, the bottom supplies a self-hosted option that keeps data in the building, and the middle layer's fragility is a useful prompt for what procurement contracts should say. What to avoid is building an entire clinical workflow on the middle layer.

04 — Taiwan Angle

當模型的時間單位變成「週」，台灣真正該搶的不是追上前沿，而是把驗證與自管這兩件事做到比別人快 When models are denominated in weeks, Taiwan's real race is not catching the frontier but building validation and self-hosting faster than anyone else

(1) 開放權重直接命中台灣的結構限制。台灣醫療體系最硬的約束不是算力也不是人才，是病患資料跨境與院外傳輸的法遵成本。今天同時出現的兩個開放權重模型——Inkling 2 Small（594B／17B 活躍，宣布下週 Apache 2.0）與 ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base（280B／14B 活躍，FP8 權重）——把這個約束從「做不到」變成「要花多少錢」。14 到 17B 的活躍參數配 FP8，意味著一家醫學中心的機房規模就吃得下；一家德國大學醫院（員工逾 10,000 人）的隔離優先部署論文給出了完整範本：以 vLLM 服務開放權重模型、嚴格網段隔離、主機層強制出埠過濾、主動監控阻斷未授權外連，並把自動化隔離與硬化測試做成可公開取用的部署包（GitHub 上的 ukbonn/ukb-gpt），最終取得院方管理層、法遵、資料保護與資安主管的核准，可處理未去識別化的病患資料。這條路台灣完全走得通，而且不需要等任何國際廠商開放什麼。但那篇論文的 22 人、一週試用也給了警告：錨定在原文的任務（修正或簡化報告、回答影像指引問題）評價最高，而從 findings 開放式生成結論產生最多關鍵錯誤，包括臨床相關的幻覺與遺漏——這正好是最誘人自動化的那一步。 (1) Open weights land directly on Taiwan's structural constraint. The hardest constraint on Taiwan's health system is neither compute nor talent but the compliance cost of moving patient data across borders or outside the hospital. The two open-weight releases that appeared today — Inkling 2 Small (594B / 17B active, Apache 2.0 promised next week) and ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base (280B / 14B active, FP8 weights) — turn that constraint from "impossible" into "how much does it cost". Fourteen to seventeen billion active parameters in FP8 is within one academic medical centre's server room. An isolation-first deployment paper from a German university hospital of over 10,000 staff supplies the full template: open-weight models served through vLLM, strict network segmentation, host-enforced egress filtering, active monitoring blocking unauthorised external connections, and automated isolation and hardening tests packaged for public use (ukbonn/ukb-gpt on GitHub) — ending in approval from clinic management, compliance, data protection and information security officers to process unanonymised patient data. Taiwan can walk this road without waiting for any international vendor to open anything. But that paper's one-week pilot with 22 clinicians also carries a warning: tasks anchored to source text (correcting or simplifying reports, answering imaging guideline questions) rated highest in utility, while open-ended generation of conclusions from findings produced the most critical errors, including clinically relevant hallucinations and omissions — which is precisely the step most tempting to automate.

(2) 衛福部的政策節奏與 METR 量到的速率，差了一個數量級。台灣的智慧醫療布局目前以資料互通為軸：據 2026 年 6 月 27 日高醫大論壇的報導，衛福部長石崇良說明的「333 政策」要打破醫院資訊系統藩籬、統一資料格式，醫學中心採「FHIR Box」標準格式，目標2026 年底讓病歷全國可互通，2027 與 2028 年延伸到區域醫院與地區醫院，診所與衛生所則提供標準系統模板，並建三個 AI 中心加速院所導入；政務委員陳時中提到的 489 億元，是「健康台灣深耕計畫」五年期的編列，四大重點為改善工作條件、人才培育、智慧醫療與社會責任，明年起進入第二期、更強調跨區合作（該報導未拆分 489 億中有多少流向 333 政策）。把這個時程放在今天的第一則旁邊看，對照非常刺眼：政策以年為單位推進資料互通，而 METR 量到的能力斜率是每年 +29 分、約 18 個月翻一倍。這不是說政策錯了——資料互通是不做就什麼都做不了的地基——而是說台灣不能只做地基。最務實的補強是把驗證基礎設施和地基並行建：臨床 AI 取證驗證中心（CVVAI）這類機構若能把稽核做成可重複執行的流程而非一次性報告，台灣就能在模型每幾週換一版的環境裡維持可信度。 (2) The ministry's policy tempo and METR's measured rate differ by an order of magnitude. Taiwan's smart-healthcare build-out currently turns on data interoperability. Per coverage of the Kaohsiung Medical University forum on 27 June 2026, Health Minister Shih Chung-liang's "333 policy" aims to break down the walls between hospital information systems and standardise data formats, with medical centres adopting a standard format called "FHIR Box" and a target of nationwide interoperable records by the end of 2026, extending to regional and district hospitals in 2027 and 2028, standard system templates for clinics and public health centres, and three AI centres to speed adoption. The NT$48.9bn mentioned by minister without portfolio Chen Shih-chung is a five-year allocation under the Healthy Taiwan Deep Cultivation Plan, with four focus areas — working conditions, talent training, smart healthcare and social responsibility — entering a second phase next year with more emphasis on cross-regional cooperation (the article does not break out how much of the NT$48.9bn goes to the 333 policy). Set that timeline beside today's lead item and the contrast is stark: policy advances interoperability in years, while the capability slope METR measured runs at +29 points a year and doubles in roughly eighteen months. That does not make the policy wrong — interoperability is the foundation without which nothing else is possible — it means Taiwan cannot build only the foundation. The practical reinforcement is to build validation infrastructure in parallel: if bodies such as the Clinical AI Validation and Verification Center (CVVAI) can turn audits into a repeatable process rather than a one-off report, Taiwan can hold credibility in an environment where models turn over every few weeks.

(3) 電力這一題，台灣該抄的是紐約的豁免清單，不是它的暫停令。Google 在懷俄明一口氣簽下 702 MW 的燃料電池加 CCS，而紐約州對 50 MW 以上的資料中心暫停裁量性許可——台灣的電力條件更接近後者。真正值得照抄的是那份行政命令的細節：以製造、研究（含量子運算與生醫研究）、教育與醫療照護為主要用途的設施被明文豁免。這給了台灣一個現成的立法語言：在能源有限的前提下，不是禁止 AI 算力，而是替醫療與生醫研究算力劃一條優先車道，並要求非豁免的大型案件完成能源需求、用水與水質、空氣品質、對弱勢社區影響與噪音的評估。對台灣的醫學中心還有一層直接好處：若院內自管模型（見第 1 點）被歸類為醫療照護用途而非商業資料中心，其擴充路徑會比外部商業算力順暢得多。這一節的政策對照屬本報推論，台灣尚無對應規範；11 月 20 日在成功大學舉行的「AI 驅動健康台灣高峰論壇」（國科會與衛福部指導，國科會主委吳誠文、衛福部長石崇良、健保署長陳亮妤等人出席，議程含法律風險、責任界定與法規調適）正是把這些問題擺上桌的場合。 (3) On electricity, what Taiwan should copy from New York is the exemption list, not the moratorium. Google signed 702 MW of fuel cells plus CCS in Wyoming in one go, while New York suspended discretionary permits for data centres at 50 MW and above — and Taiwan's power position resembles the latter. What deserves copying is the detail of that order: facilities used primarily for manufacturing, research including quantum computing and biomedical research, education and medical care are expressly exempt. That hands Taiwan ready-made legislative language: under an energy constraint, do not ban AI compute — draw a priority lane for medical and biomedical research compute, and require non-exempt large projects to assess energy demand, water use and quality, air quality, impacts on disadvantaged communities and noise. There is a direct benefit for Taiwan's academic medical centres too: if a self-hosted in-house model (see point 1) is classified as medical-care use rather than a commercial data centre, its expansion path is far smoother than external commercial compute. This policy comparison is this report's own inference — Taiwan has no corresponding rule yet — and the AI-Driven Healthy Taiwan Summit at National Cheng Kung University on 20 November (guided by the National Science and Technology Council and the Ministry of Health and Welfare, with NSTC Minister Wu Cheng-wen, Health Minister Shih Chung-liang and NHIA Director-General Chen Liang-yu among the speakers, and an agenda covering legal risk, allocation of liability and regulatory adaptation) is precisely the venue for putting these questions on the table.
