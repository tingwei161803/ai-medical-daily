2026 年 11 月 22 日（日）· 涵蓋 11/16–11/22 · 台北時間 Sunday, November 22, 2026 · Covering Nov 16–22 · Taipei time

這一週有兩條線同時走到端點，而它們指向相反的方向。一條是價格：11 月 19 日 Anthropic 以每股 83 美元賣出 9.8 億股 Class A、募得 813.4 億美元，是史上最大的 IPO，開盤 111.50 美元、收盤 97.17 美元（+17.1%），BNN Bloomberg 當天的標題寫的是「以 1.5 兆美元的公司身分掛牌」。隔天價格就把這件事翻過來：到 11 月 20 日收盤，費城半導體指數單週跌約 17%、自 9 月 30 日以來蒸發約三分之一市值，Nasdaq 100 當日跌 5.61%、收在距離熊市不到一步，Nvidia 自 10 月 6 日高點已蒸發約 2.7 兆美元市值。 Two lines reached their end points this week, and they point in opposite directions. The first is price: on November 19, Anthropic sold 980 million Class A shares at $83 and raised $81.34bn — the largest IPO on record — opening at $111.50 and closing at $97.17, up 17.1%; BNN Bloomberg ran it that day as a company arriving at a $1.5tn valuation. The next day the price took it back: by the close on November 20, the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index was down about 17% on the week and had lost roughly a third of its value since September 30, the Nasdaq 100 fell 5.61% in the session to finish a whisker short of a bear market, and Nvidia had shed about $2.7tn of market value since its October 6 peak.

另一條是證據，而它一週都沒有移動。OpenAI 11 月 16 日把 computer-use 代理人送進 CVS Health、Cigna Healthcare、Abridge 與 Ambience 的既有軟體，公告裡唯一的量化結果是 1,300 多次醫師盲測在八個維度上給了更高分；Isomorphic Labs 11 月 17 日的 50 億美元 C 輪沒有估值、沒有 program 數、沒有臨床時程；11 月 20 日七家大藥廠報出的時程壓縮全是公司自述。本週唯一真正約束賣方的文件不在華府——而在台北。 The second is evidence, and it did not move all week. 史上最大 IPO 落地：813.4 億美元、收盤 +17.1%，而掛牌後第一個完整交易日就是 AI 類股近六年最壞的一天 The largest IPO ever closes up 17.1% — and its first full trading day after listing was the worst day the AI trade has had in years

發生什麼What

11 月 18 日晚間 SEC 宣告註冊生效並定價，11 月 19 日 Anthropic 在 Nasdaq Global Select Market 以代號 ANT 掛牌。定價每股 83 美元（原先行銷區間 70–78 美元），賣出 9.8 億股 Class A（公司 956,351,354 股、既有股東 23,648,646 股），總募資 813.4 億美元、公司淨得 788.4 億美元，承銷折讓約 5.5 億美元；另有 260 億美元的無投票權 Class N 以同價私募給七家科技公司。開盤 111.50 美元（較定價高約 34%），收盤 97.17 美元（+17.1%），成交逾 8.46 億股，零售配售約占 15%。按定價計約 1.5 兆美元，收盤市值約 1.8 兆。亞馬遜持股約 14.6%、Google 約 13.4%；七位共同創辦人透過一股 Class F 握有 50.1% 投票權，Long-Term Benefit Trust 的 Class T 可選出過半董事。 The SEC declared the registration effective and the deal priced on the evening of November 18; Anthropic began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under ANT on November 19. It priced at $83 a share, above a $70–78 marketing range, selling 980 million Class A shares (956,351,354 from the company, 23,648,646 from selling holders) for $81.34bn gross and $78.84bn net to the company after roughly $550m of underwriting discount, alongside a separate $26.0bn private placement of non-voting Class N stock at the same price to seven technology companies. The stock opened at $111.50, about 34% above the offer, and closed at $97.17, up 17.1%, on more than 846 million shares, with retail taking about 15% of the offering. That is about $1.5tn at the offer price and roughly $1.8tn at the close. Amazon holds about 14.6% and Google about 13.4%; the seven co-founders control 50.1% of the vote through a single Class F share, and the Long-Term Benefit Trust's Class T stock elects a majority of the board.

為什麼重要Why it matters

把這一週擺在一起看才對：掛牌隔天，費城半導體指數單週跌約 17%（2001 年以來最慘）、Nasdaq 100 當日跌 5.61%、Nasdaq Composite 跌 5.28% 並抹掉 2026 全年漲幅，Nvidia 自 10 月 6 日高點蒸發約 2.7 兆美元市值，而同一篇報導指出 Nvidia 在 Jensen Huang 離任後的首份財報營收成長逾一倍、股價仍跌。多家財經媒體把這一週定調為 Nasdaq 100 自 2020 年以來最壞的一週。換句話說：一級市場給 AI 的定價創下史上紀錄的同一週，二級市場正在重新定價同一個故事。對醫療 AI 的意義很直接——下一輪的募資條件、併購價格與客戶的採購耐心，都會從這一週的折現率重新長出來。 The week only makes sense taken together. The day after the listing, the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index was down about 17% on the week — its worst since 2001 — the Nasdaq 100 fell 5.61% in the session and the Nasdaq Composite fell 5.28%, erasing its 2026 gains; Nvidia had lost some $2.7tn of market value since its October 6 peak, and the same report notes that its first results since Jensen Huang's departure showed revenue more than doubling while the stock still fell. Financial outlets framed the week as the Nasdaq 100's worst since 2020. In other words: the primary market set an all-time record price for AI in the same week the secondary market began repricing the same story. For medical AI the consequence is direct — the next round's terms, acquisition prices and customers' procurement patience will all regrow from this week's discount rate.

代理人落地Agents in production OpenAICVS Health · Cigna11/16

OpenAI 把 computer-use 代理人送進四家醫療客戶的既有軟體，21 個外掛同步上線——而官方公告裡只有一個量化數字 OpenAI puts computer-use agents inside four healthcare customers' existing software, with 21 plugins — and its own announcement carries exactly one quantitative result

發生什麼What

OpenAI 11 月 16 日發布 ChatGPT for Healthcare 三項更新：能讀畫面、點擊、打字並在應用程式間移動的 computer-use 代理人；改進後的臨床文獻檢索；以及「20 個以上」（頁面實列 21 個）醫療外掛，包括 UpToDate Expert AI、Redox、Availity Extend、IMO Health MCP Server、Oracle HDI Overview、PenguinAI Medical Coding、Stedi、Trizetto、Turquoise 等。公告點名四家正在部署的組織：Abridge、Ambience、Cigna Healthcare、CVS Health，用途是處理轉診、在系統之間搬文件、彙整照護資訊。公告中唯一的量化結果是臨床文獻檢索的醫師盲測：1,300 多次評分中，醫師在包含事實正確性、安全性、可行動性與來源權威性的全部八個維度上給了更高分。 OpenAI announced three updates to ChatGPT for Healthcare on November 16: computer-use agents that read screens, click, type and move between applications; improved trusted clinical search; and "more than 20" healthcare plugins — the page lists 21, among them UpToDate Expert AI, Redox, Availity Extend, the IMO Health MCP Server, Oracle HDI Overview, PenguinAI Medical Coding, Stedi, Trizetto and Turquoise. Four organisations are named as deploying agents: Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare and CVS Health, for processing referrals, moving documents between systems and bringing care information together. The single quantitative result in the announcement concerns clinical search: in over 1,300 blinded reviews, physicians rated the updated experience more highly across all eight dimensions, including factual correctness, safety, actionability and source authoritativeness.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本週最具體的一步，而它買的不是模型能力，是位置。二手報導描述的部署細節說明了為什麼：CVS 有一個代理人在單一週末清掉積了數月的文件寄送佇列，另一個每天處理數萬件專科諮詢的軟體步驟，並用驗證檢查、人工監督與螢幕截圖／動作紀錄確認應用程式確實到了正確狀態；Cigna 的個案管理師用代理人跨十個以上系統取回保險給付、醫療提供者、預約與過往互動資訊；Ambience 在病歷語意不清時會停下來問臨床人員。這一層不需要通過 FDA，也不改寫病歷——它只是替人點滑鼠，因此繞過了幾乎所有既有的證據門檻。 This is the week's most concrete step, and what it buys is not model capability but position. The deployment detail in secondary coverage shows why: at CVS one agent cleared a months-old document-sending backlog over a single weekend while another handles the software steps for tens of thousands of specialist consultations a day, with validation checks, human oversight and screenshots plus action records confirming that applications reached the correct states; at Cigna, case managers use agents to pull coverage, provider, appointment and prior-interaction details from more than ten separate systems in seconds; Ambience's agent pauses to ask a clinician when the record is ambiguous. This layer needs no FDA clearance and rewrites no chart — it only clicks the mouse for a human, and so it bypasses almost every evidentiary threshold that exists.

要打折的地方Discount this

這是本週最該校正的一則。被各處引用的安全數字——GPT-6 Astra 在 325 件 EHR 收件匣任務中 99% 獲醫師評為安全、646 次評估中 93% 被評為非常或極為有用、HealthAdminBench 上 Astra 94.3% 對 Luna 91.8%、每題成本約 8.5 美分——並不在 OpenAI 的官方公告裡，而是來自二手報導轉述的 OpenAI 自家評測；本報告逐字核對過官方頁面，頁面上唯一的數字是那 1,300 多次盲測。這些評測沒有第三方稽核、沒有公開方法學，HealthAdminBench 雖由史丹佛研究者建立，報導中亦未說明評分由誰執行。部署成效（清掉積壓、每日數萬件）全為客戶與廠商自述，無獨立量測。 This is the week's most necessary correction. The safety figures quoted everywhere — 99% of 325 EHR inbox tasks rated safe by physicians for GPT-6 Astra, 93% of 646 assessments rated very or extremely helpful, 94.3% for Astra against 91.8% for Luna on HealthAdminBench, about 8.5 cents per benchmark task — do not appear in OpenAI's own announcement. They reach us through secondary coverage of OpenAI's internal evaluations; we checked the official page line by line, and the only figure on it is those 1,300-plus blinded reviews. None of these evaluations carries third-party audit or published methodology, and while HealthAdminBench was built by Stanford researchers, the coverage does not say who ran the scoring. The deployment outcomes — backlogs cleared, tens of thousands of consultations a day — are customer- and vendor-reported with no independent measurement.

生技史上最大創投輪：Isomorphic Labs 50 億美元 C 輪，估值、program 數與臨床時程一律未揭露 Biotech's largest venture round ever: Isomorphic Labs raises $5bn Series C and discloses no valuation, no programme count and no clinical timeline

發生什麼What

Isomorphic Labs 11 月 17 日宣布 50 億美元 C 輪，由 Greenoaks 與 Sequoia Capital 共同領投，Andreessen Horowitz 等新投資人加入，Alphabet 繼續加碼，既有投資人 Thrive Capital、MGX、GV、CapitalG、Temasek 與英國主權 AI 基金參與。資金用於擴大自有藥物設計引擎 IsoDDE 與 AI 設計的候選藥物管線。R&D World 稱其為生技史上最大的一輪創投募資。 Isomorphic Labs announced a $5bn Series C on November 17, co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with Andreessen Horowitz among new investors, continued investment from Alphabet, and participation from existing backers Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek and the UK Sovereign AI Fund. The money goes to scaling its proprietary drug design engine, IsoDDE, and its pipeline of AI-designed therapeutic candidates. R&D World calls it the largest venture round in biotech history.

為什麼重要Why it matters

50 億美元買的是時間，而不是任何已經證明的東西。AI 製藥的賭注在於把前臨床階段壓縮，但真正決定價值的是臨床成功率——而這一點全世界目前都還沒有數據。把本週另一則放在旁邊看最清楚：同一週的回顧研究算出，129 個 AI 開發的藥物資產（來自 69 家公司）進入人體試驗，其中 24 個（18.9%）完成第二期，只有 5 個走到第三期。在那 5 個變成核准藥物之前，50 億美元的定價依據仍然是前臨床的速度數字。 The $5bn buys time, not anything yet proven. The AI-pharma bet is that the preclinical stage can be compressed, but value is decided by clinical success rates — and on that nobody anywhere has the data yet. The clearest way to see it is to set another of this week's items beside it: a review published in the same window counts 129 AI-enabled drug assets from 69 companies in human trials, of which 24 (18.9%) have completed Phase 2 and just five have reached Phase 3. Until some of those five become approved medicines, a $5bn price is still being set on preclinical speed figures.

藥物開發Drug development Novartis · Novo Nordisk · SanofiReuters11/20

七家大藥廠報出近半的時程壓縮，每一個數字都由自己量——同一篇裡唯一經同儕審查的數字指向「只有 5 個走到第三期」 Seven big drugmakers report timelines cut by up to half, every figure measured by themselves — while the only peer-reviewed number in the same story says just five have reached Phase 3

發生什麼What

11 月 20 日，Novartis、Novo Nordisk、Sanofi、Bristol Myers Squibb、AstraZeneca、GSK 與 Eli Lilly 的高管對外報出 AI 在前臨床階段的時程壓縮，整體說法是「最多縮短一半」。具名的數字有三個：Sanofi 說氣喘藥 lunsekimig 因為跳過一個中間劑量選擇階段而至少省下一年；Novartis 在腎臟科把「計畫啟動到候選藥物選定」從三年壓到 18–24 個月；Novo Nordisk 把 design–make–test–analyze 循環從八次減到三次、單次從八週縮到四週，先導優化階段從 64 週縮到 12 週。BMS 研究長 Robert Plenge 說有一個鎌刀型貧血候選藥物若沒有內部 AI 就不會被找到；Eli Lilly 負責 TuneLab 平台的 Aliza Apple 則提醒，說 AI 很快就能治好疾病的講法已經超前科學。 On November 20, executives at Novartis, Novo Nordisk, Sanofi, Bristol Myers Squibb, AstraZeneca, GSK and Eli Lilly described AI-driven compression of preclinical timelines, summarised as cuts of up to half. Three figures carry a name. Sanofi says it saved at least a full year on the asthma drug lunsekimig by skipping an intermediate dose-selection stage. Novartis cut project initiation to candidate selection in nephrology from three years to 18–24 months. Novo Nordisk reduced its design–make–test–analyze cycles from eight to three and each cycle from eight weeks to four, taking lead optimisation from 64 weeks to 12. BMS chief research officer Robert Plenge said a sickle cell candidate would not have been identified without internal AI, while Aliza Apple, who runs Eli Lilly's TuneLab platform, cautioned that claims of AI swiftly curing disease run ahead of the science.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本週賣方自報數字最密集的一則，也是最容易誤讀的一則。所有具名數字都落在「找到候選藥物」之前，而產業的失敗主要發生在之後：同一篇引用的 PhRMA 基準是每個藥物 10–15 年、臨床失敗率約九成。把前段縮短一半，在失敗率不變的情況下並不會讓更多藥物上市，只會讓同樣多的失敗來得更快、更便宜。真正該追蹤的指標不是「幾個月找到候選藥物」，而是那 129 個已進入人體試驗的資產在未來兩三年的第二、三期通過率。 This is the week's densest concentration of seller-measured numbers, and the easiest to misread. Every named figure sits before candidate identification, while the industry's failures happen mostly after it: the same story cites PhRMA benchmarks of 10–15 years per medicine and a roughly 90% clinical failure rate. Halving the front end, with the failure rate unchanged, does not put more medicines on the market — it makes the same number of failures arrive sooner and more cheaply. The metric worth tracking is not how many months it takes to find a candidate, but the Phase 2 and Phase 3 pass rates over the next two to three years for those 129 assets already in human trials.

STAT's Pharmalittle column the same day is paywalled and was used only to confirm the story exists.
