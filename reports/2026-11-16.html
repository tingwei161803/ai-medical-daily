發生什麼What

11/7，Cleerly 在芝加哥 AHA Scientific Sessions 2026 的 Late-Breaking Science 公布 PARAMOUNT（NCT06713239）的初步結果：2025 年 1 月至 2026 年 2 月於 21 個中心收案 530 人、523 人納入分析，其中 57%（300 人）為女性、中位年齡 57 歲、中位基線 LDL-C 127 mg/dL（納入條件為 LDL-C ≥ 100 mg/dL）。介入組接受以 AI 為基礎的定量冠狀動脈電腦斷層分析（AI-QCT），對照組依現行 AHA/ACC 指引接受壓力測試。180 天的預先指定階層式複合終點 win ratio 2.00（95% CI 1.37–2.56，p<0.0001）；未配對的兩兩比較中，斑塊組病人結果較好的比例為 46.7%，對照組 23.4%。降脂藥使用率在 AI-QCT 組由 31% 升至 75%，對照組由 32% 升至 47%。在接受侵入性冠狀動脈攝影的病人中，未再進行 PCI 或繞道手術者，AI-QCT 組 38%、對照組 60%（Business Wire, 2026-11-07；Medical Device Network）。 On 7 November, Cleerly presented preliminary results from PARAMOUNT (NCT06713239) as Late-Breaking Science at AHA Scientific Sessions 2026 in Chicago. Between January 2025 and February 2026, 530 patients were randomized at 21 sites and 523 analyzed: 57% women (300), median age 57, median baseline LDL-C 127 mg/dL, with enrollment requiring LDL-C of at least 100 mg/dL. The intervention arm received AI-based quantitative coronary CT analysis (AI-QCT); the control arm received stress testing under current AHA/ACC guidelines. The prespecified 180-day hierarchical composite endpoint gave a win ratio of 2.00 (95% CI 1.37–2.56, p<0.0001). In unmatched pairwise comparisons, the plaque-based patient had the better result 46.7% of the time against 23.4% for usual care. Lipid-lowering therapy rose from 31% to 75% in the AI-QCT arm and from 32% to 47% in usual care. Among patients who underwent invasive angiography, 38% in the AI-QCT arm did not proceed to PCI or bypass surgery, against 60% in usual care (Business Wire, 2026-11-07; Medical Device Network).

為什麼重要Why it matters

過去十年的影像 AI 幾乎都靠回溯性 AUC 說話，隨機分配的臨床試驗屈指可數，這是把 AI 影像判讀當成「照護策略」而非「讀片工具」去隨機化的第一例。但要看清它贏了什麼：對 win ratio 貢獻最大的單項，是「胸痛是否由冠心病引起的診斷確定性」，win difference 16.6 個百分點——也就是說，這份試驗主要證明的是醫師更有把握、更多病人拿到降脂藥、更少人做了白做的侵入性攝影，而不是心肌梗塞或死亡變少了。180 天的窗口本來也看不到硬終點。對台灣的健保給付討論來說，這正是最難處理的一類證據：流程改善明確、硬終點未知。 For a decade, imaging AI has argued its case with retrospective AUCs; randomized trials have been rare. This is the first to randomize AI image interpretation as a care strategy rather than a reading tool. But it is worth being precise about what it won. The single largest contributor to the win ratio was diagnostic certainty about whether chest pain was caused by coronary disease, a win difference of 16.6 percentage points. What the trial mainly demonstrates, then, is that physicians were more certain, more patients left on lipid-lowering therapy, and fewer invasive angiograms ended without an intervention — not that infarctions or deaths fell. A 180-day window could not have shown hard outcomes anyway. For reimbursement debates in Taiwan this is the hardest class of evidence to handle: the process gain is clear and the hard endpoint is unknown.

發生什麼What

Its HearO system analyzes speech recorded on an ordinary smartphone. The reported results: more than 70% of heart-failure events predicted, an average of 24.6 days in advance; 85% of scheduled daily recordings completed; usability rated 4.7 to 4.9 out of 5; 2.65 unexplained alerts per patient-year against a prespecified goal of fewer than three; and no device-related adverse events (PR Newswire, 2026-11-09). The system holds FDA Breakthrough Device designation and a European CE mark and is approved in Israel; in the US it remains investigational, with a De Novo submission and a 2027 launch planned.

為什麼重要Why it matters

心衰再住院是給付政策的核心標的，而現行的居家監測幾乎都要耗材或穿戴裝置：體重計、血壓計、植入式肺動脈壓感測器。語音是唯一一種「病人已經擁有的硬體」就能採樣的生理訊號，邊際成本接近零、語言可擴充——這份研究用五種語言收案，正是衝著這一點。24.6 天的提前量若成立，落在門診可以介入的時間尺度上（調整利尿劑、提早回診），而不是只能叫救護車。 Heart-failure readmission is a central target of payment policy, and current home monitoring almost always needs consumables or wearables: scales, cuffs, implanted pulmonary artery pressure sensors. Voice is the one physiological signal that can be sampled with hardware the patient already owns, at a marginal cost near zero and across languages — which is precisely why this study enrolled in five. If the 24.6-day lead time holds, it falls on a timescale where outpatient care can act (adjusting diuretics, bringing the visit forward) rather than one where the only option is an ambulance.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本週唯一一個樣本數量級到十萬以上的臨床 AI 數字，而且它量的不是「模型比醫師準」，而是一個更有用的問題：在醫師已經簽出報告之後，模型還能多找出多少經專家確認的病灶。EDR 這個指標把討論從 AUC 移到了「漏診的增量」，這正是放射科最在意的那一面。但同一份新聞稿也示範了這個產業最大的方法論問題：出資方、研究執行方、模型使用方與商業受益方是同一家公司的不同部門——對照組是這批醫師自己過去的表現，不是另一批醫師。真正能解讀這些數字的，是 11/29–12/3 RSNA 2026 之後放出的那兩篇論文細節。 This is the only clinical AI figure of the week with a sample in six digits, and it measures something more useful than "the model beats the doctor": after a radiologist has already signed the report, how many additional expert-confirmed findings does the model surface? EDR shifts the argument from AUC to incremental missed findings, which is the side radiology actually cares about. The same release also demonstrates the field's central methodological problem: funder, investigator, user and commercial beneficiary are divisions of one company, and the comparator is these same radiologists' own past performance rather than a separate group. What would let anyone read these numbers properly is the detail in the two papers after RSNA 2026 on 29 November to 3 December.

Pedro Orsini led the work; Francisco Marchi gave the oral presentation on 13 November and the poster on 14 November (Abstract H071) (Clinical Lab Products, 2026-11-13).

為什麼重要Why it matters

血液腫瘤的分類目前要靠形態學、流式細胞術、細胞遺傳學與次世代定序的多步驟組合，耗時且分散在不同檢體與不同實驗室。甲基化不是形態學，而是在 DNA 上直接讀細胞起源與分化狀態，理論上可以用單一檢體一次完成分型。真正的賣點不是 99.5% 的敏感度（偵測有無腫瘤相對容易），而是 90.9% 的亞型正預測值——81 個亞型是一個相當細的分類粒度，世界衛生組織的血液腫瘤分類本身就不斷在改版。如果這個數字在前瞻性樣本上站得住，分類路徑會從多步驟壓縮成單一檢測。 Classifying blood cancers today takes a multi-step combination of morphology, flow cytometry, cytogenetics and next-generation sequencing — slow, and spread across specimens and laboratories. Methylation is not morphology; it reads cell of origin and differentiation state directly off the DNA, so in principle a single specimen could carry the whole subtype call. The selling point is not the 99.5% sensitivity, since detecting whether a tumor is present is comparatively easy, but the 90.9% subtype PPV: 81 subtypes is a fine granularity, and the WHO classification of hematolymphoid tumors is itself revised repeatedly. If that number holds prospectively, the classification pathway collapses from several steps into one test.

  • 優勢Strength ：目前唯一拿得出隨機分配證據的 AI 影像產品——21 個中心、523 人、180 天複合終點 win ratio 2.00（95% CI 1.37–2.56，p<0.0001）。這在給付談判桌上的份量，遠超過任何回溯 AUC。 : the only AI imaging product with randomized evidence in hand — 21 sites, 523 patients, a 180-day composite win ratio of 2.00 (95% CI 1.37–2.56, p<0.0001). At a reimbursement table that outweighs any retrospective AUC.
  • 優勢Strength ：行為改變是直接可量的——降脂藥使用率 31%→75%，對照組僅 32%→47%；侵入性攝影後未做介入的比例由 60% 降到 38%，省下的是最貴的那一段。 : the behavior change is directly measurable — lipid-lowering therapy went from 31% to 75% against 32% to 47% in usual care, and angiograms that ended without an intervention fell from 60% to 38%, which is where the expensive waste sits.
  • 疑慮Concern ：贏的主要項目是「診斷確定性」（win difference 16.6 個百分點），而非心肌梗塞或死亡。180 天看不到硬終點，而公司明寫完整數據要等 2027 年初、結果尚未經同儕審查。 : the winning component was diagnostic certainty (a 16.6-point win difference), not infarction or death. A 180-day window cannot show hard outcomes, and the company states that full data are due in early 2027 and the results are not yet peer reviewed.
  • 疑慮Concern ：階層式複合終點的組成項目、權重與順序完全沒公布——這類終點的結論對項目排序極為敏感，不公開就無法複核。安全性、輻射劑量與成本一項都沒有。 : the components, weights and ordering of the hierarchical composite are not published at all — conclusions from such endpoints are highly sensitive to that ordering, and without it the result cannot be checked. Safety, radiation dose and cost are all absent.

  • 疑慮Concern ：研究由自家執行，對照組是同一批醫師導入前的表現，沒有信賴區間、統計方法、專家小組人數，CT 的具體錯誤率缺漏。公司說會監測過度依賴，但沒公布任何監測結果——而這恰好是 AI 草擬報告最該量的風險。 : the study was run in house, the comparator is the same radiologists before adoption, and there are no confidence intervals, statistical methods or panel sizes, with CT error rates left out. 04 — Taiwan Angle

台灣這一週同時是三種角色：共識草案的主辦者、臨床試驗的執行者、以及別人模型的驗證場 Taiwan played three roles this week at once: host of a draft consensus, conductor of its own trials, and validation site for other people's models

(1) 主辦者：台北在審的那 14 項原則，正好是本週國際證據缺口的反面。11/12–15 WCIM 2026 在南港展覽館二館舉行，11/13 下午的全球論壇審議《人工智慧、醫療與人文台北聲明》草案，14 項原則明文要求醫師查證 AI 產出、並保有質疑、修正或停用系統的能力（TVBS 健康 2.0）。把這一條放回本週的材料上讀，它的執行難度就現形了：Cleerly 沒公布複合終點的組成項目、Mosaic 沒公布信賴區間與 CT 錯誤率、ALMA 沒公布獨立測試集樣本數、Cordio 沒公布 specificity——醫師要「查證產出」，得先有東西可查。這份草案真正該被追問的，不是原則寫得對不對，而是有沒有配套要求廠商揭露到可查證的程度。 (1) Host: the 14 principles under review in Taipei are the mirror image of this week's evidence gaps. WCIM 2026 ran 12–15 November at Hall 2 of the Nangang Exhibition Center, and a global forum on the afternoon of 13 November reviewed the draft Taipei Declaration on Artificial Intelligence, Medicine and Humanity, whose 14 principles explicitly require physicians to verify AI output and keep the ability to question, correct or stop a system (TVBS Health 2.0). Read against this week's material, the difficulty of enforcement appears immediately: Cleerly did not publish the components of its composite endpoint, Mosaic published no confidence intervals or CT error rates, ALMA no independent test set size, Cordio no specificity. To verify output, a physician needs something to verify. The question worth pressing on this draft is not whether the principles are right, but whether anything obliges vendors to disclose enough to make them operable.

(2) 執行者：台灣已經做過本週國際消息裡沒有的那種試驗。三軍總醫院的《AI-enabled ECG alert for potassium imbalance treatment》是一個在急診執行的務實型隨機對照試驗，AI 嵌進醫院電子病歷、以醫師層級隨機分配以避免打斷臨床流程，納入近 15,000 位病人，結果為在 AI 標記為高血鉀高風險的病人中，介入組在一小時內獲得治療的比例顯著高於對照組，且未增加過度治療，論文刊於 Nature Communications（衛福部智慧醫療中心，2026-01-09）。這份研究的設計正是 Mosaic 做不到的那一半：隨機分配、嵌入既有流程、以「病人多快拿到治療」為終點而非偵測率。同樣的制度性安排來自衛福部自 2024 年起推動的 AI 影響性研究制度，五個示範中心為北榮、中榮、三總、成大與台大，並設中央審查會（C-IRB）與多中心資料串流分析，明確把「試驗—證據—給付」接成一個循環、由健保署參與（衛生福利部，2025-11-20）。 (2) Conductor: Taiwan has already run the kind of trial this week's international news lacks. Tri-Service General Hospital's "AI-enabled ECG alert for potassium imbalance treatment" is a pragmatic randomized controlled trial in emergency care, with the AI embedded in the hospital's electronic medical record and randomization at physician level so clinical workflow was not interrupted. Nearly 15,000 patients were analyzed, and among those the AI flagged as at high risk of hyperkalemia, the intervention group was significantly more likely to be treated within one hour, without an increase in overtreatment; the paper appeared in Nature Communications (MOHW smart healthcare center, 9 January 2026). Its design is exactly the half Mosaic could not supply: randomized, embedded in existing workflow, with how quickly the patient got treated as the endpoint rather than a detection rate. The same institutional arrangement comes from the AI impact research system the Ministry of Health and Welfare has run since 2024, with five demonstration centers — Taipei Veterans General, Taichung Veterans General, Tri-Service General, National Cheng Kung University Hospital and National Taiwan University Hospital — a central review board (C-IRB) and multi-center data-streaming analysis, closing the loop from trial to evidence to reimbursement with the National Health Insurance Administration taking part (Ministry of Health and Welfare, 20 November 2025).

(3) 驗證場：台灣的資料在國際研究裡出現的位置值得注意。本週被重新廣傳的那篇 Nature 心電圖猝死模型，外部驗證隊列之一是台灣的醫院登錄資料，用來預測未來的心律不整性心搏停止（Nature, 2026-06-24）。台灣的心電圖與死亡登錄資料品質足以當國際論文的外部驗證集，這是實力的證明；但在同一篇論文裡，開發、建模與發表都發生在瑞典與美國，台灣的角色是驗證。這個分工不是壞事，卻值得問一句：既然資料品質夠做驗證，為什麼不夠做開發？答案通常不在資料，而在串接資料所需的治理與算力安排——這也正是 AI 影響性研究制度與 C-IRB 想處理的事。 (3) Validation site: where Taiwanese data appears in international research is worth noting. One external validation cohort of the Nature ECG sudden-death model recirculating this week was a Taiwanese hospital registry, used to predict future arrhythmic cardiac arrest (Nature, 24 June 2026). That Taiwan's ECG and mortality registry data is good enough to serve as the external validation set for an international paper is a demonstration of capability; but in that same paper, development, modeling and publication all happened in Sweden and the United States, and Taiwan's role was to validate. The division of labor is not in itself bad, though it invites a question: if the data is good enough to validate with, why not to develop with? The answer usually lies not in the data but in the governance and compute arrangements needed to link it — which is precisely what the AI impact research system and the C-IRB are meant to address.

(4) 落地端：院級導入仍然缺數字。11/9 的報導指出，為恭紀念醫院導入腦出血及腦中線偏移的 AI 影像輔助偵測系統，病人完成無顯影劑頭部電腦斷層後由系統標示疑似異常切片回傳 PACS、並透過院內資訊系統通知醫師；院長表示急性出血性腦中風病人從完成 CT 到啟動神經科會診的時間較導入前縮短，但報導未提供具體秒數或百分比、未提準確率與案例數，也未提廠商（蕃新聞，2026-11-09）。這跟本週的國際消息其實是同一個毛病的兩端：國外的問題是數字很多但無法複核，國內的問題是流程改善確實發生但沒有數字。對有能力做臨床研究的醫院來說，這類導入正是最容易轉成一份可發表的前後對照或務實型試驗的素材。 (4) Deployment: hospital-level adoption still lacks numbers. A 9 November report describes Wei Gong Memorial Hospital adopting an AI imaging aid for intracranial hemorrhage and midline shift: after a non-contrast head CT, the system marks suspect slices back into PACS and notifies the physician through the hospital information system. The superintendent says the time from completed CT to neurology consultation for acute hemorrhagic stroke patients has shortened since adoption, but the report gives no seconds or percentages, no accuracy figure, no case count and no vendor (Yam News, 9 November 2026). This is the other end of the same fault as the international news this week: abroad, plenty of numbers that cannot be checked; at home, real process improvement with no numbers at all. For hospitals able to run clinical research, adoptions like this are the easiest raw material to turn into a publishable before-and-after or pragmatic trial.
