為什麼重要Why it matters

Google is not selling a system of record; it is selling a read-and-orchestrate layer above one, so its only possible moat is the breadth of its connectors — which is exactly what the partner announcements of November 17–19 were filling in: Keragon wiring 400+ healthcare applications through a single MCP connection, and Verato turning patient identity resolution into an MCP server. The demand-side numbers are all in the release: clinicians lose nearly 28 hours a week to documentation and administrative work (a Harris Poll/Google Cloud study), 41.9% of physicians reported burnout (2025 AMA report), and administrative costs run 25% to 35% of US health spending (AHA). Northwell CEO John D'Angelo, an emergency physician himself, says about 400 employees fight insurance denials full time and overturn more than 90% of them — pure labor cost in what he calls \"a 1% margin business at best.\" Highmark says 78% of approved outpatient prior authorizations submitted digitally are already processed automatically.

The Assistant answers clinical questions with citations to peer-reviewed literature and clinical guidelines, covering screening guidelines, treatment options and recommended workups, and supports differential diagnosis, medication comparison and clinical calculators, leaving the final decision with the clinician. Abridge says it was founded in 2018, expects its platform to support more than 120 million patient-clinician encounters this year across more than 300 US health systems, has validated encounters in 28+ languages, and won Best in KLAS for ambient AI in both 2025 and 2026. 為什麼重要Why it matters

這是一次通路占領，不是一次慈善。Abridge 的本業是語音病歷，而 Assistant 是臨床問答——後者的現任霸主是 OpenEvidence，一個對醫師全面免費、由藥廠與醫材廣告養的產品，據報在 2025 年 12 月已有約 76 萬名註冊美國醫師、超過美國醫師的四成。要在一個免費產品面前搶使用者，你只能也免費，而且要帶著對手沒有的東西進場：Abridge 帶的是它已經在病人床邊錄音的那條管線，以及 Wiley 的指引證據。社區健康中心是最好的灘頭堡——預算最緊、最不可能自己付錢買臨床問答，而一旦臨床人員習慣在同一個介面裡問問題，文件產品的銷售就變成順水推舟。 This is a distribution land-grab, not charity. Abridge's core business is ambient documentation; the Assistant is clinical question-answering, a category whose incumbent is OpenEvidence — free to all physicians and funded by pharmaceutical and device advertising, with reportedly about 760,000 registered US physicians by December 2025, more than 40% of American doctors. To take users from something already free, you have to be free too, and arrive carrying something the incumbent lacks: in Abridge's case, the pipeline it already runs at the bedside, plus Wiley's guideline evidence. Community health centers are the right beachhead — the tightest budgets, the least likely to buy clinical Q&A on their own — and once clinicians are asking questions inside the same interface, selling them the documentation product is downhill work.

為什麼重要Why it matters

04 — Taiwan Angle

今天這些產品共同缺的那樣東西，台灣剛好蓋了一個機構專門生產它 What every product above is missing is the one thing Taiwan built an institution to produce

(1) 台灣把「誰來量」做成了制度，而不是廠商的自願。衛福部自 2024 年 10 月 7 日起成立三大 AI 中心（衛福部公告、三大中心網站）：負責任 AI 執行中心負責資安與隱私管理辦法、公開模型與資料與效能資訊、可解釋性分析與 AI 模型生命週期管理；臨床 AI 取證驗證中心組成醫院聯盟、串接跨層級電子病歷資料協助廠商驗證模型；AI 影響性研究中心建立跨體系臨床試驗機制，評估臨床效益並建立「科學性的定價基礎」以爭取健保給付。徵案過程是 30 家醫院提了 48 案、三階段評選後 16 家醫院的 19 案通過決選（臺大、中榮、北榮、成大、中醫大、三總、林口長庚等）。把今天的新聞對照著看：Suki 要自己掏錢做保證、Ambience 只能拿一家客戶的 CMI 說話，而台灣的制度設計是先假設廠商自述不可採信，再蓋一個第三方來量。 (1) Taiwan turned \"who does the measuring\" into an institution rather than a vendor's volunteer act. Since October 7, 2024 the Ministry of Health and Welfare has run three AI centers (MOHW announcement, center site): a Responsible AI Implementation Center covering security and privacy rules, disclosure of models, data and performance, explainability analysis and AI model lifecycle management; a Clinical AI Validation Center that forms a hospital consortium and links EHR data across care levels to help vendors validate models; and an AI Impact Research Center that builds cross-system clinical trial machinery to assess clinical benefit and establish a \"scientific basis for pricing\" in pursuit of National Health Insurance coverage. In the call for proposals, 30 hospitals submitted 48 proposals and, after three rounds, 19 proposals from 16 hospitals were selected (NTU Hospital, Taichung and Taipei Veterans General, NCKU Hospital, China Medical University Hospital, Tri-Service General and Linkou Chang Gung among them). Read against today's news: Suki has to fund its own guarantee and Ambience can only speak through one customer's CMI, while Taiwan's design starts from the assumption that vendor self-reporting is not credible and builds a third party to do the measuring.

(2) 那條給付路徑的時間尺度是三年，不是一次發表會。AI 影響性研究中心與國際論壇於 2025 年 11 月 20 日舉行聯合啟動（聯合報），主題是「以臨床實證推動 AI 醫療創新」，由北榮、中榮、三總、成大、臺大五家醫學中心執行本土臨床試驗與醫療科技評估——官方的說法是過去的評估依賴國外 RCT 資料。五家醫院的試驗資料會送健保署的 HTA 評估中心，再進健保共擬會（成員含專家、學者與公民代表）決定是否納入給付；衛福部資訊處長李建璋表示預計約三年後送件，並指出健保一旦納入給付的藥品與服務極少被移除，所以試驗設計必須謹慎。把今天的三個數字放進這條管線想一遍：「最多減少 36% 收件匣時間」「CMI 拉升 6.5%」「約 3 倍 ROI」沒有一個帶著方法學、樣本數與期間，也就沒有一個進得了 HTA 的門。對台灣廠商而言，這是一個很實際的提醒：你在美國市場學來的那套新聞稿數字，在這條路徑上不構成證據。 (2) That reimbursement path runs on a three-year clock, not a launch event. The AI Impact Research Center held its joint launch and international forum on November 20, 2025 (UDN) under the theme of advancing AI medical innovation through clinical evidence, with five medical centers — Taipei Veterans General, Taichung Veterans General, Tri-Service General, NCKU Hospital and NTU Hospital — running domestic trials and health technology assessment, officials noting that past evaluations leaned on overseas RCT data. Trial data from the five goes to the NHI Administration's HTA evaluation center and then to the NHI Joint Drafting Committee, whose members include experts, scholars and public representatives, which decides on coverage; MOHW Information Department director Lee Chien-chang expects submission in about three years, noting that drugs and services once covered are rarely removed, so trial design has to be careful. Now run today's three numbers through that pipeline: \"up to 36% less inbox time,\" \"6.5% CMI lift\" and \"about 3x ROI\" carry no methodology, sample size or period between them, so not one of them gets through the HTA door. For Taiwanese vendors that is a practical warning: the press-release arithmetic learned from the US market does not count as evidence on this path.
