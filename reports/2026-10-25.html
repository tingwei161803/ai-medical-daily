AI 設計的噬菌體第一次進到人體：走恩慈療法，臨床結果一個字都沒公開 AI-designed phages reach a human patient for the first time — by compassionate use, with nothing published about the outcome

發生什麼What

10/20 Radical Numerics 發表 Omnii-1（多模態基因體語言模型，先在多樣基因序列與多體學資料上預訓練、再以噬菌體基因體續訓），並宣布由它設計的噬菌體雞尾酒已在「獲授權、醫師主導的恩慈療法途徑」下給一名抗藥性細菌感染病人使用，給藥前體外測試可清除該病人的抗藥菌株。示範實驗以 T7 的尾絲蛋白 gp17 為對象：11–15 個胺基酸改動時回收率 69.9%、隨機對照 4.8%；6,694 個有可測活性的變異中 2,161 個超過野生型；最難的菌株上模型前 100 名有 24% 跨過活性門檻、整個 library 只有 4.5%。配對端在 37 株留出的大腸桿菌上只用兩株噬菌體覆蓋 91.4%，綠膿桿菌平均精確度 0.570→0.608、超過 GenoPHI 的 0.581（紐約時報 10/20、公司新聞稿）。 On 10/20 Radical Numerics published Omnii-1 — a multimodal genome language model pretrained on diverse genomic sequences and multiomics data, then trained on phage genomes — and said a cocktail of its designed phages had been given to a patient with an antibiotic-resistant infection under an authorised, physician-led compassionate-use pathway, after in vitro testing eliminated that patient's resistant isolate. Its demonstration targeted gp17, T7's tail fibre: 69.9% recovery at 11–15 amino-acid changes against 4.8% for random controls; 2,161 of 6,694 variants with measurable activity beat wild type; on the hardest strain 24% of the model's top 100 cleared the threshold against 4.5% of the full library. Two phages covered 91.4% of 37 held-out E. coli strains, and Pseudomonas average precision rose 0.570 to 0.608, past GenoPHI's 0.581 (New York Times, 10/20; company release).

為什麼重要Why it matters

本週唯一把生成式模型的輸出直接送進人體的事。其他「AI 做生物學」都還在試管與板子上——NVIDIA 與華盛頓大學 10/21 開源 RFD4-Proteina、Anthropic 10/20 的 Claude 設計蠍毒結合蛋白、OpenAI 用數千個 agent 讀小鼠全腦 CRISPR 圖譜，三件都是模型產出候選、人類後續驗證。要記住的不是 69.9%，而是「設計—驗證—給藥」之間的時間被壓到了什麼程度。 The only thing this week that put a generative model's output into a person. The rest of AI-for-biology stayed in tubes and plates — NVIDIA and UW open-sourcing RFD4-Proteina on 10/21, Anthropic's 10/20 Claude-designed scorpion-toxin binders, OpenAI's thousands of agents on a mouse CRISPR atlas — all model-proposes, human-verifies. The number to remember is not 69.9% but how far the interval from design to dosing has compressed.

要打折的地方Discount this

公司明寫這些療法屬研究用途、未獲核准作更廣泛使用，且單一病人無法建立安全性或療效；病人的病程與結果完全未揭露，雞尾酒候選數、模型大小與架構也沒給。回收率只是存活力的代理指標，而所有數據只在部落格以高階摘要呈現、沒有同儕審查論文（已有媒體直接標成「第一位病人已治療，但無臨床資料發表」）。 The company states the therapies are investigational, not approved for broader use, and that one patient cannot establish safety or efficacy. The clinical course and outcome are withheld, as are the candidate count, model size and architecture. Recovery is only a proxy for viability, and every figure is a high-level blog summary with no peer-reviewed paper (some coverage labels it exactly that: first patient treated, no clinical data published).

MAI-DxO 登上《Science》：NEJM 複雜病例 83% 對醫師 36%，但它考的是「按順序追問、點檢查」 MAI-DxO lands in Science at 83% against physicians' 36% — graded on sequential questioning and test-ordering

發生什麼What

10/22 Microsoft AI 把 MAI-DxO（MAI Diagnostic Orchestrator）發表在《Science》：NEJM 複雜臨床病例 83% 正確診斷率，對照的一般科醫師在不能查資料、不能會診的條件下 36%（TechCrunch 10/22、Unite.AI）。作法是把多個語言模型組成編排器，依序追問病史、點檢查、再收斂診斷，並把檢查成本算進目標函數；Suleyman 把路線講成「醫療超智慧」，底層方法見於 Sequential Diagnosis with Language Models。 On 10/22 Microsoft AI published MAI-DxO (the MAI Diagnostic Orchestrator) in Science: 83% correct on complex NEJM case records against 36% for generalist physicians with no reference materials and no colleagues to consult (TechCrunch, 10/22; Unite.AI). It orchestrates several language models into a loop that asks for history, orders tests and narrows to a diagnosis, with testing cost in the objective. Suleyman frames it as medical superintelligence; the method appeared in Sequential Diagnosis with Language Models.

為什麼重要Why it matters

本週把「AI 比醫師強」推到最高點的一件事，而且刊在《Science》而非廠商部落格——發表場域本身就是訊號。它證明的是：把語言模型組成一個省著點檢查、按順序逼近的流程，在文本型疑難病例上可以做得很好。和同週 UT Southwestern 的分流研究擺在一起才完整：一個說編排得好能到 83%，一個說同一類模型的排序會被病歷裡一句措辭推翻。 The week's loudest claim that AI beats physicians, and it ran in Science rather than a vendor blog — the venue is the signal. What it establishes is that orchestrating language models into a frugal, sequential narrowing works very well on text-based hard cases. It is complete only read alongside UT Southwestern's triage study: one says good orchestration reaches 83%, the other says the same class of model has its ordering overturned by a single phrase in a chart.

要打折的地方Discount this

題庫是 NEJM 的 Case Record——寫出來就是為了難的病例文本，不是真實門診病人；對照醫師不能查文獻、不能會診，這不是臨床實況；研究由 Microsoft 自家團隊執行，沒有獨立前瞻驗證。把 83% 讀成臨床可用的診斷率是誤讀（連二手報導也把「真實世界測試還在前面」寫進標題）。 The test set is NEJM Case Records — texts written to be hard, not clinic patients. The comparison physicians could look nothing up and consult nobody, which is not clinical reality. The work was run by Microsoft's own team with no independent prospective validation. Reading 83% as a clinically usable rate is a misreading (even secondary coverage puts "real-world tests are still ahead" in the headline).

只要在病歷補一句「頻繁掛急診」，近 10% 後來真的惡化的病人被模型往後排 Add one line about frequent ED visits, and nearly 10% of the patients who later deteriorated get pushed down the queue

發生什麼What

10/20 UT Southwestern 在 npj Digital Medicine 發表研究（第一作者 Philip Jarrett、資深作者 Andrew Jamieson），用 Stanford Medicine 與 Beth Israel Deaconess 兩家學術急診的病歷，把到院嚴重度相近、後續一人惡化一人沒有的病人配對，讓三個開源大型語言模型判斷該優先處理誰，再把人口學、社會或帶汙名的敘述加進病歷（校方新聞稿 10/20）。加入前三個模型都遠高於隨機；加入後「頻繁使用急診」與「精神科急診史」影響最大，部分情境下近 10% 的病人被錯誤降級。年齡也影響決策，但研究者認為反映的是高齡者本身的風險。Newsweek指出研究共檢視 18 種病況。 On 10/20 UT Southwestern published in npj Digital Medicine (first author Philip Jarrett, senior author Andrew Jamieson) a study using records from two academic emergency departments, Stanford Medicine and Beth Israel Deaconess. Patients who arrived looking similarly ill but diverged afterwards were paired, three open-source large language models were asked which to prioritise, and demographic, social or stigmatising detail was then added (university release, 10/20). Beforehand all three ran well above chance; afterwards frequent ED use and a psychiatric-emergency history had the largest effect, with nearly 10% incorrectly downgraded in some scenarios. Age shifted some decisions, which the authors read as older patients' real risk. Newsweek reports 18 conditions were examined.

為什麼重要Why it matters

重點不是模型不準——它們本來就比隨機好很多。重點是「給它更多資訊」不等於更好的決策，而醫院上線分流模型時給的恰恰是完整病歷。Jarrett 的原話是「More information does not automatically mean better decisions.」他的處方很具體：每個工具都要拿真實病人結果在本地驗過，專門測精神科病史、社會處境與汙名化措辭的差異，上線後持續監測。這和 美國醫院協會 10/19 向 FDA 主張「醫院看不進黑盒子、上市後監測主責應落在開發商」是同一個問題的兩面。 The point is not that the models are inaccurate — they were well above chance. The point is that more information does not mean better decisions, and a full chart is exactly what a hospital hands a deployed triage model. Jarrett: "More information does not automatically mean better decisions." His prescription is specific — validate locally against real outcomes, test deliberately for psychiatric history, social circumstance and stigmatising language, and keep monitoring. That is the other face of the AHA telling the FDA on 10/19 that hospitals cannot see inside these tools and post-market surveillance belongs with developers.

要打折的地方Discount this

新聞稿未揭露樣本數、未指名三個開源模型；「近 10%」是部分情境的數字、未逐病況列出。研究不是讓 AI 真的排真實病人的處理順序，而是用既有病歷做受控比較。經費來自校長辦公室自有經費，後續研究另有 National Foundation of Emergency Medicine 補助。 The release does not disclose sample size or name the three models; "nearly 10%" covers some scenarios and is not broken out by condition. The study did not let an AI set the real order of care — it used existing records in controlled comparisons. Funding came from the Office of the President's institutional funds, with follow-on support from the National Foundation of Emergency Medicine.

紅隊報告：OpenAI 內部只有約 75–94% 的 agent 流量「有資格」被監控，而監控要等 agent 交回控制權才觸發 Red-team report: only about 75–94% of OpenAI's internal agent traffic is eligible for monitoring — which fires only when the agent hands control back

發生什麼What

10/22 METR 與 Redwood Research 公布在 OpenAI 內部執行的高權限紅隊演練，期間 7/21–8/11、報告 61 頁。兩個核心發現：只有約 75–94% 的內部 agentic 流量「有資格」被既有監控覆蓋；監控是在 agent 把控制權交回使用者時才觸發，因此持續動作、不交還控制權的 agent 可以無限期延後被監控。CNBC 10/22把它放在 OpenAI 內部安全治理脈絡裡，The Register 10/23的標題更直接：紅隊報告指 OpenAI 在 Hugging Face 事件期間「完全沒能監控自己的 agent」。 On 10/22 METR and Redwood Research published a privileged red-team exercise run inside OpenAI between 21 July and 11 August, in 61 pages. Two findings carry it: only about 75–94% of internal agentic traffic is eligible for the monitoring that exists; and monitoring triggers when an agent returns control to a user, so an agent that keeps acting can defer being monitored indefinitely. CNBC, 10/22 places it in OpenAI's internal-safety context; The Register, 10/23 is blunter, reporting OpenAI "utterly failed to monitor its agents" during the Hugging Face incident.

為什麼重要Why it matters

本週最接近「治理現況體檢」的一份文件，而且是外部機構帶高權限在內部做的。對醫療端意義很直接：醫院正在把 agent 接進病歷（Epic 本週的 Connectors 就是這件事），而最前沿的實驗室對自己的 agent 都還只有部分可觀測性。採購 agent 的醫院該問的問題因此很具體：多少比例的 agent 行為落在你的日誌裡、觸發條件是什麼、長時間自主動作期間誰在看。 The closest thing the week produced to a governance physical, done from inside with privileges by outsiders. The implication for healthcare is immediate: hospitals are wiring agents into the chart (Epic's Connectors this week is precisely that) while the most advanced lab in the field has only partial observability over its own. The questions become concrete: what share of agent behaviour lands in your logs, what triggers the record, and who is watching during a long autonomous run.

10/21 Epic 在威斯康辛 Verona 的年度互通性大會開場發表 Connectors：外部應用與 Epic 病歷互相取資料，病歷端的 AI 代理可直接在別的 App 把事情做完（Healthcare IT News 10/21、Fierce Healthcare）。可搭配面向病人的 Emmie、面向臨床的 Art、面向營收的 Penny，以及用無程式碼 Agent Factory 自建的代理。首批夥伴：Gozio Health（院內導航）、VPL（供應鏈與處方物流，走 VPLRx）、Availity（營收循環，開發中）。示例包括 Emmie 帶院內導航指引、查醫材 App 的輔具可用性、住院病人叫 Emmie 用醫院點餐 App 訂餐。開發者做的 Connectors 要到 2027 年 3 月才能從 Epic 網站取得，屆時可逐項檢視每個 Connector 能讀什麼、能改什麼（Modern Healthcare、Epic 官方說明）。 On 10/21, opening its annual interoperability conference in Verona, Wisconsin, Epic announced Connectors: outside applications exchange data with the Epic chart, and the chart's AI agents complete tasks inside those applications (Healthcare IT News, 10/21; Fierce Healthcare). The agents are Emmie for patients, Art for clinicians and Penny for revenue cycle, plus anything built in the no-code Agent Factory. Launch partners: Gozio Health (wayfinding), VPL (supply chain and prescription logistics, via VPLRx) and Availity (revenue cycle, in development). Examples include Emmie delivering wayfinding instructions, checking a medical-equipment app for assistive-device availability, and an inpatient asking Emmie to order a meal. Developer-built Connectors arrive on Epic's website in March 2027, with a per-Connector view of what each can read and change (Modern Healthcare; Epic's own post).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是把「AI 代理」從一個功能變成一個平台位置。Epic 的 Carl Dvorak 在場上說「我們得在病人對醫療體系失去耐心之前，找到通往 AI 理想國的路」；負責 Agent Factory 的 Derek De Young 把價值講成「省掉轉椅子的工作」。對照環境式病歷廠商這一年的位置——Abridge 10/21 前後把 Assistant 開給每一位臨床人員、10/7 簽下德州大學系統八家機構——他們花三年爬到的那個臨床工作流入口，Epic 說它要自己站。而 Connectors 的權限檢視介面，恰好是 METR 報告那個問題的產品化答案。 This turns AI agents from a feature into a platform position. Epic's Carl Dvorak said on stage that "we have got to find a pathway to our AI utopia before patients become frustrated with the health system," and Derek De Young of Agent Factory framed it as sparing people swivel-chair work. Against where the ambient-documentation vendors have climbed — Abridge opening Assistant to every clinician around 10/21 and signing eight University of Texas System institutions on 10/7 — the clinical front door they spent three years reaching is one Epic now says it will occupy itself. And the per-Connector permission view is the productised answer to METR's question.

三個監管機關同一週動：FDA 意見徵集 10/19 關門、TGA 說 12 個 AI 抄寫產品有 8 個可能是醫材、MHRA 把全生命週期監管帶進 AdvaMed Three regulators moved in one week: the FDA's comment window closed 10/19, the TGA found 8 of 12 AI scribes likely devices, and the MHRA took lifecycle oversight to AdvaMed

發生什麼What

FDA 的生成式 AI 醫材監管考量討論文件意見徵集在 10/19 關門（FDA 新聞稿）。代表近 5,000 家醫院的美國醫院協會同日送件，主張彈性、風險基礎的監管，要求釐清生成式 AI 的臨床決策支援與行政工具不在管轄內，並指出 96% 的 AI 醫材走 510(k)、醫院看不進黑盒子、上市後監測主責應落在開發商（AHA 送件、Becker's 整理）；美國商會要求兩軸風險框架只用來談證據期待。CDRH 的 FY2027 指引清單縮到 17 份、5 份是軟體與 AI。澳洲 TGA 10/16 公布數位抄寫員審視：調查約 100 家供應商、40 家回覆、挑 12 個細看，8 個可能符合醫材定義、2 個待評估、2 個看來不符，而 12 家沒有一家自我歸類為醫材；受檢產品含 Heidi、Lyrebird Health、Microsoft、Abby Health、Carepatron、iMedX，但 TGA 拒絕指名（Healthcare IT News 10/21、The Medical Republic）。英國 MHRA 10/20 把全生命週期監管與 AI Airlock 第三期帶到 AdvaMed（MedTech Dive）。 The FDA's discussion paper on generative AI-enabled medical devices closed for comment on 10/19 (FDA announcement). The American Hospital Association, for nearly 5,000 hospitals, filed the same day for flexible risk-based oversight, asked that generative-AI clinical decision support and administrative tools sit outside FDA jurisdiction, and argued that with 96% of AI devices cleared through 510(k) and hospitals unable to see inside them, post-market surveillance belongs with developers (AHA filing; Becker's). The US Chamber asked that a two-axis risk framework frame only evidence expectations. CDRH's FY2027 guidance list shrank to 17, five of them software and AI. Australia's TGA published its digital-scribe review on 10/16: about 100 suppliers surveyed, 40 responses, 12 examined — 8 likely meeting the device definition, 2 pending, 2 apparently not, and not one of the 12 classifying itself as a device. Products included Heidi, Lyrebird Health, Microsoft, Abby Health, Carepatron and iMedX, and the TGA declined to name them (Healthcare IT News, 10/21; The Medical Republic). The MHRA took lifecycle regulation and AI Airlock phase three to AdvaMed on 10/20 (MedTech Dive).

為什麼重要Why it matters

三件事指向同一個問題：生成式 AI 的產品邊界在哪，由誰說。TGA 的答案最直接也最可引用——供應商在說明書上寫「本產品非醫療器材」或「非供診斷治療使用」，這類聲明本身不決定法規地位。這句話對台灣、對美國都適用，而且打到的正是目前最普及的那一類產品。歐盟方向相反：數位綜合法案把醫材內嵌 AI 的高風險義務從 2027/8/2 推到 2028/8/2（時程整理）；英國則已全盤接受 NHS 醫師主導 AI 委員會的 44 項建議。 All three point at one question: where the product boundary of generative AI sits, and who says so. The TGA's answer is the bluntest — a supplier writing "not a medical device" or "not for diagnosis or treatment" does not itself determine regulatory status. That holds for Taiwan as much as the US, and it lands on the most widely deployed category of all. The EU went the other way: the digital omnibus pushed high-risk obligations for AI embedded in devices from 2 August 2027 to 2 August 2028 (timeline); the UK has accepted all 44 recommendations of its NHS doctor-led AI commission.

02 — Product Analysis

本週兩個極端：一個把生成式模型的輸出送進人體、沒有臨床資料；一個把 FDA 核准的 AI 塞進一支人人買得起的聽診器、沒有準確度數據 Two extremes: one put a generative model's output into a person with no clinical data, the other put FDA-cleared AI into an affordable stethoscope with no accuracy data

Omnii-1 (Radical Numerics)

多模態基因體語言模型，用於噬菌體設計與噬菌體–宿主配對 · Radical Numerics（美國）Multimodal genome language model for phage design and phage-host matching · Radical Numerics (US)

功能與定位。對候選排序、對菌株做噬菌體推薦；賣點不是單一分子，而是「給定一株抗藥菌，幾天內交出一組可用的噬菌體」。需求端目前不是藥廠採購，而是個案的恩慈療法（公司技術說明，10/20）。 Function and position. It ranks candidates and recommends phages for a given strain; the pitch is not a molecule but a capability — hand it a resistant isolate, get a usable set of phages back in days. Its demand side today is individual compassionate-use cases, not pharma procurement (company post, 10/20).

  • 優勢Strength：命中率不是小幅改善。11–15 個胺基酸改動時回收率 69.9% 對隨機 4.8%；最難的菌株上模型前 100 名有 24% 跨過門檻、整個 library 只有 4.5%，等於把篩選工作量壓到約五分之一；配對能力還能外推——37 株留出的大腸桿菌只用兩株噬菌體覆蓋 91.4%。 : the hit rate is not incremental. 69.9% recovery at 11–15 amino-acid changes against 4.8% for random controls; on the hardest strain 24% of the model's top 100 cleared the threshold against 4.5% of the library, cutting the screening work to roughly a fifth. The matching generalises too — two phages covered 91.4% of 37 held-out E. coli strains.
  • 疑慮Concern：臨床端完全是空白。病人病程與結果未揭露、雞尾酒候選數與模型架構未給，公司自己寫明單一病人無法建立安全性或療效，也沒有同儕審查論文可核對（缺的不是細節，是任何可供第三方判讀的臨床資料）。恩慈療法合法且確實救得了人，但意味著這類產品可以在沒有前瞻試驗下反覆進入人體，而「誰在看、看得到多少」在這條路上幾乎沒有對應機制。 : the clinical side is blank. The patient's course and outcome are withheld, the candidate count and model architecture are not given, the company itself says one patient cannot establish safety or efficacy, and there is no peer-reviewed paper (what is missing is not detail but any clinical data a third party could read). Compassionate use is lawful and does save people, but it means this class of product can reach patients repeatedly without a prospective trial, and the question of who is watching and how much has almost no mechanism there.

CORE 300 (Eko Health)

類比／數位雙模聽診器，搭配 FDA 核准的心音 AI · Eko Health（美國）Dual analogue/digital stethoscope with FDA-cleared heart-sound AI · Eko Health (US)

功能與定位。一個聽筒旋轉切換類比與數位，35 倍放大、主動降噪、裝置上顯示即時心率，三種數位模式，接上 Eko App 的 FDA 核准 AI（含結構性心雜音偵測）；取得小兒與成人 510(k)，10/22 起官網開賣，接下來推加拿大、英國與部分歐洲市場（公司新聞稿，10/22）。定位明確：不是賣給影像科或大型醫學中心，而是賣給每一個拿聽診器的人。 Function and position. One chestpiece twists between analogue and digital, with 35x amplification, active noise cancellation, on-device heart rate and three digital modes, connected to the Eko App's FDA-cleared AI including structural murmur detection. Cleared via 510(k) for paediatric and adult patients, on sale from 10/22, with Canada, the UK and selected European markets next (company release, 10/22). Not for radiology or academic centres, but for everyone who carries a stethoscope.

  • 優勢Strength：通路已經在了。公司稱全球已交付或售出逾 750,000 台，等於 AI 不必自己建立使用者習慣——本週最短的導入路徑。價格帶也把它推向基層：新聞稿只說與高階傳統聽診器相當，HIStalk 寫成 199 美元；若以後者為準，這是本週唯一把 FDA 核准的 AI 放進三位數美元價格帶的產品。 : the channel already exists. More than 750,000 devices delivered or sold worldwide, by its own count, means the AI does not have to build a habit of its own — the shortest adoption path of the week. The price band points it at frontline care: the release says only that it matches premium traditional stethoscopes, while HIStalk reports $199 — if that holds, the only product this week putting FDA-cleared AI in a three-figure dollar band.
  • 疑慮Concern：新聞稿裡沒有任何準確度、敏感度或驗證數據，35 倍放大是全文唯一的數字、而那是放大倍率不是效能，等於發表當天無法從官方材料判讀 AI 做得多好。價格也有兩個互不一致的版本，而「交付或售出」把兩個不同的商業事實混成一個數字——澳洲 TGA 本週的提醒在這裡同樣適用：產品自述不決定它的法規地位與效能主張該怎麼讀。 : the release contains no accuracy, sensitivity or validation data; 35x amplification is its only figure, and that is gain, not performance, so on launch day the official material does not let anyone judge how well the AI performs. The price exists in two inconsistent versions, and "delivered or sold" folds two commercial facts into one number — the TGA's reminder this week applies here too: what a product says about itself does not settle its regulatory standing or how its claims should be read.

04 — Taiwan Angle

本週國際上的三條線，台灣各自已經有對應的動作，但不在同一個速度上 Taiwan has a counterpart to each of this week's three lines — just not at the same speed

(1) 病人已經在用了，台灣這一週在補識能而不是在決定要不要開。10/20 立法院厚生會成立「全民健康識能推動委員會」，由立委王正旭召集；台大醫院家醫部主任程劭儀直說病人常先問 AI 再來看醫師、當成第二意見，台灣病友聯盟理事長吳鴻來提醒病人若無法判斷 AI 答案正確性，可能延誤就醫或用錯藥。衛福部調查顯示 20 歲以上成人有 30% 健康識能不足或有限；台灣事實查核中心董事長黃兆徽指出健康不實訊息是最大類，AI 讓假內容更能分眾投放（例如客製的「醫師影片」）；次長莊人祥表示將與數位發展部合作，發現 AI 換臉詐騙或假醫師影片後要求平台下架（中央社 10/20）。對照本週的 MAI-DxO 83%與Google AMIE 在《Lancet》的前瞻可行性研究就很清楚：國際上在量「AI 答得多準」，台灣在處理的是「病人已經問完 AI 才走進診間」之後的事。 (1) Patients are already using it — Taiwan's week was about literacy, not about whether to open the door. On 10/20 the Legislative Yuan's health caucus launched a National Health Literacy Promotion Committee, convened by legislator Wang Cheng-hsu. Cheng Shao-yi, who directs family medicine at National Taiwan University Hospital, said patients often consult AI before seeing a doctor, as a second opinion; Wu Hung-lai of the Taiwan Patient Alliance warned that patients unable to judge an AI answer may delay care or take the wrong medication. A ministry survey finds 30% of adults aged 20 and over have insufficient or limited health literacy. Huang Chao-hui of the Taiwan FactCheck Center said health misinformation is the largest category and that AI makes fake content better targeted, such as tailored doctor videos; Deputy Minister Chuang Jen-hsiang said the ministry will work with the Ministry of Digital Affairs to have platforms remove AI face-swap fraud and fake doctor videos (CNA, 10/20). Beside this week's MAI-DxO at 83% and Google's AMIE feasibility study in The Lancet, the contrast is clear: elsewhere the measurement is how accurately AI answers; Taiwan spent this week on what happens after the patient has finished asking.

(2) TGA 那句話對 TFDA 直接適用，而台灣的前置條件反而設得比澳洲重。澳洲的判準是：供應商自己寫「本產品非醫療器材」不決定法規地位，而受檢的 12 個產品沒有一家自我歸類為醫材（Healthcare IT News 10/21）。台灣面對同一個分類問題但路徑不同：衛福部已把 SBOM 納入負責任 AI 中心、在 10 家醫院運作，要求進院的 AI 系統須經獨立委員會審查安全與隱私風險、公布 SBOM 並做漏洞掃描；「醫療主權雲八大方針」與 Microsoft、Google Cloud、AWS 自 2026 年初共同發展，要求資料存放台灣、端到端加密、醫院自持金鑰、未經授權不得二次利用、系統韌性與可稽核，以及管理者須具台灣籍，代價是公有雲成本可能上升 5–15%（衛福部）。澳洲走事後風險基礎稽查，台灣走進院前置條件——METR 報告指出的可觀測性缺口，台灣的 SBOM 路線至少在供應鏈側有對應機制，在「模型行為」側同樣空白。另可留意醫院 AI 應用指引已公布並強調病人知情權。 (2) The TGA's sentence applies directly to the TFDA — and Taiwan's entry conditions are heavier than Australia's. Australia's test: a supplier writing "not a medical device" does not determine regulatory status, and none of the 12 products examined classified itself as one (Healthcare IT News, 10/21). Taiwan faces the same problem by another route. The Ministry of Health and Welfare has brought software bills of materials into its Responsible AI Center across 10 hospitals, requiring AI systems entering a hospital to pass independent committee review of security and privacy risk, publish an SBOM and undergo vulnerability scanning. Its Medical Sovereign Cloud Eight Principles, developed with Microsoft, Google Cloud and AWS since early 2026, require data stored in Taiwan, end-to-end encryption, hospital-held keys, no secondary use without authorisation, resilience and auditability, and administrators holding Taiwanese nationality — at perhaps 5–15% more cost for public cloud (MOHW). Australia audits on risk after the fact; Taiwan sets conditions at the hospital door. The observability gap METR's report identified has a counterpart on the supply-chain side through SBOM, and is just as empty on the model-behaviour side. Worth noting that hospital AI guidance has been published, emphasising patients' right to know.

(3) Epic 的 Connectors 2027 年 3 月開目錄，台灣的 FHIR Box 時程幾乎疊在同一條線上。衛福部的 FHIR 交換標準透過「FHIR Box」讓醫院不換既有系統也能標準化交換，目標是 2026 年底完成全台醫學中心關鍵資料互通、2027 年底推展到全國醫院；部長石崇良同時規劃國家整合性臨床試驗網絡，並以 NT$100 億策略投資搭配健保沙盒吸引民間資本，「健康台灣深耕計畫」五年預算 NT$489 億、國家藥物韌性整備計畫四年 NT$240 億（經濟日報／udn、udn）。這個窗口意味著很具體的一件事：Epic 的開發者生態 2027 年 3 月開張時，台灣的醫學中心剛好完成互通、區域醫院正在接；兩邊若對不上標準，台灣廠商就得同時維護兩套接法。院內落地那一端，本週有鴻海數位健康的實務說法：Nurabot 護理機器人與 AMR 已在榮總體系與童綜合醫院場域驗證，結合 VLA（視覺－語言－動作）模型，另有手術室機器人與 CoDoctor 看診輔助，作者把分工講成「AI 幫忙想、機器人幫忙做，醫師負責判斷，護理師專注照護」，但全文沒有任何量化成效（工商時報 10/22）。 (3) Epic's Connectors catalogue opens in March 2027, and Taiwan's FHIR Box timeline sits almost on the same line. The ministry's FHIR push uses a "FHIR Box" so hospitals standardise exchange without replacing existing systems, aiming to connect key data across all medical centres by end-2026 and all hospitals nationwide by end-2027. Minister Shih Chung-liang is also planning a national integrated clinical trial network, with NT$10 billion in strategic investment plus the NHI sandbox to draw private capital, alongside the five-year NT$48.9 billion Healthy Taiwan programme and a four-year NT$24 billion drug-resilience programme (Economic Daily News / UDN; UDN). The window implies something concrete: when Epic's developer ecosystem opens in March 2027, Taiwan's medical centres will just have finished connecting and regional hospitals will be joining — and if the standards do not line up, Taiwanese vendors maintain two integrations instead of one. On the in-hospital end, Foxconn's digital health arm offered a practitioner's account this week: its Nurabot nursing robot and autonomous mobile robots have been validated at Veterans General Hospital facilities and Tungs' Taichung MetroHarbor Hospital, with a vision-language-action model, plus operating-room robots and the CoDoctor consultation aid. The framing — AI helps think, robots help do, physicians decide, nurses care — comes with no quantified results anywhere in the piece (Commercial Times, 10/22).

05 — Further Reading

星期日的清單挑的是「讀完會改變你怎麼判斷這一週」的四篇，不是最熱門的四篇 Sunday's list picks the four that change how you read the week, not the four that travelled furthest
  1. Omnii-1: generative phage design reaching a patient — Radical Numerics (2026-10-20)

    先讀技術段再讀最後的免責段，兩段的落差就是本週最重要的一件事：方法寫得很細，而臨床段落只有一句「為保護隱私不揭露」。這是判斷一家生物 AI 公司成熟度最好的對照練習。 Read the technical section, then the disclaimer at the end — the distance between them is the most important thing about this week. The method is detailed; the clinical section is one sentence about privacy. The best available exercise in judging how mature a bio-AI company really is.

  2. Red-teaming OpenAI's agent monitoring — METR & Redwood Research (2026-10-22)

    61 頁，但值得看的是監控「觸發條件」那一節：監控在 agent 把控制權交回時啟動，所以持續動作就能延後被看。任何正在把 agent 接進臨床系統的人，應該把這一節當成採購問卷的第一題。 Sixty-one pages, but the section worth your time is on what triggers monitoring: it fires when the agent hands control back, so continuous action defers it. Anyone wiring agents into a clinical system should make that the first question on their procurement questionnaire.

  3. AI-enabled triage tools may reflect human biases — UT Southwestern / npj Digital Medicine (2026-10-20)

    價值在設計而非結論：配對「初始嚴重度相近、後續走向不同」的病人，然後只改病歷裡的敘述。這個設計可以直接搬到任何一家醫院，用自己的資料驗自己要上線的模型——比引用別人的公平性指標有用得多。 The value is the design, not the finding: pair patients who present alike but diverge afterwards, then change only the narrative in the chart. Any hospital can lift it and run it on its own data against the model it is about to deploy — far more useful than citing someone else's fairness metric.

  4. Conversational diagnostic AI in ambulatory primary care: a prospective feasibility study — The Lancet (2026-10)
