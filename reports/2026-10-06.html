這一週醫療 AI 的錢全部繞過模型、去買「誰在用」——9/22 退伍軍人事務部那張 7 億 7,572 萬美元的五年企業合約是一個天花板、不是一張支票，Abridge 只拿到席位，得靠 170 家醫療中心逐張任務訂單才兌得到錢，同席還有 Knowtex；9/29 Oura 把 5,500 萬股、每股 40–44 美元、上限 22 億美元的 IPO 整個撤下來，把約 150 億美元估值留在門外，手上卻有 570 萬付費訂閱與前一年 9.079 億美元營收；而同一週私募市場改用 ARR 倍數把三家重新定價：OpenEvidence 2.5 億美元／150 億美元、EliseAI 3.5 億美元／40 億美元（ARR 逾 2 億美元）、Heidi 3.4 億美元／9 億美元（ARR 兩年從 100 萬爬到 5,000 萬美元） This week the money in medical AI went around the models entirely and bought distribution instead. The VA's five-year, $775.72M enterprise vehicle awarded on Sep 22 is a ceiling, not a cheque: Abridge won a seat and must still win task orders from 170 medical centres, alongside Knowtex. On Sep 29 Oura pulled a $2.2B IPO — 55 million shares at $40–44 — leaving a roughly $15B valuation outside the door while holding 5.7 million paying subscribers and $907.9M of prior-year revenue. And in the same week private capital repriced three companies on ARR multiples: OpenEvidence at $250M/$15B, EliseAI at $350M/$4B on $200M+ ARR, and Heidi at $340M/$900M on ARR that went from $1M to $50M in two years.

這一週值得記住的不是任何一個新模型，而是錢的流向：它全部繞過模型本身，去買「誰已經在用」。最大的一筆通路事件由政府開出——9 月 22 日退伍軍人事務部把 ambient 臨床 AI 放進一張五年期、上限 7 億 7,572 萬美元的多廠商企業合約，Abridge 拿到席位，但 The Inference 把它拆開後的結論是：那是天花板、不是支票，要靠 170 家醫療中心逐張任務訂單才兌得到錢。出口那一側同時關上：9 月 29 日 Oura 把 5,500 萬股、每股 40–44 美元、上限 22 億美元的 Nasdaq IPO 整個撤下（TechCrunch），而 Rock Health 統計 2026 上半年數位健康 IPO 掛零、74 億美元資金分給 244 筆交易、其中 45% 的資本被 20 筆 megadeal 吃掉（Healthcare Dive）。於是定價場所整個回到私募市場，一週之內三筆：OpenEvidence 2.5 億美元／150 億美元（Forbes）、EliseAI 3.5 億美元／40 億美元（新聞稿）、Heidi 3.4 億美元／9 億美元（Fierce Healthcare），合計 9.4 億美元。而 10 月 5 日 HHS 又透過 ARPA-H 開出四項 AI 臨床試驗計畫（Nextgov/FCW），同樣以「上限」計價。今天這份日報要問的是一個問題：當通路比模型貴，誰真的收到錢了。 What is worth remembering about this week is not any new model but where the money went: around the models entirely, to buy who is already using something. The largest distribution event came from government — on September 22 the VA put ambient clinical AI onto a five-year, multi-vendor enterprise vehicle with a $775.72 million ceiling, and Abridge won a seat. But The Inference takes it apart and concludes it is a ceiling, not a cheque: converting it means winning task orders from 170 medical centres one at a time. The exit closed in the same week: on September 29 Oura pulled its entire Nasdaq IPO of 55 million shares at $40–44, up to $2.2 billion (TechCrunch), while Rock Health counted zero digital health IPOs in the first half of 2026, $7.4 billion spread across 244 deals, and 45% of all capital absorbed by 20 mega-deals (Healthcare Dive). So price discovery moved wholesale into private markets — three rounds in one week: OpenEvidence at $250M/$15B (Forbes), EliseAI at $350M/$4B (release) and Heidi at $340M/$900M (Fierce Healthcare), $940 million in all. Then on October 5 HHS opened four AI clinical-trial programmes through ARPA-H (Nextgov/FCW), also priced as ceilings. The question this issue asks is simple: when distribution costs more than the model, who actually got paid.

7 億 7,572 萬美元的 VA 企業合約，是一個花費上限、不是一張支票 A $775.72M VA enterprise vehicle is a spending ceiling, not a cheque

發生什麼What

退伍軍人事務部在 9 月 22 日把 ambient 臨床 AI 納入一張五年期、多廠商的企業合約，花費上限 7 億 7,572 萬美元，Abridge 取得其中一個席位（Nextgov/FCW、公司新聞稿）。關鍵在結構：The Inference 把這張合約拆開後指出，上限是整張載具的天花板、不是保證收入，Abridge 分到多少未揭露，實際入帳要靠 170 家醫療中心逐一發出任務訂單才兌現。自 2025 年 10 月開始的試點目前已擴到 75 家以上醫療中心，涵蓋初級照護、行為健康、復健與外科次專科，而且所有臨床紀錄仍須醫師核准才會進入退伍軍人病歷。同一批 2025 年 10 月試點廠商之一的 Knowtex 也拿到同一張載具的席位。 On September 22 the Department of Veterans Affairs put ambient clinical AI onto a five-year, multi-vendor enterprise vehicle with a $775.72 million spending ceiling, and Abridge took one of the seats (Nextgov/FCW, company release). The structure is the story: The Inference takes the award apart and points out the figure is the ceiling on the whole vehicle rather than guaranteed revenue, Abridge's own share is undisclosed, and converting it into payments means winning task orders from 170 medical centres one at a time. The pilot that began in October 2025 has now reached more than 75 medical centres across primary care, behavioural health, rehabilitation and surgical specialties, and every note still requires physician approval before it enters a veteran's record. Knowtex, a fellow October 2025 pilot vendor, holds a seat on the same vehicle.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本季最大的單一醫療 AI 通路事件，但它示範的是「政府當買家」的真實形狀：天花板數字會上頭條，實收要看任務訂單。對照組就在隔壁——Oracle 的電子病歷合約上限在 2026 年 8 月被調升到 270 億美元，接著引來國會傳票與年底前的作證（The Inference）。一個上限被當成營收預期來報導，最後是信任成本。Abridge 自述 2026 年將支援逾 1 億次就診對話、覆蓋 300 家以上醫療系統，2025 年 6 月 E 輪估值 53 億美元——比 2024 年 2 月的 8.5 億美元漲了 524%，而這張 VA 合約目前還沒有對應的已實現金額。 This is the quarter's largest single distribution event in medical AI, and it demonstrates the real shape of government-as-buyer: the ceiling makes the headline, the task orders make the revenue. The cautionary case sits next door — Oracle's EHR contract ceiling was raised to $27 billion in August 2026, which drew congressional subpoenas and testimony expected before year-end (The Inference). A ceiling reported as a revenue expectation ends up costing trust. Abridge says it will support over 100 million patient conversations across 300-plus health systems in 2026, and its June 2025 Series E valued it at $5.3 billion — up 524% from $850 million in February 2024 — with no realised figure yet attached to this VA award.

要打折的地方Discount this

7.7572 億美元是整張多廠商載具的上限，不是 Abridge 的合約金額；「逾 1 億次就診對話」與「300 家以上醫療系統」是公司自述，未經第三方稽核。 The $775.72M is the ceiling on a multi-vendor vehicle, not Abridge's contract value; the 100-million-conversation and 300-health-system figures are company-reported and not independently audited.

Oura 把 22 億美元的 IPO 整個撤下來——不是賣不掉，是不想在這個價格賣 Oura pulled a $2.2B IPO outright — not for want of demand, but of price

發生什麼What

智慧戒指廠 Oura 在 9 月 29 日無限期延後 Nasdaq 上市。原本的規模是 5,500 萬股、每股 40–44 美元、最多募 22 億美元，中值對應約 150 億美元估值（TechCrunch、CNBC）。業務面並不弱：付費訂閱 570 萬（6 月為 500 萬），前一年營收 9.079 億美元、2026 年預估成長 90%，會員營收毛利率 89%，6 月底手上現金 3.72 億美元。IPO 收益原本主要用途是支付員工股權 vesting 的稅負，而不是營運。早期投資人 Forerunner Ventures 持股 9.3%，原本可在這次出清約 12 億美元。執行長 Tom Hale 的說法是公司有「選擇時機的餘裕」。 Smart-ring maker Oura postponed its Nasdaq listing indefinitely on September 29. The deal was 55 million shares at $40–44, raising up to $2.2 billion, implying roughly a $15 billion valuation at the midpoint (TechCrunch, CNBC). The business is not the problem: 5.7 million paying subscribers, up from 5 million in June; $907.9 million of prior-year revenue with 90% growth projected for 2026; 89% gross margin on membership revenue; $372 million of cash as of June. The proceeds were earmarked largely to cover employee tax obligations on vesting share grants rather than operations. Early backer Forerunner Ventures, holding 9.3%, was set to sell its entire stake for around $1.2 billion. CEO Tom Hale said the company has "the luxury of choosing our moment".

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是今年健康科技最清楚的一次出口訊號。Rock Health 的 2026 上半年統計裡，數位健康的 IPO 數是零，而 Oura 當時正是最接近送件的那一家（Healthcare Dive）。一家有近 10 億美元營收、89% 毛利、訂閱還在成長的公司都選擇不在這個價格上市，等於把估值發現的場所整個推回私募市場——這正好解釋了同一週另外三筆巨額私募輪。另一層意義更實際：當 IPO 被用來解決員工的 vesting 稅負，延後上市就是把流動性壓力留在公司內部。 This is the clearest exit signal in health tech this year. Rock Health counted zero digital health IPOs in the first half of 2026, with Oura the company closest to filing (Healthcare Dive). A company with nearly a billion dollars of revenue, 89% gross margin and still-growing subscriptions declining to list at this price pushes price discovery back into the private market entirely — which is exactly what the other three mega-rounds of the same week look like. There is a more practical implication too: when an IPO is the mechanism for settling employees' vesting tax bills, postponing it keeps the liquidity pressure inside the company.

OpenEvidence 確認 2.5 億美元新一輪、估值 150 億美元——而產品對美國醫師依然免費 OpenEvidence confirms $250M at a $15B valuation — and the product is still free to US physicians

發生什麼What

創辦人 Daniel Nadler 在 9 月 30 日確認 OpenEvidence 又募了 2.5 億美元，估值 150 億美元（Forbes）；Axios Pro 早在 9 月 25 日就先報出這筆交易由 a16z 參與。這家公司的臨床決策支援工具目前對美國醫師免費，並與 Anthropic 合作把工具推向近 100 個國家、同樣免費，並針對當地臨床指引做調整，重點放在非洲與亞洲。直接競爭者是帶著新 AI 功能的老牌 UpToDate，以及擁有 300 萬名以上醫療用戶的 Doximity。 On September 30 founder Daniel Nadler confirmed OpenEvidence had raised another $250 million at a $15 billion valuation (Forbes); Axios Pro had reported the round, with a16z participating, on September 25. The company's clinical decision support tool is free to US physicians, and a partnership with Anthropic is taking it into nearly 100 countries at no cost, tuned to local clinical guidelines, with the emphasis on Africa and Asia. Its direct competitors are UpToDate, the legacy incumbent now shipping AI features, and Doximity, with more than three million healthcare users.

為什麼重要Why it matters

150 億美元估值配上「對使用者免費」，意味著投資人買的不是訂閱營收，而是醫師的注意力佔有率與日後的變現選擇權——這跟同一週 EliseAI、Heidi 被用 ARR 倍數定價是兩種完全相反的邏輯。值得注意的是 Dealroom 的整理點出這一輪伴隨著往腫瘤藥物開發方向的延伸，如果成立，那條路的收入規模與臨床搜尋完全不是同一個量級，也解釋了為什麼免費分發撐得起這個估值。 A $15 billion valuation attached to a product that is free to its users means investors are not buying subscription revenue; they are buying share of physician attention and the option to monetise later — the exact inverse of the ARR-multiple logic applied to EliseAI and Heidi in the same week. Worth noting: Dealroom's write-up flags the round as accompanying a move toward oncology drug development. If that holds, the revenue scale on that path is in a different order of magnitude from clinical search, and it explains how free distribution can carry this price.

發生什麼What

EliseAI 在 9 月 29 日宣布 3.5 億美元 F 輪，估值 40 億美元，由 Andreessen Horowitz 與 Bessemer Venture Partners 共同領投，Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan、Sapphire Ventures、Navitas Capital 跟投（GlobeNewswire 新聞稿、Fierce Healthcare 募資追蹤）。這家公司把 AI 用在醫療與住房兩個領域的營運自動化，並揭露 6 月 ARR 突破 2 億美元、連續五年營收年年翻倍。 On September 29 EliseAI announced a $350 million Series F at a $4 billion valuation, co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and Bessemer Venture Partners, with Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan, Sapphire Ventures and Navitas Capital participating (GlobeNewswire release, Fierce Healthcare fundraising tracker). The company applies AI to operational automation across both healthcare and housing, and disclosed that ARR passed $200 million in June, having doubled year over year for five consecutive years.

為什麼重要Why it matters

在一週裡有三筆巨額輪的情況下，EliseAI 是唯一同時給出估值與 ARR 的那一家：40 億美元對 2 億美元 ARR，大約 20 倍。這個倍數現在是私募市場的參照點，也是判斷其他沒公布 ARR 的公司（Abridge、OpenEvidence）估值合不合理的唯一外部錨。另一個產業訊號是「跨垂直」：同一套語音與訊息自動化同時賣進醫療與住房，說明這一代公司真正的資產是作業流程的接線，而不是醫療專屬的模型。 Among three mega-rounds in one week, EliseAI is the only company to put a valuation and an ARR on the table at once: $4 billion against $200 million, roughly 20x. That multiple is now the private market's reference point, and the only external anchor for judging the valuations of companies that disclose no ARR at all — Abridge and OpenEvidence among them. The second industry signal is cross-vertical reach: the same voice and messaging automation sells into healthcare and housing alike, which says the real asset in this generation of companies is the wiring into operational workflow, not a healthcare-specific model.

發生什麼What

Heidi 的 3.4 億美元由兩塊組成：1 億美元 C 輪（Blackbird 領投，Phoenix Court、Point72 Private Investments、Headline 跟投）加上 2.4 億美元成長輪（General Catalyst 的 Customer Value Fund 領投），C 輪估值 9 億美元，較 2025 年 10 月的 4.65 億美元翻倍（Fierce Healthcare、Digital Health Wire）。營運數字相當具體：ARR 兩年內從 100 萬美元到 5,000 萬美元；每週 280 萬次病人互動，橫跨 110 種語言、190 個國家；累計支援 1.75 億次就診（B 輪時為 7,300 萬次）、逾 6,700 萬臨床小時，後者自 2025 年 9 月以來成長三倍；2026 年 3 月推出的 Evidence 工具已回答逾 1,000 萬次查詢。部署面包括澳洲、紐西蘭、英國（NHS England Midlands 獨家供應商）與美國（Beth Israel Lahey Health 全系統導入），並與 R1 RCM 整合。 Heidi's $340 million came in two parts: a $100 million Series C led by Blackbird with Phoenix Court, Point72 Private Investments and Headline, plus a $240 million growth investment led by General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund. The Series C valued the company at $900 million, double the $465 million of October 2025 (Fierce Healthcare, Digital Health Wire). The operating numbers are unusually specific: ARR from $1 million to $50 million in two years; 2.8 million patient interactions a week across 110 languages and 190 countries; 175 million patient visits supported, up from 73 million at the Series B, and over 67 million clinical hours, tripled since September 2025; and more than 10 million evidence queries answered since the Evidence tool launched in March 2026. Deployments span Australia, New Zealand, the UK — where it is sole supplier to NHS England Midlands — and the US, with a system-wide rollout at Beth Israel Lahey Health, plus an R1 RCM integration.

為什麼重要Why it matters

Heidi 是 ambient 這一層裡最清楚的「自下而上」樣本：不靠一張聯邦大單，靠 190 個國家的臨床醫師個別採用再往上長成系統級合約與 NHS 獨家。對照 Abridge 的路徑（美國大型醫療系統＋聯邦載具、高單價、長銷售週期、ARR 不公開），這兩條路在同一週各拿到一筆錢，而只有其中一條把 ARR 攤在桌上。值得盯的是「不只做紀錄」這個轉向：從 scribe 走向 agentic 與臨床決策支援，就直接撞進 OpenEvidence 的地盤；加上穿戴裝置 Remote，等於同時在三條線上開戰。 Heidi is the clearest bottom-up case in the ambient layer: no single federal award, but individual clinician adoption across 190 countries growing upward into system-level contracts and NHS exclusivity. Set against Abridge's path — large US health systems plus a federal vehicle, high contract value, long sales cycles, undisclosed ARR — the two routes each took money in the same week, and only one of them put its ARR on the table. The move worth watching is "beyond documentation": going from scribe to agentic work and clinical decision support walks straight into OpenEvidence's territory, and adding the Remote wearable means fighting on three fronts at once.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本期主線的第二個證據：產業最大的新增買家是政府，而政府的錢同樣以「上限」形式發出。HHS 自己給的理由是藥物開發通常要十年以上、花掉最多 20 億美元、失敗率超過九成——換句話說，這些標案真正在賣的是試驗週期，而不是模型精度。值得注意的是集中度：同一批公告裡，一家 Evidence Health 就主導兩項、佔上限金額的九成以上。這跟私募端「megadeal 吃掉大半資本」是同一種形狀。 This is the second piece of evidence for the week's through-line: the industry's largest new buyer is government, and government money also arrives as a ceiling. HHS's own framing is that drug development typically takes more than a decade, costs up to $2 billion and fails over 90% of the time — meaning what these awards are really buying is trial cycle time, not model accuracy. The concentration is notable: in one batch of announcements a single company, Evidence Health, leads two of the programmes and over 90% of the committed ceiling. That is the same shape as mega-deals absorbing most of the private capital.

04 — Taiwan Angle

同一天台灣也有一條產業消息，而它的估值邏輯跟美國那三筆完全不一樣 Taiwan had an industry item the same day, and its valuation logic is nothing like those three US rounds

(1) 10 月 5 日的智捷，走的是「演算法授權」而不是 ARR 訂閱。由宣明智出任董事長的智捷醫電，核心是 Anatomy Cloud 技術：把傳統 CT、MRI 的 2D 灰階影像在 3 到 5 分鐘內轉成高解析彩色 3D 模型，可辨識 260 種以上器官與組織，並支援瀏覽器、行動裝置與混合實境頭戴顯示器檢視。目前在衛福部資料中心測試，目標 2028 年導入全台 29 家醫學中心；公司並已完成對日本醫療影像業者 Holoeyes 的併購，接上其 100 家以上日本醫院的網絡，收入方式規劃為演算法授權、設備租賃與手術機器人共同開發（新頭殼 Newtalk）。對照本期美國那三筆——用 ARR 倍數或注意力佔有率定價——台灣這條走的是硬體＋授權，現金流結構完全不同，所以不能拿 20 倍 ARR 的框架去看它。 (1) Chihjet, on October 5, is selling algorithm licences rather than ARR. Chihjet Medical Technology, chaired by Sun Jixun, is built on its Anatomy Cloud technology: converting conventional 2D greyscale CT and MRI images into high-definition colour 3D models in three to five minutes, identifying over 260 organs and tissues, viewable in a browser, on mobile, or through a mixed-reality headset. It is in testing at the Ministry of Health and Welfare's data centre, aiming to reach all 29 of Taiwan's medical centres by 2028. The company has also completed an acquisition of the Japanese medical imaging firm Holoeyes, plugging into a network of more than 100 Japanese hospitals, with revenue planned through algorithm licensing, equipment leasing and co-developed surgical robotics (Newtalk). Against this week's three US rounds — priced on ARR multiples or share of attention — Taiwan's route is hardware plus licensing, a completely different cash-flow structure, so a 20x-ARR frame does not read it correctly.

(2) VA 那張合約的結構，台灣廠商應該逐字看一遍。一張五年期、多廠商、以「花費上限」計價、再靠各院逐一發任務訂單兌現的載具（The Inference），在形式上跟衛福部與各醫學中心的標案邏輯很接近：得標只是拿到資格，收入要看各院實際採購。智捷說的「2028 年導入 29 家醫學中心」就是同一種句型——它描述的是資格與目標，不是已簽的合約金額。這一點在對外溝通與對內估算時最好分開記帳。 (2) The structure of the VA award is worth reading word by word in Taiwan. A five-year, multi-vendor vehicle priced as a spending ceiling and converted only through task orders issued hospital by hospital (The Inference) closely resembles how tenders work between the Ministry of Health and Welfare and individual medical centres: winning a place grants eligibility, while revenue depends on what each hospital actually buys. Chihjet's "29 medical centres by 2028" is the same grammatical form — it describes eligibility and intent, not contracted value.
