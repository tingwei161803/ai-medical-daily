The NIM deployment lists seven tasks — generation, motif scaffolding, enzyme design, small-molecule binder design, antibody design, partial diffusion and inverse folding — and supports reward-guided beam search during diffusion. Weights are dated 10/19, diffusers support merged 10/20, and the NIM listing went up on build.nvidia.com and NGC the same day (unite.ai’s technical write-up).

為什麼重要Why it matters

重點在授權與尺寸的組合。BSD 3-Clause 是最寬鬆的一檔，NIM 卡片直接標註可商用；1.74 GB 的權重意味著一張消費級或單張資料中心卡就跑得動，不需要叢集。這把「設計一個 binder」的邊際成本壓到幾乎是電費，也讓它能被塞進 agent 的工具箱——同一份新聞稿裡，Genedata 的 AI co-scientist 就是用 RFD4-Proteina 在 Danaher 的自主實驗室裡協調設計與測試，而模型同時上架 Amazon Bio Discovery、Anthropic 的 Claude Science、Benchling、Databricks、Google Cloud、Hugging Face、Nebius、OpenAI 與 Tamarind Bio（GlobeNewswire 全文）。一個模型同時出現在三家互為競爭對手的 AI 實驗室的科學產品裡，這件事本身比 benchmark 更值得記下來。 The combination of licence and size is the point. BSD 3-Clause is about as permissive as it gets, and the NIM card marks the model ready for commercial use; 1.74 GB of weights means one data-centre card — or a good consumer one — rather than a cluster. That pushes the marginal cost of designing a binder down towards the electricity bill, and it makes the model small enough to sit in an agent’s toolbox. In the same release, Genedata’s AI co-scientist uses RFD4-Proteina to coordinate design and testing inside Danaher’s autonomous lab, while the model ships simultaneously through Amazon Bio Discovery, Anthropic’s Claude Science, Benchling, Databricks, Google Cloud, Hugging Face, Nebius, OpenAI and Tamarind Bio (full GlobeNewswire text). One model landing inside the science products of three rival AI labs at once is the more quotable fact here.

Claude 自主跑 64 小時設計抗蠍毒結合蛋白：318,484 個設計進去，17 個真的黏住——而 Anthropic 自己先把話說死 Claude runs 64 autonomous hours designing scorpion-antivenom binders: 318,484 designs in, 17 that actually bind — and Anthropic closes the door on overclaiming first

發生什麼What

10/20 Anthropic Science 團隊公布，用一個內部研究版 Claude 在 Claude Science 環境裡設計對抗蠍毒神經毒素的 minibinder（Anthropic 研究報告）。流程：初始 prompt 之後 Claude 自主運作約 24 小時，之後只收到四次追加指示，全程約 64 小時、約 10,000 GPU 小時；Claude 自己挑毒素結構與 hotspot、自訂品質門檻、選工具、寫報告。標的是四個地區的五種神經毒素：Cn2（墨西哥）、Ts1（巴西）、AaH I 與 AaH II（北非）、LqhIII（中東）。計算量：12 個設計工具、318,484 個設計、co-fold 篩過 553,052 組 design–toxin 配對，Claude 自選 180 個、Anthropic 人工補 31 個，共 211 個送 Adaptyv Bio（無細胞表現）與 Twist Bioscience（哺乳類細胞、Fc 融合）以 SPR 實測。判定門檻訂得很硬：兩間實驗室、每次重複都要是 hit，KD ≤ 1 µM，且對 off-target 蛋白 CLEC12A 無訊號。結果 17/211 過關（Cn2 七個、AaH II 四個、AaH I 四個、LqhIII 兩個），單毒素組 11/60（18%），而所有多毒素設計沒有一個對一種以上毒素達標。最強的 Cn2-B1 與 Cn2-B2 達個位數 nM，競爭實驗顯示其結合位與已知可在小鼠中中和 Cn2 的抗體 LR 重疊或相鄰。 On 10/20 Anthropic’s Science team described using an internal research Claude, inside Claude Science, to design minibinders against scorpion neurotoxins (Anthropic’s research report). The run: Claude worked autonomously for about 24 hours after the initial prompt, then took four follow-ups, for roughly 64 hours and about 10,000 GPU hours in all, choosing the toxin structures and hotspots, setting its own quality thresholds, picking the tools and drafting the report. Targets were five neurotoxins from four regions: Cn2 (Mexico), Ts1 (Brazil), AaH I and AaH II (North Africa), LqhIII (Middle East). The compute: 12 design tools, 318,484 designs, 553,052 design–toxin pairs co-folded and screened, 180 selected by Claude plus 31 added by staff for 211 total, sent to Adaptyv Bio (cell-free expression) and Twist Bioscience (mammalian cells, Fc fusions) and measured by SPR. The bar was set hard: a hit at both labs in every replicate, KD at or below 1 µM, and no signal against the off-target protein CLEC12A. 17 of 211 cleared it — seven against Cn2, four AaH II, four AaH I, two LqhIII — and 11 of 60 (18%) in the single-toxin arm, while no multi-toxin design met the bar for more than one toxin. The best two, Cn2-B1 and Cn2-B2, bound at single-digit nanomolar, and competition experiments put their site on or near that of antibody LR, reported to neutralise Cn2 in mice.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是目前公開紀錄裡，把「agent 自主時數」與「濕實驗驗證」綁在同一份報告裡最完整的一次。18% 的單毒素命中率，Anthropic 自己說與一般蛋白質設計活動的水準相當——意思是 agent 沒有變魔術，但它在沒有人逐步指導的情況下，把一個通常需要團隊數週規劃的設計活動跑完了。更關鍵的是失敗的部分也被完整報出來：多毒素泛特異性設計全軍覆沒，這恰好是抗蛇毒／抗蠍毒最需要、也最難的那一塊。全部 211 個設計、預測結構、分數與實驗數據已公開在 Hugging Face。背景尺度：蠍螫每年影響至少 120 萬人、造成逾 3,250 人死亡，多數是醫療可近性不足地區的兒童。 This is the most complete public account yet of autonomous agent-hours tied to wet-lab verification in one report. The 18% single-toxin hit rate is, by Anthropic’s own framing, in line with ordinary protein-design campaigns — meaning the agent performed no magic, but it did run to completion a campaign that normally takes a team weeks to plan, without step-by-step human direction. More usefully, the failures are reported in full: the pan-specific multi-toxin designs failed across the board, and that is precisely the hard, valuable part of antivenom work. All 211 designs, predicted structures, scores and experimental data are public on Hugging Face. For scale: scorpion stings affect at least 1.2 million people a year and cause more than 3,250 deaths, mostly children with limited access to care.

Anthropic 自己列了限制，而且列得比多數廠商誠實：只測重組毒素，未在細胞或動物測試中和，也未對毒液純化的毒素測試；公司明文表示「未確認中和，不宣稱做出抗蛇毒血清」。下一步需要毒液結合、patch-clamp 電生理與小鼠實驗。另外，211 個裡有 31 個是 Anthropic 員工補的，「完全自主」要打一點折；而 prompt 的改寫由投資研究公司 Citrini Research 協助，這層關係報告有揭露但值得讀者自行評估。 Anthropic lists its own limitations, and more honestly than most vendors: only recombinant toxins were tested, neutralisation was not tested in cells or animals, and nothing was tested against venom-purified toxin. The company states plainly that it has not confirmed neutralisation and does not claim to have produced an antivenom. Next steps would be venom binding, patch-clamp electrophysiology and mouse studies. Separately, 31 of the 211 designs were added by Anthropic staff, so “fully autonomous” needs a small discount, and the prompt was adapted with Citrini Research, an investment research firm — disclosed in the report, but worth the reader’s own weighing.

數千個 agent、32 分鐘、800 萬顆細胞：OpenAI 第一次給出「分散式 vs. 單一 agent」的對照數字 Thousands of agents, 32 minutes, 8 million cells: OpenAI finally puts a control arm next to “more agents”

發生什麼What

10/21 OpenAI 與 PerturbAI、Scripps Research 公布一組他們稱為「distributed biological reasoning」的實驗：數千個由 GPT-Rosalind 驅動的 agent 平行讀一份小鼠全腦 CRISPR 圖譜（約 800 萬顆細胞），每個 agent 負責一個基因擾動，分析細胞反應、檢索文獻、提出機制解釋，最後彙整成一份連結基因、細胞效應、文獻與候選機制的「reasoning atlas」。整段跑完 32 分鐘（OpenAI 公告）。對照組是關鍵：在模型與資源固定的條件下，單一 agent 在最大工作量時只引用 11 篇文獻，分散式版本引用超過 700 篇；盲測人類專家在兩種報告之間，75% 偏好分散式產出。產出的兩條具體假說：小腦中 Tsc2 被擾動改變了數十個神經傳導相關基因的活性、而 Tsc1 幾乎沒變（兩者同屬 mTOR 調控，可能與結節性硬化症的學習與行為困難有關）；以及 Dctn1 擾動後膽固醇合成基因表現上升，被連到膽固醇運輸障礙的既有研究，指向 Perry 症候群的神經退化機制。atlas 公開在 GitHub、資料集在 Hugging Face、論文在 bioRxiv。 On 10/21 OpenAI, PerturbAI and Scripps Research described what they call “distributed biological reasoning”: thousands of agents powered by GPT-Rosalind, running in parallel over a mouse whole-brain CRISPR atlas of roughly 8 million cells, each agent taking one gene perturbation — analysing the cellular response, searching the literature, proposing explanations — and the outputs assembled into a “reasoning atlas” linking genes, cellular effects, publications and candidate mechanisms. The whole run took 32 minutes (OpenAI’s announcement). The control arm is the interesting part: with model and resources held fixed, a single agent cited only 11 references at the largest workload, while the distributed agents cited more than 700, and in blinded comparison human experts preferred the distributed reports 75% of the time. Two concrete hypotheses came out: in the cerebellum, disrupting Tsc2 shifted the activity of dozens of neuronal-communication genes while disrupting Tsc1 barely moved them (both regulate mTOR, with possible bearing on the learning and behavioural difficulties of tuberous sclerosis); and disrupting Dctn1 raised expression of cholesterol-synthesis genes, which the agents tied to existing work on cholesterol transport defects and thus to neurodegeneration in Perry syndrome. The atlas is on GitHub, the dataset on Hugging Face, the paper on bioRxiv.

為什麼重要Why it matters

「多開幾個 agent 會不會更好」過去一直是信仰問題，因為大家只報最終效果、不報單 agent 基線。11 篇 vs. 700+ 篇是這一年來少見的乾淨對照：固定模型、固定資源，差異只來自把工作切開平行跑。它同時也點出分散式的真正代價——700 篇引用意味著 700 條需要人去抽查的推論鏈，而 75% 的專家偏好率反過來說，有 25% 的情況下專家寧可要單 agent 那份。對醫療 AI 的意義是方法論上的：如果 agent 數量能這樣換到文獻覆蓋度，那麼「臨床決策支援能不能靠多 agent 補齊 guideline 覆蓋」就從哲學問題變成可設計的實驗。 Whether more agents actually helps has been an article of faith, because papers report the final result and skip the single-agent baseline. Eleven references against 700+ is the cleanest control the year has produced: same model, same resources, the only difference being that the work was cut up and run in parallel. It also shows what distribution costs — 700 citations means 700 reasoning chains someone has to spot-check, and the 75% preference rate says that a quarter of the time the experts wanted the single-agent report instead. The methodological consequence for medical AI is direct: if agent count buys literature coverage this cleanly, then “can multi-agent setups close guideline coverage in clinical decision support” stops being a philosophical question and becomes a designable experiment.

要打折的地方Discount this

「數千個」沒有確切數字；GPT-Rosalind 的架構與訓練方式公告沒有描述。「人類團隊要做數月到一年」是團隊自估，不是量測。新穎性與價值評估「與盲測專家判斷一致」只有定性描述，沒有給一致性統計量。單 agent 對照只跑了一組固定設定，泛化性未建立。兩條假說都還沒有實驗驗證，公告自己也說科學家仍是發現的中心。這是生物研究，不是臨床應用。 “Thousands” is never given a number, and the announcement does not describe GPT-Rosalind’s architecture or training. “Months to a year for a human team” is the team’s own estimate, not a measurement. The claim that novelty and value assessments “aligned” with blinded expert judgement is qualitative, with no agreement statistic. The single-agent comparison ran at one fixed setup, so generality is not established. Neither hypothesis has been experimentally validated, and the announcement itself says scientists remain central to discovery. This is biomedical research, not clinical use.

一句話收束：本週的競爭結構是一個分層的三明治。最底下 NVIDIA 用 BSD 3-Clause 把模型層壓成公共財、綁住硬體；中間 Anthropic 與 OpenAI 搶的是 agent 的「深度」（64 小時自主）與「寬度」（數千個並行），兩家同時都是 NVIDIA 模型的下游通路；最上面 Epic 用 MCP 與 Showroom 收費閘門把醫院端的入口握在手裡，而 Abridge 這類應用層廠商得在 Epic 的規則下跟 Epic 自家的 Art 競爭。真正沒人站的位置是「濕實驗驗證」——三份技術報告的限制欄全部指向同一個缺口，而那恰好是目前最貴、最慢、也最難用算力換的一段。 In one sentence: this week’s competitive structure is a layered sandwich. At the bottom NVIDIA uses BSD 3-Clause to commoditise the model layer and lock in the hardware. In the middle Anthropic and OpenAI compete on the agent’s depth (64 autonomous hours) and its width (thousands in parallel), while both are simultaneously downstream distribution channels for NVIDIA’s model. At the top Epic holds the hospital entrance with MCP and a paid Showroom, leaving application-layer vendors like Abridge to compete against Epic’s own Art under Epic’s rules. The position nobody occupies is wet-lab verification — the limitations section of all three technical reports points at the same gap, and it is the slowest, most expensive stretch, and the one least convertible into compute.

04 — Taiwan Angle

開放權重、MCP 與「用現成資料跑 agent」這三件事，對台灣各有不同的門檻高度 Open weights, MCP, and running agents over data you already hold — three different heights of barrier for Taiwan

(1) RFD4-Proteina 的 1.74 GB 把門檻降到國內實驗室可承受的區間。BSD 3-Clause 加上單張資料中心卡可跑，意味著台灣的學研單位與生技公司第一次能在不簽授權、不租叢集的前提下把蛋白質設計納入自己的流程。真正的瓶頸會立刻從算力移到濕實驗驗證產能——Anthropic 那份報告把 211 個設計送去 Adaptyv Bio 與 Twist 做 SPR 雙重複驗，這種「兩間獨立實驗室、每次重複都要命中」的驗證規格，在台灣目前並沒有對應的商業化服務可以直接買到。能補上這一段的單位，會比買更多卡的單位更有價值。 (1) RFD4-Proteina’s 1.74 GB brings the barrier inside what a Taiwanese lab can absorb. BSD 3-Clause plus a single data-centre card means academic groups and biotechs here can fold protein design into their own pipelines for the first time without a licence negotiation or a cluster rental. The bottleneck moves immediately from compute to wet-lab verification capacity — Anthropic sent its 211 designs to Adaptyv Bio and Twist for duplicate SPR, and that specification, two independent labs with a hit in every replicate, has no directly purchasable commercial equivalent in Taiwan today. Whoever closes that gap will matter more than whoever buys more cards.

(2) Epic 選 MCP，對台灣醫院資訊廠商是一個明確的技術時程訊號。衛福部今年主推的 FHIR Box 跨院病歷互通解的是資料交換層，而 Epic 這一步解的是上面那一層：讓 agent 在別的系統裡做事。兩者不衝突，但順序很重要——如果國內只把力氣放在 FHIR 而沒有同步規劃 agent 呼叫工具的權限與稽核模型，等到 2027 年 3 月 Epic Showroom 開放、國際廠商的 Connector 生態成形時，台灣的 HIS 會發現自己有資料標準卻沒有 agent 介面標準。Epic 那套「醫院自行檢視 Connector 能讀什麼、能改什麼」的治理設計，是現成可以直接參考的規格。 (2) Epic choosing MCP is a clear timing signal for Taiwan’s hospital IT vendors. The FHIR Box cross-hospital record exchange the Ministry of Health and Welfare has pushed this year solves the data-exchange layer; Epic’s move solves the layer above it: letting an agent act inside another system. The two do not conflict, but the order matters. If the effort here goes into FHIR without a parallel plan for the permission and audit model around agent tool calls, then by March 2027, when Epic Showroom opens and an international Connector ecosystem forms, Taiwanese HIS vendors will find they have a data standard and no agent-interface standard. Epic’s governance design — each hospital reviewing what a Connector may read and may change — is an off-the-shelf specification worth copying.

(3) OpenAI 那組實驗示範的是「已有資料 + 大量 agent」，這條路台灣的門檻最低。800 萬細胞的圖譜是既有資料，agent 只是把人力讀不完的部分平行讀完。台灣手上最接近這個形狀的資產是健保資料庫與各醫學中心的影像與病理庫。10/5 衛福部的「2026 國產 AI 3D 醫療影像暨元宇宙應用大會」上，台大醫院院長余忠仁表示 2028 年要完成全院自動化 AI 3D 影像轉譯架構，部長石崇良同場提到 FHIR Box 與次世代 3D PACS，智捷醫電的 Anatomy Cloud 平台約 5 分鐘把 2D 掃描轉成 3D 解剖模型、並捐贈 10 台工作站供部署為示範設備。這些都是基礎建設，但要把它變成「數千 agent 在上面跑」，缺的不是模型而是可程式化存取的資料介面與治理授權——也就是第 (2) 點講的那一層。 (3) OpenAI’s run demonstrates “data you already have plus a lot of agents”, and that is the lowest barrier of the three for Taiwan. The 8-million-cell atlas was existing data; the agents simply read in parallel what people could not finish reading. Taiwan’s closest assets of that shape are the National Health Insurance database and the imaging and pathology archives of its medical centres. At the ministry’s 2026 Domestic AI, 3D Medical Imaging and Metaverse Application Conference on 10/5, NTUH president Yu Chung-jen said the hospital aims to complete a hospital-wide automated AI 3D imaging translation framework by 2028, Minister Shih Chung-liang discussed FHIR Box and next-generation 3D PACS, and Zhijie Medical’s Anatomy Cloud platform turns 2D scans into 3D anatomical models in about five minutes, with ten donated workstations to be deployed as demonstration equipment. All of that is infrastructure. Turning it into something thousands of agents can run over requires not a model but programmatically accessible data interfaces and the governance to authorise them — which is to say, point (2).
