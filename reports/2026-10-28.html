2026 年 10 月 28 日（三）· 涵蓋 10/21–10/28 · 台北時間Wednesday, October 28, 2026 · Covering Oct 21–28 · Taipei time

這一週的監理新聞有一個共同形狀：透明度的義務正在從法規移到合約裡。10 月 26 日，HHS 定案 HTI-5，把預測型決策支援的 31 項「來源屬性」、開發者維護義務與漂移監測稽核一次從 EHR 認證裡移除，60 條基準刪 14 改 7，官方估淨現值逾 15.5 億美元；ONC 的理由是演算法安全該由 FDA 與民權法規（45 CFR §92.210）管，而 STAT 指出多處變更是在評論者反對下做成的。隔天 10 月 27 日，EFF 公布訴訟取得的第二批 WISeR 檔案逾 800 頁，顯示 CMS 在這個 AI 事前審查示範上線的頭三個月，就對六家供應商中至少四家發出資訊請求或矯正行動計畫，Virtix、Zyter 與 Innovaccer 各有被點名的具體缺失。往外看，歐盟的 Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 已把醫材所在的附件一高風險義務推到 2028/8/2，英國則在 10 月 21 日確認無限期承認 CE 標誌、10 月 6 日全盤接受 44 項 AI 監理建議。同一個問題，四個答案。 This week's regulatory news has one shape: the duty of transparency is moving out of regulation and into contracts. On 26 October HHS finalized HTI-5, removing the 31 source attributes for predictive decision support, the developer maintenance obligation and the drift-monitoring audit from EHR certification in one pass, cutting 14 of 60 criteria and revising 7, for a stated net present value above $1.55 billion. ONC's reasoning is that algorithmic safety belongs to FDA and to civil-rights rules at 45 CFR §92.210 — though STAT reports many of the changes were made over commenters' objections. The next day, 27 October, EFF published a second tranche of litigation-obtained WISeR records, more than 800 pages, showing that in the first three months of that AI prior-authorization demonstration CMS sent requests for information or corrective action plans to at least four of its six vendors, Virtix, Zyter and Innovaccer each named for specific failures. Further out, the EU's Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 has already pushed Annex I high-risk obligations, where medical devices sit, to 2 Aug 2028, while the UK confirmed indefinite CE-mark recognition on 21 October and accepted all 44 of its AI regulation recommendations on 6 October. One question, four answers.

七件事，一條主線：寫在法規裡的透明度正在退場，而取代它的東西還沒長出來。 Seven items, one through-line: transparency written into regulation is retreating, and what replaces it has not grown in yet.
最重要Lead ONC / ASTP HTI-5 10/26

HTI-5 定案：預測型 AI 的 31 項來源屬性與漂移稽核，一起退出 EHR 認證 HTI-5 is final: all 31 source attributes for predictive AI, and drift-monitoring audits with them, leave EHR certification

發生什麼What

HHS 於 10 月 26 日（週一）公告 HTI-5 最終規則（Health Data, Technology, and Interoperability: ONC Deregulatory Actions to Unleash Prosperity），HHS 新聞稿。ONC 在 60 條認證基準中刪除 14 條、實質修改 7 條，39 條維持不變，Healthcare Innovation。對 AI 影響最直接的是 §170.315(b)(11) 決策支援介入（DSI）：自 2025 年 1 月 1 日起，通過認證的健康資訊系統必須向臨床端提供預測型工具的 31 項「來源屬性」與證據型工具的 13 項，healthsystemCIO；HTI-5 把預測型的揭露義務整個移除，連同 §170.402(b)(4) 的開發者維護義務與 §170.315(b)(11)(vi) 的介入風險管理（含漂移監測稽核）一併廢止，並把會觸發預測型 DSI 的資料類別收窄到臨床病歷與評估暨治療計畫兩項。官方估計淨現值逾 15.5 億美元，換算年化約 1.081 億美元（7% 折現、2027 年起算），AHA 同日摘要。 HHS released the HTI-5 final rule — Health Data, Technology, and Interoperability: ONC Deregulatory Actions to Unleash Prosperity — on Monday 26 October, per the HHS press release. ONC removed 14 of 60 certification criteria, substantially revised 7 and left 39 untouched, Healthcare Innovation reports. The sharpest edge for AI sits in §170.315(b)(11), decision support interventions: since 1 January 2025 certified health IT has had to surface 31 source attributes for predictive tools and 13 for evidence-based ones, healthsystemCIO notes. HTI-5 deletes the predictive disclosure duty outright, and with it the developer maintenance obligation at §170.402(b)(4) and the intervention risk management requirements at §170.315(b)(11)(vi), drift-monitoring audits included; the data classes that trigger a predictive DSI narrow to clinical notes and the assessment and plan of treatment. The stated net present value is more than $1.55 billion, about $108.1 million annualized at a 7% discount rate from 2027, and the AHA summarized it the same day.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是美國第一次把「模型卡要不要給臨床看」從合規問題降級成商業問題。ONC 的理由是演算法安全監督屬於 FDA 與民權法規（45 CFR §92.210）的管轄，而非認證計畫；實務後果是，想知道一個院內預測模型的訓練資料、驗證方式與效能，CMIO 與 chief AI officer 從今以後得直接向廠商要，沒有法規作為後盾。STAT 報導，多處變更是在評論者反對下做成的——反對意見正是擔心削弱敏感健康資訊的安全與隱私，以及倚賴 AI 的產品之安全性，而 ONC 只說「未來規則制定可能恢復部分來源屬性要求」。值得注意的是被刪掉的不只是揭露——漂移監測稽核是整份認證裡唯一一條要求開發者持續檢查模型是否退化的條文。 This is the first time the United States has demoted "must the model card reach the clinician" from a compliance question to a commercial one. ONC's reasoning is that algorithmic safety oversight sits with FDA and with civil-rights rules at 45 CFR §92.210 rather than with the certification program. The practical consequence is that a CMIO or chief AI officer who wants a predictive model's training data, validation method and performance must now ask the vendor, with nothing behind the request. STAT reports that many of the changes were made over commenters' objections, those objections being precisely that they would weaken the security and privacy of sensitive health information and the safety of AI-reliant products, and ONC says only that it "may consider restoring some source-attribute requirements in future rulemaking." Note what went besides the disclosure: the drift-monitoring audit was the one clause in the whole program obliging a developer to keep checking whether a model had decayed.

要打折的地方Discount this

15.5 億美元是 HHS 自估的法規成本節省淨現值，只計入開發者端的合規成本，未量化醫院端的直接節省或新增成本。此外 HTI-5 裡的資訊阻斷（information blocking）提案並未定案，仍在審酌中；生效日分層：多數條文於 10 月 28 日聯邦公報刊登後 60 天生效，其餘 2027 年 1 月 1 日，過渡照護（transitions of care）相關修正延到 2030 年 1 月 1 日。 The $1.55 billion is HHS's own estimate of net regulatory-cost savings, counting developer-side compliance only — it does not quantify direct savings or new costs on the hospital side. The information-blocking proposals in HTI-5 were not finalized and remain under consideration. Effective dates are layered: most provisions take effect 60 days after Federal Register publication, scheduled for 28 October; the rest on 1 January 2027; and the transitions-of-care revision on 1 January 2030.

調查Investigation CMS / CMMI WISeR 10/27

EFF 再取得逾 800 頁：WISeR 上線頭三個月，六家供應商有四家收到 CMS 的 RFI 或矯正計畫要求 EFF obtains 800-plus more pages: in WISeR's first three months CMS sent RFIs or corrective action plans to four of six vendors

發生什麼What

電子前哨基金會（EFF）於 10 月 27 日公布其訴訟取得的第二批 WISeR（Wasteful and Inappropriate Service Reduction）檔案，逾 800 頁；首批約 1,000 頁已於 9 月 8 日公布。新檔案包含 CMS 對六家供應商中四家發出的資訊請求（RFI）或矯正行動計畫（CAP），以及 66 家申請公司的申請書與評估、實施、監測的任務書與合約。具體而言：Virtix 的 RFI 日期為 2026 年 3 月 17 日，被指週轉時間超出準則、部分否准看來不正確、客服不足；Zyter 的 RFI 日期為 2 月 27 日，被指裁決通知信遲發、升級流程可能不足，該公司使用 Palantir 的軟體基礎設施；Innovaccer 收到兩份 CAP 要求與一紙日期為 2 月 20 日的不合規績效通知，原因是未能交付功能完整的醫事人員入口網站，Innovaccer 表示數月前就已警告 CMS 可能趕不上上線。 The Electronic Frontier Foundation published a second tranche of litigation-obtained WISeR (Wasteful and Inappropriate Service Reduction) records on 27 October, more than 800 pages; the first tranche, roughly 1,000 pages, came out on 8 September. The new material includes requests for information or corrective action plans CMS sent to four of the model's six vendors, applications from the 66 companies that applied, and the task orders and contracts for evaluation, implementation and monitoring. In detail: Virtix's RFI is dated 17 March 2026 and cites turnaround times beyond guidelines, denials that appear inaccurate and inadequate customer service. Zyter's, dated 27 February, cites late determination letters and possibly inadequate escalation processes; the company runs on Palantir's software infrastructure. Innovaccer received two CAP requests and a performance notice of non-compliance dated 20 February for failing to deliver a fully functional provider portal, and says it warned CMS months earlier that it might not be ready for launch.

為什麼重要Why it matters

WISeR 是傳統 Medicare 第一次把事前審查交給 AI 輔助流程，2026 年 1 月 15 日於亞利桑那、紐澤西、俄亥俄、奧克拉荷馬、德州、華盛頓六州啟動。這批檔案的意義不在單一供應商出包，而在四家同時出包——指向的是上線時程，不是個別廠商的能力。CMS 自承時程「極度壓縮」：宣布後六個月即上線，期間醫界要求延後未果。EFF 另指出，CMS 的常見問答說否准必須經人類臨床醫師複核，但複核時間、推翻權限等細節並未公開；該文引用的「平均每案 1.2 秒」案例來自其他體系而非 WISeR，是類比而非本案數據。 WISeR is the first time traditional Medicare has routed prior authorization through an AI-assisted process; it launched on 15 January 2026 in Arizona, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Texas and Washington. What matters here is not that one vendor stumbled but that four did at once, which points at the launch timetable rather than at any single firm's competence. CMS itself acknowledged an "extremely condensed timeline": six months from announcement to go-live, over providers' objections. EFF also notes that CMS's FAQ says a non-affirmation requires review by a human clinician, while the details of that review — time spent, authority to override — are not public. The "1.2 seconds per case" figure EFF cites comes from a different system, offered as analogy, not as WISeR data.

脈絡Context

政治面上，GAO 已於 5 月 12 日認定 WISeR 屬行政程序法下的「規則」；民主黨提出的國會審查法（CRA）否決決議於 7 月 16 日在參議院以黨派票數受阻，Fierce Healthcare 報導；眾議院撥款委員會則於 6 月 9–10 日以口頭表決通過修正案，禁止 CMS 動用 2027 財年經費於 WISeR 或類似模式，但該撥款法案尚未在任一院通過（Manatt 健康 AI 政策追蹤）。 Politically, GAO determined on 12 May that WISeR is a "rule" under the Administrative Procedure Act; the Democrats' Congressional Review Act resolution of disapproval was blocked along party lines in the Senate on 16 July, as Fierce Healthcare reported; and the House Appropriations Committee adopted an amendment by voice vote on 9–10 June barring CMS from spending FY 2027 funds on WISeR or similar models, though that bill has passed neither chamber (per Manatt's health AI policy tracker).

歐盟EU European Commission Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 7/24

數位總括法已生效：醫材所在的附件一高風險義務推到 2028/8/2，附件三推到 2027/12/2 The Digital Omnibus is in force: Annex I high-risk duties, where medical devices sit, move to 2 Aug 2028 and Annex III to 2 Dec 2027

發生什麼What

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744（AI 數位總括法）修正 AI Act（Regulation (EU) 2024/1689），2026 年 7 月 24 日刊登於歐盟公報、7 月 27 日生效。新期程為：附件三獨立系統（含健康與人壽保險定價）自原訂 2026/8/2 延至 2027/12/2（第 113(c)(i) 條）；附件一受規管產品（AI 作為安全組件，醫療器材即屬此類）自 2027/8/2 延至 2028/8/2（第 113(c)(ii) 條）；公部門使用中的系統則為 2030/8/2（第 111(2) 條）。已依附件一第 A 節部門法規指定的驗證機構，須於 2028/1/28 前申請 AI Act 下的指定（第 43(3) 條）。罰則維持不變：禁止行為最高 3,500 萬歐元或全球營業額 7%，其他多數義務最高 1,500 萬歐元或 3%。 Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the Digital Omnibus on AI, amends the AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689); it was published in the Official Journal on 24 July 2026 and entered into force on 27 July. The new schedule: Annex III standalone systems, including health and life insurance pricing, move from 2 Aug 2026 to 2 Dec 2027 (Art. 113(c)(i)); Annex I regulated products, where AI is a safety component and medical devices sit, move from 2 Aug 2027 to 2 Aug 2028 (Art. 113(c)(ii)); systems already in service with public authorities get to 2 Aug 2030 (Art. 111(2)). Notified bodies already designated under Annex I, Section A sectoral law must apply for AI Act designation by 28 Jan 2028 (Art. 43(3)). Penalties are unchanged: up to €35M or 7% of worldwide turnover for prohibited practices, up to €15M or 3% for most other duties.

為什麼重要Why it matters

對醫材業者而言，真正的變數不是延期本身，而是延期把 AI Act 與 MDR/IVDR 的接合點往後推了一年——MDCG 2025-6 本就是處理兩套制度如何疊合的文件，而驗證機構能力在 2028 年前是否到位，仍是未解的瓶頸。Hunton 的分析把附件一延期讀成給供應鏈與驗證機構的喘息空間，而非義務減輕。同時要注意的是，延期不等於什麼都沒動：第 50 條透明度義務自 2026/8/2 已適用，生成式系統於 2026/8/2 前上市者的機器可讀標記寬限至 2026/12/2（第 111(4) 條），兩項新增禁止行為同日生效。 For device makers the variable is not the delay itself but that it pushes the seam between the AI Act and MDR/IVDR back a year — MDCG 2025-6 already exists to handle how the two regimes stack, and whether notified-body capacity arrives before 2028 remains the open bottleneck. Hunton reads the Annex I deferral as breathing room for the supply chain and notified bodies rather than as lighter obligations. And a delay is not a pause: Article 50 transparency duties have applied since 2 Aug 2026, machine-readable marking for generative systems placed on the market before that date is granted until 2 Dec 2026 (Art. 111(4)), and two new prohibitions take effect the same day.

英國UK MHRA / DHSC AI Airlock 10/21

英國 10/21 確認無限期承認 CE 標誌，10/6 全盤接受 44 項 AI 監理建議，AI Airlock 第三梯次 10/31 截止 The UK confirmed indefinite CE-mark recognition on 21 Oct, accepted all 44 AI regulation recommendations on 6 Oct, and closes AI Airlock Phase 3 on 31 Oct

發生什麼What

MHRA 於 2025 年 9 月成立的「醫療 AI 監理國家委員會」在 2026 年 9 月 10 日發表報告，提出 44 項建議；英國政府於 10 月 6 日回應，確認將全數實施，Covington 的 10/21 整理。10 月 21 日，政府另行確認對英格蘭、蘇格蘭、威爾斯市場上 CE 標誌醫療器材與體外診斷器材的「無限期承認」。接下來的時程密集：AI Airlock 監理沙盒第三梯次申請於 10 月 31 日截止；MHRA 將於 2026 年 12 月發布 AI 醫材變更管理的指引草案；2027 年初就 AI 醫材的「合格認定與分類」展開諮詢；2027 年春季公布 44 項建議的完整實施路線圖。 The National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare, which the MHRA set up in September 2025, published its report with 44 recommendations on 10 September 2026; the Government responded on 6 October confirming it intends to implement all of them, per Covington's 21 Oct note. On 21 October it separately confirmed indefinite recognition of CE-marked medical devices and IVDs on the Great Britain market. The calendar from here is dense: applications for Phase 3 of the MHRA's AI Airlock regulatory sandbox close on 31 October; draft guidance on managing changes to AI-enabled devices arrives in December 2026; a consultation on qualifying and classifying AI-enabled devices opens in early 2027; and a full implementation roadmap follows in spring 2027.

為什麼重要Why it matters

英國正在做的事和美國這週做的事剛好相反：一邊把持續監測寫進未來制度（變更管理指引、全生命週期監測），一邊把漂移稽核從認證裡刪掉。對跨市場廠商而言，這意味著同一個模型的更新流程在 2027 年後會出現兩套截然不同的要求。另一個訊號是無限期承認 CE 標誌——英國選擇不建立平行審查負擔，把監理資源集中在 AI 特有的問題（變更、分類、沙盒）上，MHRA 同月也把這套規劃帶到美國 AdvaMed 場合交流。 Britain is doing the opposite of what Washington did this week: writing continuous monitoring into the future regime — change-management guidance, lifecycle monitoring — while the US deletes drift audits from certification. For a manufacturer selling into both, the same model's update process will face two quite different sets of requirements after 2027. The second signal is the indefinite CE-mark recognition: the UK is choosing not to build a parallel review burden, concentrating regulatory capacity on the questions specific to AI — change, classification, sandboxing — and the MHRA took the same plan to AdvaMed in the US that month.

美國 FDAUS FDA CDRH GenAI / CADe 10/19

FDA 兩條線同時推進：生成式 AI 醫材討論文件徵詢於 10/19 截止，而放射 CAD 的 510(k) 豁免已在 9/17 被正式駁回 Two FDA tracks at once: comments on the generative-AI device discussion paper closed 19 Oct, while the 510(k) exemption for radiology CAD was formally denied on 17 Sep

發生什麼What

FDA 於 2026 年 8 月 18 日開啟生成式 AI 醫材的公眾徵詢，FDA 新聞稿，討論範圍涵蓋風險評估、上市前評估、上市後監測，以及基礎模型與代理式 AI；徵詢於 10 月 19 日截止，兩黨政策中心 等機構均已提交意見，MD+DI 報導。另一條線上，FDA 已於 2026 年 9 月 17 日以聯邦公報最終命令駁回放射科電腦輔助偵測／診斷（CADe／CADx）與電腦輔助分流通知（CADt）器材的上市前通知豁免請願，命令全文。該請願由澳洲 Harrison.ai 透過顧問公司 Rubrum Advising 提出，主張現行途徑「繁瑣且不適合 AI」；公開評論近 40 件，RSNA、Patient Alliance for Safe Imaging 與擁有近 3,000 個成員組織的 Coalition for Health AI 均表反對。 FDA opened a public docket on generative-AI-enabled medical devices on 18 August 2026, per the FDA announcement, covering risk assessment, premarket evaluation, postmarket monitoring, and foundation models and agentic AI. Comments closed on 19 October, with submissions from bodies including the Bipartisan Policy Center, as MD+DI reported. On the second track, FDA formally denied the petition to exempt radiology computer-aided detection and diagnosis (CADe/CADx) and computer-aided triage and notification (CADt) devices from premarket notification, in a Federal Register final order dated 17 September 2026, the full order. The petition came from Australia's Harrison.ai through the consultancy Rubrum Advising and argued the current pathway is "burdensome and ill-suited" to AI; nearly 40 public comments were filed, with RSNA, the Patient Alliance for Safe Imaging and the Coalition for Health AI — which represents close to 3,000 member organizations — opposing.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這兩件事放在一起，說明 FDA 目前的立場是「生成式 AI 的架構還在問，但既有影像 AI 的前門不鬆」。駁回 CAD 豁免的意義尤其大：若核准，將直接修改分類法規而毋須一般的通知評論程序，等於讓已有一張 CAD 類許可的廠商把後續同類產品免 510(k) 上市。FDA 選擇不走這條路，與 HTI-5 把安全責任推回 FDA 的敘事放在一起看，形成一個尷尬的缺口——認證端鬆了，上市前端沒鬆，而上市後端誰來盯，目前兩邊都沒有明確答案。 Put together, the two actions say FDA's position is that the architecture for generative AI is still an open question while the front door for existing imaging AI stays shut. The CAD denial matters most: had it been granted, the final order would have amended the classification regulation directly, without ordinary notice-and-comment, letting any manufacturer holding one CAD-type clearance ship successors without a 510(k). FDA declined. Read alongside HTI-5's framing that safety belongs to FDA, that leaves an awkward gap — certification loosened, premarket did not, and neither side has a clear answer on who watches the postmarket.

給付Payment CMS CY2027 PFS 待定案

另一隻還沒落地的鞋：CMS 的「軟體即醫療服務」給付框架，CY2027 最終規則預計秋季出爐 The other shoe still in the air: CMS's "Software as a Medical Service" payment framework, with the CY2027 final rule due this fall

發生什麼What

CMS 於 2026 年 7 月 14 日發布 CY 2027 醫師費用表提案規則（CMS-1848-P），首次使用「軟體即醫療服務」（Software as a Medical Service, SaMS）這個分類，定義為「透過演算法分析支援臨床決策的軟體技術」，與處方數位治療、遠距生理／治療監測分開。Wilson Sonsini 的分析指出，PFS 不使用門診的 APC 與狀態指標，而以相對價值單位訂價，或在沒有全國費率時交由承保商訂價（contractor pricing）；PFS 端最主要的實質動作，是把若干從臨床實驗室費用表移出的「純演算法」分析（例如基因體重新判讀、AI 數位病理複閱）改以承保商訂價支付，並就是否要建立跨照護場域一致的 SaMS 給付框架提出資訊徵詢（RFI）。評論期已於 9 月 14 日截止，McDermott+ 的提案規則重點整理。 CMS released the CY 2027 Physician Fee Schedule proposed rule (CMS-1848-P) on 14 July 2026, using the term "Software as a Medical Service" (SaMS) for the first time and defining it as software-based technology that supports clinical decision-making through algorithmic analysis, distinct from prescription digital therapeutics and from remote physiologic and therapeutic monitoring. As Wilson Sonsini notes, the PFS does not use the outpatient system's ambulatory payment classifications and status indicators; payment runs through relative value units, or through contractor pricing where no national rate exists. The substantive move on the PFS side is to pay certain algorithm-only analyses being removed from the Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule — genomic reanalysis, AI-based digital pathology review — through contractor pricing, alongside a request for information on whether a consistent cross-setting SaMS framework should exist at all. Comments closed on 14 September, per McDermott+'s read of the proposed rule.

為什麼重要Why it matters

法規核准與實際使用之間的那道縫，長年是靠給付填的。SaMS 這個詞如果在最終規則裡站住，等於 CMS 第一次承認演算法分析本身是一項「服務」而非儀器的附屬耗材——HIMSS 把它與 MVP、互通性一起列為 CY2027 數位健康轉向的三根支柱。但要注意 CMS 自己把這套安排描述為過渡性的，永久方法留給後續規則制定；承保商訂價也意味著同一個 AI 分析在不同地區可能拿到不同價格。 The gap between clearance and actual use has always been filled by payment. If the term SaMS survives into the final rule, it is the first time CMS has treated algorithmic analysis as a service in its own right rather than an accessory to a device — HIMSS lists it alongside MVPs and interoperability as one of three pillars of the CY2027 digital-health turn. But CMS describes the arrangement as interim, leaving a permanent methodology to later rulemaking, and contractor pricing means the same AI analysis may be paid differently from region to region.

要打折的地方Discount this

本則描述的是提案規則，不是最終規則。CMS 通常於 11 月初發布 CY 最終規則，截至撰稿時（台北時間 10/28 上午）尚未發布；最終內容可能與提案不同。 This describes a proposed rule, not a final one. CMS normally issues the CY final rule in early November and had not done so as of writing, the morning of 28 October Taipei time; the final text may differ from the proposal.

02 — Product Analysis

兩套試圖填補「規則還沒寫好」這段空白的機制：一個由監理者自己經營，一個由民間經營且已經失敗過一次。 Two mechanisms built to fill the gap before the rules are written: one run by the regulator itself, one run privately and already failed once.

MHRA AI Airlock

英國醫材監理沙盒 · MHRA（英國）The UK medical-device regulatory sandbox · MHRA (United Kingdom)

功能與定位。AI Airlock 是 MHRA 建立的監理沙盒，讓難以用現行框架評估的 AI 醫材在受監督環境中測試，產出的證據回頭供監理方設計規則。第三梯次申請於 2026 年 10 月 31 日截止，Covington 整理的時程顯示。它賣的不是上市許可，而是「在規則寫定之前先把問題找出來」——客戶是 MHRA 自己，和願意拿自家產品當測試案例的廠商。 Function and position. AI Airlock is a regulatory sandbox the MHRA runs so that AI devices awkward to assess under current frameworks can be tested under supervision, with the evidence feeding back into how the rules get written. Phase 3 applications close on 31 October 2026, per the timeline Covington compiled. What it sells is not market access but finding the problems before the rules are fixed — the customer is the MHRA itself, and manufacturers willing to make their product a test case.

  • 優勢Strength ：它是目前少數把「變更管理」當成核心問題處理的機制，MHRA 預告 2026 年 12 月就會出 AI 醫材變更管理指引草案、2027 年初再就合格認定與分類諮詢（來源），順序是先測試、後成文。 : it is one of the few mechanisms treating change management as the central problem; the MHRA has flagged draft guidance on managing changes to AI-enabled devices for December 2026 and a consultation on qualification and classification for early 2027 (source), testing first and drafting after.
  • 疑慮Concern ：沙盒的產出是知識不是許可，廠商參加的直接商業回報有限；而 44 項建議的完整實施路線圖要到 2027 年春季才公布（來源），中間這段時間「規則會長什麼樣」仍是空白。公開資料未揭露各梯次的申請與錄取家數，難以判斷實際規模。 : a sandbox produces knowledge, not authorization, so the direct commercial return on participating is thin; and the full implementation roadmap for the 44 recommendations does not arrive until spring 2027 (source), leaving the shape of the eventual rules blank until then. The public record does not give application or acceptance counts per phase, so the real scale is hard to judge.

CHAI Assurance Resource Provider

民間健康 AI 保證體系（已轉向）· Coalition for Health AI（美國）Private health-AI assurance (since pivoted) · Coalition for Health AI (United States)

功能與定位。CHAI 原本要建立全國性的 AI 保證實驗室網路，由獨立實驗室在採購前驗證模型；Duke、Mayo Clinic、Stanford Health 與 Johns Hopkins 四家創始醫療系統承諾 125 萬美元，規劃約 30 間實驗室、至少 32 家公司表態有意送驗。Fierce Healthcare 的事後覆盤指出這個概念在 2025 年初即告瓦解，後改以「保證資源提供者」（ARP）形式運作並轉向治理，2026 年 2 月 11 日 Signal1 甚至被告知停止自稱 CHAI 認證。 Function and position. CHAI set out to build a national network of AI assurance labs — independent labs validating models before procurement. Four founding health systems, Duke, Mayo Clinic, Stanford Health and Johns Hopkins, pledged $1.25 million; roughly 30 labs were planned and at least 32 companies said they wanted tools validated. Fierce Healthcare's post-mortem reports the concept collapsed in early 2025, after which it ran as an "assurance resource provider" program and pivoted to governance; on 11 February 2026 Signal1 was told to stop describing itself as CHAI-certified.

  • 優勢Strength ：轉向治理與部署後監測，剛好對上這週 HTI-5 留下的空缺——當 §170.315(b)(11)(vi) 的漂移稽核被廢止，持續監測就只剩民間機制可依靠（對照）。CHAI 創辦人事後自承，真正有需求的是部署後監測而非採購前保證。 : the pivot to governance and post-deployment monitoring lands exactly on the hole HTI-5 left this week — with the drift audit at §170.315(b)(11)(vi) rescinded, continuous monitoring has only private mechanisms to lean on (compare). CHAI's founder has since said the real demand was post-deployment monitoring, not pre-procurement assurance.
  • 疑慮Concern ：這是一個失敗過一次的模式。2025 年 10 月 HHS 副部長 Jim O'Neill 與 FDA 署長 Marty Makary 在《Washington Examiner》投書批評 CHAI、明言 HHS 不會資助保證實驗室，Microsoft 與 Amazon 隨後退出（來源）；缺少政府背書與付費方，自願性保證體系的覆蓋率與公信力都無從驗證，而公開資料也沒有任何 ARP 驗證量的數字。 : this is a model that has already failed once. In October 2025 HHS Deputy Secretary Jim O'Neill and FDA Commissioner Marty Makary criticized CHAI in a Washington Examiner op-ed and said HHS would not fund assurance labs; Microsoft and Amazon then left (source). Without a government backer or a payer, a voluntary assurance scheme has neither verifiable coverage nor verifiable authority, and no figures on ARP validation volume are public.

04 — Taiwan Angle

衛福部的醫療 AI 細則草案，自我設定的期限就落在下週。 The MOHW's draft rules for medical AI are due, by its own clock, next week.

(1) 衛福部的 AI 醫療細則草案，時間上就是現在。2026 年 8 月 6 日立法院衛環委員會成立智慧醫療委員會當天，衛福部資訊處長李建璋表示將於三個月內提出醫療 AI 的實施細則草案，且明言該細則具拘束力，不同於衛福部 5 月發布、僅屬建議性質的醫療機構生成式 AI 使用指引（中央社，2026-08-06）。以 8 月 6 日起算，這份草案的自我設定期限落在 11 月初——也就是下週。今天美國 HTI-5 示範的那個問題（把模型卡揭露留給市場，還是寫進法規），正好是這份草案要回答的題目之一。 (1) The MOHW's draft AI implementing rules are due, by its own clock, now. On 6 August 2026, the day the Legislative Yuan's Health and Welfare Committee stood up a Smart Medical Care Committee, MOHW information department head Lee Chien-chang said a draft of implementing rules for AI in healthcare would come within three months, and said plainly that those rules would be binding — unlike the ministry's May guidelines on generative AI use in medical institutions, which are advisory only (CNA, 6 Aug 2026). Counting from 6 August, the self-imposed deadline lands in early November, which is next week. The question HTI-5 just answered in Washington — leave model-card disclosure to the market, or write it into regulation — is one of the questions that draft has to answer.

(2) 台灣已經有風險分級的骨架，缺的是醫療這一節的肉。《人工智慧基本法》於 2025 年 12 月 23 日三讀通過，確立永續發展與福祉、人類自主、隱私保護與資料治理、資安與安全、透明與可解釋、公平與不歧視、問責等七項原則，並由數位發展部接手推動 AI 風險分類框架（數位發展部新聞稿）。基本法是框架法，真正決定醫療 AI 怎麼管的是各部會子法。歐盟把醫材放在附件一、英國打算用沙盒先測再寫規則、美國把透明度交還市場——台灣的細則要在這三條路線之間選，而選擇的時間窗口正在關上。 (2) Taiwan already has the skeleton of risk tiering; what is missing is the healthcare flesh on it. The AI Basic Act passed its third reading on 23 December 2025, establishing seven principles — sustainable development and wellbeing, human autonomy, privacy protection and data governance, information security and safety, transparency and explainability, fairness and non-discrimination, and accountability — with the Ministry of Digital Affairs taking over work on an AI risk classification framework (MODA press release). A framework law is a framework; what decides how medical AI is actually governed is each ministry's subordinate rules. The EU puts devices in Annex I, the UK intends to test in a sandbox before drafting, the US has handed transparency back to the market. Taiwan's rules have to choose among those three routes, and the window for choosing is closing.

(3) 給付這一側，台灣其實走在前面一點。CMS 到 CY2027 提案才開始用「軟體即醫療服務」這個詞（Wilson Sonsini），而台灣的智慧醫療器材專案辦公室（衛福部）與 AI/ML 醫材資訊暨媒合平台（TFDA）已經在處理審查與媒合；健保給付端也早已在評估 AI 輔助診斷工具的納入。值得警惕的是 CMS 這次選的是承保商訂價——同一個 AI 分析在不同地區可能拿到不同價格。台灣是單一付費者，不會有這個問題，但相對的，一次訂價錯誤就是全國性的。 (3) On payment, Taiwan is arguably a step ahead. CMS only began using the term "Software as a Medical Service" in its CY2027 proposal (Wilson Sonsini), while Taiwan's smart medical device project office (MOHW) and its AI/ML medical device information and matchmaking platform (TFDA) are already handling review and matchmaking, and the single-payer system has been assessing AI-assisted diagnostic tools for coverage for some time. The cautionary note is what CMS chose: contractor pricing, meaning the same AI analysis may be paid differently by region. A single payer avoids that — but it also means one pricing mistake is a national one.
