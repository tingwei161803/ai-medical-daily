HoloCT：把 CT 判讀與分割合成一個 3D 模型，346 個任務、五院 11,455 例外部驗證，篩檢 85.1% 勝過四位放射科醫師 HoloCT: interpretation and segmentation fused into one 3D model, 346 tasks, an 11,455-scan five-hospital validation, and 85.1% screening accuracy above all four radiologists

發生什麼What

11 月 4 日投到 arXiv（arXiv:2611.06471，Yulu Xie 等 17 位作者）。論文的出發點是：放射科醫師讀 CT 時同時在判斷「是什麼」與「在 3D 解剖的哪裡」，但現有模型把判讀與分割分開做。HoloCT 把兩者合成一個 3D 基礎模型，並且把 3D 遮罩當成輸出的證據。訓練集 HoloDATA 來自 73 個公開資料集與 6 個臨床世代，共 195,921 份 CT、620 萬筆指令對話、280 萬組 CT–mask 配對。評估涵蓋 346 個任務（分割、病灶篩檢、視覺問答、報告生成），用 61 個內部與 12 個外部資料集。五家醫院、11,455 次掃描的多中心驗證中，病灶分割與報告生成分別勝過競爭模型 18.2 與 15.3 分；甲狀腺腫瘤與大腸病灶篩檢平均準確率 85.1%，在回溯閱片研究中高於四位參與的放射科醫師；對未見過結構與瀰漫性病變的語言引導分割勝出領先方法 22.1 分。作者另外報告：要求模型在產出文字的同時產出 3D 遮罩，判讀本身也變好了。 Submitted to arXiv on November 4 (arXiv:2611.06471, Yulu Xie and 16 co-authors). The premise: a radiologist reading a CT judges what a finding is and where it sits in 3D anatomy at the same time, while existing models handle interpretation and segmentation separately. HoloCT fuses both into one 3D foundation model and emits 3D masks as the evidence for its output. The training set, HoloDATA, draws on 73 public datasets and six clinical cohorts: 195,921 CT scans, 6.2 million instruction dialogues and 2.8 million CT-mask pairs. Evaluation spans 346 tasks (segmentation, lesion screening, visual question answering, report generation) across 61 internal and 12 external datasets. In a multicentre validation of 11,455 scans from five hospitals it beat competing models by 18.2 points on lesion segmentation and 15.3 on report generation; it reached 85.1% mean accuracy screening thyroid tumours and colon lesions, above all four radiologists in a retrospective reader study; and language-guided segmentation of unseen structures and diffuse findings beat the leading approach by 22.1 points. The authors also report that making the model produce 3D masks alongside text improved the interpretation itself.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是今天四則新聞裡唯一把「可稽核性」直接做進模型輸出的一則。一份 AI 生成的 CT 報告過去只能用文字跟原始影像對照，現在每個說法都附一塊體素層級的遮罩，複核者可以指著問「你說的病灶在哪」。而「加上遮罩之後判讀變好」這個結果比準確率更值得注意——它意味著強迫模型指認證據不是對效能的稅，而是一種監督訊號。這一點正好反駁了業界常見的那句「可解釋性要用準確率換」。 This is the only one of today's four stories that builds auditability into the model's output rather than around it. An AI-written CT report could previously only be checked by reading it against the images; here every claim comes with a voxel-level mask, so a reviewer can point and ask where the finding is. The result that adding masks improved interpretation matters more than the accuracy number — it suggests that forcing a model to point at its evidence is a supervision signal rather than a tax on performance, which cuts directly against the familiar claim that explainability has to be paid for in accuracy.

發生什麼What

11 月 9 日投稿的 arXiv:2611.12066（Maximilian Lindholz 等 15 位作者，標題直接問「我們還需要訓練資料嗎」）測試一件極端的事：讓 LLM 單憑醫學知識，在一次回覆裡寫完一個 LeNet 風格小型 CNN 的全部權重與偏值，不做任何最佳化、不呼叫工具、也不給看影像。作者稱此為 weight-space programming（WSP）。受測者是 Claude Opus 5.5 與 GPT-6 Astra，對照組為零樣本 MedSigLIP 與同架構用 Adam 訓練的網路。任務共 7 項、4 種模態：MedMNIST 的胸部 X 光、乳房超音波、腹部 CT（6 項）加上 SynthUterus 的骨盆 MRI（1 項）。六項 MedMNIST 任務平均測試 AUC：Opus 0.719、Astra 0.711，兩者都在 6 項中的 5 項勝過 MedSigLIP，而參數量少了一萬倍以上。但只用 256 張標註影像以 Adam 訓練的同架構網路平均 AUC 0.828，在每一項 MedMNIST 任務上都更高。SynthUterus 上 Opus 拿 0.764，Astra 因系統性錯誤掉到 0.136——低於隨機。協定、紀錄與所有生成的權重都放上 GitHub。 arXiv:2611.12066, submitted November 9 (Maximilian Lindholz and 14 co-authors, titled with the blunt question of whether training data is still needed), tests something extreme: can an LLM, from medical knowledge alone, write out every weight and bias of a small LeNet-style CNN in a single reply — with no optimisation, no tool use and no access to images? The authors call this weight-space programming (WSP). The subjects were Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, against zero-shot MedSigLIP and the same architecture trained with Adam. Seven tasks across four modalities: chest X-ray, breast ultrasound and abdominal CT from MedMNIST (six tasks) plus pelvic MRI from SynthUterus. Mean test AUC on the six MedMNIST tasks: Opus 0.719, Astra 0.711, both beating MedSigLIP on five of six while using more than ten thousand times fewer parameters. But the same architecture trained with Adam on just 256 labelled images reached a mean AUC of 0.828, higher on every MedMNIST task. On SynthUterus, Opus scored 0.764 while Astra fell to 0.136 — below chance — from a systematic error. The protocol, logs and every generated weight set are on GitHub.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這篇把一個通常只能定性討論的問題換算成了價格：模型裡的醫學知識，值多少張標註影像？答案是「少於 256 張」。這不是在貶低 WSP——反過來看，在罕病、新興適應症、或任何根本湊不到標註的場景裡，一個 AUC 0.72 的零資料起點是從無到有；它也可以當成訓練的初始化先驗。但它同時替醫療 AI 畫出一條清楚的下限：只要還能拿到兩百多張標註，傳統訓練依然完勝，而「我們用了最強的模型所以不需要資料」這種說法在這組實驗裡站不住。 The paper converts a usually hand-waved question into a price: how many labelled images is the medical knowledge inside a model worth? Fewer than 256. That is not a dismissal of WSP — read the other way, in rare disease, a novel indication, or any setting where labels simply cannot be assembled, an AUC of 0.72 from zero data is something from nothing, and it can serve as a prior to initialise training. But it also draws a clear floor under medical AI: as long as a couple of hundred labels can be obtained, conventional training still wins outright, and the claim that a strong enough model removes the need for data does not survive this experiment.

稽核臨床預測模型，黑箱存取跟看權重一樣有效——而一支寫得好的普通腳本就打平了 LLM For auditing a clinical prediction model, black-box access works as well as reading the weights — and a well-written ordinary script matched the LLM

發生什麼What

11 月 5 日投稿、6 日改版的 arXiv:2611.07847（Zhiyu Liu、Xinyu Yang）處理一個此前被混在一起的問題：前人展示「LLM 能從權重檔稽核臨床預測模型」時，同時混用了讀權重與執行程式碼兩種能力。這篇把兩者分開做預先登錄的模擬實驗。受測模型為 GPT-6 Astra 與另外四個 GPT 模型，標的是在模擬蜘蛛網膜下腔出血世代上訓練的 tanh 小網路，參數量 105 到 38,401。三種條件：權重直接放進提示而沒有工具；給一個只能算數的工具並開放權重（白箱）；同樣的工具但只給預測（黑箱）。結果：沒有工具時，Astra 在 353 個權重的網路上還能還原被植入的關係，到 3,457 個權重就不可靠了，而在預設系統指令下有 21 項稽核與修復任務中的 17 項被它以「無法識別」為由拒答，改用明確指令後拒答消失。有工具時，在最多 38,401 個權重的網路上 39 次執行全部還原了植入關係，21 次空網路執行零誤報，6 次中 6 次找出被低估死亡數的偏誤子群。黑箱表現與白箱相同。另外，一支設計良好的傳統腳本就打平了對照模型的稽核結果與 LLM 最好的修復結果。程式碼、模擬網路、提示與回覆都公開在 GitHub。 arXiv:2611.07847 (Zhiyu Liu, Xinyu Yang), submitted November 5 and revised November 6, separates two things earlier demonstrations had blended: when people showed an LLM auditing a clinical prediction model from its weight file, they mixed reading the weights with running code. This paper splits them in pre-specified simulation experiments. GPT-6 Astra and four other GPT models were pointed at small tanh networks, 105 to 38,401 weights, trained on simulated subarachnoid haemorrhage cohorts. Three conditions: weights in the prompt with no tools; a calculation-only tool with white-box weight access; the same tool with black-box access to predictions only. With no tools, Astra recovered the planted relationship in 353-weight networks but became unreliable at 3,457, and under the default system instruction declined 17 of 21 audit and repair tasks as unidentifiable — refusals that an explicit instruction removed entirely. With the tool, it recovered the planted relationship in all 39 runs on networks up to 38,401 weights, raised no false alarms across 21 null-network runs, and found the biased subgroup in 6 of 6 runs. Black-box access performed as well as white-box. Separately, a well-designed conventional script matched the comparator models on the audit and the best LLM repair. Code, simulated networks, prompts and replies are published on GitHub.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這篇的政策含意比技術含意大。過去一年「要不要強制揭露模型權重」在歐美監管辯論裡反覆出現；如果只給預測的黑箱存取就能查出植入的關係與偏誤子群，那麼稽核制度可以不必要求廠商交出權重，只要求一個可以重跑的計算介面。另一半的結論同樣重要且較少被說出口：既然一支普通腳本就能打平，LLM 在這裡提供的是方便性而不是新能力——而拒答行為會隨系統指令的措辭大幅擺動，這對任何想把 LLM 寫進稽核流程的制度都是一個可重現性風險。 The policy implication outruns the technical one. Whether model weights must be disclosed has run through regulatory debate on both sides of the Atlantic for a year; if black-box access to predictions alone is enough to surface a planted relationship and a biased subgroup, an audit regime can ask for a re-runnable calculation interface instead of the weights. The other half of the finding is just as important and less often said aloud: since an ordinary script matched the models, what the LLM supplies here is convenience rather than new capability — and the refusal behaviour swings sharply on how the system instruction is worded, which is a reproducibility risk for any regime that writes an LLM into the audit loop.

要打折的地方Discount this
