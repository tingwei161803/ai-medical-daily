同一張病歷疊了三層 AI：OpenAI 把 ChatGPT 接進 Epic 的那一週，OpenEvidence 推出自家模型，而 2.2 億美元流向了沒人搶的預約層 Three AI layers on one chart: the week OpenAI plugged ChatGPT into Epic, OpenEvidence shipped its own models, and $220M went to the layer nobody was fighting over

2026 年 9 月 4 日（五）· 涵蓋 8/28–9/4 · 台北時間 Friday, September 4, 2026 · Covering Aug 28–Sep 4 · Taipei time

本週有三件事發生在同一個地方——醫師看診前打開的那張病歷。9 月 1 日，OpenAI 宣布 ChatGPT Health 可以唯讀讀取 Epic 病歷，Epic 系統覆蓋逾 3.25 億名病人，UCSF Health 是目前唯一具名的試點（TechCrunch, 2026-09-01）。9 月 3 日，估值 120 億美元、宣稱逾四成美國醫師每日使用的 OpenEvidence 推出自家臨床模型家族（STAT Health Tech, 2026-09-03）。而 9 月 2 日拿到最大一張支票的，是把影像檢查「預約」這件事自動化的 Scan.com——2.2 億美元，年化營收 1.65 億美元、年增一倍（Fierce Healthcare, 2026-09-02）。三層 AI 疊在同一個工作流上，付錢的醫院要為同一件事買三次單；而唯一沒有人在搶、卻確實在收錢的那一層，根本不在病歷裡。 Three things happened this week in the same place: the chart a clinician opens before a visit. On September 1, OpenAI announced ChatGPT Health can pull read-only data from Epic — an EHR covering more than 325 million patients — with UCSF Health the only named pilot so far (TechCrunch, 2026-09-01). On September 3, OpenEvidence — valued at $12B and claiming daily use by more than 40% of US physicians — shipped its own family of clinical models (STAT Health Tech, 2026-09-03). And the biggest cheque of the week, on September 2, went to Scan.com for automating imaging bookings: $220M against a $165M annualised run rate that doubled year over year (Fierce Healthcare, 2026-09-02). Three AI layers land on one workflow, and the hospital paying for all of them buys the same job three times — while the one layer nobody is fighting over, and the one reliably collecting cash, sits outside the chart entirely.

平台整合Platform OpenAIEpic9/01

OpenAI 把 ChatGPT Health 接上 Epic 病歷：唯讀、不回寫、UCSF 先試 OpenAI wires ChatGPT Health into Epic charts: read-only, no write-back, UCSF first

發生什麼What

OpenAI 於 9 月 1 日宣布 ChatGPT Health 新增 Epic 連接器，臨床端可匯入門診紀錄、檢驗值、用藥與專科文件，用於彙整病歷、標出病史變化、看診前準備。整合為唯讀，AI 不回寫任何內容到病歷。Epic 系統涵蓋逾 3.25 億名病人；ChatGPT Health 上個月才對全美消費者開放，每週約有 3 億則健康相關提問（TechCrunch, 2026-09-01）。目前唯一具名的臨床試點是 UCSF Health（healthsystemCIO, 2026-09-01）。 On September 1 OpenAI announced an Epic connector for ChatGPT Health, letting clinicians import visit notes, lab results, medications and specialist documentation to summarise records, surface changes in history and prepare for appointments. The integration is read-only; the AI writes nothing back to the chart. Epic covers more than 325 million patients, and ChatGPT Health — opened to all US consumers only last month — already fields roughly 300 million health-related queries a week (TechCrunch, 2026-09-01). UCSF Health is the only named clinical pilot (healthsystemCIO, 2026-09-01).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本週唯一一件會讓醫院 CIO 立刻打開試算表的事。同一個「看診前病歷整理」的動作，現在有三個供應商在賣：Epic 原生 AI、已經導入兩年的環境語音記錄廠商、以及新加入的 ChatGPT。三層疊在一個工作流上，代表重複授權費、各自獨立的驗證流程，以及臨床端各有偏好的分裂使用習慣（healthsystemCIO）。真正的治理問題不是模型好不好，而是這條連線算「官方合作」還是「一般第三方介接」——兩者要過的審查關卡完全不同。 This is the one item this week that sends a hospital CIO straight to a spreadsheet. The same job — pre-visit chart review — is now sold by three vendors at once: Epic's native AI, the ambient documentation vendor deployed two years ago, and now ChatGPT. Three layers on one workflow means duplicate licensing, separate validation processes, and clinicians splitting by preference (healthsystemCIO). The governance question is not whether the model is good; it is whether this connection counts as a sanctioned partnership or an ordinary third-party interface — the two clear entirely different approval gates.

要打折的地方Discount this

OpenAI 引用的安全數字全是自評：27 種臨床用例、4,363 筆醫師評分中 99.1% 判定為安全，資料連接器準確率 93.2%–98.6%，均未經第三方稽核（healthsystemCIO）。同時，OpenAI 仍面臨數起指控其消費端產品給出有害醫療建議的訴訟，包括劑量錯誤（TechCrunch）。企業版是否需簽 BAA、與消費版如何切割，各院要自己問清楚。 Every safety figure OpenAI cites is self-reported: 99.1% of responses rated safe across 4,363 physician ratings on 27 clinical use cases, with data-connector accuracy of 93.2%–98.6%, none independently audited (healthsystemCIO). Meanwhile OpenAI still faces lawsuits alleging its consumer product gave harmful medical advice, including dosing errors (TechCrunch). Whether the enterprise path requires a BAA, and how it is walled off from the consumer product, is a question each system must ask for itself.

模型發表Models OpenEvidence9/03

OpenEvidence 推出自家臨床模型家族：從「別人模型的介面」變成「自己的模型」 OpenEvidence ships its own clinical model family: from interface-on-someone-else's-model to its own

發生什麼What

STAT Health Tech 於 9 月 3 日報導，OpenEvidence 發表一套新的臨床用 AI 模型家族。該公司 2026 年 1 月完成 2.5 億美元 D 輪、估值翻倍至 120 億美元（CNBC, 2026-01-21；Fierce Healthcare），並宣稱逾四成美國醫師每日使用、覆蓋一萬家以上醫院，年化營收在 2026 年 7 月估達 3 億美元（Sacra）。 STAT Health Tech reported on September 3 that OpenEvidence has released a new family of clinical AI models. The company closed a $250M Series D in January 2026 at a doubled $12B valuation (CNBC, 2026-01-21; Fierce Healthcare), and claims daily use by more than 40% of US physicians across 10,000-plus hospitals, with annualised revenue estimated at $300M as of July 2026 (Sacra).

為什麼重要Why it matters

時機不是巧合。OpenEvidence 的護城河一直是內容授權——NEJM、JAMA、Wiley、Cochrane、ACC、ADA、NCCN 的授權組合（Sacra）——而不是模型本身。當 OpenAI 帶著自己的模型直接走進 Epic 病歷，「介面 ＋ 授權內容」這個組合的其中一半就暴露在外。自己訓模型，是把最後一塊外包環節收回來，也是把毛利率從別人的 API 帳單裡拿回來。 The timing is not a coincidence. OpenEvidence's moat has always been content licensing — the NEJM, JAMA, Wiley, Cochrane, ACC, ADA and NCCN bundle (Sacra) — not the model. With OpenAI walking into the Epic chart carrying its own model, half of the "interface plus licensed corpus" bundle is suddenly exposed. Training your own is how you take the last outsourced piece back in-house, and how you take gross margin back off someone else's API bill.

要打折的地方Discount this

STAT 該篇為 STAT+ 付費牆內容，本報僅依公開可見的標題與導言撰寫，未取得模型名稱、規模、benchmark 或定價。OpenEvidence 過去的能力宣稱（例如 USMLE 滿分）為公司自行發布（OpenEvidence 官方公告），而考試分數與臨床結果之間沒有已建立的對應關係。 The STAT piece sits behind STAT+; this report is written from the publicly visible headline and deck only, and does not have model names, sizes, benchmarks or pricing. OpenEvidence's past capability claims — the perfect USMLE score, for instance — are company-published (OpenEvidence announcement), and no established mapping runs from exam scores to clinical outcomes.

Scan.com 拿下 2.2 億美元：85% 的影像檢查還在用傳真跟電話預約 Scan.com raises $220M: 85% of imaging scans are still booked by fax or phone

發生什麼What

9 月 2 日，醫學影像網路平台 Scan.com 宣布募得 2.2 億美元，結構為 9,000 萬美元 C 輪股權加 1.3 億美元債權。股權由 Noteus Partners 領投，Aviva、Concord Health Partners、YZR Capital、Oxford Capital 參與；債權來自 VerisFi Capital 與 Atempo Growth。公司 2017 年於英國創立、2023 年進入美國，全球累計服務逾 90 萬名病人，營收年增一倍至年化 1.65 億美元（Fierce Healthcare, 2026-09-02）。 On September 2, medical imaging network Scan.com announced $220M: $90M of Series C equity plus $130M in debt. Noteus Partners led the equity, with Aviva, Concord Health Partners, YZR Capital and Oxford Capital participating; VerisFi Capital and Atempo Growth provided the debt. Founded in the UK in 2017 and in the US since 2023, the company has served over 900,000 patients globally, with revenue doubling year over year to a $165M annualised run rate (Fierce Healthcare, 2026-09-02).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是本週最誠實的一則消息。Scan.com 的 AI 不讀影像、不做診斷、不寫病歷——它做的是把轉診單自動比對到網路內的可預約時段與價格，因為85% 的影像檢查仍靠傳真或電話預約。共同創辦人 Charlie Bullock 的說法把定位講得很清楚：「檢驗幾十年前就有 Quest 和 Labcorp 了。影像從來沒有，那就是我們蓋的東西。」在所有人搶著當醫師的第二意見時，有人去當了排程總機——而那門生意有明確的計價單位、明確的付款人，以及一年翻倍的營收。 This is the most honest item of the week. Scan.com's AI does not read images, make diagnoses or write notes — it matches referrals against network availability and pricing, because 85% of scans are still booked by fax or phone. Co-founder Charlie Bullock puts the position plainly: "Labs got that decades ago with Quest Diagnostics and Labcorp. Imaging never did, and that is what we have built." While everyone else races to be the physician's second opinion, someone went and became the switchboard — a business with a clear unit of billing, a clear payer, and revenue that doubled in a year.

要打折的地方Discount this

1.65 億美元為公司自述的年化跑率（run rate），非經審計的年度營收；2.2 億美元中有 1.3 億是債權，不是股權估值的分母。報導未揭露本輪投後估值。 The $165M is a company-stated annualised run rate, not audited annual revenue, and $130M of the $220M is debt rather than equity. The report does not disclose a post-money valuation for the round.

Cureety 併購 Reimagine Care：腫瘤科的「AI 分流 ＋ 遠距照護」合體，250 家癌症中心 Cureety acquires Reimagine Care: AI triage meets virtual oncology care across 250 cancer centres

為什麼重要Why it matters

Cureety 營運長 Misha Kaur 的一句話值得抄下來：「檢驗的不是 AI 能不能講出一段令人印象深刻的對話，而是對的那個人有沒有在對的時刻進入這段對話。」這是本週最清楚的一次「產品定義」——腫瘤科的 AI 價值不在生成，在路由。它也解釋了為什麼這類公司走併購而非自建：分流演算法可以自己寫，但 24/7 值班的腫瘤科護理人力買比較快。 Cureety COO Misha Kaur's line is worth copying down: "The test is not whether AI can have an impressive conversation… it is whether the right human enters the conversation at the right moment." It is the week's crispest product definition — in oncology the value of AI is not generation but routing. It also explains why these companies buy rather than build: you can write a triage algorithm yourself, but a 24/7 oncology-trained nursing bench is faster to acquire.

要打折的地方Discount this

95%、97%、2.4% 均為公司自述的導入數據，未見同儕審查或第三方稽核；「未升級即解決」的定義由廠商自訂，且未揭露漏接（false negative）率——在腫瘤科，那才是真正該看的數字。交易金額未公開，無從評估估值合理性。 The 95%, 97% and 2.4% are all company-stated implementation figures, with no peer review or third-party audit; "resolved without escalation" is a vendor-defined measure, and no false-negative rate is disclosed — in oncology that is the number that actually matters. With terms undisclosed, the valuation cannot be assessed.

證據落差Evidence gap PLOS Digital HealthFDA8/19

1,357 件 AI 醫材過關，只有 3 件量過病人會不會活下來 1,357 AI devices cleared; three were tested on whether patients live

發生什麼What

PLOS Digital Health 於 8 月 19 日刊出的系統性分析指出，截至 2025 年 12 月 5 日，FDA 已核准 1,357 件 AI/ML 醫材，其中僅 34 件（2.5%）登記過前瞻性臨床試驗、12 件（0.9%）在 ClinicalTrials.gov 公布結果、3 件（0.2%）評估過死亡率或再住院這類以病人為中心的結果。已有試驗中近 74% 收案不足 500 人、68% 僅在美國進行，孕婦、非英語使用者與兒科病人被系統性排除（PLOS Digital Health, 2026-08-19）。 A systematic analysis published in PLOS Digital Health on August 19 found that of 1,357 AI/ML-enabled devices cleared by the FDA through December 5, 2025, only 34 (2.5%) had a registered prospective clinical trial, 12 (0.9%) posted results on ClinicalTrials.gov, and three (0.2%) evaluated patient-centred outcomes such as mortality or readmission. Of the trials that exist, nearly 74% enrolled fewer than 500 participants and 68% were US-only, with pregnant women, non-English speakers and paediatric patients systematically excluded (PLOS Digital Health, 2026-08-19).

為什麼重要Why it matters

把這篇擺在上面四則旁邊，本週的形狀就出來了：分發問題正在被解決（OpenAI 進 Epic），模型問題正在被解決（OpenEvidence 自己訓），錢的問題正在被解決（Scan.com 的 1.65 億跑率），而證明問題一件都沒解決。作者的結論很直接——「準備好」不該再由「取得 FDA 核准」單獨定義，並提出退回性驗證、前瞻性安全研究、多中心結果試驗的三階段框架。 Set this beside the four items above and the week takes shape: distribution is being solved (OpenAI into Epic), the model problem is being solved (OpenEvidence training its own), the money problem is being solved (Scan.com's $165M run rate) — and the proof problem is not being solved at all. The authors are blunt: readiness should no longer be defined by FDA clearance alone, and they propose a three-phase framework of retrospective validation, prospective safety studies and multi-centre outcome trials.

02 — Product Analysis

兩個產品都想成為醫師打開的第一個視窗，但它們賣的東西完全不同：一個賣通用能力的擴散，一個賣專屬語料的稀缺 Two products both want to be the first window a clinician opens — but one sells the reach of a general model, the other the scarcity of a licensed corpus

ChatGPT Health（Epic connector）

通用模型下沉到病歷 · OpenAI（美國）A general model reaching down into the chart · OpenAI (US)

功能與定位。唯讀匯入 Epic 的門診紀錄、檢驗值、用藥與專科文件，供醫師做看診前彙整與病史變化辨識；部分部署直接嵌在病歷工作流內，不必離開病人圖表。買方是醫院與醫療組織，但產品的重心其實在已經存在的使用習慣——ChatGPT Health 上個月才開放全美消費者，每週約 3 億則健康提問（TechCrunch）。 Function and position. Read-only import of Epic visit notes, labs, medications and specialist documentation for pre-visit synthesis and change detection; in some deployments it is embedded directly in the chart workflow so the clinician never leaves the patient record. The buyer is the health system, but the product's real weight sits in habits that already exist — ChatGPT Health opened to US consumers only last month and already handles roughly 300 million health queries a week (TechCrunch).

  • 優勢Strength ：分發成本近乎為零。Epic 覆蓋逾 3.25 億名病人，而醫師早就在用 ChatGPT——不需要教育市場，只需要接一條線（TechCrunch, 2026-09-01）。 : distribution costs almost nothing. Epic covers more than 325 million patients, and clinicians already use ChatGPT — no market to educate, just a wire to connect (TechCrunch, 2026-09-01).
  • 優勢Strength ：唯讀是聰明的自我設限。不回寫病歷，等於把責任邊界劃在正式文件之外，也讓法遵審查的難度降一級（healthsystemCIO）。 : read-only is a smart self-limit. Writing nothing back to the chart draws the liability boundary outside the documented record and drops the compliance review a full notch (healthsystemCIO).
  • 疑慮Concern ：安全數字全部自評，且沒有醫院端獨立驗證——27 種用例、4,363 筆醫師評分中 99.1% 安全、連接器準確率 93.2%–98.6%，都是廠商提供（healthsystemCIO）。同時公司仍有因消費端醫療建議致害而起的訴訟在身（TechCrunch）。 : every safety figure is self-evaluated with no health-system validation — 99.1% safe across 4,363 physician ratings on 27 use cases, connector accuracy 93.2%–98.6%, all vendor-supplied (healthsystemCIO). The company also carries live lawsuits over harm from consumer-side medical advice (TechCrunch).
  • 疑慮Concern ：客戶名單只有一個。UCSF Health 是唯一具名試點，其餘未揭露——在健康照護領域，「已可使用」和「有一家在試」之間差了兩年（healthsystemCIO, 2026-09-01）。 : the customer list has one name on it. UCSF Health is the only disclosed pilot — and in healthcare the gap between "available" and "one site is trying it" runs about two years (healthsystemCIO, 2026-09-01).

OpenEvidence

授權語料 ＋ 廣告變現的醫師答案層 · OpenEvidence（美國）A licensed-corpus answer layer monetised by advertising · OpenEvidence (US)

功能與定位。對通過驗證的美國醫師免費，靠製藥與醫材廣告加企業訂閱變現——CPM 落在 70 至 1,000 美元以上，遠高於社群媒體的 5 至 15 美元，換算每用戶年均收入約 124 美元（Sacra）。這個模式讓它繞開了醫療業約 18 個月的銷售週期，也解釋了為何營收能從 2024 年的 790 萬美元跳到 2026 年 7 月估計的 3 億美元。9 月 3 日發表自家臨床模型家族（STAT）。 Function and position. Free to verified US physicians, monetised through pharma and device advertising plus enterprise subscriptions — CPMs of $70 to $1,000-plus against social media's $5–15, working out to roughly $124 average revenue per user (Sacra). That model let it bypass healthcare's ~18-month sales cycle, and explains revenue going from $7.9M in 2024 to an estimated $300M by July 2026. Its own clinical model family shipped September 3 (STAT).

  • 優勢Strength ：內容授權是真的難複製。NEJM、JAMA、Wiley、Cochrane、ACC、ADA、NCCN 的授權組合，有錢也不一定買得到——期刊出版商把內容授權給一個不會變成自己競爭者的小公司，意願遠高於授權給通用模型廠商（Sacra）。 : the content licences are genuinely hard to copy. The NEJM, JAMA, Wiley, Cochrane, ACC, ADA and NCCN bundle is not simply purchasable — publishers are far readier to license a small company that will not become their competitor than a general-purpose model vendor (Sacra).
  • 優勢Strength ：已經進到病歷裡了。Sutter Health（2026 年 2 月）、Mount Sinai（3 月）、Cedars-Sinai（5 月）的 Epic 部署，代表 OpenAI 9 月才走的那條路，它半年前就走完了（Sacra）。 : it is already inside the chart. Epic deployments at Sutter Health (Feb 2026), Mount Sinai (March) and Cedars-Sinai (May) mean it walked the path OpenAI took in September six months ago (Sacra).
  • 疑慮Concern ：廣告變現與臨床中立之間的張力，沒有任何第三方在管。當製藥廠付出 70 至 1,000 美元的 CPM 買醫師在決策當下的注意力，「答案」與「廣告」的邊界由誰稽核，至今沒有公開機制（Sacra）。 : the tension between ad revenue and clinical neutrality has no third-party referee. When pharma pays $70–$1,000 CPMs for a physician's attention at the moment of decision, no public mechanism audits where the answer ends and the advertisement begins (Sacra).
  • 疑慮Concern ：120 億美元估值對上約 3 億美元年化營收，是 40 倍上下的營收倍數，而護城河的一半（模型）剛剛被通用廠商商品化（CNBC）。新模型家族的規模、benchmark 與定價尚未公開，本報無法評估它是否真的補上了這個缺口。 : a $12B valuation against roughly $300M annualised revenue is about a 40x multiple, at the moment half the moat — the model — is being commoditised by a general-purpose vendor (CNBC). The new family's size, benchmarks and pricing are not public, so this report cannot judge whether it actually closes that gap.

今天的競爭結構是一個倒過來的漏斗。離醫師滑鼠最近的那一層（病歷內的答案與摘要）擠了三家以上，估值最高、營收倍數最誇張、證據最薄；離滑鼠最遠的那幾層（預約媒合、腫瘤科分流、語音外撥）競爭者最少、計價單位最清楚、現金流最實在。PLOS 那 0.2% 正好落在擁擠的那一端——被搶的是注意力，不是結果。 Today's competitive structure is an inverted funnel. The layer closest to the clinician's cursor — answers and summaries inside the chart — holds three or more contenders with the highest valuations, the wildest revenue multiples and the thinnest evidence. The layers furthest from it — booking, oncology triage, outbound voice — have the fewest competitors, the clearest units of billing and the most real cash. That PLOS 0.2% sits squarely at the crowded end: what is being fought over is attention, not outcomes.

04 — Taiwan Angle

台灣正在蓋的是最底層的那一塊，而時程表跟這週的美國新聞剛好對得上 Taiwan is building the bottom layer, and its timetable lines up with this week's US news

(1) 病歷互通的時程，決定台灣醫院會不會遇到同一個「三層疊一層」問題。衛福部部長石崇良提出「333 政策」，主軸是打通不同醫療資訊系統之間的資料結構，並推「FHIR Box」格式標準化；規劃 2026 年底前全國醫學中心病歷互通，2027 至 2028 年擴及區域醫院、地區醫院，以及診所與衛生所的公版系統（聯合新聞網；iThome）。美國這週的爭議之所以成立，前提是 Epic 已經是單一巨大資料池；台灣還在把池子接起來，這反而給了一個難得的順序優勢——治理規則可以在圖層堆疊之前先寫好，而不是像美國 CIO 現在這樣事後補。 (1) The interoperability timetable decides whether Taiwanese hospitals hit the same three-layers-on-one-workflow problem. Health Minister Stone Chung-liang's "333 policy" centres on unifying data structures across medical information systems, with a "FHIR Box" format standard; the plan is nationwide medical-centre record interoperability by end-2026, extending to regional and district hospitals plus a public system for clinics and health stations across 2027–2028 (UDN; iThome). This week's US argument only exists because Epic is already one enormous pool; Taiwan is still connecting the pools, which is a rare ordering advantage — governance rules can be written before the layers stack, rather than retrofitted the way US CIOs are doing now.

(2) 錢的量級決定台灣買不起「同一件事買三次」。「114–118 年健康臺灣深耕計畫」五年編列 489 億元新台幣（約 15 億美元），四大主軸涵蓋精準醫療與遠距醫療（GeneOnline；行政院）。對照一下：這是全國五年的數字，而 Scan.com 一輪就拿了 2.2 億美元、OpenEvidence 累計募了約 7 億美元。台灣不可能在答案層跟這些估值競賽，但也因此不必——重複授權費是台灣醫院最負擔不起的浪費，而先把互通標準訂死，正是避免這種浪費最便宜的做法。 (2) The order of magnitude means Taiwan cannot afford to buy the same job three times. The 2025–2029 Deepening Health Taiwan Plan allocates NT$48.9 billion (about US$1.5 billion) over five years across four pillars including precision and telemedicine (GeneOnline; Executive Yuan). For scale: that is the national five-year figure, while Scan.com took $220M in a single round and OpenEvidence has raised roughly $700M. Taiwan cannot win a valuation race at the answer layer — and does not need to. Duplicate licensing is the waste Taiwanese hospitals can least afford, and nailing down interoperability standards first is the cheapest way to avoid it.

(3) 「AI 醫療細則草案」該把證據門檻寫進去。衛福部已規劃提出 AI 醫療相關細則草案（中央社, 2026-08-06），同時設有三大智慧醫療中心（衛福部智慧醫療中心）。PLOS 那篇給了現成的參考框架：1,357 件過關、3 件量過病人結果。如果台灣的細則只複製「取得核准即可上線」的邏輯，就會原封不動繼承同一個證據落差；反過來，要求以病人結果為終點的多中心試驗雖然慢，卻是小市場唯一能建立的差異化——健保資料的完整度在國際上本來就是稀缺資產。 (3) The draft AI-in-medicine rules should write the evidence bar into the text. The health ministry has signalled draft regulations for AI in medicine (CNA, 2026-08-06) alongside three national smart-healthcare centres (MOHW Smart Healthcare Center). The PLOS paper hands over a ready-made reference: 1,357 cleared, three measured against patient outcomes. Rules that merely copy "cleared means deployable" inherit that evidence gap intact. Demanding multi-centre trials with patient outcomes as the endpoint is slower, but it is the one differentiation a small market can actually build — the completeness of Taiwan's single-payer claims data is already a scarce international asset.
