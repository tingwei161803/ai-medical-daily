Epic 自己做了 AI 病歷之後，環境式 AI 的位子就不能再照原價賣：Ambience 把軟體費綁上 3 倍 ROI 與 45%→31% 的醫師燒竭率、Abridge 往收入循環與護理站鑽——而唯一一份跨五家醫學中心、8,500 名臨床人員的同儕審查研究算出來的收益，是每位醫師每月 167 美元，約為廠商案例的六分之一 Once Epic shipped AI charting of its own, the ambient seat could no longer be sold at the old price: Ambience put its fees behind a 3x ROI and a burnout rate falling from 45% to 31%, Abridge dug down into the revenue cycle and the nursing station — while the one peer-reviewed study across five academic systems and 8,500 clinicians puts the gain at $167 per clinician per month, about a sixth of what the vendor case studies claim

2026 年 9 月 18 日（五）· 涵蓋 9/11–9/18，並回溯本年度形成此局面的關鍵節點 · 台北時間 Friday, September 18, 2026 · Covering Sep 11–18, with the earlier turning points that produced this structure · Taipei time

環境式 AI（ambient AI）過去兩年賣的是同一樣東西：一個醫師一年約 2,500 美元的授權位子，換走一天一小時的打字。今年 2 月 5 日，Epic 把 AI Charting 直接放進自家的 Art 平台——同一段對話、同一份病歷，不必再把病人的語音交給第三方。Epic 手上是全美超過 42% 的急症醫院市佔與近 55% 的急症病床，這一步被 HIT Consultant 形容成「Sherlocking」：平台自己做了，外掛就沒有價錢。七個月後的今天回頭看，兩家頭部公司給了兩個完全不同的答案，而本週在醫院裡真正上線的東西（Oracle 的護理 agent、Sentara 的 30 人試點）也正在把同一個問題往前推：這東西到底值多少錢，誰說了算。 For two years ambient AI sold one thing: a licence seat at roughly $2,500 per clinician per year, in exchange for an hour a day of typing. On February 5 this year Epic put AI Charting straight into its own Art platform — same conversation, same chart, and no need to hand patient audio to a third party. Epic holds over 42% of the U.S. acute-care hospital market and close to 55% of acute-care beds, which is why HIT Consultant called the move a "Sherlocking": once the platform builds it, the add-on has no price. Seven months on, the two leading companies have given two entirely different answers — and what actually went live in hospitals this week (Oracle's nursing agent, Sentara's 30-clinician pilot) pushes the same question further: what is this worth, and who gets to say so.

平台擠壓Platform squeeze EpicAI Charting / Art2/05

Epic 把環境式病歷變成 EHR 的內建功能，第三方 scribe 的訂價前提當天消失 Epic turned ambient charting into a built-in EHR feature, and the third-party scribe's pricing premise vanished that day

發生什麼What

2026 年 2 月 5 日，Epic 推出 AI Charting，屬於面向臨床端的 Art 平台；它在看診過程中聆聽、起草病歷、並依對話內容排隊開立醫囑，醫師可以用語音下「把現有病況改成條列」這類格式指令，3 月再加上把評估與計畫綁定特定診斷的 diagnosis-aware notes。Art 裡的病歷摘要功能單月使用次數已超過 1,600 萬次，約為前一年 11 月的三倍。Epic 的三個 AI 代理人分別是給臨床端的 Art、給收入循環的 Penny、給病人的 Emmie，其中 Penny 已在 200 家以上機構使用、宣稱讓拒付減少 20%。 On February 5, 2026, Epic released AI Charting inside Art, its clinician-facing platform. It listens through the visit, drafts the note and queues orders from the conversation, and clinicians can reformat by voice — "make the current conditions a bulleted list" — with diagnosis-aware notes tying assessment and plan to specific diagnoses following in March. The chart-summary feature inside Art already passes 16 million uses a month, roughly triple its level the previous November. Epic's three agents are Art for clinicians, Penny for revenue cycle and Emmie for patients; Penny is in use at more than 200 organisations and is credited with a 20% reduction in denials.

為什麼重要Why it matters

Epic 掌握超過 42% 的美國急症醫院市場與近 55% 的急症病床（KLAS 2024 資料）。當「不必把病人語音送出院外」本身就是一項資安論點、而價格又更低時，獨立 scribe 廠商剩下的空間只有兩種：要嘛做 Epic 不做的深度（特定科別、收入循環、護理），要嘛把價錢跟結果綁在一起。今年下半年這兩條路都被走出來了。 Epic holds more than 42% of the U.S. acute-care hospital market and close to 55% of acute-care beds, on KLAS's 2024 figures. When "patient audio never leaves the building" is itself a security argument and the price is lower too, an independent scribe vendor has only two moves left: go deeper than Epic will bother to go — specialty, revenue cycle, nursing — or tie the price to the outcome. Both routes have now been taken, in the second half of this year.

要打折的地方Discount this

Epic 未公布 AI Charting 的定價，也未公布採用機構家數；Penny 的「拒付減少 20%」出自 Epic 自述，無第三方稽核。1,600 萬次是「使用次數」而非「獨立使用者數」，不能直接換算成醫師採用率。 Epic has published neither pricing nor an adoption count for AI Charting, and Penny's "20% reduction in denials" is Epic's own figure with no independent audit. The 16 million is a count of uses, not of distinct users, and cannot be converted into a clinician adoption rate.

商業模式Business model Ambience HealthcareThe Ambience Standard8/19

Ambience 把軟體費從「用多少算多少」改成「做到才收」，並把五家醫院的數字攤在合約桌上 Ambience moved its software fee from consumption to delivery, and put five health systems' numbers on the contract table

發生什麼What

8 月 19 日 Ambience 發表「The Ambience Standard」，把平台費直接綁在經查核的臨床、營運與財務結果上，取代傳統的每席次或按 token 計費，等於把財務風險從醫院移回廠商身上。架構分三層：一是 value-at-risk 績效合約，費用取決於雙方議定的指標（病歷時間縮減、編碼正確率、利潤改善）；二是派駐醫院內部的 forward-deployed 團隊，由臨床、工程與轉型專員混編；三是名為 Chorus 的代理人調度平台，支援 200 個以上的內外科次專科。公司同時公布五家客戶的查核數字：Ardent Health 3 倍 ROI；Cleveland Clinic 4,000 名臨床人員維持 70% 使用率、NPS 60；Onvida Health 每位醫師年淨值 24,000 美元；MultiCare 採用率 92%、NPS 較競品高 63 分；St. Luke's 醫師燒竭率從 45% 降到 31%、離職意向從 31% 降到 18%。 On August 19 Ambience published "The Ambience Standard," tying platform fees directly to verified clinical, operational and financial outcomes instead of per-seat or per-token billing — which moves the financial risk back from the health system to the vendor. It has three parts: value-at-risk performance contracts whose fees depend on jointly agreed metrics (documentation time, coding accuracy, margin improvement); forward-deployed teams of clinicians, engineers and transformation specialists embedded inside the health system; and Chorus, an agent-orchestration harness covering more than 200 medical and surgical subspecialties. The company published verified numbers from five customers alongside it: 3x ROI at Ardent Health; 70% sustained utilisation across 4,000 clinicians at Cleveland Clinic with an NPS of 60; $24,000 net annual value per physician at Onvida Health; 92% adoption at MultiCare with an NPS 63 points above competitors; and physician burnout falling from 45% to 31% at St. Luke's, with turnover intent down from 31% to 18%.

為什麼重要Why it matters

共同創辦人 Mike Ng 的一句話把這件事講得最清楚：「從一個更好的模型到更好的照護之間，隔著一百萬英里。模型自己不會改善醫療。」這是第一次有頭部環境式 AI 廠商公開承認：模型能力已經不是差異化來源，交付能力才是。值得注意的是它跟付費方的方向完全同調——CMS 的 ACCESS 模式同樣以結果而非服務量計酬。 Co-founder Mike Ng put it plainly: "There's a million miles between a better model and better care. Models don't improve healthcare on their own." This is the first time a leading ambient AI vendor has said out loud that model capability is no longer the differentiator and delivery capability is. It also runs exactly parallel to where the payer is going — CMS's ACCESS model likewise pays on outcomes rather than on services rendered.

要打折的地方Discount this

五家客戶的數字全部由 Ambience 自行揭露，「經查核」的查核方、方法與樣本數皆未公開；value-at-risk 的比例（是把 5% 還是 50% 的費用押上去）也沒有揭露，沒有這個比例，「把風險移回廠商」只是一句話。St. Luke's 的燒竭率變化未提供對照組。 All five customer figures are disclosed by Ambience itself; who verified them, by what method and on what sample is not published. Nor is the share of fee actually at risk — 5% or 50% — and without that number, "the risk moves back to the vendor" is only a sentence. The St. Luke's burnout change comes with no control group.

產品縱深Product depth AbridgeRevenue Cycle / Nursing5/06

Abridge 的答案不是降價，是往 Epic 不會鑽的兩個方向鑽：帳單與護理站 Abridge's answer was not a lower price but two directions Epic will not bother to dig into: the bill, and the nursing station

發生什麼What

Abridge 的收入循環產品把診間對話直接轉成可稽核、可請款的文件，且在照護當下完成而非事後回溯，分成三塊：住院端 CDI（在照護過程中即時提示文件specificity，減少出院後的回溯審查）、門診端 Encounter Integrity（即時產生 ICD-10 與 HCC 碼、自動計算 E&M 等級、在醫師簽名前更新病歷）、以及 Care Signals（看診前先標出風險缺口、追蹤診間實際談到的病況），官方頁面舉 Sutter Health 為例，稱 78% 的臨床人員感到工作滿意度提升。護理端則是另一條線：5 月 6 日 Abridge 宣布與 Mayo Clinic 共同開發的護理 AI 平台在 250 家以上健康系統夥伴普遍可用，Mayo 的病人對環境錄音的同意率落在「百分之八十幾」，Corewell Health 採用率最高的護理師每班省下近 30 分鐘文書時間。 Abridge's revenue-cycle product turns the encounter itself into audit-ready, billable documentation at the point of care rather than retrospectively, in three pieces: inpatient CDI, surfacing documentation specificity in-line during care instead of after discharge; Ambulatory Encounter Integrity, generating ICD-10 and HCC codes in real time, auto-calculating E&M levels and updating the record before the note is signed; and Care Signals, flagging risk gaps before the visit and tracking which conditions were actually discussed. The product page cites Sutter Health, where 78% of clinicians report improved work satisfaction. Nursing is the second line: on May 6 Abridge made the nursing AI platform it built with Mayo Clinic generally available across 250-plus health system partners. Mayo patients opt into ambient recording at rates in the high eighties, and the highest-adopting nurses at Corewell Health save close to 30 minutes of documentation a shift.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這兩條線都不是「更好的病歷」，而是「新的預算科目」。病歷時間省下來的錢很難在財務報表上找到對應欄位，但 CDI 與編碼正確率直接影響營收，護理離職率直接影響人力成本——這是把採購決策從 CMIO 手上，移到 CFO 與護理部主管手上。Abridge 團隊在 Newsweek 的說法點出了護理端的真正難處：「很多護理師已經對科技失去信任……信任是一滴一滴賺來的，一桶一桶失去的。」 Neither line is about a better note; both are about a new budget line. Time saved on charting is hard to locate on a financial statement, whereas CDI and coding accuracy hit revenue directly and nurse turnover hits labour cost directly — which moves the buying decision off the CMIO's desk and onto the CFO's and the chief nursing officer's. Abridge's own framing in Newsweek names the real obstacle on the nursing side: "a lot of nurses have lost trust in tech… trust is earned in drops and lost in buckets."

要打折的地方Discount this

Sutter 的 78% 與 Corewell 的 30 分鐘都是廠商或夥伴自述，非同儕審查。「250 家以上健康系統夥伴」指的是可用範圍而非實際部署量：同一篇報導裡，Mayo 只有 7 個單位完成部署、全院啟用 170 名護理師，Corewell 21 家醫院中僅 2 家在用。把「可用」讀成「已上線」會高估實況一個數量級。 Sutter's 78% and Corewell's 30 minutes are vendor- or partner-reported, not peer-reviewed. "250-plus health system partners" describes availability, not deployment: in the same report Mayo has seven units fully deployed and 170 nurses activated enterprise-wide, and only two of Corewell's 21 hospitals are using it. Reading "available" as "live" overstates the reality by an order of magnitude.

實證落差Evidence gap JAMA5 家醫學中心4/2026

同一個問題，廠商案例說每月 862–1,004 美元，同儕審查研究說 167 美元 Same question: the vendor case studies say $862–$1,004 a month, the peer-reviewed study says $167

發生什麼What

由 UCSF 的 Lisa Rotenstein 主持、發表於 JAMA 的五院研究，涵蓋 Mass General Brigham、Emory Healthcare、UCSF、UC Davis 與 Yale New Haven 共超過 8,500 名臨床人員，其中約 1,800 人實際採用 AI scribe。結果：每 8 小時班別減少 13 分鐘 EHR 時間、16 分鐘文書時間，每週看診量增加 1.7%，換算成 E&M 請款約為每位臨床人員每月多 167 美元；採用率超過 50% 的高頻使用者，EHR 時間減幅為兩倍、文書時間減幅為三倍。對照組是同一批廠商的案例研究：KLAS 在 1 月驗證的 Suki 資料顯示，McLeod Health 每位提供者每月增加 1,004 美元、FMOL Health 每位使用者 862 美元，McLeod 的 Level 3 編碼下降 18.2%、Level 4 與 5 分別上升 7.3% 與 5.0%，看診量增加 18.3%。 The five-site study led by Lisa Rotenstein at UCSF and published in JAMA covers more than 8,500 clinicians at Mass General Brigham, Emory Healthcare, UCSF, UC Davis and Yale New Haven, roughly 1,800 of whom actually adopted an AI scribe. The result: 13 fewer minutes of EHR time and 16 fewer minutes of documentation per eight-hour shift, weekly visit volume up 1.7%, and about $167 per clinician per month in additional evaluation-and-management billing; high-frequency users above 50% adoption saw twice the EHR-time reduction and three times the documentation reduction. Set that against the vendor case studies: the Suki data KLAS validated in January shows $1,004 per provider per month at McLeod Health and $862 per user at FMOL Health, with McLeod's Level 3 codes down 18.2%, Level 4 and 5 up 7.3% and 5.0%, and encounters up 18.3%.

為什麼重要Why it matters

六倍的落差不必然是誰在說謊，更可能是兩件事在量不同的東西：廠商案例通常來自導入最積極、且刻意調整排班以填滿省下時間的組織，而 JAMA 那五家都沒有要求醫師多看病人——作者自己說 167 美元是「保守下界」。但這正是 Ambience 那套 value-at-risk 合約真正的壓力測試：如果醫院照著簽，被押上的就不是模型好不好，而是省下的時間有沒有被組織換成錢。這也是編碼上移（Level 3 降、Level 4/5 升）必須被獨立稽核的理由——同樣的曲線，可以叫做「文件終於反映真實複雜度」，也可以叫做 upcoding。 A sixfold gap need not mean anyone is lying; more likely two things are being measured. Vendor case studies tend to come from the most committed adopters, organisations that deliberately rebooked schedules to fill the time freed up, whereas none of the five JAMA sites asked clinicians to see more patients — the authors call $167 a conservative lower bound. But that is exactly the stress test for Ambience's value-at-risk contracts: sign one, and what is at risk is not whether the model is good but whether the organisation converts saved time into money. It is also why the coding shift — Level 3 down, Level 4 and 5 up — needs independent audit, because the same curve can be called documentation finally reflecting true complexity, or upcoding.

Nurses can search the record in natural language, pull an AI summary of history and current condition, and complete structured documentation in near real time at the bedside. Oracle says its clinical note AI tools have saved physicians more than 400,000 hours across U.S. health organisations in the nearly two years since launch. BayCare Health System is the early adopter; its CIO Lynnette Clinton says it has the potential to reduce after-shift charting. Oracle bought Cerner for $28 billion in June 2022 and rebranded it Oracle Health; more healthcare AI is due at its Health and Life Sciences Summit in Orlando next week.

為什麼重要Why it matters

這則新聞的重點不在功能，在計分單位。Epic 與 Oracle 兩家 EHR 都已經自己做了環境式 AI，而且都還在用「節省小時數」當績效指標——那正是 Ambience 宣稱要離開的那把尺。EHR 內建功能可以用小時計分，因為它本來就不是獨立收費品項；獨立廠商不行，因為 CFO 不會為小時數簽採購單。這條分界線，就是本週整份日報的骨架。 The point here is not the feature set but the unit of account. Both major EHR vendors now build ambient AI themselves, and both still score it in hours saved — the very yardstick Ambience says it is walking away from. A built-in EHR feature can be scored in hours because it was never a separately priced line item; an independent vendor cannot, because no CFO signs a purchase order for hours. That dividing line is the spine of today's report.

要打折的地方Discount this

40 萬小時為 Oracle 自述，未說明分母（多少機構、多少醫師、多少份病歷），也未經第三方驗證；BayCare 目前是唯一具名的導入者，沒有已完成部署的規模數字。 The 400,000 hours is Oracle's own figure, with no denominator given — how many organisations, how many physicians, how many notes — and no third-party verification. BayCare is the only named adopter so far, with no figure for how much of it is actually deployed.

付費方Payer CMS ACCESSOura / Counsel Health9/16–9/17

CMS 把 ACCESS 擴大到心衰竭與 COPD，而第一批以 AI 加穿戴切入的組合已經跑出 36,000 人的上線數字 CMS widened ACCESS to heart failure and COPD, and the first AI-plus-wearable pairing already has 36,000 people onboarded

發生什麼What

9 月 16 日，CMS 宣布把 ACCESS 模式擴大到心衰竭、COPD 等更多慢性病，官員並表示正評估延伸至 Medicaid。隔天，智慧戒指廠 Oura 與 AI 原生的初級照護公司 Counsel Health 宣布以早期心腎代謝（eCKM）軌道參與該模式，頭九週已有 36,000 名 Oura 會員完成上線、上線體驗滿意度 70%、照護體驗評分 4.6 分（滿分 5）、收到症狀警示的會員有 15%（逾 10,000 人）實際接上照護服務。ACCESS 是十年期的聯邦給付計畫，2027 年初上路，依病人結果而非服務量計酬；2026–2027 年要拿到全額給付，須有至少 50% 的歸屬受益人達成全部結果指標，而 3 月公布的給付費率低於業界預期。Oura 目前有 500 萬名付費會員。 On September 16 CMS extended its ACCESS model to heart failure, COPD and other chronic conditions, with officials saying they are weighing an extension into Medicaid. A day later the smart-ring maker Oura and the AI-native primary care company Counsel Health said they would enter the model's early cardio-kidney-metabolic track, reporting that 36,000 Oura members completed onboarding in the first nine weeks, with 70% satisfaction on onboarding, a 4.6-out-of-5 care experience rating, and 15% of members receiving symptom alerts — over 10,000 people — connecting to care. ACCESS is a ten-year federal payment initiative starting in early 2027 that pays on patient outcomes rather than services; for full payment in 2026–2027 at least 50% of aligned beneficiaries must hit every required outcome target, and the rates published in March came in below industry expectations. 對醫師免費、靠廣告獲利的臨床答案引擎A clinical answer engine, free to doctors and funded by ads		 1 月 D 輪 2.5 億美元、估值 120 億美元，一年三輪約 7 億美元；年營收破 1 億美元；2025 年 12 月單月約 1,800 萬次臨床諮詢；逾 40% 美國醫師每日使用、覆蓋一萬家以上醫院（Fierce Healthcare）。 A $250 million Series D in January at a $12 billion valuation, roughly $700 million across three rounds in a year; annual revenue past $100 million; about 18 million clinical consultations in December 2025 alone; over 40% of U.S. physicians using it daily across more than 10,000 hospitals (Fierce Healthcare). 護城河是醫師心佔率與 AMA、NEJM 的內容授權，完全繞開醫院採購。弱點是廣告支撐的臨床決策工具本身的利益衝突，目前無監管框架可依。 The moat is physician mindshare plus content licensing from the AMA and NEJM, bypassing hospital procurement entirely. The weakness is the conflict of interest inherent in an ad-funded clinical decision tool, for which no regulatory framework exists.
Suki
以第三方驗證 ROI 為主打的中型挑戰者A mid-sized challenger leading with third-party-validated ROI		 KLAS 於 1 月驗證三家客戶成果：McLeod Health 文件時間減 26.8%、每位提供者每月增收 1,004 美元、看診量增 18.3%、擴至 249 名使用者；FMOL Health 減 21%、每位使用者 862 美元、有機成長 22%；Rush 當日結案率改善 5.5%、使用者逾 240 名（HIT Consultant）。 KLAS validated results at three customers in January: McLeod Health cut documentation time 26.8%, added $1,004 per provider per month, raised encounters 18.3% and scaled to 249 users; FMOL Health cut 21%, added $862 per user and grew organically 22%; Rush improved same-day chart closure 5.5% across more than 240 users (HIT Consultant). 護城河是願意讓第三方揭露細顆粒度 ROI 的透明度，這在目前市場是稀缺品。弱點是規模：249 名與 240 名使用者的量級，跟 Kaiser 的 24,600 名醫師不在同一張表上。 The moat is a willingness to let a third party publish granular ROI, which is scarce in this market. The weakness is scale: 249 and 240 users are not on the same page as Kaiser's 24,600 physicians.

今天的競爭結構是一個夾心。上層是 Epic 與 Oracle，用分發權把環境式文件變成內建功能、用「小時數」自證；下層是 OpenEvidence，用免費與廣告繞開採購、把醫師注意力變成商品。被夾在中間的獨立廠商只剩兩種活法：Ambience 選擇把費用押在結果上，賭自己的交付能力；Abridge 選擇往 Epic 懶得去的縱深走，賭 CFO 與護理部的預算。兩種賭注在 2026 年都還沒開牌，而 JAMA 那份每月 167 美元提醒的是：如果多數醫院最終落在同儕審查的那個數字附近，而不是廠商案例的六倍，那麼今天所有人的訂價都太高。 Today's competitive structure is a sandwich. On top sit Epic and Oracle, using distribution to make ambient documentation a built-in feature and justifying it in hours saved. Underneath sits OpenEvidence, free and ad-funded, skipping procurement and turning physician attention into the product. The independent vendors caught in between have two ways to live: Ambience stakes its fee on outcomes and bets on its own delivery; Abridge digs into the depth Epic cannot be bothered with and bets on the CFO's and the nursing department's budget. Neither bet has been settled in 2026 — and the $167 a month in that JAMA paper is the reminder that if most hospitals land near the peer-reviewed number rather than six times it, then everyone's price today is too high.

04 — Taiwan Angle

台灣其實已經把「結果決定給付」寫進制度，缺的是能被稽核的本土數字 Taiwan has already written outcome-determines-payment into its institutions; what is missing is auditable local numbers

(1) 我們比美國更早有了「結果換給付」的機構，但還沒有資料餵它。衛福部的三大 AI 中心裡，AI 影響性研究中心的任務被寫成「跨院收集臨床成效與成本資料，進行健康經濟分析與保險定價，加速健保納入給付」——這正是 Ambience 的 value-at-risk 合約在私部門要做的同一件事，只是台灣把它放在公部門。另外兩個中心分別處理落地（負責任 AI 執行中心）與取證驗證（臨床 AI 取證驗證中心），三者對應的是同一組問題：落地、驗證、給付。制度先行的好處是，本土廠商不必像美國同業那樣自己發明查核機制；但反過來說，只要沒有跨院的臨床成效與成本資料，這個中心就無法定價。 (1) Taiwan built the outcome-for-payment institution earlier than the U.S. did, and still has to feed it data. Among the Ministry of Health and Welfare's three AI centres, the Center for Clinical AI Impact Evaluation is chartered to collect clinical effectiveness and cost data across hospitals, run health-economics analysis and insurance pricing, and accelerate National Health Insurance coverage — precisely what Ambience's value-at-risk contracts are trying to do in the private sector, except that Taiwan put it in the public one. The other two handle implementation (Responsible AI) and external validation (Clinical AI Validation), and together they address one set of problems: deployment, validation, reimbursement. The advantage of leading with institutions is that local vendors need not invent an audit mechanism the way their American counterparts must. The flip side is that without cross-hospital effectiveness and cost data, the centre cannot price anything.

(2) FHIR Box 是那份資料的前置工程，年底見真章。衛福部資訊處推動的 FHIR Box 預計今年底上線，目前三家醫學中心試辦，目標是全台醫學中心病歷互通，並在三到五年內推及約八成醫療院所。負責人李建璋的說法是，台灣選了第三條路——國家提供平台與轉換工具，而不是換掉所有電子病歷系統，也不跟隨歐盟的十年計畫；他給的理由很直白：「數據是所有 AI 發展的石油。」把這件事跟今天的主題接起來看：美國廠商能公開 3 倍 ROI、45%→31% 燒竭率這種數字，是因為 Epic 生態裡的資料已經對齊了；台灣的本土環境式 AI 廠商要拿出同等級的舉證，必須等 FHIR Box 把分母準備好。 (2) FHIR Box is the groundwork for that data, and it is due by year-end. The Ministry's information department expects FHIR Box to go live at the end of this year, with three medical centres piloting, aiming for record interoperability across all of Taiwan's medical centres and roughly 80% of healthcare institutions within three to five years. Lee Chien-chang's framing is that Taiwan took a third road — the state supplies the platform and the conversion tools, rather than replacing every EHR or following the EU's ten-year plan; his reason is blunt: "data is the oil of all AI development." Connect that to today's theme: American vendors can publish 3x ROI and burnout falling from 45% to 31% because the data inside the Epic ecosystem is already aligned. A Taiwanese ambient AI vendor cannot produce evidence of that grade until FHIR Box has prepared the denominator.

(3) 給台灣醫院採購者的一句實話。本篇最值得帶走的數字不是任何一家廠商的成功案例，而是 JAMA 五院研究裡「沒有任何一家機構要求醫師多看病人」這個設計細節。廠商案例的六倍收益，多半來自組織主動把省下的時間換成診次；台灣的健保支付結構與門診量壓力跟美國的 E&M 分級制度不同，直接把 862 到 1,004 美元換算成新台幣去做投報估算，會嚴重高估。合理的做法是先問：省下來的時間，我們打算拿去做什麼？如果答案是「讓醫師早點下班」，那這筆錢的回收就不會出現在財報上——那仍然可能是對的決定，但要用留任率而不是 ROI 去核算。 (3) One plain sentence for Taiwanese hospital buyers. The figure worth taking from this report is not any vendor's success story but a design detail inside the JAMA five-site study: none of the institutions required clinicians to book additional patients. The sixfold gain in vendor case studies comes largely from organisations that actively converted saved time into extra encounters. Taiwan's reimbursement structure and outpatient volume pressures differ from the American E&M levelling system, so converting $862 to $1,004 straight into New Taiwan dollars for a payback estimate will badly overstate the case. The right first question is what the saved time is for. If the answer is letting clinicians go home earlier, the return will not appear on a financial statement — which may still be the right decision, but it should be accounted for in retention, not ROI.
