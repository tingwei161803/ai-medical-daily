AI 心臟影像在同一週交出三種等級的證據：隨機試驗的 win ratio 2.00、登錄研究的「少 34%」、回溯佇列的 0.004 Three grades of evidence for cardiac AI in one week: a trial's win ratio of 2.00, a registry's "34% fewer", and a cohort's 0.004

2026 年 11 月 9 日（一）· 涵蓋 11/02–11/09 · 台北時間 Monday, November 9, 2026 · Covering Nov 2–9 · Taipei time

本週的臨床 AI 新聞幾乎全部擠在芝加哥。AHA Scientific Sessions 2026 於 11 月 6–9 日舉行，而三件事在同一個窗口裡發生：11/7 Cleerly 的 PARAMOUNT 成為第一個把 AI 定量冠狀動脈電腦斷層（AI-QCT）導向照護拿去隨機對照的試驗，180 天分層複合終點 win ratio 2.00（95% CI 1.37–2.56，p<0.0001）；11/2 Heartflow 的 DECIDE 登錄研究用 6,000 多名配對病人報出一年少 34% 主要心血管事件，卻一個信賴區間都沒附；而 Lancet Digital Health 的 27,382 人回溯研究把 AI 與醫師的差距量到小數第三位：校正後 C-index 0.781 對 0.777。三個數字一起看，今天真正的主題不是「AI 行不行」，而是「你願意用哪一級的證據換哪一級的結論」。 Almost all of this week's clinical AI news was crowded into Chicago. AHA Scientific Sessions 2026 ran November 6–9, and three things landed inside the same window. On Nov 7, Cleerly's PARAMOUNT became the first randomised trial of care guided by AI quantitative coronary CT (AI-QCT), with a win ratio of 2.00 on its 180-day hierarchical composite (95% CI 1.37–2.56, p<0.0001). On Nov 2, Heartflow's DECIDE registry reported 34% fewer major cardiovascular events at one year across more than 6,000 matched patients, without attaching a single confidence interval. And a 27,382-patient retrospective in The Lancet Digital Health measured the gap between AI and clinicians to three decimal places: an adjusted C-index of 0.781 against 0.777. Read together, today's subject is not whether AI works, but which grade of evidence you are willing to trade for which grade of conclusion.

PARAMOUNT：第一個把 AI 斑塊導向照護隨機化的試驗，win ratio 2.00，但資料自己說「尚未同儕審查」 PARAMOUNT: the first randomised trial of AI plaque-based care, win ratio 2.00 — and the data says of itself that it is not peer-reviewed

發生什麼What

11 月 7 日 AHA 2026 late-breaking session，維吉尼亞大學心臟科教授 Todd C. Villines 發表 PARAMOUNT（NCT06713239）：前瞻、多中心、開放標籤隨機試驗，21 個中心收案 530 人隨機化（2025 年 1 月至 2026 年 2 月），523 人納入初步分析。受試者是有症狀、懷疑冠狀動脈疾病、LDL-C ≥ 100 mg/dL 且無 CAD 病史的門診病人；中位年齡 57 歲、57% 為女性（300 人）、基線 LDL-C 中位數 127–128 mg/dL。AI-QCT 導向的斑塊照護對照「以功能性運動測試為基礎的常規照護」，主要終點是 180 天的預設分層複合終點，結果 win ratio 2.00（95% CI 1.37–2.56，p<0.0001）。未配對比較下，斑塊導向組病人有較好結果的比例為 46.7%，常規照護組 23.4%（HCPLive, 2026-11-07；Business Wire 新聞稿, 2026-11-07）。 On November 7, in an AHA 2026 late-breaking session, Todd C. Villines of the University of Virginia presented PARAMOUNT (NCT06713239): a prospective, multicentre, open-label randomised trial that enrolled 530 patients at 21 sites between January 2025 and February 2026, with 523 in the preliminary analysis. Participants were symptomatic outpatients with suspected coronary artery disease, LDL-C of at least 100 mg/dL and no prior CAD. Median age was 57, 57% were women (300), and median baseline LDL-C was 127–128 mg/dL. AI-QCT-guided plaque-based care was compared with usual care built on functional stress testing. The primary endpoint, a prespecified hierarchical composite at 180 days, gave a win ratio of 2.00 (95% CI 1.37–2.56, p<0.0001). In unmatched comparisons the plaque-based patient had the better outcome 46.7% of the time against 23.4% for usual care (HCPLive, 2026-11-07; Business Wire release, 2026-11-07).

為什麼重要Why it matters

AI 影像產品過去十年幾乎全靠「讀片準確度」與觀察性登錄研究立足，1,357 個 FDA 核准的 AI 裝置裡只有 3 個測過病人結果。PARAMOUNT 第一次把這類工具放進隨機框架，而且它證明的不是「AI 看得更準」，是「照著 AI 看到的斑塊去改處方與改決策，病人的綜合處境更好」——降脂藥使用率從 31% 升到 75%（常規照護組 32%→47%），做了侵入性冠狀動脈攝影的人裡有 38% 最後沒做血管重建（常規照護組 60%），也就是少挨了不必要的介入。對終端的意義是：給付方第一次有東西可以談。 For a decade, AI imaging products have stood almost entirely on reading accuracy and observational registries — of 1,357 FDA-cleared AI devices, only three had tested patient outcomes. PARAMOUNT is the first to put such a tool inside a randomised frame, and what it demonstrates is not that AI reads better but that acting on the plaque AI sees changes prescriptions and decisions for the better: lipid-lowering therapy rose from 31% to 75% against 32% to 47% in usual care, and among patients taken to invasive angiography, 38% left without revascularisation against 60% — that is, fewer unnecessary interventions. The practical consequence is that payers finally have something to negotiate over.

要打折的地方Discount this

三件事必須扣分。第一，開放標籤：病人與醫師都知道自己在哪一組，而主要終點裡「診斷確定性」這一層（貢獻最大，win difference 16.6 個百分點）本質上是主觀判斷。第二，試驗由 Cleerly 自己贊助，Villines 本人揭露為 Cleerly、HeartFlow 與 Milestone 擔任顧問。第三，新聞稿明確寫了這是 preliminary、尚未同儕審查，完整結果要等 2027 年初；而 HCPLive 的報導把「診斷確定性的貢獻」寫成「win ratio 16.6%」——win ratio 不是百分比，這個數字在正式論文出來前不該被引用。另外，以 530 人、180 天的規模，這個試驗沒有能力談死亡或心肌梗塞。 Three deductions are mandatory. First, the trial was open-label: patients and physicians knew their arm, and the layer of the composite that contributed most — diagnostic certainty, a win difference of 16.6 percentage points — is a subjective judgement. Second, Cleerly sponsored the trial, and Villines discloses consulting for Cleerly, HeartFlow and Milestone. Third, the release states plainly that the data are preliminary and not yet peer-reviewed, with full results due in early 2027; HCPLive also renders the diagnostic-certainty contribution as "a win ratio of 16.6%", and since a win ratio is not a percentage, that figure should not be quoted before the paper. And at 530 patients over 180 days, the trial is not powered to say anything about death or myocardial infarction.

27,382 人、追蹤 5.6 年：AI 左心室分割預測死亡只比醫師好 0.004，但快了 200 倍 27,382 patients, 5.6 years of follow-up: AI left-ventricular segmentation beats clinicians by 0.004 — and is 200 times faster

發生什麼What

Joy G 等人在 The Lancet Digital Health 發表〈Performance of artificial intelligence versus clinician left-ventricular segmentation of cardiac MRI for mortality prediction〉（DOI 10.1016/j.landig.2026.101042），研究團隊來自 UCL 與 City St George's（倫敦），11 月 6 日由 News-Medical 整理報導。回溯、多中心、預後型研究，收入英國與美國兩個中心的 27,382 名做過心臟 MRI 的病人，中位追蹤 5.6 年、13% 死亡（另一份報導寫 3,458 人死亡）。結果：以射出分率預測死亡的 C-index，AI 0.669 對臨床醫師 0.637；校正年齡與性別後，AI 0.781 對臨床醫師 0.777。射出分率中位數 AI 57.8%、醫師 62.1%。速度上 AI 每人約 3–4 秒，而同團隊先前量到專家分割中位數 13 分鐘；600 人隨機抽樣中有 6 例（1%）分割錯誤（News-Medical, 2026-11-06；Resultsense, 2026-11-03）。 Joy G and colleagues published "Performance of artificial intelligence versus clinician left-ventricular segmentation of cardiac MRI for mortality prediction" in The Lancet Digital Health (DOI 10.1016/j.landig.2026.101042), from UCL and City St George's in London, written up by News-Medical on November 6. It is a retrospective, multicentre prognostic study of 27,382 patients who had cardiac MRI at two centres, one British and one American, with a median follow-up of 5.6 years and 13% mortality (another report counts 3,458 deaths). The mortality C-index from ejection fraction was 0.669 for AI against 0.637 for clinicians; adjusted for age and sex, 0.781 against 0.777. Median ejection fraction was 57.8% by AI and 62.1% by clinicians. AI took about 3–4 seconds per patient, against a median of 13 minutes for expert segmentation measured by the same group earlier; in a random sample of 600 patients, 6 (1%) had segmentation errors (News-Medical, 2026-11-06; Resultsense, 2026-11-03).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是今天最誠實的一份資料，因為它量出的差距小得不好賣。校正後 0.004 的 C-index 差異，用任何臨床標準看都不是「AI 比醫師強」；真正的賣點是 3–4 秒對 13 分鐘，也就是把 AI 當第一線量測、醫師做監督的流程價值，而不是取代。更有意思的是次族群：阻塞性冠狀動脈疾病病人身上 AI 模型配適更好、加進 AI 量測有增益；非缺血性心肌症增益縮小；而肥厚型心肌症（HCM）次族群裡醫師的射出分率反而略勝，作者推測 AI 沒能完整處理肌小梁增生與乳頭肌肥大。這正是「平均表現好」與「我這個病人能不能用」之間的落差。 This is the most honest dataset of the day, because the gap it measures is too small to sell. An adjusted C-index difference of 0.004 is not, by any clinical standard, AI beating doctors. The real pitch is 3–4 seconds against 13 minutes — the value of using AI as the first-line measurement under clinician oversight, not as a replacement. The subgroups are more interesting still: in obstructive coronary artery disease the AI model fit better and added information; in non-ischaemic cardiomyopathy the advantage shrank; and in the hypertrophic cardiomyopathy subgroup the clinicians' ejection fraction performed slightly better, which the authors attribute to the AI not fully handling hypertrabeculation and enlarged papillary muscles. That is precisely the distance between good average performance and usable for this patient.

要打折的地方Discount this

回溯設計、沒有死因分類資料、臨床醫師的分割手法可能不一致、未評估校準度（calibration）、未評估對臨床決策或病人結果的影響、未評估不同族群表現；另外多數受試者來自同一個英國中心，而數位作者揭露與 MycardiumAI 有財務關係——該公司與這項分割技術相關，資深作者 Rhodri Davies 同時是工具發明人。經費來自 NIHR 與 British Heart Foundation，不是廠商。 Retrospective design, no cause-specific mortality, possible inconsistency in clinician segmentation technique, no assessment of calibration, no assessment of effects on clinical decisions or patient outcomes, and no evaluation by ethnic group. Most participants also came from a single UK centre, and several authors disclose financial relationships with MycardiumAI, the company tied to the segmentation technology; senior author Rhodri Davies also invented the tool. Funding came from the NIHR and the British Heart Foundation rather than a vendor.

發生什麼What

11 月 2 日，Heartflow（Nasdaq: HTFL）發布 AHA 2026 資料：前瞻性 DECIDE 登錄研究納入超過 6,000 名良好配對的病人，比較「冠狀動脈 CT 加 Heartflow Plaque Analysis」與「單用冠狀動脈 CT」。一年結果：主要心血管事件（心血管死亡、心肌梗塞、血管重建或中風）少 34%、心肌梗塞發生率減半；90 天內逾 50% 的病人用藥被改、50% 達到 LDL-C < 70 mg/dL（單用 CT 組 35%）、達標人數早期增加 41%、有用藥調整者 LDL-C 平均降 18.7 mg/dL，並維持到一年。共同主持人為西奈山 Icahn 醫學院的 Leslee Shaw，11 月 9 日 11:06（中部時間）發表。同一份新聞稿還帶了 GAMEFILM 登錄研究：200 名退役 NFL 球員中 85% 驗出斑塊、平均近 90% 為軟性（非鈣化）斑塊、42% 有阻塞性 CAD（>50% 狹窄）、29% 有功能顯著缺血，但不到 10% 有運動時症狀；進攻／防守線鋒的斑塊體積年齡百分位為 63.3，非線鋒 31.9（GlobeNewswire, 2026-11-02）。 On November 2, Heartflow (Nasdaq: HTFL) released its AHA 2026 data. The prospective DECIDE registry enrolled more than 6,000 well-matched patients and compared coronary CT plus Heartflow Plaque Analysis against coronary CT alone. At one year: 34% fewer major cardiovascular events (cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, revascularisation or stroke) and half the rate of myocardial infarction. By 90 days, management had changed in more than 50% of patients, 50% reached LDL-C below 70 mg/dL against 35% with CT alone, the number at goal rose 41% early, and those whose management changed saw LDL-C fall by an average of 18.7 mg/dL, sustained at one year. Co-lead investigator Leslee Shaw of the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai presented it on November 9 at 11:06 CT. The same release carried the GAMEFILM registry: among 200 retired NFL players, 85% had detectable plaque, on average nearly 90% of it soft (non-calcified), 42% had obstructive CAD (over 50% stenosis) and 29% had functionally significant ischaemia, while fewer than 10% reported exertional symptoms; linemen sat at the 63.3rd percentile of plaque volume for their age against the 31.9th for non-linemen (GlobeNewswire, 2026-11-02).

為什麼重要Why it matters

把 DECIDE 跟 PARAMOUNT 並排看，就看懂這個市場的證據策略分歧了：Cleerly 花兩年做 530 人的隨機試驗，換到一個有信賴區間的 p 值但只能談 180 天的過程指標；Heartflow 用 6,000 多人的登錄研究，換到「少 34% 事件、心肌梗塞減半」這種可以直接進業務簡報的硬結果，代價是配對而非隨機、無法排除殘餘混淆。兩家都是 FDA 核准產品、都在同一個會場、都在搶同一批心臟科醫師與同一批給付碼，而它們選了相反的路徑。 Place DECIDE beside PARAMOUNT and the market's split evidence strategy becomes legible. Cleerly spent two years on a 530-patient randomised trial and bought a p-value with a confidence interval that can speak only to 180-day process measures. Heartflow used a 6,000-patient registry and bought hard outcomes — 34% fewer events, half the heart attacks — that drop straight into a sales deck, at the cost of matching rather than randomisation and of residual confounding it cannot exclude. Both products are FDA-cleared, both were at the same meeting, both are chasing the same cardiologists and the same reimbursement codes, and they chose opposite routes.

要打折的地方Discount this

新聞稿完全沒有提供 hazard ratio、信賴區間或 p 值，也沒說明配對方法、納入排除條件與中心數；「良好配對」是廠商自述。GAMEFILM 的 200 人是自願受測的退役球員，選樣偏誤極大，而「88% 未服用降脂藥者在看過結果後願意開始治療」是意願而非行為。另外新聞稿裡的 Plaque Analysis「與 IVUS 有 95% 一致性」、Plaque Staging「在 23,000 名病人、最長 16 年追蹤中驗證」、「1,800 多家機構、逾 750,000 名病人、625 篇以上同儕審查論文」全部是公司自述的行銷數字，本報未另行查證。 The release provides no hazard ratios, confidence intervals or p-values, and says nothing about the matching method, inclusion and exclusion criteria, or number of sites; "well-matched" is the vendor's word. GAMEFILM's 200 retired players volunteered, so selection bias is severe, and "88% of those not on lipid-lowering therapy were willing to start" is an intention, not a behaviour. The release's further claims — Plaque Analysis at 95% agreement with IVUS, Plaque Staging validated in 23,000 patients with up to 16 years of follow-up, more than 1,800 institutions, over 750,000 patients and more than 625 peer-reviewed publications — are all company marketing figures that this report has not independently verified.

發生什麼What

AHA 2026 摘要 MDP2290（海報 P6539），來自杜克大學醫學院與杜克臨床研究所影像核心實驗室，主持人 Gerald S. Bloomfield，屬 RURAL（Risk Underlying Rural Areas Longitudinal）研究的一部分。14 名非超音波技師（護理師、護理助理、電腦斷層技術員）完成兩小時、四段式的線上加實作 AI 導引超音波課程後，在一台行動醫療車上，於 2021 年 11 月至 2026 年 6 月走訪阿拉巴馬、肯塔基、路易斯安那與密西西比十個偏鄉郡，為 4,597 名自願參加的 25–65 歲成人（平均 48 歲、67% 女性）各做一次 3.5 小時的檢查。結果：98% 的檢查包含全部六個必要視圖、核心實驗室專家評定逾 99% 的檢查足以量測、單一視圖的診斷級品質率介於 77% 至 94%、81% 在 15 分鐘目標內完成、中位掃描時間 9.5 分鐘；133 份檢查發現危急結果（最常見為射出分率低於 50%），其中 99% 在三天內回報給受檢者。有三名操作者在首批檢查被審閱後需要再訓練（American Heart Association 新聞稿, 2026-11-02）。 AHA 2026 abstract MDP2290 (poster P6539), from Duke University School of Medicine and the Duke Clinical Research Institute Imaging Core Laboratory, with principal investigator Gerald S. Bloomfield, is part of the RURAL (Risk Underlying Rural Areas Longitudinal) study. Fourteen non-sonographers — nurses, nursing assistants and CT technicians — completed a two-hour, four-part virtual and hands-on course in AI-guided ultrasound, then worked from a mobile health unit that visited ten rural counties in Alabama, Kentucky, Louisiana and Mississippi from November 2021 to June 2026, scanning 4,597 volunteer adults aged 25–65 (mean age 48, 67% women) in one 3.5-hour appointment each. Results: 98% of studies contained all six required views, core-lab experts rated more than 99% adequate for measurement, diagnostic-quality rates for individual views ranged from 77% to 94%, 81% finished inside the 15-minute target, and median scan time was 9.5 minutes. 133 studies turned up critical findings, most often an ejection fraction below 50%, and 99% of those were returned to participants within three days. Three operators needed retraining after their first scans were reviewed (American Heart Association release, 2026-11-02).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這是今天唯一一則把 AI 的價值放在「誰能做」而不是「做得多準」的研究，也因此是對台灣最有參考價值的一則。心臟超音波的瓶頸從來不是判讀演算法，是會握探頭的人；而這份資料說的是「兩小時訓練 + AI 即時導引 + 離線核心實驗室覆核」這三件事加起來，就足以讓非專業人員在沒有心臟科的地方產出可量測的影像。133 份危急結果、99% 三天內回報，等於在十個郡裡做出了一條原本不存在的篩檢路徑。 This is the only study today that locates AI's value in who can do the scan rather than how accurately it is read, which also makes it the most transferable one. The bottleneck in echocardiography has never been the interpretation algorithm; it is finding someone who can hold the probe. What this dataset says is that two hours of training, real-time AI guidance and off-site core-lab review, taken together, are enough for non-specialists to produce measurable images in places with no cardiology service. The 133 critical findings, 99% of them returned within three days, amount to a screening pathway that did not previously exist across ten counties.

要打折的地方Discount this

作者自列的限制很完整：沒有與超音波技師取得的影像直接對照、量測再現性只在內部評估而未與其他核心實驗室驗證、同一受檢者由不同操作者掃描的變異未系統性評估、只用了一個軟體品牌與一個核心實驗室（新聞稿未揭露品牌名）、可能無法外推到非偏鄉族群或研究核心實驗室架構之外的場景，而且沒有評估回報結果是否改變了受檢者的照護或健康。這是一份摘要，尚未同儕審查。 The authors' own limitations are thorough: no direct comparison with images acquired by sonographers, reproducibility assessed internally rather than against other core labs, no systematic assessment of between-operator variability on the same participant, only one software brand and one core lab (the release does not name the brand), likely limited generalisability beyond rural populations or outside a research core-lab setting, and no assessment of whether returning results changed participants' care or health. This is an abstract and has not been peer-reviewed.

The company reports more than 1,800 institutions, over 750,000 patients and more than 625 peer-reviewed publications, and DECIDE alone drew on more than 6,000 matched patients — a volume that lets it report outcomes like 34% fewer major cardiovascular events and half the myocardial infarctions at one year, which Cleerly's trial is not yet sized to produce (GlobeNewswire, 2026-11-02).
  • 優勢Strength ：產品線縱深。同一份新聞稿還帶出 TCT 2026 的 P4 試驗——PCI Navigator 改變了約 70% 病人的介入計畫，意思是 Heartflow 已經從「診斷前的風險分層」延伸到「導管室裡的手術規劃」，而 Cleerly 目前仍集中在前者（GlobeNewswire, 2026-11-02）。 : depth of product line. The same release carries the P4 trial from TCT 2026, in which PCI Navigator changed the intervention plan in about 70% of patients — meaning Heartflow has extended from pre-diagnostic risk stratification into procedural planning in the cath lab, where Cleerly remains concentrated on the former (GlobeNewswire, 2026-11-02).
  • 疑慮Concern ：DECIDE 是配對的登錄研究而非隨機試驗，而且新聞稿連一個 hazard ratio、信賴區間或 p 值都沒給，也沒說配對用了哪些變項。「34%」在這種設計下無法排除殘餘混淆——會被選去做斑塊分析的病人，本來就可能是醫師更積極照顧的那一群。 : DECIDE is a matched registry, not a randomised trial, and the release carries no hazard ratio, confidence interval or p-value, nor the variables used for matching. Under that design the 34% cannot exclude residual confounding — the patients selected for plaque analysis may simply be the ones their physicians were already treating more aggressively.
  • 疑慮Concern ：GAMEFILM 這類高曝光度研究（200 名退役 NFL 球員、85% 驗出斑塊）行銷效果極強但科學價值有限——自願受測、無對照組、無結果追蹤。把它與 DECIDE 放在同一份新聞稿裡發布，會讓讀者把兩者的證據強度混為一談。另外 Q3 財報定於 11 月 12 日公布，屆時才看得到這些臨床資料有沒有轉成營收。 : high-visibility studies like GAMEFILM — 200 retired NFL players, 85% with detectable plaque — market extremely well but carry limited scientific weight: volunteers, no control arm, no outcome follow-up. Publishing it in the same release as DECIDE invites readers to conflate the two evidence grades. Q3 results are due on November 12, which is when it becomes visible whether any of this clinical data has turned into revenue.

04 — Taiwan Angle

台灣的醫療 AI 制度已經在管「怎麼證明」，缺的是「誰付錢」那一段 Taiwan's medical AI framework already governs how to prove it works; what is missing is who pays

(1) 偏鄉心臟超音波：RURAL 這份資料幾乎是為台灣寫的。杜克的做法是「兩小時訓練的非技師 + AI 即時導引 + 離線核心實驗室覆核」，在十個沒有心臟科的郡裡做出 4,597 份合格檢查、揪出 133 份危急結果（AHA, 2026-11-02）。台灣的對應問題不是沒有機器，是離島與山地鄉沒有會做心臟超音波的人；而「核心實驗室覆核」這一環，台灣其實有現成基礎建設——衛福部正在推的新一代 3D PACS 與 FHIR Box（把醫院資料轉成 HL7 FHIR 而不必換掉核心系統）就是為了跨院影像調閱與傳輸（聯合報, 2026-10-05）。真正要補的是「誰覆核、覆核多久內回報、回報後誰接手」這條責任鍊，而 RURAL 的 99% 三天內回報，就是這條鍊的規格書。 (1) Rural echocardiography: the RURAL dataset reads as if it were written for Taiwan. Duke's recipe — non-sonographers with two hours of training, real-time AI guidance, off-site core-lab review — produced 4,597 usable studies across ten counties with no cardiology service and surfaced 133 critical findings (AHA, 2026-11-02). Taiwan's equivalent problem is not a shortage of machines but the absence, on the outlying islands and in mountain townships, of anyone who can perform a cardiac ultrasound. The core-lab review link, meanwhile, already has infrastructure under construction: the Ministry of Health and Welfare's new-generation 3D PACS and FHIR Box, which converts hospital data into HL7 FHIR without replacing core systems, exist precisely for cross-institutional image retrieval and transfer (United Daily News, 2026-10-05). What still has to be built is the chain of responsibility — who reviews, within what turnaround, and who takes over afterwards — and RURAL's 99% returned within three days is the specification for it.

(2) 「負責任 AI」這套制度，正好對上 FDA 11/5 那張特別管制清單。衛福部設有三個中心處理醫療 AI 的落地、取證與給付：負責任 AI 執行中心、臨床 AI 取證驗證中心、AI 影響性研究中心。其中負責任 AI 執行中心已在 10 家醫院完成認證制度建置（林口長庚、台北榮總、高醫大附設中和醫院、中國醫藥大學附設醫院、彰基、奇美、聯新國際、嘉義長庚、土城長庚、新北仁康），架構包含九項透明性原則、資安與隱私保護、AI 全生命週期管理，衛福部並宣稱已部署至少 10 項負責任 AI 應用、使用量達 229,086 次（自由健康網）。把這套東西跟 FDA 新類別的特別管制並排看，台灣在「使用端治理」上其實走得比美國早——美國那張清單管的是廠商送審時要交什麼，台灣這套管的是醫院用起來要負什麼責。兩者缺的是同一塊：測試資料集是否獨立於訓練資料這種技術性驗證，台灣的九項透明性原則沒有逐條規定。 (2) Taiwan's "responsible AI" framework lines up precisely against the FDA's special-controls list of November 5. The Ministry of Health and Welfare runs three centres covering deployment, evidence and reimbursement for medical AI: the Responsible AI Execution Center, the Clinical AI Evidence Validation Center and the AI Impact Research Center. The first has completed a certification framework across ten hospitals — Linkou Chang Gung, Taipei Veterans General, Kaohsiung Medical University's Chung-Ho Memorial, China Medical University Hospital, Changhua Christian, Chi Mei, Lien Hwa International, Chiayi Chang Gung, Tucheng Chang Gung and Xinbei Renkang — built on nine transparency principles, information security and privacy protection, and lifecycle management, with the ministry reporting at least ten deployed responsible-AI applications and 229,086 uses (Liberty Times Health). Set that beside the new US device type's special controls and Taiwan is in fact ahead on governance at the point of use: the American list governs what a manufacturer files for review, while Taiwan's governs what a hospital answers for once it is running. Both are missing the same piece — technical validation such as whether the test dataset is independent of the training data is not spelled out line by line in those nine transparency principles.

(3) 給付這一段，台灣已經在明講。台中榮總榮譽院長陳適安在 10 月那場 3D 影像會議上直接點出：要普及，得先有服務與支付機制，有些醫院已經申請 3D 影像的自費收費碼，若能接上健保計畫才可能擴大使用；台大醫院院長余忠仁則說 2028 年要完成全院 AI 驅動的 3D 影像自動轉換架構；智捷醫學科技的 Anatomy Cloud AI 把 2D 影像自動轉成 3D 解剖模型約需 5 分鐘，該公司捐出 10 台工作站供衛福部布建示範點（聯合報, 2026-10-05）。而 11 月 20 日成功大學將舉行「AI 驅動．健康台灣」論壇，健保署長陳亮妤將就健保數位轉型、慢性病照護與健康平權發表專題演講（蕃新聞, 2026-11-05）。這一條跟今天的國際新聞是同一件事：Cleerly 做隨機試驗、Heartflow 堆登錄研究，爭的都是給付碼；台灣的差別只在於，這場談判在制度上還沒有一張對應的「臨床效益換給付」的表。 (3) On reimbursement, Taiwan is already saying it out loud. At October's 3D imaging conference, Taichung Veterans General Hospital honorary president Chen Shih-an said plainly that wider adoption requires a service and payment mechanism: some hospitals have applied for self-pay billing codes for 3D imaging, and only linking these to National Health Insurance programmes could expand use. National Taiwan University Hospital president Yu Chung-jen said the hospital expects to complete an AI-driven automated 3D imaging conversion framework across the whole institution by 2028. Chijie Medical Technology's Anatomy Cloud AI converts 2D scans into 3D anatomical models in about five minutes, and the company donated ten workstations for the ministry to deploy as demonstration units (United Daily News, 2026-10-05). On November 20, National Cheng Kung University hosts the "AI-Driven, Healthy Taiwan" summit, where National Health Insurance Administration director Chen Liang-yu will give a keynote on digital transformation of the insurance system, chronic disease care and health equity (Yam News, 2026-11-05). This is the same fight as today's international news: Cleerly runs a trial and Heartflow stacks registries, and both are competing for a payment code. The difference in Taiwan is only that the negotiation still has no institutional table on which clinical benefit is exchanged for reimbursement.
