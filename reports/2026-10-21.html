主線Lead AHAFDA CDRH10/19

意見徵集關門日，美國醫院協會把「上市後監測」的帳單寄回給開發商 On the day the docket closed, the AHA posted the post-market monitoring bill back to the developers

發生什麼What

2026-10-19，美國醫院協會（AHA）就 Docket FDA-2026-N-7874 送出意見書，收件人是 CDRH 主任 Michelle Tarver，由公共政策分析與發展資深副總裁 Ashley Thompson 具名。AHA 自述代表近 5,000 家會員醫院與醫療體系、逾 270,000 名合作醫師、200 萬名護理與照護人員、43,000 名醫療主管。核心主張只有一句：上市前與上市後監測的主要責任應落在開發商，因為醫院「缺乏獨立評估這些工具表現所需的資訊」，而黑盒子模型讓影響輸出準確度的瑕疵難以被發現。信裡附的數字包括：逾 96% 的 AI 醫材是走 510(k) 清關、AHA 自家健康資訊科技補充調查顯示 74% 的醫院得動用多個團隊（含高階主管、部門主管、資訊人員）評估預測式 AI、以及一篇被引述的 Nature 研究算出某些需要急診照護的病人誤檢傷率達 52%、非急症的過度檢傷率達 64%（AHA, 2026-10-19；Becker's Hospital Review）。 On 19 October 2026 the American Hospital Association filed comments on Docket FDA-2026-N-7874, addressed to CDRH Director Michelle Tarver and signed by Ashley Thompson, Senior Vice President for Public Policy Analysis and Development. The AHA says it speaks for nearly 5,000 member hospitals and health systems, more than 270,000 affiliated physicians, 2 million nurses and caregivers and 43,000 healthcare leaders. Its core claim is one sentence: primary responsibility for premarket and post-deployment monitoring belongs to developers, because hospitals lack the information needed to assess independently how these tools perform, and black-box models hide the flaws that degrade output accuracy. The figures in the letter: over 96% of AI-enabled devices clear through 510(k); the AHA's own Health IT supplemental survey finds 74% of hospitals need multiple teams — senior leaders, department heads, IT — to evaluate predictive AI; and a cited Nature study puts mis-triage for certain patients needing emergency care at 52% and over-triage of non-emergent cases at 64% (AHA, 19 Oct 2026; Becker's Hospital Review).

細項主張The asks

五項具體建議：(1) 修掉 FDA 提的風險啟發式，加入可逆性、下游防護、輸出可追溯性、錯誤發生機率，並拿掉或改寫「活動層級」這個變數——因為純資訊型工具也可能帶來嚴重風險；(2) 與聯邦貿易委員會（FTC）共同制定標示指引，區分資訊型與會採取行動的裝置、醫療與非醫療裝置，並載明臨床人員監督的程度；(3) 上市前與上市後活動統整進全產品生命週期（TPLC）；(4) 明確把生成式 AI 的臨床決策支援與行政工具排除在 FDA 監管之外；(5) 開發商負主責。另外 AHA 支持保留 ONC 認證健康資訊科技計畫裡的 AI 透明度條件，反對 HTI-5 規則草案把 model cards 拿掉，並要求跨部會協助偏鄉、關鍵接取與安全網醫院（AHA）。 Five concrete recommendations: (1) rework the FDA's risk heuristic to add reversibility, downstream safeguards, output traceability and likelihood of error, and update or drop the "activity level" variable, since informational tools can also carry serious risk; (2) write labelling guidance jointly with the Federal Trade Commission, separating informational from action-taking devices and medical from non-medical ones, and stating how much clinician oversight a tool carries; (3) fold premarket and post-deployment activity into a total product lifecycle approach; (4) state clearly that generative-AI clinical decision support and administrative tools fall outside FDA oversight; (5) put primary monitoring duty on developers. The AHA also backs keeping the AI transparency criteria in ONC's certified health IT programme, opposing their removal in the proposed HTI-5 rule, and asks for cross-agency help for rural, critical access and safety-net hospitals (AHA).

為什麼重要Why it matters

這不是在吵要不要監管，是在吵監管的成本掛在誰的帳上。FDA 討論文件丟出的四條可能路線裡，有一條就是「上市前放輕、上市後加重」；而「上市後」在實務上發生在醫院的伺服器裡、由醫院的資訊人員盯。AHA 等於提前把這條路線的財務與責任含意講明白：若 FDA 選它，監測義務必須以法規文字綁在開發商身上，否則 5,000 家醫院會自動繼承一份沒有編列預算的工作。這也是為什麼 AHA 同時反對 HTI-5 拿掉 model cards——那是醫院目前唯一能看進模型的制度化窗口。 The argument is not whether to regulate but on whose ledger the cost of regulating lands. One of the four paths floated in the FDA's discussion paper is lighter premarket review paired with heavier post-market monitoring — and post-market monitoring happens, in practice, on hospital servers, watched by hospital IT staff. The AHA is pricing that path in advance: if the FDA picks it, the monitoring duty has to be written onto developers in regulatory text, or 5,000 hospitals inherit an unbudgeted job by default. It is also why the AHA simultaneously opposes HTI-5's deletion of model cards — that is currently the only institutionalised window hospitals have into a model.

AHA 是醫院方的遊說團體，「責任歸開發商」本身就是它的利益位置。74% 這個數字出自 AHA 自家的補充調查，新聞稿未公開樣本數與抽樣方法；52%／64% 的檢傷數字由 AHA 信件轉述一篇 Nature 研究，本日報未取得原始論文，無法核對研究設計與適用範圍。 The AHA is the hospitals' lobby, and "developers own it" is its interest position stated as principle. The 74% comes from the AHA's own supplemental survey, with no sample size or sampling method in the release; the 52% and 64% triage figures reach us through the AHA's summary of a Nature study, and this report has not obtained the paper, so neither the design nor the scope has been checked.

對照Counterpoint U.S. ChamberPCCP10/19

同一天，美國商會要求：兩軸風險框架只能談證據期待，不能拿來決定分類與審查路徑 The same day, the U.S. Chamber asked that the two-axis risk framework shape evidence expectations only — never classification or pathway

發生什麼What

2026-10-19，美國商會就同一份 Docket 送出意見，開場就寫明「校準不當的框架——無論往哪一邊偏——都有延遲病人取得有益工具的風險」。具體要求包括：兩軸風險框架只用於風險管理與證據期待，不得用來決定裝置分類、前案（predicate）或上市前途徑；公布落軸的客觀判準與實作範例；不要預設面向病人的工具風險較高，風險應反映「輸出到臨床行動」的實際路徑，含人工覆核與急迫性；評估標準應針對特定任務在特定預期用途下的表現，而非籠統的「能力（competency）」，且 FDA 應與 AAMI、IEEE、HL7、ISO 等標準組織共同建立基準，「而非單方面制定評估準則」（U.S. Chamber of Commerce, 2026-10-19）。 On 19 October 2026 the U.S. Chamber of Commerce filed on the same docket, opening with the line that a framework "poorly calibrated, in either direction, risks delaying patient access to beneficial tools." Its asks: use the two-axis risk framework for risk management and evidence expectations only, not to set device classification, predicates or premarket pathway; publish objective criteria for placing a device on each axis, with worked examples; do not treat patient-facing tools as higher risk by default, since risk should track the actual path from output to clinical action, including human review and urgency; set evaluation on task-specific performance under a defined intended use rather than general "competency"; and build benchmarks with AAMI, IEEE, HL7 and ISO "rather than developing evaluation criteria unilaterally" (U.S. Chamber of Commerce, 19 Oct 2026).

最硬的一段The sharpest ask

變更管制。商會要求 FDA 講清楚 prompt 變更如何落進預定變更管制計畫（PCCP），並公布什麼算是需要重新送件的重大變更——信裡明寫「prompt 最佳化」不能一概視為小改；上游基礎模型更新何時需要重新評估或重新送件，也要有風險分級指引；FDA 對「不生產成品醫材的基礎模型開發商」究竟有多少管轄權，必須講明界線。基礎模型資訊的提交應為自願、範圍窄，且 FDA 應承諾不提交不會被視為缺失或拖慢審查，並提供含免於資訊自由法（FOIA）揭露的保密保護。至於 agentic AI，商會認為現在建框架為時過早，要求先開公開工作坊、與 ONC 和 CMS 協調，並支持 HHS 建立由各州選擇加入的全國臨床 AI 沙盒（U.S. Chamber）。 Change control. The Chamber wants the FDA to say how prompt changes fit inside a Predetermined Change Control Plan and to publish what counts as a significant change requiring a new submission — the letter states plainly that "prompt optimization" is not categorically minor. It wants risk-based guidance on when an upstream foundation-model update forces re-evaluation or resubmission, and a clear statement of the limits of FDA authority over foundation-model developers who build no finished device. Any foundation-model information programme should be voluntary and narrowly scoped, with an FDA commitment that non-submission will not be scored as a deficiency, plus confidentiality protection including against FOIA disclosure. On agentic AI the Chamber calls a formal framework premature: hold a public workshop first, coordinate with ONC and CMS, and back an HHS-run voluntary National Clinical AI Sandbox with state opt-in (U.S. Chamber).

為什麼重要Why it matters

把兩封信疊起來看，雙方其實在同一個支點上用力：上市後監測。AHA 要它成為開發商的法定義務；商會要它成為換取上市前減負的對價，而且要「事前公布合格監測計畫的內容」。差別在責任歸屬與成本落點。真正會決定結局的是第三件事——FDA 願不願意把「監測計畫」寫成可執行的法規要件，而不只是審查時的加分項。 Lay the two letters side by side and both press on the same fulcrum: post-market monitoring. The AHA wants it made a statutory duty of developers; the Chamber wants it treated as the consideration paid for a lighter premarket burden, with the contents of a qualifying monitoring plan published in advance. The difference is where responsibility and cost land. What actually decides the outcome is a third thing — whether the FDA is willing to write "monitoring plan" as an enforceable regulatory requirement rather than a credit earned at review.

要打折的地方Discount this

商會網頁全文約 14.3 萬字元，本日報單次擷取讀到約 10 萬字元，尚有約 4.3 萬字元未讀，以下敘述僅涵蓋已讀段落；另外商會是產業遊說方，「不要預設面向病人的工具風險較高」這條主張直接關係到消費端生成式產品的監管強度。 The Chamber's page runs to roughly 143,000 characters and this report read about the first 100,000, leaving some 43,000 unread; the account above covers only what was read. The Chamber is also the industry's lobby, and its ask that patient-facing tools not be presumed higher risk bears directly on how hard consumer-facing generative products get regulated.

That the list fell from 29 to 17 while actual output halved means the timing remains unknown — which is exactly what the Chamber was aiming at when it asked the FDA to publish a timeline for authoritative guidance.

英國UK MHRAAdvaMed10/20

MHRA 把「全生命週期＋互認」的議程帶進美國 AdvaMed，並在華府設一名常駐 FDA 聯絡官 The MHRA takes lifecycle regulation and mutual reliance to AdvaMed in the US, and will station an FDA liaison in Washington

發生什麼What

英國藥品與醫療產品管理局（MHRA）於 2026-10-19 預告，AI 與軟體副主任 Jessica Paulsen 將於 10/20（週二）在 AdvaMed 說明其軟體與 AI 醫材監管路線：更有彈性的生命週期監管、監理沙盒、以及更緊密的國際合作。MHRA 2030 策略底下的項目包括：為「AI as a medical device」建立現代且可調適的框架、成立新的 MHRA Centre for AI and Digital Health、改善醫材資料與可追溯性、擴大與受信任監管機關（含 FDA）的依賴與承認路徑。Paulsen 的原話是：「AI is changing medical technology, and regulation must keep pace.」（GOV.UK, 2026-10-19） The UK's Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency trailed on 19 October 2026 that Jessica Paulsen, its Deputy Director for AI and Software, would set out its approach to software and AI-enabled devices at AdvaMed on Tuesday 20 October: more flexible lifecycle regulation, regulatory sandboxes, closer international collaboration. Under the MHRA 2030 Strategy that means a modern, adaptive framework for AI as a medical device, a new MHRA Centre for AI and Digital Health, better device data and traceability, and expanded reliance and recognition routes with trusted regulators including the FDA. Paulsen's line: "AI is changing medical technology, and regulation must keep pace." (GOV.UK, 19 Oct 2026)

具體機制The machinery

MHRA–FDA Pioneer Programme（2026 年 6 月宣布）在兩家監管機關互設聯絡官；MHRA 正在招募一名常駐華府的 Regional Deputy Director of US Partnerships，作為其派駐 FDA 的聯絡官。雙方仍各自獨立做決定，但 MHRA 明說想探索更多國際依賴路徑，包括針對已獲 FDA 核准的醫材。沙盒方面，AI Airlock 已被用來測試生命週期取徑，並新增與 NHS London、Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust 的區域夥伴關係，在真實醫療場域測試技術（GOV.UK）。 The MHRA–FDA Pioneer Programme, announced in June 2026, creates reciprocal liaison roles at both agencies; the MHRA is recruiting a Regional Deputy Director of US Partnerships based in Washington, D.C. to serve as its liaison officer to the FDA. Both agencies keep making independent decisions, but the MHRA says openly that it wants to explore greater use of international reliance routes, including for devices already approved by the FDA. On the sandbox side, AI Airlock has already been used to test the lifecycle approach, and new regional partnerships with NHS London and Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust will test technologies in live care settings (GOV.UK).

為什麼重要Why it matters

在 FDA 自己還在徵集意見、歐盟剛把高風險義務往後推一年的時候，MHRA 選擇把「我們已經想清楚了」這件事直接賣到美國醫材產業的主場。對廠商而言，真正的誘因不是沙盒，是互認：如果 FDA 已核准的醫材能走國際依賴路徑進英國，英國就從「第三個要跑的市場」變成「第一個市場的延伸」。這也是英國在脫歐後能拿出的少數結構性籌碼。 With the FDA still taking comment and the EU having just pushed high-risk duties back a year, the MHRA chose to sell "we have already worked this out" on the American medtech industry's home turf. For manufacturers the real inducement is not the sandbox but reliance: if a device already approved by the FDA can enter the UK through an international reliance route, Britain stops being a third market to run and becomes an extension of the first. 沒有改的部分What did not move

The Article 6(1) classification logic from MDCG 2025-6 is unchanged, and devices going through notified body assessment are generally still high-risk. The Article 50 transparency duties still apply from 2 August 2026, with a watermarking grace period to 2 December 2026 for systems already on the market before that date. Article 5 prohibitions and the GPAI rules are unchanged, with AI Office enforcement over GPAI models starting 2 August 2026, and a new Article 5 prohibition on AI-generated child sexual abuse material and non-consensual intimate imagery takes effect 2 December 2026 (not device-specific). Separately the safety-component definition is narrowed, so functions that merely assist users or optimise performance are no longer automatically high-risk, and a simplified registration duty for systems claiming the Article 6(3) exemption is reinstated; the Commission may use implementing acts to limit overlapping requirements where sectoral law already imposes equivalents — those acts have not been issued (COSM).

為什麼重要Why it matters

時間差本身就是訊號。延後的理由是調和標準（harmonised standards）遲到，不是義務放鬆——歐盟等於承認：在沒有可依循的技術標準前，要求廠商證明「資料治理、穩健性、人為監督」合規，是不可執行的。對照英國把答案押在沙盒與上市後監測、美國還在問要不要管，歐盟是唯一已經把義務寫成法條、卻發現自己缺少量尺的那一個。而對廠商最實際的影響是：高風險那一包可以慢一年，但面向病人或家屬的生成式互動（第 50 條標示義務）12 月初就要到位。 The gap between the two dates is itself the message. The deferral is blamed on late harmonised standards, not on softened duties — the EU is in effect conceding that, absent technical standards to work to, asking manufacturers to demonstrate compliance on data governance, robustness and human oversight is unenforceable. Set against Britain betting on sandboxes and post-market monitoring, and America still asking whether to regulate at all, the EU is the only one that has written the obligations into law and then found it has no measuring stick. The practical consequence for manufacturers: the high-risk package slips a year, but generative interactions facing patients or families — the Article 50 labelling duties — have to be in place by early December.

In the same window, the "Guideline for Inspection under the Medical Device Good Manufacturing Practices" was issued on 15 September 2026 and takes effect 1 November 2026, breaking the new GMP into 200 inspection items — 39 critical, 88 major, 73 general; the revised GMP itself was published in November 2025 and took effect 1 October 2026, with an accompanying GMP/GVP suite shifting post-market oversight toward continuous inspection. A further 130 Class II device draft guidelines were published on 1 September 2026 (16 IVD, 55 non-active, 59 active), with feedback closing 8 October 2026 (China Med Device, NMPA Roundup October 2026). At policy level, the NMPA issued its "Implementation Opinions on 'Artificial Intelligence + Drug Regulation'" in August 2026 (NMPA English).

為什麼重要Why it matters

把四個管轄區排在一起，只有中國大陸同時推出了「AI 專屬指導原則」與「可執行的檢查項目表」。美國在問該不該管、歐盟承認缺標準、英國把答案押在沙盒與路線圖；NMPA 的做法是先給框架再給檢查清單，200 項裡 39 項關鍵的設計，本質上就是把生命週期監管變成可稽核的動作。對出口廠商而言，這也意味著中國市場的合規門檻在 11/1 之後是具體可量的——這一點，目前其他三個管轄區都還給不出來。 Of the four jurisdictions, only China has put out both AI-specific guidance and an enforceable inspection checklist in the same season. America is asking whether to regulate, the EU concedes it lacks standards, Britain is betting on a sandbox and a roadmap; the NMPA's move is framework first, then checklist — and a 200-item scheme with 39 critical findings is, in substance, lifecycle regulation turned into auditable actions. For exporters that also means the compliance bar in China becomes concretely measurable after 1 November, which is more than the other three can currently offer.

02 — Product Analysis

法規日沒有新模型可拆，但有兩個產品正好站在不同管轄區的斷層線上：一個被英國的醫材定義擠出市場，一個被美國某一州的沙盒放進市場。 A regulation day offers no new model to dissect, but two products sit squarely on different jurisdictional fault lines: one squeezed out of a market by a device definition, the other let into one by a single state's sandbox.

Ash — Slingshot AI

消費端大型語言模型心理支持產品 · Slingshot AI（美國）Consumer LLM mental-health product · Slingshot AI (US)

功能與定位。Ash 是直接面向消費者的大型語言模型心理支持應用，公司自我定位為「wellbeing（身心健康）」產品而非醫療器材。Slingshot AI 累計募得 9,300 萬美元，投資人包括 Andreessen Horowitz。2026-01-21，公司宣布因可能與英國醫療器材法規衝突而退出英國市場，2026-01-23 之後 Ash 在英國不再提供；執行長 Daniel Reid Cahn 致信使用者時寫道「像我們這樣的 wellbeing 產品沒有清楚的法規路徑」，公司並表示正與政府商討解方（STAT News, 2026-01-21）。 Function and position. Ash is a direct-to-consumer LLM mental-health app that its maker positions as a "wellbeing" product rather than a medical device. Slingshot AI has raised $93 million, with investors including Andreessen Horowitz. On 21 January 2026 the company said it was withdrawing Ash from the United Kingdom because the app might conflict with the country's medical device rules, and that Ash would not be available there after 23 January; CEO Daniel Reid Cahn emailed users that "there isn't a clear regulatory pathway for wellbeing products like ours," and the company said it was in talks with the government about a remedy (STAT News, 21 Jan 2026).

  • 優勢Strength ：在美國，它正好落在今天這場爭論的空白處。FDA 2026 年 1 月放寬 CDS 指引、商會又主張「不要預設面向病人的工具風險較高」（U.S. Chamber, 2026-10-19），而加州 SB 903 遭否決，等於把「AI 心理治療的專法」這條路暫時關掉——消費端產品因此仍有運作空間。 : in the US it lands precisely in the gap today's argument leaves open. The FDA loosened CDS guidance in January 2026, the Chamber is arguing that patient-facing tools should not be presumed higher risk (U.S. Chamber, 19 Oct 2026), and California's SB 903 was vetoed, shutting off a dedicated AI-psychotherapy statute for now — so consumer products still have room to operate.
  • 疑慮Concern ：「不是醫材」是一種主張，不是一個地位。英國已經示範過：當主管機關不同意這個分類，產品就只能退場。而 FDA 已把「Generative AI-Enabled Conversational Devices for Mental Disorders: Evidentiary Recommendations」排進 FY2027 草案清單（FDA Law Blog, 2026-10-18），加州 AB 1979 又把消費者醫療聊天機器人業者就 CMIA 目的視為醫療提供者——兩邊都在往這類產品的方向收。此外，STAT 報導未揭露 Ash 的使用者數，本日報也無法評估其退出英國的營收影響。 : "not a device" is a claim, not a status. Britain has already demonstrated what happens when the regulator disagrees with the classification — the product leaves. And the FDA has put "Generative AI-Enabled Conversational Devices for Mental Disorders: Evidentiary Recommendations" on its FY2027 draft list (FDA Law Blog, 18 Oct 2026), while California's AB 1979 treats consumer health-care chatbot businesses as providers for CMIA purposes. Both are closing in. The STAT report also gives no user numbers, so the revenue impact of the UK exit cannot be assessed here.

Doctronic — Utah AI Policy Sandbox

州級監理沙盒下的 AI 處方續開系統 · 美國猶他州AI prescription-renewal system inside a state regulatory sandbox · Utah, US

功能與定位。Doctronic 在猶他州人工智慧政策辦公室（Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy）的監理沙盒下，處理已由持照醫師開立藥物的 30 日、60 日或 90 日續方。系統不能開立新處方、不能處理管制或成癮性藥物、不能變更治療計畫。目前仍在第一階段，每一筆續方都須由持照醫事人員核准後才發出；州方表示每個階段都有「廣泛的身分與處方驗證程序」（Utah News Dispatch, 2026-04-28）。 Function and position. Under the Utah Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy's regulatory sandbox, Doctronic processes 30-, 60- and 90-day renewals of medications already prescribed by licensed physicians. The system cannot issue new prescriptions, handle controlled or addictive substances, or change a treatment plan. It remains in phase one, in which a licensed practitioner must approve every renewal before it is issued; the state says each phase carries "extensive identity and prescription verification protocols" (Utah News Dispatch, 28 Apr 2026).

  • 優勢Strength ：這是全美第一個把 AI 推進處方續開的州級沙盒，而沙盒本身正是今天各方都在要的東西——美國商會向 FDA 要「全國臨床 AI 沙盒」（U.S. Chamber），英國則把 AI Airlock 做到第三期並加碼三年經費（GOV.UK, 2026-10-06）。州方也表示迄今無嚴重安全事件，且上線前已有醫療專業人員審查、採納其多項調整建議。 : it is the first state-level sandbox in the US to push AI into prescription renewal, and the sandbox is exactly what everyone is asking for today — the Chamber wants a National Clinical AI Sandbox from the FDA (U.S. Chamber), while Britain has taken AI Airlock to a third phase with three more years of funding (GOV.UK, 6 Oct 2026). The state also reports no serious safety incidents so far, and says medical professionals reviewed the pilot before launch with many of their suggested adjustments built in.
  • 疑慮Concern ：沙盒的授權來自行政部門，而執照的授權來自醫事委員會——猶他州醫事執照委員會已致函人工智慧政策辦公室要求立即暫停，理由是委員會應在實施前被諮詢，而非系統上線後；委員會並主張續方需要醫師重新評估劑量調整、副作用監測、禁忌症與新增的藥物交互作用，並寫下「我們不能讓 AI 或其他財務動機凌駕這項義務」。一份第三方紅隊測試報告指出「在對抗性使用下的潛在弱點」，但未公布數量。州方拒絕現在暫停，理由是仍在需逐筆醫師覆核的第一階段，並稱若未達安全基準可修改或取消計畫（Utah News Dispatch；STAT News, 2026-04-24）。 : the sandbox's authority comes from the executive branch while licensure's comes from the medical board — and Utah's Medical Licensing Board has written to the Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy demanding immediate suspension, on the grounds that it should have been consulted before implementation rather than after go-live. The board argues refills require physician reassessment for dose adjustment, side-effect monitoring, contraindications and new drug interactions, and wrote that "we must not allow AI or other financial motivations to override this obligation." A third-party red-teaming report found "potential vulnerabilities under adversarial usage," with no count given. The state declined to suspend, citing phase one's per-refill physician review and saying it may modify or cancel the programme if safety benchmarks are not met (Utah News Dispatch; STAT News, 24 Apr 2026).

兩個案例指向同一個缺口。Ash 證明了「自稱不是醫材」在遇到分類權時毫無抵抗力；Doctronic 證明了「拿到沙盒許可」在遇到執照權時同樣沒有抵抗力。今天各方都在談上市後監測與沙盒，但兩件事都預設了一個前提：誰有權認定這個東西是什麼。這個前提在美國還沒有答案，而這正是 FDA 與各州分歧的根。 Both cases point at the same gap. Ash shows that calling yourself "not a device" offers no resistance when someone else holds the classification power; Doctronic shows that holding a sandbox permission offers no resistance when someone else holds the licensure power. Everyone is talking about post-market monitoring and sandboxes today, but both presuppose an answer to a prior question: who gets to decide what the thing is. America has not settled that, and it is the root of the FDA-versus-states split.

All four major markets agree AI devices need continuous watching; they disagree only on who pays, who classifies and who penalises. Britain gathers all three into one agency, with the MHRA running sandbox, monitoring and reliance together; China turns it into 200 inspection items; the EU has written the law and admits it has no measuring stick; and America splits in half between a federal government regulating the product and states regulating practice — and the crack is exactly where Ash and Doctronic are standing.

04 — Taiwan Angle

台灣在這一輪已經不是「還沒開始」，而是三條線各自跑得不錯、但沒有綁在一起 Taiwan is not behind in this round so much as running three lines in parallel that have never been tied together

(1) 指引這一條，台灣已經做了 AHA 要 FDA 做的一半。衛福部於 2026-05-29 以衛部醫字第 1151663164 號函發布《醫療機構應用生成式人工智慧指引》，適用於準備導入或已導入生成式 AI 的公私立醫院與診所，涵蓋病歷撰寫輔助、臨床決策支援、行政文書與病人溝通，並明確暫不納入具自主決策能力的 AI Agent 系統。指引列出六類風險（基礎模型偏差、外部資料來源風險、幻覺、提示注入與資料外洩等安全攻擊、過度依賴侵蝕臨床判斷、外部模型或雲端服務中斷）、五大原則與橫跨三個生命週期階段的九項作為，並要求與廠商的契約管理包含揭露所使用的大型語言模型、由持照醫事人員負最終臨床判斷與病歷責任、生成式 AI 與病人互動或錄音錄影時須告知目的與限制（理律法律事務所整理, 2026-06-26）。缺的那一半，正是 AHA 信裡最硬的一句——它是行政指導而非強制，也沒有把監測主責分配給開發商。 (1) On guidance, Taiwan has already done half of what the AHA is asking the FDA to do. On 29 May 2026 the Ministry of Health and Welfare issued its Guideline on the Use of Generative AI in Medical Institutions (letter No. 衛部醫字第1151663164號), covering public and private hospitals and clinics preparing to adopt or already using generative AI across record-drafting assistance, clinical decision support, administrative documents and patient communication, and expressly leaving out AI agent systems with autonomous decision-making for now. It sets out six risk categories (foundation-model bias, external data source risks, hallucination, security attacks such as prompt injection and data leakage, overreliance eroding clinical judgment, and outages of external models or cloud services), five core principles and nine provisions across three lifecycle phases, and requires vendor contract management to include disclosure of the large language model used, licensed clinicians to bear final responsibility for clinical judgment and records, and patients to be told the purpose and limits whenever generative AI interacts with them or records audio or video (Lee and Li newsletter, 26 Jun 2026). The missing half is the hardest line in the AHA letter: the guideline is administrative advice, not a mandate, and it assigns no primary monitoring duty to developers.

(2) 歐盟那一年的緩衝，對台灣醫材輸歐業者是真的，但不要讀成全面延後。Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 把 Annex I 內嵌型高風險系統（含 MDR／IVDR 項下的 AI 醫材）的義務推到 2028-08-02，多出一年來處理資料治理、技術文件、記錄保存與人為監督的佐證；但 第 50 條的透明度義務自 2026-08-02 已經上路，既有系統的浮水印寬限期 2026-12-02 到期。換句話說，台灣廠商若在歐盟市場提供會與病人或家屬直接互動的生成式功能，要先處理的是標示，不是技術文件——而這件事距今不到七週（COSM）。另外值得盯的是，執委會原本提議把 MDR／IVDR 從 Annex I Section A 移到 Section B 的 COM(2025) 1023 並未被採納，AI 醫材在歐盟仍是高風險，分類邏輯照 MDCG 2025-6 不變。 (2) The EU's extra year is real for Taiwanese exporters, but it is not a blanket postponement. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 pushes the obligations for Annex I embedded high-risk systems — AI devices under the MDR and IVDR among them — to 2 August 2028, buying a year to assemble evidence on data governance, technical documentation, logging and human oversight. But the Article 50 transparency duties have applied since 2 August 2026, and the watermarking grace period for systems already on the market expires on 2 December 2026. For a Taiwanese manufacturer shipping generative features that interact directly with patients or families in the EU, the first thing to fix is labelling, not technical documentation — and that is under seven weeks away (COSM). Worth watching too: the Commission's COM(2025) 1023 proposal to move the MDR and IVDR from Annex I Section A to Section B was not adopted, so AI-enabled devices stay high-risk in the EU and the classification logic of MDCG 2025-6 is unchanged.

(3) 實證、給付、查驗登記三條線都在跑，但沒有像 MHRA 那樣綁成一套。衛福部自 2024 年起建置 AI 影響性研究中心體系，2025-11-20 在「台灣臨床 AI 影響力論壇 2025」正式啟動，由衛福部資訊處處長李建璋代表；北榮、中榮、三總、成大、台大五家醫學中心為示範場域，目前正執行 AI 相關臨床試驗，預計約三年後把本土資料送進健保 HTA 評估中心、再進共擬會討論是否納入給付——李建璋並點出關鍵：給付「一旦納入幾乎不會退場」，所以早期設計要謹慎（聯合報, 2025-11-20）。查驗登記端，食藥署的智慧醫療器材專案辦公室早在 2021-05-07 配合《醫療器材管理法》施行而設立，提供 AI／機器學習醫材的單一窗口與全生命週期管理輔導，並負責研擬 AI 醫材政策指引（衛福部）。對照 MHRA 把沙盒、上市後監測與國際互認收在同一個機關、同一份 44 項路線圖底下，台灣的三條線各自健全，但「在沙盒裡跑出來的實證能不能直接接上健保共擬會、能不能回頭簡化查驗登記」這個接口，目前沒有制度化。而這正是今天四個管轄區全都在搶的東西。 (3) Evidence, reimbursement and registration are all moving, but none is tied to the others the way the MHRA has tied them. The Ministry has been building an AI impact research centre system since 2024, formally launched at the Taiwan Clinical AI Impact Forum 2025 on 20 November 2025 by Li Chien-chang, Director of the Ministry's Information Technology Office; five medical centres — Taipei Veterans General, Taichung Veterans General, Tri-Service General, National Cheng Kung University Hospital and National Taiwan University Hospital — serve as demonstration sites running AI clinical trials, with local data expected to reach the NHI HTA evaluation centre and then the joint drafting committee in about three years. Li put the crux well: once something is covered by the NHI it almost never leaves, so the early design has to be careful (United Daily News, 20 Nov 2025). On the registration side, the TFDA's Smart Medical Device Project Office was set up on 7 May 2021 alongside the Medical Devices Act, offering a single window and lifecycle-management counselling for AI and machine-learning devices and drafting AI device policy guidance (MOHW). Set against the MHRA holding sandbox, post-market monitoring and international reliance inside one agency under one 44-point roadmap, Taiwan's three lines are each sound — but the joint between them, whether evidence generated in a pilot can feed the NHI committee directly and whether it can simplify registration in turn, is not institutionalised. That joint is precisely what all four jurisdictions were competing over today.
